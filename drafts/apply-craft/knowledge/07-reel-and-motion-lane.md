# 07 · Reel and the motion design lane

The specialty vertical. It combines research from this thread with priors from the existing `motion-design-career-strategist` skill. Those priors are labeled **EST** where that skill's reference marks them as estimates.

---

## 1. Sub-lanes (motion is not one job)

| Sub-lane | Typical employers | What decides | Reel role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand and launch motion | Big-tech marketing, AI labs, Netflix Brand Creative Studio, agencies | Campaign craft, systems across formats, idea plus execution | Opens doors (EST) |
| Product and UI motion | Big-tech product orgs, startups | Written case studies with shipped outcomes, specs, engineering handoff, reduced motion | Supports; case studies decide (EST) |
| 3D / CG / launch films | Hardware brands, studios, agencies | Craft, lighting, render quality, breakdowns | Central; breakdowns required |
| Broadcast / live / title | Streamers (e.g., Netflix "Live"), networks | Systems, packages, real-time constraints, multi-format | Central |
| Studio / freelance | Motion studios | Reel plus taste plus speed | Central |

**Routing rule:** identify the sub-lane from the postings first (`apply-target`). Reel strategy and portfolio mode follow from it.

## 2. Reels

**Length**

| Source | Guidance | Strength |
|---|---|---|
| School of Motion | About 20–60 seconds; never more than 2 minutes | C |
| Artisan Talent (creative recruiter) | Ideally under a minute; at most 60–90 seconds | C |
| Netflix Animation | The first minute must catch the reviewer's eye | A |

**First seconds**
- Several sources say reviewers decide in the first 10–30 seconds. *(C/D)*
- So the best and most role-relevant work goes first.

**Netflix Animation guidelines** *(A)*
- Contact info at the start and end.
- Breakdowns, with software noted on screen.
- Tailor to the role.
- Name, contact, and portfolio URL (with password) always visible.

**Role clarity**
- Label your role on each shot (on screen or in a linked shot list).
- "Unclear personal contribution" is a common rejection reason (EST).

**Review workflow** (the model can't watch video):
1. `[ME]` Export a shot list: timecode, project, your exact role, tools.
2. `[ME]` Export key frames at 0, 5, 10, 20, and 30 seconds, plus the final frame.
3. `[AI]` Audit order, relevance, role labels, padding, and fit to the sub-lane. Optionally hand frames to the `composition` skill.
4. `[ME]` First-20-seconds test: show it to two people who don't know your work; write down what they say you do.
5. `[AI]` Produce an edit list: keep / cut / reorder, with timecodes; title and end cards; on-screen role labels.

**Hosting**
- Links must work without a login.
- Password protection only if needed, and tested in a private window.
- No autoplay with sound; captions where there's dialogue; a poster frame.
- Open research: Vimeo vs. YouTube privacy and quality (`11`).

## 3. Case studies for motion (Staff-grade template, from the strategist reference)

```
## [PROJECT NAME]
- Context and stakes: [the problem; who it affected]
- Role and scope: [teams and engineers involved; what I owned]
- Constraints: [platforms, performance budget, accessibility, timeline]
- System or principles: [springs, tokens, choreography rules I defined]
- Build and handoff: [Rive files, code prototypes, specs; how engineering implemented it]
- Outcome: [adoption, engineering time saved, metrics, qualitative evidence]
- What I'd change: [trade-offs, judgment]
```

Open every study with a ~100-word summary block (`06`) before this structure.

## 4. Common rejection reasons (EST, from the strategist reference)

- Beautiful clips with no problem or outcome.
- Can't explain why a motion choice exists.
- Never worked with engineers through to shipping.
- Ignores reduced motion and performance.
- All single-feature scope (gets leveled at Senior, or rejected for Staff).
- Unclear personal contribution on team work.

## 5. What top postings want (EST, cross-checked against Netflix postings)

- 7–10+ years for senior roles, with motion shipped at scale.
- After Effects, plus Rive and/or Lottie, plus prototyping (Figma, ProtoPie, Origami, Framer, SwiftUI).
- Specs and tokens that engineers can implement; comfort with springs and physics.
- Evidence of building or evolving a motion system.
- Netflix Brand Creative Studio motion manager: motion across campaigns, systems thinking, multi-format work (VERIFIED).
- 3D (Cinema 4D, Blender) for brand and hardware work; basic code for design-technologist roles.

## 6. Interview loop (EST: big tech and AI labs)

| Stage | How to do well |
|---|---|
| Recruiter and hiring-manager screen | Lead with the scope narrative; state level expectations |
| Portfolio presentation (45–60 min) | Two deep case studies; spend the time on decisions and trade-offs |
| Craft deep dive | Defend timing, easing vs. springs, choreography; know your spring parameters |
| App critique | Critique a live product's motion against principles; rank fixes by impact and effort |
| Exercise (take-home or live) | Keep it scoped and interactive; include a one-page spec |
| Cross-functional | Feasibility, performance, roadmap trade-offs |
| Leadership (Staff+) | Influence without authority; mentoring |

Netflix-specific loop: **not yet verified** (see `11`).
