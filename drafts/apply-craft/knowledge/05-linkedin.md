# 05 · LinkedIn lane

**Prompt:** `prompts/linkedin-challenge-v2.md` (ready)

---

## Key difference from the resume

One public page serves every application. Tailor it to the **role family**, keep it consistent with every resume, and let it be broader than any single resume. Jobscan notes LinkedIn doesn't need re-tailoring for each application.

## Evidence

**How recruiters find profiles**
- 75% of recruiters source candidates on LinkedIn; 73.3% always or often check a candidate's profile; about 47% check it against the application (Jobscan 2025). *(C)*
- **AI-Assisted Search** is now the default: recruiters describe the role in plain language. **Hiring Assistant** (generally available September 2025) ranks candidates, reportedly weighing trajectory, related skills, and engagement, and can surface previous applicants. *(C)*

**Spotlights** (filters recruiters can turn on; LinkedIn Recruiter materials, older but primary; A–)

| Spotlight | Who it surfaces |
|---|---|
| Open to Work | People who shared they're open within the last 90 days |
| More likely to respond | Based on messaging history and engagement |
| Engaged with your talent brand | Followed the company page, or liked, shared, or commented on its posts or job posts |
| Has company connections | Connected to people at the company |
| Past applicants | Previous applicants |

**Skills**
- Up to 100 skills, each attachable to positions.
- **Skills Match** compares a job's skills with the explicit Skills list (LinkedIn Help; A) and, per a third-party explainer, with implicit skills extracted from the headline, About, titles, and descriptions (B).
- 5+ skills: up to 17x more profile views (LinkedIn marketing deck; correlational).

**LinkedIn's "Rock Your Profile" figures** (April 2025; all "up to"; correlational)

| Profile element | Lift |
|---|---|
| Photo | 21x views, 36x messages, 9x connection requests |
| Up-to-date positions | 8x views, 10x messages |
| Industry listed | 9x views |
| Volunteer experience | 6x views |

**Verification** (identity, workplace, or school): LinkedIn Help says verified members see 60% more profile views and 50% more engagement on average.

**Open to Work**
- The public frame made members about 40% more likely to get recruiter InMails (LinkedIn data, 2020; repeated to Fortune in 2024).
- The "recruiters only" mode tries to hide the signal from recruiters at the user's current company but can't guarantee complete privacy (LinkedIn Help).
- The view that the public frame looks desperate, especially at senior levels, is opinion, not data.

**Editing without broadcasting**
- Settings & Privacy → Visibility → "Share job changes, education changes, and work anniversaries from profile." Turn it off before editing.
- Job updates can reach the network within two hours (LinkedIn Help).

**Field limits**

| Field | Limit |
|---|---|
| Headline | 220 characters (cards and search show roughly the first 60–120) |
| About | 2,600 characters (a few lines show before "see more") |
| Position title | 100 |
| Position description | 2,000 |
| Each skill | 80 |
| Connection note | 300 |

**Profile export:** on desktop, More (or Resources) → Save to PDF. It covers headline, About, experience, education, and skills. It does **not** include Featured, photo, activity, or settings.

**Content and photo**
- Originality.ai estimated 54% of long LinkedIn posts in late 2024 were likely AI-written (detector vendor; C).
- Ringover 2024 (1,087 recruiters): in blind tests, 76.5% preferred AI headshots; 66% would be put off by an undisclosed AI headshot they recognized; 88% want disclosure. Reported policy: the photo must be your real likeness. *(C)*

**Scams**
- Malwarebytes found #OpenToWork posts attract fake recruiters leading to phishing and malware. *(B)*
- FTC-reported job-scam losses rose from $90M (2020) to $501M (2024). *(A–)*

## Rules (from challenger passes)

- **Headline names truthful work** (AC-L-003). Target titles go in Open to Work preferences, Skills, and the About closing line.
- Level numbers are stripped from Open to Work titles and search terms.
- If the search is confidential: recruiters-only Open to Work, sharing turned off before edits, no job-hunt signal in public text.
- Attach the role family's must-have skills to the current position where truthful.
- Refresh Open to Work preferences at least every 90 days.
- Reply to relevant InMails promptly, even to decline.
- Engage with target companies only where there's something real to say.
- Scam screen before engaging with any recruiter outreach.
