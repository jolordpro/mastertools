# SCOUT — Case against

**Built by: Opus 4.8 · July 19, 2026 · v0.1**
Pre-registered at build time, per house law. Where this skill fails, and what
guards each failure.

1. **Process tax on small questions.** The doctrine makes a 10-minute question
   cost 40. Guard: the tier ladder with L0 dormancy, and the standing rule
   that tier is stated out loud — a mis-tiered hunt is a correctable event,
   not a silent one. If the user says "quick," it's L0/L1, full stop.

2. **Ritual without rigor.** Templates can be filled with theater — a source
   log of aggregators, verdicts graded "clean" in name only. This risk is
   proven live: the skill's own second research pass claimed "run under scout
   v0.2" while resting load-bearing claims on unfetched search excerpts (law
   2) and leaving a single-source claim unflagged (law 5). Guard: the
   spot-check contract (the user opens links), the ladder-rung column, the
   clean-context flag on every verdict block, and the standing rule that any
   compliance claim names which laws were exercised.

3. **The doctrine rots.** Principles cite 2025–26 sources; tool behavior,
   ranking dynamics, and model capabilities all drift. Guard: the principles
   encode why over what; every claim is dated and labeled; staleness check
   rides the quarterly system audit. The kill word applies to the skill's own
   examples.

4. **Overlap creep.** Scout's laws rhyme with shakedown's checks and challenger's
   research labels; drift could produce double-fired protocol. Guard: the
   jurisdiction section is explicit — scout produces evidence, companions
   consume it — and the eval battery includes negative fixtures where scout
   must stay silent.

5. **Single-agent ceiling.** The strongest sourced result (90.2% multi-agent
   gain) comes from an architecture scout usually doesn't have. The lane-spec
   transfer is Inferred, not measured. Honest label: scout captures the
   decomposition discipline, not the parallel token budget. When subagents
   exist (Cowork, Code), use them.

6. **The evidence base is thin where it matters most.** Two primary
   engineering posts, one survey, and an internal research corpus — strong sources,
   small N. No public benchmark measures research-skill quality for this use
   shape. All effectiveness claims about scout itself are unmeasured until
   the eval battery and a live program run say otherwise. Same standard as
   Adobe-agent claims: unmeasured means unmeasured.
