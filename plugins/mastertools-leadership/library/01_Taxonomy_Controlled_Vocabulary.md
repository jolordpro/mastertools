## Controlled Vocabulary, Concept Hierarchy & Relationship Map
### Leadership & Talent Cascading Knowledge Library
**Version:** 1.2 (Hardened Control Layer — July 2026)  
**Status:** Canonical Control Document  
**Parent Document:** 00_START_HERE.md (v1.2)  
**Purpose:** Provide a stable, non-ambiguous, retrieval-optimized controlled vocabulary and concept hierarchy so that every Super Page, diagnostic, behavioral signal, and AI Coach response uses consistent language and explicit relationships. This prevents semantic drift, improves RAG precision, and enables multi-hop reasoning across the library.

---

## 1. Design Rules for This Taxonomy

1. **Controlled, not exhaustive** — We define only the terms that appear repeatedly across Super Pages and that carry precise meaning in this library. Ordinary English words are not redefined.
2. **Hierarchical + Relational** — Terms live in a hierarchy (broader → narrower) and also have explicit “enables”, “requires”, “cascades into”, “threatens”, “contrasts with”, “integrates with”, and “is moderated by” relationships.
3. **Level-Aware** — Many concepts differentiate by leadership level: Individual Contributor → Leading Others → Leading Leaders → Enterprise / Board.
4. **AI-Era Annotated** — Terms that change meaning or gain criticality under AI transformation are flagged.
5. **Behavioral Anchor Preference** — Abstract concepts are always linked to observable behaviors wherever possible.
6. **Person-vs-System Aware** — Constructs that support the core decision of person-focused coaching vs. system redesign are explicitly named and first-class.
7. **Versioned** — New terms or relationship changes are logged; old meanings are never silently overwritten.

## 7. How This Taxonomy Is Used by the AI Coach

1. Query understanding and intent classification map to these controlled terms.
2. Retrieval prefers Super Pages and chunks that match exact controlled vocabulary.
3. Multi-hop reasoning follows the explicit relationship types defined in Section 3.
4. Responses must use preferred terminology.
5. When a user uses a near-synonym, the Coach should gently map it to the controlled term and proceed with library precision.
6. New concepts proposed in conversation are evaluated against this taxonomy before being treated as canonical.
7. Person-vs-System decision points are always evaluated when action is recommended.
8. Escalation triggers and anti-patterns from 00_START_HERE are binding.
