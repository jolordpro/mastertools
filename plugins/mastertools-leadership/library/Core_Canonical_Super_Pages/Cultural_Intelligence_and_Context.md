## 1. Core Definition & Scope

**Cultural Intelligence (CQ)** is the capability to function effectively across national, ethnic, organizational, and other cultural contexts. It includes metacognitive, cognitive, motivational, and behavioral dimensions.

**Contextual Intelligence / Contextual Fluency** in this library is the broader ability to read, adapt to, and wisely intervene in the specific cultural, power, historical, and organizational context in which cascade health must be built — without losing the universal requirements of psychological safety, energy sustainability, Enabling Leadership, clean judgment, and generativity.

This Super Page does **not** relativize away the core substrates. Safety, energy surplus for leadership work, capacity for complexity, Enabling Leadership, clean sponsorship, and long-term human evaluative capacity remain non-negotiable for healthy cascades. What varies is *how* these are expressed, sensed, developed, and governed across cultures, and what counts as signal vs. noise.

This Super Page equips Leaders of Leaders and the AI Coach to:
- Calibrate diagnosis and intervention for power distance, collectivism/individualism, uncertainty avoidance, and other relevant dimensions
- Avoid ethnocentric imposition of practices that damage trust or miss real cascade risk
- Avoid cultural relativism that excuses genuine toxicity, safety collapse, or Judgment Depreciation
- Adapt sensing, sponsorship, generativity, Passage support, board challenge norms, and AI trust/override practices to context with concrete design patterns
- Develop individual and systemic CQ as cascade-health capability
- Integrate cultural calibration into every other Super Page’s application

Cultural miscalibration is a primary source of false positives (labeling tough-but-fair or culturally different leadership as toxic) and false negatives (missing real safety collapse or toxic patterns because “that’s just the culture”).

## 2. Key Frameworks & Models

### 2.1 Core Cultural Dimensions Relevant to Cascade Health
High-leverage dimensions for this library (drawing on Hofstede, GLOBE, and related work, applied specifically to cascade dynamics):

- **Power Distance**: Acceptance of unequal power distribution. High power distance shapes voice, challenge, skip-level sensing, board upward truth-telling, reverse mentoring, and what “psychological safety” looks and feels like.
- **Individualism–Collectivism**: Preference for individual vs. group goals and identity. Shapes sponsorship (who is seen as “ours”), generativity, credit/blame, and loyalty dynamics that can mask or enable toxic patterns.
- **Uncertainty Avoidance**: Tolerance for ambiguity and unstructured situations. Shapes Adaptive Space, experimentation, AI override willingness, and response to Decision Drift or novel judgment challenges.
- **Performance Orientation / Assertiveness** (GLOBE): How directness, toughness, and results emphasis are valued — critical for toxic vs. tough-but-fair differential diagnosis (DARK-001).
- **In-Group Collectivism & Institutional Collectivism**: Loyalty and preference for in-group — affects sponsorship networks, reverse flows, and who receives protection.
- **Future Orientation**: Investment in long-term vs. short-term — shapes generativity, energy surplus protection, and board willingness to govern long-term Judgment Health and adaptive capacity.
- **Face dynamics** (especially relevant in many East Asian and hierarchical contexts): Protection of social dignity and avoidance of public embarrassment shape how challenge, override documentation, error reporting, and identity work can be safely performed.

No single dimension determines cascade health. The interaction of dimensions with organizational history, industry, and leadership identity produces the local context.

### 2.2 Universal Requirements vs. Culturally Contingent Expressions
**Universal (non-negotiable for healthy cascades in this library):**
- Multi-layer psychological safety sufficient for error reporting, challenge, and AI voice
- Energy surplus available for Enabling Leadership, sponsorship, and generativity (not systematically cannibalized)
- Capacity for complexity matched to role demands
- Enabling Leadership and Adaptive Space (not pure command-and-control at scale)
- Clean (non-toxic) high performance; justice consistency
- Long-term human evaluative capacity in AI-mediated systems
- Generative investment in the next generation of leaders and judgment capability

**Culturally contingent (must be calibrated):**
- How voice and challenge are expressed and received
- What “sponsorship” and “protection” look like and who is eligible
- How reverse mentoring and upward wisdom transfer are framed without status threat
- What counts as tough-but-fair vs. toxic or justice-variable
- How AI trust, override, and “the model said so” land in high vs. low power-distance or high uncertainty-avoidance contexts
- Board challenge norms and what information can safely reach governance
- Passage expectations, identity shifts, and development methods
- How energy depletion and recovery are acknowledged or stigmatized

### 2.3 High-Risk Miscalibration Patterns
- **Ethnocentric imposition**: Exporting low-power-distance voice practices, direct challenge norms, or individualistic sponsorship models into high-power-distance or collectivist contexts without adaptation → resistance, loss of face, or underground safety collapse.
- **Cultural relativism excuse**: Treating genuine fear, justice variability, Adaptive Space capture, or results-shielded toxicity as “just the culture” → false negatives and cascade damage.
- **False positive toxicity labels**: Reading directness, high performance orientation, or hierarchical clarity as toxic when multi-layer safety and justice consistency actually hold.
- **False negative safety assessments**: Assuming silence or deference equals Artificial Harmony or fear when it may reflect respectful high-power-distance norms — *or* missing real fear because deference is expected.
- **Sponsorship and generativity distortion**: Networks that systematically exclude out-groups under the banner of cultural fit or in-group loyalty.
- **AI trust polarization**: Over-trust or under-trust of AI that tracks cultural attitudes toward authority, uncertainty, and technology rather than judgment quality.

### 2.4 CQ Development for Leaders of Leaders
Individual CQ components applied to cascade work:
- **Metacognitive CQ**: Awareness of one’s own cultural assumptions when diagnosing safety, toxicity, sponsorship, or AI resistance. Highest-leverage individual capability.
- **Cognitive CQ**: Knowledge of relevant dimensions and local organizational/national culture.
- **Motivational CQ**: Willingness to adapt and to invest energy in cross-cultural cascade building.
- **Behavioral CQ**: Ability to adapt communication, challenge, sponsorship, and development behaviors without losing core cascade-health intent.

Systemic CQ: organizational processes, metrics, and development systems that are themselves culturally calibrated rather than one-size-fits-all.

### 2.5 Domain Calibration — Operational Patterns

**Psychological Safety (SAFE-001)**  
- High power distance: Prefer private or mediated voice channels first; design face-saving challenge formats; sequence leader vulnerability carefully; do not equate public silence with Artificial Harmony without multi-layer triangulation.  
- What good looks like: Error and risk surface still occurs (possibly through different channels); learning loops close; fear of retaliation is low even if public challenge is rare.  
- Failure: Imposing open-mic challenge norms that produce compliance theater or face loss while real fear goes underground.

**Sponsorship & Generativity (SPONSOR-001, GEN-001)**  
- Collectivist / high in-group contexts: Frame sponsorship as collective capacity building and continuity; expand “in-group” deliberately; protect reverse flows from status threat by senior learning accountability.  
- What good looks like: Non-obvious and out-group talent receive visibility, advocacy, and protection; reverse flows influence senior decisions without public status damage.  
- Failure: In-group loyalty systematically excludes; reverse mentoring becomes performative or status-threatening.

**Toxic vs. Tough-but-Fair (DARK-001)**  
- High performance orientation / assertiveness: Baseline directness and toughness are higher; differential diagnosis *must* use multi-layer safety, justice consistency, Adaptive Space capture, and cascade-damage data — never style alone.  
- What good looks like: Tough-but-fair leaders show consistent justice, predictable emotional climate, and intact multi-layer safety; toxic patterns show fear, justice variability, and contagion regardless of cultural toughness norms.  
- Failure: False positives (cultural toughness labeled toxic) or false negatives (real toxicity excused as cultural).

**AI Trust & Override (AI-001)**  
- High power distance / high uncertainty avoidance: “The model said so” can carry extra authority; override and documentation may threaten face or hierarchical clarity. Design Judgment Loops with private override channels, senior modeling of evaluative questions, and explicit protection for those who challenge AI.  
- What good looks like: Overrides occur and are learned from; Decision Drift is detectable; human evaluative capacity is practiced.  
- Failure: Over-compliance with AI or underground resistance; Judgment Depreciation accelerated by cultural authority dynamics.

**Passage, Identity & Development (PIPE-001, CAP-001, PEOPLE-001)**  
- High face / high power distance: Public vulnerability and rapid identity reconstruction methods can backfire. Prefer private coaching, gradual identity work, and development framed as role excellence and collective contribution rather than personal reinvention.  
- What good looks like: Three-part transitions still occur (skills + time + values); capacity for complexity grows; role redesign frees capacity.  
- Failure: Development methods produce face loss or resistance while transitions remain incomplete.

**Board Challenge & Sensing (BOARD-001)**  
- Cultural variation in what counts as respectful upward truth-telling is large. Design board sensing with multiple channels, protect board-facing translators, and calibrate challenge norms to context while still reducing Cascade Distortion.  
- What good looks like: Multi-layer cascade-health signal reaches governance; board-facing leaders can surface reality without career damage.  
- Failure: One-size board challenge norms produce silence in some contexts and noise in others.

**Energy & Recovery (ENERGY-001)**  
- Stigma around depletion and legitimacy of recovery vary. Always-on norms interact with cultural expectations of dedication and face. Acknowledge depletion in culturally legible ways; protect surplus without requiring public admission of “not coping” where that is face-threatening.

### 2.6 Systemic CQ — Global Process Calibration
Global metrics, high-potential systems, development curricula, and board reporting often embed one cultural profile. Systemic CQ requires:
- Explicit assumption checks on global processes
- Local adaptation rights for voice channels, sponsorship framing, and development methods while holding universal cascade-health requirements
- Multi-source and multi-layer data that reduce single-culture bias in succession and toxicity diagnosis
- Metrics that do not systematically favor one cultural expression of leadership
- Enterprise architecture that treats calibration as design work, not after-the-fact translation

### 2.7 Concrete Calibration Tactics (Leaders of Leaders)
1. **Assumption check protocol** (before contested diagnosis): Name the cultural dimensions in play; state the universal requirement being protected; identify the local expression risk (ethnocentrism or relativism); require multi-layer data.

**Reusable Cultural Assumption-Check Worksheet (5 Steps — run before contested safety / toxicity / sponsorship / AI-override / Passage diagnoses):**
1. **Name the dimensions in play** here (power distance, collectivism/in-group, uncertainty avoidance, performance orientation/assertiveness, face, future orientation, other local/organizational).
2. **State the universal cascade-health requirement** being protected (e.g., safety sufficient for error reporting and learning; non-toxic high performance with justice consistency; long-term human evaluative capacity; generativity; energy surplus for Enabling Leadership).
3. **Identify the primary miscalibration risk** in this context: ethnocentric imposition (exporting a practice that will produce face loss, resistance, or underground behavior) or cultural relativism (excusing genuine fear, justice variability, or cascade damage as “just the culture”).
4. **Require multi-layer data** before finalizing the diagnosis — what cascade-health evidence (fear, voice channels actually used, justice consistency, Adaptive Space capture, contagion, override learning, etc.) exists beyond style or single-culture interpretation?
5. **Choose the calibrated move**: adapt expression/channels/framing while holding the universal requirement; or refuse the relativist excuse and protect the cascade; or both. Document the assumption check briefly so it can be revisited.

This worksheet is intentionally lightweight. It is a forced metacognitive pause, not a bureaucracy.
2. **Voice channel adaptation**: In high power distance, install private, mediated, or written risk-surface channels; measure whether learning and risk still surface, not whether public challenge looks Western.
3. **Reverse-flow framing**: Senior learning accountability + collective capacity language; avoid public status inversion where face is critical.
4. **Toxic-vs-tough differential**: Require evidence of multi-layer fear, justice variability, Adaptive Space capture, or contagion before toxicity labels; require evidence of safety and justice consistency before “tough-but-fair” clearance.
5. **AI Judgment Loop adaptation**: Private override paths, senior modeling of evaluative questions, explicit protection for challengers, face-safe documentation.
6. **Passage/identity adaptation**: Private coaching, gradualism, role-excellence and collective-contribution framing in high-face contexts.
7. **Global process review**: For any global metric, high-potential, or development system, ask who it systematically favors and how local expression of cascade health is still possible.

### 2.8 Minimum Viable Cultural Calibration (90-Day)
- Explicit assumption check on every major safety, toxicity, sponsorship, or AI diagnosis
- Multi-layer data triangulation required for contested cultural readings
- One adapted voice or reverse-flow design in the highest-stakes local context
- One toxic-vs-tough differential run with cascade-damage evidence (not style)
- CQ metacognitive prompt built into Leader of Leaders reviews
- Refusal of both ethnocentric imposition and relativist excuse in writing or spoken norm
- 90-day review of whether multi-layer cascade health and inclusion improved

## 3. Behavioral Indicators Library

### Positive Signals (Healthy Cultural & Contextual Fluency)
**Micro-behaviors**
- Before diagnosing safety or toxicity, leaders name cultural assumptions and require multi-layer data; style alone is rejected.
- Voice and challenge designs are adapted (private/mediated channels in high power distance) and still produce real error reporting and learning.
- Sponsorship and reverse flows expand beyond in-group; seniors demonstrate learning from below without public status damage.
- Tough-but-fair vs. toxic distinctions are made with cascade-damage evidence (fear, justice variability, contagion) rather than assertiveness or directness.
- AI override practices include face-safe or private paths; seniors model evaluative questions; “the model said so” does not end discussion.
- Passage and identity work is private or gradual where face is critical; three-part transitions still advance.
- Board-facing leaders use calibrated channels and are protected when surfacing multi-layer reality.
- Metacognitive CQ is visible: leaders can articulate how their own cultural background shapes their reading of the situation.
- Global processes are reviewed for cultural bias; local adaptation rights exist while universal requirements are held.

### Red Flags (Cultural Miscalibration)
**Early Warning Micro-Signals**
- Practices are exported without adaptation and produce resistance, face loss, or underground behavior.
- Silence or deference is automatically coded as Artificial Harmony/fear — or automatically coded as healthy respect — without multi-layer triangulation.
- Sponsorship and high-potential systems systematically favor cultural in-groups while claiming meritocracy.
- Direct or high-assertiveness leadership is labeled toxic without cascade-damage evidence; or real toxicity is excused as cultural toughness.
- AI trust/override patterns are interpreted only through a single cultural lens; overrides are rare or punished in high power-distance settings without design adaptation.
- Leaders cannot articulate their own cultural assumptions when challenged on a diagnosis.
- Development methods require public vulnerability in high-face contexts and produce resistance or compliance theater.

**Active Damage**
- Ethnocentric programs damage trust and produce compliance theater rather than cascade health.
- Cultural relativism allows genuine safety collapse, justice variability, or toxic high performers to persist.
- Systematic exclusion of out-groups under cultural-fit or in-group loyalty banners.
- Cross-cultural Leaders of Leaders create Cascade Distortion by misreading local signals.
- Board or enterprise governance imports one-size practices that fail or backfire across regions.
- AI-era judgment practices ignore cultural authority dynamics and produce either over-compliance or underground resistance.
- Global metrics and succession systems systematically favor one cultural profile of leadership.

### Level Differentiation
- **Leading Others**: Basic CQ awareness; adaptation of team voice and development practices to local norms while holding safety and justice requirements.
- **Leading Leaders (primary)**: Systematic cultural calibration of sensing, sponsorship, generativity, Passage support, AI practices, and toxic-vs-tough diagnosis across the cascade; concrete design patterns; development of CQ in other leaders; refusal of both ethnocentrism and relativist excuse; systemic process review.
- **Enterprise / Board**: Architecture of global processes that allow local calibration while protecting universal cascade-health requirements; board challenge and sensing norms that work across cultural contexts; metric and succession systems that do not systematically favor one cultural profile.

## 4. Diagnostic Question Bank

**Calibration & Assumptions**
1. What cultural assumptions (power distance, collectivism, uncertainty avoidance, performance orientation, face) are we importing with this diagnosis or intervention, and how might they land in this context?
2. Are we at risk of ethnocentric imposition or of cultural relativism that excuses real cascade damage?
3. What multi-layer data (not style alone) supports our reading of safety, toxicity, sponsorship quality, or AI resistance here?

**Domain-Specific Calibration**
4. How is voice, challenge, and error reporting actually expressed and received in this cultural context? What adapted channels (private, mediated, written) still produce real learning and risk surface?
5. How do sponsorship, protection, and reverse flows need to be framed to expand beyond in-group preference without violating local relationship or face norms?
6. What distinguishes tough-but-fair from toxic or justice-variable leadership *in this context*, using cascade-damage evidence (fear, justice variability, Adaptive Space capture, contagion)?
7. How do power distance, uncertainty avoidance, and face shape AI trust, override willingness, and “the model said so” dynamics here? What Judgment Loop adaptations are required?
8. How must Passage development, identity work, and board upward truth-telling be adapted while still achieving the required transitions and sensing quality?

**System & Capability**
9. Do our metrics, high-potential systems, and global processes systematically favor one cultural profile while claiming universality?
10. What CQ (especially metacognitive) exists among Leaders of Leaders who operate across contexts, and where are the gaps?
11. How will we know calibration is improving cascade health (multi-layer signals, inclusion of non-obvious talent, accurate toxic-vs-tough diagnosis, functional AI Judgment Loops across contexts) rather than just producing culturally comfortable theater?

**Action Design**
12. What is the highest-leverage move right now — explicit assumption check, adaptation of voice/sponsorship/reverse-flow/AI override design, multi-layer data triangulation for a contested diagnosis, CQ development for key leaders, or redesign of a global process that is creating ethnocentric or exclusionary effects?
13. What would minimum viable cultural calibration look like for this cascade or decision in the next 90 days?
14. How will cascade amplification work — will Leaders of Leaders be developed to calibrate and to teach calibration downward?
15. For a contested safety or toxicity diagnosis, what specific multi-layer evidence would confirm cascade damage regardless of cultural style interpretations?

## 5. Failure Modes & Edge Cases

- **Ethnocentric Imposition**: Export of voice, sponsorship, reverse mentoring, AI challenge, or board practices without adaptation → resistance, face loss, compliance theater, or underground cascade damage.
- **Cultural Relativism Excuse**: Genuine fear, justice variability, Adaptive Space capture, or results-shielded toxicity accepted as “just the culture.”
- **False Positive Toxicity Labels**: Direct, high-assertiveness, or hierarchical leadership labeled toxic without multi-layer cascade-damage evidence.
- **False Negative Safety Assessments**: Real fear or Artificial Harmony missed because deference is culturally expected; or healthy respect misread as silence/fear.
- **In-Group Sponsorship Capture**: Sponsorship and generativity systems that systematically exclude out-groups under cultural fit or loyalty norms.
- **AI Trust Polarization by Culture**: Over-trust or under-trust of AI driven by authority and uncertainty norms rather than judgment quality design; overrides rare or punished without adapted Judgment Loops.
- **One-Size Global Processes**: Enterprise systems (metrics, high-potential, development, board reporting) that favor one cultural profile and produce systematic exclusion or failure elsewhere.
- **Low Metacognitive CQ**: Leaders unaware of how their own cultural assumptions shape diagnosis of safety, toxicity, and readiness.
- **Development Method Backfire**: Passage or identity work that requires public vulnerability or rapid self-reconstruction in high-face or high-power-distance contexts without adaptation.
- **Board Sensing Failure Across Cultures**: Upward truth-telling and challenge norms that work in one context and produce distortion or silence in another.
- **Face-Threatening Override or Error Documentation**: AI or safety practices that require public admission of error or challenge in contexts where face is critical → underground behavior.
- **Minimum Viable Neglect**: Under pressure, cultural calibration is skipped and either pure ethnocentrism or pure relativism fills the gap.

## 6. Person-Focused vs. System-Focused Decision Guidance

**Default Bias:** Hybrid. Individual CQ development is person-focused; calibration of processes, metrics, voice channels, sponsorship systems, and global standards is system-focused. Most sustained cascade damage from cultural miscalibration is systemic.

**System-focused or Hybrid (system first) when:**
- Global or multi-site processes (metrics, high-potential, development, board reporting, AI governance) produce ethnocentric or exclusionary effects.
- Voice, sponsorship, or reverse-flow designs are failing across cultural contexts.
- Toxic-vs-tough or safety diagnoses are systematically biased by style or cultural assumptions.
- Board sensing or succession systems favor one cultural profile.
- AI Judgment Loop and override practices ignore local authority and face dynamics.

**Person-focused when:**
- Specific Leaders of Leaders lack metacognitive or behavioral CQ while system processes are reasonably calibrated.
- Cross-cultural relationship or translation skill is the primary gap.
- Developmental coaching on assumption awareness and adaptive behavior is the highest-leverage path.

**Hybrid Sequence (most common):**
1. Explicit cultural assumption check on the current diagnosis or intervention (protocol in §2.7).
2. Triangulate with multi-layer cascade-health data (do not rely on style or single-culture interpretation).
3. Adapt voice, sponsorship, reverse-flow, Passage, AI override, or board sensing designs to local context using concrete patterns (§2.5–2.7) while holding universal cascade-health requirements.
4. Develop CQ (especially metacognitive) in Leaders of Leaders who operate across contexts.
5. Redesign global processes that systematically favor one cultural profile or create ethnocentric effects (systemic CQ).
6. Refuse both ethnocentric imposition and relativist excuse for genuine cascade damage.
7. Cascade calibration capability: develop other leaders to calibrate and to teach calibration.
8. Sense whether multi-layer cascade health and inclusion improve across contexts.

**Required Output Format:** Always use the Intervention Mode block + dosage when recommending action. Explicitly state cultural assumptions being checked and how universal requirements are being held while expressions are adapted.

## 8. AI Coach Application Notes

- Default to Path I (Cultural / Contextual Calibration) or integrate CQ checks into every other path when cross-cultural, multi-country, high power-distance, high collectivism, or high-face contexts are present.
- **Always run an explicit cultural assumption check** before finalizing diagnosis or intervention on safety, toxicity, sponsorship, generativity, AI resistance, Passage readiness, or board sensing.
- Force multi-layer cascade-health data triangulation; never diagnose toxicity or safety on style or single-culture interpretation alone.
- Hold universal cascade-health requirements (safety sufficient for learning and risk surface, energy surplus, non-toxic high performance, long-term judgment capacity, generativity) while adapting expressions with concrete design patterns.
- Refuse both ethnocentric imposition and cultural relativism that excuses genuine cascade damage.
- Develop metacognitive CQ in Leaders of Leaders; surface their own assumptions.
- Offer concrete tactics: assumption check protocol, voice channel adaptation, reverse-flow framing, toxic-vs-tough differential with cascade-damage evidence, AI Judgment Loop face-safe design, Passage adaptation in high-face contexts, global process review.
- Cite as `CQ-001`. Communicate evidence_strength accurately (cultural dimensions and CQ Strong; cascade-specific calibration Moderate; full-library multi-country applications Emerging).
- Watch for Coach-side failure modes: ethnocentric default to one cultural model, relativist excuse of real harm, style-based toxicity labels, ignoring in-group sponsorship exclusion, applying Passage/identity methods that backfire in high-face contexts, or skipping assumption checks under time pressure.
