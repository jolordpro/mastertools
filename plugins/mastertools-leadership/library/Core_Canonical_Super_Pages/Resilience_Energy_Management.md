## 1. Core Definition & Scope

**Energy** is the fundamental capacity to do work — physical, emotional, mental, and spiritual/meaning. Sustainable high performance and cascade health depend less on time management than on **energy management**: the deliberate oscillation between expenditure and recovery across multiple energy dimensions.

This Super Page treats energy as a **multi-layer cascade substrate**, not only an individual wellness issue:
- Individual energy state and oscillation quality
- Energy as a leading indicator of cascade health (Dashboard domain)
- Capacity depletion cascades (leader depletion → middle-manager capacity → team safety & performance)
- Energy–Safety–Adaptive Space vicious and virtuous cycles
- Energy surplus as a prerequisite for generativity, vertical development, Enabling Leadership, and Adaptive Space
- AI-era cognitive load, decision fatigue, and Judgment Health implications
- System design for recovery infrastructure vs. pure individual responsibility

It is a foundational **substrate** (with Psychological Safety and Capacity for Complexity). Without adequate energy and oscillation, safety declines, capacity for complexity collapses, Adaptive Space dies, generativity becomes impossible, and AI judgment degrades.

## 2. Key Frameworks & Models

### 2.1 Corporate Athlete Four-Energy Model (Loehr, Schwartz and successors)
High performance requires management of four interdependent energy dimensions:
1. **Physical**: Sleep, nutrition, movement, recovery, physical stress load. Foundation for all other dimensions.
2. **Emotional**: Quality of emotions (positive/negative, high/low arousal), emotional regulation, relationship quality, psychological recovery. Directly shapes Supportive/Consultative behaviors and safety climate.
3. **Mental**: Focus, cognitive endurance, decision quality, creativity, learning capacity; highly sensitive to sleep, stress, multitasking load, and decision volume. Primary casualty of decision fatigue.
4. **Spiritual / Meaning**: Sense of purpose, alignment with values, connection to something larger than immediate tasks; provides directional energy and resilience under adversity. Often the first dimension cannibalized under chronic load, yet critical for generativity and long-term stewardship.

Depletion in any dimension reduces capacity in the others. Leaders of Leaders who ignore physical and emotional recovery while demanding high mental and meaning performance create predictable cascade failures.

### 2.2 Oscillation (Expenditure ↔ Recovery)
Sustainable performance is not continuous expenditure. It is **oscillation**: deliberate cycles of high engagement followed by true recovery.  
- Ultradian rhythms (~90 minutes) apply to cognitive work.  
- Daily, weekly, and seasonal recovery are required for cascade-level sustainability.  
- **True recovery** restores energy. It is not the same as “time off while still cognitively/emotionally loaded” (email, rumination, unresolved conflict, always-on AI tools).  
- Fake recovery is a high-frequency failure mode: calendar shows time off, energy does not restore.

Chronic under-recovery produces decision fatigue, emotional volatility, reduced capacity for complexity, lower psychological safety behaviors, and eventual capacity depletion cascades.

### 2.3 Energy as Leading Indicator and Cascade Substrate
Energy appears on the Cascade Health Dashboard (BRIDGE-001) because:
- Leader and middle-manager energy trajectories predict later engagement, safety, judgment quality, and performance declines — often months in advance.
- Depletion is contagious across layers (capacity depletion cascade).
- Energy surplus is required for Adaptive Space, generativity, vertical development, sponsorship, and high-quality Person-vs-System decisions.
- Energy interacts bidirectionally with Safety (Energy–Safety vicious/virtuous cycles) and with Capacity for Complexity (depletion reduces demonstrated capacity; recovery can restore it).

### 2.4 Capacity Depletion Cascade — Stages and Interruption
A common multi-layer pattern with early-warning points:

1. **Stage 1 (Leader under-recovery)**: Visible sleep debt, declining late-day/week decision quality, emotional flatness or irritability, deep-work colonization.  
   *Interruption*: Immediate recovery protection + load triage for the Leader of Leaders.
2. **Stage 2 (Middle-manager impact)**: Increased load language, reduced support availability, modeling of unsustainable pace, early safety or Adaptive Space erosion.  
   *Interruption*: Load redesign for middle managers + explicit recovery modeling + Adaptive Space protection.
3. **Stage 3 (Team cascade)**: Team energy and safety decline, voice drops, Artificial Harmony or blame rises, generativity and sponsorship cannibalized.  
   *Interruption*: Full cascade intervention — system load, safety, energy, and containment if toxic patterns have emerged.
4. **Stage 4 (Lagging damage)**: Turnover, quality failures, AI judgment failures, pipeline damage. Much more expensive to reverse.

Early detection at Stage 1–2 is high-leverage. Waiting for Stage 3–4 is a common and costly failure.

### 2.5 Energy–Safety–Adaptive Space Dynamics
- **Vicious cycle**: Depletion → reduced capacity for productive conflict and voice → safety declines → more problems and firefighting → further depletion. Adaptive Space shrinks. Person-vs-System quality falls.
- **Virtuous cycle**: Protected recovery → higher emotional and mental availability → better Supportive/Consultative behaviors and voice protection → higher safety → more Adaptive Space → better problem-solving and less unnecessary load → more sustainable energy.

Leaders of Leaders must actively steward both sides of this loop. Energy work that ignores safety (or safety work that ignores energy) is incomplete.

### 2.6 AI-Era Energy and Cognitive Load
AI systems change energy dynamics:
- Potential reduction in certain cognitive loads (if well-designed and decision rights are clear).
- New loads: oversight, exception handling, prompt/iteration work, verification of outputs, decision-rights complexity, and the emotional labor of Hybrid Cognitive Alignment.
- Risk of decision fatigue from high volume of AI-mediated micro-decisions.
- Risk of Judgment Depreciation if recovery and evaluative practice are not protected.
- “Always-on” AI tools can erode recovery boundaries if not deliberately managed.

**Practical AI energy tactics**: Design human–AI workflows with explicit verification/recovery boundaries; batch AI-mediated decisions; protect deep evaluative time; treat “AI said so” as a potential energy and judgment risk signal.

### 2.7 System vs. Individual Responsibility + Concrete System Tactics
Energy is both personal skill and system design. Over-emphasizing individual resilience while leaving toxic load, always-on culture, unclear decision rights, and missing recovery infrastructure intact is incomplete and often unjust.

**Concrete system-level tactics Leaders of Leaders can implement or sponsor**:
- **Meeting architecture**: Default shorter meetings, no-meeting blocks, agenda discipline, decision-only vs. generative distinction, protected deep-work time on calendars.
- **Decision-rights clarity**: Reduce decision thrash and escalation load by clarifying who decides what; push decisions to the appropriate level with support.
- **Load triage rituals**: Regular (weekly/biweekly) explicit review of what to stop, pause, or simplify; protect energy surplus for Adaptive Space and generativity.
- **Recovery infrastructure**: Norms and coverage that make true recovery possible (not just “unlimited PTO” that no one takes); after-action recovery after major heat experiences.
- **AI workflow boundaries**: Explicit rules for response expectations, verification time, and off-hours AI use; batch processing of AI outputs where possible.
- **Modeling and status**: Make sustainable pace and recovery high-status; interrupt heroic non-recovery stories; reward cascade energy stewardship, not only heroic delivery.

### 2.8 Minimum Viable Recovery Architecture (High-Demand or Crisis)
Even under heavy load:
- One protected daily/weekly recovery block that is actually observed.
- Explicit load triage (“what will we stop or simplify this week?”).
- Leader modeling of at least minimal oscillation.
- Rapid interruption of Stage 1 capacity depletion signals.
- Simple principle: “Energy surplus is required for generativity and Adaptive Space; we will not cannibalize it without conscious decision.”

## 3. Behavioral Indicators Library

### Positive Signals (Healthy Energy & Cascade Capacity)
**Micro-behaviors — Individual / Leader**
- Protects deep-work and recovery blocks in real time (does not immediately surrender them to urgency).
- Decision quality and emotional regulation remain relatively stable late in day/week; second-order observation still accessible under load.
- Names own energy state and its impact (“I’m depleted — let’s protect this decision for tomorrow” or “I need recovery before I can hold this Adaptive Space well”).
- Models recovery without apology; makes sustainable pace visible and high-status.
- Maintains or renews meaning connection under load rather than pure task execution.

**Cascade / Multi-Layer**
- Middle managers report manageable load-vs-capacity and visible recovery; early Stage 1–2 signals are noticed and interrupted.
- Energy surplus is occasionally visible and protected for generativity, Adaptive Space, and sponsorship.
- Meeting load, decision load, and always-on expectations are treated as design issues, not just personal endurance tests.
- AI workflows show verification and recovery boundaries, not only speed.
- Capacity depletion cascade is discussed and interrupted early rather than denied.

**Dashboard Trajectory**
- Energy domain reviewed as trajectory; early yellows trigger load or recovery interventions before safety and capacity collapse.

### Red Flags (Energy Damage or Cascade Risk)
**Early Warning (Stage 1–2)**
- Visible sleep debt, late-day/week decision quality drop, emotional flatness or irritability, deep-work colonization.
- Recovery time exists on calendar but is still cognitively/emotionally loaded (fake recovery).
- Leader models heroic non-recovery or apologizes for recovery.
- Generativity, sponsorship, and Adaptive Space are the first things cut when load rises.
- Middle managers begin mirroring unsustainable pace or reporting increased load without support.
- AI tools increase always-on expectations without compensating recovery design.

**Active Damage (Stage 3–4)**
- Capacity depletion cascade underway: leader depletion → middle-manager overload → team safety and energy decline, voice drops, Artificial Harmony or blame rises.
- Energy–Safety vicious cycle visible and unaddressed.
- Decision fatigue producing binary thinking, deferral, poor Person-vs-System decisions, or Judgment Depreciation.
- Vertical development, Enabling Leadership, and generativity stall because energy surplus is absent.
- Measurement of “resilience” or energy creates stigma or performative recovery (Goodhart).
- Cultural or role norms punish recovery and reward unsustainable expenditure; recovery is low-status.

### Level Differentiation
- **Leading Others**: Personal oscillation skill, team energy climate, modeling recovery, protecting team deep work and recovery.
- **Leading Leaders (primary)**: Personal + cascade energy stewardship, load design for middle managers, interruption of capacity depletion cascades at Stage 1–2, protection of energy surplus for Adaptive Space and generativity, AI workflow energy design, modeling sustainable pace as high-status.
- **Enterprise / Board**: Organizational recovery infrastructure, meeting and decision load architecture, cultural norms around sustainable pace, resource allocation that protects energy substrates, board-level modeling.

## 4. Diagnostic Question Bank

**Individual Oscillation & True vs. Fake Recovery**
1. What is the current quality of oscillation (expenditure ↔ true recovery) across physical, emotional, mental, and meaning dimensions for this leader / key middle managers?
2. Where is recovery being faked (time off while still loaded) vs. actually restorative? What would true recovery require right now?
3. How stable are decision quality, emotional regulation, and second-order observation across the week and under peak load?

**Cascade & Early Warning**
4. Are we seeing Stage 1 or Stage 2 signals of a capacity depletion cascade? What would interruption look like this week?
5. How is current energy interacting with psychological safety (vicious or virtuous cycle)? With demonstrated capacity for complexity?
6. Is energy surplus available for generativity, sponsorship, Adaptive Space, and vertical development, or is everything being consumed by delivery?

**System Design & AI**
7. What system factors (meeting load, decision rights clarity, always-on norms, unclear priorities, AI workflow design) are most driving energy expenditure right now?
8. How are AI tools currently affecting cognitive load, decision fatigue, recovery boundaries, and long-term Judgment Health?
9. Where is energy being treated as purely individual responsibility when system redesign is the higher-leverage move?

**Action Design & Dosage**
10. What is the highest-leverage move right now — individual oscillation skill, immediate recovery protection, load redesign, meeting/decision hygiene, AI workflow redesign, cultural norm change, or cascade interruption?
11. If we demand more Adaptive Space, generativity, or vertical development without protecting energy, what is likely to happen?
12. What recovery load does any proposed intervention itself create, and how will we protect against net energy loss?
13. How will we know energy trajectory is improving (behavioral and cascade signals) rather than just activity or wellness metrics looking better?
14. How will cascade amplification work — will sustainable pace and recovery practices be modeled and expected downward?

## 5. Failure Modes & Edge Cases

- **Individual Resilience Trap**: Heavy investment in personal resilience training while toxic load, always-on culture, and missing recovery infrastructure remain untouched (Shifting the Burden / Fixes that Fail).
- **Capacity Depletion Cascade Ignored**: Leader depletion treated as private until middle-manager and team damage is already advanced (Stage 3–4). Early interruption at Stage 1–2 is far cheaper.
- **Energy–Safety Vicious Cycle**: Depletion and declining voice/safety reinforce each other unnoticed.
- **Fake Recovery**: Time off or “wellness” activities that do not actually restore energy (still cognitively/emotionally loaded). Common and often invisible.
- **Heroic Non-Recovery Modeling**: Leaders of Leaders model and reward unsustainable expenditure, making recovery unsafe or low-status.
- **Generativity Cannibalization**: Energy surplus for Adaptive Space, sponsorship, and pipeline is the first thing cut under pressure — exactly when it is most needed for long-term cascade health.
- **AI Always-On Creep**: AI tools erode recovery boundaries and increase decision micro-load without compensating design; Judgment Depreciation risk rises.
- **Measurement Distortion**: Energy or “resilience” metrics create stigma, performative recovery, or hide true depletion (Goodhart).
- **Cultural Misalignment**: Recovery norms that conflict with cultural or role expectations are imposed without translation, or unsustainable cultural norms are left unchallenged.
- **Substrate Neglect in Development**: Vertical development, Enabling Leadership, or major change initiatives launched without energy surplus → stall or backlash.
- **Crisis Overload Without Recovery Plan**: Acute high-demand periods with no deliberate recovery architecture → long-tail cascade damage.
- **Intervention Load Blindness**: New coaching, change, or development initiatives that themselves increase net energy load without recovery protection.

## 6. Person-Focused vs. System-Focused Decision Guidance

**Default Bias:** Hybrid. Individual oscillation skill is person-focused; load design, recovery infrastructure, meeting/decision hygiene, AI workflow design, and cultural norms are system-focused. Cascade energy problems are usually system-first.

**System-focused or Hybrid (system first) when:**
- Energy problems appear across multiple people or layers (capacity depletion cascade signals, especially Stage 2+).
- Load, meeting architecture, decision rights, always-on norms, or AI workflows are primary drivers.
- Recovery infrastructure is missing or unsafe (heroic non-recovery culture).
- Energy–Safety or Energy–Capacity vicious cycles are active.
- Generativity and Adaptive Space are being systematically cannibalized.
- Interventions themselves risk increasing net load.

**Person-focused when:**
- Specific leader lacks oscillation skill or recovery practices while system conditions are reasonably supportive.
- Individual physical/emotional/mental health factors are primary (still integrate with system support).
- The developmental target is personal energy awareness, second-order observation of energy state, and modeling skill.

**Hybrid Sequence (most common and highest leverage):**
1. Protect immediate recovery and interrupt active capacity depletion cascades at the earliest visible stage (system + person).
2. Diagnose system drivers of expenditure (load, meetings, decisions, AI, norms) and redesign the highest-leverage ones (concrete tactics in §2.7).
3. Build individual oscillation skill and true recovery practices; eliminate fake recovery.
4. Protect energy surplus for Adaptive Space, generativity, vertical development, and sponsorship; make this protection explicit and high-status.
5. Design AI human–machine workflows that protect oscillation and Judgment Health.
6. Model and cascade sustainable pace; interrupt heroic non-recovery stories.
7. Recheck Safety and Capacity for Complexity as energy improves (virtuous cycle opportunity).
8. Assess recovery load of every new intervention before adding it.

**Required Output Format:** Always use the Intervention Mode block + dosage when recommending action. Be explicit about recovery load of any new intervention and about cascade amplification.
