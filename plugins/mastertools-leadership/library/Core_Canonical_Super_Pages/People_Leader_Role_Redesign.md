## 1. Core Definition & Scope

**People Leader** in this library means any leader whose primary value is created through the leadership quality, climate, capacity, and performance of other people — especially Leaders of Leaders whose work is cascade health stewardship.

**Role Redesign** is the deliberate restructuring of decision rights, work content, time allocation, span, support infrastructure, metrics, and expectations so that the role makes Enabling Leadership, Passage transitions, sponsorship, generativity, multi-layer sensing, and cascade health stewardship *possible and rational* rather than heroic extras performed on top of an overloaded operational role.

**Capacity vs. Capability** distinction:
- **Capability**: What the leader *can* do (skills, knowledge, vertical capacity, identity flexibility). Developed through learning, experience, coaching, and vertical development.
- **Capacity**: The finite energetic, attentional, and temporal resources available to exercise capability. Determined by role design, load, decision rights, recovery infrastructure, span, meeting architecture, AI workflow design, and system demands.

Most organizations treat People Leader underperformance as a capability problem and respond with training. In reality, many failures are capacity problems: the role is designed so that even highly capable leaders cannot sustainably do the Enabling Leadership, sponsorship, multi-layer sensing, and generative work the cascade requires. Adding expectations without redesigning capacity is the most common and most damaging pattern.

This Super Page equips Leaders of Leaders and the AI Coach to:
- Diagnose whether the primary constraint is capability, capacity, or both — with calendar, decision-load, and metric evidence
- Redesign People Leader roles so cascade-critical work is feasible
- Free capacity for Enabling Leadership, Passage support, sponsorship, generativity, and Judgment Health stewardship
- Integrate role design with energy surplus protection, Passage 2 time/values shifts, safety, Adaptive Space, and AI-era demands
- Avoid the “do both” trap and the training-only response to capacity problems
- Cascade redesign practices downward

## 2. Key Frameworks & Models

### 2.1 Capacity vs. Capability — The Core Distinction
| | Capability | Capacity |
|--|------------|----------|
| Definition | What the leader can do | Resources available to exercise what the leader can do |
| Primary levers | Development, selection, vertical growth, identity work | Role design, load, decision rights, span, recovery, meeting architecture, AI workflow, support infrastructure |
| Common misdiagnosis | Underperformance treated as missing skill | Overloaded capable leaders treated as needing more training |
| High-leverage response | Coaching, curricula, stretch, vertical development | Redesign work, remove or reassign lower-value work, protect recovery, clarify decision rights, reduce unnecessary complexity |

**Rule**: Diagnose capacity before (or at least alongside) capability. Training a leader whose role leaves no capacity for Enabling Leadership produces frustration, not cascade health.

### 2.2 The “Do Both” Trap
The most frequent organizational instruction to People Leaders: continue to deliver full operational results *and* perform high-quality leadership, coaching, sponsorship, Adaptive Space creation, multi-layer sensing, and generativity.

Because capacity is finite, “do both” without redesign produces:
- Chronic energy depletion (ENERGY-001)
- Cannibalization of Enabling Leadership, sponsorship, and generativity (the first things cut)
- Incomplete Passage transitions — especially failure of the time and values shifts (PIPE-001)
- Safety and Adaptive Space erosion
- Toxic compensation or quiet disengagement
- Cascade health decline masked by short-term operational metrics

Role redesign is the alternative to “do both.” Explicitly reject unbounded “do both” contracts.

### 2.3 What People Leader Work Actually Requires (Cascade-Critical Content)
For Leaders of Leaders, the cascade-critical work includes:
- Enabling Leadership and Adaptive Space creation/protection (SYS-CAS-001)
- Multi-layer sensing and Person-vs-System diagnosis (BRIDGE-001)
- Coaching other leaders on how *they* lead (Passage 2 skill — PIPE-001)
- Sponsorship of other leaders and developing them as sponsors (SPONSOR-001)
- Cascade health stewardship (Safety, Energy, Capacity, Judgment Health)
- Generative investment and wisdom transfer (GEN-001)
- AI-era: Judgment Loop design, Agent Manager coaching, Decision Drift detection, safety for AI voice (AI-001)
- Clean Containment vs. Recovery decisions when toxic patterns appear (DARK-001)

If the role does not allocate real time, decision rights, energy protection, and accountability for these activities, they will not happen at scale.

### 2.4 Capacity Audit Method (Lightweight, Repeatable)
A practical diagnosis Leaders of Leaders and coaches can run:
1. **Time map** (1–2 weeks): Categorize calendar into previous-level/operational heroics vs. cascade-critical work (leader development, Adaptive Space, multi-layer sensing, sponsorship, generativity, Judgment Health). What percentage is each?
2. **Decision load map**: What decisions land on this role that could be pushed down or eliminated? Where is the leader a bottleneck for low-leverage traffic?
3. **Recovery & surplus check**: Is there protected recovery? Is energy surplus for people leadership visible or routinely cannibalized? (ENERGY-001)
4. **Metric alignment check**: What do recognition, promotion, and informal status actually reinforce — operational heroics or cascade health?
5. **AI load check**: Are AI tools and expectations net-reducing low-value load or increasing always-on decision micro-load?
6. **Feasibility verdict**: Given the above, is cascade-critical work feasible, residual, or impossible in this role design?

Output: Primary constraint (capacity / capability / both) + highest-leverage redesign moves.

### 2.5 Role Redesign Levers — Concrete Tactics
1. **Decision rights**: Map high-traffic low-leverage decisions; push downward with clear escalation paths; publish decision-rights clarity so the People Leader is not the default bottleneck.
2. **Work content / stop list**: Run a stop/reassign exercise with the People Leader and their manager: list activities that consume capacity without cascade-health return; stop, reassign, or redesign the top 2–4. Make the stop list visible and protected from quiet re-expansion.
3. **Time architecture**: Protected weekly blocks for leader development, Adaptive Space, multi-layer sensing, and sponsorship; meeting hygiene (shorter defaults, decision vs. generative distinction, no-meeting blocks); calendar evidence reviewed in transition and performance conversations.
4. **Span and structure**: Assess whether span and reporting structure make multi-layer sensing and coaching of other leaders realistic; adjust or add intermediate support if necessary.
5. **Support infrastructure**: Staff support, peer cohorts of People Leaders, sponsorship of the People Leader themselves, passage curricula, access to multi-layer sensing data.
6. **Metrics and accountability**: Add cascade-health indicators (climate, leadership quality below, generative contribution, Judgment Health signals) to evaluation and recognition; reduce pure operational-heroics weight; review metric effects for Goodhart risk.
7. **Energy and recovery design**: Role-level recovery infrastructure; load triage that treats surplus for people leadership as non-negotiable; after-action recovery after major heat; model sustainable pace as high-status (ENERGY-001).
8. **AI workflow design**: Batch AI-mediated decisions where possible; explicit verification and recovery boundaries; protect deep evaluative time; treat “AI said so” as a potential capacity and judgment risk; redesign so AI nets capacity for Enabling Leadership rather than consuming it (AI-001).
9. **Adaptive Space protection**: Explicit protection of generative and evaluative time from operational colonization; simple principles that make colonization visible and interruptible (SYS-CAS-001).

### 2.6 Capacity Diagnosis Before Capability Intervention — Sequence
1. Run capacity audit (time, decision load, recovery, metrics, AI load).
2. Identify cascade-critical work that is missing or residual.
3. Assess whether role design makes that work feasible.
4. If capacity is primary constraint → redesign first (levers above).
5. Only then (or in parallel once capacity is addressed) assess capability gaps (skills, vertical capacity, identity) and design development (CAP-001, PIPE-001).
6. Hold accountability for cascade health with metrics that match the redesigned role.
7. Cascade the practice downward.

### 2.7 Integration with Passage 2 Time & Values Shifts
Clean Passage 2 transitions require real time for next-level work and a values shift toward cascade health (PIPE-001). Role design that still rewards previous-level heroics makes the time and values shifts systemically irrational.  
Redesign must therefore:
- Free capacity specifically for the time application Passage 2 requires
- Align metrics so that cascade health and leadership quality below are valued
- Protect energy so the identity/values work can occur
- Make “previous-level heroics as primary success” no longer the rational path

### 2.8 AI-Era Role Evolution
People Leader roles are changing:
- New work: Agent Manager coaching, Judgment Loop stewardship, Decision Drift detection, AI voice safety, hybrid cognitive alignment
- New loads: AI-mediated decision volume, always-on expectations, verification and exception handling
- New capacity requirements: energy protection for evaluative work, Adaptive Space for judgment quality, decision rights that preserve long-term human capacity
- Risk: Adding AI-era expectations on top of already capacity-constrained roles without redesign → faster depletion and Judgment Depreciation

Role redesign must explicitly incorporate AI-era work *and* protect or increase net capacity for it.

### 2.9 Minimum Viable People Leader Role Redesign (90-Day Package)
Even under constraint:
1. Capacity audit (time + decision load + metric alignment)
2. One explicit stop or reassignment of lower-value work that frees measurable capacity
3. One protected time block for cascade-critical work (leader development, sensing, sponsorship)
4. Clarification of the highest-traffic decision rights
5. One metric or accountability signal that values climate/leadership quality below (even lightly)
6. Energy/recovery protection for the People Leader
7. Explicit rejection of unbounded “do both”
8. 90-day review of time allocation and cascade-health signals

## 3. Behavioral Indicators Library

### Positive Signals (Well-Designed People Leader Roles & Healthy Capacity Use)
**Micro-behaviors — Role & System**
- Cascade-critical work has visible, protected time on calendars; not residual or “when we have time.”
- Leaders of Leaders can name work they stopped or reassigned and show the capacity that was freed.
- Decision rights are clear enough that the People Leader is not the default bottleneck for low-leverage traffic.
- Metrics and recognition moments include climate, leadership quality below, or generative contribution — not only operational heroics.
- Energy surplus for people leadership is protected in load triage; recovery is role-supported, not only individually heroically managed.
- AI workflows show batching, verification boundaries, or other net-capacity design; always-on creep is interrupted.
- “Do both” without redesign is named and rejected in real conversations.
- Capacity diagnosis is run before (or alongside) capability interventions.

**Individual Level (within a well-designed role)**
- Allocates significant, protected time to developing other leaders, Adaptive Space, sensing, and sponsorship.
- Uses capacity for Person-vs-System diagnosis and cascade health stewardship.
- Models sustainable pace; protects recovery; coaches other leaders on capacity management and role design.
- Calendar evidence matches the redesigned expectations.

### Red Flags (Capacity Failure or Misdiagnosis)
**Early Warning Micro-Signals**
- Cascade-critical work is expected in language but has no protected time, decision rights, or metric weight; calendars remain dominated by operational heroics.
- Leaders of Leaders are chronically depleted and still measured primarily on short-term operational delivery.
- Training and capability interventions are the default response when Enabling Leadership, sponsorship, or multi-layer sensing are missing.
- “Do both” is the explicit or implicit contract; stop lists do not exist or are not protected.
- AI-era expectations are added while lower-value work is never removed; decision micro-load rises.
- Span or decision traffic makes multi-layer sensing and coaching of other leaders unrealistic.
- Metric and recognition moments only celebrate operational heroics; cascade health work is invisible or low-status.

**Active Damage**
- Enabling Leadership, sponsorship, generativity, and Adaptive Space are systematically cannibalized.
- Incomplete Passage transitions persist because the role still rewards previous-level work (time and values shifts remain irrational).
- Safety, energy, and Judgment Health decline under People Leaders who have no capacity to steward them.
- Capable leaders burn out, disengage, or leave; leaders who prefer operational control or who tolerate toxicity remain.
- Capability development investments produce little cascade-health return because capacity was never addressed.
- Role redesign is discussed or announced but expectations, metrics, and calendars do not actually change (redesign theater).
- Capacity diagnosis and redesign practices are not applied to the leaders below (no cascade amplification).

### Level Differentiation
- **Leading Others**: Role design that protects time for team leadership and development; early capacity awareness; stop-list practice at team level.
- **Leading Leaders (primary)**: Full role redesign for Enabling Leadership, multi-layer cascade stewardship, sponsorship of other leaders, Passage support, generativity, and AI-era judgment work; capacity diagnosis before capability intervention; rejection of “do both”; cascade amplification of redesign practices.
- **Enterprise / Board**: Organizational architecture, metric systems, decision-rights regimes, and resource allocation that make People Leader work feasible at scale; board-level attention to capacity constraints on cascade health; anti-“do both” governance.

## 4. Diagnostic Question Bank

**Capacity vs. Capability Diagnosis**
1. Is the primary constraint on cascade-critical work capability (what the leader can do) or capacity (resources available to do it)? What calendar, decision-load, and metric evidence supports the distinction?
2. How is this People Leader actually allocating time and energy? What percentage is cascade-critical vs. previous-level/operational heroics?
3. Does the current role design, metrics, decision rights, and load make Enabling Leadership, multi-layer sensing, sponsorship, and generativity feasible — residual — or impossible?

**Role Design, Stop Lists & “Do Both”**
4. What lower-value work could be stopped, reassigned, or redesigned to free capacity? Has a protected stop list been executed?
5. Where is “do both” (full operational load + full people leadership) the explicit or implicit expectation? What is the cascade cost?
6. What decision rights, meeting architecture, span, or AI workflow changes would most increase capacity for Enabling Leadership and sensing?

**Metrics, Energy, Passage & AI-Era**
7. What do current metrics and recognition actually reinforce — previous-level heroics or cascade health (climate, leadership quality below, generative contribution, Judgment Health)?
8. Is energy surplus for people leadership protected in load triage, or routinely cannibalized? What role-level recovery infrastructure exists?
9. How does current role design support or undermine the time and values shifts required for clean Passage 2 transitions?
10. How are AI-era demands being added to the role, and has capacity been redesigned so they net-increase rather than consume capacity for judgment and Enabling Leadership?

**Action Design**
11. What is the highest-leverage move right now — capacity audit, stop/reassign lower-value work, protect time for cascade-critical work, clarify decision rights, change metrics, redesign span/structure, install recovery infrastructure, redesign AI workflows, or (only after capacity is addressed) targeted capability development?
12. What would minimum viable People Leader role redesign look like here in the next 90 days?
13. How will we know capacity for cascade-critical work has increased (time allocation evidence, multi-layer cascade-health trajectories, reduced cannibalization, systems that hold when operational pressure rises) rather than just more expectations or more training activity?
14. How will cascade amplification work — will capacity diagnosis and role redesign practices be applied to the leaders below?

## 5. Failure Modes & Edge Cases

- **“Do Both” Without Redesign**: Full operational expectations plus full people-leadership expectations with no capacity change → depletion, cannibalization of Enabling Leadership, cascade damage. Highest-frequency failure.
- **Capability Misdiagnosis**: Capacity problems treated as skill gaps; training layered on overloaded roles → frustration and low return.
- **Expectation Inflation**: New cascade-critical and AI-era expectations added continuously while lower-value work is never removed.
- **Metrics That Punish Next-Level Work**: Operational heroics and short-term results remain the dominant recognition system; cascade health work is invisible or career-limiting.
- **Decision Rights Congestion**: Leaders of Leaders remain bottlenecks for low-leverage decisions, destroying capacity for multi-layer work.
- **Span/Structure Impossibility**: Span or organizational structure makes multi-layer sensing and coaching of other leaders unrealistic even for capable leaders.
- **Energy Neglect at Role Level**: Recovery and surplus are treated as individual responsibility while role design systematically depletes.
- **AI Load Without Redesign**: AI tools and expectations increase decision micro-load and always-on pressure without freeing capacity elsewhere → faster Judgment Depreciation and depletion.
- **Redesign Theater**: Role redesign is discussed, announced, or partially piloted but expectations, metrics, and calendars do not actually change.
- **Capable Leader Exit / Toxic Retention**: High-capability leaders leave capacity-constrained roles; leaders who prefer operational control or who tolerate toxicity remain.
- **Passage 2 Undermined by Role Design**: Roles that still reward previous-level heroics make the time and values shifts of Passage 2 systemically irrational; incomplete transitions persist.
- **Cascade Amplification Failure**: Role redesign stays at one level; leaders below continue to operate in capacity-starved roles.
- **Minimum Viable Neglect**: Under pressure, even a capacity audit + one stop + one protected block is abandoned for pure operational hope.

## 6. Person-Focused vs. System-Focused Decision Guidance

**Default Bias:** Strong system orientation. Capacity is primarily a system/role-design property. Capability development is person-focused but only high-leverage when capacity exists to exercise it.

**System-focused or Hybrid (system first) when:**
- Cascade-critical work is missing or residual across multiple People Leaders → role design, metrics, decision rights, load, or support infrastructure problems.
- “Do both” is the prevailing contract.
- Energy depletion and cannibalization of Enabling Leadership/sponsorship/generativity are widespread.
- Metrics still primarily reinforce previous-level heroics.
- AI-era expectations have been added without capacity redesign.
- Span or decision traffic makes multi-layer work unrealistic.
- Passage 2 time and values shifts are systematically failing because roles still reward previous-level work.

**Person-focused when:**
- Role design and capacity are adequate and the primary gap is skill, vertical capacity, or identity (CAP-001, PIPE-001).
- A specific leader needs coaching on capacity management, prioritization of cascade-critical work, or boundary-setting within a reasonably designed role.
- Second-order observation of one’s own capacity and load is the developmental target.

**Hybrid Sequence (most common):**
1. Run capacity audit (time, decision load, recovery, metrics, AI load) with multi-layer cascade-health data.
2. Redesign role (stop/reassign work, decision rights, time architecture, metrics, span, recovery, AI workflows) so cascade-critical work is feasible; reject unbounded “do both.”
3. Align redesign with Passage 2 time and values requirements and with energy surplus protection.
4. Only then (or in parallel once capacity is addressed) invest in capability development for remaining skill/identity gaps.
5. Hold accountability for cascade health with metrics that match the redesigned role.
6. Cascade the practice: apply capacity diagnosis and role redesign to the leaders below.
7. Re-sense capacity and cascade health (including performance when operational pressure rises); iterate on minimum viable redesign.

**Required Output Format:** Always use the Intervention Mode block + dosage when recommending action. Explicitly state whether the primary constraint is capacity or capability and what redesign moves come first.

## 7. Cascading Implications

- **PIPE-001**: Role redesign is essential for clean Passage 2+ transitions. Roles that still reward previous-level heroics systematically produce incomplete transitions (especially failed time and values shifts). Capacity must exist for next-level work.
- **CAP-001**: Capability (including vertical capacity and identity) only produces cascade health when capacity exists to exercise it. Role-complexity fit includes capacity design, not only individual stretch.
- **ENERGY-001**: Capacity is largely energetic. Role redesign is a primary energy intervention. Surplus for people leadership must be designed into the role, not hoped for as individual resilience.
- **SAFE-001**: Capacity-constrained People Leaders cannot steward safety; depletion and overload are major safety risks. Role redesign is a safety intervention.
- **SPONSOR-001 / GEN-001**: Sponsorship and generativity are capacity-expensive. Without role redesign they are the first cannibalized. Portfolios and generative investment require freed capacity.
- **BRIDGE-001**: Multi-layer sensing and Person-vs-System work require time and energy capacity. Dashboard domains reveal whether capacity for cascade stewardship exists.
- **SYS-CAS-001**: Enabling Leadership and Adaptive Space creation require capacity. Role design either enables or starves them.
- **AI-001**: AI-era work must be designed into roles with corresponding capacity; otherwise it accelerates depletion and Judgment Depreciation.
- **DARK-001**: Capacity overload increases toxic compensation risk; role redesign is partly a toxic-prevention intervention.
- **BOARD-001**: Enterprise metric systems, decision-rights regimes, and resource allocation determine whether People Leader work is feasible at scale.
- **CQ-001**: Cultural variations in what People Leader work is valued, resourced, and expected require calibration of redesign approaches.
