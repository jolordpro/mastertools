## 1. Core Definition & Scope

**Toxic High Performers** are individuals who deliver strong short-term results (or are perceived to) while systematically damaging psychological safety, energy, multi-layer sensing, Adaptive Space, generativity, and the capacity of those around them.  

**Jekyll-and-Hyde Leadership** (justice variability) is a related pattern: inconsistent fairness, emotional predictability, or response to bad news/challenge that destroys safety more effectively than consistent toughness.

**Dark Side** in this library refers to the set of leader and high-performer patterns that produce cascade contagion — not merely individual abrasiveness, but system-level erosion of the substrates (Safety, Energy, Capacity for Complexity) and of Enabling Leadership.

This Super Page equips Leaders of Leaders and the AI Coach to:
- Detect toxic high-performer and Jekyll-and-Hyde patterns early (including via multi-layer sensing)
- Distinguish person-generated toxicity from system-enabled or system-amplified toxicity, and from tough-but-fair or culturally different styles
- Make the critical **Containment vs. Recovery** decision with operational criteria
- Protect the cascade (safety, energy, Adaptive Space, sponsorship, generativity) while acting
- Design recovery only when conditions support it, with tight boundaries and pre-agreed failure triggers
- Redesign system enablers after containment to prevent recurrence
- Prevent AI opacity from becoming a shield for toxic behavior
- Avoid common failures (protecting results at the expense of cascade health, endless “coaching” of the uncoachable, colluding in multi-party character attacks)

It is a high-stakes domain: delayed or incorrect action produces outsized cascade damage.

## 2. Key Frameworks & Models

### 2.1 Toxic High Performer Patterns — Micro-Behavioral Clusters
Common observable clusters (not exhaustive):
- **Results + Fear**: Strong delivery metrics accompanied by elevated fear, silence, or turnover in their span of control. Micro: people visibly self-censor or pre-emptively accommodate when the person is present or mentioned.
- **Credit Capture / Blame Displacement**: Visibility and reward flow upward or inward; failure and load flow downward or outward. Micro: public credit language vs. private blame or load-shifting; skip-level stories of “we take the hits, they take the wins.”
- **Adaptive Space Capture**: Generative forums and decision processes are dominated or chilled. Micro: idea flow stops or becomes performative when the person enters; conflict is redirected or shut down.
- **Sponsorship Distortion**: Sponsorship used selectively to build personal power bases rather than cascade talent; dissenters lose protection. Micro: who gets air cover, visibility, and stretch vs. who is left exposed.
- **Justice Variability (Jekyll-and-Hyde)**: Unpredictable fairness, emotional climate, or response to challenge. Micro: same behavior praised one week and punished the next; people spend energy predicting mood/phase rather than doing work; “which version will I get today?” language.
- **Multi-Layer Contagion**: Pattern visible not only in direct reports but in skip-level signal quality, middle-manager behavior, and team climates below. Micro: middle managers start modeling or accommodating the pattern; skip-level signal becomes sanitized or fearful.
- **AI Opacity Shield** (AI-era): AI systems or “the model” used to obscure accountability, justify unilateral decisions, or suppress challenge. Micro: “the model decided” ends discussion; override or challenge of AI-mediated decisions by others is costly; documentation of human judgment is thin.

### 2.2 Containment vs. Recovery Decision Framework — Operationalized

**Containment** (limit damage, protect the cascade, often remove or tightly bound the actor’s scope of influence) is usually the correct first move when **any** of the following are true:
- Multi-layer safety, energy, or Adaptive Space damage is already significant or accelerating
- The individual has shown repeated resistance to feedback or has weaponized prior “development” efforts
- Justice variability or fear is high and trust is broken across multiple people
- The role gives high leverage over information, sponsorship, decisions, or AI-mediated processes
- Recovery attempts would require the cascade to continue absorbing damage for an extended period
- Legal, harassment, or integrity issues are present → escalate immediately (do not coach)

**What good containment looks like:**
- Clear, timely decision communicated with cascade-health rationale (not only personal)
- Scope of influence tightly bounded or removed (role, decision rights, people leadership, AI authority as needed)
- Immediate protection of safety and energy of people around the situation (see §2.4)
- Simultaneous start of system enabler diagnosis and redesign
- Support for cascade recovery (safety, energy, Adaptive Space, talent, trust)
- Clean documentation and appropriate escalation; no prolonged ambiguity

**Recovery** (developmental investment aimed at lasting behavior change while the person remains in role or a similar role) is only appropriate when **all** of the following are true:
- Damage is still localized and early
- The individual demonstrates genuine ownership, second-order observation, and willingness to be coached (not primarily image management or charm)
- Strong containment boundaries and cascade protection can be maintained *during* the recovery attempt
- Energy and safety substrates for surrounding people are actively protected
- There is a clear, time-bound experiment with explicit, observable success/failure criteria and a pre-agreed containment trigger if recovery fails
- The Leader of Leaders has the capacity, energy, political support, and skill to hold the process

**What a tightly bounded recovery experiment looks like:**
- Written behavioral contract with 3–5 observable behaviors, success metrics, and time box (typically 60–90 days max for first experiment)
- Pre-agreed containment trigger and who will execute it
- Intensified multi-layer sensing during the period (not only self or direct manager view)
- Explicit cascade protection plan for safety and energy of others
- Skilled developmental coaching focused on second-order observation, justice consistency, and impact
- Removal or neutralization of key system enablers that previously protected the behavior
- Go/no-go review at time box based on multi-layer behavioral data, not hope or short-term results alone

**Default bias in this library when signals are multi-layer or accelerating: Containment first, then re-evaluate recovery later if conditions change.**  
Endless coaching of a toxic high performer while the cascade absorbs damage is a high-cost failure mode.

### 2.3 Cascade Contagion Mechanisms
Toxic patterns spread through:
- Modeling (others copy or adapt)
- Fear and silence (voice drops; Artificial Harmony rises; multi-layer sensing distorts)
- Energy depletion (emotional and cognitive load of managing around the toxic actor)
- Sponsorship and opportunity distortion (talent misallocated or exits)
- Adaptive Space collapse in local and adjacent areas
- Normalization (“that’s just how high performers are”)
- Protection by short-term results and “high potential” labels

Treat contagion risk as a first-order system variable.

### 2.4 Concrete Cascade Protection Tactics During Process
While Containment or Recovery is underway, Leaders of Leaders must actively protect:
- **Safety**: Explicit re-affirmation that voice about climate and impact is protected; rapid response to any retaliation signal; multi-party ethics (no gossip pile-on, no unprotected third parties).
- **Energy**: Reduce unnecessary load on people who have been managing around the individual; provide recovery space; do not add performative “healing” activities that create more load.
- **Adaptive Space**: Protect or restore generative forums that were captured or chilled; make it safe to think and speak again.
- **Sensing**: Intensify skip-level and multi-layer sensing temporarily so reality remains visible.
- **Talent**: Prevent further sponsorship distortion or quiet exits of high-value people during the process.
- **Communication**: Clear enough process communication to reduce rumor and fear, without violating dignity or legal constraints.

### 2.5 System Enablers of Toxic High Performers + Redesign Guidance
Common enablers and redesign directions:
- **Results-only / short-term metrics** → Add cascade-health, multi-rater, and multi-layer climate indicators to evaluation and reward; make climate damage career-limiting.
- **Weak multi-layer sensing** → Install or intensify skip-level and cross-layer sensing as standing practice (BRIDGE-001).
- **Concentrated decision rights / information control without accountability** → Redesign decision rights and transparency; reduce single-person choke points.
- **Sponsorship and high-potential systems that protect “stars”** → Require multi-source cascade-health data for advancement; remove unilateral protection power.
- **Low safety for speaking about the toxic actor** → Explicitly protect and reward voice about climate and justice variability; model it.
- **Energy and load designs that deplete resistance** → Address capacity depletion cascades (ENERGY-001); do not run people so hot they cannot surface problems.
- **AI opacity** → Require human accountability and override transparency in AI-mediated domains; prevent “the model decided” as accountability end-point (AI-001).
- **Cultural or leadership norms that equate toughness with toxicity or punish climate concerns** → Leadership narrative and modeling change; tough-but-fair is not the same as justice variability or fear-based compliance.

Containment of an individual without enabler redesign often produces the next toxic high performer in the same structure.

### 2.6 Jekyll-and-Hyde / Justice Variability as Special Case
Inconsistent fairness and emotional predictability destroy psychological safety more efficiently than consistent (even if tough) leadership. People cannot calibrate; risk-taking collapses; energy is spent on prediction and self-protection.  

This pattern is often harder to confront because the “Jekyll” phases produce loyalty and the “Hyde” phases are explained away as stress or high standards. Multi-layer sensing and pattern data over time are essential. Do not accept single-episode or single-source explanations.

### 2.7 Differential Diagnosis (Toxicity vs. Other Patterns)
Before labeling:
- **Tough-but-fair + high standards**: Consistent justice, predictable emotional climate, challenge is directed at work not person, voice is still possible, multi-layer safety holds.
- **Cultural style differences**: Calibrate with CQ-001; directness or hierarchy preferences are not automatically toxic. Look for fear, justice variability, and cascade damage, not style alone.
- **Capacity / role-complexity compensation** (CAP-001): Some controlling, credit-capturing, or abrasive patterns are compensatory responses to overload or under-capacity. Still requires intervention, but the system lever may be role redesign or capacity support alongside or instead of pure character framing.
- **True toxic high performer / Jekyll-and-Hyde**: Multi-layer fear or silence, justice variability, Adaptive Space capture, contagion, results shield, resistance to multi-source feedback.

Mislabeling in either direction (false positive or false negative) is costly. Use multi-layer data.

### 2.8 AI-Era Interactions
- AI opacity can become a shield for toxic decision-making or credit/blame distortion.
- Toxic actors may dominate or chill AI-related voice and override practices.
- Judgment Loops and decision rights can be captured.
- Detection may be harder if AI-mediated work reduces visibility of interpersonal behavior.
- Containment and accountability design must explicitly include AI-mediated domains.
- Post-containment, restore healthy Judgment Loops and AI voice safety.

### 2.9 Post-Containment Cascade Recovery Sequence
After containment (removal or tight bounding):
1. Immediate safety and energy protection for those who were most exposed.
2. Restore Adaptive Space and voice; interrupt Artificial Harmony that may have formed.
3. Diagnose and redesign system enablers (metrics, sponsorship, decision rights, sensing, AI accountability).
4. Rebuild trust through consistent justice and transparent process (without turning the former actor into permanent public enemy).
5. Re-assess talent and sponsorship flows; prevent quiet exits of people who were managing around the toxicity.
6. Capture learning (appropriately anonymized) to strengthen detection and prevention systems.
7. Monitor multi-layer Dashboard domains (Safety, Energy, Adaptive Space, Sensing, Generativity) for recovery trajectory.

### 2.10 Minimum Viable Containment & Protection (High Constraint)
Even under political or resource pressure:
- One clear bounding of the most damaging scope of influence
- Explicit protection of voice about climate for people around the situation
- Intensified multi-layer sensing for a defined period
- Removal of at least the most obvious results-only shield or unilateral protection
- Pre-agreed trigger for stronger action if multi-layer damage continues
- Leader of Leaders energy and support protected so they can hold the line

## 3. Behavioral Indicators Library

### Positive Signals (Healthy Handling of Dark Side Risk)
**Micro-behaviors**
- Multi-layer and skip-level sensors are used in real time to detect climate and justice variability; single-source views are not sufficient.
- Results are explicitly weighed against cascade-health data; “high performer” is never a permanent free pass.
- Containment vs. Recovery is named and reasoned with cascade-health as primary criterion.
- During any process, safety and energy of surrounding people are actively protected (not assumed).
- Sponsorship and high-potential processes include multi-rater and cascade-health reality checks.
- AI-mediated work has human accountability and override transparency.
- “That’s just how high performers are” normalization is interrupted.
- When containment is chosen, it is clean, timely, and accompanied by enabler redesign and cascade recovery support.

### Red Flags (Toxic Pattern or System Failure to Act)
**Early Warning Micro-Signals**
- Strong results coexist with elevated fear, silence, self-censorship, or skip-level sanitization in the same span.
- Justice variability: same behavior praised then punished; “which version today?” language; people predict mood more than they do work.
- Credit/blame asymmetry visible in language and load.
- Adaptive Space chills when the person is present; idea flow becomes performative.
- Challenge or bad news directed at the person is costly; workarounds proliferate.
- “High potential” or results labels are used to deflect climate or justice concerns.
- AI “model decided” language ends discussion; human override or challenge is rare or punished.

**Active Damage / Contagion**
- Multi-layer safety, energy, and Adaptive Space damage is visible and accelerating.
- Talent is exiting or under-using itself; middle managers model or accommodate the pattern.
- Sponsorship is distorted into personal power-base building.
- Prior coaching or development plans have been gamed or produced no lasting multi-layer improvement.
- Leaders of Leaders delay action because of short-term results, political cost, or hope that recovery will work without containment boundaries.
- Multi-party process turns into unprotected gossip or character attack (itself a safety failure).

### Level Differentiation
- **Leading Others**: Local climate protection, early escalation of justice variability or fear patterns, refusal to normalize toxicity, protection of own team’s voice and energy.
- **Leading Leaders (primary)**: Multi-layer detection, operational Containment vs. Recovery decision, cascade protection tactics during process, system enabler redesign, coaching other leaders on detection and boundaries, clean containment execution, post-containment recovery stewardship.
- **Enterprise / Board**: Culture and metric systems that do not permanently protect toxic high performers; governance of high-potential and sponsorship systems; escalation paths for cascade-level toxic risk; modeling of cascade health over pure short-term results.

## 4. Diagnostic Question Bank

**Detection, Pattern & Differential**
1. Where do strong results coexist with fear, silence, turnover, justice variability, or skip-level signal distortion?
2. What do multi-layer and skip-level sensors actually say about climate and energy around this individual (not just direct manager or self-view)?
3. Is justice variability (Jekyll-and-Hyde) present? How do people describe predictability of fairness and response to challenge over time?
4. How is Adaptive Space, voice, and generativity affected when this person is in the room or in the decision path?
5. Are we looking at true toxicity, tough-but-fair leadership, cultural style difference, or capacity/role-mismatch compensation? What multi-layer data supports the distinction?

**Containment vs. Recovery Decision**
6. Has the pattern already produced significant multi-layer safety, energy, or Adaptive Space damage?
7. What is the history of feedback, coaching, or “development” attempts, and what lasting multi-layer change resulted?
8. Does the individual demonstrate genuine ownership, second-order observation, and coachability, or primarily image management?
9. Can the cascade be adequately protected *during* any recovery attempt, or will others continue to absorb damage?
10. What would good containment look like here? What would a tightly bounded recovery experiment look like (contract, time box, success metrics, failure trigger, cascade protection plan)?
11. What is the pre-agreed containment trigger if a recovery experiment fails, and who will execute it?

**System Enablers, Contagion & AI**
12. What system factors (metrics, sponsorship, decision rights, safety for speaking about this person, AI opacity, load) are enabling or protecting the pattern?
13. How far has contagion spread (modeling, silence, talent distortion, middle-manager accommodation)?
14. Is AI opacity being used to reduce accountability, justify unilateral moves, or chill challenge?
15. How are short-term results and “high potential” labels currently functioning as shields?

**Action Design & Cascade Protection**
16. What is the highest-leverage move right now — clean containment, tightly bounded recovery experiment with cascade protection, system enabler redesign, multi-layer sensing intensification, or escalation?
17. How will we protect the safety and energy of people around this situation during whatever process we choose? What concrete tactics will we use?
18. After containment (if chosen), what is the cascade recovery sequence and enabler redesign plan?
19. How will we know whether the cascade is recovering (behavioral multi-layer signals) rather than just the individual’s short-term compliance or results?

## 5. Failure Modes & Edge Cases

- **Results Shield**: Short-term performance or “irreplaceable” status permanently protects cascade-damaging behavior.
- **Endless Coaching of the Uncoachable**: Repeated developmental investments without containment boundaries while multi-layer damage continues.
- **Delayed Containment**: Action postponed because of political cost, hope, or incomplete data until contagion is advanced and expensive.
- **Containment Without Enabler Redesign**: Individual is removed or bounded but the metric, sponsorship, decision-rights, or safety systems that enabled the pattern remain → next toxic high performer appears.
- **Recovery Without Cascade Protection**: Developmental effort proceeds while surrounding people continue to absorb fear, load, and Adaptive Space loss.
- **Justice Variability Minimization**: Jekyll-and-Hyde pattern is reframed as passion or high standards; multi-layer data is ignored.
- **Multi-Party Collusion / Character Attack**: Observation process turns into gossip or pile-on that itself damages safety and due process.
- **AI Opacity Shield**: Toxic patterns hide inside AI-mediated work or “model” justifications; accountability and voice are reduced.
- **High-Potential Distortion**: Talent systems continue to accelerate or protect the individual based on incomplete or gamed data.
- **Leader of Leaders Capacity Failure**: The Leader of Leaders lacks the energy, safety, political support, or capacity for complexity to hold a clean containment or recovery process → avoidance or messy execution.
- **Cultural Mislabeling**: Tough-but-fair or culturally different leadership is misread as toxic (or true toxicity is excused as cultural).
- **Crisis Exception Forever**: Temporary tolerance during a crisis becomes permanent normalization.
- **Post-Containment Neglect**: Containment occurs but cascade recovery (safety, energy, Adaptive Space, trust, talent) and enabler redesign are left undone → residual damage and recurrence risk.

## 6. Person-Focused vs. System-Focused Decision Guidance

**Default Bias:** Strong system orientation. Multi-layer toxic patterns are rarely solved by person-focused coaching alone. Containment is a system-protective move. Recovery, when appropriate, is a conditional person investment inside a protected system.

**System-focused or Hybrid (system first / containment first) when:**
- Multi-layer safety, energy, Adaptive Space, or sensing damage is present or accelerating.
- Contagion is visible (modeling, silence, talent distortion).
- System enablers (metrics, sponsorship, decision rights, safety for voice about the person, AI opacity) are active.
- Prior person-focused efforts have failed to produce lasting multi-layer improvement.
- Justice variability is high and trust is broken across multiple people.
- The Leader of Leaders cannot adequately protect the cascade during a long recovery attempt.
- Legal/harassment/integrity signals are present → escalate.

**Person-focused (recovery coaching) only when:**
- Damage is still early and localized.
- Genuine ownership, second-order observation, and coachability are demonstrated (not just compliance or charm).
- Strong containment boundaries and cascade protection (safety and energy of others) can be maintained throughout.
- Clear time-bound experiment with pre-agreed failure → containment trigger exists.
- System enablers are simultaneously being addressed.

**Hybrid Sequence (most common when any recovery is considered):**
1. Intensify multi-layer sensing and protect safety/energy of people around the situation immediately (concrete tactics in §2.4).
2. Make explicit Containment vs. Recovery decision with cascade-health as primary criterion; document the reasoning.
3. If containment: execute cleanly (what good containment looks like), redesign enablers, support cascade recovery sequence.
4. If recovery experiment: install tight boundaries, cascade protection plan, behavioral contract, time box, success metrics, and failure trigger; coach second-order observation and justice consistency; remove enablers; hold go/no-go on multi-layer data.
5. Re-evaluate on multi-layer behavioral signals, not hope or short-term results alone.
6. Capture learning to strengthen detection, sponsorship, metric, and AI-accountability systems.
7. Monitor cascade recovery trajectory (Safety, Energy, Adaptive Space, Sensing, Generativity).

**Required Output Format:** Always use the Intervention Mode block + dosage when recommending action. Explicitly state Containment vs. Recovery choice, cascade protection measures, and (if recovery) the failure trigger. Escalate immediately on legal/harassment/integrity triggers.

## 7. Cascading Implications

- **BRIDGE-001**: Toxic patterns and justice variability are high-severity Dashboard signals. Bridge must convert them into Containment vs. Recovery decisions and cascade protection, not default developmental coaching. Multi-party ethics apply.
- **SAFE-001**: Toxic high performers and Jekyll-and-Hyde leaders are among the highest-leverage destroyers of multi-layer safety. Artificial Harmony often masks the damage. Containment is frequently required to restore safety.

- Validated decision aids for Containment vs. Recovery under real political and results pressure.
