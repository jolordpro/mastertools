## 1. Core Definition & Scope

The **Leadership Pipeline** (Charan, Drotter, Noel and successors) models leadership as a series of passages, each requiring a distinct shift in:
- **Skill requirements**
- **Time application**
- **Work values**

Failure to make the full three-part transition produces the common pattern of leaders who were excellent at the previous level and underperform (or become toxic) at the next.

This Super Page focuses especially on **Passage 2 — Managing Others → Managing Managers (Leading Leaders)** because it is the primary audience and highest-leverage transition for cascade health. It also covers the broader pipeline architecture, selection, support, and AI-era passage demands.

**Core claim of this library:** Most cascade failures (safety collapse, energy depletion cascades, Adaptive Space starvation, sponsorship distortion, generativity failure, AI judgment neglect) trace in significant part to incomplete Passage 2 transitions and to systems that promote for previous-level excellence without supporting the new skill/time/values requirements.

## 2. Key Frameworks & Models

### 2.1 The Leadership Pipeline Passages (Core Model)
Major passages (simplified and cascade-relevant):
1. **Managing Self → Managing Others**: From personal contribution to getting work done through others; first-line leadership.
2. **Managing Others → Managing Managers (Passage 2 / Leading Leaders)**: From leading individual contributors to leading leaders; Enabling Leadership, Adaptive Space, multi-layer sensing, sponsorship of other leaders, cascade health stewardship. **Primary focus of this page.**
3. **Managing Managers → Functional Manager**: Leading a full function; cross-boundary integration, functional strategy.
4. **Functional Manager → Business Manager**: P&L / enterprise unit leadership; business acumen, cross-functional trade-offs.
5. **Business Manager → Group Manager / Enterprise**: Portfolio of businesses; capital allocation, enterprise culture, generativity at scale.
6. **Enterprise → Board / Governance** (extended): Oversight, judgment stewardship, long-term adaptive capacity.

Each passage requires simultaneous shifts in skills, time application, and values. Partial transitions (skills only, or skills + time without values) are the norm and the primary source of pipeline leakage and cascade damage.

### 2.2 Passage 2 Deep Dive — The Critical Transition
**From:** Leading individual contributors (Managing Others)  
**To:** Leading leaders (Managing Managers)

**Required shifts:**

**Skills**
- From direct coaching of individual performance to coaching other leaders on how *they* lead
- Enabling Leadership: creating Adaptive Space, brokering, linking, simple principles (SYS-CAS-001)
- Multi-layer sensing and skip-level signal interpretation
- Person-vs-System diagnosis and intervention design (BRIDGE-001)
- Sponsorship of other leaders (four acts) and developing them as sponsors (SPONSOR-001)
- Cascade health stewardship (Safety, Energy, Capacity, Judgment Health)
- Holding other leaders accountable for the climate and capacity they create
- AI-era: coaching other leaders on Agent Manager capability, Judgment Loops, and Decision Drift detection (AI-001)

**Time Application**
- Significant reduction in direct personal contribution and heroics
- Increase in time spent on: developing other leaders, Adaptive Space design, multi-layer sensing, sponsorship, cascade-health diagnosis, and system redesign
- Protection of time for generative and Enabling Leadership work (often the first thing cannibalized)
- **Concrete time-redesign tactics**: Calendar audit of previous-level vs. next-level work; protected blocks for leader-development and Adaptive Space; explicit stop/reduce list of direct-contribution activities; peer accountability for time shift; load triage that treats Enabling Leadership time as non-negotiable

**Work Values**
- From “my team’s results through my direct leadership” to “the cascade’s health and the capacity of the leaders I lead”
- From personal expertise/heroics as primary value to Enabling Leadership and generativity as primary value
- Willingness to be measured by the quality of leadership and climate *below* oneself
- Identity shift away from expert/hero/rescuer toward orchestrator, developer of leaders, and steward of cascade capacity (CAP-001)
- **Coaching the values/identity shift**: Surface current identity construction and its cascade limits; second-order observation of what feels like “real work”; heat experiences that reveal over-dependence on personal heroics; redefine success as the capacity and climate of the leaders below; protect energy and safety during the identity transition

**Common incomplete transitions (differential):**
- **Skills partially acquired, time still previous-level**: Leader can talk Enabling Leadership but calendar and meeting behavior remain heroics and direct contribution.
- **Time shifted, values still previous-level**: Calendar looks different but identity and what is experienced as meaningful work remain personal results and expertise; Enabling Leadership feels like overhead.
- **Unsupported transition**: Promotion with no sponsorship, energy protection, Adaptive Space, or passage-specific development → high failure rate and cascade cost.
- **Bypass pattern**: Continues to coach individual contributors directly while under-developing or bypassing the managers in between.

### 2.3 The Three-Part Transition Requirement
Every passage demands concurrent change in:
1. **Skills** (what the leader can do)
2. **Time application** (how the leader allocates attention)
3. **Work values** (what the leader believes is important and how identity is constructed)

Most development systems over-index on skills and under-invest in time redesign and values/identity work. The result is leaders who know what they “should” do but still spend time and derive identity from the previous level. Diagnosis and development must address all three; skills-only interventions systematically underperform.

### 2.4 Role-Complexity Fit, Selection & Decision Tree
Passage failure is frequently a selection and role-design problem as much as a development problem.

**Selection evidence that predicts Passage 2 success better than current results alone:**
- Demonstrated (or strongly evidenced) Enabling Leadership behaviors and multi-layer thinking
- Willingness and early evidence of identity flexibility (not locked on expert/hero)
- Capacity for complexity (CAP-001) under ambiguity and interdependence
- Track record of developing other people, not only delivering through them
- Multi-source data on climate created in previous roles
- AI-era: early Agent Manager or hybrid judgment behaviors if relevant to the context

**Role-complexity fit decision tree (aligned with CAP-001):**
1. **Develop**: Transition gaps are primarily skill/time/values and role complexity is within stretch range with support → targeted three-part development + sponsorship + energy/Adaptive Space protection.
2. **Redesign role**: Complexity exceeds current capacity even with support, or role still rewards previous-level heroics → reduce unnecessary complexity, clarify decision rights, add Adaptive Space and support, pair with higher-capacity partners.
3. **Re-assign**: Mismatch is severe, timeline/risk does not allow development, or cascade damage is already significant → move to a better-fit role; do not leave the cascade under an incomplete transition indefinitely.
4. **Hybrid**: Short-term role support + longer-term developmental plan with clear go/no-go.

Never default to “coach harder” without testing this tree.

### 2.5 Support Infrastructure for Passage Success — Concrete Package
High-leverage supports (minimum viable and full):
- **Explicit Passage 2 curriculum** addressing skills + time + values/identity (not skills only)
- **Sponsorship** of the transitioning leader (SPONSOR-001) with protection and advocacy during the vulnerable period
- **Protected energy and Adaptive Space** during transition (ENERGY-001, SYS-CAS-001); load triage that treats transition work as non-negotiable
- **Multi-layer sensing and Bridge skill** so the new Leader of Leaders can diagnose cascade health
- **Peer cohort** of others making the same passage
- **Clear accountability** for the climate and leadership quality the new leader creates below them
- **Identity and second-order observation coaching** (CAP-001, BRIDGE-001)
- **AI-era modules**: Agent Manager coaching of other leaders, Judgment Loop design, Decision Drift detection, safety for AI voice
- **Transition review practice**: Periodic (e.g., 60/90-day) review of skills progress, actual time allocation (calendar evidence), values/identity markers, and multi-layer cascade-health signals under the leader

**Minimum viable Passage 2 support** (resource-constrained): Sponsorship + calendar/time-redesign experiment + one multi-layer sensing practice + explicit accountability for climate below + energy protection for the transition period + light identity coaching.

### 2.6 AI-Era Passage Demands — Operationalized
New or intensified requirements at Passage 2 and above:
- **Agent Manager capability**: Ability to direct, evaluate, and integrate AI agents and to coach other leaders on the same
- **Judgment Loop design and coaching**: Building healthy human evaluation, override, and learning cycles across layers
- **Decision Drift detection**: Sensing and interrupting quiet erosion of standards in AI-mediated work
- **Psychological safety for AI voice**: Creating climate where errors, overrides, and judgment concerns can be raised
- **Energy management** in high decision-volume / always-on hybrid environments
- **Capacity for complexity** to hold Amplified Oversight and Hybrid Cognitive Alignment
- **Generativity** regarding next-generation judgment capability

These must appear in selection criteria, Passage 2 curricula, time expectations, and accountability — not only in optional AI training.

### 2.7 Pipeline Health as Cascade Health
A healthy pipeline is not only a list of ready-now successors. It is:
- High-quality three-part transitions at each passage
- Cascading sponsorship and generativity
- Multi-layer cascade health (Safety, Energy, Capacity, Adaptive Space, Judgment Health) as both input to and output of pipeline decisions
- Selection and development systems that refuse to promote pure previous-level excellence when next-level requirements are unmet
- Protection against toxic high performers advancing through results shields (DARK-001)
- Evidence that systems and successors perform when leaders step back (capacity was actually transferred)

## 3. Behavioral Indicators Library

### Positive Signals (Healthy Passage Transitions & Pipeline)
**Micro-behaviors — Passage 2 Complete Transition**
- Calendar and meeting behavior show significant time on developing other leaders, Adaptive Space, multi-layer sensing, and sponsorship — not majority previous-level heroics or direct contribution.
- Coaches other leaders on *how they lead* (Person-vs-System, safety, energy, sponsorship, AI judgment) with behavioral specificity; does not primarily bypass to coach individual contributors.
- Values and identity language reference cascade health and the capacity of leaders below; success is described in terms of what those leaders and their teams can do.
- Seeks, protects, and acts on skip-level and multi-layer signal; names and interrupts filtering and Cascade Distortion.
- Performs the four sponsorship acts for other leaders and develops them as sponsors.
- Protects energy and Adaptive Space for Enabling Leadership work; interrupts cannibalization.
- AI-era: coaches Judgment Loops, override quality, and Agent Manager practice; treats AI voice safety as part of climate accountability.

**Pipeline / System Level**
- Selection and promotion decisions explicitly assess next-passage requirements (skills + time + values/identity + AI-era) with multi-source evidence, not only current results.
- Transitioning leaders receive the support package (sponsorship, energy/Adaptive Space protection, passage curriculum, peer cohort, transition reviews).
- Pipeline conversations include cascade-health trajectories under Leaders of Leaders and generative contribution.
- Incomplete transitions are diagnosed (which of the three parts is missing) and supported or reversed.
- Systems and successors continue to perform when leaders step back.

### Red Flags (Passage Failure or Pipeline Damage)
**Early Warning Micro-Signals — Passage 2**
- Calendar still dominated by personal contribution, firefighting, and previous-level meetings; Enabling Leadership and leader-development time is residual or absent.
- In meetings with their managers, the Leader of Leaders primarily solves operational problems or coaches individual-contributor issues rather than coaching leadership quality and climate.
- Identity language remains “I am the one who delivers / knows / rescues”; resistance to measurement by the leadership quality and climate below.
- Multi-layer sensing is weak or avoided; relies on filtered direct-report views; skip-level signal is low quality or unused.
- Bypass: regularly reaches around managers to direct or coach individual contributors.
- Energy is visibly depleted by role complexity; Adaptive Space and generativity are the first things cut.
- Sponsorship of other leaders is absent or only advice (mentorship labeled as sponsorship).
- AI-era: no coaching of Judgment Loops or Agent Manager practice; AI voice safety is unaddressed.

**Active Damage**
- Cascade health deteriorates under the Leader of Leaders (safety, energy, Adaptive Space, voice, judgment quality).
- Managers below are under-developed, bypassed, or become conduits of distortion and Artificial Harmony.
- Toxic compensation patterns emerge (control, credit capture, justice variability) as response to role-complexity overload.
- Pipeline is a list of names without evidence of successful three-part transitions or cascade-health contribution.
- Selection continues to promote previous-level stars who lack next-level capacity or values shift.
- Results shields protect incomplete transitions or toxic patterns.
- AI-era passage requirements are ignored; hybrid judgment capability is under-developed across the pipeline.

### Level Differentiation
- **Managing Others (pre-Passage 2)**: Focus on team results through direct leadership; early exposure to Enabling Leadership concepts, multi-layer awareness, and identity flexibility as preparation.
- **Leading Leaders (Passage 2 — primary)**: Full three-part transition; Enabling Leadership; cascade health stewardship; sponsorship of other leaders; multi-layer sensing; identity shift; AI-era judgment coaching of other leaders; time redesign; accountability for climate below.
- **Later Passages / Enterprise**: Broader portfolio, capital and culture stewardship, generativity at scale, board-level judgment and succession architecture; still requires solid Passage 2 foundation.

## 4. Diagnostic Question Bank

**Three-Part Transition Quality (Passage 2)**
1. How is this Leader of Leaders actually spending time — previous-level work and heroics, or developing other leaders, Adaptive Space, multi-layer sensing, and cascade health? What does the calendar evidence show?
2. What evidence exists of a values and identity shift toward the capacity and climate of the leaders below them? What still feels like “real work” to them?
3. How effectively do they coach other leaders on *how those leaders lead* (Person-vs-System, safety, energy, sponsorship, AI judgment) vs. only on operational results or by bypassing to individual contributors?
4. What is the quality of multi-layer and skip-level sensing? How filtered is the information they act on?
5. Which of the three parts (skills, time, values/identity) is most incomplete right now?

**Selection, Role Fit & Support**
6. Were next-passage requirements (skills + time + values/identity + AI-era demands) explicitly assessed in selection/promotion with multi-source evidence, or was previous-level excellence the dominant criterion?
7. Is the current role complexity a fit, a stretch with support, or a mismatch? What does the develop / redesign / re-assign tree suggest?
8. What support infrastructure (sponsorship, energy protection, Adaptive Space, passage curriculum, peer cohort, transition reviews) exists, and what is missing?
9. How healthy are the cascades under the Leaders of Leaders in this pipeline (Safety, Energy, Capacity, Adaptive Space, Judgment Health)?

**System, Metrics & AI-Era**
10. What system factors (metrics, role design, load, decision rights) are reinforcing previous-level behavior and punishing or starving next-level work?
11. How are AI-era requirements (Agent Manager capability, Judgment Loop design, Decision Drift detection, safety for AI voice) being built into passage transitions and selection?
12. Do high-potential and succession systems incorporate cascade-health contribution and generative orientation, or primarily personal results and readiness ratings?

**Action Design**
13. What is the highest-leverage move right now — targeted three-part development for a specific incomplete transition, time-redesign experiment, values/identity coaching, role redesign, selection criteria change, support infrastructure installation, energy/Adaptive Space protection, or multi-layer sensing accountability?
14. For a leader stuck in an incomplete transition, is the better path development support, role redesign, or re-assignment?
15. What would minimum viable Passage 2 support look like here in the next 90 days?
16. How will we know pipeline health is improving (successful three-part transitions, cascade-health trajectories under Leaders of Leaders, systems that hold when leaders step back, generative contribution) rather than just more names on a list?

## 5. Failure Modes & Edge Cases

- **Previous-Level Promotion**: Best individual contributor or best first-line manager promoted without assessment of or support for Passage 2 requirements → role-complexity mismatch, cascade damage, or toxic compensation.
- **Skills-Only Transition**: Leader acquires some next-level language and frameworks but continues to spend time and derive identity from previous-level work.
- **Time Shift Without Values Shift**: Calendar changes but identity and what is experienced as “real work” remain previous-level; Enabling Leadership and generativity feel like overhead.
- **Unsupported Transition**: Promotion occurs with no sponsorship, energy protection, Adaptive Space, or passage-specific development → high failure rate and cascade cost.
- **Bypass Pattern**: Leader of Leaders continues to coach individual contributors directly and under-develops or bypasses the managers in between → weak pipeline and Cascade Distortion.
- **Results Shield for Incomplete Transitions**: Short-term operational results protect leaders who are damaging cascade health through incomplete transitions.
- **Pipeline as Name List**: Succession and high-potential systems track names and readiness ratings without evidence of successful three-part transitions or cascade-health contribution.
- **AI-Era Passage Neglect**: Passage requirements and development ignore Agent Manager, Judgment Loop, and hybrid cognitive demands → future fragility.
- **Energy & Safety Neglect During Transition**: Transitions attempted under chronic depletion or low safety → stalled values/identity work and higher toxic risk.
- **Cultural Misapplication**: Passage expectations and development methods imposed without cultural calibration of authority, development, and identity norms (CQ-001).
- **Toxic Acceleration**: High-performing but toxic individuals advance through passages because results shields and weak multi-layer sensing protect them (DARK-001).
- **Role Design That Rewards Previous Level**: Metrics, decision rights, and recognition still reinforce heroics and direct contribution, making the time and values shift systemically irrational.
- **Cascade Amplification Failure**: Leaders who made clean transitions are not developed or expected to support others through the same passage; system capacity remains thin.

## 6. Person-Focused vs. System-Focused Decision Guidance

**Default Bias:** Hybrid. Individual skill/time/values/identity work is person-focused; selection criteria, role design, support infrastructure, metrics, and cascade-health accountability are system-focused. Incomplete transitions that damage cascades are system problems as much as person problems.

**System-focused or Hybrid (system first) when:**
- Multiple leaders show similar incomplete Passage 2 patterns → selection, role design, support infrastructure, or metric problems.
- Cascade health is deteriorating under Leaders of Leaders as a group.
- Promotion criteria still over-weight previous-level excellence.
- Energy, Adaptive Space, or sponsorship support for transitions is missing.
- Role design and metrics still reward previous-level heroics.
- AI-era passage requirements are absent from the system.
- Pipeline is a name list without transition quality or cascade-health data.

**Person-focused when:**
- A specific leader is mid-transition and needs targeted skill, time-redesign, values, and identity support while system conditions are adequate.
- Role-complexity fit is acceptable and the primary gap is developmental.
- Second-order observation and identity work are the highest-leverage individual moves.

**Hybrid Sequence (most common):**
1. Diagnose transition completeness (skills + time + values/identity) and cascade-health impact with multi-layer data and calendar evidence.
2. Assess role-complexity fit (CAP-001 decision tree); consider redesign or re-assignment if mismatch is severe.
3. Install or strengthen system supports (selection criteria, sponsorship, energy/Adaptive Space protection, passage curricula, multi-layer sensing accountability, metrics that do not punish next-level work).
4. Provide targeted person-focused development on the missing transition elements (especially time redesign and values/identity).
5. Hold accountability for the climate and leadership quality the Leader of Leaders creates below them.
6. Integrate AI-era passage requirements into development and selection.
7. Cascade the capability: develop other leaders to make clean passages and to support those who follow.
8. Sense pipeline health as cascade health and as system performance when leaders step back.

**Required Output Format:** Always use the Intervention Mode block + dosage when recommending action. Be explicit about which part of the three-part transition is missing, about cascade protection during development, and about develop vs. redesign vs. re-assign.

## 7. Cascading Implications

- **PEOPLE-001**: People Leader role redesign is largely about enabling clean Passage 2+ transitions and freeing capacity for Enabling Leadership and cascade stewardship.
- **CAP-001**: Passages are vertical developmental and identity transitions; capacity for complexity and role-complexity fit are gating; identity work is central to the values shift.
- **SPONSOR-001**: Sponsorship of transitioning leaders and development of those leaders as sponsors are core pipeline mechanisms. Passage 2 includes learning to sponsor other leaders.
- **GEN-001**: Later passages require generative shift; succession must value generative contribution and cascade health left behind.
- **ENERGY-001**: Transitions are energy-intensive; surplus and protection are required; depletion increases failure and toxic risk.
- **SAFE-001**: Incomplete Passage 2 is a major source of multi-layer safety damage; safety is both input and output of pipeline health.
- **BRIDGE-001**: Passage 2 skill is substantially Bridge skill + multi-layer sensing + Person-vs-System fluency. Dashboard domains reveal transition quality.
- **SYS-CAS-001**: Passage 2 is the transition into Enabling Leadership and Adaptive Space creation as primary work.
- **AI-001**: AI-era passage requirements (Agent Manager, Judgment Loops, Decision Drift, hybrid alignment) must be designed into transitions and selection.
- **DARK-001**: Incomplete transitions and role-complexity overload increase toxic compensation risk; results shields protect under-transitioned or toxic leaders.
- **BOARD-001**: Enterprise pipeline and succession governance must assess transition quality and cascade health, not only readiness ratings and personal results.
- **CQ-001**: Cultural calibration of passage norms, authority, development methods, and identity transitions is required.

## 8. AI Coach Application Notes

- Default to Path E (Middle Manager / Passage 2 Development) or Path C (Talent System Design) when pipeline or transition issues appear; integrate with CAP-001, SPONSOR-001, ENERGY-001, SAFE-001, BRIDGE-001, and PEOPLE-001.
- Always diagnose the three-part transition (skills + time + values/identity) with calendar and multi-layer evidence; do not treat passage failure as skills-only.
- Force multi-layer cascade-health data as evidence of transition quality; do not rely on self-report or previous-level results alone.
- Check energy and safety as prerequisites for values/identity work; incomplete transitions under depletion are high-risk.
- Assess role-complexity fit early using the develop / redesign / re-assign tree; development alone cannot solve severe mismatch.
- Surface AI-era passage gaps (Agent Manager, Judgment Loops, Decision Drift, AI voice safety) explicitly and build them into development and selection.
- When toxic patterns or results shields appear around incomplete transitions → integrate DARK-001.
- Offer concrete tactics: time-redesign experiments, transition review practices, minimum viable support packages, values/identity coaching sequences.
- Cite as `PIPE-001`. Communicate evidence_strength accurately (Pipeline model Strong; Passage 2 cascade dynamics Moderate; AI-era Emerging).
- Watch for Coach-side failure modes: skills-only diagnosis, ignoring time and values/identity, promoting previous-level excellence without next-level support, neglecting energy/safety during transitions, treating pipeline as a name list, or skipping role-complexity fit assessment.

**Strong**

**Emerging**  
AI-era passage requirements and development methods (Agent Manager, Judgment Loop stewardship, hybrid cognitive alignment across layers); field evidence of passage systems that measurably improve cascade health trajectories; cultural variations in passage norms and identity transitions at scale; lightweight sensing of three-part transition completeness for busy Leaders of Leaders; long-term pipeline health metrics that capture transition quality and system performance when leaders step back; selection predictors beyond current results and similarity.

**Residual Gaps to Track**  
- Validated, lightweight diagnostics for three-part transition completeness usable in organizational settings.  
- Field evidence of Passage 2 curricula that reliably produce Enabling Leadership and cascade-health stewardship.  
- Selection methods that predict Passage 2 success beyond current results and similarity.  
- Practical role redesign patterns that reduce unnecessary complexity while preserving necessary Adaptive Space for Leaders of Leaders.  
- AI-era passage architectures that build hybrid judgment capability without accelerating Judgment Depreciation.  
- Cultural calibration tools for passage expectations and identity work.  
- Longitudinal evidence of minimum viable Passage 2 support packages under constraint.
