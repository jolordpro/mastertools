## 2. Tier 2 Domain Expansion Backlog

Core set is complete. Tier 2 domains should be added only when:
- Golden Question testing or field use shows repeated retrieval failure or reasoning gaps, **or**
- A domain is clearly high-leverage for the primary audience (Leaders of Leaders) and not adequately covered by synthesis of existing pages.

### Priority Tier 2 Candidates (ordered)
1. **Skip-Level Leadership & Multi-Layer Sensing Systems** — Deep operationalization of skip-level practice, sensing architecture, and Cascade Distortion interruption beyond what BRIDGE + SAFE already cover.
2. **Executive Presence & Communication Under Pressure** — Especially for Passage 2+ leaders; links to CAP identity, ENERGY, and BRIDGE second-order observation.
3. **Storytelling & Narrative Leadership** — For generativity, change, and board-facing translation; links to GEN, BOARD, CQ.
4. **Leader Standard Work / Lean Leadership Cadences** — Practical rhythms that protect Adaptive Space, energy, and Enabling Leadership time (links to PEOPLE, ENERGY, SYS-CAS, BRIDGE).
5. **High-Potential Identification & Differentiation** — Beyond Pipeline; learning agility vs. GMA, potential vs. performance, bias interruption (links to PIPE, SPONSOR, CQ, DARK).
6. **Reverse Mentoring & Wisdom Transfer Systems** (full Super Page) — Currently covered inside GEN/SPONSOR; elevate if field demand is high.
7. **Crisis & Discontinuity Leadership** — Beyond DARK Path F; large-scale adaptive challenges, temporary hierarchy, recovery of Adaptive Space.
8. **Compensation, Recognition & Metric Design for Cascade Health** — System lever that currently sits across PEOPLE, BOARD, SPONSOR; may deserve dedicated treatment.

### Explicitly Deferred (unless strong field signal)
- Detailed competency models by function
- Individual contributor high-performance habits
- Generic coaching skills (already partially in BRIDGE)
- Country-specific cultural playbooks (better as CQ addenda than separate Super Pages)

## 3. Decision Rules for New Work

1. **Core stability first.** Do not expand Tier 2 until Golden Question success criteria (CTRL-07) are substantially met and critical HA items are either done or consciously deferred.
2. **Evidence discipline.** New Super Pages follow the same Mandatory Template, evidence_strength honesty, and CTRL-05 logging.
3. **Synthesis before proliferation.** If existing pages can answer the Golden Question through cross-links and Bridge synthesis, prefer hardening retrieval and Coach notes over new pages.
4. **Audience filter.** Primary audience remains Leaders of Leaders and those who design systems for them. Domains outside that focus stay lower priority.

## 7. Hardening Addenda Addressed via Skill Instruments (2026-07-26)

Three open High-Priority Addenda were addressed by building executable skill instruments rather
than by expanding Super Page prose. The instruments read the existing Super Pages at runtime and
add procedure, sequencing, and output discipline — they add no content, and no Super Page was
modified.

| HA ID | Super Page | Instrument | Status |
|-------|------------|-----------|--------|
| HA-01 | AI-001 | `judgment-drift-check` — six-signal Decision Drift screen, override-quality sampling, "AI-off" evaluative exercise, four hybrid misalignment modes, Agent Manager sequence | **Addressed as procedure.** Validated Decision Drift *leading indicators* remain absent from the literature; the CTRL-05 residual gap stands. |
| HA-02 | DARK-001 | `containment-decision` — criterion-by-criterion Containment/Recovery determination, four-way differential with CQ assumption check, bounded recovery contract generator, enabler mapping, post-containment sequence | **Addressed.** Satisfies the "decision worksheet + post-containment sequence detail" intent. |
| HA-03 | PEOPLE-001 | `capacity-audit` — six-step audit with feasibility verdict, "do both" detection, nine redesign levers, 90-day package with protected stop list | **Addressed.** Satisfies the "capacity audit template + worked stop-list example" intent. |

Two further instruments were built in the same pass: `leadership-coach` (governance, routing, and
conversation discipline compiled from 00/02/05) and `cascade-diagnostic` (the BRIDGE-001
seven-domain health pass).

**Important boundary.** These instruments improve *conversion* — how reliably the library's
decision rules get applied in practice. They do **not** change evidence strength and generate no
new field evidence. The residual gaps recorded in CTRL-05 remain open exactly as written,
specifically: validated lightweight Decision Drift leading indicators usable without Goodhart
risk; validated decision aids for Containment vs. Recovery under real political and results
pressure; and field evidence that capacity-first redesign outperforms capability-first on cascade
health. These items are therefore recorded as **"addressed as procedure," not "closed."** Closing
them would imply an evidence improvement that has not occurred, which this library's own evidence
discipline forbids.

**Validation.** All five instruments passed a blind Golden Question pass on 2026-07-26 — both
question sets, with the skill deliberately unnamed so routing was tested alongside answer quality.
Logs in `Test_Results/`. Two skill defects were found and fixed during that pass (an omitted
toxic-actor cause in the silence differential; unsourced review cadences stated with library
authority), and five corpus-integrity defects were found and corrected under the Control Layer
Change Protocol.
