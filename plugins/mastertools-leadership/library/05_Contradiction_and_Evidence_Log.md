## 2. Known Tensions & Contradiction Resolutions

### 2.1 Speed / Efficiency vs. Long-Term Evaluative Capacity (AI-001 ↔ ENERGY-001 ↔ CAP-001)
- **Tension:** AI adoption is often justified by speed and headcount efficiency; healthy Judgment Loops and anti-Judgment-Depreciation work require Adaptive Space, energy surplus, and capacity for complexity.
- **Resolution:** Default to Hybrid. Short-term efficiency gains are real; they become net-negative if they systematically erode human evaluative capacity. Board and Leader of Leaders governance must treat long-term Judgment Health as a strategic asset (BOARD-001, AI-001). Efficiency-only framing is a failure mode.

### 2.2 Results vs. Cascade Health (DARK-001 ↔ PIPE-001 ↔ PEOPLE-001 ↔ BOARD-001)
- **Tension:** Short-term results (and “irreplaceable” high performers) frequently shield cascade damage.
- **Resolution:** Results are necessary but never sufficient. Multi-layer cascade-health data (Safety, Energy, Adaptive Space, Judgment Health, Generativity) must be visible at Leader of Leaders and board level. Containment vs. Recovery decisions and selection/promotion criteria must weight cascade health. Results shields are an explicit failure mode.

### 2.3 Individual Development vs. System / Role Design (CAP-001 ↔ PIPE-001 ↔ PEOPLE-001)
- **Tension:** Organizations default to training and capability development when the primary constraint is capacity (role design, load, metrics, decision rights).
- **Resolution:** Diagnose capacity before (or at least alongside) capability. “Do both” without redesign is the most common damaging pattern. Person-focused development is high-leverage only when capacity exists to exercise it.

### 2.4 Universal Cascade-Health Requirements vs. Cultural Expression (CQ-001 ↔ All)
- **Tension:** Safety, energy surplus, non-toxic high performance, Enabling Leadership, and long-term judgment capacity are treated as universal; their expression is culturally contingent.
- **Resolution:** Hold the universal requirements. Calibrate expression, sensing channels, sponsorship framing, Passage methods, AI override design, and board challenge norms. Refuse both ethnocentric imposition and cultural relativism that excuses genuine cascade damage.

### 2.5 Generativity / Legacy vs. Short-Term Delivery (GEN-001 ↔ ENERGY-001 ↔ BOARD-001)
- **Tension:** Generative investment and wisdom transfer require energy surplus and Adaptive Space that are the first things cannibalized under delivery pressure.
- **Resolution:** Energy surplus for generativity must be designed (role and load triage), not hoped for. Board metric architecture and Leader of Leaders accountability must give generativity and cascade-health standing weight, or they will remain rhetorical.

### 2.6 Sponsorship as Organic vs. Designed System (SPONSOR-001)
- **Tension:** Purely informal sponsorship reproduces similarity and proximity networks; heavy formalization risks patronage accounting and Goodhart.
- **Resolution:** Light-accountability cascading systems (portfolio discipline, four-acts visibility, reverse flows, multi-source reality in succession) are the target. Measure for cascade-health impact, not volume of sponsorship meetings.

### 2.7 Containment vs. Recovery (DARK-001)
- **Tension:** Developmental optimism often defaults to recovery coaching when multi-layer damage is already significant.
- **Resolution:** Default bias when multi-layer safety, energy, or Adaptive Space damage is present or accelerating: Containment first. Recovery is a conditional, time-bound experiment with cascade protection and pre-agreed failure triggers. Endless coaching of the uncoachable is a high-cost failure mode.

### 2.8 Board Short-Term Fiduciary Duty vs. Long-Term Adaptive Capacity (BOARD-001)
- **Tension:** Boards are structurally oriented to near-term financial and compliance oversight; cascade health and Judgment Health are longer-cycle.
- **Resolution:** Cascade health and long-term human evaluative capacity are strategic assets that affect durable performance and risk. Metric and discussion architecture must give them standing weight. Pure results-only board framing is a cascade-level failure mode.
