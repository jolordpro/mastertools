# Clay detail: sets that earn depth of field (Oct 2026)

Read when: a clay or geometry-stage frame is on the table, or a playbook names a CLY move. Clay is judged before any
material or light (geometry-first rule), so these moves land at clay, not at look stage.

Calibration: one project's clay mock-ups, round 3 → 3c. The art director on round 3: "not complex or sophisticated
or detailed enough scenes". On round 3c: "i love the detail you put into the first and last frames". Those frames were
a row of six locker stalls up a 24 mm lens, and a seating bowl of 14 raked tiers cut into 24 sections by aisles.
Mapping "first and last" to those two frames is Claude's reading of the board order.

| CLY-01 | Hero object on a floor, nothing behind it | Build the whole place: the room, the row, the bowl. Put the hero inside a repeat of its own unit | DOF and camera moves need depth to work on; an empty backdrop blurs to nothing | A | AD note 2026-10-27 | C→S | — |
| CLY-02 | Repeated units read as clones | Vary the repeats: mirror every other one, turn some, leave some empty, change the dressing | Real spaces are used and uneven | A | AD note 2026-10-28 | C→S | — |
| CLY-03 | Proportions feel toy-like or vague | Model at real dimensions: rink 61 x 26 m with 8.5 m corners, stall pitch 120 cm, seat tread 85 cm and rise 40 cm | Scale cues (rows, posts, seams) only sell when their counts and pitch are right | D | NHL rink spec; measured refs | FIX | — |
| CLY-04 | Smooth slabs where a real thing has construction | Give each surface its build: board gaps, seat backs and pans per tier, cap rails, glass stanchions every 150 cm, fillets on every edge | Clay shows form through small shadows; construction lines are the detail the eye reads | A | — | C→S | — |
| CLY-05 | Cloth hangs as a flat sheet | Drape folds that grow toward the hem and are absent at the hold points; sleeves fold less than the body | A hung garment folds under gravity below its support | P | — | C→S | — |
| CLY-06 | Wrong shape found only after a GPU render (a round rink, a camera inside a wall) | Check shape and framing in a CPU preview first, with the real cameras | Cheap previews catch topology and framing errors before GPU time | P | project run r3c | FIX | — |
