# Typography (TYP)

Taught in: SCAD MOME 729 (2026–27: Motion Design Typographic Integration), MOME 501, 369, 746 · SOM Photoshop + Illustrator Unleashed.

## Read
- **F:** The first read; hierarchy.
- **C:** Kerning at display size; widows; legibility of the smallest type.
- **X:** Read time needs in and out points.
- **R:** Families and weights in use; size; tracking; leading; animators; in/out points; motion blur on text; C4D text extrusion and bevel.
