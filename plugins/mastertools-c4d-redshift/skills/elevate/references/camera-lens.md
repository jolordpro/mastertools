# Camera and lens (CAM)

Taught in: SOM Lights, Camera, Render m01, m05 · SCAD MOME 709, VFX 503, MOME 360 · Gnomon Previsualization and Animatics.

## Read
- **F:** Lens feel (bulge or compression); camera height; parallax; how depth of field is used; screen direction across cuts.
- **X:** Motion blur and move quality can't be judged from stills; they need the readout or a frame strip.
- **R:** Focal length; sensor; DOF on/off, f-stop and focus target; shutter; camera height; F-curves on moves.
