# PB-LOGO: extruded logo or type resolve

A frequent hero shot in broadcast sports packages. Stages: approach → sweep/glint → settle → hold.

## Check order (lead with the highest-tier fixes)
0. **Whole-frame sweep:** MAT-14, MAT-15 on the set around the mark (`vision-protocol.md`)
1. **Geometry:** BEV-01, BEV-02, BEV-05, BEV-08, BEV-09, BEV-11, TYP-14
2. **Light on the mark:** LGT-04, LGT-01, LGT-03, LGT-13
3. **Material:** MAT-13 (4x crop of every camera-facing surface, side walls included), MAT-16, MAT-17 for clear or frosted marks, MAT-03, MAT-04, MAT-06, MAT-01, MAT-02
4. **Motion of the resolve:** MOT-03, MOT-04, MOT-08, MOT-10
5. **Frame and finish:** CMP-12 (mark sits in its surface, not on it), CMP-11, FIN-01, FIN-02, FIN-03

Load `bevels-edges.md` and `lighting.md`. Add `materials-texture.md` if the mark is metal or painted.

## Stage notes
- **Approach:** the camera move has to reveal something (CAM-11); long-lens compression (CAM-01).
- **Sweep:** one highlight travels the bevel on the beat (LGT-13). In 2.5D AE work, use a glint pass (AE-06).
- **Settle:** an overshoot scaled to the weight (MOT-03), never a dead stop.
- **Hold:** the brightest value sits on the mark. Drift keeps the speculars alive (LGT-14, CAM-13).

## World-class anchor
In IF's CBS Sports open, 3D movement and reflective light turn the open into an abstract journey into the logo (`moments.md`, IF-CBS).

## Brand rule
Team and network marks are brand-governed. Never alter the letterforms (TYP-14). Elevate the light, material and motion instead.

## Gold examples needed
At least 2: one metal mark and one painted or flat-color mark.
