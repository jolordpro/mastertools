MacDonald cites two customers [05:24-06:29]. Milk Effects: five months to deliver 650 shots with a relatively small team, and spare capacity let them take other projects in parallel. Fuse Effects on Fox's The Orville: 220 shots, over 1,000 man-hours, eight weeks, rendered with Deadline peaking onto AWS. His hybrid layout: Deadline's database and repository stay as is, local render nodes and AWS workers appear the same to the user, so cloud workers are treated like on-prem workers [08:13].

**Use it:** useful as scale references when explaining what a small team can get through; vendor-selected examples, so not a typical result.
