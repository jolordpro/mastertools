Tom's Hardware's general tests put the PRO 6000 about 10-15% ahead of a stock 5090. No Redshift-specific comparison was found.
Memory is the bigger difference: 96 GB vs 32 GB, so VRAM decides whether a scene fits, not speed.

**Use it:** choose the card by scene VRAM first; do not claim a Redshift speed ratio from this.
