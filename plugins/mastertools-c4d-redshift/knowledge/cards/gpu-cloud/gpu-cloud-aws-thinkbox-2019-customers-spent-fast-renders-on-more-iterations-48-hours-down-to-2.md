MacDonald showed real data from one large studio: submissions waited as long as about 48 hours at peaks, and the expected plan was to send overflow to the cloud [02:43-03:15]. What customers did instead was spread work across more capacity, up to about 1,600 workers, bringing a submission from 48 hours to about 2 hours, and then run more iterations, take more reviews and notes and raise the quality bar [03:46-04:53]. Mac Moore (Conductor) adds that after proof-of-concept rushes, customers got a 150-frame shot back in 15 minutes and could tweak it three or four more times with the director, where on-prem they had one more shot [35:00-36:03].

**Use it:** the pitch to a motion designer is 'more looks per day', not 'cheaper frames': plan a deliverable around extra rounds of a shot before the deadline.
