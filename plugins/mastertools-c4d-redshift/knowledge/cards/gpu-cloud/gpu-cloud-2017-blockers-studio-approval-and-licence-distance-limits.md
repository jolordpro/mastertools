Prives [11:32-15:39]: even if cloud is scalable and cheaper, a vendor cannot put a client's content there without the client studio's approval. MPAA best-practice guidelines exist, but following them or passing a self-audit or third-party audit does not give clearance; Google handled it one studio at a time (Disney, MPC's client work named). The other blocker: software licences were typically limited to on-prem or within a set distance of the facility, and vendors (Deadline in 2015, Autodesk Maya on GCP in 2016) were reworking terms and products for cloud.

**Use it:** a delivery checklist item for cloud rendering: confirm the client allows cloud use and that licence terms cover the cloud region before the job lands.
