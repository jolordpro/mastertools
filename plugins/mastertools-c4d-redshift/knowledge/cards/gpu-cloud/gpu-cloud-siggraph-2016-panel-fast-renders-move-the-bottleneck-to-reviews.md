Chu relays a customer who got so efficient that they ran review sessions twice a day and the limit became how many reviews the supervisor could sit through, not how fast renders came back [29:17-29:49]. The panel's stated goal: do not wait on renders, get things back when needed [29:49].

**Use it:** when render turnaround stops being the constraint, the next place to look is review capacity: batch frames per review and decide who approves before sending.
