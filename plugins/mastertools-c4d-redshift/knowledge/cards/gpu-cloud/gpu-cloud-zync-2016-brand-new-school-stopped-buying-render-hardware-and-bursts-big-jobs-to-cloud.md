Prives says the studio (a motion design shop in the C4D world) has a farm he recalls as about 20 or 30 nodes that serves most work; for massive jobs they used to rent machines or turn work away, partly because the New York office had no space for another rack [12:29-13:01]. They stopped buying hardware and use cloud for those jobs, taking on bigger work [13:01]. The same trend for others is to not refresh dying hardware [13:32]. Studios building their own farms on Google then: Framestore, MPC and Zoic [14:34-15:04]. He frames the cloud farm as something a freelancer could not otherwise afford [14:03].

**Use it:** direct reference for a broadcast-motion shop: keep a small local farm for daily work and burst for the oversize job; vendor-reported.
