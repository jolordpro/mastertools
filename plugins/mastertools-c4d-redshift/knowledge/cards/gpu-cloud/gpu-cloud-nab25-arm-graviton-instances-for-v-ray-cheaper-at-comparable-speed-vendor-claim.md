Phillip Miller (VP product management, Chaos), March 2025: cost is the first question customers ask about cloud rendering; Arm processors help because they use less electricity and are more affordable; all of Chaos's tests so far show comparable speed and always cheaper; a V-Ray solution was promised for "later in the summer" [06:53-07:58]. Vendor claim, no numbers or scene named. Chaos also says licensing worked remotely from the pandemic on, that the cloud suits running AI models and denoising, and that generative tools will give artists a better brush and not replace them [05:19-06:21, 22:53-23:24]. Not a Redshift or C4D statement; Redshift on Graviton was not mentioned.
