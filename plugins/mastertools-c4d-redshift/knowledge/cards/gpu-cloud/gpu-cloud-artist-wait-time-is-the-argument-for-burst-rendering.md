Three talks make the same point. Prives [09:58-10:29]: the largest studio cost is people, so instead of waiting overnight or three to four hours for a render stuck behind other jobs, you spin up as many VMs as frames (500 frames, 500 VMs) and artists turn shots faster. Kember [05:51]: artists remain the very expensive part. Petty [17:39]: taking the rush job turned a good month into a great one. Framestore's quote: burst capacity gave breathing room at peaks for creative refinement [09:27, TP].

**Use it:** lead cloud estimates with hours saved per artist, not only GPU-hour price.
