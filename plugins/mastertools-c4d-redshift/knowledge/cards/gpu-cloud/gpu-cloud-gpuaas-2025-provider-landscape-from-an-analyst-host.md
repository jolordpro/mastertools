David Linthicum (theCUBE Research) lists non-big-three GPU clouds [04:24-08:01]: Lambda (research teams), Paperspace (notebooks, competitive pricing), Vast.ai (peer-to-peer marketplace, RTX 6000 to H100), JarvisLabs (India, no card needed to try), Fluidstack (some instances under $1 an hour), TensorDock (launched 2020, short pay-per-use jobs, mentions VFX rendering), RunPod (multi-GPU, fine-tuning), DataCrunch (Europe, renewable power, A100/H100). CoreWeave: founded 2017 as crypto mining, GPU cloud from 2022, Nvidia holds a minority stake, over 60% of revenue from Microsoft, 32 data centres heading past 40 in 2025, IPO talked of at about $35B, 2024 net loss $863.4M [01:42-03:51]. Caption figures for revenue are garbled.
Risks he names: power cost, regional concentration (latency and compliance), reliance on Nvidia [10:34-11:38]. An AI-business overview with little on rendering.

**Use it:** a market map only; check prices and reliability on the provider's own page before using.
