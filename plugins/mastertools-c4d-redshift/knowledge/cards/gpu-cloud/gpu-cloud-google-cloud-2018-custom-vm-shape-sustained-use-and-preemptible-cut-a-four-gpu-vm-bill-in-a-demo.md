Rival clouds tie GPU count to a fixed CPU/memory bundle; Google lets you pick CPU, host memory, GPU model, GPU count and disk type separately [20:44-21:16, 24:25-24:56].
Demo of four P100s: about 4,455 dollars a month at list; trimming cores and memory moved roughly 4,300 to 3,100 (numbers heard in auto-captions); preemptible took about another 1,000 [24:56-25:58].
Billing is per second, and sustained use gives an automatic discount rising to 30 percent over a month with no contract [21:16-21:48, 25:27].
He also says 1,000 K80 GPUs for an hour cost about 500 dollars on demand and 250 or less preemptible [06:33-07:04].

**Use it:** when the estimator compares clouds, ask whether the GPU can be sized apart from the CPU, and whether billing is per second or per hour.
