Nathan Robinson [11:34-12:35]: he once spent roughly $550,000 on five Supermicro blades and got two to three years of useful life; he used to amortise on a three-year lease, but GPU and CPU generations change too fast, so now he would spin up the fastest hardware when a deadline demands it, or a slower, cheaper type when the schedule allows. He also says a cloud setup means a studio can start with no multi-million-dollar capital outlay, quoting two million dollars as what a start-up once needed [15:40-16:41]. Figures are his recollection, undated (he started Entropic 27 years before 2025). The AWS moderator then prompts the answer, so this is a friendly panel.
