The customer keeps only a local licence to build the scene. GarageFarm calls its price all-inclusive of licence cost; RebusFarm says it has been a fully licensed farm since 2017.
Fox Renderfarm's page lists Redshift but says certain software may carry extra charges; iRender's page is silent, and you can log into your own Maxon account on the node.

**Use it:** treat these as vendor statements; the deal terms with Maxon are not public.
Transcript support: iRender provides a free combined Cinema and Redshift licence on its rented 4090 package (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mK3nXETuNII [04:24-05:43])
