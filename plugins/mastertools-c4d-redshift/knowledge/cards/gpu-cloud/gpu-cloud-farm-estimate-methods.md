Drop & Render: benchmark-score calculator plus one test frame. GarageFarm: animation-only best-case calculator plus a reduced-res test. Fox and Ranch: calculators. Conductor: scout frames before the full run. Deadline Cloud: budgets and a usage explorer after the fact. iRender: none.
GarageFarm itself calls its calculator best-case; an iRender blog quotes about 30% error (unverified).

**Use it:** the estimator should give a range and tie it to a measured draft, not copy a calculator.
Transcript support: a vendor estimate that renders sampled frames and multiplies by frame count (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sh8UhQ1zofg [35:11])
