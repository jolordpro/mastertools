Mike Tanzillo and a student sCDCs5At8uY [04:22-05:23], April 2021: the school pointed students to the Conductor render farm (about 200 credits each, as said). A student noticed the bill seems to come only afterwards and render-hours mean little. Advice: talk to the farm's contact and give a rough number: minutes per frame at home and frame count, and ask for a cost estimate. Farm only the long environment render. No price basis was given.

**Use it:** Cost estimator: state local minutes per frame and frame count, and compare to a vendor quote.
