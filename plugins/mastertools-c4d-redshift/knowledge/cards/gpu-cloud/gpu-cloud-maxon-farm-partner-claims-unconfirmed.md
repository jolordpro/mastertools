Drop & Render and Super Renders Farm claim partner status; Fox and iRender make similar claims. Maxon's partners page shows no farm list in its text and only says partners who wish to be listed should contact Maxon. A farm blog says only those two appear on a Maxon list.
No Maxon render-partner programme page was found at the URLs tried (404s).

**Use it:** never repeat a farm's partner claim as fact; ask Maxon directly.
