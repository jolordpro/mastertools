Stated by OTOY at GTC 2016 (a roadmap talk): cloud rendering is built into Octane 3 and its plugins; a "test drive" subscription starts at $9.99 a month with the first month free [07:38-10:30].
Rough value: ten dollars covers about "a thousand GPU minutes", and as an estimate about eight GTX 980s for an hour; the minutes were expected to rise by as much as 50 percent by the end of the year as better pricing was negotiated [09:59-11:01]. The two figures do not match as plain minutes (eight GPUs for an hour is 480), so the unit is probably OctaneBench-normalised; unclear from the captions.
The unit is OctaneBench: everyone who downloaded Octane had benchmarked their machine, so cloud cost could be mapped to it [10:30]. Other 2016 prices: Octane 3 licence $399, $49 upgrade for a period; plugins about $10 a month; enterprise tier up to 200 GPUs and a private network [11:32-12:03, 18:18-18:48].

**Use it:** history for the Render Network entry: it started as benchmark-point billing. Do not quote these prices; they are ten years old.
