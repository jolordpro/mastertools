Calahan [01:16-02:17]: the director knew the film was outdoors and that she painted landscapes in her spare time. She kept putting ideas in front of him and brought a big box of lighting painting studies done from real locations. She let him sift through them to see what he liked and to spark a dialogue about what the film could look like.
Her studies were her own, from places she knew, so they carried light logic and colour relationships from real observation.
Transfer: arrive at a look review with a stack of real-light studies, not only a finished frame, so the client reacts to specifics.

**Use it:** The coach recommends a reference board of observed light studies as the first step in a look conversation.
