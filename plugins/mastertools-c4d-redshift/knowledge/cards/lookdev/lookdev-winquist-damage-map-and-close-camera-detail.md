A damage map told each shot which wounds and burns applied, and the look teams made dozens upon dozens of variants from it [§4]. Because the huge animals were seen close, months went into look dev so wide views matched concept art while the detail still held with the camera a few metres away [§4].

**Use it:** Plan a damage or state map for hero assets across a sequence and test the close view as well as the wide.
