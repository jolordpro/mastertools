Guest: Erik Messerschmidt. Film was considered and dropped. Fincher dislikes unexpected accidents. Messerschmidt says for each pleasant surprise he has met ten disappointments, and a director placing a jar to the millimetre needs a monitor, not ground glass [33:35-34:37].
Going digital allowed grain to be played with, lighting at about 100 foot-candles instead of 600, and gate weave set to taste. He says no surprises were involved in how it looks, as all was tested [34:37-35:38].

**Use it:** Same stance as CG: keep imperfections as dials you set, not accidents you accept.
