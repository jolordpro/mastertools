Razon: with no good reference, work will look fake [39:43].
Soho goes on set whenever it can to take photos of what something looks like in that lighting, and builds lighting from HDRs shot in place [40:17].
Example: a slow-motion bullet hit used a supplied high-speed reference (captions say 1 million fps, unchecked), with muzzle flash and shock ring matched over many iterations [37:38].
Counter-intuitive build: the cornfield was cleared in the plate, then CG stalks filled the cleared area so they could be blown around [41:51].

**Use it:** Ask for set photos and HDRs at shoot time and keep the reference side by side in review.
