Weeks of concepts felt too literal. The team took a simple glacier model from photo reference and used a Maya space-filling routine to fill it with architectural blocks, height set by elevation. They then added rules for street divisions, scale variation and damage, all from glacier samples, and reviewed after every rule. The VFX art director painted over the CG renders and those paintings fed the next rules. Collapse animation in Houdini was referenced from glacier footage, not building demolitions. [§4-§5]

**Use it:** When literal concepts fail, build a rule set and look at the result after each rule.
