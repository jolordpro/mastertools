Cole calls the Metkayina reef village the hardest design on Avatar 2 [33:50]. James Cameron had a blurry idea (mangrove roots, village suspended below, tensile walkways) and the team had to bring it into focus; the aim is a design that feels inevitable, and it is easy to overdesign alien beauty so it looks forced [34:21].
After going round in circles he called an arts-and-crafts day: plastic tubing for mangrove roots, wire, pantyhose stretched over them [34:53]. His reason: stop imagining, start visualizing, because the object does what it does [35:23].
The breakthrough was realising the walkways and the huts (marui) are one structure, so stop designing them separately [35:23]. Another round of concept art then went faster with a game plan [35:54].
Transfer: a quick physical or blockout proxy settles structure faster than more paintings.

**Use it:** When a hero object or set will not resolve, the coach suggests a crude proxy model and a single unifying rule before more finished renders.
