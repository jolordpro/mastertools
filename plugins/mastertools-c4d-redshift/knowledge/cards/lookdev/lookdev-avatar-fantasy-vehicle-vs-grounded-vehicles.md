Lead vehicle designer TyRuben Ellingson says the Samson helicopters took scale from the Blackhawk rather than the Huey, and the AMP suit looked to manufacturing robotics and military vehicles [§2-3]. The mining vehicles began from Bagger 288 excavators, then got extra cable-tower complexity and four times the bucket wheels for scale and aggression [§5]. The Dragon was described by Cameron as a deliberate "fantasy vehicle": rules could bend to make it bigger than life, echoing WWII flying fortresses [§7]. Intakes were placed for a menacing look, not for the action beat [§7].

**Use it:** Ground the supporting objects in real references; spend the exaggeration on one hero object and say which one it is.
