Still pond water (no sim) used Houdini's Ocean-spectrum-style surface, described as fancy 3D noise plus good lighting [44:26].
When forces are applied the water boils and rises; shots that are out of focus are easy, while far, sharp ones needed special sims to hold an art-directed circular rip-curl shape [45:00, 45:32].
Mist and fine detail at distance are what sell it, but not too much, so it does not cover the shot [46:03].
A heavy hero sim was reused by taking one frame, adding extra sims, and sculpting the look for a slow-motion shot [48:08].
Razon does not recommend students start with water; push-button water teaches little [42:55].

**Use it:** For art-directed liquid shots, budget sim time per shot shape and use depth of field and mist where the brief allows.
