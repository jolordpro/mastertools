Kapustin adds an Octane noise to Bump with a box projection and very small scale; too strong, so a gradient is placed between with the white point pulled very dark, finally "just three percent" [05:18-06:53]. It breaks up perfect CG and reads as photoreal grain at 2K [19:54-21:29].
Finish: he says Octane has a bit too little contrast, so render with alpha (save alpha in settings), convert to smart object, Camera Raw: a tiny cool shift, whites up and blacks down, pull highlights if overblown, texture up, and a little grain; vignette only if the background is not black [18:51-21:29].

**Use it:** Matches a quiet finish pass: clean render, micro-bump, restrained grade, small grain.
