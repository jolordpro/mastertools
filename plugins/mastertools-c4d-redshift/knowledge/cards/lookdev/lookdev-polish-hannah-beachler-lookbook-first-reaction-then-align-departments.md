Her sequence: sit with the script until she connects, then make a deck or lookbook of her first reaction and notes, and share it with the director; if the director connects, they move forward [§3]. Then she sits with the cinematographer and costume designer so the three jobs read as one aesthetic [§3]. She still puts about a thousand pictures on the wall at any budget and gives each project the same intensity [§4-5].

**Use it:** Get early agreement on a visual statement and share it across departments before detail work.
