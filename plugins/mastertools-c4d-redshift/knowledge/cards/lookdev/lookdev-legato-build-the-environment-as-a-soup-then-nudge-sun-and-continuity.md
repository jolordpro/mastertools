The environments team (MPC, led by Audrey Ferrara with art director Chris Glass) set the scenario and crude pre-picks; the hard part was the perfection of it: why the flowers go there, creating randomness that does not look stilted, and knowing what a real jungle looks like at that time of day [10:01-10:33].
Everything goes into a giant soup, then 'rearrange the deck chairs' to keep it consistent from shot to shot. What he was handed was already working, so he only massaged it: sun in the right spot, a push here and there [10:33-11:03].
He compares the job to a director with a good actor: guide, do not manufacture [11:03-11:34].

**Use it:** Review environments for random-looking placement and time-of-day, then spend the last pass on continuity of sun position between neighbouring shots.
