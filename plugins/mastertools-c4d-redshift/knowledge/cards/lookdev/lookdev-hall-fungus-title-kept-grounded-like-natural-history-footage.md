The showrunners wanted it to look like natural history footage so it feels real, and they asked the team to keep every shape recognisably fungal even when it forms a face or a map of America [03:37-05:11]. The team went through "an awful lot of footage" to find textures and motions [04:39]. One guest (speaker unlabeled) notes growth in lab video follows an efficient grid-like path, which naturally resembles human networks [07:48]. Details are meant to be fleeting and mostly caught on repeat views, linked to the show [06:47-07:48]. Nature makes things look simple, so making it believable took research up to full production; the final build mixed Houdini, Cinema 4D and Octane [14:37-15:08].
