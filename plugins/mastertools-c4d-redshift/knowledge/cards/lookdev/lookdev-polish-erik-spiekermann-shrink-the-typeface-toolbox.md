Erik Spiekermann says type is intimidating because it used to be specialist knowledge, but people read words all day and a typeface carries a subliminal message (sweet, hard, soft); the right word in the wrong face sends the wrong message and context shapes it [00:00-01:04]. Many terms (leading, points, picas) come from metal type and no longer matter; do not try all 5,000 faces, choose one you are comfortable with and use it until bored or told it is bad; certain things must be mechanical and precise, others pretty; trust your instincts [01:04-02:05].
**Use it:** For title packages, limit the type palette to one or two faces and check the chosen face fits the message and the context.
