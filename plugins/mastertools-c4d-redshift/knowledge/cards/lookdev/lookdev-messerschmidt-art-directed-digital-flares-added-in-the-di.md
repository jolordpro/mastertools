He and Fincher liked 1970s anamorphic qualities but avoided anamorphic: wide lenses at close range, shallow depth, lots of focal lengths and matching sets were hard to get; so they shot spherically on lenses that almost cannot flare [1:14:12-1:14:43]. They took existing highlights, added flares in the DI where appropriate, and gave notes such as more magenta here, less green there; chromatic aberration was painted on too [1:15:14-1:15:45]. Kit: one set of Leicas, with duplicates of the common 29, 40 and 65 mm; 21, 18, 75 and 100 mm were used a handful of times [1:15:45-1:16:45].

**Use it:** Choose the flare per highlight and tint it to the grade, not as a random preset; keep a few focal lengths for consistency.
