MPC's Bangalore team surveyed over 40 locations, producing hundreds of thousands of photos: trees, rocks, plants, leaves and twigs for ground debris. Some rock walls became textures or photogrammetry. Sets began as sketches, got a hero model such as one hero tree, then were scattered with plants, pebbles and dead leaves. A few key pre-comp images per set went to the director before render optimisation. Totals quoted: 54 species, 284 sets, about 500 plants, 800+ MPC artists, about 1,984 TB of data and 240 million render-farm hours for the film. [§3] (Scale numbers added at the gate spot read.)

**Use it:** Collect real reference for the small stuff (debris, bark, leaf edges) before dressing a set; the ground clutter sells scale.
