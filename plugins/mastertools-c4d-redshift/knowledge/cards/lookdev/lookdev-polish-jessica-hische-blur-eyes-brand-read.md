Jessica Hische says to judge a logo or brand with glasses off or eyes blurred, so only overall look and colour read [56:06]. She asks the client for the broad reason first (why does it feel off?) and only then goes to small bugs like one letter [54:34]. Colour story is the first thing that survives blur, so she keeps it similar in a refresh [19:15].
Transfers: the same blur-first, big-to-small order works for a styleframe or end-card review.

**Use it:** In a review, run a blur or squint pass on the frame before any pixel notes, and make the reviewer name the overall problem before the detail.
