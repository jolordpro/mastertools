Spacecraft designs came from the art department, and everything was grounded in archive material from Apollo missions and the shuttle, and in physics: the colour of sunlight, its harshness and how shadows look when light has not passed through an atmosphere [02:15-02:47]. For the abstract tesseract, he looked at how artists show time and then at slit-scan photography, where the horizontal axis becomes time, leading to an alternating lattice of rooms as moments in time [02:47-03:17].

**Use it:** For any space or vacuum scene, set hard sunlight with no atmospheric falloff, and justify choices with archive references.
