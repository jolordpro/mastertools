Two reds can look very different on camera under different light, and a grey suit against a blue background can merge [28:22-28:52]. With the costume and production designers he tested hundreds of setups: iPhone black-and-white mode as a quick preview, actors in front of seamless backgrounds lit from 15 directions, and about 40 green tile swatches against a brown suit [28:52-29:55]. Lens tests ran for weeks alongside colour tests, with grade work intertwined [32:03].

**Use it:** For monochrome or heavily graded looks, check luminance separation of subject vs. background in greyscale before committing materials.
