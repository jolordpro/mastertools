Andy's Illustrator type effects share a few rules [02:05-04:09; 05:11]. Black is hard to light, so use a brighter or light-grey base colour and judge the look after the lighting. The lit render shifts colour, so the colour picker on the object gives the wrong swatch; match the background by sampling the rendered result (he pastes a screenshot into the document). Roughness 1 and ambient light off give darker shadows and more contrast [02:36]; roughness 0 on round (inflated) forms shows reflections [09:53]. Repeated variants (isometric left and right, each with its own light rotation) are saved as graphic styles so the settings are applied in one click [06:47-07:18]. Complex fonts render slowly and can crash [10:24].

**Use it:** The two craft lessons (no black base, match backgrounds to the lit result) hold in C4D or AE too.
