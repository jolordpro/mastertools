He says stock 3D elements that work seamlessly in the same scene are almost impossible to find. The answer was a family of simple, beautiful, fun models built together (The Happy Toolbox, 150+ in the launch set) so style and scale agree. [§2]

**Use it:** When building a scene from parts, pick or build assets from one family so scale, shading and detail agree.
