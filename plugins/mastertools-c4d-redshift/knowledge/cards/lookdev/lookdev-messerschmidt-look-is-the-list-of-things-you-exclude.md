He says a look is about what you choose to exclude: for Mindhunter, minimal handheld, no Steadicam, structured frames where actors move within the frame, lots of top light, and certain tonal colours excluded [16:44-18:16]. An open list of 'things we will do' is harder to manage [17:15]. He argues it is unhealthy for a filmmaker to have the option to do anything, because the audience knows anything is possible, and recommends picking the limits you admire in reference films (few cranes, only top light, only hard window light) [1:05:27-1:07:00]. The set then needs fewer decisions [1:07:00].

**Use it:** Write a 'will not' list in the style brief (camera moves, colours, light directions, lens range) and use it as the first QC check on each shot.
