The same unnamed guest spent two to four weeks on a shot where glue with glitter lands on a character's head, with no glitter reference in the film and the team in another city working on glitter for a holiday special [43:42-44:44]. Starting from the supplied shader and reference, he kept pushing and circled. The look only fitted once he stripped the process back and simplified; going wild made the glitter look nice but wrong for how light had to interact on a film that needed to be physically accurate [44:13-44:44].
Also on that show: miniature scale needed scratches and detail, comp work often decided whether a render met the art director's note ('1980s wonderland super funk') [41:37-42:08].

**Use it:** When a hero material stalls past a few iterations, reset to the simplest version that reads and add one thing at a time.
