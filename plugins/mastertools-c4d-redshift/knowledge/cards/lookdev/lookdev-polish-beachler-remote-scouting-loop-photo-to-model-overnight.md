On the South Africa trip Beachler scouted 12 or 13 hours a day while her team stayed in Los Angeles; her assistant sent every photo back [§4]. At day's end, which was morning in LA, they held an hour-and-a-half meeting on the day's work, and she sent back sketches, notes and revised colour concepts [§4]. Photos of Oribi Gorge rock went straight to an art director to start modelling it, using Maya, Photoshop and Illustrator across the team [§4]. Notes from director Ryan Coogler during location moves were relayed to LA at once, so changes did not wait [§5].

**Use it:** For location-based reference, send photos with a short note saying where each will be used the same day.
