Before shooting a film he makes a lookbook of palette, texture and colour ideas drawn from the script and reading, as a hypothesis; the film either supports it or debunks it, and either result teaches him [40:06-40:36]. Then come tests (blood colours with the makeup department, how skin responds under the film's LUT and colour science) [42:43]. He repeats the same process on every film and calls the redundancy comforting: on set he moves from "a place of knowing," which leaves him free to say "I don't know" on the day [47:00-47:30]. Without that base, confusion is chaotic; with it, confusion is productive [47:30-48:02].

**Use it:** Do the same for a package: lookbook (palette, texture), then a few lit test frames on the real colour pipeline, before building the sequence. Stronger evidence than the shorter lookbook card.
