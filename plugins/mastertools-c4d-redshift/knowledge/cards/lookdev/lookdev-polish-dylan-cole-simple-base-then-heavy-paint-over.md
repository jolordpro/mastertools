The Metkayina sea wall began as a simple procedural terrace base, duplicated, then heavily painted. Coral was one large texture on a few alpha cards. He keeps the raw render and the painted result side by side to see how much the paint changed the image. Combine photobashing and 2D fractal renders, finish by hand.

**Use it:** Keep base geometry cheap and spend effort on the final surface pass; always A/B against the raw.
