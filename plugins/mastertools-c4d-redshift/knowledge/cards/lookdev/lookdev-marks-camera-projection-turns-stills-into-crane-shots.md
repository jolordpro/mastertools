For the final shots of the opener, and for True Detective's Richard Misrach photos, Marks built low-resolution geometry and projected the stills onto it in 3D space, then moved a camera through to get an equivalent to a crane shot. He must paint in occluded surfaces so the camera does not see double images. Cinema 4D's Camera Calibrator makes the setup simple; before it he matched projections by eye with patience and guesswork [§2].

**Use it:** For a still-based sequence, build the simplest geometry that holds the parallax, then paint fix-ups only where the camera can see.
