The team had a Westeros map and a photocopied hand-drawn Essos map. Wall scaled both in Photoshop until they matched, then used their dimensions, positions and terrain as given because fans would check. The author first saw the sequence at the premiere. [§5]

**Use it:** When a world has a canon, line the canon up in 2D before modelling anything.
