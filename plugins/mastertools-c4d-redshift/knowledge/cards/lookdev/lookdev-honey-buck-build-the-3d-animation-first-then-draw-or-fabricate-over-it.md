Honey [02:13-04:19] gives two examples. On a Nike basketball festival piece, the team animated everything in 3D first and made guidelines for the character look and motion, then drew over frame by frame (characters came from an illustrator). On the Sundance "Renewed Rebellion" piece, they animated the whole thing in CG first to fix exact timings, built the shapes in Illustrator, laser-cut them from wood, spray-painted every piece and matched the 3D previs in camera; another element was stop-motion fibre-optic cables rising through the ground to form the word. Both lock timing in cheap CG before costly handwork.

**Use it:** the order for any craft-heavy piece: fix timing and layout in 3D, then spend on hand work; supports Buck cockroach card.
