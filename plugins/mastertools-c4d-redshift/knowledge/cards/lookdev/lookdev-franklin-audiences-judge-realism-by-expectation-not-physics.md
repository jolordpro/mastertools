Franklin notes people expect a landing to hover and bounce as helicopters do; a real lander coming straight down looks wrong [54:16]. Moon photographs look fake because there are no atmospheric depth cues, the dust travels in arcs and does not hang [54:47-55:19].
For Interstellar, Kip Thorne stayed as executive producer through the shoot to keep the science, and the team published a paper on the black-hole rendering; IMAX resolution was the new part [55:19-57:53].
He also says spaceships in the Star Wars style move believably but not realistically [53:15].

**Use it:** Decide whether a shot needs to read as physically right or as expected; test with viewers before polishing the physics.
