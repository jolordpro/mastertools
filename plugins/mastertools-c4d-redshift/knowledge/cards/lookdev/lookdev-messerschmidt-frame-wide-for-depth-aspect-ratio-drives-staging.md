They considered 4:3 or 1.37, but a long dinner table scene would not work in a square frame, and 2.2:1 allowed overs with depth and perspective wide shots [11:33-14:10]. Aspect ratio affects staging, blocking and set building [12:38]. He accepts the criticism, but says a historically strict frame would have meant a different film [14:10].

**Use it:** Decide aspect ratio before layouts; test the hardest wide composition in the chosen frame.
