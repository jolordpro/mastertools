Fraser and director Gareth Edwards rewatched A New Hope and Empire in 4K at Lucasfilm [08:26-09:27] and found Vader looked a bit plastic and the production design cheap by today's measure; it did not match how they remembered it, which was more solid and gritty. They chose to base every decision on the remembered film: the newly released Alexa 65 and ultra panatar lenses (Richardson had just used them on Hateful Eight) for texture, with production design in keeping with the memory. The result was dirty sci-fi realism that suited the story [09:59].

**Use it:** when a client says "like X", ask for the remembered qualities, not a frame-for-frame copy; supports the "look before the film era, avoid cloning" guidance.
