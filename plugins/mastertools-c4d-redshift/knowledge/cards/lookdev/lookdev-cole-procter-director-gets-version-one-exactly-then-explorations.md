Cole [13:06-13:38]: Cameron describes something like a blurry photo in his mind; v1 is always exactly what he asked for, then the team explores. He may hybridise it with his idea or say "not what I imagined but I like it". Extra self-started designs sometimes get written into the script, the designer's dream. Procter [12:05-12:35]: the art department pulls the blurry image into focus in an interactive process; the director is discovering at the same time.

**Use it:** a note-handling pattern: deliver the literal ask first, then offer one or two explorations beside it.
