For roughly 10,000 antique-shop items the team defined three ages (fairly new, moderately aged, extremely aged) and applied texture to each by that vocabulary, while key props were hand-shaded [04:43-05:46]. Plastic toys got a light-penetrating translucency algorithm [05:46-06:16]. Wear is specific to story: frayed threads and smudges, scratches at joints, peeling stickers, scuffs on a doll's shoes from how an old doll walks, crazing on porcelain [06:16-06:46]. Atmosphere is varied per set (more dust in the shop, kicked-up dirt at the carnival) and the air around the toys is given richer texture than around humans to sell their small scale [11:54-12:25]. Scale was checked by photographing real models of toys with real objects [03:41].

**Use it:** elevate checks: does each object's wear match what it does; for a set of many props define 3 wear levels and a rule for which gets which.
