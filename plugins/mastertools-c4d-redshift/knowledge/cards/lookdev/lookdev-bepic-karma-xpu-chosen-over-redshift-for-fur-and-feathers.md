For an ostrich for O2 shown too close for cards [16:17-16:50], the team built a prototype first: render tests, feather deformation in shots, motion blur in different render engines, and the Houdini feather procedural [16:50-17:55]. They had used Redshift for six years and call it fast, but chose Karma XPU for the first time as they already had GPUs; reasons given were strong fur shading in recent versions, a good AOV set that the comp artist liked, and performance [17:55-18:57]. Groom came from an outside artist while look dev for crisp close-ups ran in parallel [18:57-19:29]. Single speaker, version-dependent; recheck.

**Use it:** Do a one-week render-engine and deformation prototype before locking a creature pipeline; weigh fur shading, AOVs and cost.
