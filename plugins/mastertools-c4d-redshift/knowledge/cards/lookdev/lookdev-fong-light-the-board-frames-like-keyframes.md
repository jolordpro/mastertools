Fong says Kyle Cooper kept pushing the team to figure out the lighting of each frame, treating Photoshop as a way to simulate light rather than a print-layout tool [13:41]. Lit boards looked like keyframes, and a colleague's lighting made a frame come alive [14:12]. She had come from flat Quark and Illustrator work, so painting a frame with light to look filmic was new [15:15].

**Use it:** Before a styleframe goes to the client, check that light direction, falloff and a dark side are decided, not left to the render. Feeds elevate and composition notes.
