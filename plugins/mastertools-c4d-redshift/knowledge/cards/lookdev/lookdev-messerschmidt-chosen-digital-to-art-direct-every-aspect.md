Fincher wanted a bloomy softness where blacks bleed slightly into whites, as in older processes, and prep time went into achieving that [52:32-53:35]. They examined grain structure and exposure range, and chose digital because they wanted to art direct every aspect, not accept what film gives [54:05-54:36]. He says there is no 'that's what we ended up with' in this working style [53:35].

**Use it:** When a vintage look is the target, build its parts (bloom, black bleed, grain) as controllable layers and sign them off in prep.
