Calahan says a raw render looks computer-generated however real the shading: the colour in the darks goes grey and muddy where eyes see rich colour [08:03-08:39].
For Ratatouille she studied good and bad food photography: good food never shows grey in the darks. She added processing to get rich colour in shadows and applied it to the whole film, especially skin tones and sets, not only the food, so the film has a juicy low end [08:39-09:47].
Why she chose this: "go with what the computer gives me" or build tools to get an enhanced reality [08:39].
Supports lookdev-calahan-tint-the-darks.

**Use it:** In a lookdev or grade pass, check whether the darks are grey, then lift saturation in the lows across the entire frame set for consistency.
