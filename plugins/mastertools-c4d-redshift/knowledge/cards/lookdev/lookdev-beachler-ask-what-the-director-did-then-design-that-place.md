Place tells you about characters without exposition and can work with or against them [01:05-01:36]. On location, the director had photographed the actor on a beach; she asked what he did when he went to the water as a kid; the answer was the rocks, so the rocks went in, seeing through his and the character's eyes [05:58-06:50]. She contrasts two directors' versions of the same city [06:50]. A neighbourhood she found beautiful he called not a good part of town, a reminder that the design reads through the lived view [07:52].

**Use it:** When styling a place for a team or client, ask what they actually did there and shoot that, not the postcard.
