Young is a big proponent of look-books: he compiles source material from photography or advertisements to realise his own vision before stepping on set and to have something handy to communicate with his directors [§1]. The profile adds that the director-DP relationship matters, and that being able to communicate, listen and learn from directors is key beyond a strong vision for a single shot [§2].

**Use it:** Build a source-image board before the first test and use it as the language in reviews.
