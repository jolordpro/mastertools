Fong's team built a 3D paper town as if it were a Rankin/Bass stop-motion special, in CG so the characters could be placed in it [37:34-38:35]. The creators fell for a snowy town frame; the town had never been shown whole, so the story became a travelogue through it [39:40-40:42]. It was vital that the buildings and the paper textures matched the look the boys' team had developed over the years; the South Park team animated all the gags and Fong's team supplied the environments [40:42-41:13]. Fans split between love and hate [38:05].

**Use it:** When extending an established look, match the original's surface texture first and let the original team animate the signature performance.
