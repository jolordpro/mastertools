Guest: Danielle Feinberg. The director (Domee Shi) asked for a look unlike other Pixar films, partly because the lead is a teenager going through big emotions. Every department had to support that story: camera, lighting and effects differ from Pixar's usual, so the look serves the story and creates the director's own world, drawn from her life [05:21-06:23].
The design aimed to be relatable not perfect: patchy eyebrows, moles and a "chunky cute" feel taken from the director's and a co-writer's own features [06:23-08:25].
As a former lighting DP, she found the challenge of a different look appealing [09:26-09:57].

**Use it:** When a brief asks for "different from our usual look", change camera, light and effects together so they agree, not lighting alone.
