Aiming away from overused glitch tricks, Marks pictured the characters as if re-entering the atmosphere in a digital world: detail shakes, rips and smears but stays tactile, and keeps the order digital artefacts carry. That order came from restricting movement to horizontal and vertical lines. [§4]

**Use it:** Give a glitch a rule such as axis-locked motion so it reads as digital, not random.
