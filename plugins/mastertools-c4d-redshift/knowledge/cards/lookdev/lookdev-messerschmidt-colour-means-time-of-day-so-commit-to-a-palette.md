He thinks of colour contextually: a morning scene needs morning light that serves the story, such as hard light on a hungover face [1:02:54-1:03:56]. He does not credit colour with the audience's emotional read, which he leaves to performance and editing [1:03:56]. Magenta and pink were excluded from Mindhunter even though he and Fincher love them; the point is choosing a palette and committing to it [1:04:26-1:04:56].

**Use it:** Put an excluded-colour list in the look doc; check frames against it.
