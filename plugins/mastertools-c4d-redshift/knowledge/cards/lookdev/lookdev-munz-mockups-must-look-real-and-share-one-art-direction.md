He calls out mockups where the banner, lanyard or candle flame is obviously fake and says the target is the realism of the best one in the set (the ticket) [21:06-21:37, 26:55]. Drop shadows all at one opacity and distance look flat; angled mockups look better [39:11].
Same mockup template reused for every thumbnail makes the fakery obvious: vary angles [47:32].
Photography in a set should be art-directed the same way: same backdrop, same time of day, same white balance (one pair went cool white versus orange) [43:23-43:53, 1:04:24-1:04:58]. Phone shots of printed work look cheap for a portfolio [25:22].
Transfers: hero stills and product renders should share one lighting and white-balance recipe across a set.

**Use it:** When judging a set of stills, check that backdrop, white balance and lighting match across all of them and that composites carry plausible shadows.
