Because fans would punish a wrong Kratos, she stayed in close touch with the game publisher on his defining features, such as the nose, and had a makeup team fabricate those [47:34-48:06]. The performer was chosen so his real brow bone matched, since a fake brow is hard to make expressive [48:37]. Stylists and costumers from a big fantasy film handled the transformation [49:08].

**Use it:** Pick the hard-to-fake feature first and cast or model to it; fabricate the easy ones. Applies to athlete likeness work too.
