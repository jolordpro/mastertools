He matched Cameron's real wreck photography, which showed two Mir subs, one filming the other. His version used an 18-foot replica of the wreck hanging upside down, two dollies for the sub models, smoke in a garage, supports painted out using repeat passes without them [27:43-28:43]. He lit it using what film and pre-flashing do to match the attenuation of underwater lights, then added particulate; intercut with the real footage you cannot tell which is which [28:43-29:15]. His principle: if done well it transcends how it was done and becomes about whether it affects you.

**Use it:** Match a hard reference by copying how its light falls off and what floats in the volume, and shoot clean passes to remove rigs.
