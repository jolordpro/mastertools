He revisited the reference film for plot structure, cutting pattern and composition as well as look, then built a look book of several hundred images over a few weeks. The director said which fit and which did not, which became the movie's vocabulary [§1]. The director's stance on the homage: inhabit, not ape [§1].

**Use it:** Send many images and have the director sort them; use the rejects as information.
