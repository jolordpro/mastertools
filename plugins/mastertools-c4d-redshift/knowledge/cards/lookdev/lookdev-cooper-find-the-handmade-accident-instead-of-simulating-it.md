He studies cells, teeth, bones, skin and macro details, and when he sees a complex CG fluid sim he asks what happens if you just drop ink in water [02:50-03:22].
Instead of fractal animation he asks to shoot through a piece of broken glass; he likes the imperfection of handmade things [03:22-03:52].
For Darkness Falls he took a blowtorch to the back of a daguerreotype; the glass cracked right through the girl's eyes, an accident that felt like part of a larger plan [03:52-04:22].
He is not anti-digital; he says he likes working with what is already there [04:22-04:55].

**Use it:** Try a practical capture or a real material before building a simulation; keep happy accidents.
