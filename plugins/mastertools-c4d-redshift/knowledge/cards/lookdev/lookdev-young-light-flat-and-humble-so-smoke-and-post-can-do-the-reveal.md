The team chose not to make the aliens presentational: the reveal stays a mystery, told through how much of it the lead could see in the gas [10:39-11:10]. It was mostly CG and took balance to decide how much to take away and add; he later valued its relative lightness against the dark rest of the film [11:10-11:43].
