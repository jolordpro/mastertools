Season 1 used 6K Dragon sensors and Season 2 an 8K Helium sensor, with the same Summilux primes [21:23-21:54]. Coverage was limited to 25, 29, 40 and 65 mm; he says restricting focal lengths informs the aesthetic [21:54-22:24]. The crew became fluent in those focal lengths.

**Use it:** Set a small focal-length list per show or package in C4D and keep every camera to it; odd lengths then flag a mismatched shot.
