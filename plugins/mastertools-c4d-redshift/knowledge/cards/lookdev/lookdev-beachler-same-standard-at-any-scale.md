Black Panther gave her eight months of prep and about 300 people at any moment; Moonlight had three weeks and five people including her, as dresser, painter and carpenter [§4]. She says the list of steps stays the same and the one constant is her: same professionalism and intensity regardless of budget [§4]. Moonlight's budget was $1.5 million [§5].

**Use it:** Keep the same review checks for a small piece as for a big one; scale the time and resources, not the standard.
