Her first research for Wakanda was people, not technology and stone: history, geography, topography, provinces, population (13 million, set between Congo and Rwanda/Burundi), then tradition over technology so tech serves life [11:01-12:03]. Buildings fit landscape and ecology in a symbiotic balance, using biomimetics [12:03]. Example: the informal African minibus becomes a pavement-free vehicle that sinks rain in instead of flooding; the train runs on vibranium vibration [15:47-16:50]. The market is open, light and communal, deliberately not a dark future market [16:50-17:51].

**Use it:** Start a fictional-world or stadium-world look with who uses it and what they already do, then upgrade those things; do not invent technology first.
