Townsend: the effect has to be elegant, not look like "someone vomited on screen" [08:31]. Readability came mostly from more dramatic lighting to motivate shaded and lit areas that give form to individual strands [08:31].
Framestore built a system that took pixel values from the environment (album covers in a record store) and extruded them as the base colour, then lit and rendered for physicality [09:02].
Palette elsewhere was muted [09:02].
For lighting: keep overhead practical lights as long as possible, then let the ambient go dark; his answer to "where is the light from" is the same place as the music [10:03].

**Use it:** Plan colour sources from the plate for abstract elements and add one motivated key to give a form read; avoid pure background-colour matching that loses edges.
