They debated how stylised Moonlight should be, using David LaChapelle's twisted-normal photography as one influence [46:18-47:19]. The goal was to mix realism with a stylistic vision, with the colour combining Miami architecture and harsh neon, applied through bold walls such as pink in a character's house with no explanation needed [49:52-50:26]. The palette was a joint effort with the DP [50:26].

**Use it:** Stylise by giving key spaces one bold colour decision inside an otherwise grounded world; don't justify it in the scene.
