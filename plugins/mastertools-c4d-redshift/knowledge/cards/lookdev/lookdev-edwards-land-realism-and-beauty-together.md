Director Gareth Edwards says every shot, however different, is the same two jobs at once: look real, and look beautiful, like the luckiest cameraman got the moment [06:49]. A phone photo is real but not beautiful; the more beautiful a shot, the less real it feels [07:19]. Real footage guarantees real, and the team then makes it more beautiful; a matte painting gives composition and light, and the work is making it look real [07:51]. Everyone has a 'spidey sense' for fake, often in green screen and car shots, and the hard part is working out what it is [08:22]. VFX supervisor David Vickery adds his note style: an establishing shot may look great, but the light should be as if the photographer had waited three weeks for it [08:54]. Edwards shared a bible of reference images with lighting and rock studies [09:25].

**Use it:** A final-pass check for stills and boards: ask 'is it real, is it beautiful' separately and fix the weaker. Keep a reference set of light studies for notes.
