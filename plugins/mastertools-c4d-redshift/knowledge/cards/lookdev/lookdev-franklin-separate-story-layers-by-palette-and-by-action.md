Franklin explains that the dream levels had different palettes: city always raining, snow fortress white, hotel warm, limbo grey and concrete; but when two levels showed similar collapses the audience lost track, so action also has to differ [48:35-49:07].

**Use it:** When two scenes in a package look alike in colour, differentiate by motion type too, and check at thumbnail size.
