Dylan Cole and Ben Procter (production designers) say Cameron's rule is that things must not just look cool. Architecture has to hold up to the monsoon season on Pandora even though no scene shows one [01:42-02:14]. Reality is used both as creative inspiration and as a technical limit: "fantasy within the limits". The same discipline applies to creatures, vehicles and architecture, which is why the film feels grounded as a live-action film with VFX rather than an animated one [06:24].

**Use it:** a plausibility check for CG environments: ask what weather, load and use the object would face and whether the design shows it.
