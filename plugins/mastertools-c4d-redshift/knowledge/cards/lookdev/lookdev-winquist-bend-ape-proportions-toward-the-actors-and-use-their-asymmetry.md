A gorilla on a horse looks absurd with stumpy legs and mass up top, and there was a lot of horse riding, so limb lengths were subtly adjusted toward the human actors [07:52-08:22].
The change also signals where the story sits between the Caesar films and the 1968 film, with actors in costume at one extreme [07:19-08:54].
Designers kept bringing in the actor: the asymmetry in Owen Teague's face went into Noah's eyes and the character began to read as the performer [08:54-09:25].

**Use it:** When a CG character has to match a performer, push proportions and one signature asymmetry toward the performer.
