Modern lenses are almost clinical and hard to flare predictably, so after painted flares on Mindhunter he wanted the defined halo seen in 1940s simple-lens work [33:06-34:09]. He sent sample frames to the director and post team, VFX sent back around 15 variants, and they art directed the ring size and diffraction [34:41-35:11]. Nothing happened on set; the intent came first.

**Use it:** Flare and halation: define the reference frame and tune ring size and diffraction in comp, rather than hoping a render or lens will do it.
