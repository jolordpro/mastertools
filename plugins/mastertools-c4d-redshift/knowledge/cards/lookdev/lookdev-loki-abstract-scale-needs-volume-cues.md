The Loom was first conceived at galactic size in nothingness [28:10]; Townsend says that read as a matte painting [28:10].
They shrank it to roughly Central Park size for a human-scale feel and filled the void with wisps of energy that describe volume the way snow or dust does [28:41].
Rainbow light on faces that the DP wanted was given a story reason: prismatic flares from over-intense energy, which also drove the character's suit disintegration [29:12, 29:44].
Concept work to settle the logic took several months before visualisation [27:40].
Transfers: depth cues (particles, haze) make abstract graphics read as deep space.

**Use it:** For abstract hero graphics, add atmospheric particles and a human-scale reference, and give any unusual coloured light a motivated source.
