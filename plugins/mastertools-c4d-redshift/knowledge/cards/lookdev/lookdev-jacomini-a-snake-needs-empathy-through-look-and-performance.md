He says characters live with the team for many months or years. The reptile world was the big challenge for animation performance, visual rendering and how the character photographs. A snake is unlike anthropomorphic leads, so making it integrate, bring empathy and be identifiable was a main task. [§1]

**Use it:** For an unfamiliar character type, test empathy early in look and motion, not just model fidelity.
