Cole [02:45-03:49]: Pandora is a metaphor for Earth, so designs begin with things we recognise (the lagoon reef began as Bora Bora "on steroids"), keep touchstones (coral that relates to fish) and then expand them as far as possible. Later [14:40-15:41] he says Cameron wants the "too far" version of everything even when he likes the first, so the team knows where the edge is; too alien means no one can relate, and much of the work is reining in. They think "National Geographic", not fantasy-film design, and use animals as gesture references for vehicles because their shapes already suit water and read as emotional.

**Use it:** supports the "go too far then scale back" card from a second discipline; on a frame, test a "too far" version to find the limit before settling.
