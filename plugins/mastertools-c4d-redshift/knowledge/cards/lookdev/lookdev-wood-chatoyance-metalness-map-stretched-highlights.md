Wood's fibre structure can stretch and shimmer highlights depending on how the fibres pack, an almost metallic effect [00:00]. Scans of unfinished wood capture this in a metalness map, e.g. the red cedar used here [00:31]. Claim comes from a material vendor's presenter, not tested.

**Use it:** when wood looks dead, check whether the material has a metalness map before adding more lights.
