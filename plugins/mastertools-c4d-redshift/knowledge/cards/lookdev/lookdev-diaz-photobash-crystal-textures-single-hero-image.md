For HP's 'power to reinvent beauty' brief she made one image: a block going from rocky to organic, with the idea that process is beautiful [16:46-17:48].
She bought crystals and shot them on white paper in direct sunlight as texture, tried full photo-bashing, then sculpted in C4D, rendered different materials and mixed them in Photoshop [18:18-19:21]. She kept adding elements (branches, flowers) until she judged enough [19:21-19:51].

**Use it:** a recipe for texture sources: photograph physical samples under one clean light and use them as bashing layers.
