The team scouted both cities with photos to catch their aesthetic rules: from San Francisco the bridge, fog, hills, downtown and pavements; from Tokyo the architecture, street life, colour and vitality. Because both are dense, interiors and exteriors were made complex and richly detailed, while characters stayed more stylised than in earlier Disney films. [§2]

**Use it:** When the set carries heavy detail, keep the characters simpler so they read.
