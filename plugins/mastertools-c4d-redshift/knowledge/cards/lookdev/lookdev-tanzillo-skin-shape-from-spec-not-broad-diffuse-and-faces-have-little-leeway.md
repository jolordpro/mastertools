Mike Tanzillo [13:09-15:45]: the light on skin had become too broad and softened the face. Darken the main diffuse and carry the form with spec. Skin is hard for two reasons: a subtle balance of bump and spec, and the fact that everyone knows what a good face looks like, so there is much less leeway than on a ruby.
A magenta tint was too saturated and too perfectly pure; darks read dark rather than blue.

**Use it:** Skin notes: lower diffuse first, then spec shaping, then tint saturation.
