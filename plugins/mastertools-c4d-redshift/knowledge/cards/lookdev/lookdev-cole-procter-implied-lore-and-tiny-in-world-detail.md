They say it matters less that viewers know what the Three Brothers rocks mean than that the rocks feel meaningful [06:54-07:58]. Cameron wrote about 1500 pages of notes for the sequels after exploring the ocean, which is where such lore came from. Procter, who designs the antagonist faction, says his artists thought about how the RDA sees itself and put small bits of propaganda in corners of shots, sometimes illegible, so there is more to poke at around every corner [09:00-09:31].

**Use it:** add layers of small, mostly unreadable detail in a world build; it reads as depth without being a story beat.
