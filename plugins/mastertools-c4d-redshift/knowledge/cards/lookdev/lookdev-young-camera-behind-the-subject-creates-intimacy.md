He says the camera should be an active presence, not a passive observer: operate it close, walk behind the subject, and use proximity (how near the lens sits) to close the distance to figures who seem untouchable [15:00-16:02]. The decision came from the director conversation about what the film should do for its audience, then was turned into a technical position [16:02-16:33].

**Use it:** Camera brief: state where the viewer is standing (observer, follower, participant) and let that decide the move and lens.
