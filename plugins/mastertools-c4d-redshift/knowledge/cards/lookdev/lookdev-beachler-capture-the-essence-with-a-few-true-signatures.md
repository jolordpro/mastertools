She studied photos of the subject's homes for what was always present in each period [16:22]: one built-in half-circle wood couch he was often photographed with, and a late-50s house with no corners, only curves [16:53-17:25]. Test of success: a family member walked the converted-church set and said it captured his essence [17:55-18:26]. She says it is a fine line between story needs and true feeling.

**Use it:** When recreating a real look (venue, athlete, team), identify two or three constant, recognisable signatures and keep them even if everything else is stylised.
