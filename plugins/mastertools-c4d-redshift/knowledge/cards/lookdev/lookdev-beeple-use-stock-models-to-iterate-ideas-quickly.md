Most of the models in the demo came from TurboSquid; Beeple says the assets may be dull but TurboSquid is a super quick tool to iterate on ideas [07:38-08:12].
A crystal pack cost about four dollars [16:46-17:18], and a phone app scan gave a usable head model for free in seconds [13:08-13:40].
He admits he dislikes modelling things, such as balloons [10:51-11:21].

**Use it:** For ideation or a style frame, buy or scan a stand-in asset rather than modelling it, and only model what survives.
