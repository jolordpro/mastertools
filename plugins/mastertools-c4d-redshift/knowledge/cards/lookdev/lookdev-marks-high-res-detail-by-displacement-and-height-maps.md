Marks sourced very high-resolution Mars height maps to build the rocky surfaces in many shots [§1]. For asteroid and crater detail he had light and shadow moving over very complex sub-polygon displaced surfaces [§2]. Steam, gas and dust clouds were made with Cinema 4D and the TurbulenceFD plugin [§2].

**Use it:** Use measured data for large form and displacement for the last layer of unevenness.
