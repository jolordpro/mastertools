He trained two LoRAs himself (Krea 2): a thermal-imaging look and a studio-photography look. Used together, one 'not full strength' at about 25 percent thermal, they give an otherworldly glow and nearly no shadows on the face [47:07-48:11].
He says Krea 2 handles style better than any model, and is great for evolving new styles by mixing [50:47].

**Use it:** Mix two simple trained looks at fractional strengths to find a third look, not one over-trained look.
