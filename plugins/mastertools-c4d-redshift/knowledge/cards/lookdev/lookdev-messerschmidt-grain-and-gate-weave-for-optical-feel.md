The custom monochrome cameras were rated at ASA 3200, and pushing them adds a little noise that looks like grain; it was supplemented in post or reduced when a scene did not call for it [Part I §2]. On dissolves they added more grain and gate weave than the rest of the film, as optical printing once did, as a wink to informed viewers [Part II §2]. [§2 Part I; Part II §2]

**Use it:** Texture is a per-shot choice, not a global filter.
