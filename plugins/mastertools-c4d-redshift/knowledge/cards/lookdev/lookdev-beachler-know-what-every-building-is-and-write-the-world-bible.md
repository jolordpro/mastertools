Ten months of design (a little over a year on the production) went into the country, not just the city [10:30-11:01]. She needed to know what every building was and did and where everything went. The records hall exists because she wanted a place where the culture's history could never be lost [12:35-14:09]. She wrote a 500-page book with history, reference pictures, the stages of vibranium from raw to fabric, and which tribes migrated from where; the cast got it [14:09-15:12].

**Use it:** A bible of purposes and sources for every element helps keep a long project consistent and lets any artist answer 'why is this here'.
