Cole [03:49-05:53]: to test a design for the Sully's marui house, they had master weavers build it with real bent cane; it was not shot as a miniature, but was heavily scanned and photographed and became the basis of the digital models (a 16-foot model is mentioned). Underwater performance-capture sets were kept as small as possible because the cameras must see the markers, using speed rail, foam or metal sculpts that give actors hard contact points. Procter adds many finished sets were built for live action, plus flooded cockpits and wave machines [05:22-06:24].

**Use it:** build a physical or rough 3D proxy to test scale and function before final modelling; scan it for the final.
