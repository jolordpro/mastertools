Early on Marks looked at more expressive type, but the imagery was already detailed and cinematic, so ornate lettering felt like too much [§6]. He says he needed to go there first to feel confident in the simplicity he ended with, Din Mittel [§6].

**Use it:** When a user picks plain type, check they have tried a louder option; that comparison is what makes the simple choice defensible.
