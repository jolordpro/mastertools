She designed brand assets while designing the logo because the avatar was the main use, and some versions were too detailed to shrink [20:49-22:22]. Her legibility rule: a mark must read at a fast glance, and mis-reads (one client's logo reads as a rude word) matter more for less-known brands; also stress test both very large and very small [12:30, 16:39-17:42]. Her goal is a logo easy enough that a non-expert designs well with it, without a 500-page brand book [42:00]. Her quit rule: nothing reaches 100%; she stops near 99.8 [1:19:07].
**Use it:** Add a smallest-size and fast-glance read to the end-card/bug review, and stop when it reads clean instead of polishing past that.
