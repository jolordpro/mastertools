He started lookbooks of photos, paintings or playlists so that if he could not say an idea to a director he could flip to an image [00:04-00:36]. Later the books became redundant because his recurring ideas (darkness, softness of light and image) carry over [01:08-01:39]. He now chooses a few things to focus on each film, not necessarily visual; recently lenses: on a large-sensor camera he tests glass he could not before, and he helped shape lenses made for the format [01:39-03:12]. His prep time goes to looking through lenses, not pulling images off the internet [03:12].

**Use it:** Pick one or two things to push each project and test them in a lookdev round; keep a reference book as a fallback for words.
