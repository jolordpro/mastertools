The process starts with a casting session of what real animals look like in all their phases: which is most appealing, and why. The chosen one is then adjusted a little and built from scratch [03:08].
Artists dissect the structure (muscles and so on) so that when an animator moves something, the appropriate muscle and hair move [03:39]. He puts the build for one character at roughly six months [03:39].
Once shot, a character's quirks are expensive to change, so he augments rather than rebuilds, with every animal getting the same attention and everything running concurrently [04:10].

**Use it:** Lock character design and reference choice before animation starts; after that only small augments are affordable. Run the casting step with real reference side by side.
