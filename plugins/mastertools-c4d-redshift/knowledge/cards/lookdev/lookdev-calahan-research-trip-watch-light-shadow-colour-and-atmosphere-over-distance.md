On Ratatouille she and production designer Harley Jessup walked Paris for about five days: she studied weather, light quality, bounce between buildings, shadow colour and atmosphere over distance (how soft the clouds feel), while he photographed small details like sewer grates [03:07-04:13].
Also coffee-table books, paintings, illustrations and films shot there, plus talks with the director about overall tone and what inspires them (edit, score) [04:13-04:44].
Goal: strong sense of place so locals feel at home [03:07].

**Use it:** Look-dev research list: the five things to photograph on site are light quality, bounce, shadow colour, haze with distance and weather.
