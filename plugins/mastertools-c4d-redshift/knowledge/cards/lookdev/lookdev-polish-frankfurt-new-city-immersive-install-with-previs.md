For the New City show at MoMA (2008) the team rejected a flat screen on a wall as boring and built a cave that surrounded the viewer [§5]. It used irregular trapezoid scrims lit by short-throw projectors arranged from a CG previsualisation [§5]. The content came from unbuilt architecture and the idea of the whole world's population on one looping manifold [§5].

**Use it:** For a physical display, previs the projector layout in CG before building the screens.
