For South Park's 3D title Fong's team wanted to honour the original cut-paper tactileness, like paper sculptures, but without overdoing it, with the town's trees as an example of a distinctive look to keep [§5-6]. The show's animation team did all the character animation and cel work; her team storyboarded, built an animatic, then comped their layers into the 3D environment [§6]. She says she would not take on the characters' animation: it is the actor, the lifeblood of the character [§6].

**Use it:** When adding depth to a stylised look, protect the part the audience already loves (the performance, the paper edge) and build the 3D around it.
