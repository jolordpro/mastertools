To shoot deep focus he looked for lenses that stay sharp at f8 or f11, even f16. He tested larger-format lenses, Master Primes and Signature Primes, but kept the Summilux spherical lenses used on the earlier series, which held high resolution at deep stops with minimal diffraction [§3]. Frame was 2.20:1 [§3].

**Use it:** Test lenses at the stop you will use, not at their sweet spot.
