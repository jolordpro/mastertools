Shots of the lead were based on photography, yet the stylisation could stretch her a long way while her look and presence still came through. The eye shot was built from CG assets. Footage came from aerials, CG and photos projected onto CG, all pulled into one look by the filter. [§5]

**Use it:** With a strong face, test the distortion limit; a recognisable silhouette and eyes can carry heavy treatment.
