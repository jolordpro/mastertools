He says other movies done entirely in the virtual world are usually stylised: the skies are too rich and the colours too rich, it looks like a commercial with a fantasy element, and it does not look like a conventionally shot film [01:07-01:38].
Favreau and he shared the view that things done in camera, or that look done in camera, are the most realistic, and the goal was a photoreal film with naturalism carried through the camera, the animals and the light [03:12].

**Use it:** Pull sky saturation and colour richness back toward what a lens would record; richness is the first tell of CG.
