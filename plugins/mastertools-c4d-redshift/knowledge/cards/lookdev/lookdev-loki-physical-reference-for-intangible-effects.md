Directors wanted time effects to feel as if they could be touched [05:56]. Reference for the spaghettification: strings and strands, play-dough extruded through a die [06:28].
For time slipping: Francis Bacon portraits, cubism, long-exposure photography, plus a taffy-like pull [13:43, 14:14].
Process: concept art and animatics from the vendor, then early shoot tests with the lead actor, then back to the vendor; by the real shoot the effect was about 50% defined and tested [13:43, 14:45].
Set scanning (clear angle scans of full worlds and individual pieces) and witness cameras gave data for the digital replacements [07:30].

**Use it:** For abstract effects, gather non-obvious physical reference early and shoot a small test with the performer before committing to the full build.
