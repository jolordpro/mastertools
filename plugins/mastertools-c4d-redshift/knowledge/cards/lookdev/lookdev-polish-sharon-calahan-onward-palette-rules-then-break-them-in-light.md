Sharon Calahan (Pixar director of photography) says a palette is planned per film. On Onward they reserved purple for the dad and anything about him (it appeared nowhere else in sets or characters) and held the surface colours to about four hues in different shades and saturations [13:40-14:11]. She echoed it somewhat in lighting, but by the light-colour stage "you set your rules aside" and choose what makes the strongest feeling for that moment [14:11]. Home was kept normal and friendly so colours off the home palette (green cube, red drink machine) hit harder on the quest [15:45-16:17].
**Use it:** In a package palette, reserve one colour for one meaning, keep surface hues few, and let lighting colour be the exception chosen per beat.
