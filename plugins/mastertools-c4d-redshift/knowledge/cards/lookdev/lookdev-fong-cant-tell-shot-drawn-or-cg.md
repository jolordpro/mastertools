Fong says one of the biggest compliments is when someone asks "is that part digital or did you guys do that in CG?" She likes hybrid techniques where analog and digital meet [§3]. For Counterpart she went on set, which had Cold War period details such as German typewriters with umlaut keys and a primitive fax machine, and merged surreal CG with things shot from the set [§3]. She says technology does not mean making everything in CG from the start; her first jobs were collage on a computer with transparencies, illustration and photography [§3]. (Boardwalk compositing, God of War prosthetics and the wind story come from other sources.)

**Use it:** When a CG frame feels sterile, ask what real, shot or hand-made element could carry the texture. Check that no layer announces its own method.
