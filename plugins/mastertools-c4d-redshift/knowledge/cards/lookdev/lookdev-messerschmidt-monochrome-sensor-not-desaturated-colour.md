After testing colour and black and white cameras they chose black and white, and RED built five 8K Super 35 monochrome bodies. He says it has tonal depth you do not get by desaturating colour. Concessions follow: no blue screen to build tonal separation in the DI. Super 35 was chosen for depth of field since large format would work against it [§2].

**Use it:** Test a real monochrome source before faking black and white by desaturating.
