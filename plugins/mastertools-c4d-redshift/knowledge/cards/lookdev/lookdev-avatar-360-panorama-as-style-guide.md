Steve Messing and Dylan Cole made a giant 360-degree panorama of the whole floating-mountain region. It served as the virtual set during motion-capture filming and as a style guide for the visual effects [§2]. Messing also photographed mountains in China for reference [§1].

**Use it:** For a multi-shot environment, build one master view that every shot is matched to.
