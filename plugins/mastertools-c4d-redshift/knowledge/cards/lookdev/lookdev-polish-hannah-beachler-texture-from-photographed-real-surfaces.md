She photographs real surfaces on location, such as the texture wanted for the Hall of Kings, and uses layered stone at Oribi Gorge and dark streaks from the Golden Gate Highlands for Warrior Falls. On the Sentinel mountains the snow was drawn packed, not loose, so it does not drift across the peak. Colour came from African textiles, Senegalese architecture and Kirby's comics.
