Notes during the build: you need a figure for size reference [17:18-17:49], a foreground element to tie the scene together [13:40], and 'one of these should be bigger'; the repeated heads needed a bigger difference in compositional scale [25:19-26:25].
Repeats were scattered with render instances and random scale [20:58-22:02]. He says he instances everything, the head model is about 300,000 (polygons, presumably) so scattering many copies without instances is heavy [12:25-13:08, 18:20-18:52].
Material fix: heads were too shiny, so he roughened them with a sand or stone texture; the colour read chocolatey until corrected [33:28-35:38].

**Use it:** Break up repeated objects with random scale and one very different size, and include a recognisable-scale figure in the frame.
Transcript support: Say's default human figure toggled on and off for scale (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Zg4lpwZGe0 [09:25])
