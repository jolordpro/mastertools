Blender Guru (2014) [04:13-07:19]. Bright saturated areas deliberately lead the eye: red mountains the camels walk toward, a red-pants figure pulling the eye to the background, a saturated parrot in the foreground that anchors the viewer so the rest of the scene can be explored. Mood: vibrant oranges, pinks and fluorescent grass for a happy moment, then greys and browns for sadness (a cited animated film, The Incredibles and The Matrix start, and desaturated game palettes). Bright saturation also signals 'unreal' and suits cartoons.

**Use it:** In composition notes, name the one element allowed to be the most saturated, and keep the rest quieter. Fits with the 'one gold thread' habit.
Transcript support: Tanzillo pulls about 10% saturation from a background so everything settles and the subject leads (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SORLKsHaHSM [07:55])
