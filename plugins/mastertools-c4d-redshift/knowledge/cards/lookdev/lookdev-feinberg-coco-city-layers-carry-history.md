The team spent a year and a half on the Land of the Dead and kept returning to research photos of Guanajuato. Its vertical towers have levels that symbolise events and periods such as Spanish colonialism and the Mexican Revolution, so the spectacle carries meaning. The director's brief was a world like no one has seen [§1-§2]. The team worried about depicting a culture that was not theirs and checked with feedback from Mexico [§2].

**Use it:** Give a set piece an internal logic, such as layers by era, that a viewer can read.
