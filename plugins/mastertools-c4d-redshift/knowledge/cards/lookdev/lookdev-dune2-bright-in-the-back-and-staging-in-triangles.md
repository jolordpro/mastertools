Justin Phillip's read of a wide cave frame [05:43-09:19]: foreground dark, a small pool of light mid-frame, and the hottest part of the image in the background, behind two silhouetted people. Highlights there run four to six stops over [07:15]. Staged figures form triangles with other elements, which he links to theatre directing and to painting (brightest area in the background pulls the eye in) [08:17-08:48, 21:18].
Pools of light also form triangles; he warns against treating this as a formula and says to study paintings and old photography [17:39, 21:50-23:22].
Walls can be backlit with transparency, and spill is skirted so the dark top stays clean [06:44-07:15].
Outside analyst, low confidence.

**Use it:** The composition check asks where the brightest area is (ideally deep in the frame) and whether dark foreground layers lead into it.
