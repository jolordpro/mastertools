Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour): the special-effects supervisor built a large turntable so the real car could be craned onto it, lit and shot like a digital turntable, then compared side by side with the CG car [22:46-23:18].
He did a similar rotation of an F-18 on a previous film [23:18].
Worst nightmare: it works in one race but fails in another lighting situation, so he does this homework first [23:48-24:20].

**Use it:** Look-dev gate: side-by-side with a photographed reference under at least two lighting setups.
