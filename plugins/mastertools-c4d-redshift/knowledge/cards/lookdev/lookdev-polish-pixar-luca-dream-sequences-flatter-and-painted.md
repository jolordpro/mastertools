Dream sequences were pushed further than the film's look, "dialled to 11" [20:53]: lit flatter so they read like illustration [23:27]; skies built by layering hand-painted brush strokes with parallax so strokes feel palpable [21:24]; and a glow (the moon fish) done as a pastel painting with parallax and glints added, not a plain bloom or light [21:55]. Small teams tried, pushed, showed the director, who said too far or keep going [22:56].
**Use it:** Give a surreal or flashback beat its own look rule (flatter lighting, texture carried into glow) so it separates from the main piece.
