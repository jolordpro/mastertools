Will Gibbons (KeyShot trainer) steps IOR on a glass slab over a striped card: at 1 the slab disappears; at 1.1 stripes barely shift; 1.2 more shift and a bit shinier; 1.5 more; 2 more still, all because light slows in denser material [03:08][03:38][04:10]. Raising IOR also makes the outer surface more reflective and harder looking; at 10 it reads almost like a mirror [05:11]. Real values: look up the material; glass is 1.5 to 1.6 for realism, water 1.333, diamond about 2.5 [05:43][09:23]. Without refraction enabled the glass looks artificial and flat [01:04].

**Use it:** IOR table for the coach and material checks: flag glass at 1.0 or values outside 1.5-1.6. Check against the Redshift ledger at the gate.
