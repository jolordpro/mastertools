Mike Tanzillo [15:45-18:43]: a pink lens haze lowers contrast over the eyes every time, so take the glasses off until the eye read is solid. On Rio, where both leads wore glasses, lenses were often rendered separately from the eyes and put back over, after dev on how much IOR was too much.
Design fixes: isolate tint to the edges and clear the centre. A later version [46:19] with the heads-up display around the outside let the haze come down and the eyes read through.

**Use it:** On eyewear or visors, render lens separately and keep the centre clear of tint.
