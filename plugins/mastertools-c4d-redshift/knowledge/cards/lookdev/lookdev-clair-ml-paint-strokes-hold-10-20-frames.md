Older Photoshop filters made a different stroke every frame, which read like stop motion. Antibody's newer tool keeps a stroke in place for roughly 10 to 20 frames until the form changes.
Marks's pipeline: paint a single style frame (Akvis Artwork), add pixel sorting and distortion layers, push it through the shot with EbSynth, finish in After Effects. A filter over aerials, full CG and projected photos unified them into one look.
AI upscaling reached 5K with convincing brush texture on painterly sources but did not help realistic ones.

Page spot: §4-§5.

**Use it:** Judge any stylisation filter on motion, not on a still: does the texture stay with the form? Use one hero style frame as the target.
