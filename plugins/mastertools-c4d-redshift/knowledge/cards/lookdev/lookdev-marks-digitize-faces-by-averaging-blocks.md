The cast photos were manually de-rezzed until barely visible. The simple method: make a rectangular selection over a facial region, apply Average to flatten the colour, and repeat bit by bit across the whole image, which takes patience. The artwork was then taken into 3D with basic face geometry, giving depth that sat between a flat graphic and a fully immersive scene. [§4-§5]

**Use it:** Try a hand-made digital approximation before reaching for a preset glitch filter.
