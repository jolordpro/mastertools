Cole [11:02-11:33] describes the early days of the sequels as so much to design that you never got stuck on one thing: do a pass on everything, see how it looks, get reviews, then another pass across all. That kept it fresh. Everything still has to serve a story, metaphor or theme, not just look cool, and designers knew the whole saga was planned so they could keep a consistent direction [09:31-10:32].

**Use it:** for a package of many elements, schedule whole-package passes with a review between, not finish-one-then-next.
