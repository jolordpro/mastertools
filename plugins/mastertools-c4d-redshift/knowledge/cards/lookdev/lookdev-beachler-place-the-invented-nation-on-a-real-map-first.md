She says the long research process started with where on the continent it sits, since that defines the migrations into a fictional nation; they placed it on the border of the DRC with Lake Victoria, Kenya, Rwanda, Burundi, Ethiopia, Uganda and Sudan around it, so East Africa [04:42-05:13]. From there they drew on specific peoples and practices (Congolese fishing, river tribes of the Omo Valley, Suri, Maasai, Mursi, Dogon for the Jabari) [05:13-06:16].

**Use it:** Invented worlds (a team identity, a fictional league) need a stated "where and who" before the style: it gives rules for materials, colour and pattern.
