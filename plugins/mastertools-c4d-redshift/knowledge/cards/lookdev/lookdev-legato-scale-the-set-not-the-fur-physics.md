For the whimsical end-credits sequence the director wanted it to feel like a stage set, so real-world physics did not matter. Weta asked whether Louie was a plush toy or a shrunken Louie, kept the fur physics identical, and made a gigantic version of the environment around him. The live-action credits book was shot as a tabletop on velvet, with an archivist in white gloves. [§11]

**Use it:** For a scale gag, change the world size around the character rather than retuning the character's sim.
