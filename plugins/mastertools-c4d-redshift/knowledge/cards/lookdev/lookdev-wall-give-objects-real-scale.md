Earlier seasons left scale vague. For season 8 each object got a stated scale, so the Red Keep stands about 20 feet tall in the model.
The rebuild reused nothing from earlier seasons. Data was roughly 20 to 25 times larger than season 1, according to art director Kirk Shintani.

**Use it:** Fix a scale for each hero object so depth of field, bevels and detail size follow from it.
Transcript support: Say keeps a six-foot figure in the scene to check bevel, texture and shadow scale (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0Zg4lpwZGe0 [09:25])
