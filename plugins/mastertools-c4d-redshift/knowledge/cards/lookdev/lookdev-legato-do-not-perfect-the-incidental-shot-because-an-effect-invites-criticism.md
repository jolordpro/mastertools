He wants effects to feel shot, not controlled: real cutaways are never moved two inches this way and that, whereas an effect is perfected and calls attention to itself, inviting disbelief [23:33-24:36]. His career summary: aim for the cinema of the moment, not the effect of the moment; do not cut to anything that makes viewers go wow, because then they have left the movie [26:10-26:42]. On second unit the work must look as if Scorsese directed and the DP shot it; the more nobody knows he was there, the better [26:42-27:43]. Feeling is his gauge: if he feels something it carries into the film, if not it has no staying power [00:04, 06:55-07:26].

**Use it:** On a hero CG shot, ask whether a camera operator would have framed or lit it so neatly; loosen what is too perfect.
