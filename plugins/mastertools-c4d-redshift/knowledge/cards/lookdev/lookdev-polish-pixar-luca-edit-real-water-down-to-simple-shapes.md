The panel (Daniela Strijleva, Kim White, David Ryu, Deanna Marsigliese; captions do not label who speaks) says they compared real water and Finding Dory water, found real water so detailed it now looks fake against the stylised film, and simplified caustics and shapes [08:25-09:58]. Effects and lighting isolated shapes and layered them; people calling it realistic means the edit worked [09:58]. Rule: once the character style is set ("stake in the ground"), every other element (water, props) must follow it [08:57]. Everything down to the last prop was designed with care [10:29].
Transfers: for stylised CG liquids or glass, keep behaviour and motion from reference but delete detail until it matches the character style.
**Use it:** In a look-dev review, check that the weakest-styled element (water, smoke, glass) matches the hero's level of simplification.
