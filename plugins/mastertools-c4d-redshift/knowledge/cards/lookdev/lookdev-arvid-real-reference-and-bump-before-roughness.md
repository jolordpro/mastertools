Host Arvid Schneider (working lookdev/lighting artist; opinion, medium-low) [1:11:57-1:15:34]: believable objects need real reference: keep a mood board, and macro photos of the surface (wood close-up). Don't reference other CG, since you can't know it is right [1:15:34-1:16:04]. Common mistake: raising roughness to look rough, when up close wood is highly specular; rely on strong bump or displacement, using roughness only to help the renderer [1:13:31-1:14:32]. Yann adds that diffuse is really very rough spec, and a lookdev supervisor once said good lookdev is 90% bump and roughness quality, with bad bump giving visible fake light and dark sides [1:14:32-1:15:34].

**Use it:** coach tips for materials; also check bump/roughness response under raking light.
