Computer animation puts distance between audience and image, so a believability that lets them relate matters more than realism [07:02-07:33]. Some directors want highly stylised, some tighter realism [07:33-08:03]. Toy Story 4 went closer to photorealism with live-action lens and filter behaviour; on Onward they took a lens package and exaggerated parts of it [11:50-12:39]. The look is chosen first and tools are made to reach it [10:37-11:08].

**Use it:** Choose where a frame sits between stylised and real before choosing the render settings.
