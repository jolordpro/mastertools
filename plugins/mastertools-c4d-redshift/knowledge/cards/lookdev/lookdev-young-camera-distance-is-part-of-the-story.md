He compares two photographers of the same subject: closeness and the maker's assumptions show in the picture, so read the distance when analysing images [49:15-50:16]. For his own work: shooting across the room on long lenses tells one story; close to the performer on wide or tight lenses tells another, and the physical, technical, spiritual and story parts must find harmony [50:16-50:47].

**Use it:** When reviewing a camera setup, ask what the distance says about the subject (observed from afar or engaged), and justify the lens by the story. Supports existing stay-close card.
