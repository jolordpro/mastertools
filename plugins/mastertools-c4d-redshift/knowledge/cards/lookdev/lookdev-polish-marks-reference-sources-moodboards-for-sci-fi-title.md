Marks built moodboards named Apollo Tech, Retro Future and Texture and Tone [§2]. Floating tower blocks came from Katsuhiro Otomo's drawing book Genga, and the astronaut fall from Thomas Kane's 1960s trampoline photos of body dynamics, grainy black and white published in LIFE in 1968 [§3-§4]. He treats the astronaut as a trope for the existential and layered in homages as a challenge, such as the 2001 bay-door shot [§3, §5].
