Say calls a plain extrude 2.5D [13:37-14:09]. His example of true 3D is the NBC Sports Group Sochi Olympics logo, where the edge is curved rather than flat to camera, a warm light shows on the contour and the type has volume; it was rendered in the standard renderer, curves did the work [14:09-15:10]. Circle-rounded sweeps pick up many reflections as lights roll round them, and rounded surfaces help it stop looking like 3D text [13:06-13:37]. The achievement is volume, weight and depth, but the logo must still read well flat [25:37-26:08]. On a test letter, an offset-path loft face gave much more weight and reflection than neighbouring flat letters [46:26-46:56]; his joke that six more extrude layers and twelve more textures would complete it.
Transfers: tell the client how the form catches light, not just how thick it is.

**Use it:** Review sports-logo stills for edge curvature and light pick-up, plus a flat-view readability pass.
