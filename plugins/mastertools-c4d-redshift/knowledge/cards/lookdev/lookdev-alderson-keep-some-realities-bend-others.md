Anchor in something viewers already understand, then twist perspective or physics. He describes it as correct, correct, incorrect: for example a shot that looks photoreal while its physics are wrong, which holds attention and stays easy to sell. A fully invented world is hard to sell to a client. Fantastical touches are there to show what cannot be shot. [§31-§32]

**Use it:** Decide per shot which of look, motion and physics stays true, and bend only one.
