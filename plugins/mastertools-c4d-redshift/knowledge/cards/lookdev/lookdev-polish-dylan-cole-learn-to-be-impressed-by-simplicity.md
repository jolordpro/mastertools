He aims for at least one painting a day on concept work and treats each as disposable if it fails. He says to learn to be impressed by simplicity, and notes there is no secret button. 3D should support design, not replace drawing and perspective understanding.

Page spots: at least a painting a day for concept art, and big finished pieces in a day or two [§5]; confidence, not questioning every decision, because it is one painting and another can follow [§5]; it took ten years to be impressed by simplicity, it is as much about what you leave out [§5]; no secret button, only concepts and practice [§2]; 3D should not be a crutch for weak drawing or perspective [§2].

**Use it:** Cut first when a frame is busy; speed of iteration beats polishing a weak idea.
