He says the CG temptation is to control everything, which gives a computer sensibility. The Jungle Book's secret was relinquishing control and acting as if it was done with a real camera, getting the randomness of light and the remnants of how a camera is operated instead of art directing every last moment [11:34-12:05].
Those leftovers, he says, remove the uncanny valley from the process [12:05].

**Use it:** Leave some uncontrolled variation in light and camera (shake, slight miss, drift) on photoreal CG shots; do not fix all of it.
