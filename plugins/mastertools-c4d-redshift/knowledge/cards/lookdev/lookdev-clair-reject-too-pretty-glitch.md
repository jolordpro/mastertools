On Halt and Catch Fire, when a designer showed a frame and asked whether it looked nice, Clair's answer was that it looked gorgeous and to try again: unless your eyes feel like they are bleeding, it is not glitchy enough yet. He used the same principle on The Peripheral. The machine-learning output looked like a crude impressionist painting, and the challenge was to disrupt it until it had a fizzing, super-digital intensity while keeping the painted stroke.

Page spot: §6.

**Use it:** A stylised effect that looks pleasant is usually under-cooked. Push one notch past comfortable, then tame it with a rule such as axis-locked movement.
