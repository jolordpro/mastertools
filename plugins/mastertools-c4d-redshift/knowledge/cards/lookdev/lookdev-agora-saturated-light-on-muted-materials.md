Materials were low-saturation concrete, brushed metal and polished marble with little set decoration, while lighting was saturated and dynamic: red for combat and danger, blue for the heroes' calm state [05:11-05:42]. The lens look used anamorphic streaks, flares and subtle bloom to add imperfection to a sterile frame, wide-angle lenses for depth and movement, and cuts timed to music beats with 2D graphic elements on top [05:42-06:43]. The animation art direction treated robots as cartoonier and clunky, heroes more grounded [04:39].
