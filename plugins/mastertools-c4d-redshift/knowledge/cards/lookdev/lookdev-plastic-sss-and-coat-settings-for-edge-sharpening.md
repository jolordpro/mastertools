MographPlus: base colour matters most for plastic (example RGB 252,190,10) [00:33-01:04]. Subsurface weight 0.5-0.6 depending on reference, radius about 1 cm, SSS colour copied from base [04:45-05:58]. A coat at weight 0.5, coat IOR 1.2 and roughness 0.05 sharpens only the perpendicular-facing areas, adding realism [09:41-10:16]. Wood parquet coat about 0.3 weight, roughness 0.1, IOR 1.2 [15:42]. Gold and copper: metalness 1 and the standard surface presets, then roughness map [18:21-21:04]. Red plastic is a duplicate with changed base and SSS colour and a ramp tweak [10:47-11:55].

**Use it:** Starting numbers to sanity-check a plastic/lacquer material, then judge against reference.
