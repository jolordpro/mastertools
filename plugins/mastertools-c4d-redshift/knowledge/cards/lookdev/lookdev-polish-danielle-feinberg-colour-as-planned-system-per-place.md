On Turning Red production designer Rona Liu and her group planned colour for each location and each character's palette around what served the story, and held to it. Feinberg, the VFX supervisor, calls herself a colour nerd and says the plan frees a thing from its real-life colour: 'Something doesn't have to be the color it is in real life.' The Ratatouille goal of making food look delicious was lighting director Sharon Calahan's; Feinberg lit only a little there. [§1,§4]

**Use it:** Define a palette per location before lighting; colour is a story choice that can override physical reality.
