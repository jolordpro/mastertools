Working on a whole invented civilisation, she took the job one step at a time because all at once felt overwhelming. While writing in Africa, Ryan Coogler sent her photos of people, such as feet with ankle bells and a man in a mask. She read each as a statement of how he feels and what the essence is, then designed from that [§3]. She frames the job as world building plus capturing an essence, and adding to the story, with the set treated as a character [§4].

**Use it:** For a hero look, collect director references as moods first, name the feeling each one carries, then build the set or scene from that note.
