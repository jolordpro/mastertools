Cole says a signature style is easier for a comic artist; his own is a kind of heightened reality [§4]. He thinks it is really about consistency so people can count on a certain look, which he calls professionalism, while adapting subject and style to the project so you are not a one-trick pony [§5].

**Use it:** Advise users to pursue one reliable quality of look rather than one trick, and to flex it per job.
