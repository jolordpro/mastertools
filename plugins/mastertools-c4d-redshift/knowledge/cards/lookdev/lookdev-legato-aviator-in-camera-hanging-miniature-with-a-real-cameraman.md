He shot the Hercules in camera: a radio-controlled plane about 25 feet wide, versus the enormous Spruce Goose, with a newsreel cameraman in a boat [29:15-29:47]. For a hanging miniature to work it must look out there, not here, so the cameraman was made to look as if he photographs something distant while the plane was six or seven inches from him [29:47-30:17]. Because it is in camera there are no matte lines or lighting mismatch.
