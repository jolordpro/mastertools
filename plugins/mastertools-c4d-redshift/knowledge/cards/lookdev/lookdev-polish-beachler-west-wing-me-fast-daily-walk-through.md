Each morning Beachler walked the hall with her team while they put work in front of her, and she answered quickly yes, no or next; she called it 'West Wing me' [§5]. She picked all finishes on the film, so these calls covered how everything looked [§4]. Her team: about seven illustrators, set designers drafting, a supervising art director and six art directors each running one set with a coordinator and foreman [§4].

**Use it:** A fast stand-up pass, with one decision per item, keeps a large department moving where long reviews would stall.
