To try a wildly colourful look, Chad Ashley used the Redshift render settings global material override: one material replaces every material, with roughness raised so it is not glossy, and a Jitter node driving colour [55:45-56:16]. Nick Campbell suggested Jitter may need a mode such as name/ID to vary per object [56:50].
It did not work at first. The hosts' fix was to drag the material into the override's custom material field, and to check the view was not locked [57:23]. Shuffle then changed the palette [58:25]. Hosts noted the trick usually means plain grey clay, not colour [59:26].
The demo was exploratory and the details are loose, so test before trusting.

**Use it:** offers a fast look-exploration pass before committing to final materials; the coach can name the override and custom material field.
