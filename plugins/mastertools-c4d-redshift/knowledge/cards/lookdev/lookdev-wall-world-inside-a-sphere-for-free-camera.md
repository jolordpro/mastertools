Early flat maps could not tilt up far, because that raised the question of what lay beyond the map. Wall's pragmatic answer was to put the whole world on the inside of a sphere (two bowls put together), which also gave the lighting a natural place at the centre. The decision took a while to reach. [§2-§3]

**Use it:** If the camera must move freely, solve what the viewer sees beyond the set first.
