Steps [24:05-26:43, 41:18-45:27]: start with MTF, since lenses lose contrast on fine detail; he says the 60 lp/mm band is normally untouched and most lenses start falling at about 90 to 120 lp/mm, with a gradual fall, not a step [42:51-43:23]. Add slight edge distortion and defocus so the centre reads sharper and draws the eye [43:54-44:55]. Vignette about 1.6 aspect-matched with a larger falloff, via an optical vignette that is cleaner than a window [25:40-26:12]. An amorphic or 35-50 mm choice, no tint, no halation here. He also raised 60 lp/mm positive and cut higher frequencies to regain skin detail without harshness [30:50-31:53].
**Use it:** Give CG a lens pass in this order: frequency softness, edge defocus, vignette; check the softness with a before/after toggle.
