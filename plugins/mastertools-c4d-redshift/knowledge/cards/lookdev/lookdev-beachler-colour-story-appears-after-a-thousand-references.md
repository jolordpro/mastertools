For Moonlight she gathered photographs of Miami from many places and decades, and once about a thousand were on the wall the palette emerged [48:21]. Neon pinkish purple and electric blue came from nightlife; teal and a hard yellow from Cuban and Caribbean architecture [48:52]. She pulled those colours into wardrobe, with variations across the three time periods [49:22].

**Use it:** Colour-research step: build a big board of reference, then read the dominant hues and ratios from it instead of picking a palette first.
