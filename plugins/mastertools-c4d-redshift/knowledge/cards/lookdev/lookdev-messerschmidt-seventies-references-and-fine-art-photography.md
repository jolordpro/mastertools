Fincher asked him to watch All the President's Men; Willis is a lasting influence for patient filmmaking and nuance. Also Mississippi Burning and Three Days of the Condor, and fine-art photography: William Eggleston and Stephen Shore [11:34-13:40]. Costume, art direction and picture cars carry a large part of the period feel [12:07]. Locations were chosen months ahead; one property owner let the grass grow for about ten weeks, and a scout 4-5 months before the shoot sized the lighting job [18:41-19:14; 27:37-28:08].
