The London and New York crews pass work across the time difference: when one team finishes its day the other picks up, keeping momentum going [§1]. The studio throws newcomers in at the deep end and says the best ideas come from the most determined, not the loudest; hierarchy is flexible and any good idea is heard [§1]. To avoid stylistic pastiche it keeps collaborating with new creatives for fresh perspectives [§2].
