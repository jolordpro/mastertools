Put the context at the top (client, goal, problem, role, tools) so the viewer can tick boxes while scrolling the assets; context at the bottom feels too late [17:00-17:28, 24:20].
Break the story up: 2-3 sentence overview, work, values/mission, more work, then issues met, instead of one big intro [48:39-49:13]. Reviewing a project with no text he says single images with no brief, problem or result are not impactful [55:31].
Add results and numbers where you can (click-through, engagement, stats) [33:15, 51:50]. Show the process: sketches, early concepts, evolution to final [10:05].
Say what you did and cut work you did not make, or the viewer cannot tell what you made [25:53].
Add detail shots of big layouts and posters next to the zoomed-out view [12:08, 31:42].

**Use it:** Case-study template for a reel breakdown: brief, role, process frames, final, result; one idea per scroll.
