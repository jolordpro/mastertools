Boards: one board for the style frames with notes, another for colour variants [06:09][08:42]. Notes and descriptions let the client share it with colleagues when the designer is not there [06:40]. For the depth of field option he listed a pro (looks sophisticated) and a con (adds about two hours of render time), noting a daily-upload schedule might force half-resolution renders [07:41][08:11]. Over-label: feedback like "speed" could mean frame rate, edit, motion or easing; naming options (2c) lets clients say which, and asking them to comment makes it visible to all [09:14][09:44]. Export boards to PDF at highest quality [09:44].

**Use it:** use the pro/con line and option names in every review deck; makes costs visible at approval.
