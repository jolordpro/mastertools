The first Saber build was already a good lightsaber plugin, but the team spent three extra weeks adding energy turbulence, wind distortion, motion blur, presets, and support for masks and text [27:45-28:48]. He calls this their trademark: ambitious goals, then one more week to get the finishing touches. They have no calendar-driven product cycle; products ship when R&D has matured into something next-level, so releases read as considered [20:00-21:03]. Contrast with his lock-the-edit lesson: more time is justified when it changes what the thing can do, not when it re-tweaks the same idea.

**Use it:** Extend a deadline only for work that adds capability (robustness, masks, edge cases), and cut tweaks that only repeat the same idea.
