In a studio essay after 13 years, the team says standards, curiosity and storytelling matter more than medium. Each project is treated as an audition for the next.
As content gets easier to generate, work should be worth stopping for. Briefs should be a two-way conversation. The line: "the difference is in strong ideas, and stronger execution."

**Use it:** Treat each delivery as the portfolio piece. Judge by whether the work would make someone stop scrolling.
