Early in concepting the animator only answers one question: can this style be executed? [26:54]. Once boards or first style frames exist he reviews them before the client sees them [30:30-31:01].
Red flags: a waterfall that implies 200 ft of depth between two scenes, too many characters, four characters that need ten unique rigs. A fix can be a changed perspective that removes rigging [30:30-31:32].
He laid the boards out in Milanote in columns by rig type and merged several into one master rig covering about 80% of the main character [31:32-32:02]. It keeps scope creep in check.

**Use it:** before a client round, count unique rigs or hero assets per board and check if a camera change removes one.
