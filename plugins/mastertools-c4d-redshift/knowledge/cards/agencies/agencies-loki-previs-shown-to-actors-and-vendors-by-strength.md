Townsend explains the record-store collapse was the most tightly previsualised sequence and he could show the actors the camera and action shot for shot [12:07]. Before takes he walks the actor through what will happen and what they are doing emotionally [11:04].
Vendors matched to strengths: one for animation, one for big environment, one for time doors and deletions, one for the loom, one for time slipping and most spaghettification [03:13, 03:45].
Six episodes meant six delivery dates, similar to closing reels on a feature [02:10].
The VFX producer served as the continuity "logic person" for a non-chronological, repeated-scene story [23:29].
Fits: on screens (Cantina did graphics on monitors) the content must tell where in time the scene is [24:32].

**Use it:** Show actors or presenters the plan in previs before shooting, and give each vendor or freelancer the sequence that fits their strength.
