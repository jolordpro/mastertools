Nike came with an initial idea and a moodboard. The studio happened to be working on a personal project that lined up almost perfectly, so it augmented that work to fit the brief. Erlingsson says that aligned the result with what the studio already wanted to make. Nike was one of its most consistent clients and let the team contribute to the creative. [§3]

**Use it:** Keep a few self-initiated pieces alive; they become ready-made answers when a matching brief lands.
