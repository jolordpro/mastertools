Jorge Canedo Estrada says the hardest part is growing at the right pace while keeping people fulfilled and balanced. His method: know when to say no, and ask the team before accepting a project that shifts the balance so nobody is surprised. He learned the habit from Jay at Giant Ant. Hires came one at a time, which kept each step manageable [§2].

**Use it:** Treat team capacity as a gate on taking a job: ask the people who will carry it before saying yes.
