When the studio offered shots on Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban, DNEG pointed at the night bus and said they could do it well, instead of bidding on the hippogriff or quidditch; the aim was to show they deserved to be part of the team, do great work, and build relationships [17:49-18:51].

**Use it:** Take the first piece for a new client to be one you can finish at high polish; the larger work follows.
