Evan Abrams (motion designer and teacher, 2019; single creator, low). A style frame shows the finished look before building starts, so the client signs off before money is spent [01:05]. Script and style frames are made together: after the mood board, draft the script beats and pick frames from them [03:42-04:13]. What to choose [05:14-06:18]: the establishing shot, the final shot (often the call to action), where action is most intense, any change of space (physical to mental) shown visually, and a frame that puts the key assets together (hands, pieces, clock) so later asset lists follow. Making them [06:49-08:54]: same frame ratio as final but not necessarily full 4K resolution; make more artboards than scenes (he made 9 for 3 scenes) to explore colour waves and detail levels; throw away the weak ones; show the same scene in two or three clearly different looks (hard vs soft corners, abstract vs realistic hands). Presenting [09:25-11:29]: export, one focal frame larger, others aligned, labelled Style One/Two/Three with a few descriptive words each (e.g. simple, geometric, abstract) so the review language is shared; clients comment directly on the frame; refine as Style 1.1. Put in contract that style frames are not unlimited because they are billable hours [11:29-12:00].

**Use it:** Package-pitch template for a broadcast open: frames chosen from beats, variations labelled, a hard cap on rounds.
