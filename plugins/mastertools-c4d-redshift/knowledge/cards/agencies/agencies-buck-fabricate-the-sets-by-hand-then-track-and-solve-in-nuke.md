The talk [29:30-31:06] describes the order of a mixed 3D and practical piece: storyboards, concept paintings, previs and blocking, measurements gathered for 3D, hand-fabricated sets, filming and animating in camera, tracking, photogrammetry, and "the magic" in Nuke. They chose to build every set by hand "to make it as big a pain as possible", and said it took more work than expected.

**Use it:** the stage order to copy when real-world textures are the goal: lock measurements before building so tracking and CG match later.
