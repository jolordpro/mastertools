He recalls the post-production projects he hated most: the client who asked to nudge two pixels, then orange, then purple, and he now catches himself doing the same to his own team [1:09:48-1:10:51].
People lose energy for the project; sometimes the best thing a lead can do is stay quiet and let them do it their way, even choosing a colour he would not [1:07:41-1:09:17].

**Use it:** frame notes around the problem and the goal; avoid a stream of tiny taste swaps.
