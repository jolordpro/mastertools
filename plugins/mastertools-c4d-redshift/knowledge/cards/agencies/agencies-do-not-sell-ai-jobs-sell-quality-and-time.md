Summers [50:48-52:50]: AI is cheap now because investors subsidise it, and prices may jump later. If a studio sets a low 'AI job' price now, it hurts the whole industry later.
Instead quote what the client gets: the fidelity level, the performance level, and the time it takes. Talk about five iterations instead of one, not about which node graph or service made it.
Sarofsky adds [50:18] that AI may not even be faster at first because research is front-loaded; speed comes after the workflow exists. Summers' history example: ILM lost its mystique when it talked about computers more than magic [52:20].
Speakers' view; no price numbers given.

**Use it:** when the estimator or coach sees an AI line item, suggest pricing the outcome and iteration count, and flag the front-loaded research days.
