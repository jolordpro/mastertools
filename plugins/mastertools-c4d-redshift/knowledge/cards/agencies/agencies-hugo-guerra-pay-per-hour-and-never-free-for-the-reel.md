Charge the going rate of the city you live in, remote or not, since you live on that country's costs, and you may negotiate for a longer job or a back-to-back booking [1:01:43, 1:02:13]. Film often pays less than commercials with 'it's a Nolan film' as the reason, which he rejects [1:02:46, 1:03:46]. At 500 pounds a day, an 18-hour day nets 250 for an 8-hour equivalent, so long days are a pay cut [1:07:24, 1:07:55]. Never accept 'it will be good for your reel': it is a hobby if unpaid, so ask for symbolic pay and shoot your own plates if needed [58:06, 1:05:51, 1:06:22]. He says mid-level London comp rates had not risen in 12 years [36:08].

**Use it:** For quotes and rate cards, overtime and rush should raise price rather than hide in the day rate. Rates are 2019.
