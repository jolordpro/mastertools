Studios still owe freelancers within 30 days whether or not the client has paid, and TJ calls 'we get paid when you do' policies a good way to lose good artists [49:53]. His contracts: net 30 plus a small penalty after that, or 50% on signing and 50% on final delivery [49:21, 50:55]. If the client insists on net 90, he asks for 75-80% at signing, so hard costs are covered and the last 20% is the margin [51:00]. Large clients can run net 45, 90, even 120 for car makers [50:24, 52:00]. Having a producer who can argue with lawyers helps [52:00].

**Use it:** Delivery and invoice wording on the platform can mention staged payment so a long net term never carries the whole job.
