Kyle Krieger (Yond executive producer) opens with calls that test whether a project is real: why it matters, the key messages, whether doing nothing is an option, and whether the client could approve the best idea within two weeks [09:19-10:22, 15:59-16:30].
Only with serious buyers and budgets does creative director Alissa Heaps build a pitch: references plus a typed director's treatment. A request for full mock-ups or spec projects counts as a red flag [10:52-11:23, 14:58-15:29].
Some leads get only a ballpark bid. Clients who will not explain their decision process get less invested time [16:30-17:32]. EP and animator also test the timeline for weak spots before any price goes out, so the client is not asked for overages later [12:25-13:00].

**Use it:** put the same questions in the intake step of a pitch or bid: real project, who signs off, by when, and how much design time the lead has earned.
