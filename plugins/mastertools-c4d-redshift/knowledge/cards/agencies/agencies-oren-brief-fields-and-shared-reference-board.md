Oren's working brief (a Notion page per request with in-progress, completed and archive folders) [11:52-12:52]: brief title, creative or ad type, purpose, number of assets, ratios, links to the exact content to use, any other info, references. His previous team (8 people, ClickUp) used an intake form that dropped into designer queues, visible to other departments, with him approving whether it ran [12:22]. The same shared Pinterest board briefs both designers and photographers, so a reference the designer picks becomes the shot list for the next shoot [13:23-13:53]. He sometimes attaches a reference and sometimes just says "your ideas" and the designer pulls from the agreed board [14:55]. Shared kits (fonts, sizes, buttons) are kept in one place so a change updates everywhere [14:24]. Outcome tracking: ads that work get more of the same style [17:30-18:01].

**Use it:** Template for a platform or sports-package brief to an artist: fixed fields plus ratios plus links, with one shared board.
