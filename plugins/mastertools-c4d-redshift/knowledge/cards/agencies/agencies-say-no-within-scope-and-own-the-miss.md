Walter Volpatto (feature colorist, Picture Shop; captions spell him "Valpado") was told by his boss never to say no to a client; he said no to an impossible track on a handheld shot and the director later said he liked that no was no and they could move on [1:26:18-1:29:00].
His rule: if the time, tech and budget cannot do it, no is the answer; he skips the compliment-problem-compliment opener and wants the problem stated [1:27:21-1:28:23].
When a timeline or shot is not set up, he says the collective made the mistake rather than blaming the person who loaded the material [1:30:26-1:31:30].

**Use it:** Escalation script: state the limit in time and money terms, offer an alternative, and avoid naming upstream people.
