[02:04 to 03:05] Under an NDA you probably cannot share project material with third parties, AI services included. Agencies often have AI policies; direct clients should be asked, ideally in writing.
'No training on your data' does not equal permission. Rule of thumb: never share confidential or proprietary assets, and ask broad questions that cannot identify the client or project.

**Use it:** add an AI-allowed line to the job intake and warn before upload.
