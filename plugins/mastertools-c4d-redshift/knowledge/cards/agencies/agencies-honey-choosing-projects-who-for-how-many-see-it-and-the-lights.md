Projects turned down are the ones that fail his criteria rather than a blanket creative judgement, since different creative directors find different jobs interesting [29:46-30:18]. The questions: who is it for, how many people will see it, what is the financial reward, will it keep the lights on, does it do something for culture (a cause) or creative opportunity [30:18]. For passion work for others the discussion is always whether it is worth it and whether the studio can afford it [22:27]. Finance staff keep the studio in line [22:27-23:00].

**Use it:** Use these five questions as a go/no-go screen for pitches and passion projects.
