It is much harder to move from executor to the studio people come to for its creative thing than the reverse; once a client has the 'execution company' label they struggle to connect it to something new [06:43-07:43]. Even the biggest studios probably get 90 percent of their work on other terms [07:43].
Golden Wolf went hard on Instagram early (first animation studio over 100k followers, he says) with mainly original work, so clients ask for what the feed shows instead of a two-year-old portfolio piece [08:45-09:16]. Expect to do other stuff, and eventually people come for the work you post; it takes trust [09:16-09:49]. When he posted his own-style AI work after some meme work, inquiries flooded in [08:14].

**Use it:** Make the public reel or feed the work wanted next year, not the work billed last year.
