Josh Parks (Blue Bolt) says a senior templates their own work while a lead templates for everybody and must know what makes sense to the team; small shops blur the two roles [04:18-04:50].
An ILM lead says seniors work very hard on big shots; leads spend time on the floor with artists, helping with technical issues [05:21-05:57]. Her hard part was passing on subjective notes (a bit bluer, a bigger explosion) from a director through a supervisor, since everyone pictures them differently [07:01-08:34].
Victor Wade (DNEG training head) says seniors are expected to support the team too, not just wear headphones [09:04-09:35]. DNEG adds a checker as a first tech-check layer that doubles as learning [21:33-22:06].

**Use it:** when reviewing, separate the director's note from the translated note to the artist; both need a record.
