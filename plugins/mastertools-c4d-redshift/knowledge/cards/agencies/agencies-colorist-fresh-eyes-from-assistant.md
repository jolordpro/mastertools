Walter Volpatto (feature colorist, Picture Shop; captions spell him "Valpado") says a lone colorist at home has nobody to ask when stuck; at his shop he sometimes calls an assistant over to say what they see because he is losing track [1:18:29-1:19:32].
Sometimes the answer is that it is fine; sometimes it is not [1:18:59]. He values a group of people working together toward the cinematographers' goals over an individualistic shop [1:18:00-1:18:29].

**Use it:** Habit: schedule a fresh-eyes pass by someone not in the grade before sign-off.
