Buck's people say that over about five years the work became less transactional; clients now bring product visioning, UX and illustration systems, not only a finished brief (Facebook Portal from visioning to launch campaign) [14:22-16:29].
The host cites a figure heard of about 87 percent direct-to-client in the US and 50 percent in Australia and Amsterdam; treat as hearsay [18:50-19:21].
A Buck guest (captions do not mark who speaks): clients do not care how the sausage is made, so put the right people on each job and avoid silos between makers and strategists [20:25-21:27]. Later: when handed an ever-growing deliverables list, ask what problem it solves and the budget, or build a tool that produces infinite deliverables [28:13-29:16].

**Use it:** Offer a templated system (render and comp preset) that outputs the variants, rather than pricing every variant by hand.
