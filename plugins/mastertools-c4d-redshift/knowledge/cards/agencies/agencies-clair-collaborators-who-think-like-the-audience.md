Clair hired Raoul Marks for The Division trailer; Raoul is a designer and animator and a filmmaker who understands storytelling, and his ability to put himself in the audience's mind became a model for what Clair seeks in collaborators [§6]. After True Detective demand grew, he built a team of designers, modelers, compositors, editors, storyboard artists and animators, so he stopped putting hands on the design himself [§6-7]. The writer's point: no single person has all the skills for titles like Westworld or American Gods [§7]. His studio Antibody was started for stability when Ubisoft work and a first child arrived together [§3].

**Use it:** When building a review or crew list, choose people who can judge from the viewer's side, not only craft skill.
