When a live call is not possible, Hugo Guerra sends a full keynote: inspiration, rough animatic, rough renders, latest comp, ideas, with his voice explaining each [35:16-36:17]. He says he won each cinematic pitch with such a deck, not a PDF [36:17-36:50].
Examples: a Gran Turismo trailer look-keynote with cars switching tracks [36:50]. Since adopting it about ten years ago, he gets fewer questions and fewer changes [37:22]. He sends even rougher boards and reference shots, and clients reply 'copy this one, mix this with that', feeling involved [37:54].

**Use it:** Async review recipe for a motion board: references, rough, current frame, with voice-over.
