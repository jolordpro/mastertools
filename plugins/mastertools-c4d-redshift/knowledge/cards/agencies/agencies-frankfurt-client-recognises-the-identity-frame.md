Frankfurt describes feedback as a decisive moment: on Mad Men the showrunner recognised one board frame as the show's identity, and the team moved it to the end of the sequence.
Marvel animation editing meant iteration after iteration, per Tosh Kodama.

Page spots: Mad Men's board frame of Don Draper's silhouette on the sofa sat earlier in the sequence until Matthew Weiner called it his show, so it moved to the end and later became a marketing signature [§3]. On the Marvel animation Tosh Kodama describes constant iteration while editing hundreds of images to 3D animation; 'many rounds are normal' is our paraphrase of that [§4].

**Use it:** Watch which frame the client reacts to and make that the anchor.
