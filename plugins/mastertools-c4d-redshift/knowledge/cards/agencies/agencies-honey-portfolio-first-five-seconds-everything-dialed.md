He says Buck gets roughly 20 to 30 applications a day and, past talent, the thing that gets a link onto the discuss list is that everything is dialled in: working site, no email mistakes, no dead links [31:26-32:28]. A CG artist with weak design can use a template service and learn to make it well. Producers also pass a first test if they appreciate design and details [32:28-32:59]. He rarely reads the fine print, only where someone is and how they define themselves [33:29-34:01]. Next step is a video call, and checking mutual connections for culture fit [34:01-34:33].

**Use it:** Portfolio QC list: links resolve, site loads fast, email clean, first five seconds show the best frame.
