He quotes David C. Baker: confidence is highest when incoming opportunity is larger than your capacity [20:52]. To get there, be positioned in one narrow area of expertise so the right clients come, and choose a lane rather than take all lanes [21:24]. He also says an independent business sells results, not hours, and can use other people to deliver [19:19-20:21]. On social posting: pick one platform and concentrate [26:38]; reply early after posting, since the first 24 hours matter for reach [25:36].
**Use it:** For plan or positioning reviews, check whether the offer is narrow enough to say no to work, and whether capacity is protected.
