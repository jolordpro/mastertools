On client product briefs Roberts delivers options [22:21-22:52]: first a stylised but safe version, then a gobo version with dappled sun-through-trees light, then a gradient or moody 'gamer' curveball for a razor-style product (a darker version for a logo that is orange) [29:21-29:52]. He also shows the same rig from several camera angles and focal lengths to prove the lights still work and to sell the set-up [25:02-25:37].
Gobo scene: a tape recorder on a table, an area light on top detail and one strong spotlight with a gobo raking across [11:38-13:50].

**Use it:** Pitch three looks plus a camera sweep, not one finished look.
