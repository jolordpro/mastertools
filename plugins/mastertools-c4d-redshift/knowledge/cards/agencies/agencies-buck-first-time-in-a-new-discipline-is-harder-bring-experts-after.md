A Buck guest: the first time Buck is asked for something new, they take a lot of risk and it is harder than their usual work; by the second and third they bring in people with experience to do it efficiently [22:30-23:31]. For an AI visioning job they added a linguistics expert and a fine artist [22:30].
The culture is described as checking your ego at the door, with ideas from everyone [22:30].

**Use it:** Scope a first-time job with extra time, or partner with a specialist and log the lessons.
