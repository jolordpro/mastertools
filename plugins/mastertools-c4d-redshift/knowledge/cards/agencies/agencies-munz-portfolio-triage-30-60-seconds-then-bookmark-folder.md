Munz (art director, 15+ years, Artful Ruckus) says a hiring manager facing 100-200 portfolios a day gives each 30 to 60 seconds to see what you can do, then either digs in or moves on [00:32-01:36].
What he checks first: typography and grid (tells him you understand layout), a short intro of who you are, and work that starts right away [01:36-02:07].
He saves liked portfolios to a bookmark folder and returns to it for the next hire or freelance job, so a good impression pays even without a job now [03:42-04:14].
Transfers to motion: a reel or site is judged on the first minute, and a "no" today can become a freelance call later.

**Use it:** Portfolio and reel QC: best frame and one-line pitch in the first screen; keep the site updated because it is bookmarked and revisited.
