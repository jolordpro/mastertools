Cole and co-designer Ben Procter keep a clear delineation: Procter owns all things Earth, Cole owns all things Pandora, so there is no territorial friction [24:00]. On Avatar 1 the split had been hard surface vs organic.
In early development they were lead concept artists in offices next door, doing rounds and then going back to painting [25:02]. Cole says he wanted everyone in his office to look at his work and "rip it to shreds", because the team were friends and some of the best artists in the world, and he did not want hierarchy [25:02].
He was new to the role and admits imposter syndrome, which he says artists carry throughout their lives [25:02].

**Use it:** Reviews on the platform can borrow this: each lead has a clear zone of ownership, and critique is open to all team members.
