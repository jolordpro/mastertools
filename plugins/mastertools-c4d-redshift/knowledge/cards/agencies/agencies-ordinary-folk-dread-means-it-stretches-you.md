Jorge Canedo Estrada says the team wants to feel scared at the start of a project and not know yet how to build it; once they know exactly how, they are getting too comfortable [§19]. Greg Stewart's first job with them was a heavily detailed illustrated piece he had no idea how to animate, and Jorge told him that is what working there is like [§19]. Victor Silva says the dread is shared and softened by knowing the others will help work it out [§20].
Counter-measure they pair with it: ask "how would you build this?" among the three, expecting several workable answers [§17].

**Use it:** When a brief makes the team nervous, treat it as the right size of job and break it down; if nothing feels uncertain, add a stretch element.
