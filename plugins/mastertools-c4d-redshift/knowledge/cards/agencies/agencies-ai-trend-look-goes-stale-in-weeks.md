Summers [38:52-39:54]: generative trends eat themselves faster than anything he has seen. One tool lets everyone make Pixar-style characters, and for three months TV is full of badly animated Pixar-like spots.
Anyone selling that look is selling something tired and not ownable by a brand. Old cycles took a year or two; he says this one takes three or four weeks.

**Use it:** in a pitch review, ask whether the AI look is the same one currently flooding feeds, and what makes it ownable.
