Nidia Dias (3D art director, ex-Future Deluxe, Territory, Man vs Machine; host Hayley Akins): a motion background means she knows how a design will be executed, and sometimes simplifies for budget, time or team [06:48-07:20].
If she knows the team is great she does not need to hold back [07:20]. She tried to learn what each team member does, to talk to them and explain the project [05:46, 09:54].

**Use it:** Style frame sign-off: ask who builds each frame, in what time, and simplify what that team cannot afford.
