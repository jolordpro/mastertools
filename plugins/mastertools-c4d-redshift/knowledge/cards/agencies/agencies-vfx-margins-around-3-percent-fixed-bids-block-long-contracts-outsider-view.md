Ryan Kelsey (Center Grid, ex-telecom; an outsider to VFX) says the first thing he noticed was that VFX income is a fixed budget per project, with margins he put at 3% ("that's the margin we're operating on", agreed to by the host) [16:02-17:03]. His reading: a studio cannot sign three-year service contracts because its workload is lumpy, so vendors have to flex month to month; he sees that as healthy pressure on the vendor to keep earning the account [17:03-18:35]. The host adds that studios laying off after a film is done fits this [17:03]. This is anecdote from a vendor, not data. His other point: people, not projects, are what VFX veterans remember about shows [19:35-20:05].

**Use it:** treat 3% as a rough, unverified talking point; the useful part is that burst or monthly cloud pricing suits project-shaped income, which the estimator can reflect by offering a per-month option.
