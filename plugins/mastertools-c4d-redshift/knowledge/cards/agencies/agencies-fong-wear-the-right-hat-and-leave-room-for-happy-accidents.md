Guest: Karin Fong. She is a designer first, but changes hat by job: with a film director she is one department among others (like production design or costume); in commercials and brand films, or on set, she acts as director [06:27-07:30].
Live shooting brings accidents that computer graphics do not: with marionette performers, nuances and slips (a spilled glass) can be better than the plan, and pure CG work makes thinking narrow [07:30-08:32].
When type leads or supports depends on the project; the job still must show cast, crew and title names, and type sets the feel even when viewers cannot say why [08:32-09:04].

**Use it:** In CG-only work, add a loose, human step (a filmed pass, a hand-made element) to invite accidents.
