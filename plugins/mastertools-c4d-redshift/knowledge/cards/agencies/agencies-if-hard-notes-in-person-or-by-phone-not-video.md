Denny does not want to give bad notes over Zoom; he wants to sit with the filmmaker, look them in the eye, and say he will work through it with them [1:14:17-1:14:48].
Creatives can be herd animals, so a one-to-one pulls out what a group will not, though it feels uncomfortable [1:26:22]. He calls people unbooked on the phone for 5-10 minutes; they pay more attention than on a scheduled video call [1:26:53].
Chip changed this year: do not email back, call or walk over, even for 10 minutes, because hearing the person describe the problem is where the answers are [1:27:24-1:29:00]. Denny's tip: promise to leave before they want you to.

**Use it:** For a difficult note, speak live to the artist first and put the written note afterward.
