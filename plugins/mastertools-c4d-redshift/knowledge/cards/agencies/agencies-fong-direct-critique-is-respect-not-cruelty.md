She was shy about giving notes early on and did not want to be the person who squashes an idea [30:04]. Her view now: a creative director cannot waffle, because a note like "start over" or "fix this" steers the ship. Being straight is a sign of respect and of trust that the team can get through it together; treating someone as fragile is the opposite [29:34]. Her gentle line for work that misses: it is fantastic for another project [29:03]. Be positive and appreciative, but give the opinion; sometimes the other person's idea turns out better and everyone moves to it [30:35].
Clients who state a firm opinion (the Spider-Man director wanting titles hand-done from the start) are giving direction she can run with, not an obstacle [31:08].

**Use it:** Coach tooltip for reviewers: name the verdict (keep, fix, restart) in the first line, then the reason. A firm client opinion is an input, not a problem.
