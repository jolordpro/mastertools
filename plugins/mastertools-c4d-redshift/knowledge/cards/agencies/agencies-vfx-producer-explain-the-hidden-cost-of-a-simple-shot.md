Brooke Noska (VFX producer, The Orville) says the biggest challenge is that VFX is not cookie-cutter: people think a green screen makes the magic happen and ask to copy a moon from another show without seeing how much creative work, hardware, time, render time and money sits behind two frames on screen [07:22-09:29]. Different creatives drive different looks, so it is hard to settle on one way to do a thing; her toolkit grows from reusing a method from earlier shows, such as a rain shoot adapted from another series [08:25].
She also says she asks teams on other shows how they are working when a new workflow (such as remote) arrives [18:18-18:49].

**Use it:** A line for client notes: when a client points to another show's shot, ask what it took to make it, then price the match.
