Chip: IF started in the film and optical era; they listened to how the business worked, then used the freedom of not having done it before [1:00:14-1:01:20]. For Guillermo del Toro's film Mimic they tested a title on a Macintosh, del Toro loved it, but the studio post supervisors refused, a turning point toward digital titles [1:01:20-1:02:56].
Simon's reading: it was not about the Mac, it was that it solved the problem (control, efficiency, a different kind of artist) [1:04:00-1:04:30]. Judge a tool by what it solves.

**Use it:** When proposing a new tool in the pipeline (a render engine, an AI step), show a test on the real shot and say what problem it solves.
