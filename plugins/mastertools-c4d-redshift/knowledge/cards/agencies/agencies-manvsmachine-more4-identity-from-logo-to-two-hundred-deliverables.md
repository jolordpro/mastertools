The More4 job covered logo, colour palette, a custom font, animating the logo, typography with the logo, and a deliverables list that can reach 200 items [04:02-04:34]. A live-action ident set mirrored the animated information system, so a day of viewing flows together [04:34-05:05].
They say audience of that channel is more than 5 million a week and they wanted people to relate to it, with humanity, not only technical impressiveness [05:05-06:07].

**Use it:** Plan the deliverables matrix at the start of a brand system job; it can be hundreds of variants.
