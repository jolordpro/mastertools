On Spider-Man 2 they did not know up front what they would end up doing; there was a dialogue in which things were shown, got rejected, and other things were tried [04:55-05:25].
He calls it a creative exchange that lasts the whole course of post-production [05:25].

**Use it:** Plan a title project as an exchange, with review rounds and spare time for rejected directions, not a single pass.
