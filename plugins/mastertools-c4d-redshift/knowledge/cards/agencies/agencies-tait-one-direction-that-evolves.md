On the David Blaine opener there was no formal brief. The team boarded a few ideas, picked one direction, and grew it as artists joined. Shared references (old posters, broadsheets) held the look together. The job took about 8 weeks and animators sometimes got final styleframes just before cleanup. [§2-§3]

**Use it:** Lock one direction and one reference set early; a late styleframe is a schedule risk.
