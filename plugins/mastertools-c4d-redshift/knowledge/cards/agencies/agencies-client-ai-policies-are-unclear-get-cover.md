A studio creative director on the stream said some clients have a stated no-AI policy that does not say whether Photoshop generative fill counts [06:06-07:09]. The studio wants a clear line on what is allowed plus insurance in case it is ever called out.
Summers adds that clients who dabble with Gemini or Midjourney often get stuck and ask the studio to take them further; playing together for an hour lowers fear on both sides [07:40-08:41].

**Use it:** ask for the policy in writing and ask about common cases (fill, upres, roto) by name, not just 'AI'.
