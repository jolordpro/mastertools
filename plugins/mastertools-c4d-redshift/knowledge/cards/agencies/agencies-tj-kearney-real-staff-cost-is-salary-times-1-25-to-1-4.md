A $70k hire lands near $90k once taxes and benefits are in; TJ puts the multiplier at 1.25-1.4 depending on region, nearer the top in San Francisco [40:33]. Beyond that: paid time off, healthcare, 401k, machines, rent, and four to six weeks with no work still means full pay [35:17]. Staff rates look like a third to a half of a freelancer's day rate, but only until you count these [35:47]. At about 10-15 people the studio needs a real office, because clients want to sit in [36:18]. Every day the team is not working comes out of the owner's pocket [39:30].

**Use it:** Use 1.25-1.4 as the loaded-cost multiplier if the estimator ever prices staff time instead of freelance days.
