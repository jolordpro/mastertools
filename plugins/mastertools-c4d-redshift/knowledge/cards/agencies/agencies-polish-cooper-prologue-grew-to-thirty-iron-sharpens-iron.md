Prologue was meant to stay small but grew to a cadre of about 30 creatives, moving twice to larger premises in Venice, California [Eye §2]. Cooper repeatedly returns to gathering the best people and tempering creative freedom with meaningfulness, quoting that iron sharpens iron [Eye §2]. His reasons for starting over were to put the work first, creatively and holistically [Eye §1]. He directed about 40 titles at R/GA and 46 more in six years as Imaginary Forces partner [Eye §1].

**Use it:** Cite as a benchmark for how a title boutique scales: people first, freedom bounded by meaning.
