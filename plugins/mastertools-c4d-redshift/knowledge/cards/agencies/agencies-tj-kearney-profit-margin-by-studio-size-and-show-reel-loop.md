Small studios bake in little or no profit [1:00:20]. Mid-size average about 20-25% profit; the big names make at least 30% and often about 50% by padding rates as well as listing profit. Per Ryan Honey (Buck), only roughly 10% of what a top studio makes is the work you see on the reel; the rest pays the bills [1:00:55]. The money from routine jobs (end cards, tags) funds over-delivering on showcase pieces that win the next client, a loop he calls a sales loop [1:01:25]. A 20% markup covers client and staff management plus future investment, not greed [1:03:33].

**Use it:** Explains why a studio spends render hours on a showcase piece at a loss. Use as context for the coach, not as a rule for quotes.
