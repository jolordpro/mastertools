He says he would not work as an editor unless hired for the colour too, since he did not want uncorrected work going to air under his name [04:45-05:15]. When one client skipped correction he told them to take his name off the credits; two days later they called back to colour correct and keep the credit [05:15-05:49]. Clients often see colour and audio mix as the two easy places to save money [10:26-10:58].
Host: John Lux, Film Florida podcast.

**Use it:** Set the minimum polish pass as a condition of the work, and say so before the job starts.
