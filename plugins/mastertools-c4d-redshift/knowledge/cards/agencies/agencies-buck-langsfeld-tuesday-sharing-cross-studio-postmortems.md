Ben Langsfeld (group creative director, BUCK New York): a weekly Tuesday session, started for work in progress and now mostly finished work, where offices share process, decision-making and a sort of post-mortem [49:00] [49:30]. He says presenting to peers makes him the most nervous because their opinion matters most, and he advises keeping people who give candid feedback and who respect the process, not those who scare you off with fear of failure [49:30] [50:01]. He also says to focus on what you can control instead of critics [47:28].

**Use it:** Review ritual to copy: a regular show-and-tell of finished work with the decisions behind it, not only the pretty result.
