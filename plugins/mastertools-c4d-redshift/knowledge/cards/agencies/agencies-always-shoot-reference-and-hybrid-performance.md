Chris Maclean [32:46-35:22]: a stunt performer in a suit with a giant head played the cat creature on set so actors could react and everyone could see the scale. For the spacers, season one was full CG; in season two the interaction grew, so they kept the actors' mouths and much of the performance and replaced the rest, after an image-engine vendor proposed a hybrid. They built an anti-gravity space on set with wires and mats and shot reference. His rule: do not decide to figure it out later; get some real-world basis for the effect.

**Use it:** For motion work: shoot or gather reference before animating, and keep the human part of a performance when replacing the rest.
