Greg Stewart says his first day with the team brought frame-by-frame, over-the-shoulder feedback ("I don't like this frame"), and he read the care behind it as intent to make quality really good [§12]. Jorge also emails very long notes; Victor Silva took them well and explained back, and the two clicked [§29].
Greg's rule: a good piece is not style frame, transition, style frame, so how the animation gets between style frames matters as much as the frames themselves, though not every frame is equally important [§12-13]. Victor adds that taking heavy notes without wrapping identity in the work is a skill [§39].

**Use it:** Give notes frame by frame when needed, but tie each to the whole shot; review the in-betweens, not just the hero frames.
