Cooper [22:42-26:06] says he has never recruited people away from another company. He looks for someone who really wants to be there and has a good visual sense (an editor with good judgement or a technical person can work too). He leans toward people who draw, set beautiful type, make strong Photoshop frames or present clever ideas, over someone who is only an animator, "maybe to a fault". A co-panelist (Chris Do, who is the host) adds that he looks for one thing today: command of typography that is interesting and timeless. The Chris Do line is a host opinion.

**Use it:** the portfolio check for motion hires: look at the stills and the type before the animation.
