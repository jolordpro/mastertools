Edwards says the T-Rex had six good colour schemes. To get the path of least regret, they sent all of them to Spielberg, so his pick was the official look [27:00, 27:32]. His early role was to say what was and was not 'Jurassic' in design; later he watched dailies [27:32, 28:04]. On homage, they avoided a greatest-hits remake and kept a sweet spot between copying and drifting, nudging left and right [30:37, 31:40]. The team treated the film as if it had been made in the early 90s and shelved [29:35].

**Use it:** In brand work, bring equal options to the brand owner at once, so the decision-maker picks and nobody has to defend a choice. Set the brand edges by what is not on-brand.
