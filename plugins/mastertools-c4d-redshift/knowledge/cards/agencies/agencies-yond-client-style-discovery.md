Research on the client's business is only a skim; going deep gets the team bogged down, so a client specialist works with the scriptwriter [23:47-24:19].
At kickoff Alissa asks what styles they have seen. Many assume 'animation' means cartoon, so part of the job is a short art-history lesson and references to react to [25:21-25:51].
Sketching helps but can surprise a client who expected clean vector art [25:51-26:23]. For ideas beyond budget she answers with a yes-and variant rather than a flat no [24:19-24:50].

**Use it:** kickoff checklist: ask for seen examples, send 3-5 references, state the style in words, then sketch.
