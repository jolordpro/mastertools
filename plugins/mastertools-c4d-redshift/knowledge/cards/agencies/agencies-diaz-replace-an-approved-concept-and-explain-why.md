On an inflatable-castle loop for a shoe brand the client approved the first version, but she disliked it: the product was a fixed size, so the environment felt out of proportion [29:38-30:10].
She built another version with better scale and more window light, sent it with a plain reason, and the client said fine [30:10-30:40].

**Use it:** if the maker's own eye says the approved version is wrong, a short written reason makes a late swap safe.
