After delivering the True Detective titles, HBO sent a note that the sequence looked a bit too much like another title, and named one [47:45].
Clair says he has since learned that this is a note you get on almost every title sequence [47:45-48:15].
He spent the weekend in dread because the note landed on Friday and the call was Monday (Tuesday in Australia) [47:45-48:15]. Before delivery he had only hoped to be competent, not good [48:15].
Transfers: sports opens and packages draw the same 'reminds me of X' note.

**Use it:** Treat a looks-like note as routine: keep a short list of what differs from the named reference (idea, subject, execution) ready before the call, rather than treating it as a verdict.
