Justin Mitchell (Scanline VFX supervisor) says some notes cannot be done literally. He reads the intent: the director wants energy or a big explosive beat, not necessarily the hit at that exact moment [17:47]. He describes sim work as guiding, not driving: like bouncing a ball down stairs into a cup, it takes many tries [16:12-17:15]. Over-art-directing a sim can leave it looking fake [18:19].

**Use it:** when a client note conflicts with physical sense (e.g. a splash), ask what feeling they want and offer a plausible route to it. Extends the existing diagnose-not-prescribe rule from the artist's side.
