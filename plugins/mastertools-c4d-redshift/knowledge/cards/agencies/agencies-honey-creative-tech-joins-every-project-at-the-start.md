Now, whatever the project, someone from creative tech joins at the beginning to see what they can add: make production smoother or create a style not done before [11:35-12:06]. Reason: clients expect more and faster, so technology and pipeline people are essential [12:06-12:37].

**Use it:** Invite a pipeline/tech person to kickoff for every look-dev or sports package, not only to fix problems later.
