Ben Langsfeld (group creative director, BUCK New York): the hardest ongoing challenge is not selling the same look twice because clients ask for "the same as BBC" [10:32]. His team resists because it dislikes repeating itself; they push some envelope on each job [11:04].
The rule: the job is the boss, so be style-agnostic and start from the idea; the execution is an expression of the concept [11:04] [11:34]. The host adds that understanding strategy and what the brand stands for must come before visuals [11:34].
Interviewer's questions are low confidence; guest statements are medium.

**Use it:** Coach / agency answer when a client wants a cloned style: reframe to the problem the old job solved, and build the concept before the look.
