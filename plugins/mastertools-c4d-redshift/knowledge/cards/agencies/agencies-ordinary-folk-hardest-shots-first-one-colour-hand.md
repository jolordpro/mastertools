On Join the Movement, Ordinary Folk wanted a visual progression in complexity and different techniques without a disconnected montage [§3]. Three designers each explored the same boards so ideas passed from one to the next, then divided the styleframes under one shared palette with constant feedback [§3-4]. Jay Quercia had the special role of unifying the piece: colour scripts, new ideas, and most of the final styleframes [§4].
For animation, the three team animators first took the shape-heavy shots to establish the animation mood, spending more than a week on each. Those shots showed they needed many more hands, so students and freelancers took shots; animation alone took one month [§4].

**Use it:** Name one owner of colour across a multi-shot piece; set the mood with the shots that define it before handing out the rest.
