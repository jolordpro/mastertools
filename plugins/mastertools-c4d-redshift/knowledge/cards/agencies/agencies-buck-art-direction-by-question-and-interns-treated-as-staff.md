Bernd Bousard (animator, ex-intern, now full-time at Buck Sydney) says the creative directors Gareth and Lucas ask 'what do you think, could we try this' rather than give orders, so people impose the standard on themselves ('make the best frame ever' becomes a personal goal) [08:38-10:15]. Interns get real projects from day one with extra coaching, credit on the Buck site, and no coffee runs [03:42-04:46]. His six-month internship ended with a coffee chat with the creative director and a yes; he credits eagerness, openness to feedback with no ego, being easy to work with, and luck about headroom [05:19-08:07].
Application advice he gives: one polished showreel, short emails, no weekly chasing, and use the studio's application form because it reaches the right mailbox; a past 'not ready yet' reply was fair and led to later freelance work [02:39, 10:48-11:54].

**Use it:** for the coach, a model of low-ego review: phrase notes as options with a reason; the career details are context only.
