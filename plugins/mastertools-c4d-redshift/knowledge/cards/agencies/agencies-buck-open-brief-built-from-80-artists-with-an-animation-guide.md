For a piece about Buck itself [36:34-42:06], about 80 people touched it. Process [39:16-41:25]: a loose concept about the birth of an idea; storyboard each section with a particular artist in mind; style frames from the boards; a lead animator times everything using plain shapes for pacing; different animators previs each shot in their style; transitions were thought out so motion stays fluid. The ending was 95 frames: one animator made a guide so motion could be tracked by eye, every frame was exported, and about 50 artists each drew one or more frames in their own style.

**Use it:** pacing before style; timing locked with placeholder shapes, then styles layered on.
