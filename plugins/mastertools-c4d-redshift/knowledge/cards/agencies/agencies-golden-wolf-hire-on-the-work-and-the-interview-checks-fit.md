In the SNE stream [19:07-21:23] a Golden Wolf guest says nearly all hiring decisions come from the work: if the animator or designer is excellent, they probably get in. The interview mostly checks personality and fit, or splits otherwise tied candidates. In early lockdown they leaned on freelancers because full-time hiring felt unwise while the next month was unknown. Earlier [18:06-19:07] they describe a flatter structure than a director-led one, with roles (director, art director, designers, animators) but not one person's ego deciding everything.
