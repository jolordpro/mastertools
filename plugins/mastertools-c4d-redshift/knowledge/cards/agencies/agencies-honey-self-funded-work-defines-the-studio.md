Honey says Buck's defining moments were fuelled by self-funded or "investment" creative projects, naming Good Books, Seed Matters, Childline, f5, David Blane and Tinder. Separately, his proudest recent moment was Apple's iMac Pro film, a commission where the brief was to do what Buck does on Apple hardware, and over 90 staff artists took part. Buck calls itself style-agnostic and encourages artists to experiment and mix mediums. The studio grew from about 80 to 250 people in five years and works daily to keep its culture.
