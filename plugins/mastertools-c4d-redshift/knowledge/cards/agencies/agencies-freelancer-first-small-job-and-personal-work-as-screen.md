Nidia Dias (3D art director, ex-Future Deluxe, Territory, Man vs Machine; host Hayley Akins) says a studio's first job for a new remote freelancer was small to test the waters, then recurring work followed [27:07-27:38, 29:43].
When she hires, she looks at personal projects because client work shows the studio style while personal work shows what the person is good at and likes [30:14-30:46].
She keeps a list of freelancers, passes names when busy, and says that sharing names does not cost her work [25:33-26:36, 29:11].

**Use it:** Producer step: schedule a small paid test before a large job; use personal-project reel as a style screen.
