Austin Bauwens says posting only as the founder made team members feel unseen, so the studio moved to many personal accounts under the studio banner, about 200,000 followers across them [33:04-34:36]. Commission is 5% of a project to the person who brought it in; he says it has barely been triggered, since leads still come through his account [35:40-36:11].
Finances are open to the whole team: pay, project values and the founders' income [40:50-41:22]. The team is contractors, all remote, in many time zones [43:24].
Austin's reasoning for team accounts: it keeps the studio from being tied to one brand and lets teams form around what people are excited about [38:45].
