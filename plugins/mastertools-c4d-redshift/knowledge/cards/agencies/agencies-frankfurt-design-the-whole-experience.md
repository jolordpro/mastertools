In a pitch for IBM he argued that films on screens would look foolish in a dated conference room, so he proposed designing the media, the space and the hospitality together.
He sees good work as considering the whole experience, not a single medium.

Page spots: IBM wanted briefing-centre films, Imaginary Forces argued a video in a poor conference room would look silly and proposed the whole experience, from media and delivery to the space, client meals and host clothing [§2]. They partnered with architect George Yu; the project took most of 18 months and the furniture, floors and ceilings were custom built [§2].
