Fong's number one rule is to work with people better than you, because that is how work improves [§6]. Her mentor Kyle Cooper told her to stop animating since she could work with people better at it, which she says is true [§6]. She wants dialogue partners who elevate ideas and says a leader takes the best ideas from wherever they come [§6]. On Counterpart the showrunner banned mirrors in the first call, and an animator later used a grid of mirrors near the end so it was unclear what was illusion [§5]. Tools: Octane, Cinema 4D, X-Particles, Flame, After Effects, Photoshop, Illustrator, Premiere; shot on an ARRI Alexa Mini [§5].

**Use it:** Frame creative-director advice around hiring and delegating, not doing every task.
