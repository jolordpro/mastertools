He says no commercial brief is fully open (MTV idents were close). Studios are hired either to execute or for their creativity; with 'we want that Golden Wolf thing' there is freedom [03:40-04:41].
Often by the time a studio is brought in, a storyboard and script have been approved by 20 layers of stakeholders. The risk is just executing; the better route is to find where to inject creativity and make it better [04:41-05:12]. He believes the best directors are not those who never get told no, but those best at navigating the client and moulding the brief into something great [05:12-05:42].

**Use it:** On a prescriptive board, list where there is room to improve (timing, transitions, texture, camera) and propose only those, tied to the client's goal.
