Cooper [11:10-14:45, 18:21-19:23]: he followed Paul Rand's idea that good design is goodwill: some clients still call him unprompted years later. He admits his never-compromise stance made him a sucker for people who took advantage, and that he understands the anxiety of payroll. Now he looks at who is talking to him, not the brand, takes the meeting, collects information, and notes that saying no used to be fear-based. In one overtime dispute he laid out hourly cost against the budget to a producer rather than stop mid-animation. [This is his own account.]
Later [50:43-52:16]: he no longer takes jobs or partners he is uncomfortable with just because someone says he needs the money.

**Use it:** a client-fit question before bidding: who is the person, and do they come back and respect the work?
