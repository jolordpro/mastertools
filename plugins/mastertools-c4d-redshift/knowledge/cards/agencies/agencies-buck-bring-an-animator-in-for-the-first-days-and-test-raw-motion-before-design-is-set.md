[14:58-28:01] Normal timeline: brief, art direction, design, animation last as an afterthought with no voice. BUCK wants animation direction in parallel. Two tools: animatics (pacing) and early motion tests, which carry feel (shape, interaction, opacity, colour) not design. Examples: Logitech reel (a button press and response for a gaming feel), Zapier ("zappy" word from a client call, three days of small tests as a motion mood board) [20:35-26:29]. Stripped back, the team interprets it their own way, giving surprises and consistency. Motion festival identity: animators and designers worked in parallel for two weeks to make a logo; the logo was built on its motion potential (a wave), then designers staggered the PNG sequence across posters [14:58-18:26]. They share work in Google Slides because it shows GIFs live [16:08]. Closing questions: is there a motion language in this brief, can motion come earlier, can we make a small reference (a GIF, curve, sound or word) [26:50-28:01].

**Use it:** the early test belongs in the first days of a package, before styleframes lock.
