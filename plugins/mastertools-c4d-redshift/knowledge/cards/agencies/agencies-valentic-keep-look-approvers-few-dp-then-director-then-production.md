One of the biggest look-development mistakes is sharing with too many people: too many cooks, and you do not need all those opinions [38:28-39:02].
Chain he uses: cinematographer (who holds the creative decision delegated by the director), then director, then production or commissioner; a big studio then asks for a DCP of tests for the biggest screen, and he says one studio keeps a room of different brands of TVs to play a test file on [39:02-39:35].
Bigger studio, slower approval, but approving before shooting avoids a technical rejection after the grade is finished [39:35-40:07].

**Use it:** Review plan: name one decision-maker per stage and one reviewer per approval step; test the delivery on more than one display.
