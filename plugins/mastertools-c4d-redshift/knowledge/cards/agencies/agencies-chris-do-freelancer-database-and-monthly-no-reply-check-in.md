Blind adds every reel to a database with rating and rates, shared internally, because emails get lost [50:30]. Chris Do's tip for freelancers: do not send one email and wait; once a month send one cool still frame (reuse an older project if you have nothing new) with a line like "so I'm on your radar" [50:56]. Host Joey Korenman: every month, two to three months at the least, never ask for anything, and add "no reply necessary" (a tip he credits to the Four Hour Work Week) [51:33][52:07]. If the studio has a job then, they will probably call [52:07]. Their survey for the host's Freelance U course: 83% of 150 studio people said finding freelancers is sometimes hard [59:33]. Treat the 83% as the host's secondhand quote.
