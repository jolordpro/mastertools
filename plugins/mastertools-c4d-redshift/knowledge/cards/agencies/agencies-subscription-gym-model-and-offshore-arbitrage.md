Host Joey Korenman (an opinion, low weight) compares subscriptions to gyms and car-wash memberships. The model works only because most members never use it [56:28-57:31].
He says design subscriptions often hire offshore designers for about $500 a month, which makes the numbers work [1:01:10]. School of Motion tried Design Pickle and one other; the quality was only okay and needed a lot of pushing [1:01:40]. Brands with real budgets have taste and will not accept that [1:02:11].
Austin Bauwens' scheduling idea: let clients request in a window and set the delivery date on the back end so the studio stays profitable [57:31-58:01]. It still did not solve the price size, which is far above a $10 gym fee.

**Use it:** A reminder for any plan to productise motion work: the capacity limit, not the price list, decides whether it is profitable.
