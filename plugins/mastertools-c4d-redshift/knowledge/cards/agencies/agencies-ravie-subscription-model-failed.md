Austin Bauwens tried plans priced by seconds of animation. The top plan was about $8,000 a month for 60 seconds, under a quarter of their normal price for that runtime [51:46-52:17]. Their normal quotes are flat rates set by video length [51:14].
Problems: the community disliked it at launch [52:49]; buyers distrusted a price that low, such as a prospect at Apple [53:19, 59:35]; raising it made it too expensive.
The maths: a $70,000 project is about one or two months of work. A subscription asks the same studio to do more work for less money [54:21]. Ten clients each wanting 60 seconds in one month is about $70k of work for a team of eight [55:24]. Monthly plans can be cancelled after one delivery [1:00:07].
Austin: it might work for a freelancer with two or three clients who covers a target salary, if the limits on what the money buys are very clear [55:56].
