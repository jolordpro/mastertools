The showrunner described Louisiana as a poisoned, broken landscape standing for broken people. Clair, looking at a book cover with a double exposure, had the phrase 'broken landscapes making broken people' and most of the pitch done by that night [45:08-46:13].
He pitched after a bad-feeling 7 am phone call with a four-month-old baby at home, yet knew exactly what he wanted to do [44:36-45:08].
After the win he brought Raoul Marks back in and put every cent of the budget into the job, which he calls not a sensible business decision but the right one to try [46:13-46:44].
Transfers: a title or open pitch can hang on one sentence plus one visual device.

**Use it:** When pitching an open, write the single sentence that joins the story's theme to a visual device, and say out loud whether the budget justifies going all in.
