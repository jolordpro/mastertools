He objects to meetings where ten cinematographers are on the list, because the director is not clear about the work; ten people will not interpret it the same way [36:58-37:29]. He advises: instead of answering a stack of questions, ask the director what they do, so you feel comfortable that your process and vulnerability will be supported [38:32-39:03]. Collaboration should exist because it needs to, with a specific reason for choosing you.

**Use it:** Intake question for freelancers: ask what the lead needs from you and what decisions they will protect, before agreeing.
