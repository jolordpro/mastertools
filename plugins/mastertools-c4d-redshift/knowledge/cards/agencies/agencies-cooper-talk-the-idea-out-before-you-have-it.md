Cooper [26:07-31:24] quotes Chuck Close ("inspiration is for amateurs") and says he starts by understanding the problem, then researching, cutting and sketching, not scrolling through tear sheets. Reference is for emergencies (a three-hour deadline). He keeps a file of good ideas that went unused, as Paul Rand kept a bulletin board. When he does not yet have an idea, he forces himself to talk about it, even to a client; once the words are out he often finds he has one. He calls this taking the next indicated action.

**Use it:** at pitch prep, talk it through aloud or in a note before opening the mood board; keep a "good ideas not used" folder per studio.
