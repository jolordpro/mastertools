Ben Langsfeld (group creative director, BUCK New York): they favour generalists who can think, write, design and illustrate, 'filling the leak' like crew on a ship [27:05] [27:37]. In portfolios they want unique work with a concept behind it, high consistency, and collaboration, since a solo reel may not translate to a studio with many fingerprints [28:08] [28:39].
Advice to applicants: show what the work feels like, cut through noise; it need not be the most technical but should have a point of view [51:04] [51:35]. Story: a freelancer doing basic After Effects work was hired after BUCK saw his experimental, odd animation posted after hours; they wanted that thinking, not the AE skill [51:35] [52:08] [52:41].
They look for people committed to learning, trying, failing and working in a big team [52:41]. BUCK has a separate Instagram handle used for submissions, reviewed weekly [54:13].

**Use it:** Portfolio review heuristic: after-hours experiments often show more than client work; show collaboration and concept, not only craft.
