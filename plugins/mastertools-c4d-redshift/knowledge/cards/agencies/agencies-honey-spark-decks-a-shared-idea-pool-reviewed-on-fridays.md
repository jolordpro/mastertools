Started about six months before the talk (2020): invite people from different disciplines to throw ideas into a deck, from an installation to a game, then meet on Fridays so contributors can talk through the idea and take questions [13:41-14:43]. The aim is connections and synergy between disciplines. Rule he repeats: be totally ego-free; coders should not feel their role is only implementing [14:43].

**Use it:** A light recurring idea pool with a weekly walkthrough is a cheap way to surface R&D ideas.
