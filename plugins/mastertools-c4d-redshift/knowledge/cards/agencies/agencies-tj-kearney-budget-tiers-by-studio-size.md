Guest TJ Kearney (EP, ex-Oddfellows co-founder, 2018 figures). Tiny studio (2-5): $5-20k jobs, sweet spot about $15k, which only covers day rates with no profit [28:05]. At 10-15 staff the monthly overhead passes $100k, so the floor is about $60k, healthy range $80-120k, about 90% of work in $60-100k, and a few $250-500k pitches a year [34:46, 42:08]. At 30-50 staff, multiple offices, overhead is hundreds of thousands a month and typical jobs are $200-500k [53:03]. He says this is for one kind of animation work, so it varies by market.

**Use it:** The cost estimator can label a quote against these tiers and warn when a job sits below the floor for the studio size that would deliver it. Treat the dollar figures as 2018.
