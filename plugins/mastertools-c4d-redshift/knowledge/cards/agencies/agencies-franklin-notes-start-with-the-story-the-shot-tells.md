At a company of about 8,000 people, he says the thing to be clear about is what story the shot tells: even a five-second shot has something travelling, transforming or performing in a certain way, and everything else follows from that [39:10-40:11]. He reviews at every stage, including grey-shaded blocking, and has to imagine how it will look lit and cut in [40:11].
On set he spends time with the costume designer to learn what they wanted, so effects work protects it instead of overwriting it [37:36-38:08].

**Use it:** Start each note with what the shot is for; then name changes in terms of that story, not only look.
