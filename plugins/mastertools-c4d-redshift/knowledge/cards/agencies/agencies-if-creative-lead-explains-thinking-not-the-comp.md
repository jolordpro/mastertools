Denny says it drives him mad when a creative director describes the frames visibly in front of the client [1:35:46].
The job is to explain the thinking, say what was dropped, and ask whether any dropped idea is worth revisiting [1:36:17].
He adds that holding a real conversation about the work is a skill that weakened over video calls, and the goal of a presentation is to learn what the partner needs, not just "do you like it" [1:36:48].

**Use it:** In a review, spend the talk on intent, trade-offs and rejected options. Keep two or three rejected frames ready.
