Core app Cinema 4D; V-Ray main renderer with Octane where speed helps; After Effects and Nuke for comp by project. RoyalRender runs the in-house farm, quality checks and data flow from 3D to compositing.
A full-time developer extends Cinema 4D with custom tools. 12 permanent staff in 2015, 400 sq m office, over three minutes of photoreal 3D for one Red Bull Racing film. Article is a 2015 archive; tools may have changed.

**Use it:** Automate the checks that catch finish errors on the farm; note this is a 2015 snapshot.
