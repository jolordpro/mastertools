Clair wanted to tell science-fiction stories that cannot get funded as live action, so he used the tools of title design in the broadest sense: stock footage from existing subscriptions, text-to-speech scratch voices, later voice-over from freelancers hired through Fiverr [1:08:04-1:09:38].
He supervises storyboards and hands them to Marks, which leaves him spare time [1:09:08-1:09:38].
The strike gave real time because most clients are showrunners who could not talk to anyone; pieces run 1 to 10 minutes and range from motion graphics to CG to stop-motion, and went on Vimeo and Instagram; three were out at recording [1:09:38-1:12:17].
Transfers: a studio slow patch is a window for self-initiated showreel pieces.

**Use it:** When client work thins, schedule a self-initiated short with a locked storyboard and cheap placeholder assets, so the studio ships something that shows range.
