Keep the product front and centre. For a formula brand (Alderson's example is Pepsi) list the boxes that must be ticked: the ice shot, the consumption shot and the "ahhh" moment. Do them well and do not spend energy fighting them. That buys the other 40-50% of the spot to play in, as long as the structure stays coherent. He adds that crafted eye candy can be enough without a full beginning, middle and end. Separately, he says he is wary of anything longer than 90 seconds and has no urge to move to long form.

**Use it:** Protect the hero product frame first; judge experiments only after the hero beats pass.
