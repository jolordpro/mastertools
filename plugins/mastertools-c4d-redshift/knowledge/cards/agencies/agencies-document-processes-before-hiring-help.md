Hayley's worst year came after hiring with no documented systems, so she could not explain how she wanted work done [53:37-55:11].
Write processes down, record yourself doing tasks, and automate or template them first [55:11-56:45]. Before hiring, make three lists (what you can't do, don't like, shouldn't do) and group tasks into roles [57:16-58:49].
A virtual assistant for a couple of hours a week can do admin and break a case study into social posts [1:03:29-1:05:00].

**Use it:** same method for pipeline hand-offs: write the steps before giving them away.
