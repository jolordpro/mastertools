Ross Denner was creative on a ten-part, 40-minute pure-CG series (the speaker is unsure of the title; transcript reads 'what lived inside', for Intel, director Robert Stromberg) [11:58-12:31]. It used MPC Bangalore, London, New York and LA to process round the clock. His day ran from about 6-7 am (London) to about 2 am [13:02]. He credits the many leads who later headed departments, and the director's advice to embrace the crazy [13:32].

**Use it:** Gives scale for a long-form CG job: a global hand-off chain at the cost of one person's day.
