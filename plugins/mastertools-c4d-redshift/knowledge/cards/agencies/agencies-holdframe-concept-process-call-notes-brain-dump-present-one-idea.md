From a Holdframe workshop where a director walks through a conference title job for Semi-Permanent (speaker not named in the transcript). Step 1: call the client, ask many questions, write down everything, and reuse their repeated words in the concept [00:14][00:44]. Step 2: brain dump anything that comes to mind, even weak ideas; he wrote three general ones [01:46][02:16]. Step 3: choose the idea felt strongest and present only one, to focus energy and avoid a client picking the other option [02:48][03:19]. The choosing took longer than most jobs; a library book (Haeckel's biology illustrations) finally provided the key reference [03:19][03:50]. Step 4: mood boards for texture, colour, type and the science idea; add a narrative skeleton even for abstract pieces (follow a microorganism from birth to death as a metaphor for the theme) [04:22][04:54].

**Use it:** a pitch order the coach can recommend: listen, dump, pick one, build a metaphor spine.
