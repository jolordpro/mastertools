Order [11:58-14:34]: (1) settle the goal (invisible fix vs audience shift); (2) pick how broad round one is, because every later round cascades from it; (3) each later round is narrower and nerdier (weight, height, details). She hands working files to clients early to try in real use for utility fixes, which most studios refuse [13:33]. She is fine with mixing parts of options (menu of dishes) and with letting a client walk down a path they need to see, even if it proves the earlier option right [15:05-16:09]. Her own client rejected the late narrowed logo and the team widened again [14:34].
**Use it:** In a review plan, name what each round settles, and keep early options mixable so a late "no" can step back one round without a restart.
