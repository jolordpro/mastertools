Yann Dupont [37:35-45:49, 1:25:55-1:28:34, 1:38:58-1:40:30]: be yourself and kind; interviewers want a connection. He screens a reel for what the next 6 months of the show need (e.g. forest shots for Jungle Cruise), not a fixed list. Explain the shot's problem and how it was solved, even if a lead found the fix, instead of "I placed the light" [38:37-41:43]. Do not put a lighting breakdown on the reel; use a separate PDF with thumbnails and timecodes, and never take internal passes home, as that is theft [41:43-45:49]. References from friends carry little; legal checks CVs at big companies. Tenure and promotion within a company matter more, and many short jobs look bad [1:25:55-1:28:34]. Scripting is not a must, but helps decide between equal candidates [1:39:28-1:40:30]. Asked why you lack V-Ray/Redshift experience, answer that modern renderers are similar [1:22:50-1:25:25].
