Guest Walter Volpatto (feature colorist, Picture Shop; captions spell him "Valpado"), host Cullen Kelly. Kelly describes a client pushing saturation past where he liked it, and his own choice to say plainly that he did not think it looked good and would still follow if they wanted [1:31:30-1:32:33].
Volpatto's way to steer without cornering anyone: when the client's note is going too far, go further yourself, then say "I think I went too far, what do you think?". The mistake sits on you and the client gets room to reflect without being told they were wrong [1:32:33-1:33:34].
He says this is learned over years and no class teaches the subtlety of saying it so nobody is hurt [1:33:34-1:34:06].
Transfers: the same move works in a motion review when a director keeps adding glow, bloom or contrast: show the overshoot, then pull back with them.

**Use it:** Coach tip for client reviews: if a note takes the frame past good taste, render the overshoot, own it ("did I push this too far?") and let the client walk it back.
