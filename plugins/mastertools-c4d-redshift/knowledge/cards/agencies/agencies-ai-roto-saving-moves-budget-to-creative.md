Sarofsky [47:13-49:17]: the fastest wins are small procedural jobs, such as selecting a face or hand, generative fill and expand canvas in Photoshop, Topaz upres and frame-rate change, and AI roto stacked in Comfy.
Her budget view: money not spent on roto goes to what an artist does creatively, so the job gets more time on the idea. Summers wants more of the money on screen: get through the 'no, not like that' rounds faster, then spend three weeks instead of one on the 'yes'.

**Use it:** a job plan can list which procedural tasks are candidates for AI and re-allocate the saved days to look and polish.
