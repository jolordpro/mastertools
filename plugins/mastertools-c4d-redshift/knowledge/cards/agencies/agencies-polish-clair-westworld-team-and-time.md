Design development took about five weeks, longer than usual, with a design team that swelled to seven or eight [§8]. Three artists then built the final sequence in a room for an obscenely long stretch [§8]. Clair says more time with the creators than on any other show; he credits the creators' willingness to talk by phone or in the room as what produces good main titles [§3, §6]. After Elastic presented its first aesthetic, co-creator Jonathan Nolan pitched more emotionally challenging ideas than Clair would have pitched [§4].

**Use it:** Use as a benchmark: a prestige title can get five weeks of exploration and direct creator access.
