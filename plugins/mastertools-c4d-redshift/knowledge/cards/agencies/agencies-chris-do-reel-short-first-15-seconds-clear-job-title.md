Put your name and what you do in plain industry words (texture artist, lighting, compositor), not "visual communication expert" [53:45]. Profile the people who decide, give 10-30 minutes to find the right contact (a producer, not the info address) and follow the posting's instructions [54:18][57:56]. Focus on the roughly 20% of studios you suit (Pareto) [54:49]. Both men say persistence without aggression works [55:49]; the host: a personal note that shows you looked at their work gets read [56:52].
