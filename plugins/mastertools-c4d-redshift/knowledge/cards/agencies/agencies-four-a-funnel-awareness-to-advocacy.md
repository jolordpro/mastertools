Awareness posts speak to industry trends, attraction posts show process and behind-the-scenes to the ideal client, action posts are the availability call, advocacy turns past clients into referrers and testimonials [14:38-18:46].
In a CRM, put targets you want in awareness, move them by comments and chats, then to action; past clients sit in advocacy [40:05-41:40].
A thanks email with no ask warms a brand [47:24-48:27]; asking a past client for a referral doubles as a re-engagement [50:29-51:00]. Batch posts and put the batch in the calendar [30:14-31:14].

**Use it:** a plain checklist for keeping a job pipeline warm without begging for work.
