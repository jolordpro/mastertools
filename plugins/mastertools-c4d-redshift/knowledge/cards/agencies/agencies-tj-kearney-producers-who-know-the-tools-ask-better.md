TJ came up through editing, animating, Flame and post, and says the hands-on time lets him judge how hard a request is [11:28, 11:59]. He says producers raised only in agencies are rarely taught to push back, since overtime is available [12:32, 13:02]. Exec producer vs producer: the EP works sales, contracts and the MSA/SOW and watches the client trajectory; the producer runs the project day to day [14:02]. An MSA is the umbrella contract; an SOW is per project and sets timeline, deliverables and process [15:35, 16:07].

**Use it:** Terms for the glossary; also supports asking a human what a request really costs before agreeing to it.
