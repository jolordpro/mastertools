Every project starts with references to set the goal and a video sample so the client sees the visual identity [00:01]. They moved from Illustrator with Overlord to Figma with the aeux plugin, which pushes SVG into After Effects, because they do more tech-client UI work [00:32-01:06]. They moved from Cinema 4D to Blender for efficiency, export short 3D assets on transparent background and finish speed and timing in After Effects; about four or five 3-second 3D bits in a 90-second explainer [01:06-02:10]. Revisions are always expected, so clients get Frame.io links to timestamp and annotate [02:10-02:42]. Project files and renders go to a shared drive at the end [02:42].
