Staff get DigitalTutors and CMIVFX access plus in-house training; interns work on commercial jobs immediately within their ability, and most former interns return as freelancers. Beta testing for many products keeps the team current, and staff attend FMX and Siggraph [§4-5]. The studio also supports working hours with flexible core time and compensates for late nights or weekends [§4]. Every employee can supervise a project from start to finish [§4].

**Use it:** Give juniors real, bounded work early; it builds the freelancer pool.
