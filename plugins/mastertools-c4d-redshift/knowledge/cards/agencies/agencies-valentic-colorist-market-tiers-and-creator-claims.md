Guest: Dado Valentic (vendor of AI colour tools, so a biased forecast, from 2023). He calls the industry a pyramid [37:25-38:24].
Top end: blockbuster colourists become more in demand because AI-assisted work lifts what they can do [37:53].
Middle: reality and cooking shows and sitcoms. His claim is that final grading can be mostly automated because AI is more precise than a human at balancing; he cites one episode of a reality show with about 2,000 shots on ten cameras in a single day, which he calls cover-up work, not a job. AI frees time for finesse [38:54-41:00].
Low end: he cites a New York investment report of over 5 million full-time creators who want a "magic button" and will drive software development. He advises not looking down on them, since they can pay colourists well [41:29-43:35, 48:44-49:50].
Two paths for a working colourist: build a secret-sauce look nobody can reproduce, or serve the volume market. Remote grading with a decent monitor on both ends is now accepted [44:37-46:09].

**Use it:** Treat as one vendor's forecast. The relevant steer: balance and match work gets cheap, look design and taste stay the paid part.
