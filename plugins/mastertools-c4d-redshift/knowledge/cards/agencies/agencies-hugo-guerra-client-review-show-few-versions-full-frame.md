Hugo Guerra: load every version, then choose only those that change visibly; a 0.05 percent luminance change just confuses a client, who, in his words, are like parents who do not know the craft [28:30-29:01].
Show full frame only, never Nuke, the timeline or node trees. Clients care about the picture, not the gizmo [29:32-30:03]. A real session was shown sped up 1000 times to show how slow they are; they can take hours [29:32].
Treat them as an audience. On remote calls, a decent webcam or more light helps, since the face is how you present yourself [32:08-34:13]. He streams either desktop or the video-out signal in full screen with himself in a corner [34:44].

**Use it:** Review prep: pick 2-3 clearly different options per shot, and play full-frame.
