Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer) led a roughly two-minute promo with two other animators in about two weeks; he animated a first chunk himself and passed that file so the others matched the feel [03:19-07:58].
The designer's frames were all shared early, and each animator was asked which frames excited them, because giving people work they want raises energy [08:29-09:33].
With little time for revision rounds the team built a board animatic in Premiere with the voiceover and got client approval of timing before animating [09:33-10:04].
He also designed a small set of custom hand-painted transitions once so every animator could reuse them [06:55-07:26].

**Use it:** Hand-off pattern for a multi-animator job: lead sets the feel in one chunk, animatic locks timing, shared transition kit.
