Casting starts before the script is locked, using a marketplace or past voice artists for custom auditions after client casting specs (gender, tone, delivery) [56:57-57:29].
If the final read is not back, the team times with a scratch track; the final VO arrived after style frames on the big job, and animatic round two used it [57:29-58:30].
Their sound designer joined early, by animation round one, as a cherry on top that supports key moments [1:04:43-1:05:43].

**Use it:** put a sound pass early on the review timeline; it also locks timing before animation.
