Tim Bolland (lead designer, Man vs Machine London) [04:16-05:27]: the studio works as a directorship; everyone can propose concepts, storyboards and animatics. The Bang & Olufsen treatment: overview, narrative structure, art direction with colour and materiality references, lighting references, one idea per product (folding and twin synchronicity for the TV, uninterrupted flow for the soundbar, physical tactility for headphones) and materials from two sources, real plus a fictional hyper-real pair [09:41-14:07]. References are labelled as not the studio's work; the treatment sells the idea internally and to the client [09:10]. Audio designer receives early arrangements and animatics and bounces ideas back [14:40]. R&D: a dailies folder where each artist posts every day; treatments get updated with new references and reels built from the dailies; odd fragments sometimes survive to the final film [16:19-17:30]. Treatment themes can change after client feedback [18:01]. Animatics run in parallel and keep changing [19:12].
