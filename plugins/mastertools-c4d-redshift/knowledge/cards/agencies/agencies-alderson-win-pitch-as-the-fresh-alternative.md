Pitch rule given: boil the pitch down to a palatable thing and strip the filler. When another studio has done the last three versions of the job, hunt for small percentage differences that make the studio a refreshing choice. He admits pitches are not purely a creativity contest. The studio stays small on purpose because the underdog role makes it easier to get up for a pitch, and it presents as one collective, not named directors. [§21-§22, §28]

**Use it:** In a pitch pack, cut to the single thread, and ask who the incumbent is before choosing the angle.
