Do: anchoring means the first number heard sticks. If your price keeps drawing "too high", say it first as a bracket, high figure leading (his example: a logo "between ten thousand and six thousand", or six to ten thousand) so they negotiate down from your number [04:16-07:22]. If you ask for their budget first and they say two thousand, you end up pleading from 2,500 up to 3,500. For a big client where you do not know the scale, run discovery, ask many questions and let them state the budget first, because a large client's number will beat yours [06:51-07:22]. He says both schools work, depending on where you are. Figures are his illustration, not a market rate.
**Use it:** In estimator copy and bids, show a bracket with the higher figure first; use the budget-first route only when the client is far larger than your usual.
