They treat sound as the other half or more of a piece. The composer, Ambrose Yu, joined once the script was locked to help find the voice and establish the music. With few ideas for music, they picked a reference track as a mood direction, then went back and forth with the composer so music and visuals matched as closely as possible [§4-5].

**Use it:** Book sound at script lock, not after picture lock; send a mood reference track and iterate both sides together.
