The studio wants a finite timeline and a clear problem, then solves it by making and trying things, closer to sculpting than to the write, storyboard, hand-to-animators line. Even a senior 3D artist is invited to the brainstorm, because a tool built in spare time can become the concept. The first hire was a generalist with an eye. For years there were no job titles and no promotion: the work was the marketing. [§16, §18-§19]

**Use it:** Before a brief starts, name the deadline and the one problem, then prototype in the real tool early instead of boarding everything first.
