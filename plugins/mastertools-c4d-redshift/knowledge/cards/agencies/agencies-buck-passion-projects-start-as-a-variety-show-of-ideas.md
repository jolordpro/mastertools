Because clients rarely fund experimentation, Buck started self-funded passion projects [15:33-16:06]. The 2014 F5 opener used a "variety show" approach: four New York creative directors each pitched techniques and styles they wanted to play with, and a script was built from those cores [16:06-16:38]. The result mixed puppets, stylised live action and several 2D styles; sets were made at the office ping-pong table, and every frame of one sequence was hand painted [23:43-24:16]. The talk notes that clients then see such work on the site and ask for the approach, as with the Onward Internet spot [24:16-24:47].
