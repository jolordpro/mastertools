Denny's rule from years in marketing teams: "I don't like it" takes no skill and is not a note. He wants the reason, for example the work is off brief, or it needs a live shoot [39:31-40:01].
His first tone-setting move at IF was to beat up the problem in the middle of the table, not the person [14:49, 19:05-20:41]. Chip says that felt scary at first because naming a problem sounds like blame, and it has to be repeated many times a day before people relax [21:13].
Word choice counts: he says to call extra work a change order, not an overage, and to treat a review as a conversation, not a set of notes [40:33].

**Use it:** Write notes as: what is wrong, why it misses the brief, optional fix. Say 'the shot' or 'the brief', not 'you'.
