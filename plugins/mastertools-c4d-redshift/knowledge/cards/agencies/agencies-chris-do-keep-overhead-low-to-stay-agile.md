His top tip for someone starting a studio in 2017: skip the fancy office, work from home or a cheap loft, and keep the runway long [36:37][37:10]. If you live month to month, every decision gets driven by rent and you accept risky jobs you know are wrong and cannot leave [37:41][38:12]. A three to four person studio billing near a million a year is still workable; growing to $4-5M puts you in competition with your own clients [36:07]. His belief: agility lets you invest when a new opportunity shows up [37:41]. Also: do not spend $18 of every $20 on looking good [38:44].

**Use it:** a costing check for a small studio or a freelancer's decision on when to say no.
