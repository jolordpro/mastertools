Clair says he will never tire of main titles but cannot do only that; he wants a little corporate work to keep him honest when his brain is tired, plus self-initiated work [1:51:26-1:51:57].
He notes that many title designers he came up with had done a stint of quick-turnaround broadcast work: it did not have to look cool, only be functional, and that was good discipline [1:52:30-1:53:00].
The host (Ash Thorp) adds that client work keeps one sharp because people work harder for a stranger than for themselves [1:52:00].
Transfers: broadcast graphics turn-arounds are a legitimate training ground.

**Use it:** When planning an artist's mix of work, keep some fast functional jobs in the rotation; they build speed that bigger pieces then use.
