Yann Dupont: at least a third of his time is meetings of more than two people. A daily morning sync with leads, supervisor and production goes through yesterday's list of about 10 points and what is done, taking an hour or more [14:35-16:07]. Afterwards he notes his items and asks each owner whether it needs time or something blocks them, acting as "the sweeper removing sand from the mechanism" [16:40-17:43]. On set (rare for a CG supervisor) he does not tell the director what to do but asks whether something such as constant camera shake is intentional, since it costs later; production may overrule [04:44-06:18]. He tries not to step on artists who know their field better, nor on the VFX supervisor who holds the final word on look [02:10-02:40]. He keeps a few non-urgent shots to understand what artists can do and which department to ask [12:00-13:34].

**Use it:** a coach for studio leads: daily list, blocker questions, cost-aware questions to directors.
