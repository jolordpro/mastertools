Denny (IF CEO, ex-CMO) says a review that that opens by asking what people think invites everyone in the chain to give notes, even when the work already meets the brief [1:31:05-1:33:11].
He wants the creative process built on getting the one person who must approve it, not on every approval in the middle, and says extra feedback rounds bloat review and cost money [1:32:08-1:32:39].
If a question is needed, make it specific: is anything legally wrong, did we miss part of the brief. Asking if anyone has questions after a long session adds nothing [1:33:11].
The host adds that a meeting labelled a review makes people feel they must give an answer, even a poor one [1:34:14].

**Use it:** Before sending a cut, write what it achieves and what you need back. Ask a named person a named question instead of a general 'thoughts?'.
