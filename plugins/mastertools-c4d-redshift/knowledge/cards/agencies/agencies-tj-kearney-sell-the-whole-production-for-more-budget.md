Studios that only do design and animation leave money and control on the table. TJ's version: hire the writer, book the VO talent online, add a markup on each, and you control the creative [44:42]. VO is cheap to book and easy to mark up [45:13]. When the client keeps some tasks, it pads its own rates and offers the studio less; when one vendor takes everything, the client passes on 90% or more of the budget and keeps a small safety amount [45:43]. This also lowers the client's felt risk [46:16].

**Use it:** Quote the extras as line items the studio owns, and say what the client is not carrying.
