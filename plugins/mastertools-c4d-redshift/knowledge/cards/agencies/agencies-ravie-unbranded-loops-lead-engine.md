Austin Bauwens (co-founder, Ravie) says the studio was at a low after year one: chasing clients that would make the portfolio look good gave bad client experiences and boring corporate work [22:38-23:41]. He then posted a colourful grid loop made for fun, with no logo and no client. It got about 1,500 views against the usual couple of hundred on about 700 followers [24:13].
Followers went about 700 to 10,000 in a month and about 28,000 a month after, with 150-200k views per post for a month [26:48-27:19]. Leads came from agencies and direct brands, including a real-estate giant, not just other studios [25:46].
Revenue as he states it: about $7k Aug-Dec 2020, about $100k in 2021 (before the social push), about $500k in 2022. Average deal rose from under $10k to about $30k, mostly short explainers [27:49-28:53]. Instagram's push faded around 25k followers; the team was building a network so it would not depend on it [30:27].
