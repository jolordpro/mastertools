Korenman says the hardest thing was giving feedback other than "holy crap, keep going" because the design and animation were so good. One alumni call-out section was not working, and it took several rounds; he felt guilty but kept pushing because the piece had a specific job [§7]. He also chose to let Ordinary Folk do their thing: too many cooks water down projects, and when the studio said it would look cool he had to trust a vision clearer than his own [§7-8].
