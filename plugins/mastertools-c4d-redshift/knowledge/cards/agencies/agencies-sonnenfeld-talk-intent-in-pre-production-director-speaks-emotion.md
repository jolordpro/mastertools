Guest: Stefan Sonnenfeld. The work begins in planning: talk intentions, workflow and cost, then set LUTs and colour profiles so the on-set image is close to, or knowingly different from, the final intent. In the grade the image is then pushed as the DP intended, or sometimes exactly the opposite [03:46-05:20].
Directors speak in different languages: some technical, some like Michael Mann in emotion (what a character is feeling, what just happened). The colourist interprets that into colour quickly because money and effort ride on it; the director says "that's it" or "no, it should be this" [05:20-06:24].
He says the nuance of this is why text-style prompting only gets part of the way [02:43-03:13].

**Use it:** In a brief review, ask for the feeling and the story beat, then answer with a look; do the translation quickly and show it.
