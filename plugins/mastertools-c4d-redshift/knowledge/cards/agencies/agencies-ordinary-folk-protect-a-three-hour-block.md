Jorge Canedo Estrada says direction, feedback and clients take around 60% of his day. He aims for at least a three-hour block of pure animation, accepts some days give none, and the studio only gets six-hour flow runs mid-project when they are saying no to everything else [§21].
Greg Stewart took from the book Deep Work that even half an hour with no interruption beats scattered time: he checks email once, mutes notifications for a couple of hours, or quits Slack for 30 minutes [§21-22]. Victor Silva varies it by task: music or a podcast on for gap-filling work, full focus for expressions [§20].

**Use it:** Put polish time in a named block with notifications off, and plan director days as mostly notes.
