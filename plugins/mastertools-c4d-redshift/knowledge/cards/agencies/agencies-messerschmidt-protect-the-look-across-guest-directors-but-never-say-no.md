Messerschmidt says an episodic DP has an obligation to protect the show's aesthetic as one unit, while directors must bring their own sensibility; his rule from day one with Fincher was never to say no to a director, but to use what was done before [1:10:03-1:11:36]. Directors should also look forward in the script so what they set up works later [1:11:05-1:11:36].
Gonzalez recalls a tone meeting where Fincher told every department head they were responsible for the look: if a costume, extra or prop looked wrong, they should tell each other and fix it [1:12:07-1:13:09].

**Use it:** A look guide plus a shared sense that anyone may flag an off-look element keeps a multi-artist package consistent without a single gatekeeper.
