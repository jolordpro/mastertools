Alderson says much of the studio's work starts as something made by accident or left on a shelf from another project, then the concept is written so it reads as if it came before the visual. First-time clients are not told, since they want a clean idea. Repeat clients are let in on the play and shown the failures too. He would teach this if he ever taught. [§19-§20]

**Use it:** When a strong frame exists before the idea, write the rationale tightly against the brief; do not pretend the order, but do not lead with it either.
