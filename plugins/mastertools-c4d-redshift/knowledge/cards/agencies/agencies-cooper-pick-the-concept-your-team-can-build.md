Cooper [19:53-22:00] describes starting from the people on hand (an illustrator, someone good with Smoke, one editor) and finding an idea that makes the most of them, rather than demanding an ideal crew. He uses a Henry V image ("unspotted soldiers"): every team member has flaws, so choose an idea that does not need four specialists you cannot get. He contrasts this with early years where a producer said the budget only allowed cards and cross dissolves.

**Use it:** at pitch, scope the concept to the artists and tools actually booked; add a risk note where it needs a specialist you do not have.
