Guest: Karin Fong. Her mentor Kyle Cooper said "iron sharpens iron": good people sharpen those next to them. Late at night it is almost done, but one thing bothers everyone; they ask "should we change that?" and the answer is yes [11:38-12:45].
She runs three or four projects at once, mixing staff and freelancers (Houdini, Cinema 4D, After Effects, editors, live-action crews) and remote people across time zones [09:04-11:08].

**Use it:** Final-pass ritual: before sign-off, ask the team to name the one thing still bothering them, and fix it if time allows.
