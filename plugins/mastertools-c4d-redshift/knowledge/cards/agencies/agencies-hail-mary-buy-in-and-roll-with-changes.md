The team sat under the production designer, then under VFX and DP, acting as "creative glue" between departments, and kept early exploration from running off to VFX before the director committed [07:46].
Zaman's lesson: get heads-of-department buy-in, then work out what they want rather than enforcing strict rules [52:22]. Ling's: move with production, even changing direction minutes before shooting, which gave the creatives flexibility [53:54].
Postviz went through several versions and early screenings to check that hard sci-fi beats landed [47:44]; the creature's postviz concentrated on how to show emotion with no face [46:09].
Two deliveries of rushes per day on set (morning shoot, afternoon shoot) went to editorial, about 2,000 files and 60 hours of footage [39:53, 40:24].

**Use it:** State who reports where and who approves; run short review loops early so beats are tested before expensive rendering.
