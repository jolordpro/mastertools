Honey says nothing is typical any more. For an opportunity, a committee weighs pros and cons. If Buck takes it on, or pitches a concept, it picks the fitting creative director and builds a bespoke team around that person. At the time of the interview: about 80 client projects and about 10 internal projects at once across the offices. [§1-§2]

**Use it:** Use a short pros-and-cons review before accepting a job, then name one lead before staffing.
