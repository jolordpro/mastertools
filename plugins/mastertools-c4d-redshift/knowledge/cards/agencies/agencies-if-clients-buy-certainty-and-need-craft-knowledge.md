Clients with complex business problems want to know an idea can work, can win over stakeholders, and has limited risk, not creativity alone [25:15-25:55].
His own advantage as a marketer: he understood creative production from start to finish, including versioning, rendering and cost, so he did not have to take others' word on what it takes [1:21:39-1:22:09]. He says many briefs fail because the strategy is wrong, then creative gets blamed [1:22:09-1:23:11].
Also: ideas come from experience and process; a process should be repeatable without feeling like a copy machine [27:27-27:58].

**Use it:** In a bid, state the risk-reducing evidence (test, comp, proof frame) and explain cost drivers in plain terms.
