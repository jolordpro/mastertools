Chris Do says cash-rich tech firms pay in-house artists far more than studios can; they pay the agency spread otherwise, so direct hire saves them money [32:56]. He puts the spread between what clients pay and what the artist earns at roughly $500 to $3,000 a day [33:00]. Interns elsewhere reportedly got $75,000 a year (hearsay) [35:04]. Keeping launches secret is a second reason to hire in house [34:04]. The drawback is you only do one brand's work. His advice to studios: stay agile, try opportunities without over-committing [34:35]; staff stay for variety and culture, not money [35:04]. For freelancers he calls in-house at a tech firm the best financial route at that time [41:20].

**Use it:** background for rate talk and for why senior artists may be unavailable to a studio.
