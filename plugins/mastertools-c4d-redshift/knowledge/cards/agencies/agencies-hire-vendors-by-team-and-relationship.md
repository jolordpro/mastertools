Chris Maclean (VFX supervisor and second unit director, Foundation S2): work is allocated by what each team is good at. His rule is not to hire vendors on their name but on the team, and to have a working relationship with the vendor's supervisor so that when delivery gets hairy he can trust them to take it home [06:22-06:53]. Season one needed more vendors because of COVID attrition; season two used fewer [04:47-05:20]. He says the production gives VFX a seat at the table and trusts them [02:40].

**Use it:** A producer-side check when picking a freelancer or studio: ask who is on the team, and talk to the lead before the deadline pressure starts.
