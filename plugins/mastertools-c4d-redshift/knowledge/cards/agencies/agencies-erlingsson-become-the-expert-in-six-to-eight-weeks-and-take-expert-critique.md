On a Netflix kung fu training montage Golden Wolf did for a CG feature [09:03-10:38], the directors had a kung fu expert who had worked on many films critique the animation. Erlingsson says animators who do not do kung fu must act as the person they animate, and with a six to eight week commercial timeline they must become experts in the subject and then move on. He also notes that a typical job has dozens of people and a dozen software packages, so "what software" is not a useful question [10:43-11:42].

**Use it:** when a shot depends on real-world expertise (sport technique, for example), book an expert review before the final animation pass.
