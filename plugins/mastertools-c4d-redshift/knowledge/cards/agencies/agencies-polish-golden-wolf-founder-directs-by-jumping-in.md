The founder, at the studio since day one, says the clearest and most fun way to direct is sometimes to dive in and do the work, such as a simple draw-over, a mood edit or a quick storyboard [§2]. When a creative clash arises the studio avoids pettiness and offers another chance for an idea later [§2]. To recharge, the team starts a fresh brief, since change is as good as a break [§3].

**Use it:** When feedback is hard to put into words, suggest the director show it with a draw-over or a mood edit.
