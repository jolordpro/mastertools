On a first job the studio asks the client about the people above them: their personality, whether they come from a creative background, and whether they read on a phone in a cab or book an hour every two weeks. The answer sets the deck. Alderson's example: the client's own deck gets distilled to its best five or six pages and the studio aims to hand over a six to 10 page deck. With repeat clients the studio is open about experiments and mistakes as well as wins, and keeps the client on its side against the real target, the senior person who signs off.

**Use it:** Keep review packs short: five or six frames, ordered for the person who has to approve upward.
