In 2020 he said the old chain of brand, agency, rep, studio changed about four years earlier as brands built their own teams and came straight to Buck with no pitch and no director pitted against another, giving more freedom and trust [26:10-27:13, video 1]. In 2026 he says about 90 percent of Buck's work is direct to brand and agencies come only when the creative opportunity is great; in-house teams want to work with the makers, not idea people who hire others [21:45-22:45, video 2]. He expects maker companies to resurge over three to four years [38:49-39:52, video 2]. Trust varies by person: some clients check in daily, others trust after three or four jobs, and today there is more back and forth, not 'do this' [51:03-52:37, video 1].
