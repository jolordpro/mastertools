Her stated approach: skip baby steps and put a gifted artist on the demanding part of the job, because people rise to it when a deadline is real and tutorials are no longer enough [26:30-27:32]. She says she is still learning the same way. The reasoning: do not leave an intern or short-term hire on the bench; get the most out of their time, and give people the privilege of failing sometimes [28:03-28:33]. A mentor who lets go is the model she describes.
Her own estimate of her role: ideas and direction, not craft; Cooper moved her off animating her own work to lead the project and keep it consistent [18:08-18:39].

**Use it:** Hand a stretch task, a real shot rather than a practice one, and review in dailies. Single-source opinion, so treat as a team habit, not a standard.
