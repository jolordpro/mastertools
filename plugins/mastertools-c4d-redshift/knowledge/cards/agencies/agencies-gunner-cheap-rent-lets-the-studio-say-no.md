In a 2018 Q&A the founders (not told apart in the text) say Detroit's low rent, about 1,600 dollars a month for 1,600 sq ft, makes it easier to decline unappealing jobs.
They want work that delights the eye and pays off emotionally, and are drawn to longer narratives, film titles, shorts and shiny 3D. They balance time, quality and money on each job.
