Project stories needed the creative leads, not one person, so artists built a Figma prototype with key examples and a list of modules to tell their own story; at peak over 40 creatives edited at once, asking for help and feedback [12:19-12:50]. Artists also built a 100-page guideline (even sonic rules, all in one key), toolkits, pitch decks, contracts, bids, a social toolkit that snaps content to a grid, and a physical 'rotator' wheel that scrubs an animation playhead [12:50-14:55]. Process for the rebrand: curiosity-led, many fingerprints, a blank-page attempt that was rejected for losing roots (named after Buckminster Fuller; system of shapes in the logo) [07:08-08:12].

**Use it:** a pattern for studio-wide work: give contributors a shared live prototype and clear lanes, not a finished document to approve.
