Walter Volpatto (feature colorist, Picture Shop; captions spell him "Valpado") wants the client to make the final choice but to be steered toward his pick; he phrases the favourite as the option the client may prefer, which frames it as their choice [44:13-45:13].
He says it takes years and he is not close to mastering it, and that the goal is the client feeling their vision is on screen, not just receiving a result [06:12-06:43, 45:13-45:44].
He also sees clients as creatures of habit who go back to a facility that worked before [07:14-08:48].

**Use it:** Review script: present a recommended option as the lead choice, not a menu of equals. Low-risk coaching line, a softer form of persuasion.
