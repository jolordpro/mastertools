Big brands bring bureaucracy and late changes of direction, and griping about the client is the easy option. The studio treats the immediate client as a teammate and the real audience as the senior approver. Alderson's line is that work only gets out by impressing the person in the ivory tower who green-lights it. [§20]

**Use it:** Phrase internal notes as how to help the contact look good upward, and keep venting out of shared review channels.
