Gary Hartley (EVP, creative director) [02:06 to 02:36]: the theory was 'what if we took away all the levers', then iterate over coming seasons; there was negative feedback and push back.
Third New Orleans Super Bowl; Bourbon Street was central; a Lady Gaga shoot pushed things back 3 days so ops worked 24-hour shifts [01:05 to 01:35].

Hartley calls it a first swing to develop over the next seasons; the AR opener drove the creative (motion leaders study).

**Use it:** a precedent for planning a first-year package as version one with a multi-season plan.
