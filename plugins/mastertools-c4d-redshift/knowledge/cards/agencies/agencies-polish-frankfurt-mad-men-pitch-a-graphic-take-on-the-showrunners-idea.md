Matthew Weiner described the open as a man entering an office, setting down his suitcase and jumping from the window, as if shot on film [§3]. Imaginary Forces pitched the same idea as a graphic sequence, a man's life disintegrating as he falls through late 1950s and early 60s adverts, in a Saul Bass-inspired style [§3]. The final silhouette on the sofa was first placed earlier in the board; Weiner's reaction made it the closing image [§3].

**Use it:** When pitching against a stated idea, keep the idea and change the medium; then watch which frame the client latches onto.
