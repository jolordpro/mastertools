The studio had eight people. Each person has a strength but is a generalist, with equal voice [01:31-02:02]. No one is non-creative: they do project management and client dealing themselves, which keeps them close to the source so no stones are unturned [02:02-02:32].
They say few studios do both strong design (logos, typefaces) and animation or 3D without bringing outsiders [03:04]. Names of the speakers are garbled in the captions; they are the studio's two founders.

**Use it:** For a small team, keep one person from the creative side on every client call.
