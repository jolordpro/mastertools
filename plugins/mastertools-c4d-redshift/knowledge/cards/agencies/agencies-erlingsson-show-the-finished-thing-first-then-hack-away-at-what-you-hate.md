He dislikes storyboards as a ritual that exists because clients do not trust creatives [1:28:27-1:29:28]. At Systems the first thing the client sees is a finished video, then they say what they hate, and controls are added from there [1:30:29-1:31:00]. He argues making the video is now quicker than finding references [1:31:00]. Idea: use it to make a pilot that persuades a studio to fund the two-year version [1:32:03-1:33:06].

**Use it:** Where tools allow, replace boards with a rough finished pass and take the first notes on that.
