Among the small habits she took from Cooper was never meeting a client without being ready to talk about something or show something, even if the meeting is only preliminary [19:42]. Her own phrase for his teaching on time: do the best you can in the time you have and don't stop until it is up.

**Use it:** Even an early check-in gets one frame, a rough cut or a sketch on the table. Treat the last hour of a deadline as working time, not waiting time.
