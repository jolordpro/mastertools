Justin Mitchell: early television work with day-turnaround shots taught him tracking, lighting and compositing, which now help him talk to artists and know where to "lie, cheat and steal" cheaply without losing the shot [08:17-08:48]. Specialists suit big pipelines but struggle outside their tools and slow work by passing between departments [09:20-10:22]. As supervisor he communicates the filmmaker's vision (not his own) and lets artists have influence, since nobody wants to be "pushing pixels" [32:36-33:08].

**Use it:** for fast-turn broadcast packages, favour a generalist who can cover model, light and comp over a hand-off chain.
