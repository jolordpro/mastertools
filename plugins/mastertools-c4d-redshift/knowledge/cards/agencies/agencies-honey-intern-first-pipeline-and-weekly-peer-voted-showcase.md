Interns get reviewed at intervals; after six months Buck decides to extend six more months or offer a job, and he says it hires about 95 percent of its interns [54:12-54:44]. A showcase called Buckyback: artists tag work on Instagram with the hashtag, it is collected on a site where staff vote weekly, the top six are featured and the top one goes on Buck's account; they hired maybe 10 to 15 people that way [36:09-37:43]. He accepts LinkedIn requests only if the person has a good portfolio [35:39]. Remote interns were accepted in 2020 but in-office preferred [1:00:57-1:02:34].

**Use it:** A voted public showcase is a low-cost way to find junior talent; use it as a model for a studio pipeline.
