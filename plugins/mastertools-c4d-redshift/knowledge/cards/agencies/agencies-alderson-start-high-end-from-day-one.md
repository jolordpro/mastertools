Alderson and Tim Swift judged the start-mid-and-climb path a trap: TV promos now do not lead to network rebrands later. They held out for one high-concept job, won a Channel 4 channel rebrand pitch, then partnered as motion arm with a small brand studio on a Syfy rebrand. They also preferred working direct to clients, because agencies look for people who have already done the pre-written idea best. [§17-§18]

**Use it:** When choosing which work enters the reel or the plan, pick for the level wanted in a few years, not the work on offer today.
