The process: showrunner brief, then collect reference and inspiration, sketch, and long conversations [05:48-06:19]. He and Raoul Marks talk daily by voice, sharing screens to point at things, look up references and watch video; video is distracting [06:19-06:50].
He says talented collaborators misread what you mean in ways that improve it, so each person adds to the creative space [06:50-07:23].

**Use it:** For remote review, share the screen and talk; ask collaborators to reinterpret the reference instead of copying it.
