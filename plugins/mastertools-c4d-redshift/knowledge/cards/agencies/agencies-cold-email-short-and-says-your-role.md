Nidia Dias (3D art director, ex-Future Deluxe, Territory, Man vs Machine; host Hayley Akins): studios get these constantly; a copy-paste mail is cold, one that says what you like about their work is not [16:11-16:41].
Keep it short, make the ask clear (freelance database, full-time, internship) [16:41-17:13].
A talent manager at a studio told her she needs to know if the person is an art director, motion designer or something else because she cannot sort otherwise, so state your role and link a reel [17:44-18:48].

**Use it:** Pitching note: lead with role, one line of personal praise, link.
