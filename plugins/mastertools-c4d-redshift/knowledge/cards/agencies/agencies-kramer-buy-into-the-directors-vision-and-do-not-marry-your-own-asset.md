Kramer says a project is worse off if every creative makes their own decisions; buying into one vision creates the continuity [1:21:38-1:22:38]. He describes making plenty of work that never reaches the screen and not letting that discourage him; the test is what is right for the job, not for the artist [1:19:27-1:19:59]. Once a plan is set, voicing 'this will never work' adds no value; everyone in the industry does impossible jobs [1:18:26-1:20:29].
The Blender Guru host notes that making your own assets makes you attached, whereas a pack lets you swap quickly [1:20:39-1:21:38, host]. Kramer also suggests auditing which tasks you are weakest at and handing them to a specialist [1:22:38-1:23:40], and says using a bought model does not make the work less yours [1:27:17].

**Use it:** Treat a note that kills a favourite element as a swap, keep spare options ready, and hand off the task you are weakest at.
