Do's order [42:57-45:31]: the client must recognise the problem before you offer a fix. Start with a question about an opportunity they may have missed; let them voice the assumed cost and time (his example: "six figures, six months" for an app); then say it need not be so, and offer an hour to talk it through. If they are not interested, stop, because you would be selling something nobody wants. This avoids spending days on a proposal or pitch.
**Use it:** Use as the opening for any speculative idea to a network or client: a question first, and a clear stop rule before any proposal work.
