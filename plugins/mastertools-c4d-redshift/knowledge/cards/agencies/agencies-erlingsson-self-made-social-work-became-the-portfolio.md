Briefs arrived asking for portfolios full of work they did not want to do, so they made GIFs for Tumblr, then posted original work every few days on Instagram [05:43-07:49]. Interns' first job was making content for social, and some of the studio's best-known pieces were intern work; the pipeline included strong interns from schools like Gobelins [07:49-08:21]. They did not worry whether the last piece matched the next one; the reputation made agencies call as the 'up and coming' name, while competing against 100-person studios with about ten people [08:21-08:53].

**Use it:** Post the work you want next; the portfolio gets built by what is public.
