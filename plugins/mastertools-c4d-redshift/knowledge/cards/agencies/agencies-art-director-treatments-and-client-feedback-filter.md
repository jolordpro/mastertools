Nidia Dias (3D art director, ex-Future Deluxe, Territory, Man vs Machine; host Hayley Akins) says as projects start rolling she spends more time giving feedback and checking that everyone does what they should than designing [07:24-08:21].
She builds treatments so client and studio agree on the look, keeps a steady back and forth instead of weeks of silence, and avoids flooding the client with too much [08:21-08:52].
She filters client notes so the team gets a sorted list and she takes the hits herself [08:52-09:23].
Roles: a creative director oversees several projects and talks to clients; an art director stays on one project from start to end, and titles blur in small studios [14:04-15:40].

**Use it:** Review flow: one person turns client notes into a single deduped list before anyone works.
