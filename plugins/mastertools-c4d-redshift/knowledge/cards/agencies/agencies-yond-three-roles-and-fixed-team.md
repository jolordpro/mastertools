Roles: EP handles clients and schedule, CD owns the look, lead animator is the technical enabler [00:30-01:32, 18:02-19:06].
The animator joins fairly early after storyboards and script to ask what it takes in After Effects, and stays out of the creative analysis [18:02-18:36].
For scale they hire freelancers but one of the three touches everything for consistent quality and process, so it reads as a Yond product [07:14-07:46]. A fixed team also carries process notes into the next job and gives the client one studio face [1:09:20-1:09:51].
