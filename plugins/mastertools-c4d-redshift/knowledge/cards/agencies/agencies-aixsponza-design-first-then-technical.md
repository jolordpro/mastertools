The studio calls itself a design and production house, not a pure VFX shop: it takes the concept phase, design and direction as well as CG and post, and says creation and production in one company gives stronger results than the classic split [§3]. Its basic idea since 2006 was to combine a high level of design with sophisticated technical realisation [§1]. Permanent staff are generalists who master design and technology; specialists such as riggers, sculptors and fluid artists come in as freelancers [§4]. Applicants should first be good designers and at least as good at technical 3D; criticising and accepting criticism are both welcome [§4].
