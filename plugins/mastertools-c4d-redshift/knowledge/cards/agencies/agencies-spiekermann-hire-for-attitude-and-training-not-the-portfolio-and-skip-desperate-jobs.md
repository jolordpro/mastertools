Fakes in portfolios (his own work in others' books) made him ask: do I want this person round for ten hours a day for years [36:35]. He worked with people with no design training, even carpenters, butchers and chefs, and says they were the most successful [36:35-37:07].
On clients: he took jobs when too eager or desperate, with the promise of press coverage as a rubbish promise; those jobs lost money and reputation and brought fights with the studio, where nobody wanted to work on them [35:34-36:35].

**Use it:** In a hiring or client screen, ask the culture question explicitly, and say no to the job taken only for need.
