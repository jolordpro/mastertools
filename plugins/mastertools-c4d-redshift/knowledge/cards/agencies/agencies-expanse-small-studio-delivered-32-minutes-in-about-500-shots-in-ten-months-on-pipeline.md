Fredrik Fotland (CEO, owner, VFX supervisor, Expanse). Norway has no strong film incentives and a small box office, yet clients expect high quality on time, so when the studio started ten years ago it treated pipeline as the only way a small studio delivers, not a nice-to-have [20:04-20:40]. The live-action/animated Christmas film was produced January to October and released in November 2025; Expanse was sole animation vendor with 32 minutes of animated mice in nearly 500 shots, including snow, smoke, lasers, liquid and demolition [20:40-24:30]. Tool principles: tools should just work across Houdini, Maya, Nuke and Flow; they can be extended per project; artists should create, not fight the pipeline [25:07-25:38]. The studio is set to scale with freelancers [27:05]. Frequent publishes for review keep producers and supervisors in the loop and give version control and sanity checks [28:45-29:15]. Figures are the speaker's own and unaudited.

**Use it:** Benchmark a small-team schedule: about 500 shots in under ten months needs automation of scene set-up, publishing and review.
