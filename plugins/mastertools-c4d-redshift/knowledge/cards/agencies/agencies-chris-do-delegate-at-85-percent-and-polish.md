Chris Do (Blind founder; host Joey Korenman, 2017, whisper transcript): from day one he hired designers instead of doing every job himself, because the right solution was seldom him [14:36]. A hired designer gets the job about 85% of the way for roughly 20% of what the client pays; his own art direction, or "sitting on it" after, adds the last polish to his bar [15:10]. His example: a $1,000 job costing $200 leaves $800 [15:10]. The mindset shift is managing a team to produce work at your level instead of doing it. The host adds that the hard part is accepting work 10% less cool than you would have made [15:41]. Chris Do also warns that prescriptive notes ("2% bigger, now scoot over") drop an artist from 100% to 60% effort [08:05].

**Use it:** for a lead's review ritual: set the bar, accept 85% from juniors, spend the lead's time on the last 15% and give direction that leaves the artist room.
