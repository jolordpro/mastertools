Alderson's rule: work should never look as if it came from a computer. The best response he can hear is a plain "that was nice", not "what did you make that in?". He also says realism is not always the goal: some pieces are pushed slightly fantastical so they show what cannot be shot.

Page spot: §31-32.

**Use it:** Ask of a 3D frame: does it read as made, or as rendered? Fix what gives away the software (perfect edges, even gloss, clean fill) before adding anything.
