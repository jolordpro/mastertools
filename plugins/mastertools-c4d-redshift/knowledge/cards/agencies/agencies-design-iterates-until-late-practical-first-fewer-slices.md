Chris Maclean [12:34-15:45]: a sequence where a regenerating character is held apart in slices developed late. They wanted about 100 slices; six proved more elegant in the space. The production designer led and built a practical for the actors to interact with, then VFX took it over to put the character back together. Many iterations and desktop models; he compares it to Interstellar's tesseract, where the final answer came in the last three months of shooting [15:11].
Related: a fully digital space shot is easier because VFX controls it; a plate-based shot is harder if the shot coverage is not what was asked [16:50-17:56].

**Use it:** A design-process reminder: cut the number of elements until the shot reads, and settle the physical space early.
