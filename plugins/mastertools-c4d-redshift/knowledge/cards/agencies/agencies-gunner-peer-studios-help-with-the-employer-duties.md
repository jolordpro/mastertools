After years freelancing, the founders knew different schedules and client types, but the hard part was building the right team and then being responsible for healthcare and workers' comp. They credit heads of other studios (Giant Ant, Buck, Dress Code, Wonderlust, Oddfellows and more) for guidance. Their stated goal is to balance time, creative and money. [§2]

**Use it:** Before hiring, line up a peer studio head to ask about the legal and benefits side.
