2018, US agency view. Generalist AE animators: $450-800 a day, little changed for years [1:11:51]. Above $800 an agency may be stopped by a cost consultant. Specialists can reach about $2,000 a day, but few jobs need them. Designers working day rates go up to about $2,000, while some work by project or licence fee [1:12:23, 1:12:54]. Under $450 he assumes junior; at $250 he assumes extra management time that will cost more [1:14:27]. Over $1,000 he pauses and asks whether the job needs that level, which he says is usually not the case [1:13:25]. Host Joey Korenman says he began freelancing at $500 and thought $700 was high in Boston.

**Use it:** Rates decay quickly. Use only as a historical anchor for how a cost model might band freelance days.
