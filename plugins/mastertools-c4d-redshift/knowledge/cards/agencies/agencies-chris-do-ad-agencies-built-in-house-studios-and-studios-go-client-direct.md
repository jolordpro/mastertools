Chris Do's account: big brands hired agency people to manage many agencies, so agencies lost the media buy (once worth about 10% of the buy to them) and now earn only on production [19:28][20:32]. They built in-house motion teams and the studios stopped seeing the bids they used to [23:16][29:44]. Budgets fell, commercials were less creative [19:28]. Blind's best year was 2007 at close to $7M billed, and never matched since [23:49]. Experiments that failed: an explainer-video spin-off and a second brand to avoid sticker shock, because a $400k-level shop could not make 20k videos [24:55][25:26]. His answer: go client direct, learn strategy, messaging and account management, and make whatever suits the brief, not only video [28:37][29:11]. Studio had 12-15 staff in a 12,000 sq ft building [30:18][39:16]. Dated 2017 and a studio owner's view, so decay is fast.

**Use it:** context for why sports and brand work now comes through in-house teams and direct clients, and for the extra skills a small studio needs.
