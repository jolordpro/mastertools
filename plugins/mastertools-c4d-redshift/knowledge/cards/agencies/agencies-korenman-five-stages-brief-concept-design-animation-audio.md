Korenman (School of Motion) walks through a 10-15 second branded logo reveal made for his own company: brief written even though the client was himself, since no direction is harder [00:30-01:34]; concept by mind-mapping words until one idea lands ('show, teach, reinforce') [01:34-02:04]; rough pencil sketch, then key frames designed in Illustrator and Photoshop with basic composition rules, the brand palette, strong value contrast, and the final frame defined first, giving a blueprint [02:04-03:38]; animation in After Effects: hand-set path, timing and spacing for the ball, a particle plug-in for bursts, expressions to rig the distorting shapes, masking for the logo reveal [03:38-04:42]; audio treated as equally important and given to a sound designer [04:42-05:48]. He notes steps can be skipped or reordered.

**Use it:** supports the common process card; the rule to reuse is to lock the end frame as a designed key frame before animating.
