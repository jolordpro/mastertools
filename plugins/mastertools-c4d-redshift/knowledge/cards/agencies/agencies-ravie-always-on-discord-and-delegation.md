Austin Bauwens: everyone is remote, and a Discord call stays open during work hours so someone can say look at this and get quick feedback, a tap-on-the-shoulder effect [43:24-43:55]. Joey Korenman adds it works at eight people and gets harder as a studio grows [44:03].
On moving from artist to owner: trust has to be built; sometimes you present work that is not how you would have made it [41:52]. Problems are raised at once and discussed as a group [42:23]. He stays on production as a senior hand for most projects so staff do not wonder what the boss does [42:53].

**Use it:** A simple review habit to copy: a standing open channel for quick looks beats scheduled review for small questions.
