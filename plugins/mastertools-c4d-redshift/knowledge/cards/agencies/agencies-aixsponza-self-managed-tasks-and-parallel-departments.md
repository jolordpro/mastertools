A project starts as a new Basecamp project with a rough structure; artists, subcontractors and sometimes clients are invited and tasks change in dialogue. Unlike Shotgun or Ftrack, every artist can manage and improve the structure, not only the producer, so small teams partly manage themselves; Scrum was being tested in 2015. Small jobs use an artist-centred generalist flow with little coordination. Large jobs use a classic department flow because durations are short: direction/animatic, look/layout and assets/realisation run in parallel, with modelling/shading, rig and lighting split in the implementation team. Data moves from office server through project, render and compositing servers to an LTO archive [§2].

**Use it:** Match the process weight to the job: let generalists self-organise on small pieces; split into parallel departments only when the schedule demands it.
