Ben Langsfeld (group creative director, BUCK New York): jobs open with a discovery phase of R&D or blue-sky exploration; the team pulls reference and talks around a table or Zoom about the challenge, not their own interests [17:16] [17:48]. The aim is the clearest description of what the challenge is at its core, then a spin on it as the idea basis [17:48].
Next come storyboards, concept art or 3D sketching: sculpting, lighting and rendering count as sketches [17:48] [18:18]. Small teams act as a brain trust; ideas bubble up and egos stay at the door; it is messy and not for everyone [18:18] [18:49].

**Use it:** Ideation recipe for the coach: write the challenge in one plain sentence before any look-dev, then sketch in the medium you think in.
