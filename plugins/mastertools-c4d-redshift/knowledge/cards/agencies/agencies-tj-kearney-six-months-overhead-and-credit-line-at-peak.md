Target is six months of overhead saved. Banks give credit when business looks best, usually after about two years of returns, and not when the account is near empty, so apply at peak billing and let it sit unused [46:48, 47:19]. The goal lets a studio shed load or change plans without panic [48:20]. School of Motion's Joey Korenman (host) says his bookkeeper gave him the same advice [48:20].
