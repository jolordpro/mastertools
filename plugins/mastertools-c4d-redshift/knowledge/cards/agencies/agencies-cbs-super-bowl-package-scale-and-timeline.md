[04:09] Deliverable list of about 300 elements (promos, lower thirds, team and player interstitials) across the week and the game day.
[18:41] Network works a year ahead and wants one new technology each year; designers start six or seven months ahead. [21:49] Design phase: five or six designers plus two or three on 3D experiments. [22:50] Production: two or three designers and mostly animators, about six or seven total, around six months.
