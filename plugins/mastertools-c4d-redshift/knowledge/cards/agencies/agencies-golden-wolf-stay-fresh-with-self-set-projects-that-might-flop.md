A guest [22:26-24:00] says the team keeps an internal voice that flags "we have done this before", and uses social and self-set projects to try styles they are unsure about. Reactions often differ from what they expected: an Aoki-linked video they thought would land did not, which surprised them. They rely on judging their own work against doing new things.

**Use it:** reserve a small slot each quarter for a project with a real chance of failing.
