Frames come from mood board plus script, which are generated together; he starts from the script [02:36-03:39]. Pick the beats to show: establishing shot, the last shot or call to action, peak action, and the space change; include hands, props and clocks that decide what assets are needed [04:11-05:45]. Make about nine artboards in the same proportions, but 4K is overkill; explore colourways and levels of detail (abstract to realistic hands) and show only the best [05:45-07:17]. In Photoshop use artboards to keep styles consistent [07:47-08:19]. Present: export artboards, put in a board, make one the focal piece, line up the others, label Style 1, Style 2 and write words like simple geometric or abstract versus literal and detailed so feedback has a vocabulary [08:19-10:25]. Let clients comment on the board directly [10:25]. Put a provision in the contract that style frames are not unlimited because they bill hours [10:56].
