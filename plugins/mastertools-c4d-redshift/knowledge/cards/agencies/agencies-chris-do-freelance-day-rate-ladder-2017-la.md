As he states it: a starter makes about $250 a day, over $30 an hour; a pro with 5-10 years who delivers, communicates well and is easy to work with reaches about $750 [04:55][05:26]. A newer freelancer adds about $50 every three or four months, say 250 to 450 within a year [05:26][05:56]. Particle or code-destruction specialists reach $1,200-2,000 but get booked less often [05:56][06:26]. A freelancer tops out around $200-300k a year, he said [09:09]. Host says Boston is similar [05:56]. Rates decay fast; it is a studio owner's 2017 figure. Compare with agencies-tj-kearney-freelance-day-rate-bands-2018.
