Louis Moss: the art director's board is holistic (photography direction, colour territories, type mood, materials, tone; graphics are one part) and shows the vision before anything is made [08:28-09:25]. The designer's board zooms into one element, such as the type system, graphic language or composition, as evidence for the art director's argument [09:25].
When they are confused, the design board drifts into art direction and unsettles the idea, or the AD board gets too granular, and the team starts pulling in different directions [09:25-09:55]. He treats the board as the project's foundation: if it is unclear, everything after is unclear [09:55].

**Use it:** define board scope per role in a package kickoff: world board (AD) first, element boards (type, graphics, 3D look) second.
