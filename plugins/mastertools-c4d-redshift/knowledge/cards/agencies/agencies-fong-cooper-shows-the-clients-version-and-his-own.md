Fong learned by watching her mentor Kyle Cooper work late in the bay. When a client had an idea he disagreed with, he built the client's version and also a second one he thought better, then showed both [20:12]. She names the cost herself: it is double the work. The client's idea is solved, and the better one still gets seen.

**Use it:** When a note feels wrong, plan time for two takes (their ask plus the stronger one) instead of arguing in the abstract. Budget the extra pass up front.
