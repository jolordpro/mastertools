Buck used to hire contractors for particle and Houdini work, then found in-house people with a working relationship and shorthand give better, easier results [05:50-06:22]. At least four or five artists can do Houdini and particles, but they do other things too; one is a Houdini artist, colourist and compositor, so he stays at about 110 percent utilisation [06:22-06:54].

**Use it:** Prefer staff who combine a rare skill with a common one so specialist capacity is never idle.
