She joins once script and voiceover exist [00:32]; voiceover length fixes the video length and the scope and cost [01:33]. Gather brand assets, review the website, agree style and a moodboard [02:04-03:06]. Break the script into chapters then fragments of 3-6 seconds in an Excel file, with a column describing what happens; animation syncs to the audio wave, not the other way round [03:06-04:07]. Sketch ideas, then a few style frames from brand guidelines, then illustrate the rest after the client green light [04:07-04:38]. Animatic: frames in sync with the voiceover, used to pick music and see the pace [05:06]. Animation is one of the last and least time-consuming steps; planning cuts revisions [05:25-06:03]. She offers two revision rounds at each step and moves forward only when the previous step is approved, so style frames never change after animation [06:03-06:12]. Music and SFX last.
