Honey [01:11-01:41] says Buck weighs each opportunity on three things: the creative, how much money there is, and how many people will see it. As long as a project has two of the three they take it. In the year before the talk they did over 175 projects across both offices, from full commercials to animations in Times Square to taxi-cab screens.

**Use it:** the go/no-go screen for pitches and personal projects; a project with only one of the three (say, only money) is a flag.
