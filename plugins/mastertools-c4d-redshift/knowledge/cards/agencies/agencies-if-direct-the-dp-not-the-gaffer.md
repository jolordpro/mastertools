Simon says on a 50-person set he learned he is really talking to two or three people, such as the DP and client, and respects that everyone else is an expert [45:13-46:15].
He applies it to CG leads: give creative direction, do not sit in the 3D software asking for a light moved 10 degrees [49:23-49:55]. Artists need space to bring ideas, and the director does not have to see everything [49:55-50:26].
Chip's wording of the same idea is focus and trust that the team will execute [48:22-48:52].

**Use it:** Give a lighting artist the intent and the reference. Review the result, not each lamp position.
