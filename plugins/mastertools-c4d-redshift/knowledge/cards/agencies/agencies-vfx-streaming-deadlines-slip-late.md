Justin Mitchell [38:48-42:27]: Scanline did about 220 shots on Stranger Things 4 over roughly a year, with 8 or 9 months of full production. Episode 9 alone had about 100 of them (a 2.5 hour episode); he quotes about 3,000 VFX shots over the whole season (all vendors, from memory). Two volume releases meant two deadlines, and shots were being finished the day before Volume 2. He calls the lost hard cutoff "a little terrifying" because the last months are very intense.

**Use it:** ask for the real hard delivery date, not the soft one, and keep a stop-work point in the estimate.
