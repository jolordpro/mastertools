Ben Marriott (Sydney motion designer, YouTube teacher; host Caroline): research starts with a Pinterest board of 100+ images, then he cuts to the best 5 or 6 [14:04-15:39]. A broad or too specific board gives a client something to latch onto or reject for a trivial reason (an actor's costume), so remove chances for misreading [15:39-16:10]. He adds a short written description under the board. Selling the idea in writing is uncomfortable but practice helps; in a video production pitch of three ideas, the one with a stated rationale usually won because it looked lower risk [16:10-17:42]. He learned at Buck that the mood board is what clients grade and what the designer checks against [25:59-26:30]. In the mood board he edits images (shift hue, add gradient, invert) so they match [47:11].

**Use it:** coach a pitch deck as: few images, edited to one mood, one-paragraph why.
