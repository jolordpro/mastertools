London and New York teams hand work across time zones. An experiment phase follows ideation; room for "the weird" depends on the deadline. AI use is behind the scenes (versioning, references, quick mockups). The founder still does draw-overs, mood edits and storyboards.

**Use it:** a fit check at intake and a two-option habit on big decisions.
