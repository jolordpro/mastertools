Attributed by TJ to a studio owner he calls 'Jay from China' (caption garbled, name unsure) as a base level: aim for $1,000-2,500 per second of finished animation, sweet spot $1,500-2,000 [43:40]. A 60-second spot at about $90k would feel right to him [44:11]. It is a sanity check on a bid, not a build-up from days.

**Use it:** Cross-check an estimator output: total quote divided by seconds of runtime. A far lower figure means the scope or the price is off.
