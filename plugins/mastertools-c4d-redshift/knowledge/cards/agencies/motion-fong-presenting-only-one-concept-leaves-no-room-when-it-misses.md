On The Cat in the Hat she and the team presented one concept, a 3D Rube Goldberg brain, and waited while the film was in screenings and edits [20:16-20:47]. The answer was back to the drawing board: the film needed a warm childhood feeling first, the colours and the library-book memory, and only then a new place [20:47-21:51]. Her lesson was to define the emotion wanted from viewers, not only something new and dazzling [21:51]. She also says she should have talked with a colleague before pitching [20:16]. The point about presenting a single idea with total certainty is the added part; the emotion lesson is already carded.

**Use it:** Bring at least two routes that start from different emotions, and say the target emotion in one line on the first slide so a miss is cheap to redirect.
