Good animation is the entry ticket; after that, personality decides it. TJ would take an artist at 80% craft and 120% team energy nine times out of ten [1:16:01, 1:16:31]. A top-tier person who says 'let me do my thing' can add risk and friction [1:17:34]. When a note makes a shot worse, the fix is on the producer or CD: explain why, and where it fits, for example promise a director's cut version so the artist can still do the shot right [1:19:07, 1:19:40]. Freelancers who refuse and give no room do not get repeat work [1:20:11].

**Use it:** The coach can phrase review replies the same way: give the reason for the note, and offer a separate cut where the artist's version survives.
