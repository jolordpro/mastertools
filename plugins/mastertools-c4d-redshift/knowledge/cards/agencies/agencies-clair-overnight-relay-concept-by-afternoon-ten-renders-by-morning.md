Clair says Octane arrived around the time Marks became a master of it. His own strength is the concept; Marks is a craftsman who can realise anything [54:29-55:00].
Example: for American Gods Clair would send a kernel such as crucifying an astronaut at 5 pm his time and wake to about ten renders more beautiful than he imagined [55:00-55:30].
He says showrunners never found an image too provocative; the usual reply was how to push it further [55:30-56:03].
Marks was in Melbourne, Clair in Sydney, taking calls from Los Angeles every day [56:03-56:35].
Transfers: a render-capable partner turns a text kernel into options overnight; the loop works best when the request is one line.

**Use it:** Brief a render partner with a one-line kernel and ask for a spread of options by morning; use the first batch to decide direction, not to polish.
