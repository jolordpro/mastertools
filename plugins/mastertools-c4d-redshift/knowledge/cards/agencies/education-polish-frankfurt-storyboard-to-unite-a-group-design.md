For the World Trade Center competition, Imaginary Forces joined five architecture firms as a principal rather than a graphic add-on, with six weeks to build one presentation [§3]. Frankfurt broke the stalemate among strong egos by suggesting they storyboard it as a day in the life, such as walking the dog or taking the ferry; that made them think in film terms and 'lubricated' the process [§3]. They made wireframes, animations and renders in near real time, and the speed fed the form-making [§3]. At the public presentation the film produced silence, though Libeskind's napkin sketch was more effective still [§3].

**Use it:** When a group is stuck on form, suggest a rough storyboard of a person moving through the space or product before polishing.
