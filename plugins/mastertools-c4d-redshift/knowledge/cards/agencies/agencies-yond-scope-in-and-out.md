Kyle Krieger learned from Sarofsky producers to explain what is in scope and to lay out what is out of scope in plain words [20:09-20:42].
Ideas grow extra limbs; the job is to keep them inside the agreed scope, and if an idea earns it, raise scope deliberately. Both client and creative sometimes scope wrong up front [20:42-21:14].
He keeps a service mindset but still speaks up creatively, and aims for mutual understanding; letting negativity stew leads to cancelled projects or no rehire [21:45-22:46].

**Use it:** write an out-of-scope line into every bid or brief, and treat new ideas mid-job as a scope change to price, not a free add.
