For a Goodbooks spot [09:09-10:44] with 600 cockroaches coming out of a goat's head, instead of one person drawing every roach, Buck planned: rough pencil-test motion, an effects artist runs a 3D simulation, an illustrator makes textures that match the style and mapped them onto the roaches, the CG goat head serves as the collision object, render it separately, and draw over it frame by frame. The talk says the process film shows nine of about 160 edits made during production [10:44]. Another point: a zero-budget script they loved made them spend freely to make something great, which "put us on the map" [08:36].

**Use it:** the template for hybrid-look planning: sim for motion, illustration for surface, hand draw-over for the final.
