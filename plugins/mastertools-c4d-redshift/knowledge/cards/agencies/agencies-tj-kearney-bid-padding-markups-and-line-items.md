Mark every artist up to the most expensive person you might have to hire; his example is $800-1,000 a day even if most freelancers are lower, so the bid survives losing your first-choice team [1:08:13]. On top, add a base markup of 25% in plain sight, so it is something to negotiate and not the team size [1:08:44]. He dislikes tiny fees such as $150 per QuickTime upload [1:09:15]. A render-farm line is different: it is a real possible cost, you must not hit the client with an overage later, and if unused it is profit [1:10:18]. Never cut the rate to fit a budget, because the client will expect the lower rate again; cut line items instead [1:11:20]. Matches the existing Oddfellows bid card.

**Use it:** The estimator should show the render budget as its own line, separate from the artist days, so it can be cut or kept without touching day rates.
