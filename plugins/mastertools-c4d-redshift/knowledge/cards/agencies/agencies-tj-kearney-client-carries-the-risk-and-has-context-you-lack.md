Studios are artists and service providers at once. The client's job is on the line when they pick a small team, and an agency may risk a multi-million retainer on a $100k job [24:28, 25:30]. So clients pick on relationships, history and least risk, which is why Buck or Mill win even over cheaper options [26:32]. What makes a studio feel safe: a producer, infrastructure, spare capacity if scope grows, and teams that are always available to each other, not freelancers sharing a Dropbox [22:23, 22:54]. Vendors on a six-to-eight-week job miss the campaign context behind an odd change, and miss how hard the agency creatives argued for them [1:21:45, 1:22:49].
