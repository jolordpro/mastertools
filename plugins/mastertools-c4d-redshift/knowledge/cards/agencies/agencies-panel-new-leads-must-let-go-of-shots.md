A common mistake when stepping up to lead or supervisor is struggling to let go of the shot work [24:42-25:14].
The shift is to support the team in getting the volume of work done and allocate big shots to others [25:14-25:46]. It is still the leader's responsibility, and the result may differ from what they would do [25:46-26:48]. Panelists say half of progression is stepping back from the art for management [27:19].

**Use it:** a reviewer who also builds should record which shots they owned and which they only reviewed.
