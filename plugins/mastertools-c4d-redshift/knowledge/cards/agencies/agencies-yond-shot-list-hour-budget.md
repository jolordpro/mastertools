Andrew Marston (lead animator) makes a database with one row per shot; each row has sub-items for every piece of animation, with hours (his example: environment plants about 2 hours, a waterfall about 4) [28:28-29:29].
He sums the hours, asks if the total passes a common-sense test and fits the scheduled time, and if not looks for things to cut [28:59-29:29].
This happens after script and boards exist and before animation starts [28:28].

**Use it:** the cost estimator can use the same shape: shot, sub-pieces, hours each, total, then a check against the calendar.
