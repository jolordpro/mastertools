Austin Bauwens: organic social brought the right kind of lead with the right budget. Targeting the same person with paid ads mostly brought asks like a 30-second video for $400 [46:03].
Cold outreach felt foreign to the founders. A short email plus a personal video message did not raise the reply rate much [46:33-47:04]. They still do some warm LinkedIn messaging and go to industry events, which also gets the remote team together [45:31].
Host Joey Korenman's view [47:34]: social catches people who need something now; paid ads and cold outreach need a built sales process with a call that qualifies leads.

**Use it:** Treat a low-budget ask from an ad as a sign the audience is wrong, not that the ad failed; qualify before spending a day on a bid.
