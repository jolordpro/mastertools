A Buck guest: they give creatives chances to do things not usually funded, such as making puppets and painting frames by hand [04:25-04:55]. The other: long-tenured creatives mentor and reinvest in talent, and staff see this and raise their work [09:08-09:39]. About 500 staff across New York, LA, Sydney and Amsterdam, and 100-200 freelancers at a time [03:23].
For remote juniors they hold coffees with new people at about two months, random Slack 'donuts' calls, and open work sessions where people draw or animate on a shared call [10:42-11:44].

**Use it:** Reserve a small slice of studio time for hand-made experiments, and review them as part of the pipeline.
