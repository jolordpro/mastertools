Creator Oren (orenmeetsworld, brand and product creative director; single-creator view, low confidence). A mood board alone is not the idea; the step after it is to push each reference through a fixed toolkit [01:34-04:38]. The six moves: scale (much bigger or much smaller), aesthetic (apply your brand's established look, e.g. Western gothic, to the borrowed setup), beauty (make a normal thing as pleasing as possible), transportation (put it somewhere surreal and wrong for it, such as a runway in a desert), clash (take the composition from one reference and the look or setting from another), tension (an unease emotion such as shame, fear or ego). He tests each against "does this stand out?".

**Use it:** A coach prompt for stuck concept work: before copying a reference frame, name which one of the six moves is being applied. Transfers to sports open concepts (scale and transportation especially).
