Day rate times the days you could work does not apply to staff, because the studio then guarantees booking, training, insurance, paid leave and about 20% tax load on top [48:24][48:56]. Chris Do's advice: price the slot within the local market, plus or minus 10 to 15 percent [48:56]. He says many freelancers priced themselves out of full-time roles this way [47:54].

**Use it:** a rule for any offer talk that moves from day rate to staff.
