Rebellion VFX was about 30 people and growing at the time, in a larger group with games, publishing, mocap and virtual-production facilities [03:13-05:48]. The trailer had 11 shots of which the pack shot counted for 4, each in a new environment, so 11 master lighting setups [08:23].
The pipeline was built from scratch on USD with no legacy; GPU rendering was chosen so the farm hardware could be shared with real-time and games work [06:50-07:53].
It was the studio's first full-CG project after two internal shorts [04:14].

**Use it:** a benchmark for what a small team ships in a cinematic trailer; no budget or time numbers were given.
