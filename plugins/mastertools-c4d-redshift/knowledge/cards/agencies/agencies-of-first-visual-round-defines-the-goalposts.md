Jorge: until the first visuals, clients have seen only words. The first round defines the goalposts for how abstract or literal they want to go [24:19-24:50]. Their reactions let the team read between the lines and set rules for round two [25:20-25:51].
He prefers learning that a direction is too wild or too safe on the first delivery, because it sets the relationship from then on [25:51-26:22].
Abby: show things the team is excited about, and the geometric style won because it allowed modular building [22:46-23:48].

**Use it:** Make the first round deliberately broad, then record what the client reacted to before narrowing.
