Nidia Dias (3D art director, ex-Future Deluxe, Territory, Man vs Machine; host Hayley Akins): she writes a short idea and a mood board and says explore, since artists were hired because they are special at something [10:57-11:58].
On a Microsoft project freelancers suggested things she had not thought of and the team kept them [11:28-12:00].
She says an art director should step back, consider the greater good over their own style [12:00-12:31], and keep remote freelancers from waiting on feedback [10:57].

**Use it:** Brief template: idea line, mood board, one open question; not a pixel spec.
