His view of the acquisition trend: tech moats are falling but attention cannot be faked, so companies like Google, Meta and Duolingo want control of their reach beyond ads [14:37-15:39]. Outsourcing is slow, and a team that can produce an asset tomorrow, not in three months, is worth far more than the purchase price [15:39-16:41]. AI lowered the barrier to making content no more than the iPhone did [18:14-19:17].

**Use it:** Pitch in-house teams on speed and daily output, not just craft.
