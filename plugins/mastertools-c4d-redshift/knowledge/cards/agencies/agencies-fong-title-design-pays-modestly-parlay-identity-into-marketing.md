She likens it to a fashion cover paying less than catalogue work [36:20-36:51]. Imaginary Forces does not live on titles alone [32:11]. Her tactic is to parlay a show's identity into trailers and marketing, as with the Mad Men falling-man ad in New York subways [37:22-37:54]. The skills (distilling a big idea to a tight film, building a brand world) carry to Google, games (God of War 3 cinematics) and large-screen work [34:47-35:18]. Her Google small-business piece cast owners as superheroes battling bureaucracy and "no" as villains [38:27-38:57].

**Use it:** For a sports package, pitch the extension: open, promos and social cut-downs from one identity. Pay for prestige creative work often comes from the spin-offs.
