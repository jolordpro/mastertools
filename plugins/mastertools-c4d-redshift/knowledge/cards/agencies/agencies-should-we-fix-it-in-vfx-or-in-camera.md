Chris Maclean [35:54-38:34]: costs rise when costumes, sets or shot elements are wrong and the shoot continues. He tells producers VFX can do anything with time and money, but asks whether to, and whether in camera is more efficient. Fixing lazy shooting adds no production value. It depends on good faith both ways: do not ask for what you do not need, and they give you what you ask for. Season one built the process, and the team was among the first hires, so the showrunner trusted them to develop the VFX pipeline [38:02-38:34].

**Use it:** A line for a client or producer note: cost the fix against the in-camera or in-render option before accepting it.
