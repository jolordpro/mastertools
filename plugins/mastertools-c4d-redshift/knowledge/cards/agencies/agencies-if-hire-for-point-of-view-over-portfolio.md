Denny (answering someone newly in a media role who said everyone's work looks the same): ask what you think about the world, what you read and watch, and what you would talk about on a podcast [57:42-59:44].
He says they no longer hire on portfolio, and he points to the ability to navigate ambiguity and to a clear view as what separates people, since AI has flattened polished output [53:33, 58:12-59:44].

**Use it:** In portfolio review, look for a repeated point of view across pieces, not only finish.
