Allan McKay (host) describes a "bonus round": agree with the client's extra ideas, list them as "if there is time at the end", and finish the key items first [21:19].
He asks clients to review only one layer, for example "do not comment on texture or lighting, tell me if the animation moves too fast" [22:19].
Reason: the end of the project is then not spent on one tiny detailed piece while the main delivery is unfinished [21:49].
Host's method; treat as a pattern, not a measured rule.

**Use it:** In review rounds, state which layer the round judges (timing, then look, then polish) and log other comments to a parked list rather than acting on them.
