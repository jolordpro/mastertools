Rand presented logos as a book: he showed attempts in order, explained what he tried given what the client said, and finished with a recommended solution; when a client disliked a logo he made a book that explained each attempt instead of another logo, as for Ford [14:14-15:15]. The host recaps that the sequence is like a small animation, so when the client reaches the last page there is nothing else to talk about [15:45-16:16, host]. Cooper adds he does not have that leverage and works with notes [15:15-15:45].

**Use it:** Structure a review deck as a short path from brief to chosen answer, with the recommendation last and the reason for each step visible.
