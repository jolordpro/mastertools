Maria Walla (lead lighting and compositing at Axis Studios, Spain, working remotely) describes her routine [03:08-06:16]: open Houdini and Nuke first so no licence is lost, check the overnight renders for her artists and whether assets were published by other departments, then the team meeting with coordinators. Then rounds per sequence with the supervisor's notes, so every artist leaves the meeting knowing what to do that day (a day with no task is frustrating). After that she tries to work an hour on master shots; at Axis the leads prepare them, and seniors take them and set up the rest.
When a lead, she drops her own work whenever someone on her team needs help [06:47]. At the start of production she tells artists not to work overtime; overtime comes at delivery [21:10].

**Use it:** The farm check plus a morning task round is a cheap daily review pattern for a render queue.
