Order described for a brand film: creative brief; ask who it is for and why the problem is worth solving; research the audience; step into their shoes (challenges, unmet needs, what matters and why); find the emotional misbelief behind the surface need; choose a protagonist who answers it; write the script; then storyboards, style frames, animation, sound [02:06][02:38][03:09][03:39][04:41][05:13][06:16]. Example: power-washing customers' surface need was a task taken off their hands, but the real worry was whom to trust with a house they take pride in, so the owner became the protagonist [04:10][04:41]. They did all this before any design, because story sets style, mood and tone [05:44].

**Use it:** a pre-design checklist for a pitch: audience, misbelief, protagonist, then look.
