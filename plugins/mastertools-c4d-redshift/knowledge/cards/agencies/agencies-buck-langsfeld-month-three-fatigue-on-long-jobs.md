Ben Langsfeld (group creative director, BUCK New York): he has worked on jobs for eight months; by month three you are fatigued whatever you do and need an extra effort to recommit with purpose [21:24]. On a Sherwin-Williams series with some 19 spots, the team kept finding a new way in: character behaviour, portraiture, animating thousands of cards at once [21:55] [22:26] [22:57].
His tip: step back, look at the work for what it is and not personally, then give the team a new named challenge, even just in words, so they chase something and not their tail [22:26] [22:57]. He frames it as a marathon [23:57].

**Use it:** Production-planning flag: schedule a technical or creative change-up in the middle of long series work.
