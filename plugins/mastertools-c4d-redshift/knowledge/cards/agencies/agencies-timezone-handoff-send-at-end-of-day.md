Nidia Dias (3D art director, ex-Future Deluxe, Territory, Man vs Machine; host Hayley Akins): with a studio ahead of her, she has a call before the project starts, does her stuff in her normal hours, sends it as they arrive, they discuss and reply while she sleeps, and she wakes to feedback [19:50-21:23].
She prefers to avoid pulling extra hours for the other side; when she did, working to midnight for a US time zone with Europe on the same job was stressful [19:50-20:51].
Same time zone gives more chat; hers suits quiet focus [21:23-21:55].

**Use it:** Handoff rule: a short daily call window, deliver at end of day, written feedback before next start.
