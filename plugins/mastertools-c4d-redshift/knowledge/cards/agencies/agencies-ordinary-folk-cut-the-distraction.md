In the School of Motion talk, Greg Stewart told how Jorge Canedo Estrada looked at one world-building shot for the Bible Project piece, saw a dot showing on a single layer in one frame, and suggested cutting it. The shot then felt more cohesive. Greg called it the first feedback he had that was about the concept and not "your animation sucks" [§13].
Greg also names his own habit of paying too much attention to the tiniest details and missing more important things, and says teammates saying "move on" or "what if we killed this" is the fix. He does not claim every frame is equal: know which details matter most [§12].

**Use it:** Before adding polish, try removing one thing. Rank notes: whole-frame first, detail last.
