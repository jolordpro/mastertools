Guest: Karin Fong (2025 talk). She uses AI to concept, to test ideas quickly and to weed out ones that will not work; the example was a mural-style look checked before committing [14:58-16:41].
On one title sequence the team could not afford illustrators for exploration, but a quick AI mock-up let them show the client what illustrated art would look like, and the client then funded a full team of illustrators [15:31-16:01].
She wants AI used without denying people creativity, and hopes artists get paid when their work is drawn on [16:01-17:14].

**Use it:** Use AI frames as a pitch device to unlock a bigger human-made budget, and say so up front.
