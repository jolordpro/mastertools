In 1979 he and partners started MetaDesign after a job doing copy design for a German bank; the name meant designing for design [02:48-03:19].
They were 'the guideline guys', making guidelines for other designers to work from [02:48-03:19].
Earlier he acted as a typographic consultant, linking people drawing type in London with the people making fonts in Germany, because corporate programmes needed type that could be put into production [01:45-02:48].
The studio grew to about 200 people and he left after 20 years [03:19].

**Use it:** For a brand package, deliver the guidelines and templates others will work from, not only the finished pieces.
