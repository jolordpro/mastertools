Beeple [05:59-06:29]: professionally he tries not to over-promise. If he does not know how to do something and it is outside his wheelhouse, he tells the client to find someone else, because they should not pay him to learn. He stretches on small daily projects, where the worst case is wasting about 45 minutes to an hour.
He also admits that on personal work he often drops what he cannot solve and does something else, and thinks he should push through more [06:29-07:01]. The daily deadline forces a good-enough fudge when the ideal method is unknown [07:33].

**Use it:** Bid notes: flag unfamiliar techniques as a risk line and test them on a small piece first.
