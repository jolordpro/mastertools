Repeated across the review: 6 to 8 dialled-in projects is the sweet spot; he says to trim 7+ down and 4 up [29:00, 31:08, 42:18, 49:13, 57:05].
Put the strongest at the top. One reviewed portfolio had its best work at the bottom and he told the owner to reorder [50:45-51:16].
Cut the project that clashes in style or has thin context, even if it is real work [17:28-17:58]. Logo dumps invite weak entries: "a portfolio is only as good as the worst piece" [1:03:23].
Behance shows older, weaker work by default, so he prefers a standalone curated site on your own domain [37:02-37:36].

**Use it:** When curating a reel or case-study list, rank by strength, cap at 6-8, and drop anything that does not match the work you want to be hired for.
