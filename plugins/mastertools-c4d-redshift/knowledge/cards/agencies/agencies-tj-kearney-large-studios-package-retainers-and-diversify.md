At 30-50 staff, studios like Buck, Mill and Psyop bundle one-off asks into retainers or year-long accounts, so the client gets guaranteed team availability and the studio gets a bigger budget [54:05]. They sell more than animation: production, a little agency work, sometimes dev [55:09]. Need to pitch drops, so they choose clients [55:09]. Studios that survived diversified early; those that put everything into Flame work lost it when Flame faded, and Flame artists who never learned After Effects struggled [57:46, 58:16].

**Use it:** Reminder that tool-specific skill has a shelf life; relevant when the coach recommends what to learn.
