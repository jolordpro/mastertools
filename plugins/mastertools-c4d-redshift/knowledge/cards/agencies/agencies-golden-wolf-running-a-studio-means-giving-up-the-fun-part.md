A founder in the stream [28:25-31:08] warns that wanting to make cool work is not a reason to start a studio: most of the job is paperwork, money and HR, and you accept you will not do the fun part all the time. It took him three or four years to be okay with no longer designing. He has seen people ask for responsibility and then only want to draw.

**Use it:** a reality-check line for the coach when someone asks about starting a studio.
