StudioBinder with Ian Failes (befores and afters) [00:52-09:15]: special effects (SFX) are physical and mechanical, done on set (fog, pyro, animatronics, prosthetics), supervised mainly for safety; VFX is any effect added to captured imagery, including compositing, green screen, paint-outs, rotoscoping, mocap and virtual production, and the VFX supervisor is involved from pre-production and on set so footage is usable [07:06-07:38]; CGI is a subset: images wholly computer-made, so a film claiming "no CGI" may still carry heavy VFX [09:15]. The CG departments listed: art department, previs, FX simulation, lighting that matches the plate, assets (models, textures, shaders), R&D [10:19-10:54]. 1917 plane crash [12:00-13:36]: VFX supervisor Guillaume Rocheron says a CG plane in the sky was blended into a practical plane launched from a ramp 20 feet up, the actors were comped in from a separate shot, and a CG plane and barn had to match the burning set piece so takes shot in Shepperton and Glasgow joined seamlessly.

**Use it:** Use the terms precisely in client talk; mixed practical and CG shots need the CG to match the practical object exactly.
