A TV title is seen in front of a different scene every week, may run for years, and is designed with an eye on the season's arc without knowing every episode [1:10:53-1:11:54]. A feature title lives in one place and one moment and is not meant to be watched over and over; its job is to set up the scene that holds it [CM 20:52-21:24]. She says this makes them different storytelling problems.

**Use it:** Ask first how often the piece will be seen and what plays after it. Repeat-view graphics (broadcast opens, bumpers) can carry fine detail; one-off events should be bold and singular.
