The 3D ball render is a video group in Photoshop; white lines are drawn frame by frame with a pen tablet over it, only on the side where the hand-drawn world takes over (right side) [04:15-05:18]. Timing and animation principles matter because it is hard to get the motion you want [05:18-05:49].
Export via render video as an image sequence with alpha set to straight (unmatted), then bring it back into After Effects and place it over the footage, same as the 3D pass [05:49-06:51].
Two layers: 3D body, 2D accent that only appears where the story changes style.

**Use it:** Add a hand-drawn accent pass to 3D for style; export straight alpha to avoid dark fringes.
