Authors are their own worst enemies at design because they are too close. A good template, perhaps one typeface that works for whatever you publish, gets about 80% quality when the manuscript is flowed in. He says picking the right typeface and trying it takes no longer than picking the wrong one, provided you read the text, if only diagonally, and know your faces [§2].

**Use it:** For a graphics package, fix one tested type family and layout template first, then drop content in.
