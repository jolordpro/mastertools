In 1987 people began writing letters on the computer, and he found setting them in Helvetica or Times silly; they should look like a letter but use what the laser printer offers without the typewriter's disadvantages [15:45-16:46]. He proposed a correspondence face to ITC, was told to do it himself, and it became Officina; he was nervous of digital type [16:15-16:46].
Later it needed a serif, a sans and a headline cut, redone with an assistant in the mid-1990s [18:51-19:23].

**Use it:** Start a type or graphic style from the output device: what the screen, projection or LED wall renders well should drive the shapes.
