Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer) made an opening so busy that the art director said to tone it down; scaling down is easier on the ego, but harder because curves and keyframes were already set [25:17-26:50].
He argues the fix is process: build an animatic or board so clients react to the basic movement before it is polished, and explain style frames to clients who have not been through this [27:22-29:58].
Margin: finish early enough that a late change or an idea you dislike has room, and never design the whole thing inside the animation app [27:52-28:54].
Remember the audience is a product manager, not a motion designer, so nothing needs to keep moving [24:45-25:17].

**Use it:** Gate: animatic approved before detailed keyframing; flag drafts that are overbuilt for the stage.
