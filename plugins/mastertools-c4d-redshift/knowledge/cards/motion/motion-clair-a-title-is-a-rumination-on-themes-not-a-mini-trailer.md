Titles are an airlock between everyday life and the show's world, but the lasting value is on repeat viewing: express the dilemmas a character faces so the audience reflects on the drama [00:36-01:38].
His biggest mistake to avoid: making the title a little version of the show with every piece in it. A title is not a trailer; it explores one aspect of the characters or themes in a more abstract way [01:38-02:40].

**Use it:** Pick one idea per open and cut anything that only reports the plot.
