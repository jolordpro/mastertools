Andrea Go (layout lead) and editor Axel Geddes on the bear-attack shot. First plan (bush exit, pivot to camera) failed because the wide river set made the action feel far from the viewer [09:34]. Alts tried: lock-off, snap zoom, tracking with the beaver, over-the-shoulder, a pan. Finding: the pan and the over-shoulder worked, so they combined them: start on Mabel, whip pan, bear jumps toward camera [10:05-11:09]. The editor holds the shot longer so the audience sees the bush shake and works out where to look before the bear appears [26:16]. His check is "eye fix": at a cut, the new shot's attention point should be near where the eye was in the last shot [26:47]. Version with a whip pan gives an implied (not literal) point of view, which he calls a legitimate cheat [27:53].

**Use it:** Review habit: before a cut note where the eye sits at the end of shot A and the start of shot B; generate 4-5 camera alts and combine rather than pick one.
