For a chainsaw with rotating teeth, Jorge says the right approach was animating one tooth to rotate exactly, offset 360 degrees, and use time remapping so it always matches the board, not a dashed stroke with trim-path offset that would look quick and off [1:00:29-1:02:33].
He names a past case of corner-pinning a texture every frame so a cube matched the board exactly [1:02:33-1:03:03]. Joey (host) points out that two black triangles on the board are not the same, and details like the base of the teeth show who took the easy way [1:03:34-1:04:05].
Animators who find the right movement for the design are what they look for, not those who cater the design to the software [1:02:02-1:02:33].

**Use it:** In review, compare the animation frame by frame to the style frame and flag any shortcut look.
