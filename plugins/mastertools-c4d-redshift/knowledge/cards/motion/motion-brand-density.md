Dickinson uses brand density for how heavily the motion system shows up. His example: paid piece about 75%, Reels about 12.5%, where only roundness, colour or how copy enters nods to the system. His own coinage, used casually.

**Use it:** Frame cross-surface rules as a dial, not on/off.
