Mesh began when the studio, then working from a garage, tossed around goofy ideas and grew interested in 3D scans and blending cel with 3D in a new way [§1]. The spark was the dreamlike islands that Autodesk's 123D Catch made; after pulling several scans into one Cinema 4D scene they wondered whether something could interact with the raw photo scans [§1]. One partner liked expansive foggy scenes that play with scale, the other drew big frumpy huggable characters, and they hoped for magic from forcing the interests into one world [§2]. They used the project as an excuse to bring in talented friends [§1].
