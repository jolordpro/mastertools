For Arlington Road it was all about the edits: he spliced calm images (picket fences, kids on bikes, flags) and pushed harsh yellow and red hues over them. Director Mark Pellington drove through dozens of neighbourhoods with Cooper to capture the footage on Super 8 and 16mm film [§2]. For The Mummy he built a typeface that was part roman letters, part hieroglyphics, after extensive historical research [§2].
