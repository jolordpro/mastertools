For Black Sails the art director found photos of aristocratic trappings wrecked by the sea, and the team looked at altarpiece and cemetery sculpture, then put pirates and thieves where kings and saints usually sit [CM 15:15-15:46]. A gallery assemblage artist was another touchstone [CM 16:17]. They did not use the web finds directly: storyboard artists drew their own version, then CG modelling, texture and lighting made it real; they considered carving physical sculptures but went CG [CM 16:48-17:18]. Her stated reason is to free the work from what already exists.

**Use it:** Reference rule: take themes and structure from a source, then redraw in the brief's own subjects so the piece is ownable.
