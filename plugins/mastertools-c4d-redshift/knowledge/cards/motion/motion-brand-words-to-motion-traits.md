Val Head's method (Front-Trends 2017, about [28:07] to [29:10]): take the brand's own adjectives and re-read them as motion. Friendly = a little follow-through; reassuring = no bounce; minimal = a smaller travel in the same direction. Same shape, three personalities.

**Use it:** Open a motion system or case with the word list, then show one shape moving each way.
