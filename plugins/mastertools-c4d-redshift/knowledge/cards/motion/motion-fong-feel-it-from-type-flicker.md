She calls typography a huge, underused emotional tool. Michelle Dougherty's Stranger Things titles added little analog flickers to the type to replicate the optical process of shooting on an animation stand or downshooter. The actual typeface sits in the world of Stephen King books and that genre. Viewers who grew up on 80s films respond viscerally without being able to say why [§3-4].

**Use it:** When a type treatment feels dead, look for the process it would have gone through in its era and fake that artefact lightly; test whether viewers feel the era before reading the words.
