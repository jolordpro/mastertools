He says about 10 words or 50 to 60 characters per line is nice to read, and treats it as physiology, since eye movement and field of view cannot be changed by machines. Screens force a hierarchy because they show less at once, so readers hunt for what they need.

Page spot: §1.

**Use it:** For titles and lower thirds, keep lines to a comfortable count and use hierarchy so the eye finds the key line first.
