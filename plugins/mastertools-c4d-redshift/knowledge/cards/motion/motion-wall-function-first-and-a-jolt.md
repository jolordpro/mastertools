Wall calls title sequences little experimental films that actually have to function. The Thrones opening began as a need: the show spans a vast geography and viewers risked confusion, so the map works as a legend that orients you, like the map at the front of a fantasy book. He names The Sopranos open as a root, admired as functionally elegant and visually interesting, and a lead driving through his own kingdom, as Thrones travels the Seven Kingdoms. [§1]

**Use it:** Give the sequence a job in the show first; allow one deliberate jolt if it serves that job.
