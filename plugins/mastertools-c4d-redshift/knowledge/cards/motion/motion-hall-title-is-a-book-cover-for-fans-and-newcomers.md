Hall says a title gives context and personality, "the book cover" [00:31-01:33]. On The Last of Us, one part of the audience knew the game and a larger part did not, so the title had to speak to both without revealing what the show is about [01:33-02:35]. A co-designer (captions do not label speakers; Nadia Tzuo is the other guest) adds that a title must serve the story and keep people excited [16:12]. They look for the core spirit of the story and the one thing that points at it; it feels good when found, sometimes fast, sometimes slow [18:14-18:45].

**Use it:** A sports open serves two crowds the same way: committed fans (details) and casual viewers (clear feeling). Write one sentence that names the single thing the open points at.
