A producer wanted all space shots filmed before the shoot; Legato said no because he did not know the film's style or how the shots fit, and a pre-planned 200-shot library would just echo earlier films [24:36-25:07]. He scored in his head, and changed the shot at the heroic moment; one shot sent to Ron Howard looked meaningless alone, but cut between live-action pieces it matched the outgoing and incoming motion [25:07-26:10]. He says it works because it was meant for that place only, the shot you would make if you could control everything [26:10-26:42].

**Use it:** Design a VFX shot from the motion of its neighbours in the edit, and judge it in the cut, not alone.
