The God of War III director asked for flashback and title sequences based on Greek art (black-figure urns, flat art in a dimensional space) after seeing her Mummy titles [33:22-34:25]. For the elements (earth, fire, water) a master calligrapher, a friend's father, produced real brushwork [34:25-34:56]. Her point: the finished piece is in the computer, but shooting something, overlaying design, taking a brush texture and laying it into a 3D environment gives hybrid imagery worth encouraging [34:56-35:27].

**Use it:** Photograph or scan real brush, ink or paper textures to use as maps and overlays instead of generating every surface digitally.
