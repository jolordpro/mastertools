The director asked for a fade-in; he proposed a concept tied to secrets and decoding words, with a DIN industrial typeface [06:32-07:24].
The second version was too fancy, so the final was a creeping-in effect, a fade with a touch of detail around the edges [07:24-07:54]. Then the credits request arrived on a Friday night [07:54].

**Use it:** a pattern: start with a concept, accept toning down, keep one small signature detail.
