Microsoft states that a gesture seen in Windows should feel familiar in Word or PowerPoint. The work took months across brand, product and motion designers from Microsoft, Collins and Koto, with printed icons used to test shapes. No timings or tokens were published.

**Use it:** Claim consistency across surfaces, credit the partner studios, and note what was tested off screen.
