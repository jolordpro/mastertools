For a Herman Miller promo built on the company archive, boards of images set to jazz were not enough; the team wanted something that linked the designers beyond the logo [10:03-10:37]. A scribbled handwritten word suggested the answer: the designers' drawn line was their signature and was active in the blueprints, so a drawn line could carry the eye from image to image [10:37-11:10]. She frames her whole practice as the pleasure of the transition between two things, with a surprise in how one becomes the next being half of the win [08:27-08:59].

**Use it:** For a montage of unrelated assets, find one physical device that is true to the subject (a line, a tape, a lane marking) and use it as the transition vehicle in every cut.
