Cooper calls himself a graphic designer and was a student of Paul Rand at Yale, whose critiques ended with questions like 'that's not contrast!' [Eye §2]. Rand pointed him to Eisenstein's Film Form and The Film Sense, which led to his thesis on montage and the link between cinematic composition, juxtaposition and contrast and Modernist graphic design [Eye §2]. From Eisenstein he learned to live and die by the look of every frame [Eye §2]. He also says he always liked the juxtaposition of images over time, with a beginning, middle and end [reverttosaved §1].

**Use it:** Coach contrast and juxtaposition between frames, not only inside one frame.
