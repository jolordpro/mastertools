Deakins and James Deakins [24:27-27:13]: an object insert only works if it has a purpose, such as the poisoned apple or the coin that decides a fate. Conrad Hall told him an insert still fills a 40 ft screen for as long as a face would, so it deserves as much care as any other element [24:59].
An insert can raise tension by being slightly odd: the crunched packet in No Country for Old Men is trivial but unsettles because the audience asks why they are looking at it [26:43].
Inserts used only to bridge a continuity gap do not work [27:13]. The Hitchcock bomb-under-the-table idea is cited: show the bomb, and the ordinary conversation changes.
Transfer: in a broadcast sting or stat open, a close detail beat must say something about the story, not just fill time.

**Use it:** The coach asks of any cutaway or macro beat: what does this tell the viewer that the wide did not?
