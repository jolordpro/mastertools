They made stacks of boards, swinging between an LED and a filament bulb, a signal at machine-code level, and rival signals that burned up or fell behind. The rival signals were cut for a simpler story: with only 30 seconds, over-complicating the message is the risk, so ideas had to be rock solid before animating. They also kept trying to avoid copying Tron's grid-and-city feel. Era check: they avoided kitsch 1980s looks and took cues from conservative home-computer adverts, with serif fonts and classic layouts. [§2-§3]

**Use it:** Cut story beats from boards before animation starts, and name the reference you must not imitate.
