Jess (Envato Tuts+) says timing is hierarchy over time, so block first: storyboard the key moments, pre-comp each scene, then drag scene lengths in a main comp, since equal lengths tell no story [04:52]. Give the surprise beat extra time (she held the fourth scene for a 'huh' before the reveal) and expect to re-tweak timing later; frame rate also changes feel [05:24]. Then ease in the Graph Editor, mixing speed and value views; steeper curve is faster [06:05]. Add anticipation (a few frames of opposite motion, optionally squash and stretch), follow through and secondary action [06:51]. She keeps a desktop list of hierarchy, balance and tension, contrast, negative space and repetition and checks any frame that feels off against it [03:30].

**Use it:** matches the order block, ease, polish for review notes; the five-principle list is a quick frame audit.
