Kyle Cooper [27:10-28:45] says he cannot judge an edit in one viewing. He watches once for the kerning of the type, again for the colour correction, and so on, breaking the piece into tiny components (he ties this to Eisenstein's "immutable fragment of reality") and then takes the next indicated action. His worst state is thinking he has to do several things at once while everyone else wants something else; the pass method is how he cuts out the noise.

**Use it:** build the final-pass checklist as separate viewings (type spacing, grade, edges, timing), not one general "does it look good" look.
