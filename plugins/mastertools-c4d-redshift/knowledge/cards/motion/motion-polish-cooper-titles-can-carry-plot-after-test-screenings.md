Cooper says studios screen films to test audiences and find something is missing, so titles are asked to tell part of the story after principal photography [reverttosaved §2]. This is why he named his company Prologue; the Se7en and Dawn of the Dead main titles became each film's first scene [reverttosaved §2]. He treats the director as gatekeeper and says his job is to serve the director's point of view and let ideas evolve by listening, since nobody has the definitive answer [reverttosaved §1-§2].

**Use it:** Ask what the audience may have missed in the film and whether the title should carry it.
