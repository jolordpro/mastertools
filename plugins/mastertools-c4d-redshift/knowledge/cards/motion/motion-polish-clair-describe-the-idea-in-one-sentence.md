Clair recalls an executive producer's rule: an idea or story that cannot be put in a sentence is not understood yet [§9]. He expects two or three crisis moments per job when client or team says it is not working, and the fix is always to return to the core and make it simpler [§9]. He admires Alien's five letters of Swiss type as ominous through simplicity [§9].
