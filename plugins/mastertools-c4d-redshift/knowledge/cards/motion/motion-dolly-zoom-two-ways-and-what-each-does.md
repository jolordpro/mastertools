StudioBinder [12:50-15:36]. A dolly zoom combines a dolly with an opposing lens zoom, so the subject keeps its size while perspective changes. Dolly in while zooming out: the background grows around a constant-size foreground, used for internal or external conflict. Dolly out while zooming in: the foreground subject becomes dominant and the background recedes; used for a growing relationship, tunnel vision, or (in one cited concert scene) bringing the audience closer to the singer without making him bigger in frame.

**Use it:** Gives the two directions and their feel for a CG camera: animate dolly distance and focal length together so the subject's screen size stays fixed.
