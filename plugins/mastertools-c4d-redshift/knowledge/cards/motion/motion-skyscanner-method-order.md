Peter Dickinson (2025): no motion at the start. Define purpose and values (optimistic, inspiring, reliable, authentic), then principles, then test about 20 easing variants on a plain dot, then shapes, type, photo and video relations, then formats, then After Effects templates, Essential Graphics and scripts, then written guidance.

**Use it:** Use as a template order for retro-documenting a system.
