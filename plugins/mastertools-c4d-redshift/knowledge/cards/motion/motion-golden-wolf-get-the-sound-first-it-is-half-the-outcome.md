In the stream [36:24-37:26] a guest calls sound "easily fifty percent" of the final outcome. Agencies often pick the track and licensing makes it hard, but when possible they want the sound before the animation starts, and to work in tandem with the musician.

**Use it:** supports Marks's sound card; add "ask for the track at kickoff" to the pitch checklist.
