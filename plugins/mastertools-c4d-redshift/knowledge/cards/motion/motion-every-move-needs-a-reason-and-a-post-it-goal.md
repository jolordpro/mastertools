Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer)'s first agency boss asked why a dot moved through the East River on a map; he could not answer, and the habit of asking why stuck [30:00-30:59].
A reason that satisfies another motion designer (it looks cool) does not satisfy a fundraiser audience that wants figures or a feeling [31:31-32:02].
He kept a post-it on the monitor with the goal; if a flourish does not help it, skip it because there is only so much creative bandwidth per project [1:12:13-1:12:43].

**Use it:** Review question for any motion: what is this move for, and who needs it?
