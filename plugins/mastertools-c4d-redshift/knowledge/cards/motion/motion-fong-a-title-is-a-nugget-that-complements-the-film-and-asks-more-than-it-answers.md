She calls titles a space to make a unique nugget that complements the film but is not the film's visual language [09:58-10:29]. For the comedy Dead Man on Campus she began with brainstorming and board ideas (a mailbox, a cheat sheet, a mocked-up SAT textbook), showing one idea or a few approaches and then fine-tuning boards before production, all while the edit still changes [06:51-08:26]. A comedy title should give viewers the licence to laugh early [04:49]. Her Boardwalk Empire test: it should ask more questions than it answers, since the show answers the rest [46:22].

**Use it:** Start a title or opener by listing three different ideas as boards before choosing, and test each against 'does it repeat the main piece or add to it'.
