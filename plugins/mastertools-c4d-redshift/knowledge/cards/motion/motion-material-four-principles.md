John Schlemmer (2018, Mouvo) gives Material's motion foundation as four words, adapted from Disney's twelve principles: responsive (immediate feedback), natural (easing, arcs), aware (elements know their neighbours), intentional (shared element guides the eye). Each is shown on a shipped Calendar or email screen.
