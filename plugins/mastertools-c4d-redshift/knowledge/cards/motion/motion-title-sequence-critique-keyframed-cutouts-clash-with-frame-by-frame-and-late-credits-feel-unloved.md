Reviewer notes on The Roses titles [00:38-03:13]: one consistent illustration style is what Play Dirty's titles lacked; the thorns animating on last foreshadow the story; a pun and raspberry-for-letter details reward viewers. Weak spots: one illustration seems scaled down with a circular pad to compensate, and the crab claws look like cut-out layers with position and rotation keys, which clashes with the frame-by-frame drawing elsewhere; the subtitle just fades on and off and seems a late addition.
The ending transition, from elegant sheets and a typeface to a character blowing his nose, is called thematically perfect, a nod to Saul and Elaine Bass [05:06-06:01].
He adds he is nitpicking a sequence he enjoyed.

**Use it:** motion-critique checklist item: does each element use the same animation method, and do the minor credits get the same polish as the hero lines.
