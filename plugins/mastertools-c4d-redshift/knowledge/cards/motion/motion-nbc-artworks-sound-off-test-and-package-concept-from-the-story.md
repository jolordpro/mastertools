Sarah Schultz and Ventura Castro (creative directors, artworks, the graphics arm of NBC News/MSNBC, low-medium; promotional talk). Artworks makes daily graphics, show branding and package design for the news and streaming shows [00:36]. Redesigning Nightly News took over a year: new logotype, typography, a built set and an entirely new package with colours the show had not used [01:07]. Test they give: the work should tell the story so that a viewer with no audio knows generally what it is about; title sequences and transitions carry the broader story and the package (full screens, quote panels, tear-outs, lower thirds) carries the details so the audience can read and digest quickly [02:10-02:41]. Concept example: a package for a day of political violence built on redaction and erasing time, giving the day gravity [02:41-03:14]. Work is collaborative with editorial graphics; gloss is easy, but the core is breaking the concept down [03:46-04:16]. Election and debate graphics are branding history because the footage is replayed for years [01:38-02:10].

**Use it:** Broadcast review test for a sports package: mute it; can a viewer tell the story from graphics alone? Package concept comes from the event's meaning, not decoration.
