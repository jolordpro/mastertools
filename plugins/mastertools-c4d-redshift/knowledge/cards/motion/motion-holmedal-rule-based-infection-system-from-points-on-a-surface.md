For a dark, abstract shoe film (Hypervenom) he scattered points on surfaces and transferred information through them, with a rule set that runs on a surface, in a volume or anywhere [04:57-05:59]. Adding a ripple to it on a cloth-like surface turned a weird growth into something that read as fabric, because audiences already associate cloth with softness [05:59-06:30]. The same system drove a shot where the shoe peels its own skin like a snake, supporting the film's idea of things looking like what they are not [06:30-07:07]. He admits he often starts with curiosity and no destination [05:29].

**Use it:** Give an abstract effect a familiar association (cloth ripple, snake skin) so it reads as intentional.
