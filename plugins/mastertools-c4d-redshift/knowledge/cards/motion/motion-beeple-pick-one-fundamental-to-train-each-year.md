Beeple [04:56-05:27]: it is easy to hang on technical knowledge, which is binary (you know how to rig or you do not), while design is subjective and you can only get better at it. He picks a focus each year; in 2017 it was lighting, composition and colour, not new software.
He says he has a huge amount of room to grow in colour and lighting [08:04-08:34]. His dailies aim for an implied story and a cinematic, illustrative feel rather than abstract scenes [08:34].
