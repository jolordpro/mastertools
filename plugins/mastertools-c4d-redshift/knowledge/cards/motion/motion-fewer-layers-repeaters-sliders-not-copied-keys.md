Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer) once built a world shape with dozens of shape layers each carrying a maintain-scale expression; render-heavy and slow [47:09-48:13].
The redo used about three shape layers with repeaters and a slider controlling distance from centre, with extra layers of movement for depth [1:00:13-1:01:44].
A four-dot move was keyframed four times with easing copied; he later saw one duplicated layer would have saved hours [51:21, 1:06:00-1:06:58].
A particle trail forming a triangle needed linear position keys plus a time-remapped precomp to keep sharp corners, then a feathered mask wipe [1:03:50-1:05:25].

**Use it:** Rig review: count layers and copied keyframe sets; prefer repeaters, parent nulls and sliders.
