The first camera keeps mechanical, nauseating turns. A second camera follows it through a spring with a lot of drag and little stiffness, taking the edge off [47:55]. A third follows the second with little drag and some stiffness, so sharp moves overshoot and settle [48:26]. Earlier cameras stay available for precise moves, so it is non-destructive.

**Use it:** Offer as a fix when a keyed move reads robotic; the coach can name overlap and smoothing as the two knobs.
