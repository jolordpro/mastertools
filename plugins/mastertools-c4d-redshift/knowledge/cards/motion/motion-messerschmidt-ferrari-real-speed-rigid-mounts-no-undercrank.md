Messerschmidt says many of the cars were built replicas, so mounting points could be designed in and previsualised before the shoot [00:42].
Mann would not accept the cheats of undercranking or shooting everything in profile to fake speed; he wanted the racers at about 160 km/h all the time, so the mounts were rigid and bolted to the frame, not suction cups [01:05].
He wanted it visceral: handheld camera in the passenger seat and non-stabilised remote heads, so the shake sells the danger [01:22]. Crashes were heavily prepped, with a little CG; he says there was no green-screen car work, and the actors drove on real roads in northern Italy [01:43].

**Use it:** When a sports or vehicle shot has to feel fast, build the cues that come from real speed (rigid mount, vibration, shake) instead of retiming or framing tricks. In a CG shot, add camera shake and vibration rather than only speeding the move up. Transfer is by inference.
