Legato's team did a previs shoot of the whole film with the lead in a mocap suit and a virtual camera, blocking scene by scene and shooting conventional coverage: over-the-shoulder, wide, close-up and crane. The coverage was cut together like a normal film, choosing close-ups for emphasis and wides for others, and that cut became the template for the real shoot. Second-source note (hpaonline, not on this page): he values collaboration and repeated takes, since the way film has been shot for a century works.
