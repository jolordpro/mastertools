Usually she starts from style frames, but with animation on the hook she flipped the order and did C4D tests first, building components after [23:28-23:58].
She used animated splines and X-Particles for coral to avoid keyframing everything [24:29-25:00]. She sent the comp to a director who unified all artists' parts [25:00-25:30].
Host: duplicate, scale, rotate, offset with cloners is the motion-design bread and butter [26:02-26:33].

**Use it:** when design and animation are the same person, prove the motion first; then finalise the frames.
