Enebeis and Wouter Dirks (2026): every identity decision, motion included, must serve the strategy and the audience. If motion is just fluff, step back; the answer may be color or sound. The studio also still designs static work and questions clients who ask for everything to move.

**Use it:** Use it as an anti-rule and a client-conversation line.
