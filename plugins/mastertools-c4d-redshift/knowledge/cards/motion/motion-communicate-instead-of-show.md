Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer) cites a classic cartoon bit where a character walks off, building sounds are heard, and returns with the finished contraption, so the animators skip it [1:10:06-1:10:38].
Animation has always weighed cost against value; cel animators paid for every extra button on every frame [1:11:09-1:11:40].
Challenge: communicate that something happened without showing it, such as a longer hold on a face [1:12:43-1:13:44].

**Use it:** Scope tool: before building an effect, ask whether implying it with a cut, sound or hold does the job.
