Abby works on separate files for versioning, but keeps one document with all boards so she can see how they interact [43:26-44:27].
In that view she looks for colour balance (not half the frames blue) and keeps an emotion-to-time chart marking the big moments, the troughs and the normal beats, so each frame moves the story [44:27-45:00].
For ideation she sketches loosely in Procreate for at least a day if time allows, then picks the frames that feel right and matches them to the script. In the design phase she shifts to different questions, like does it read and is the level of importance right [21:10-21:42, 29:01-29:32].

**Use it:** Before approving boards, view them as a strip and check colour spread, shot size spread and the emotional curve.
