The data points live in a 3D space that physically makes sense, but the pull-back where letters slide into place cannot be real: they chart where the data goes, animate the camera, then go back and track every letter over the top, faking it so letters move into position as the camera pulls back [06:14-06:46]. It was not automated; a 10-frame camera change meant rebuilding from scratch every time [06:46-07:17]. From the side you would see giant E's and X's flying everywhere, but the viewer just accepts it, helped by magician-style swaps like a big O arriving [07:17-08:19].

**Use it:** Budget rebuild time for any cheated 3D move and lock the camera before hand-fitting the type or data.
