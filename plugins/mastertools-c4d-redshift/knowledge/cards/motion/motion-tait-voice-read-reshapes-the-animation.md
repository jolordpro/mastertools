The narrator rewrote the script for himself, delivering an inquisitive, calm read where the team expected gritty and dark. After half-hour talks between takes about the performer's work, his conversational levity shifted the tone and changed their approach to story and visuals [§4]. Feedback on a single take failed [§4].

**Use it:** Record the voice early and let the real read steer tone before locking the animatic.
