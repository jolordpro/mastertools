Her first idea was to put the title in the web, and designers ask why she did not. The director, Gary Winick, told her that the first time words appear in the web is a hugely important moment and he did not want credits to blow it [28:11-28:44]. Her takeaway: titles are part of the whole storytelling arc, so consider what each choice does to the story; a child audience would lose that wonder [28:44-29:14].

**Use it:** Before using a signature device in a title or opener, check that it does not pre-empt a reveal the piece depends on later.
