Material 1's motion page names the same four principles (responsive, natural, aware, intentional) and gives quick duration ranges: small elements about 150-200 ms, larger ones about 300-400 ms on mobile. It adds three transition qualities: quick, clear, cohesive. Fuller specs sit on a separate duration and easing page.

**Use it:** Check against Material 3 before quoting a number; the principles are a stable reference for the Material lineage Naimark and Schlemmer worked in.
