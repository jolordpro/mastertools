Fixed pieces: logo animation, end tag with sonic signature (about 2 seconds for 2B). Everything else in a promo is flexible.
Baxi's ideal split is about 30 fixed and 70 flex; clients push toward 80/20. At global scale ongoing work runs 80/20 and big campaigns open up.
Reason: with too narrow a toolset, creatives ignore the guidelines and there is no brand, only stuff.

**Use it:** State a fixed/flex ratio and name the fixed items when describing a motion system's governance.
