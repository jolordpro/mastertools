Korenman [04:22-12:38]: in the value graph, a flat curve is slow or still and the steep part is fastest. The default combined position keyframe cannot be curved per axis, so right-click and choose Separate Dimensions. Speed graph: the curve's height is speed; he finds it less informative.
Shapes: handles pulled low and flat = slow start; handle pulled up = fast start then slow; inverted curve = most movement in the first third (good for objects firing into frame); long handles on an S give a slow lead-in, a fling and a long deceleration [09:17-12:03].

**Use it:** motion-critique can describe feel as a curve: ask for the handle direction and length, not just easy ease.
