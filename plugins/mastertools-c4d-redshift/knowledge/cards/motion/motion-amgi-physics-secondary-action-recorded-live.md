Because the game engine handles it, anything with a chain of joints, such as hair, chains or an octopus, can be turned into a physics object that behaves like real physics and can touch the ground [09:56, 10:28]. Brady prefers recording it live with the actor on stage, since the performer's rhythm works well with the overlap [10:28]. Everything is exported to the animators, who can keep it on the full rig or delete it and use it as reference [11:00].

**Use it:** For C4D, use dynamics or spring on secondary parts as a first pass, keep the result as editable keyframes, and let the animator replace anything that fights the performance.
