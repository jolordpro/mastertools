The FontShop trade dress was pared down to black and yellow on purpose: digital means light on and light off, a pixel is a square, so two squares, one black and one white [05:26-05:56].
The white square was too subtle, so it became yellow, which is also the colour of light [05:56].
He credits the strength of the brand partly to its generic name and the simple reduction; franchises could use the name and trade dress for a fee [05:26-05:56].
