The screens were 4:3 on the control modules, so he filled green screens in that shape; full-screen UI shots were recreated entirely in 4:3 even though it looked inconsistent, because clear storytelling mattered [13:06-15:42].
He researched drones for two weeks so it would be believable, plus real night-vision looks made from a retouched photo, inverted, with vignetting and grain [08:26-09:30, 14:09-14:39].

**Use it:** check graphics against the real display ratio and a believable reference before style.
