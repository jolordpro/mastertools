ECD Hazel Baird and UK designer J.T. Child reviewed the script and made a detailed reference deck for the client. The client wanted an organic, non-mechanical look, which was intricate; Baird was determined it not be a soulless AI construct but deeply connected to the episode [§2-3].

**Use it:** Before building, make a reference deck that names what the look is not (mechanical, generic) and ties it to the story.
