Rules stated at [10:22-14:01]: set a key on the first and last frame and keep a simple move (translate only or rotate only); more keyframes is the fastest route to jerky motion; keep splines smooth, since unlike character work a camera rarely needs jagged organic curves; imagine a person or heavy rig holding it, so a whip needs a build-up and a slow-down; do not roll the camera (the roll axis triggers motion sickness and a boat feel) unless the Dutch angle is on purpose; choose the axis you touch on purpose instead of free-rotating on all three.
Depth of field is better left to a comp effect while animating, because it is slow to preview in some tools [09:51].

**Use it:** a camera-move review list for the coach: count keys, check for roll, check ease in and out has mass; for a demo reel skip aggressive camera moves unless the move is intentional.
