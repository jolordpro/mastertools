Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer) drops a shape that continues the previous frame's last element so the eye has something to follow; on the same cut the right-hand rectangle arrives first so it catches the eye, even though logic says the left would [33:36-34:39].
If both sides competed, viewers might miss the slider dropping in [34:08].
Principle: motion should communicate the concept and not distract from it, so overdoing it costs clarity [34:39-35:09].
Also a collapse-to-the-middle idea was dropped as too much; shapes instead popped out directly into the next layout [35:09-37:15].
