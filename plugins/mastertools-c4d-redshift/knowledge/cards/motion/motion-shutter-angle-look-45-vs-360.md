Both extremes look exaggerated; most films use 180 [03:31]. Saving Private Ryan used 45 or 90 degrees with handheld for grit [08:24]; Collateral used a wide shutter for night light and haze [09:25]; Gladiator battle used 45 degrees plus 6 fps step-printed four times [10:33-11:04].
