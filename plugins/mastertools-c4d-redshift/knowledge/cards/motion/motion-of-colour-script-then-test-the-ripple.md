Abby makes a rough colour script, often a wash of colour over the sketches in one document, to solve colour before final styleframes [37:49, 40:53-41:23].
Things she saw by placing colours side by side: teal and pink buzz when overlapped; blue neutralised the saturation of yellow and teal [37:49-38:21]. Overlaps of many colours were saved for the biggest moment [38:21-38:51].
Jorge: change one thing and think about the ripple. If shot 3's background goes yellow, the next shot cannot be yellow; if nothing works, adjust the palette slightly [39:52-40:53]. Abby spots where a frame feels off before she can say why.
Rules (complementary, triad) help, but she ends about half by feel [41:54-42:55].

**Use it:** Build a one-page colour script of the whole sequence and move one shot at a time while watching the neighbours.
