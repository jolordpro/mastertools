He admires Peter Weir, who plays classical music walking onto set, and says Scorsese pre-selects the music he shoots a scene to [34:58-35:30, 35:30-36:00]. For the Apollo 13 launch he picked heroic music from films and shot to it; after stripping it out, the scene stayed intact and the composer scored to the melody or rhythm built into the edit, the sub-edit and the shot's beginning, middle and end [36:00-36:30]. He says music is not an afterthought; the story tells composers and cinematographers the vocabulary to use [36:30-37:01].

**Use it:** Animate and cut to a temp track, then hand off the cut with the rhythm intact so the final music can follow it.
