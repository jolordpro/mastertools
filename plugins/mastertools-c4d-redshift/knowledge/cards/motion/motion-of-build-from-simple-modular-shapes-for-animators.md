Abby built characters and objects from a small set of shapes (a dinosaur, a bobsled) so animators can rearrange or build their own and still stay in style [45:15-46:31]. The idea came from an early conversation about modularity, like toy blocks that make a duck or a dinosaur [46:00-46:31].
Jorge says designers who do not know motion often give unexpected results because they do not limit themselves by how hard it will be, and animators then try any transition drawn [16:02-18:36]. Animators are checked with during design to keep it achievable [27:29-28:00].
Shape language: he mentions a book on perception for why shape pairings work (title heard as "Art of Perception", unclear) [48:04].

**Use it:** Hand animators a kit, not a locked picture, and let designers draw what looks right first.
