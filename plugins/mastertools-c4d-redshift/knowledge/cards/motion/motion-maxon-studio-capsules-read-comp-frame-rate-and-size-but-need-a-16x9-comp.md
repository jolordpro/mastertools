Chad Perkins (Maxon) applied one infographic capsule to three comps, 720p at 30, 1080p at 60 and 4K at 24 [03:39-05:44]. Inside each, the nested comp had been set to 29.97, 59.94 and 23.976 fps with no input. Any resolution works as long as the aspect ratio is 16:9 [05:11].
A capsule needs a composition to land in (blank is fine); it does not apply to a layer or a whole project [09:30].
Capsules are built so deleting layers at random did not throw errors [07:24].
Launch library: over 250 capsules, mostly by Candy Mustache [08:25].

**Use it:** when briefing template-based AE work, check aspect ratio first; non-16:9 deliverables are outside what the tool promises.
