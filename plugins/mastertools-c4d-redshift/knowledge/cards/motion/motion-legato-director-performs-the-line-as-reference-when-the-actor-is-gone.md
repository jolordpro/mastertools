Because a director who acts can perform it, they photographed Favreau lip-syncing the words and feeling like King Louie or Baloo (captions say "blue", probably Baloo); his mannerisms, movement and changes of eyeline informed the animators [08:58].
The reason: in an animated film the actors are usually finished and there is no one to call back to reshoot or show the beat [09:29].
Favreau's other contribution was a consistent eye for falsehood in a performance, a framing, or a shot that went too far toward beautiful [08:26].

**Use it:** When talent is no longer available, shoot reference video of whoever can act the beat (director, animator) with the camera on the face and eyelines.
