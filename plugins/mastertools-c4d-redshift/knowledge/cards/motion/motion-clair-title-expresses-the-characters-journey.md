His test for a good title: if it states what is fundamentally happening to the character, it stays worth returning to because more is gained as the audience learns the character. His example is Mad Men, a man in free-fall that describes Don Draper across the whole run. He calls the job finding that emotional claw [§2].

**Use it:** State the character journey in one image before choosing style.
