Fong starts with a clip track or mood music, often asked of the show's composer, who may not have composed anything yet [44:10-44:42].
She looks for dynamic changes or a build that propels the piece, and searches a composer's older work if the samples are flat [44:42-45:14].
If everyone falls for the temp track and other music fails, license it; the Bedazzled title was cut to a country song that heightened the comedy and survived a techno alternative [45:14-46:17].
Mad Men used a contemporary RJD2 track on period visuals, and on Boardwalk Empire she tried the same cut with Bob Dylan, a 1920s ditty and classical, ending on a contemporary piece so the period drama feels current [46:17-47:20].
Best case: storyboards shown to the composer, who sends a draft to time against [47:51].

**Use it:** Cut the same animatic against two or three very different tracks (one contemporary against a period piece) before locking, and give the composer boards early.
