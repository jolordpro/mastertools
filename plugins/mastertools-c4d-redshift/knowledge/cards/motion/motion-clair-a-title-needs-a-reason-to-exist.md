Clair says a title that only wraps the show becomes a skip-intro target. The brief with the showrunner was: we can make something pretty, but what can we do that is useful? For The Peripheral the usefulness was to say subconsciously that the show links one era to a far future. The idea became a thin tether stretching through a globe from America to London. He compares it to The Man in the High Castle titles, which also carried information (the map of the split US). [§2-§4]

**Use it:** Write down what information the title carries before choosing a style.
