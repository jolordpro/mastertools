Nolan contacted Franklin in early 2013, before the script was started, to talk concepts. For the ending the team studied depictions of time in cubist painting and slit-scan photography, modelled the concept in 3D, then had the art department build a physical model of part of it. It was the first thing started and the last finished, during the final month of shooting. The black-hole renderer was built with physicist Kip Thorne over nearly a year. [§1]

**Use it:** For an abstract idea, gather art-history references first and then build a physical or 3D study.
