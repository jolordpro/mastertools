SonduckFilm [00:32-03:40]. Show the waveform with LL, stretch the title across the comp, listen, and split the layer (Edit > Split Layer) at each beat, typing the next word on every segment. The author calls changing background and colour palette on the beat the most important sync technique: a new Solid per beat that ends exactly at the sync point, a title colour picked by eyedropper from the previous background, or a photo under a beat's word at lowered opacity (T key). Palette change gives the tonal shift that makes cuts read as rhythm.

**Use it:** Cheap first pass for beat-locked titles: hard cuts of text and colour on each beat, then add motion.
