The relief bands on the astrolabe first had five depictions, then three, because even three sat right on the edge of being understood. Wall likes that edge: the titles play about ten times in season one, so viewers should find something new each time. Second-source note (The Ringer, not on this page): for the season 8 version, choosing which three events to show was called the hardest part.
