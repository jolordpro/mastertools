Step one is the mocap and virtual-camera previs shoot and cut. Step two is a blueprint of each live shot from that cut (for example the boy 100 feet from camera with trees around him and fake cucoloris for shadow), shot as an element. Step three: attach the previs backgrounds to the real camera so framing is done knowing what is in frame. Step four is marrying the pieces to look believable. Final step is rendering the world and animal animation, with animals doing what real ones do and digital doubles doing real physics. [§3]

**Use it:** Order any hybrid job so each step informs the next, with the edit settled before the expensive shoot.
