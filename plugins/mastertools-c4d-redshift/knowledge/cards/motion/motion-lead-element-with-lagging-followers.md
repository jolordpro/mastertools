Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer)'s creation sequence had a dot standing for wisdom start the motion, with a rotating element behind it offset a couple of frames so they do not move exactly together [51:52-52:22].
A light parented to the dot brought on stars so they expand with the lead, and the sequence feels alive without viewers knowing why [52:22-52:52].
For richness he used grayscale gradient strokes and tinted them to different colours, so rings are slightly different without extra art [48:13-49:48].

**Use it:** Motion rule: assign a leader and let followers lag by a few frames; vary tints with one source.
