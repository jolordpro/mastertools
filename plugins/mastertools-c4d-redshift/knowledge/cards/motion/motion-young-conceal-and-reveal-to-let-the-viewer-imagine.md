In a photograph by the Pittsburgh photographer Teenie Harris (captions: "Timmy"/"Tiny" Harris), a body lies off the left edge, over a cliff, and the story is told by restraint, not showing it [13:40]. He says cinema can break time by cutting or extend it with camera movement, and gives the audience a developing story by concealing and revealing, with camera moves or with light and shadow [14:10]. Also: hidden things emerge as they move toward the light, an idea he takes from a painter he names (captions: "Lynette Item"; likely Lynette Yiadom-Boakye) [35:56].

**Use it:** In a reveal or transition, decide what stays off-frame or in shadow until the beat, rather than showing everything.
