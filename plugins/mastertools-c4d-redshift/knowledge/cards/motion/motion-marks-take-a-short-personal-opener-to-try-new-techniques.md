For the Semi-Permanent 2015 opener he took a month off usual work to explore new techniques and images he had in mind. Concept and production fit in a tight 4 weeks, which Cinema 4D's versatility helped him manage by focusing on key elements [§1]. The influences were a 1968 LIFE astronaut series and 2001: A Space Odyssey, which he says hints at something surreal and undefinable [§1].

**Use it:** Time-box a personal piece to test one new technique; use a single strong reference image as the spine.
