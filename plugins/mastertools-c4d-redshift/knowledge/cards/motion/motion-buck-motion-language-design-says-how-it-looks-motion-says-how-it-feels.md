Two BUCK animators (Amsterdam/London; first names Daniel and Joe as heard in the talk) [03:09-05:13]. Take cues from design: rough, textured design gets lower frame rate and rougher keyframes; UI or vector design gets higher frame rate and precise keyframing. Human expectations (leaf in wind, gravity) steer the rest. "Functional" motion (A to B) can be enough; the extra step is "ownability": a bespoke language that connects to the brand and brief [11:11-12:46]. Brands do not want to be defined only by how they look but how they move.

**Use it:** a start-of-project question for the motion system work: what does the design's contrast mean for timing and frame rate?
