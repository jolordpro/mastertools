Make squares of the colours needed (many blue tones, several skin tones), select all, create a new colour group from the selection, and tick convert process to global; this step is described as essential [04:04][04:36]. Apply swatches to shapes; editing a swatch with preview on (HSB) recolours all layers using it, e.g. ear, face and neck at once [05:07]. A shadow can be a shadow colour at 50 percent opacity when a darker tone is missing [04:36]. Keep the palette limited ("not 16 versions of blue") and adjust slowly until it balances [05:37]. Colour variants can come from changing just the sky and buildings plus a pink or orange rectangle at low opacity in soft light [09:44].

**Use it:** same idea as shared colour tokens in C4D or AE: one swatch change, whole frame updates.
