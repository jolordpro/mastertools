Rich subjects tempt you to include everything; the discipline is deciding what you keep and working around it [1:04:12]. Her examples: Game of Thrones stays a map and board game rather than cutting in photography; Mad Men skips sets and actors and keeps one falling man [1:04:43-1:05:13]. She calls it discipline and courage, and says the titles that win peer respect are the restrained ones [1:05:13].

**Use it:** Shakedown/elevate test for a package: name the one visual language; list what was left out on purpose. If an element does not belong to it, cut it.
