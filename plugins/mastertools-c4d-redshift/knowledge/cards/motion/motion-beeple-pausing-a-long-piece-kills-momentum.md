Beeple [10:02-11:43]: his minute-long short films come about every two years and keep getting longer because of freelance work. When he sets a piece aside for a week or two, coming back means "what was I doing", and it takes a long time to rebuild momentum.
He says he wishes he were better at deadlines and milestones for these [10:40]. He credits the daily habit's built-up momentum rather than discipline for keeping it going [11:12].
Transfer: end each work session by writing the next three steps, so a pause costs little.

**Use it:** The platform's project notes keep a 'next steps' stub on every paused project.
