Not by Ordinary Folk; a third-party reverse engineering. Square shape layers scale from 0 over 16 frames with 60 percent influence ease on the first key; six layers in the six-colour palette are each offset by two frames and pre-comped [00:33-01:36]. A duplicate comp is masked along the diagonals to separate the top and bottom quadrants from left and right, flipped, and the two sets offset by six frames, with the last shape set to a single green so the end resolves to one colour [02:09-04:15]. Hosts: MotionXP.

**Use it:** A tunnel of layered scale loops can be built from offsets and masks before any 3D is added.
