Order: collect references; thumbnail the composition; refined sketches that decide every shape (head, eyes) with no colour or effects; vector shapes drawn in grayscale to judge value and contrast; then colour; then effects [00:32][02:02][02:33][03:04]. Reason given: changing a character in one image is far cheaper than in two minutes of animation, and one pass for shape/values plus one for colour is easier than both together [01:32][06:09]. Sketch placed top layer at about 20 percent, multiply, locked, while tracing [03:04]. Design on a 1920 x 1920 artboard so it crops to 16:9, square and vertical [03:04]. Background characters get small face changes so they do not look cloned [03:34].

**Use it:** sequence for styleframe reviews: do not give colour notes before the values read.
