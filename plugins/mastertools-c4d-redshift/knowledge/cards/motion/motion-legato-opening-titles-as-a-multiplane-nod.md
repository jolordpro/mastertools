Legato had cel-animated fireworks shot a frame at a time with a still camera, each frame through red, green and blue filters to get a Technicolor-style patina. He tested the multiplane move in After Effects, composited in Nuke, and MPC refined the 3D so the old multiplane shot blended into the CG one. Intent: show respect for where the studio came from. [§11]

**Use it:** If a title has an era to nod to, shoot or fake the original process steps, not just a filter.
