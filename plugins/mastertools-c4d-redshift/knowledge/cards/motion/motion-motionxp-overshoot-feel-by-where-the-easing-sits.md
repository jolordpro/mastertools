MotionXP [05:44-08:18]. Same key pair (0 to 110 in about 4 frames, then back) feels different by where the ease is: strong easing on first and last keys makes the overshoot hit hard and bounce back like a speaker; strong easing either side of the middle key gives the opposite feel; zero ease at the start and a retimed second key with a late handle gives a locking-into-place feel. Elastic: first overshoot 120, then keys every six frames alternately above and below the end value with shrinking amplitude, then easy ease them. Rotation suits it better than scale. Pulling a value-graph handle above the zero line adds an anticipation on the anticipation. Warning: do not wobble every object.

**Use it:** Notes vocabulary for pop-ins; overusing elastic across a whole scene is a cheapness tell.
