StudioBinder, The Shot List Ep 6 [07:07-12:50]. Push in emphasises a moment or a detail (an object, text) and can show a character's inner conflict (a slow push toward a character deciding on a point of no return raises tension). Pull out de-emphasises the subject, reveals setting and context as the subject shrinks, and can read as isolation or abandonment. A zoom changes focal length rather than moving the camera; it is unnatural because human eyes cannot zoom, and slow zooms build unease in thrillers. A fast zoom is a crash zoom: a dramatic, abrupt effect that can also be comic.

**Use it:** Notes vocabulary for a camera move: say what the move is meant to do (emphasise, reveal, isolate) when approving or rejecting a push, pull or zoom.
