Fong began by visiting the Los Angeles set with the showrunner and executive producer, photographing period detail such as binders, a handbook and IBM Selectric typewriters with German keyboards [§4]. She also researched Cold War and Berlin design [§4]. With the Go idea in her head she kept picking up circles, grids and dots, joking that with a hammer everything looks like a nail [§4]. Thumbnails came first, then storyboards by a graphic-minded designer and a 3D designer, with two small figures seeking each other in abstract Berlin landscapes [§4].

**Use it:** Suggest a visit or photo reference pass first, then thumbnails, before any full boards.
