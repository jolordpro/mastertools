In the opening minutes of WWDC25 session 219 Chan Karunamuni says glass elements materialize through gradual change in lensing, keeping the material intact, rather than a fade. Glass flexes and glows on touch, lifts only during interaction (quiet at rest), and morphs between controls as a user changes context, so one floating plane persists. He says motion and look were designed together.
