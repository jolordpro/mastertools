A growing pile of the client's magic books plus alchemy, manuscript and psychedelic art sat in the conference room, and the team leafed through them at dailies and breaks so styles seeped in. Schmid says large printed illustration is processed differently from online ref and pulls from the subconscious [§2].

**Use it:** Put physical reference in the room and browse it during breaks; do not rely only on a folder of web images.
