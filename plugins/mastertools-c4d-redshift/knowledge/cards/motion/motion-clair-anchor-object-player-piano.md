The piano was the showrunner's idea. His team photographed every inch of the real prop and rebuilt it digitally for the titles, treating it as an anchor because a self-playing piano is the closest metaphor to the robot hosts and to the question of where machine ends and person begins [§5].

**Use it:** Pick one real object from the show to carry the idea, and rebuild it from photography so it matches.
