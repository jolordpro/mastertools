In the Best of Stash 2024 write-up, director Nadia Tzuo (Elastic) says the team worked with the show's creatives to find a concept that captures the tone of Shogun while reflecting the philosophy of the story. They landed on a walled garden, which they felt illustrates Japan and its political climate in 1600. As the picture became clearer with production, they made adjustments so the visual language and subtle undertone aligned with the show [§1].
