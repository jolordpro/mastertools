The first Boardwalk Empire concept was an animatic of a kingdom by the sea with a carnival day and a shady underbelly, set to period music and planned as a large shoot on the Brooklyn boardwalk set [26:30-28:07]. The creator called it beautiful but asked how unique it was and whether it was a true metaphor, and pushed it down to one idea, change, with Nucky as the one constant, seen through liquor [28:40-29:42]. The result was a two-day shoot: a stage day and a beach day, much more metaphorical [30:13]. She notes it is good when a client pushes back a bit.

**Use it:** When a note says "pretty but not specific", reduce rather than add: pick one constant and one lens, and expect the production plan to get smaller. Cheaper to ask early than to polish the big version.
