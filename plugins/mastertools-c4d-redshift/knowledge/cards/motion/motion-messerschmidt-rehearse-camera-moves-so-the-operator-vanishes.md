A dolly arriving after the actor or a tilt that follows a stand-up late signals a camera operator in the room [19:50]. Mindhunter rehearsed moves with the actors, operator and dolly grip so the camera arrives on the beats (stand up, lean in) [20:23]. The result is near-perfect composition that removes the operator and lets the actor speak to the audience through the frame [20:53]. He calls it technical acting built from practice and discipline, not magic [20:53-21:23].

**Use it:** For virtual cameras: key camera arrivals to the subject's action beat, with the move settling on or just ahead of it, never after.
