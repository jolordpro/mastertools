He says there is no special method or inspiration: he looks at the brief, takes it apart, looks at comparable briefs, makes analog sketches, discusses with colleagues and the client, and keeps condensing the sketches, at some point digitally [§2]. He never works alone and every project's setting differs [§3]. Inspiration comes from observing the visual world: technology, trends, designers and cultures [§1].

**Use it:** Teach the order: brief breakdown, comparables, hand sketches, discussion, then screen.
