In 2017 Airbnb's design language system had hundreds of reusable, named components (names match the code names), kept by a design side and an engineering side of the DLS team, with 100+ designers and engineers on one native app. A handful of components accepted Lottie animations. A designer used one of those or went through the process of adding a new component.

**Use it:** Route motion through a short list of motion-ready components so rollout stays governed.
