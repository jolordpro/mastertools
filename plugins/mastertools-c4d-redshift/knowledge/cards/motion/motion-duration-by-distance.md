Schlemmer says duration should follow distance and peak velocity rather than a fixed number. His example figures: tablets about 400-450 ms, watches about 150-200 ms; web should be very fast. Figures are from a 2017 talk, not the current spec.

**Use it:** Cite with the year; check Material 3 values before reusing a number.
