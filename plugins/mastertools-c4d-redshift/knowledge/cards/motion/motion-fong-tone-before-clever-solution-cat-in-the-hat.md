Her ordering for short title pieces: tone is the number one consideration, because the piece is an invitation that sets what to expect [46:46-47:49]. Her example: on The Cat in the Hat her team built an elaborate 3D Rube Goldberg credits machine from the production designer's props. The star and filmmakers said no, because audiences first want the feeling of the blue Dr. Seuss book on a parent's lap. The team threw it out for a 2D version drawn from Seuss's own drawings, starting with the studio logo in that style [47:49-49:22]. Intellectually correct, emotionally wrong.
A series differs from a feature: a series title is seen over and over; a feature title leads into one specific scene [47:16].

**Use it:** In a concept review, ask "what do viewers need to feel at the first frame?" before checking the idea against the design logic. Applies to sports opens too (energy and stakes, then clever).
