DIA's kinetic identity starts from the idea that a brand, like a person, is recognised by its movement. Rules come from physics (gravity, friction, bounce), music rhythm (accents on different beats, offset grids) and choreography, applied to type.

**Use it:** Use it as the framing sentence for a behavior-first motion language; tie each rule to a natural or musical reference.
