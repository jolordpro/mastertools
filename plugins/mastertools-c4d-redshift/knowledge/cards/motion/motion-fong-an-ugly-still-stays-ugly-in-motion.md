Many motion people assume movement excuses detail; she says that is false: an ugly still is ugly moving, and bad kerning is just bad type with motion blur [1:04:34-1:05:06]. Bring composition, photography, layout and typography into the work. For hiring she says nobody hires a style; they hire the thinking, so show a piece you love that reveals it, and include strong stills or posters if that is your strength [1:02:32-1:04:34]. Her own entry piece was an animated alphabet book, and its transitions are what got Kyle Cooper's attention [1:03:02].

**Use it:** Review the hero frame as a still, with type and composition judged before any motion is added.
