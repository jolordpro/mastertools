Rob Bennett (animator) [11:41-12:44]: animating the 15 ft travel physically accurately was a proof of concept that lost energy and spontaneity, so he and the director agreed to cheat: the bear scales up during the leap to fill the frame, and does not move at all while the camera moves, then moves again. Dylan Maxwell (character sim TD) [14:46]: sim must never fight a pose; the animator pulled the far lip away so the tooth reads in silhouette, because the shot is seen for a moment only. Water FX (Greg Gladstone) [15:49-17:52]: free simulation (30 million points) gave splashes in the wrong places and not in frame; hand-placing each splash where wanted and feeding them one at a time into the sim gave controllable shape and height. FX is meant to add only enough effect to tell the story without covering the performance. Lighting (Rosalinda Malabiran team) [21:36-23:08]: 20-30 lights per shot, cheated away from one sun, with a tableau of which light does what; extra light on the face to show teeth.

**Use it:** Notes vocabulary: "read in silhouette", "cheat for energy". For splash or particle accents, art-direct placement instead of trusting a full sim.
