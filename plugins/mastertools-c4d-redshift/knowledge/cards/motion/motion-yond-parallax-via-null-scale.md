Almost every shot has parallax with layers spread in 3D space. To avoid pushing a layer back then rescaling it by hand, he parents the layer to a null and scales the null up. The layer moves back in Z and scales up so it keeps the same pixels as the design [45:01-46:01].
He unparents before moving the camera. A cone of scaled layers is visible in the 3D view [46:01].

**Use it:** a quick way to depth-stack flat layers for a 2.5D camera move without ruining layout.
