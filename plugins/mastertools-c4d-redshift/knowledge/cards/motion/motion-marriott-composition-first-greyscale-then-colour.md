Ben Marriott [44:33-49:46]: while composing he thinks only of composition and light-dark relationships (dark background, lighter hero), not hue. The mood board already fixed the colour feel. He redraws thumbnails fresh in Photoshop instead of tracing them, because the thumbnails are wonky and re-drawing forces new decisions [40:27]. Then he builds grayscale vector shapes to test contrast, so when something fails he knows if it is colour or shape. Illustrator layers can be saved as PSD to keep them separated for colouring in Photoshop [50:16].

**Use it:** a composition check on a greyscale render of the frame, before judging colour. Matches the composition skill.
