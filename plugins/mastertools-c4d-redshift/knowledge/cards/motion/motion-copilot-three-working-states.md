Microsoft's Copilot identity (Oct 2026) defines motion around three moments when the assistant works for the user: thinking, planning, and retrieving context from files. Each state is one gesture, not a bespoke animation.

**Use it:** Name the states an AI product must express before designing frames; keep the list to three.
