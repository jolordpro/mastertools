From the 2018 Forward Magazine interview: asked what good design is, Spiekermann says it needs to solve the problem plus add an extra level of aesthetic pleasure [§1]. His biggest design mistake taught him never to promise more than he can actually keep [§2]. He often took complex projects that were too boring for other designers, and likes working with people smarter than him in some areas [§1].

**Use it:** Check function (reads, finds the key line) before ornament.
