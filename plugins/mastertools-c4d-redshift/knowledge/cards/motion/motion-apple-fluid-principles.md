Eight principles from one WWDC18 session: respond with no added delay; allow redirection; keep entry and exit paths symmetric; hint toward the gesture's end state; amplify a light input into a bigger output; soften boundaries (rubber-banding, smooth hand-off between two tracked things); keep adjacent frames close enough not to strobe; treat motion as behavior, not a fixed clip. Framed as aligning interface with how people think and move.

**Use it:** Use as a named, countable principle set when documenting a gesture-driven system.
