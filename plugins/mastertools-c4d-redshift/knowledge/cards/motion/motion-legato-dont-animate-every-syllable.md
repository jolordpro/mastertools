Tests with every syllable animated looked too funky. Legato points out real actors sometimes do not move their lips for the next word. The animals speak as if talking were possible with the jaw they have, and no more. MPC and animation supervisor Andy Jones also copied reference clips for mood without needing to know why, then translated for a human audience rather than for 'wolf'. [§7-§8]

**Use it:** Limit mouth shapes to a believable subset and let the eyes and head carry the line.
