Cooper in his 2014 AIGA medal profile [02:42-03:51]: the name Prologue comes from the Henry V chorus. A main title is a prologue: the title sequence becomes the first scene of the movie and sometimes seeps into the body of the film.
He says people respond to the work for an earnestness. Designers are storytellers and content creators: you must make it look good, also communicate, and try to make somebody feel something, which is hard [03:16].
He is proud of elevating main titles and of being a graphic designer who is a filmmaker; he was told the world needs more of both, and he says the best filmmakers are graphic designers because they know how to see [03:16-03:51].

**Use it:** The motion critique asks of an open or title: what does this tell the viewer before the first shot, and what should they feel.
