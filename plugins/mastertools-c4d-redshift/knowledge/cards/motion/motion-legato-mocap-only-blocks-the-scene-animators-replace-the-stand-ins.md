Motion capture was used to block where animals would go and not to inform their movement, apart from things like walking from the tree to sit over there [06:22-06:53].
They built the scene photographically with a game-like stand-in version, then handed it to animators to replace the stand-ins with the real animals (skeleton, muscle, skin, hair) and capitalise on the camera and position choices already made [06:53-07:56]. They kept exploring the scene with actors and camera until it worked, like a live set [07:25].

**Use it:** Block with cheap stand-ins, lock camera and placement, and hand the locked cut to animation as a spec.
