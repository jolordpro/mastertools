Nathan de Vries: scrolling and tap-status-bar-to-top use different drivers but the page should move the same way. A playful app should be playful throughout so learning one behavior carries to the next. Combining motion with sound and haptics changes how a scroll feels. His warning: bounciness needs a purpose and a balance with utility.

**Use it:** Write one character line per product and check each new behavior against it.
