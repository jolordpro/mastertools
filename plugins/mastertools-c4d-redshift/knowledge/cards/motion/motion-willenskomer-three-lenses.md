Willenskomer's 2015 talk lists three checks: velocity curves, start/stop timing (offset a close button a few frames to rank it), and continuity/context (motion keeps spatial orientation). In 2020 he says functional motion supports mental models and cognitive load, and additive delight is maybe five percent. Self-reported framework; he also sells training on it.
