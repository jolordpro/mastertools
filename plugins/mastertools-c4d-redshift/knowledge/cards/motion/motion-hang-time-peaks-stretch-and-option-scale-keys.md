Korenman [23:04-25:44]: select all keys, hold Option (Alt/Ctrl on PC) and drag the first or last key to scale timing; then select the key at each bounce top and pull their handles together so the object hangs longer. He notes squash and stretch would add more but did not animate it.

**Use it:** to push a move toward cartoon, lengthen the hang at the apex rather than slowing the whole move.
