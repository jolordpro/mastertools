He spends roughly one to two hours a day, often posts work he does not like, and counts a finished post as the clear end point. His stated driver is avoiding embarrassment in public. Volume is the route to taste.

**Use it:** Set a stop rule per frame; ship a finished 85 percent and let the next one improve.
