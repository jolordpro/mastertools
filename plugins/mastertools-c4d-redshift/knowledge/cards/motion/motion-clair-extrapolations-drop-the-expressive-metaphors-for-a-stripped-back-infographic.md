After reading all eight scripts (unusual; usually one or two), the first ask was world-building, so early work was footage-led and laid out the century's events [01:34-02:37]. As cuts came together the show clearly did a lot of world-building, so they tried expressive statues-and-gods metaphors, but the show already goes to many places and another place each episode felt wrong [02:37-03:08]. They fell back on his early infographic experience: something stripped back and elegant, with the researcher able to place statistics without it being dry. He stresses it is an extrapolation, not a prediction [03:08-04:09].

**Use it:** When a title or opener has to sit before varied content, ask whether it should add another place or hold a single stable device.
