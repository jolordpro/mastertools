Fraser [01:37-02:39]: for the fights they did not want a cut-fest, so they gave the editor big chunks of each fight that he did not have to edit much. Action is often cut up because the punch does not look like it lands or the stunt looks wrong from that angle, forcing a switch to another angle.
They were diligent in choosing angles that best represent what is happening so the action reads without covering cuts.
He also says he does not see Dune 2 as a visual-effects movie: he is concentrating on the minutiae of the human experience with actors and characters, and effects happen beyond those experiences [00:04-01:07].
