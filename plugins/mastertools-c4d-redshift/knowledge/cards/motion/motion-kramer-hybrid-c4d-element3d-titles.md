Kramer was building an elaborate animated title sequence for a big production company in Cinema 4D with Redshift and Octane, comping in After Effects. He says some hero shots of 3D models were rendered in Element 3D and sent to the C4D renderer on extraction, using Cineware for camera and lights (the wording in the interview is unclear). Element 3D was also used for many small details and 3D post-processing effects. On learning, he says tutorials show what is possible, but the only real way to learn is to make something.

**Use it:** Split work by the cheapest tool that holds the look; keep hero shots on the best renderer.
