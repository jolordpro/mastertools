Animators posed within the limits of normal rigs; Genndy Tartakovsky drew over frames asking for more extreme poses, and when the answer was the rig cannot do that, he told them to figure it out [55:41].
They then rigged and sculpted each frame to fit his drawings, giving the most extreme posing he has seen in 3D animation [55:41-56:11].
His point: this taste and point of view is what AI would not supply.

**Use it:** craft habit for notes: draw over a frame to show the target, and expect the tool or rig to be pushed past its comfort zone.
