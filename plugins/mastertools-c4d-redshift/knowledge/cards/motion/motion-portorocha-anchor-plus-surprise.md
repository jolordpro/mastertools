Their strategy: an iconic anchor people recognise over time, made surprising in context through animation, behavior, sound or materials. Kunsthalle Basel example: modular logo with constant push and pull inside a traditional structure.

**Use it:** Use it as a two-layer rule: fixed anchor, flexing behavior.
