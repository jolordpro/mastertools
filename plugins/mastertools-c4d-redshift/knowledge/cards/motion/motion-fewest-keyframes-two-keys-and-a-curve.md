Korenman [35:01-36:02]: people who want an object nearly there by frame 12 add a third key; with two keys and a curve it is one edit. Professional work always changes, and two keys instead of four mean half the revision time.

**Use it:** keyframe check: flag extra mid-keys that mimic a curve.
