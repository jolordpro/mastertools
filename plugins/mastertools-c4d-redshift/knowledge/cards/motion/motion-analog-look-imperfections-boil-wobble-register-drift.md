The Video Shop critique of the Fantastic Four: First Steps titles (by Perception) [03:10-10:55]. Physical titles came from a rostrum camera and optical printing, so they carry slight vibration, uneven edges, softness, contrast shifts and grain; limits shaped the style. Frames pop on one by one because that was easier than fades. Timing was deliberately linear, with only a subtle ease-out on one title (checked by plotting equal-spaced arrows that bunch up before landing). Critique: a Roughen Edges boil that stays static after the line animates looks digital; period lines wobble after they animate and cutouts are imperfect. A reused text animator preset (randomised character order) reads as time-pressure. A studio he cites printed every frame per colour register, rescanned and realigned thousands of prints, so the drift between channels is real.

**Use it:** For a retro/analog treatment, specify the imperfection as motion (wobble, boil, drift) not a static texture, and vary the text animation from title to title.
