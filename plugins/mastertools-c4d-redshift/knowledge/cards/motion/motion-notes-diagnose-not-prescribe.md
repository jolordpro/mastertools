Sarofsky: say what is not working, let the artist solve it. Oddfellows: give the reason for a note and offer alternatives such as a director's cut. Chris Do: tiny precise notes drain a creative.
Pixar's Braintrust reportedly diagnoses without prescribing (second-hand book summaries).

**Use it:** word coach notes as symptom, reason and options, never a bare instruction.
