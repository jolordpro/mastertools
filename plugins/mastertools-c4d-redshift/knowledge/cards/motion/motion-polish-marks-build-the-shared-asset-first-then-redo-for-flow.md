Marks started with the astronaut because it was in every shot and the most complex, then built the other shots' assets and played with composition, lighting and timing [§4]. To keep perpetual motion he went back and redid scenes so a fast move in one shot echoed into the next [§4]. He rendered full shots, then did comp and grade; a few shots came and went but the result stayed near the style boards [§4]. Cinema 4D plus Octane (unbiased, no flicker, GPU-driven) made a month-long solo job possible [§5, §2].

**Use it:** Plan the shared hero asset first and review shot-to-shot momentum, not just single frames.
