Asked about shorter attention spans, she says her team made several versions for Lost in Space (title-card-only reveal and full sequence) and for Castle Rock. Her preference is still a full, robust sequence that can feel like a short film, with flexibility for a cut-down. Conversations include varying titles per season, hidden Easter eggs, or per show, but schedules and budgets must catch up [§7].

**Use it:** When delivering a package, plan a full cut plus a short version up front instead of trimming at the end.
