Salih Abdul-Karim (Android Summit 2017) says the team did not drop animation in everywhere. Three tests: an educational moment (e.g. ID-photo flow that shows lighting and framing), continuity (search icon turns into a globe as the bar expands), reinforcing emotion (a city-specific animation after booking).

**Use it:** Give every motion a stated job from a short list before it is built.
