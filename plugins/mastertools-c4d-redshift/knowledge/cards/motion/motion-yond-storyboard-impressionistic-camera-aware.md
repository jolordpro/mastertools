Alissa considers camera angles, moves and composition from the first board, and may plan a wipe between frames, such as an element passing in front of the foreground [32:34-33:36].
She avoids being too prescriptive in boards or style frames: leaving uncertainty lets problems be solved later, and clients new to boards can be confused by a cartoon strip [33:36-34:40]. The host says every shot had foreground, middle and background, like a movie set [35:11].

**Use it:** board with camera and layers in mind, then label transitions as intent not final.
