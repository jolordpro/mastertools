The team first pitched a day-to-night montage, later called Nucky's Walk of Life, and the client liked it. HBO then wanted more conceptual impact than a montage, so they went back to the drawing board; Fong says she appreciated being pushed past it. A time-lapse boardwalk walk also went unproduced but fed the next idea. The sandcastle (a kingdom built on sand) belonged to the dropped montage. What carried through was a man facing the sea and Nucky's shoes staying dry. [§3-§4]

**Use it:** When feedback says the idea is not strong enough, cut the favourite, keep the one image that carried the meaning.
