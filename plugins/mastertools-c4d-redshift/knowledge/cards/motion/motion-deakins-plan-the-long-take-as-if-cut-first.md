Sam Mendes told Deakins not to think about technology but to decide where the audience should be at each moment in the story [28:14]. They planned it as if cutting, a single here, a double there, and only then worked out how to string the shots together, so it becomes a technical rather than creative challenge [28:45, 30:22].
Their worry was a gimmick: why watch someone walk A to B for four minutes when you could cut [29:49]. They storyboarded the whole film [29:49].
Deakins also notes a held shot traps the viewer with no relief, while a cut to a wide feels like fresh air [1:23:17-1:24:17].
Transfer: for a continuous camera move in C4D, block the beats as cuts first and only then link them with the move.

**Use it:** For any one-take or long camera move, the coach asks for the cut version of the beats first.
