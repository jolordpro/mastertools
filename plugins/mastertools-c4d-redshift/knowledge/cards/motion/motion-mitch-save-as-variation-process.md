DIA produces dozens of poster or motion variations in a few hours, then reviews them all the day before a presentation. Paone compares this to jazz practice supporting improvisation. (Walker lecture adds: spend about 30-60 seconds on a composition, then save-as and try again.)

**Use it:** Use it as a critique-process model: volume first, selection in one review.
