The Panther titles carried a mid-century design legacy; she kept that as a throwback but updated the look after Pixar-era backgrounds and mattes [23:28-25:00]. The characters must be depicted as people know them: the Panther always cool, the Inspector fumbling and winding himself up [26:03-26:35]. Her clever transitions read as dry design humour; at one point the client said to put a bomb down his pants, meaning the comedy needed to be broader [25:31-26:03]. She adds that a strong existing character can be an advantage as much as a limit [27:08-27:41].

**Use it:** For brand or legacy work, list the fixed character traits first and test the humour level with the client early.
