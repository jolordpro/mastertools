Williams says executive producer Gary Goetzman stayed out of the way during the pitch and gave creative permission from the start, then played a vital role once the job was won. Williams wanted the opening to be unique and ownable, yet to honour Band of Brothers and The Pacific by using actual footage from the show in a way that celebrates and does not distract from the authenticity of events [§2].

**Use it:** Ask what the new piece must honour before deciding what makes it ownable; use the show's real material where authenticity is the point.
