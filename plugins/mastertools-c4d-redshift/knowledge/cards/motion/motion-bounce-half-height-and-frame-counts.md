Korenman [13:13-22:33]: drop in about 16-20 frames at 24 fps (found by trial and error), ease into the fall but not the ground contact. Then bounce up half the height in half the frames (16 down, 8 up), then 4, 3, 2. After first contact the up-curve is a bent S: leaves the floor fast, then eases into the apex. Copy-paste each key to keep handle angles, and make each curve a smaller copy of the last.
Material matters: a rubber ball and a billiard ball differ [18:04].

**Use it:** a quick test for bounce animations: halve height and frames each time and check that contact is not eased.
