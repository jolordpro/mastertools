Rachel Nabors: docs fail when only the author cares. Invite colleagues to write a sentence into the docs, recruit an ally from the opposing team, and avoid being the sole gatekeeper. For debates (slide from right or left?) require a justification such as progression or reading direction; 'looks good' is not enough.

**Use it:** Name a champion and an ally from engineering for any system rollout.
