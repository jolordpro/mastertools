In answer to "is a distinct art style important?" [45:25-49:34] the guest argues animators must adapt, and a narrow style helps short term but may hurt long term. What stays recognisable is attitude: how you see the world (light or dark, happy or negative) comes through whatever you draw. Trend looks (he mentions small heads on big bodies) date in a year, so the people noticed are those who jump on a new style and make the best use of it. A second voice adds that being consistent matters more than being narrow.

**Use it:** supports the AI-trend card: a brand-ownable asset is the point of view, not a look.
