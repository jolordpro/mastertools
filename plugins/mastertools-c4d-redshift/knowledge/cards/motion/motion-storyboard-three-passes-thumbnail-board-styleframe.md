Ben Marriott's order: (1) thumbnail: rough, from the script, for you or your team only, not for the client; (2) board: the refined thumbnail, made so other people can understand it; (3) style frame: what the paused final animation looks like [01:33-02:05].
The edges blur in practice (style frames or animation tests can come before boards) and extra passes are fine, for example a tightening pass between thumbnail and board, or full-colour boards if the job requires them [02:05-02:36]. His warning: people jump straight to final design, skipping thumbnails or boards [02:36].

**Use it:** the coach can ask which pass a draft is in and judge it against that pass, so rough work is not criticised for lacking polish.
