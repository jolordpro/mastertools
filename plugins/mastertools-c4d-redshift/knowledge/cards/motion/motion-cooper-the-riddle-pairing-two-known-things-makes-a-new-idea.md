Example: a Yale safety poster paired George Washington's dollar-bill etching with sunglasses to say keep your valuables out of sight; A plus B does not equal A, it equals a new idea [12:10-12:41]. If the viewer gets the riddle he is drawn into the creative process, and he cites gestalt psychology, hedged with 'allegedly', for the work being more memorable [12:41-13:42]. He also relays Rand's lines that an idea is only as good as its execution and that form can be an idea [11:37-12:10].

**Use it:** Describe a concept as 'known thing + known thing' and test whether a viewer can decode it in one pass.
