Guest: Karin Fong. Streaming gives a skip button, so the brief is something unskippable. She jokes with composers that a good song stops viewers skipping [12:45-13:18].
Good titles enter culture on their own: for Mad Men a falling-man image appeared on buildings in New York when a season came out. Many people see titles outside the show because they are released online as a small trailer, so networks that treat the title as an asset get lasting value [13:18-14:58].

**Use it:** Design an open to work as a stand-alone clip: it should land with sound, without the broadcast around it.
