After brainstorming with the showrunner, themes of prohibition, the sea and an approaching storm merged into one idea. A message in a bottle became 'what if the bottles literally are the storm?'. A director's sketch of a small man facing hundreds of bottles led to a man who looks out to sea, sees his destiny and watches the chaos roll in. Nucky stays above it, so his shoes stay dry [§4].
