Fong says the editing makes the piece, and the best thing for cutting a title sequence is music with changes, layers and a build [§5]. Once the score arrived, the editor found similarities across scenes for match cuts, and he and the lead animator worked back and forth to massage the animation [§5]. She likes optical-illusion effects such as pulling out from something to be surprised, which suits the dreamlike place of a title [§5].

**Use it:** For a title sequence, lock music early and have the editor find match cuts before the animators refine transitions.
