Chris Maclean [25:23-29:36]: the long-running mural changes style by period (comic-book style in one season, an older Art Deco feel when going back in time), driven by the production designer's habit of tracking art movements through history. The giant rings around the planet are just bigger versions of an earlier bridge, so technology is shown as stagnant, and new technology is saved for the rising faction near the end of the season [28:34-30:07]. Spheres of balloons used on set were duplicated in CG [28:03].

**Use it:** A trick for motion packages: mark time or status with a single design axis (style or tech level) and keep everything else fixed.
