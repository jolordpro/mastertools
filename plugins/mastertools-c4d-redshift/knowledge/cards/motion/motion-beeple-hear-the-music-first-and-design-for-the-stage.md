He loves making a unique look for concerts that must sync and hit hard live. Stage setups mean unusual aspect ratios and requirements per tour, so each project differs. For Zedd's tour he heard the new album early, which let him conceive graphics that parallel the beats [§1].

**Use it:** Fix the screen shape and hear the track before designing; build for sync, not just for looks.
