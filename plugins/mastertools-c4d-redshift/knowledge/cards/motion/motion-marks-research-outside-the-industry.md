He starts research in fields outside the industry: photography and film first, then contemporary art, sculpture, painting and experimental forms. Work that is impenetrable to a general audience can be striking when reinterpreted in a new context, which he calls cross-pollination [§5]. His own anchors are Blade Runner, Alien, 2001, Videodrome and Akira for their tone, and Jeffrey Smart's paintings for colour and geometry [§1]. He also warns against over-managing audience reaction: a fresh idea that excites the team is a good way to make impact (viewers found Yellow King clues in the True Detective titles that the team had not intended) [§2].

**Use it:** Build a reference board from at least three non-motion sources before pulling motion references.
