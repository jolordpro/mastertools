Co-director Tosh (Kama, per captions) found a Diego Rivera mural, and the team asked how American culture once mythologised its own progress (high-speed trains, factories, grain, bank-lobby paintings in the 1930s-40s) and applied that to Greek myth [53:32-54:35]. They studied other muralists of the time (Thomas Hart Benton), the Empire State Building and Rockefeller Center, then pulled in Art Deco illustration [58:15-58:46]. Key stops on the road trip (St. Louis Arch, Las Vegas, a diner) carry hidden references the fan base knows, not in story order [54:35-55:07]. The first abstract idea (a family tree of the gods) was rejected as too intellectual: the showrunners wanted the world of the books [50:54].
