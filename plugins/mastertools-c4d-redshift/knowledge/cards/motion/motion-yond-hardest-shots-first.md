Andrew does not animate chronologically; he goes in order of difficulty, hardest technical hurdles first, so the rest gets easier and ends with simple position moves [59:00-59:31].
Before that, he rebuilds each shot and picks out hard sections, making tests or starting from the tricky bit so he is not left at the end with no solution (his example: the waterfall) [38:48-39:19].

**Use it:** schedule risky shots (sims, rigs, heavy renders) first in a job plan and prove them with a test before committing the rest.
