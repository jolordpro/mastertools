Guest: Erik Messerschmidt. The "dissolves" in Mank are not dissolves. They are a lighting cue as in theatre, which is how Welles did it in Citizen Kane [26:23-26:54].
Fincher wanted one constant device telling the audience when the film goes back in time. The cue lets the team choose which part of the frame lingers, a latent image effect. It is used five or six times [26:54-27:54].

**Use it:** In a package or a title sequence, pick one transition that only ever means one thing (time shift, state change) and keep it rare so it keeps its meaning.
