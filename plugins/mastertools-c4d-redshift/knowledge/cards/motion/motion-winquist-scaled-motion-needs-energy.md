Animation had to carry enough weight to read as enormous without being lethargic. When normal-size gorilla, wolf or croc motion was scaled to the creature's size, the director typically felt the shot lacked energy [§4]. For the biggest stage the animators almost entirely keyframed, using gorilla, wolf and croc footage and acting out beats on video [§2].

**Use it:** For oversized subjects, tune weight and speed by eye against the shot; do not scale real timing by size alone.
