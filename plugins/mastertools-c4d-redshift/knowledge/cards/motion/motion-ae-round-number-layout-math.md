Rows 675x100 with a repeater at 105 gave a visible gap between teams [F 01:04-02:38]. The repeater was dropped (hard to adjust) but its math kept: Y = base + 105 x rank, with start Y 284.5 for 14 rows and base 179 (284 - 105) [17:36-20:54].
"Big round numbers" meant every distance and size was known and portioned easily [02:38]. Asset width 550 fit the longest name (Oklahoma City Thunder) and everything else was built off that [04:16].

**Use it:** when specifying a board, choose row height and pitch up front; design the longest-name case first.
