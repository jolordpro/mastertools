Jorge wants students to cater the animation to the style, not to show all their skills; a 3D explosion is fine only if it fits [1:06:48-1:07:50].
He looks for blocky or snappy movement, a hold and then a big move, emotion that fits the design, and says it may be less motion than you think [1:07:20-1:08:22].
Many moves in this piece can use linear keyframes; not everything has to be butter smooth, since the boldness and angularity can show in the motion [1:08:22-1:08:53].

**Use it:** Match easing to the shape language: sharp, geometric design gets short, hard eases, not default smoothing.
