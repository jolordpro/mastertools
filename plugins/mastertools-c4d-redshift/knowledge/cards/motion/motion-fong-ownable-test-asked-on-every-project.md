She frames it as a litmus test: if the Percy Jackson titles could go on the front of God of War, she has failed because the work is not specific enough [51:25-51:58]. The question is "what makes this ownable and what makes this show or brand different" [51:25]. Percy Jackson's answer was a present-day road trip across America with Greek myth, so the look came from American art deco and WPA-era murals, not ancient Greece [52:31-53:32].

**Use it:** Swap test for a concept review: paste the proposed look onto a different team or show; if it still works, keep digging. Specificity comes from the subject's own world.
