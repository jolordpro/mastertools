Common product workflow he describes: prototype in After Effects, run a plugin (Inspector Spacetime) that turns keyframes into millisecond and bezier values in JSON, give that to a developer, then repeat the loop when the result is not quite right [24:17-25:13]. He says Google teams still work this way.
Rive instead ships the animated, rigged file itself: Duolingo characters (blinks, lip shapes) are animated in Rive and the developer only wires up triggers, so the designer controls exactly what the user sees [25:44-26:47].
His claim: the Rive-type handoff makes the old route unnecessary within about ten years, and product budgets are much larger than marketing budgets [31:55, 25:13].
