Each shot becomes its own pre-comp (sometimes two shots run together). All sit on a master timeline with the voiceover and boardamatic, chopped to length; global transitions go there, and shot-specific work stays inside the pre-comp [46:01-47:03].
The job had thousands of layers, so a single master timeline and pre-comps were a necessity [47:03].
One shot comp was 2300 x 1080 to give room to pan while final delivery was 1920 x 1080 [53:47].

**Use it:** a scene or comp with hundreds of layers is a flag to ask for a per-shot pre-comp structure and a master timeline.
