Reviewer Adam Bennett [04:09-05:06]: the film and titles run at 24 fps; illustrations change every other frame (twos), while the type animates on ones, and camera moves on everything keep the whole sequence fluid. Mixing rates is not a rule, it depends on the look. Forcing everything to 12 fps, he found the difference hard to see, especially in pans, but thought 12 might feel better overall.
He also liked that text is offset slightly as it travels across successive napkins [04:18].

**Use it:** rate-test a mixed-rate piece by forcing it to a single rate. Supports motion-erlingsson-animate-on-twos-when-24-fps-looks-too-smooth.
