Guest: Karin Fong. Test of a good title: it seems perfect for its own show and absurd for any other, so it is ownable [03:49-04:52].
Her number one source is the show. Some creators show several episodes, others have only scripts. She tries to get the creative arc from the showrunner, stills from the production designer, locations, and everything else, then digs for what to pull out [04:20-05:24].

**Use it:** Intake step for a new package: collect arc, key art, location or venue stills, and a stakeholder talk before the first board.
