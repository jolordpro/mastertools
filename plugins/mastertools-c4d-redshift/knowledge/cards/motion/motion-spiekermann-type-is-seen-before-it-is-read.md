Praising another speaker's work as proof, he says type and image are the same: you see it before you read it and form an impression, even of a newspaper page ('the New York Times with eyes half closed'); in packaging you sense the pack before you know if it is coffee or chocolate. Only on the next level does it inform you, even down to the ingredients [20:24-20:55].

**Use it:** Squint test: blur the frame; the type's colour and weight should already say the right tone.
