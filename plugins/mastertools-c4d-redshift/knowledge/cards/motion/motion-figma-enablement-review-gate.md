Figma Brand Studio (2021): sticker sheets, illustration libraries and component templates ('flagos') are handed to partners. Partners post what they make in one channel; the brand team gives a quick yes or no. Done to scale a four-person team.

**Use it:** A toolkit plus a light review gate is a governance story even without versioning numbers.
