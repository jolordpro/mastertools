He cannot have a concept until he fully understands the movie, so he reads the script and talks to the people involved [13:42-14:14]. Research does not mean pulling reference online and cobbling Photoshop pieces together; it means immersing in the job, and not carrying the same stylistic bag of tricks into every project because that is inappropriate [14:14-14:44].

**Use it:** Open each job with the script/brief read and a conversation with the owner before collecting any reference board.
