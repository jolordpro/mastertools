Evan Abrams [27:04-38:26]: when voice-over changes late, set markers (asterisk key) on each major beat, nest the comp, enable time remapping and key each marker; stretching the gaps slows or speeds that part. Think "which frame am I on", not time. Sub-frame sampling avoids choppiness; in Premiere stretching needs rendering at about 60 fps to avoid stuttery results [32:41]. Downside: once stretched you must snap back to edit it, and re-adjusting markers does not update live [34:45-35:49].
Alternative: Protected Regions (set work area, create protected region from work area; they turn blue). Drag the layer end and only the unprotected parts compress or stretch, keeping intro and outro motion [36:00-37:21]. Build in modular pieces so they can accordion.

**Use it:** for C4D-to-AE handoffs, keep hero motion in protected regions and let holds stretch.
