She loves that once printed you cannot open the file and fuss with it, endlessly replacing and upgrading images as on a web project. She quotes the line that "the ink never dries on the web" and says being able to conclude a project and move on is nice [§2]. She also enjoys the texture of special printing, which adds a specialness hard to get on screen [§2].

**Use it:** Set a delivery point where the file closes; after it, changes go to a new version, not endless tweaks.
