For Metal Gear Solid 3, a button press transformed the credit type (for example from a snake to a snake skeleton) and dissolved between camouflage and snake-skin texture, making the player the director. Cooper says giving that control "is like making four different titles", so choice multiplies the work [§3]. Producer Grant Curtis adds that titles are not separate from the film but part of it [§1].

**Use it:** Cost any variable or interactive option as extra full pieces, not as a small add-on.
