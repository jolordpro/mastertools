Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer) says a cut is often the better tool; when voiceover timing shifted, a cut let him just extend the end of a beat, while a morph would need all keyframes redone [21:41-22:42].
A senior animator once flagged a single frame because the dot sizes bothered him, and the fix was a cut [22:11, 1:09:02-1:09:34].
Later he admits some transitions were deliberately simple, like rounding corners until squares become circles [40:20-40:50].

**Use it:** Production tip: build transitions so timing can change late; prefer cuts and holds where the idea survives.
