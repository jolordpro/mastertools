She starts loose, with sketches, conversation and rough stories, explores widely, then tests and revises. Editors, animators and cinematographers are allowed to reshape the idea.
She avoids locking into first ideas and sees the structure of a project as part of the design problem.

**Use it:** Do not lock the first look too early. Keep a slot in the review where someone downstream may change the idea.
