Animatic cuts tried everything from a 1920s ditty to Bob Dylan to set tone and tempo [§5]. The chosen modern track gave edge; the aim was for picture and sound to complement each other rather than slip into nostalgia, and the show's creator never wanted it to feel quaint [§7].

**Use it:** Test two or three very different music choices against the animatic before locking the cut.
