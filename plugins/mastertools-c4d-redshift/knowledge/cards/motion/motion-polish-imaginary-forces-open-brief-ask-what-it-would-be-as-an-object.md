Google gave a logo and no brief ('do what you want'), so the team asked what Chrome would look like as a 3D object [§3]. Brian Mah's idea was a magical object in an art gallery that showed what Chrome could do and seemed to invite visitors to interact [§3]. A comparable pattern on Manhattan: combine very simple illustrations of daily life with complex atom-splitting diagrams for a clever juxtaposition of banality and science [§4].

**Use it:** With no brief, turn the product into a physical thing in a setting, or juxtapose two opposite visual vocabularies.
