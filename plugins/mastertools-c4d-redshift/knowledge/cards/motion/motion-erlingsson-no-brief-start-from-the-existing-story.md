For a tour intro the band had no time to brief and replied only weekly, saying: we need an intro, what do you want to do? The studio met the band's creative director for the back-story, then wrote what the story should be from there, reusing an established story in a new version [§3]. For MTV 'sight' they went deliberately alien and mental; people react well to mad work since they can take what they want from it [§3].

**Use it:** With an empty brief, get the backstory from the person who owns the brand, then pitch a new telling of it.
