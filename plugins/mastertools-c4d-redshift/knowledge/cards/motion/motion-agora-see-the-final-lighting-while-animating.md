A one-click toggle switches between a work-light level and the scene light so controls are visible [21:40]. Animators see whether the character is in focus or in the dark, avoiding detail work later lost in the shadows [22:12]. Reviews are done in context so supervisors judge how it will look [13:26]. A test of ever-shallower depth of field across consecutive shots (steps ending near f/1.2, early values garbled in captions) built tension and took about two minutes to validate; it stayed in the trailer [13:58-14:30]. A flying-ship speed problem was solved by an accidental position change that put a shadow on a rock wall [12:24-12:54].

**Use it:** Set up a work-light toggle in lighting scenes; use progressive aperture as a tension tool.
