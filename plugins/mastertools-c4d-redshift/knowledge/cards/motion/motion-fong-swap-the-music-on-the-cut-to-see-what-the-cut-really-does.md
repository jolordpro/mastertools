Her animators found the tracks. On Bedazzled a country song whose lyrics suited the devil carried the comedy; a suggested fast techno track turned the sequence intense and no longer funny, so they went back and licensed the temp track [17:42-18:13]. On Definitely Maybe the editor found the first track that sold the joke, then the song they used [18:13-18:43]. Pitfalls: you can fall for a track you cannot license, while a temp can also become the real thing [18:43-19:14].
Her habit: after editing to one piece, throw on something else for kicks; you notice different things and it is part of the design process [19:14-20:16].

**Use it:** In review, play the cut against a second, very different track and a muted pass to see which beats really carry.
