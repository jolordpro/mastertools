Imsety (Set Angle): in the Maxon 'Limitless' spot the C4D scene used floating abstract UI shapes and no exact icon, to catch the essence of Cinema 4D rather than a pitch-perfect copy [11:28-11:59].
The team wanted it fun and product-led, with the colour palette linked scene to scene (red, green, blue, star background carried over) [10:25-10:57].

**Use it:** Motion-critique: for a tool or app promo, check that the shapes read as the product's feel; do not demand pixel-exact interface.
