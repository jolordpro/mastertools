He says the hardest problem is always finding a visual voice for all of a large company's communication [§2]. It must express identity, be legible and pleasant, work technically on every platform, and apply worldwide, as he did for Nokia, Cisco, Bosch, German Railways and Heidelberg [§2]. For Deutsche Bahn the typefaces had to work from the smallest timetable to the largest poster [§2]. He made Berlin's buses and trams bright yellow rather than beige [§2].

**Use it:** For a graphics package, test the typeface at its smallest and largest uses before approving.
