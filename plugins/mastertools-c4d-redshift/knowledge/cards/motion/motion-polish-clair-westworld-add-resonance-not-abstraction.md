Clair expected to find a poetic abstraction of the show, then saw that the footage was already stunning, so the titles had to showcase it with deeper resonance rather than reinterpret it [§5]. Titles and show fed each other: Elastic designed how an eye might form, production then made its own eye shots, and Elastic evolved its idea to match [§5]. Their rule for the Western iconography: take the core of each epic convention and place it in a stark, graphic sci-fi frame, then add strangeness such as a galloping horse with an exposed ribcage [§5].

**Use it:** Before stylising source imagery, check whether it already carries the look; if it does, choose framing and context instead of a new treatment.
