They resisted the pull of one more tweak. Early on they already agreed the piece would satisfy them if a corrupted file forced this version out, but kept two more rounds of polish [1:06:15-1:06:46].
Most feedback in the later phases was internal, since the client was happy early [1:06:46]. The job finished early, with art-done as a deliberate decision.
