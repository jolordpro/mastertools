For a first-time animation client they split the process more than usual: a boardamatic (slideshow) for timing, then an animatic for blocking and pacing, then real animation [58:30-59:00, 1:00:01-1:00:32].
The aim is to solve the biggest problems early so late rounds do not hit questions like flipping a character's direction, which cost animator time [1:00:32-1:01:03].
On the big job each animation round was a status update: round one had hero characters moving and non-hero ones as paper dolls. They said so up front, so the client did not comment on unfinished walk cycles [1:03:09-1:04:12].

**Use it:** label each review build with what is final, blocked or placeholder, and limit notes to what that stage can answer.
