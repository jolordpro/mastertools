In 2012 Trollbäck+Company rebranded SVT's channels (logo, IDs, transitions, bumpers, lower thirds), with each channel given its own motif and a flexible system so content can be reused for IDs of almost any length. Jakob Trollbäck was ECD; Antfood made music moods per channel.
