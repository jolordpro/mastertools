Asked for the traits of a good main title she gives three: it describes this show and no other [57:56], it has some clever metaphor or aha moment, though a nice idea can work without a surprise [1:00:33], and it is well made, from type to shooting to animation [1:00:33]. Catch Me If You Can works because it tells the whole story up front without the viewer knowing [59:30]. Hosts' recap at [1:23:51] repeats the list.

**Use it:** Use as a three-question review for any open: ownable? has an idea? crafted? A fail on the first is the one to send back.
