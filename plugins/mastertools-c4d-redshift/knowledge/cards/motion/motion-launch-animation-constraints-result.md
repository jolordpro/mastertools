Lyft launch screen (2023, four weeks, self-reported): capped at 1.5 seconds; built in Lottie; on Android rebuilt with vector drawables without mattes; A/B test showed a 0.72% perceived time-to-interaction gain, about 1.4 s. One animation served both rider and driver apps.

**Use it:** Pair a hard time budget and platform limit with a measured result when presenting a single animation.
