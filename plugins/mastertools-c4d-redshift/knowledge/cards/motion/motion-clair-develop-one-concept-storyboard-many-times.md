The pitch took about four or five weeks, longer than usual because he wanted the job. Once hired, storyboarding ran about three or four months, and the team storyboarded at least five times where one pass is typical, digging toward the show's idea of loops, free choice and creations that supersede their makers. By the time the CGI was made the team knew what it was trying to do. [§3]

**Use it:** Spend the early time on the idea and boards; the render is not where the concept is decided.
