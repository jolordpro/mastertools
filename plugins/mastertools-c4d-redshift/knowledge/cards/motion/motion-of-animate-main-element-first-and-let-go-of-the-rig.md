Advice from a colleague: find the main thing the eye follows, make that move right, then add supporting movement [53:15-53:46].
Jorge wants animators who can step back. Style frames can sit side by side; animation cannot, so the creative director has to hold the whole [54:16-55:17]. His example: a 3-day rig that is too exciting but wrong for the direction, and the right call is a cut [55:17-56:51].
Goal: a video that feels animated by one person, so people must compromise, move things and adjust [56:51-57:22].

**Use it:** Ask at each transition 'does the piece need this', and budget time to throw away work.
