The audience tossed out many ideas (Mount Rushmore, hot-air balloon, mech, balloons, witches). Beeple's rule for ideas: they must be something he can do very fast [03:48]. Once a direction landed (a field of growing heads, [05:28]), he rechecked mid-build, "are we committed to that", got a yes, and said to lean in [11:40][12:14]. He timed the piece in ten-minute blocks to keep himself moving, and noted the piece had to be done by a fixed time [09:23][19:51]. Late on, the lighting would not work and he blamed the composition rather than keep cycling lighting [31:47][32:20] (see lighting-beeple-when-no-hdri-works-suspect-the-composition).

**Use it:** a fast-concept ritual for styleframes: collect ideas quickly, lock one with a clear yes, then spend the time on execution.
