[20:29 to 22:02] Set Angle used Magic Bullet Looks on flat 2D art, not just footage: click through built-in presets until one gives a good palette, then add a noise adjustment layer and a little grain.
It brightens and sets colours straight away; the same trick was used on a C4D phone element [26:43].

**Use it:** offer preset cycling as a fast palette exploration before manual grading.
