Sand dunes in Abu Dhabi, 17 stories high, were framed with characters in the middle like waves about to consume them to convey scale [02:33-03:05]. Handheld quickly leads viewers to believe the world is less rigid, and lets controlled, focused, steady shots stand out when wanted [03:05-03:36].

**Use it:** Give the audience a clear human-scale element in a huge frame, and keep the camera language for a reason.
