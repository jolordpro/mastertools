Alex Grigg (Animation for Anyone): with only two extreme keys, timing is hard to judge. Add one breakdown key in the middle carrying as much information about the move as possible (line of travel, stretch or smear) [01:34-02:05].
Sliding that breakdown changes the feel: a longer hold on the breakdown makes the motion feel slower, because the brain roughly doubles the hold and imagines the move taking about that long plus the same again [02:36-03:37]. Example: a 4-frame hold reads as about an 8-frame move [03:07-03:37].
So timing can be tested by moving one pose instead of drawing every in-between. Poses, breakdown and rough timing together are the blocking stage [03:37].

**Use it:** for C4D or AE, test timing with one stepped breakdown key before any easing curves are touched.
