Legato's plane-crash previs for The Aviator used MotionBuilder, cut on Avid; the keyframed Leonardo DiCaprio looked so goofy he could not hold shots as long as he thought, so mocap would make the edit decisions correct [08:27-09:30]. He pitched the mocap stage to Cameron (then planning Battle Angel) to capture 'the verb' of Cameron's directing style, since a director who cannot operate the tool is limited by the interpreter [10:02-11:35]. A camera shot takes 15-20 iterations (a little higher, move over, slide the background, move the chair), and a CG artist who does not know what the lens is cannot judge them; the computer was not real time [11:35-12:38]. Keyframing is like hitting a note, waiting three minutes, hitting another, while real time allows more, less and happy accidents [12:38-13:39].

**Use it:** Drive virtual cameras with handheld or noise-based input and iterate live, and decide lens and subject distance deliberately.
