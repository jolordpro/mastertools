Fix kinks in the curve and keep the speed curve smooth [06:16-06:47]. Align an extra key with the fastest-moving point and ease more at the back than the front [06:47].
Same for rotation and scale, not just position [08:52].

**Use it:** the keyframe check can look for creases in the speed graph.
