Imaginary Forces decided from the start that sound design would lead the storytelling on See [§5]. They talked to the show's blindness consultant, Joe Strechay, and took echolocation as the idea, testing what type looks like in a dark void revealed only where there is sound [§5]. The aim was a title that is barely there, readable only for fleeting moments, giving a sense of a vast world beyond sight [§5].

**Use it:** For an accessibility-themed piece, consult a subject expert early and let the sense in question (here sound) drive the visual logic.
