She says it is often better to give the audience something to wonder about than to explain [CM 14:15]. On Boardwalk Empire the bottle image may not make sense on first watch, and the meaning grows over seasons [CM 11:20; 1:01:36]. Black Sails was made deliberately intricate so there is something new to find on repeat viewing [CM 20:52].

**Use it:** Test an open: is there a question the viewer carries into the first scene? Layer detail for repeat viewing only on long-running series.
