Legato says he always gravitated to naturalistic realism and the shift from analog to digital was painless, since shooting miniatures and multi-layer composites translated to digital. His view of virtual production is marrying technology and creativity for more realism, and he stresses collaboration, multiple shoots, and getting the director, art director and cinematographer in a room for take after take until everything works and you get the magical moment [§2].

**Use it:** Keep a real-world camera and take-by-take mindset for CG shots; review the people together, not in separate silos.
