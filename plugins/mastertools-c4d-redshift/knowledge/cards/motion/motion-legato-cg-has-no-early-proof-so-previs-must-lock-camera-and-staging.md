In a live film you can see 15 minutes of cut footage and say it is working; here the whole movie is banked before any of that exists [05:18-05:51].
So they pre-shot it, but not with lip sync: the pre-shoot decided where characters moved, what the scene was about, how the camera moved and how to shoot the boy to place him in a later photoreal scene [05:51].
Talk was treated as throwaway: the animals have only vocal cords, so not every word or syllable is emphasised, as with real speakers [04:46-05:18].

**Use it:** Use a cheap pre-shoot to settle the things that cost most to change later (staging, camera, timing) and leave polish for after approval.
