Depth cameras (one flies on the Mars rover) measure distance and can be turned into a point cloud; the images drop out in certain places in ways that are hard to fake by hand [02:29-03:00]. The film shoot did not use them as first planned; they served as reference for the machines' view [03:00].

**Use it:** Study the failure modes of the real technology (dropouts, noise, edges) and copy those into the look, instead of a clean generic grid.
