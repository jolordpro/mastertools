Because each light is a separate layer, Roberts uses Keyframe Assistant > Convert Audio to Keyframes to make an adjustment layer with left, right and both channel sliders; pick-whip the opacity (or scale) of each light pass to the slider so the lights pulse with the music [44:35-46:46]. He says he does little of this but it suits concert visuals, and 3D keyframing would be harder [44:35-45:06].

**Use it:** Concert and broadcast show opens: pass-based light pulses are cheap vs re-rendering.
