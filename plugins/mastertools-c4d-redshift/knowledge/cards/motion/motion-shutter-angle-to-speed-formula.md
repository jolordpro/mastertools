Doubling the frame rate gives the speed [05:00-06:42]. Angle stays constant when frame rate changes, so cinema cameras use angle [06:11]. Sensors change exposure time electronically; film shutters reached about 11-180 degrees, digital up to 360 [01:43].

**Use it:** In the motion check, convert a blur setting to degrees and compare it with 180.
