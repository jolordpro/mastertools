When Officina's bold felt a little too soft and wobbly for the Economist, they redrew a heavier headline cut with a use-based name [19:23-19:54].
On variable fonts: great for interfaces; the system should switch to the small-size cut below about 16 or 17 pixels; newspapers have always known a 5-point face is cut differently from a 7.5 [46:32-47:34]. He says smaller sizes need less contrast, wider characters and more spacing, and this means type is size-specific again, as in the 1600s [47:34].
For a TV job he supplied 20 versions about 5 units apart (on a 1,000 or 2,000 unit scale), because the client had to test them on TV screens [46:01-46:32]. Half of the people will use the freedom wrongly [47:03].

**Use it:** Check text at its real display size and on the delivery screen; for lower thirds supply a few heavier or lighter weights to test on the actual monitor.
