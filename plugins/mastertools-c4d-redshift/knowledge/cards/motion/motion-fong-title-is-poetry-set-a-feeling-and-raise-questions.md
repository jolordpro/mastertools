Guest: Karin Fong (creative director, Imaginary Forces). She calls herself a concept-based designer and likens a title to a short poem against a novel: it must be concise [01:15-01:47].
Her team's phrase is "crack the code": find what means the most to this show and which metaphor gets the biggest emotional response [01:47-02:18].
Over the years she learned a title is much more about creating questions than answering them. Most of the time it should set tone and mood, not give information, unless the filmmaker demands backstory. Viewers keep how it made them feel: anticipation, fear in a thriller, permission to laugh in a comedy [02:18-03:18].

**Use it:** Review question for a package open: what does the viewer feel by second three, and what question does it leave open?
