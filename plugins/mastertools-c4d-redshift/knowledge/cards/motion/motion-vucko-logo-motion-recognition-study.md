Participants identified brands from logo motion alone. Three signals drove recognition: steadiness, pacing, uniqueness. Timing had a strong effect on interpretation. Disney scored above 80 percent, from a few repeated motions (castle, arc, light trail). Sample size not given.

**Use it:** Use it as a measured claim for 'motion is brand'; ask for sample size before citing numbers.
