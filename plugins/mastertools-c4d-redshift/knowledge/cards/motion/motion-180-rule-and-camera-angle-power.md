Draw a line through the two interacting characters and keep every cut on one side so left and right never flip [03:38-05:08]. A camera looking down on a character makes her seem lower in power, and looking up makes her heroic [05:08].
Framing hint: do not let the top of the head graze the frame, and do not crop at a joint such as the hips [02:05]. Turn on the camera preview/render border, since the viewport is not the true frame.

**Use it:** use as a quick framing checklist on character or athlete shots, in particular for key-art and cutdowns.
