Using a lecturer's line that creativity is the association of two unassociated things, he names pairs: True Detective is landscapes and people, High Castle is Nazi and American imagery, Westworld is cowboys and robots on the surface and the grotesque and the beautiful underneath [§4]. Each job starts with a reference archive: about 2000 images is usual, Westworld exceeded 10,000 across robotics, biological 3D printing, labs, sculpture, sadness and sci-fi [§4].

**Use it:** Write the obvious pair, then the deeper pair, and design for the deeper one.
