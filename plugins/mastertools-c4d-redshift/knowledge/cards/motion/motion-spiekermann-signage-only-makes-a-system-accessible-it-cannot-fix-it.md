Asked about New York's subway signs (the Vignelli system), he says it was a good, slightly complicated system now almost 50 years old; the complexity is in the system itself (express and local trains), and signage can only represent it, not improve it [44:25]. He prefers diagrams to maps [44:25]. He thinks Akzidenz Grotesk would have been much better for the signs than Helvetica, and praises Zurich Airport's signage, saying its weight is a little heavy but white on black works [44:57].

**Use it:** Fix the underlying information structure before designing the graphic that explains it.
