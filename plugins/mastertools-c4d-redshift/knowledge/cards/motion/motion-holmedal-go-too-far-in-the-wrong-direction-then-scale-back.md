The hardest part is the beginning: finding the skeleton and the dos and don'ts of a brief, and it is hard to tell when it is too much or not enough [00:45-01:16]. He goes as far as possible in the wrong direction first, then scales back, because early on you can open up to all the inspiration and only then see where it can go [00:45-01:47]. He also keeps ideas fresh by staying curious about maths and nature and applying those ideas where they do not belong [07:07-08:09].

**Use it:** Allow an exaggerated first exploration round, labelled as such, then edit back to the brief.
