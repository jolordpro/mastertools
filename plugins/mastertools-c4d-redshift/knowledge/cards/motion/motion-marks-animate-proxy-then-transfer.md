For the Semi-Permanent opener the main spacesuit was modelled in Cinema 4D and Marvelous Designer. Its high polygon count meant a different approach to animation: Raoul Marks animated low-res versions and transferred the movement to the higher-resolution model with the Mesh deformer. For close-up shots he used the Marvelous Designer simulations; for mid-range to distant shots he used the Mesh deformer process [§1]. He had 4 weeks for concept and production [§1].

**Use it:** Animate light, bake heavy sims only for the shots where the audience can see them.
