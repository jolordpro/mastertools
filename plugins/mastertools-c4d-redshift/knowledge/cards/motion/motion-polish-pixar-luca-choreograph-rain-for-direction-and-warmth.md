Process [15:13-18:50]: early on, effects, lighting and the director mapped how the rain should feel at each moment (pitter patter, ramps to deluge at the climax, calms), with a look per stage. Choreography: fast sideways moves get diagonal rain, moves into camera get rain radiating outward to support the line of action (a 2D trick). Colour: the director wanted summer rain; pushing it blue made it feel like winter [17:18-17:48]. Splash counts on faces and where raindrops sit in depth were planned per shot, with brighter drops in front, to avoid filling the frame [17:48-18:18]. Rigs in Houdini/Houdini Engine and Katana let broad strokes land fast so the director saw the sequence as a whole; it came late, so it built on all earlier work [17:17, 18:50].
**Use it:** For weather in a package, define intensity per beat, direction tied to camera or object motion, a warm or cool decision, and a per-shot density cap.
