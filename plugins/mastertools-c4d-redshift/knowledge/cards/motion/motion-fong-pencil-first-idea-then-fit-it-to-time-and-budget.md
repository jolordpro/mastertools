She jokes that the pencil is the first piece of technology: work out the idea before the tool, even if it is too big and causes production trouble later [51:29-51:59].
Example from a collaborator: a cathedral that floods with water; it could not be built, but small statues with a water level kept the idea [51:59-52:31].
Then she checks what she can afford and what is time-limited: no time for a full 3D piece but maybe one thing worth photographing [52:31-53:03].
Staying true to the world of the story keeps titles from dating; she lists chasing a plug-in look as the trap [50:26-51:29].

**Use it:** Write the unconstrained idea first, then list which part can be photographed, which built, which faked, and keep the essence in the cheapest form.
