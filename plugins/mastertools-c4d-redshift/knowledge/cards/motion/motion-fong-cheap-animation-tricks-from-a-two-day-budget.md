On a PBS show with a couple of days per interstitial she learned to show a character only from the waist up so only arms needed animating, no full run cycle [10:31]. She says that mindset stays with you at any budget because there is always a way to do it for less [11:03]. Ink illustrations were also faxed back and animated directly because the show was so low-budget [CM 05:10].

**Use it:** Scoping habit: before animating, ask which part of the shot the viewer actually reads and design the camera or crop to hide the rest.
