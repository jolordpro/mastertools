Showrunners and the network wanted something abstract and symbolic but with a human component. Clair avoided beauty shots of the cast and tied the glitching faces to the show's theme: people putting pressure on themselves and risking self-destruction through ambition. [§4]

**Use it:** When the client says abstract but human, link the human element to the show's central tension.
