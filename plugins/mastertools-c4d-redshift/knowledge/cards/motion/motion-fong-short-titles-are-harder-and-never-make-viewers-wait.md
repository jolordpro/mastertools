Guest: Karin Fong. Clients want a build, emotion and payoff; with a short title there is no runway, no time with the music to build suspense. Production is easier (less to render), but impact is easier with longer ones. A right idea can still work very well in short form [05:24-05:55].
Rule: never make a title the audience feels they must wait through; make it just the right length and maybe slightly shorter, so they want more [05:55-06:27].

**Use it:** For a short sting, drop the build and bank on one clear idea; cut length until nothing feels like waiting.
