Elastic spent a day photographing and filming the show's player piano in the production office and rebuilt it in 3D because it recurs in the show [§6]. The composer's team sent videos of the piano being played so the animators could match the notes [§7]. A designer rebuilt whole show sets in Cinema 4D from trailers; those frames set the tone and were still used for lighting cues late in the process [§8]. Pipeline: ZBrush organic modelling, Maya modelling and prep, Cinema 4D final, After Effects comp, Octane on GPU render farms the team assembled to scale [§9].

**Use it:** For a title tied to a show, collect the real props and music performance as references before modelling.
