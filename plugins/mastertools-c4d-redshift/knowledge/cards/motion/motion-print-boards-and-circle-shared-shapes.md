Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer) stares at the boards and circles similar shapes between frame one and frame two, such as three circles moving down, as a starting point for the transition [19:32-20:05].
He prefers paper because working in the software narrows ideas to what one effect can do; stepping away leaves fewer constraints [20:05-20:37].
He warns against forcing a link: if a transition is obviously contrived, it reads that way [33:05-33:36].

**Use it:** Brief for transition design: find the shared shape, then build; do not start from an effect.
