Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer) reports that a senior animator's note on a recent job was about one frame where dots were too small, not about curves [1:07:29-1:08:31].
He found it freeing: inspect every frame for fit, and often the fix is to turn the element off on that frame and bring it in on the next [1:09:02-1:09:34].

**Use it:** Final-pass checklist item: step through the piece frame by frame once, flagging frames that do not fit.
