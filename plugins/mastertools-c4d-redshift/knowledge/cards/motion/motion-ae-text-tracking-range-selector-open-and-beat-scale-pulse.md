SonduckFilm [03:40-05:12]. Add the Animate > Tracking property, raise the amount so the title opens up, then key the range selector Start from 0 to 100 over at most about five frames so letters open on the beat. For a second beat on the same title, key Scale up to about 116% one frame later and back down the following frame. The author prefers tracking for this style of video. He also promotes a paid preset plugin for the same text animations (sponsor-like; not carded).

**Use it:** A short-run pulse is one or two frames, so keep beat hits tight and key them by frame, not by seconds.
