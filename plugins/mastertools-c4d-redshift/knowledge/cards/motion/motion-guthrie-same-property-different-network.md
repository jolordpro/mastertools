The NFL has to feel premium and polished; each network package starts from that network's own brand stance.
Inspiration comes from UI, traditional art and sometimes AI prompts. Packages for competing networks must not look alike.

**Use it:** A framing line for multi-client systems: differentiate by the client's stance, not by novelty.
