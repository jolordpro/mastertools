Alissa Heaps treats vague direction as usually their own failure to explain the process [43:00-43:28].
At storyboard stage they ask for notes on general composition, what happens in each scene, and the characters. Design notes are not wanted yet [43:28-43:59].
When a client says they dislike something but not why, the answer is more questions, which slows things down but gets to the real note [43:59].

**Use it:** each review request should list what to judge now and what is not open yet (boards: layout and story; style frames: look).
