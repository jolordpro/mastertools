Parent the logo and reflection to a null; scale keys 110% to 100% about five frames apart for a slight settle (invert for a push-in) [I 07:20-07:50]. Light Sweep effect keyed from centre at about 1 s to the far side around 4 s [08:21-08:52]. Reflection: duplicate, flip Y scale to -100, drop opacity to 20%, add a feathered mask [05:44-06:50].

**Use it:** low confidence beginner recipe; use for the 'does it keep moving after it lands' note.
