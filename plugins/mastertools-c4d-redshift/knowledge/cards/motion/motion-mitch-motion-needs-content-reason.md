In a 2020 interview Paone says motion is designed into an identity from the start, not added to a finished static layout, and every aesthetic choice (including movement) needs a reason found in the project's content. Squarespace example: shifts between flat and isometric views echo the words Square and Space.

**Use it:** Use it as an anti-rule: no movement without a content reason.
