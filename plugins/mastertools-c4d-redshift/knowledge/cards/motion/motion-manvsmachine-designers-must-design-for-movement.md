Many designers make things look good but few make them move well; moving things are on phones, tablets and screens, so a new logo will have to animate [07:08-07:38].
They do not say every designer must animate, but must account for it. Old-school logo design judges a mark small and black-and-white though it will rarely appear like that [07:38-08:28].

**Use it:** Review a logo or title mark in motion context, not just as a static lockup.
