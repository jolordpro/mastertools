He admires the Mockingbird titles where marbles distort the background and rack focus, and the horizontal lines of the type contrast with the round objects [00:59-01:31]. The type does not even go behind things; it just belongs. In the cat-fight title for Take a Walk on the Wild Side (captions suggest a Saul Bass piece) the typography follows what the cats are doing and the background shot's form [01:31-02:02]. He calls type that is integrated, not an afterthought, the goal.
