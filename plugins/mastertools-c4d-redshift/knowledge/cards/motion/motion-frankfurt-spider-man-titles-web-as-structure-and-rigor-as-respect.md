Peter Frankfurt (5D) on the Spider-Man work, from a whisper transcript (wording approximate). Two jobs: the animated Marvel logo, built as a flipbook of comic print coming alive to carry the brand's comics-and-print DNA [00:00-01:13], and the title sequence, where director Sam Raimi's idea of the web as a connector led to strong, tense web strands fired into z-space that gave the type a structure to align itself in [01:13-01:45]. He says what makes good title sequences work is rigor and attention to detail, which shows respect for the material and the audience, and the title is an overture that does not pander [01:45-02:34]. The marketing was done before the titles, so the idea was already set.

**Use it:** Give animated type a physical structure from the subject's world to land in, and judge finish by rigor of execution. For a sports package, a team-owned element (a net, a lane, a crest line) can be the grid the stats land on.
