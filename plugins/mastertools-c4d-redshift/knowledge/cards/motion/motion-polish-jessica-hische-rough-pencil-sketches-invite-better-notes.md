Hische finds pencil sketches a great way to begin because they are obviously far from finished, so clients give less pushback; the closer to final the roughs are, the more revisions she has had to do [§3]. On Wes Anderson's Moonrise Kingdom titles she says the director has a vision and her job is to carry it out, with less authorship than on small projects [§2]. She likes print because it ends: you cannot reopen the file and keep upgrading as on a web project [§2].

**Use it:** Show early looks as visibly rough to keep feedback on the idea, not on polish.
