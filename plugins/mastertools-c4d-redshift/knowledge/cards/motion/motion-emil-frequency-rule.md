Emil Kowalski: keyboard-triggered and hover actions seen all day usually need no animation; rare moments can carry more. UI animation should stay under 300 ms (a 180 ms dropdown feels more responsive than 400 ms). Neighbouring tooltips open with no delay once one is open. Page text via fetch summary; undated.

**Use it:** Use frequency as a rule for deciding when motion is cut.
