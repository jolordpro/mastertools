Masks that work in a game engine are hard, so Epic offers texture masks plus a geometry mask modifier [25:49]. One demo logo reveal used only two images (a warped back plate and a black/white mask) with lens distortion keyframed in the effects layers and a small particle system [26:22]. The mask can be animated inside the Material Designer [26:22].
Another reveal used the word "motion" as a text mask cutting through layers, and a third copied a logo four or five times to cut through layers, like animated white-on-black masks in After Effects [26:54] [27:25] [27:56].

**Use it:** Reference for show-open reveals: mask-based reveals do work in real time. The geometry mask is the Unreal term for After Effects shape masks.
