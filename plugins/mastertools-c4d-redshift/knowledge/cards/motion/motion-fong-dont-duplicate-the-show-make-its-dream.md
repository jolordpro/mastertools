One of her team's rules is not to duplicate what is in the show, since the viewer is about to see the cast, production design and cinematography [CM 19:17]. The sequence should be a concept that complements and hints at the show instead, more metaphor than depiction [CM 19:47].

**Use it:** Brief check for any open/sting: if the footage already appears in the programme, the open is spending its time twice.
