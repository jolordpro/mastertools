Falling speeds up the whole way, so the curve should flatten only at the top and steepen toward the ground with no cushioning before contact [17:01]. At the contact key break the tangents so the fall is steep and the rebound starts steep and eases out toward the apex [17:32-18:34]. A stylised longer hang time is made by stretching the flat apex while keeping the sharp contact [19:05-19:36]. The pointed bottom is the aggressive change of direction; a smooth bottom looks floaty [17:01, 19:36].
Newton framing: energy leaves about as fast as it arrives [09:16].

**Use it:** for bounces and drops in a logo or lower-third animation, compare the curve to this shape; apply the same shape to anything that lands.
