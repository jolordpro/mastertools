Vox interview with Michelle Dougherty (creative director, Imaginary Forces) [00:00-02:42]: goal that the sequence "couldn't work in front of another show" [00:00]. Reference was 1970s-80s title work by R/Greenberg (Alien, The Dead Zone, Altered States) and 1980s paperback covers the Duffer brothers sent for typography, ending in ITC Benguiat (1978) [00:32-01:36]. Old titles were shot optically, frame by frame; to copy the grit they rigged kodaliths (very high-contrast film), filmed light shining through the letters, and used those plates to animate digitally, so the subtle flicker and shake is real optical inconsistency [01:36-02:42]. She calls the flaws "mistakes" kept for a tactile, organic, period-true feel.

**Use it:** For retro looks, capture a real optical flaw source and drive the CG type with it rather than adding a procedural wobble.
