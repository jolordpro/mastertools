The camera moves are 'born' from picking up a virtual camera and moving it in an analog way: choosing lenses and moves as you would on set, moved by the drama of the moment [03:12].
He contrasts that with the usual computer workflow, where you do not have the sound in front of you and set keyframes, which makes it hard to get the rhythm or the organic quality you get from seeing something and reacting to it [03:12-03:44].

**Use it:** For important camera moves, record a hand-operated pass against picture and sound and clean it up, instead of building the move from keys.
