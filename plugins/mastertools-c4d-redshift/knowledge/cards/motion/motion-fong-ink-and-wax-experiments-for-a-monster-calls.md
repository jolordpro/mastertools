The title needed an abstract journey through windows, doors and keyholes without giving away the film [CM 22:25]. They shot ink on wax drawings and blew ink with straws to get visceral, organic abstractions, then joined the pieces in the edit so the transitions run seamlessly [CM 22:56-23:28]. Her point: the computer can be a collage box, and doing parts analog or in camera adds tactile authenticity [CM 04:39; 23:28].

**Use it:** When a CG look feels sterile, shoot a few practical elements (ink, smoke, paint) and composite them as texture or transition plates.
