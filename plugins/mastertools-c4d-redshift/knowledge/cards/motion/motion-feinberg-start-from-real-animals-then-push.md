The team starts with real life as a base, then decides where to push and pull for expressiveness, since motion based on reality is more believable. They folded in how red pandas raise their arms when scared and their tube-like, agile bodies, while taking artistic licence with size and mouth shapes [§2].

**Use it:** Collect real behaviour clips first, then list the two or three things you will exaggerate.
