Fong says it is a successful title if it can't run in front of any other show: a mob title for Boardwalk Empire that also fits The Untouchables is not distinct enough [§4]. She starts by asking the showrunner "what tone?" and what the central idea is, or what feeling people should have after the titles [§4]. On Stranger Things type she notes that even if viewers cannot explain it right away, they feel it, which is the best compliment [§3-4].

Also (Art of the Title, Boardwalk Empire): the period set and details gave the distinct hook, and the team showed several quite different concept boards before narrowing [§2]. Merged from motion-fong-title-must-not-introduce-another-show.

**Use it:** Ask the same of a hero frame: would it still make sense on another brand or game? If yes, push the concept before the polish.
