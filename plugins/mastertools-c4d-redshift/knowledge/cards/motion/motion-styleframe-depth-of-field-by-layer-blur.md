Hero character stays 100 percent sharp; the closer a layer is to the imagined camera... in his words blur grows with distance from the focal subject: wall slightly blurry, background characters blurrier (he used Gaussian blur 15 on one character), city and clouds very blurry [07:10][07:41]. In AE the same plan uses a precomp per element and perhaps real camera lens blur at a render cost. Trade-off: blurred city loses the detail drawn into it [08:11]. City drawing tip: add trapezoids and diagonal cut shapes to break the horizontal and vertical lines and imply cast shadows [08:11]. Reference taken from Chromosphere's "Forms in Nature" for depth of field [01:32].

**Use it:** depth plan for flat frames: define a blur ladder per layer before animating.
