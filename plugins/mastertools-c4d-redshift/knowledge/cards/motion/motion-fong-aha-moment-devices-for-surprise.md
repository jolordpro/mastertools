She likes shapes the brain can read two ways (rabbit/duck, positive and negative space) and pushes the viewer from one read to the other [33:08]. Other devices: play between flat and dimensional, trick scale so something feels close and vast at once [34:12], flip a word by shifting blocks (Kyle Cooper's True Lies) [34:42], and silhouettes that turn out to be a face or a mountain range [35:13]. She ties it to why cinema's transitions bring you into new spaces [35:44].

**Use it:** Brainstorm prompt list for transitions and logo resolves: which two-way read, scale trick or silhouette morph fits the subject?
