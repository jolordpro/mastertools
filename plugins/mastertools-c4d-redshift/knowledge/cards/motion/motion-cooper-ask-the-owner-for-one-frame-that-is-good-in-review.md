Chris Do (The Futur host) tells of a 1990s session where Cooper, looking at a poor animation on a flame suite, asked 'is that good?', scrubbed through and asked the operator to show one frame that was good; when told 'I'm just hands', he asked why he was being paid that much to be hands [29:47-31:52]. Of his own first storyboard, Cooper said it was not an idea and told him to work on it [32:52-33:56]. Second-hand, remembered by the host; treat as low confidence.

**Use it:** In a critique, ask the maker to point to the one frame that is right and say why; separate idea notes from execution notes.
