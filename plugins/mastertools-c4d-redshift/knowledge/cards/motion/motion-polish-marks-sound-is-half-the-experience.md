A mentor told Marks audio is at least 50% of the experience, and he agrees [§6]. He tried many existing tracks, found none matching the image in his head, and built a sound bed from favourite sample sets in Ableton, aiming for isolation and exploration [§6]. He notes a recognisable sensibility when music matches image [§6].

**Use it:** Treat sound as a design pass of its own, and say what mood the sound must carry before auditioning tracks.
