The God of War III director hired her for the thinking behind the Mummy titles, not to duplicate them [47:23-47:54]. An agency that liked the comic-book movement in her Zoom prologue wanted that same movement thinking for a pop-star commercial, not a copy [47:54-48:56]. By contrast, after Se7en every product from cup noodles to diapers wanted that style, though the film was about psychopaths and the product did not share it; she says her work is driven by story and concept, with the look following from them [48:56-49:28].

**Use it:** When a note says 'like X', ask which idea in X is wanted and restate it before copying any surface.
