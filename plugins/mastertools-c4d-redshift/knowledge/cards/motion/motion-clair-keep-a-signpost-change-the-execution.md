Clair says season 2 needed a new sequence because cast, place and story all changed. The question was how far to depart from combining landscapes and footage into distorted portraits: they did not want to repeat themselves, but the audience needed a familiar signpost to welcome them back into the story world [§1]. The answer was David Maisel's aerial images, with golds, rust reds, rough textures and vivid hues of arid Southern California, and high-contrast freeway tangles that serve as a metaphor for chaotic lives; they used them to make double exposure imagery again, now with more texture and colour [§1]. (The Westworld season 2 change-count and music-stayed notes come from another source, not this page.)

**Use it:** When updating a package, decide the one thing that must stay; allow everything else to move.
