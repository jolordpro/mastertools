Justin Mitchell: a wave barrelling down a street is vague and dull; give the audience moments to follow. The wave hits a vendor's cart, turns the corner, two cars narrowly miss, one ends in a storefront [30:00-31:35]. Viewers may not name the beats but are entertained by them. The viewport is a stage you dress with props and lights [29:29]. He ties the whole to the filmmaker's story and genre [31:35].

**Use it:** review an animation by listing its beats: if the shot has one idea sustained for the whole length, add or reorder moments. Applies to long broadcast opens.
