Initial project about three months, design and animation about 6 to 8 weeks, over 300 components (Black Book Motorsport, summary).
Layout is a grid with one data point per square; Baxi calls it a 4D grid because time is the fourth axis (fireside).
Telemetry feeds a Vizrt skin; the director chooses which components run; operation handed to F1's in-house team.

**Use it:** Describe a broadcast system by component count, who triggers it live, and who owns it after handover.
