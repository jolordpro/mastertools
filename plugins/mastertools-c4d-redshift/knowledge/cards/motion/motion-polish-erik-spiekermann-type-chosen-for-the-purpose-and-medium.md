He says the same principles apply to print and screen, and that trying a good typeface costs no more than a poor one. Leading matters; he faulted a book for tight line spacing. Good design works subliminally, judged by reading experience. Asked for web body fonts, he answered the ones that look good. Aesthetics affect usability because people avoid things they find ugly.
