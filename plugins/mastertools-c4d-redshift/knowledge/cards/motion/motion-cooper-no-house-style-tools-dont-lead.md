He starts each title from the film's content, context and voice and avoids carrying one style from job to job. He prefers doing as much as possible by hand or in camera, and calls technology a double-edged sword.
He says tools do not decide the idea. Technology does not supply a good story, clear communication or a beautiful image by itself.
