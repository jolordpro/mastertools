Guest: Jessica Hische (letterer and illustrator). Steps: get the existing logo and assets, take mood boards (client's or her own from the same world), talk goals; then print the logo, hang it up and critique it, writing down every error in the letter forms and in the vector drafting as a task list [04:09-05:10].
Refresh work is iteration, so print as you go to see versions side by side on the wall, where it has been and where it is going [05:10-05:41].
Why a checklist: she treats intuition as subconscious access to facts, and she is stretched thin sometimes, so the list ensures nothing is dropped on something as important as a logo, and gives a way forward when she knows it can be better but not how [05:41-06:44].

**Use it:** Before a polish pass, print or freeze the current frame, critique it into a numbered list, then work the list.
