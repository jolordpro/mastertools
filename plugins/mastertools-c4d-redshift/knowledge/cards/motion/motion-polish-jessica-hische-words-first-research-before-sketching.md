Hische says she keeps ideas fresh by focusing on the book, article or source material when coming up with concepts, not falling in love with a visual approach before doing the research [§3]. She prefers verbal ideation before visual: word-association lists, letting the mind wander, and working through good and bad ideas in a rough exploratory way. If she began with sketches and skipped that step she would probably repeat herself [§3].

**Use it:** Collect reference and language for a title treatment before drawing letters.
