Edwards listed four or five specific camera shots per scene but the maths of the shooting day allowed only a few setups, so he merged them into one elaborate move lasting the scene [36:21, 36:53]. He felt it was a compromise, but actors stopped being self-conscious [37:24]. The museum scene is a single take of four to five minutes; the grip told him it had 19 camera moves and seven rises and falls, the hardest thing in his career [37:55, 38:27]. Not VFX-heavy.

**Use it:** Design a continuous camera pass in C4D with each beat set up as its own stop, so one move can serve what would otherwise be separate shots.
