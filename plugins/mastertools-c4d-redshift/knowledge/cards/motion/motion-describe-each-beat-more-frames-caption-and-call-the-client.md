For a client job Ben Marriott turns one panel into about six: the ship falling, the splash, the settling, the bottles rising, each with a flavourful caption, using copy-pasted elements, so it takes little extra time [11:49-12:21]. It also helps designers and animators who will not be the person who boarded it [12:21-12:51].
The point is to find out at board stage that the client imagined the ship entering from the side, not after approving and animating it, which forces re-animation [12:51-13:23]. He now puts more effort into boards even on personal work because it helps him as much as clients [13:23].
Review habit: export PDF or PNG or share a commentable board, but also talk through the boards with the client on the phone, since a call is less scary than it seems and email alone loses detail [13:23-13:53].

**Use it:** put a captioned board and a live walk-through before anything gets built in C4D.
