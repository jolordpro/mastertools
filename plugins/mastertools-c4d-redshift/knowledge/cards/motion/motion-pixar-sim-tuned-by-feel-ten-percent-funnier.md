Simulation covers fur, weather, clothing, based on physics, but the sim team balances math with art; for the carnival plush the tuft's behaviour was modelled on ostrich and Muppet feathers, and the supervisor (Henry Garcia) found letting it keep moving a little longer after the character stopped was about ten percent funnier [09:20-10:22]. Character motion came from chosen reference (dancers, gymnasts, javelin throwers for the heroine; a stiff string-doll movement from hips, shoulders and neck for the doll) [08:49-09:20].

**Use it:** when a settle looks lifeless, extend the settle on secondary elements a little rather than cutting physics; choose reference per character.
