Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer) kept the yellow specular dot in the top left of a robot eye while the pupil moved, since a reflection does not follow the iris [40:50-41:22].
He searched for eye references and found none exact, concluding that imitating real things is hard because people just know when something feels wrong [41:22-41:55].

**Use it:** Detail check on characters: highlights belong to the light, not the eye.
