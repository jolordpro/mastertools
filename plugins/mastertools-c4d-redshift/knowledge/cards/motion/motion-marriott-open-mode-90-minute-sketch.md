Ben Marriott starts concept work with a 90 minute open mode session (term from a 1991 John Cleese creativity talk), kept separate from the later closed mode of execution [18:43-19:44]. He sketches pencil-tool black and white, ideally on paper, and nothing goes to the client, so silly or off-brief ideas are allowed [19:44-20:18]. If stuck, he draws the worst possible solution for the brief, which gives permission to start and may show what the opposite would be [20:18-20:48]. Ideas build on each other across pages [21:19]. Result in his course project: three pages of thumbnails, then four chosen [18:43].

**Use it:** the coach can suggest a timed open session before any styleframe, with a rule not to judge.
