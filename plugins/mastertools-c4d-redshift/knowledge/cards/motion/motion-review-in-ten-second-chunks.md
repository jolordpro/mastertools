Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer): working frame by frame, your brain joins two shapes that are not actually that close [23:12-23:43].
He exports and watches about ten seconds to judge whether the pace is too fast and whether a connection is real [23:43-24:13].

**Use it:** Review habit: judge motion in 10-second runs at playback speed, not by scrubbing.
