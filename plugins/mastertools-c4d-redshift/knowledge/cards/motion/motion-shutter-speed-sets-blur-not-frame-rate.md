[00:39] Same shutter speed gives identical blur at 24 and 60 fps. [02:11] A 180-degree rule at 120 fps with 1/120 shows much less blur than expected.
[01:40] Keep 180 degrees at 24/25/30 fps, and at high rates if the footage will be slowed down. [02:41] For 50 or 60p delivered as is, or dropped to a slower timeline, use 1/50 or 1/60. [04:17] The speaker says taste differs, so test both.

**Use it:** when setting motion blur on 59.94 deliverables, show both shutter values in the draft.
