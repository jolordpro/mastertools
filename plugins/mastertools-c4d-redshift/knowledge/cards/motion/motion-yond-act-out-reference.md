He does not identify as a character animator, so he nearly always records reference. It does not line up frame-for-frame, but seeing weight shifts and nuance helps a lot [53:17-53:47, 54:18].
On one social clip a dip onto one arm, a look around and a raised magnifying glass would not feel natural until he acted it out [54:50-55:21]. The host notes that professional animators do the same, filming or using a mirror.

**Use it:** when a motion note says 'floaty' or 'unnatural', suggest filming a 10-second reference first.
