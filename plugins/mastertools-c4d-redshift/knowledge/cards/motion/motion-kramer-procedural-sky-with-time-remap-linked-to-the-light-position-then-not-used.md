For a title piece he wanted a dynamic sky that could go from galaxy to night to day [23:20-23:52]. He built it in After Effects from many gradient layers with controls for time of day, haze, light and fog density, exposure; it goes to a night system [23:52-24:23]. He animated a sunrise, then used time remap tied to the position of the light so that moving the light moves the animation forward; it is 360 degrees [24:23-24:55].
It was his proudest build and they did not use it; a cross-dissolve was enough [24:55-25:26]. He referenced stock sunset footage rather than going out to shoot [24:55].

**Use it:** A rig that drives one animation from one control (light position) is a useful pattern; check first whether a cheaper trick solves the shot.
