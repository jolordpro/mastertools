StudioBinder [03:12-07:07, 15:36-26:27]. Pan follows or reveals; a slow pan builds anticipation, a whip pan adds energy and can be timed to music. Tilt shows verticality, dominance or vulnerability and scale. Roll turns the camera on its long axis and unsettles (panic, a power reversal). Tracking moves with a moving subject and raises where-is-it-going tension, handheld gives a documentary feel, smooth steadicam through corridors builds dread. Trucking is the lateral version and can establish a world. Arc orbits a subject, adding energy when characters stand still. Boom moves vertically: small to reveal, large to follow action. Random movement, such as shake and incidental zooms, gives a real-time documentary feel. Mixing controlled and chaotic shots in one scene intensifies both. Static shots suit dialogue and can trap a character.

**Use it:** Use as a menu when choosing a move for a title or opener: pick the move for its intent, not for energy alone.
