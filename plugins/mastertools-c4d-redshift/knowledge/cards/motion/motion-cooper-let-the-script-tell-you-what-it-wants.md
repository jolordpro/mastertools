Cooper [32:29-37:39] gives three idea stories. Argo: no stock photos of the Shah's excesses and no budget to shoot them, so the sequence became storyboards and production drawings, tying to the film's fake-movie plot [33:01-34:02]. Spider-Man: type caught in webs like flies, after which the real problem was making it not cheesy [35:35]. Moreau: eyes that transform, unprepared pitch. The common step is staring at the material until it shows what it wants; he says designers who arrive with a signature "sauce" cannot hear it.
After the idea comes a second job: preventing it from looking cheesy; he admits it sometimes still does.

**Use it:** use the budget limit as an idea source, and treat "make it not cheesy" as a distinct pass after the concept lands.
