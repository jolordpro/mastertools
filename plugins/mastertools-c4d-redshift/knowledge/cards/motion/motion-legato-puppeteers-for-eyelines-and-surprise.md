Puppeteers who work with children kept the boy engaged, sometimes miming to the recorded voice track and sometimes ad-libbing a line off-track to draw out a surprised reaction. A tennis ball on a stick gives far less than another person. The simulcam composite let the crew check eyelines on stage, and the director liberally used in-between takes where something funny happened. [§4]

**Use it:** For any digital character reacting to a real subject, give the subject a live stand-in at the right eyeline.
