He thinks of people as shapes and elements, moving them like motion graphics, to convey an emotion through movement rather than tell a story [27:53-28:25]. He works mostly in very short bursts from a first frame to a last frame, almost keyframe animation, with each key planned for pose and distance from camera so the movement between them has a certain type [28:25-28:57].
Structure: first a clean perfectly smooth morphing loop; everything on top is added in After Effects, while tracking and normals are generated in Comfy [29:28]. Clips of about two seconds are stitched into a long one [41:24]. He thinks in sequences of images, like a storyboard or choreographer [59:32-1:00:04].

**Use it:** Plan the key poses first, then render the in-between, then add the graphics layer last on a clean base.
