Viacom loops: designers worked in Photoshop, so animators did too to keep the texture, looking directly at the design [07:54]. Nothing film: tactile and digital pillars; snappy hard cuts and sharp graphs for futuristic, slow blurred footage for human [08:31-09:58]. Riot MSI: combative and raw regardless of element, mixing stepped low frame rate with smooth high-frame backgrounds and punchy type, then a guideline dissecting the basic steps of movement [12:46-14:50].

**Use it:** match frame rate and holds to the design's contrast and texture instead of defaulting to smooth eases.
