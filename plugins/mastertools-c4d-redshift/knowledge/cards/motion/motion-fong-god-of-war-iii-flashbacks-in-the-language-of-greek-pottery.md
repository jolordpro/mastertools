Guest: Karin Fong (Imaginary Forces, 2010 talk). The game director wanted cinematics that replay past events, so the team had to use a different lens on them so they would not be confused with the present story [00:16-00:47].
He liked their painterly, handmade end titles for The Mummy 3, which used Chinese brush painting to retell the story. For this game the main inspiration was ancient Greek art, especially pottery: a vase or frieze coming to life [00:47-01:18].
Process: a back-and-forth with the studio, who sent concept sketches and models to be folded into the designs. Stylised transitions make the story a dreamlike interpretation, and the main title is the "ultimate flashback" revisiting the series [01:18-02:50].

**Use it:** Give flashbacks or recaps their own visual language, drawn from an art form tied to the story world, so viewers know instantly it is not the present.
