Principles are higher-order ideas for tone and quality of movement (weighty momentum, snap and pop), not instructions on what to move.
Tactical layer: literal best examples of how to make things move, and what not to do.
F1 set: control, snap, audacious (glitch and bloom effects), tied to data meeting emotion.

**Use it:** Show principles first, examples second; keep principles to a short list others can repeat.
