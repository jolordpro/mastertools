The Video Shop [06:03-12:48]. The reviewer says a retro brief could have drawn on computer displays, early computer graphics (Whitney, Sketchpad, early shading demos) and mocked-up magazine covers, and he values noting what a team did not do. Bespoke collectible card art was lovely but lost in the animation, and mocked-up artwork varied in era feel. Another title using analog techniques still held an audience of about 500 because its animation was narrative-driven and flowed, while the Fantastic Four titles felt jarring in places. He admits the audience is a general one, and a motion-design nerd judges differently. Low confidence: one reviewer's opinion.

**Use it:** When critiquing a period or homage piece, check for flow from shot to shot and one consistent reference era, not only for faithful techniques.
