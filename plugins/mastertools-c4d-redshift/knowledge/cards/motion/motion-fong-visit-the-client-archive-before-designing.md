For a short Herman Miller film the team toured the factory and archive in Michigan [30:01]. An archivist fetched original blueprints, a signed royalty letter and the whole logo progression on request, plus old ads and direct mail [30:32-31:34; also CM 26:40-27:10]. The film's idea (connect designers through the designer's hand drawing) came out of that material [CM 26:10]. Bigger point: ask for the real history, not a downloaded logo.

**Use it:** For brand or broadcast packages, ask the client for source history (logo versions, old graphics packages, original specs) in the kickoff and build the first board from it.
