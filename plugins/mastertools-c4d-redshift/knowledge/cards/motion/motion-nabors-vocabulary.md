Rachel Nabors: build the vocabulary from words the team already says (pop, plunk, slide), 'paving the cow paths'. Micro motions layer into macro patterns (fade in, pop, slide away = notification and dismissal). She points to the Lightning motion pages that show each with an interactive demo, and says a group conversation builds buy-in.

**Use it:** Start the glossary from team speech; publish pattern names with demos.
