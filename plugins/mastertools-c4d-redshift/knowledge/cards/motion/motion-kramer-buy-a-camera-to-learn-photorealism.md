He argues the ultimate form of motion graphics is photorealism, so shoot real things with a camera, then recreate them and learn how depth-of-field blur behaves. He says understanding real things and rebuilding them your own way is the key; a planned plugin simulates lens calibration, bokeh, camera motion, real-time motion blur, camera blur and grain [§1]. Japanese source, read in translation; 2016 interview.

**Use it:** Shoot a real reference before building a CG version, and match its blur and grain.
