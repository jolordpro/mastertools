Fong steps back to ask whether the chosen editorial style furthers the emotional mood or agenda of the story, or is only there to cover a gap [53:03-53:36].
Example: on an indie film influenced by Lower East Side punk flyers she argued for hand-done, different-looking credits, which needed extra approval work; it needs a good reason and the time to do it [41:00-41:33].
She also judges a main title by whether it is true to a very specific subculture and era, diving into the material rather than reaching for what is trendy [50:26-50:57].

**Use it:** Before locking a style, write one sentence on which emotion the style carries, and drop it if the honest answer is that it hides a limitation.
