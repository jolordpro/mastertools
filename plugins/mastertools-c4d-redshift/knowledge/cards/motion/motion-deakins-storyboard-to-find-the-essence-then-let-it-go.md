From years with the Coens he learned that storyboarding matters less for fixing exact shots than for forcing the team to find the essence of the scene. The boards often go out the window once shooting starts, but what was learned about story and characters guides the changes made on the day. [§1]

**Use it:** Board to understand the scene, then treat the boards as disposable once the work starts.
