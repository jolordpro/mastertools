She works from pre-existing faces, modifying them: the Mummy serif was adjusted to echo Chinese chops with some ornament, and logos are often custom or hand-modified (the Cat in the Hat 3D logo) [50:00-51:32]. For Boardwalk Empire a vintage type exploration was dropped because HBO wanted to avoid a period-piece feel, so more modern type contrasted; the end type came from HBO marketing [50:31-51:32]. She says the first job of a title is to show the credits, ignoring type does yourself a disservice, and nothing teaches kerning like seeing your work on a big screen [52:03].

**Use it:** Test a contrasting typeface against the subject's era before settling, and review type on the largest screen available.
