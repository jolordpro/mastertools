Titles are usually made late, near the end of production, while the cut can still change: endings dropped, characters cut, tone shifting [29:03-29:35].
She asks for dailies, set designs, costumes and the work of the other designers, and sometimes gets a rough assembly with no final music; rarely a near-final cut [29:35-30:06].
Early contact pays: you may shoot on their set, with their actors, or burn props on the last day of the shoot while pyrotechnics are still on site (she did this for a show with objects burning) [30:38-31:09].
Student title exercises mislead because the film is finished and fixed [29:03].

**Use it:** Ask production for dailies, set and costume designs and the shoot schedule on day one, and ask whether any elements can be shot on the last shoot day.
