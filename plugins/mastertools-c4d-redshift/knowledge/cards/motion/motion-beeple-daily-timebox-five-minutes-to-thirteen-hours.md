Asked live, Beeple said his shortest daily took about five minutes and his longest well over 13 hours (he says he did not do it on purpose) [41:01-41:29].
He tries to finish the animated version of a still in one day, since a loop does not deserve more than a day of life [39:48-40:22].
How long the path-traced render runs depends on how much time he has more than on any quality target [40:58-41:01].
He started the dailies on 1 May 2007, only to get better at drawing [20:27-20:58]. During the demo he set a 10-minute timer to stay on track [09:49].

**Use it:** Time-box a daily or a style frame, and fix the render budget by the schedule, not by a quality target.
