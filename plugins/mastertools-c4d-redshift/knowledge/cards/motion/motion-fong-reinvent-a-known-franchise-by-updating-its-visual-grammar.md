For Terminator she kept the idea of huge crossing letters from the first film but gave them concrete, gritty metal textures with virtual ones [00:14-01:15]. Machine-vision shots (the machine's POV) were once red-tinted with a grid and computer text; she updated them from current machine technology [01:15-02:27]. For Pink Panther the spirit and gags of the character stay, while backgrounds and effects update for today; she values cel animators for their comic timing and expressions [03:00-04:22].

**Use it:** For a returning series or team brand: pick the one familiar element to keep and a modern source for the rest, rather than a full restyle.
