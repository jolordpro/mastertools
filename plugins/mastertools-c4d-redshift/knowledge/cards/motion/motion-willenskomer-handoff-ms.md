Issara Willenskomer (2020 livestream): frames shift with project frame rate, so give ms. Options: export Lottie JSON from After Effects (vector shape layers only), or a written spec with properties, start/end, duration, easing. Shared libraries of curves can sync with engineers. He also says to check engineering limits early (an example: dissolves between apps were ruled out).

**Use it:** Put ms and engineer constraints in every handoff note.
