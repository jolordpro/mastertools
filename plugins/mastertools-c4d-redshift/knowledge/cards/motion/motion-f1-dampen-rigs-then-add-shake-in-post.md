Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour): key grip and cinematographer tuned damping so the rig kept some energy, errors on the smooth side [09:52-10:23].
In post they added shake as needed for the story beat, so they never had to rescue footage from too much shake [10:23-10:54].

**Use it:** Motion rule: add camera shake as a controllable layer rather than baking it in; cleaner to rescue.
