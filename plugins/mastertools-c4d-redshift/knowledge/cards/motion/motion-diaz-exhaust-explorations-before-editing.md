On the Microsoft 365 film she did a long design phase and many studies in Cinema 4D and Redshift: isometric clean backdrops, glass-like acrylic panels, a colour-bleed collaboration idea [05:26-07:58].
Microsoft thought the bleeding glass too abstract; she says she already guessed that but likes to try first [07:58-08:29]. Do not limit yourself early; clients may be more open than you think [08:29-09:32].
The final used straight panels, with text added to be understandable on first view, and a camera moving between the two live-action people [09:32-10:34].

**Use it:** propose one bold option beside the safe ones in a style round; keep the fallback ready.
