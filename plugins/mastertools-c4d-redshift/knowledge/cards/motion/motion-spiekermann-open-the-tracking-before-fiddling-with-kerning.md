He says kerning is overrated: if kerning were important, letters would be square like Chinese characters, and 'life is too short for kerning' [38:42]. His quick fix: whatever you do, open up the tracking by 5 or 10 units in InDesign and it looks better already [40:17].
He compares tight setting to fat guys wearing tight t-shirts, and says you can size up the type to avoid it [40:17].
(Units are his InDesign numbers, thousandths of an em as usual in that tool; not stated in the clip.)

**Use it:** For small or dense text on screen, add a little positive tracking first, then check pairs only where they fail.
