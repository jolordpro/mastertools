The Eye profile says Cooper moved from the adrenaline assault toward wonderment: he lays out a context using the signs of mass culture and then does not stop, establishing a cadence and advancing particular moments that are small experiments in perception and play [Eye §2-§3]. He admires the poetic intimacy of Stephen Frankfurt's To Kill a Mockingbird title as very hard to achieve [Eye §3]. He cites montage, image layering and multi-image screens as the dramatic efficiencies he inherits [Eye §3]. His goal for a title is to make everything else disappear except curiosity and anticipation for this film [reverttosaved §3].

**Use it:** Plan a title as a rhythm with a few designed moments, not a single repeated device.
