Production designer Alex McDowell brought Imaginary Forces in; Spielberg did not want the interface to feel 'tech' but to come straight from the cortex in a dream state [§4]. The team worked with MIT Media Lab's John Underkoffler, whose thesis was on gestural interfaces, and used edgeless planes that could be rearranged [§4]. They added 'dream time', where time is subjective and overlaps, so the user manipulates time as inputs form a jigsaw [§4].

**Use it:** For a fictional interface, begin with the feeling the director wants, then pick the interaction logic that delivers it.
