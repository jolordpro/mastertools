On open briefs his team aims for something unlike their usual work, and he thinks audiences respond to that. On work with a set look, such as Cartoon Network characters, they match the established look exactly.
On original work the hard part is defining the look, the concept and the characters. [§2-§3]

**Use it:** On an open brief, state the boldest safe idea; on an established brand, match the rules to the letter.
