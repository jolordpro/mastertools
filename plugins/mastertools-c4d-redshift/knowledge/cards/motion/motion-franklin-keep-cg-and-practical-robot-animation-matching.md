Nolan wanted the robots not to be androids but able to work with actors. Most shots used real units driven by a person who was erased in post. Full CG covered shots that could not be filmed: fast movement on water, ice or in zero gravity. The care point was keeping animation consistent between the CG and the practical versions. The ice planet was shot on a real glacier in Iceland with careful, natural set extensions. [§2]

**Use it:** When mixing practical and CG versions of one character, lock a shared movement vocabulary before animating.
