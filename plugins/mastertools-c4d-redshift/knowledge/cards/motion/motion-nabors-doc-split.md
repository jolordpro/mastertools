In 2016 Rachel Nabors found few good motion sections in design systems. She sees designer-led docs (IBM, Material) teach principles, and developer-led docs (Salesforce Lightning) give component numbers. Use storyboards (words, the why), animatics (look, not a deliverable) and prototypes (inspectable) together. She says nobody finds one right format.

**Use it:** Check each motion doc page for a 'why' and a 'numbers' part.
