He caught and kept black widows to photograph and model, and the octopus (Doc Ock) stood against the spider because both have eight legs and similar bodies; "the metaphors of these animals already existed". The titles took almost a year, opened with a five-second montage of scanned vintage comics covering the first film's arc, then the credits get caught in a web like flies [§1, §3]. The homage was to Saul Bass's black cat versus white cat in Walk on the Wild Side [§3].

**Use it:** Look for a real subject that already carries the metaphor before inventing graphics; studying it gives believable detail.
