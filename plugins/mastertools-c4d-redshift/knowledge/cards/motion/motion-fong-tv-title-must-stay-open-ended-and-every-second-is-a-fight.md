A film title knows what scene comes before and after. A series can run for years with characters killed and themes shifting, so the title should be more of a feeling or tone for the whole story and need not relate to the next scene [35:59-37:02]. Length is the other fight: HBO gave a luxurious 90 seconds for Boardwalk Empire, other networks give 30 or even 20, and with no time for a build the question becomes whether it is mostly a title card; a 30-second arc differs from a 90-second one. Extra seconds come out of advertising or the show, so she keeps fighting for them [1:00:54-1:02:32].

**Use it:** Ask for the real duration up front; design a short version as its own arc, not a trimmed long one.
