Ben Marriott learned at Buck that pre-production (sketches, animatics, design) takes more of a project than expected [40:58-41:29]. "Eating your vegetables first": by animation you are "rolling downhill" with fewer client check-ins and no surprises [41:29-42:31]. Decide placement of lines and type in loose black and white when mistakes are free [42:31-43:32]. His rule of thumb for 10 days of design plus animation is at least 6 on design and about 4 on animation, for his illustrative 2D work [1:01:50-1:02:20]. He tries not to think about animation while designing, since it makes him cut corners; animators at Buck often join after the design is approved [1:00:16-1:01:20, 1:03:51].

**Use it:** sanity-check estimates; transferable to sports packages where styleframes get approval before 3D build.
