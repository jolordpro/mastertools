Ben Marriott: after sketching, sleep on it, because excitement often hides that an idea misses the brief [37:53-38:55]. But an idea you are excited about can be made the best one with extra care. His pitch deck had four style frames: the title plus three at different scales (molecule, phage, paramecium) [38:55-39:26]. Reason: a client sees each frame as a chance to like something; one frame gives one chance to lose; variety shows "the edges of the box" the sequence will live in [39:26-39:56].

**Use it:** estimator/coach: plan three distinct options per pitch, not three tweaks of one.
