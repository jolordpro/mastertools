Fong noticed in the script that the lead plays Go daily and asked the showrunner about it; he explained Go as a metaphor, fewer moves than chess but more permutations, which became how many ways a life can go [§2-§3]. She kept one question as an idea prompt: what does the template of your destiny look like, a maze, cubicles, a pachinko machine [§3]. She scrawled on the script the idea of the same Go game played two ways at once, the game splitting [§3]. The team then explored black and white circles and a gridded board as motifs [§4].

**Use it:** Build the concept from one recurring object or question in the script and keep a single prompt phrase to return to when ideas stall.
