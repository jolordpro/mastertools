A 2018 essay credited to Jonas Naimark (mirror; Medium original blocked) says container transitions link start and end screens, duration grows with the screen area covered, a switch is about 100 ms, and standard easing is asymmetric so linear looks stiff. Attribution on the mirror is mixed.
