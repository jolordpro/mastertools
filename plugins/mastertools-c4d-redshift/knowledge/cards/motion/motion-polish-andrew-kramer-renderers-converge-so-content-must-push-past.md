His view (2017) [02:55-04:00]: results between renderers are not that different and all amazing; once a good render is available to everyone daily, the bar shifts to content quality and a next revolution that nobody can name yet. Learners should ask where to take it after the skill is learned. His tool philosophy: make cool things available without the roughly 3,000 dollars of software and training barrier [04:30-05:34].
**Use it:** Use as a prompt in plan challenges: if a render alone is the selling point, what makes this piece better than another default Redshift render?
