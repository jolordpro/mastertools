When the final music arrives varies by job: True Detective had it in the brief, other times it is last [07:23-07:54]. His animator-partner finds it essential, so temp music is used from the start of the process and the cut is adjusted every time the track changes [07:54-08:26].
He says music is in many ways more important than the pictures, with a dance between them [08:26].

**Use it:** Cut to a temp track from the first animatic and re-time when the track changes.
