For the Squarespace identity (2018) the studio animated and designed in After Effects without a fixed target, built a large library of experiments, then reduced it to six defined behaviors / containing systems (e.g. framing, moving in and out of images, slideshows, compositions). Those became the system.

**Use it:** Use it as a project-order pattern: wide exploration, then cut to a small named set (about six) that others can apply.
