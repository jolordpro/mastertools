For shots with no live plate, a mocap double was filmed over multiple takes and the take chosen was one where the boy moved enough to feel real without looking staged. The colorist insisted the shot must be live because the body was breathing; that was exactly why it was captured, since such motion is not something animators think to add. [§4]

**Use it:** Add idle breathing and small weight shifts to any held digital figure.
