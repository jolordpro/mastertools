Hoon Kim (Beeble CEO) [06:21-08:29]: filmmakers do not accept a speed-versus-control trade-off; they want full control at full speed. He splits control into four levels.
Pixel: what to keep and what to replace, via a mask. Frame: lighting and background for a frame, via a reference image. Shot: camera tracking and dynamic motion within a shot. Multi-shot: generated shots must match when cut together, or they read as slop.

**Use it:** use as a checklist when judging an AI shot: is each of the four levels controlled or left to chance.
