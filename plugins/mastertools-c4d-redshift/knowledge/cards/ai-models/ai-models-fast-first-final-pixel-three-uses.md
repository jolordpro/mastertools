Ryan Summers (guest on a ComfyUI stream, with studio owner Erin Sarofsky) sorts AI use into three kinds [34:14-35:46]. Fast pixel: quick iteration or a pitch image. First pixel: a start frame that tells the story. Final pixel: what ships.
A studio can be against AI in final pixel and still use it for the first two. His example: an agency pitch needed an animated soda bottle with a shadow; five ideas were made in two hours, dropped in the deck, and the agency won the next day [35:15].
Opinion from a working studio, not a measured result.

**Use it:** a scene-check or coach note for AI work can ask which of the three the shot is, before asking for a policy answer.
