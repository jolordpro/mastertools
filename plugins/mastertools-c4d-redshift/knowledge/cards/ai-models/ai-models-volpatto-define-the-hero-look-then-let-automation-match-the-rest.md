He says most of what colourists do will probably move to automation: once the photography is defined as pixels and rules, a colourist decides where a scene should live and AI does the rest [47:01-48:01].
His own wish: set the main scene's best look, have an AI match all shots, and review; that saves an hour but gets paid an hour less [52:10-52:40].
A risk he names: producers copying a movie's look with a one-click match, like printing millions of the David [48:01-49:05].
Opinion from 2021 and he adds that clients still hire for the relationship and judgement [49:05-49:35]. Confidence low.

**Use it:** Use automated matching for continuity between shots only after the hero look is approved by a person.
