Sarofsky [1:02:07-1:03:40]: a museum job had twelve After Effects puppet characters, a famous voice actor who was free only four days before opening, and a 9K render that could not wait. Characters without moving mouths felt uncanny with the fluid body motion.
First pass used Infinite Talk, which only flapped. About two weeks before delivery Kling's lip sync worked, and it did not hurt the performance or render quality. Her point: she could now hire any actor late and animate first.

**Use it:** flag late voice-over as a lip-sync-after risk to test early; do not hold the long render for audio.
