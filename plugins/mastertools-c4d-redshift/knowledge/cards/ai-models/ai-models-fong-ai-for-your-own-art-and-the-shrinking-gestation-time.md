She supports AI as a tool: she'd gladly use it for wire removal and to free budget for more illustrators and animators [1:12:14-1:12:46]. Best use now is feeding it your own art to make backgrounds faster (multitudes of trees, buildings) or for texturing from the team's own drawings; her team explored this on Percy Jackson but it never got there [1:03:27, 1:13:48-1:14:18]. On an AI-themed project she generated shots in Runway and others, and found it still took a lot of manipulation to get elements to cooperate [1:14:50]. Worries: copyright and consent, AI training on its own output, and speed becoming a mandate so there is no time left for nuance (her father's line about the nine months to make a baby) [1:17:26-1:19:02].

**Use it:** Policy-style note: keep generation inside licensed or owned art, and protect the review-time budget when a tool speeds one stage. Recheck: fast-moving.
