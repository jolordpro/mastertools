In Cinema 4D on a title sequence he would cap any frame at five minutes and could still hit that because he controlled the look [11:49].
In Comfy he makes the same call per shot: close-ups get settings that stay tight to the source illustration and resolution; far shots go fast and cheap because they only need to do one or two things [12:20]. He keeps the table pinned beside his graph.

**Use it:** copy the habit for renders too: write per-shot-type budgets (close, mid, wide) and give far shots the cheaper settings.
