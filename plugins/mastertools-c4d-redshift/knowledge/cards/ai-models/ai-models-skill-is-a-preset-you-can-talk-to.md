A normal preset is locked: use it as made or not at all. A skill is the same idea, but he can tell it "not quite, more like this" and it adapts [1:08:00].
Technical skills (the exploded view, the Rubik's Cube rig) seem obvious wins; "art director brain" skills (camera feel, taste) are untried, and he said even 70% of the author's ability would be impressive [1:09:01].
Nick Campbell floated subscribing to a favourite artist's skills (for example a camera-move skill) instead of watching an hour-long tutorial and rewinding it [1:05:25]. Both hosts said they had not tested whether taste can be packed into a skill.

**Use it:** shape platform presets (render settings, light rigs) as adjustable conversations, and treat packaged "taste" as unproven.
