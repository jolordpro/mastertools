Test: take a random ad prompt (his was just a list of grapes), feed Midjourney a mood board of studio and artist references, and let it generate [49:00-49:32].
Result he calls unusable for production, but valuable for ideation: his rule is that the best idea is not the first one, so he digs through debris, and the tool produces odd combinations he would not have searched for (a bleach-bypass, crime-documentary look; something leaking onto a newspaper) [50:03-51:04].
He uses voice-mode chat the same way, asking it to act as a difficult creative director and throw bad ideas that trigger good ones [1:02:52].

**Use it:** fits the concepting step before style frames; keep the output as reference prompts, not deliverables.
