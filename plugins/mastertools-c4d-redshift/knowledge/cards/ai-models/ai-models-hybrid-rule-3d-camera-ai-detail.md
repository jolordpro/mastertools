Hanno Basse's ranking of where to use AI [1:15:09-1:19:00]: real actors or mocap for humanoid performance; 3D environments for camera moves, because video models still get object relations and parallax wrong when the camera moves; gen-AI for ideation, detail, and blurry non-hero backgrounds (matte paintings); he thinks it may beat procedural sims for water, smoke and fire; ML for repetitive jobs, e.g. a neural cloth sim at Digital Domain that removed a manual artist job.
Also: do not lock to one model, mix commercial and open weights, fine-tune a model for a show's look, use LoRAs for characters or elements [1:13:37-1:14:39]. He says tooling is cheap now; the workflow is the asset [1:17:45]. Opinion of one CTO, low confidence.

**Use it:** Decide per shot: camera move and logo/hero geometry in C4D, AI only for soft background texture or exploration frames.
