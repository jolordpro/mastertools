A professional editor-filmmaker made a spec spot entirely from Google Veo 2 footage; he praises it because the editor is no longer beholden to the footage available [39:42].
Rip-o-matic = a pitch edit built from other films and ads; the slow part is finding footage, so being able to prompt about 20 takes and pick one removes the bottleneck [40:13-40:43, 48:00].
Premiere (as of the talk): auto-transcript lets him edit like a text document, about 4x faster; a beta that logs footage; AI clip extend [47:28-48:00]. His claim: editors who skip these are behind.
His caveat: motion design may benefit less because training data of motion graphics is thin compared with YouTube video [48:30].

**Use it:** pitch-stage option for mood footage; label AI shots clearly in any client deck.
