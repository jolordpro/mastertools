Stills came out realistic, but the actor's face changed on each frame [31:51-32:22]. Video attempts slid, changed time of day, added cars and streets, froze the rain, or turned the actor silver [33:24-37:34]. After 22 versions the rain never returned and the wet shirt from stills never appeared in motion [35:28-37:34]. His own tests could not extract above HD, while the delivery was 6K [34:57]. What he kept: the best reflection from the output upscaled in CopyCat, rotoed with Cryptomatte and merged into the reflection pass [38:36-40:08]. Conclusion: clients still think it is one push of a button; good, cheap and fast cannot all be had [47:33-48:35].

**Use it:** Use AI output as plates for comp pieces (a reflection, a wet pass), not the shot; say so when a client brings an AI frame as the target. Supports ai-models-hybrid-3d-or-pure-ai-decision.
