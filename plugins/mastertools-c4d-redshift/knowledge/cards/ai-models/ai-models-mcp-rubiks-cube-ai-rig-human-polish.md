Nick Campbell asked for a Rubik's Cube with the real colour layout that scrambles for 10 seconds and unscrambles into a loop; objects must stick together, release and re-stick, which he had never solved by hand [54:01-55:35].
The first try worked in one shot. He then did the parts he likes [55:35-57:09]: sped the timing, asked for easing instead of mechanical moves, added a Signal effector on the centre null for a floating orbit and made it loop on the same length, changed the camera, tweaked materials (thumbprints on the stickers) and the background, and asked the MCP for background variations.
Takeaway in his words: the helper did the technical rig; he did lighting, textures and brightness [57:09]. Chad Ashley said the MCP raises everyone's technical ceiling [58:12].

**Use it:** a loop task is a good agent benchmark: scripted mechanics from the agent, timing feel and look from the artist.
