Clients ask to save 15 percent; he sees it as multiplying what one person can do [40:05-40:37]. He never lets AI come up with the idea; he gives it jobs like splitting a storyboard into individual panels (a folder of images that would take a day) [41:09-41:50]. Offloading frees mental capacity and cuts producer interruptions so a task is not dragged from a week to six months [42:09-43:41]. He compares the director owning ideas and orchestration without having filmed [30:14-31:15].

**Use it:** In plans, state AI use as leverage on named chores, not as a blanket discount.
