Joseph Martin, June 2026, five test shots [00:00-01:02]: native 4K at 330 credits each; 720p at 68 credits then Topaz upscale at 51 credits, about a third of the 4K cost. In a blind side-by-side he struggled to tell which was which [04:16]. Pixel peeping: native 4K better on the guitar scene; the upscaled turtle looked over-sharpened and "overly digital"; on another shot the native was too sharp and fake and he preferred the upscale [04:16-04:47]. His takeaway: viewers care about composition and light more than resolution. Downside of upscaling: download and re-upload, and 15-20 minutes for one 15-second generation; a Resolve plugin does it in-app [05:18-05:49].

**Use it:** For cost modelling of AI plates, generate low and upscale unless a shot proves otherwise; prices change often.
