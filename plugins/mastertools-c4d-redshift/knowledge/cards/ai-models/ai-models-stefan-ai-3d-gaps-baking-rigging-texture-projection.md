Texturing is improving: a tool called Modif projects an AI texture from a view with a brush and understands material properties, replacing early Substance steps [16:28-17:29]. No classic high-to-low bake exists; he expects normal and AO maps to be generated from colour or geometry instead, and says Modif already makes normals from colour [18:00-18:31].
Rigging is unsolved for AAA; Mixamo and AccuRig are only starting points needing revision [18:31-19:33]. He calls rigging and animation the safest zone for jobs.

**Use it:** If a client asks for AI-generated 3D assets, budget cleanup, UV, texture fix and rigging time; the generation step is the cheap part.
