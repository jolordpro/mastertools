He generates stills first, thinking of each person's shape relative to the next, not what the person is doing [49:50-50:21]. If one pose faces left and the next right, the in-between is likely a rotation in a 65-frame clip [50:21]. The stills work like storyboard frames (a face, something entering the eye, coming back out), and he runs two at a time through a morph [50:21-50:51]. Stills stay AI; the rest is built on top.

**Use it:** Choose keyframe pairs for their silhouette difference to control the in-between motion.
