[14:28 to 15:29] Same brand, three licences: Schnell Apache 2 (do nearly anything), Dev open-weights but not for paying work, Pro hosted only. A team had to swap Flux Dev out when a project began paying.
Open weights is not open source. Buckets: permissive, copyleft (AGPL network clause), source-available, and custom model licences with bespoke, sometimes revocable terms. A stack is only as open as its least open licence [16:00].

**Use it:** check licence tier before a model touches a paid shot.
