Nuno Silva built the app with a hosted no-code agent [00:33-06:46]. Features chosen: sign-in, text plus visual feedback, JPEG/PNG, history, before/after comparison, per-category score and PDF export; later a focus field (e.g. camera angle and lighting), analytics of scores over time with category averages, project grouping, public/private share links and learning tips. Observed behaviour: a very dark test render scored 60 overall, lighting 58, with extremely dark areas highlighted on the image; the finished render scored 82 overall, lighting nearly 80, composition 81, and was flagged for an overexposed window and flat fabric texture. The 'AI improve' button makes a new image and is stated to be a visual reference only, not a final; he urges not to trust all of it. A before/after slider made it readable. The rest of the video is a sponsor read (omitted).

**Use it:** Design cues for the coach: scores per category, regions marked on the frame, the user's chosen focus, and any generated 'improved' image labelled as reference only.
