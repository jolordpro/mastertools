Her felted, puppet-like portrait look: newer models made it too photoreal and too human, so she kept older ones for a while to hold the look [20:43-21:47].
Summers compares it to film stock: after a stock upgrade the grain was tighter and better, yet five minutes in it no longer felt like the old film. He suggests locking a pipeline to the model that gives the look, and not auto-updating [21:47].

**Use it:** a look-lock note should name the exact model and version, not only the prompt.
