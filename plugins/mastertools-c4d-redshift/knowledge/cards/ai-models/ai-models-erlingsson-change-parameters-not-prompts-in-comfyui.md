He compares ComfyUI with node platforms as 'autocomplete versus an LLM': for open models you can change how the model functions; if motion blur is too high or movement slow you change parameters instead of the prompt [34:42-36:17]. He says nearly everything in After Effects can be done in Comfy except a real timeline, and many of his nodes are quicker versions of old AE tricks [36:17].
Not every job needs Comfy; closed models only allow prompts and references [34:42-35:13]. Many companies want Comfy but have nobody who can run and fix it [37:18].

**Use it:** Pick the tool by the control a note requires: if a note is a parameter, make it a parameter.
