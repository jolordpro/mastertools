Michael Rubloff (radiancefields.com): static splats work like photography, dynamic or 4D splats like video, built from many synchronised cameras [53:58] [57:06]. His own capture: over 300,000 images in about 20 seconds (around 11,000 images a second), with the dataset, COLMAP files and 1,800 trained PLY frames open for request [54:30] [55:01] [56:03].
Examples as stated: all shots of Superman's parents are dynamic splats, captured by Infinite Realities and processed by Framestore; the director watched by video call, then the team "filmed in post", choosing angles later, saving money against digital doubles [56:34] [57:06] [57:37]. Sinners used a rig of ten action cameras to train an avatar of the lead actor [51:22]. A$AP Rocky's Helicopter video uses dynamic splats for most people [58:08]. Reduced-camera methods: 4DV.ai is down to four cameras plus generative models; Netflix Eyeline has a method named DEGS; NVIDIA's QUEEN is open source [1:01:16] [1:01:49].
Some of this is hearsay from the speaker's conversations; no VFX supervisor is quoted in the transcript.

**Use it:** Context for 'could we scan a person / venue?' questions: feasible, but needs many cameras and heavy data. Use for previs and scouting more than hero shots.
