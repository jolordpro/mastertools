His test: give 100 people the same retopology or UV job with a poly budget (say 15,000) and you get nearly the same result, so it is routine and will be automated; creative choices will not repeat [25:08-26:10].
He says studios are already hiring one person instead of ten for half the assets of a large game, and that 'the market doesn't care' about authorship arguments when the cost is ten times lower [00:00, 09:47-10:47]. These are his reports from consulting, not data.

**Use it:** Position value on direction, look and polish where automation is weakest, rather than on asset production.
