Michael Rubloff (radiancefields.com): photogrammetry struggles with thin structures, reflective surfaces and foliage; splats are more forgiving, can be reprocessed from existing data, and can be georeferenced [39:23] [39:54]. He says centimetre accuracy was viable already, and Esri announced survey-grade measurement directly from splats at Geo Week, coming later in the year [40:27] [40:57]. Esri also published a JavaScript SDK with browser-rendered large-scale scenes, up to city scale [42:30].
Use cases he describes: construction progress by aligning two splats in time, stakeholder walkthroughs sent as a URL to phones, and an empty-looking site capture on a working day with only ghost artifacts of people [35:45] [36:17] [37:20].
Claims are from a vendor-friendly evangelist.

**Use it:** When asked whether a splat can replace measured survey: cite it as 'announced, not yet shipped' and keep photogrammetry in parallel.
