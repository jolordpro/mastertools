Cube Creative [07:34-09:43]. For a teaser made in less than a month, the animation tools and rigs in Blender were not yet at 3ds Max quality, so they animated in Max, transferred the animated geometry to Blender for shading, lighting, rendering and a few effects, then composited in Nuke with existing in-house tools. They chose the in-house workflow to stay efficient on the short schedule. Animation is half the time and half the budget of production [19:56].

**Use it:** Evidence that a split pipeline is a normal way to switch tools: move only the stages that are ready, and reuse the compositing tools already trusted.
