Sergei Volkov [19:30-22:08]: 80 per cent of progress takes 20 per cent of the time; the last 20 per cent is small finishing items. Industry practice: pick representative frames (first, middle, last, or four or five) and keep iterating on those, then render the full range as a push-button step once they hold. Do not marry yourself to the whole-range render.

**Use it:** A review ritual: approve on sample frames, then spend farm time once.
