On The Jungle Book the team had a virtual camera, only a portal into the virtual world. For The Lion King they added VR so people could stand in the landscape and walk around like on a live set, crouch, say 'move that rock', and find better shots [01:04].
They then had to make it a production tool so cinematographer Caleb Deschanel could walk in, pick up a camera as he is used to, lay dolly track and light it the way he normally does. The result looks as if they shot in Africa [01:35].

**Use it:** A familiar physical interface (a real camera rig, real dolly moves) gives CG shots the accident and rhythm of live work. When setting up a CG camera, mimic a real camera package and its limits.
