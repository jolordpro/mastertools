Courtney Price [42:16-46:00]: she follows an idea from Raphael Ham to store useful procedures and tools as her own cookbook, kept as backdrop groups rather than registered gizmos so she can open them in any script, so she never rebuilds a normals relight, position matte or despill tool.
She also keeps her user settings, menu scripts and toolsets in a cloud-synced folder; at a new studio she asks to load it, adds the plugin path and restarts.
Tools named: Doug's Curve Creator (camera shake), Bokeh Builder, Rob Bannister's Edge tool, Pixel Filler.

**Use it:** Do the same for After Effects: one synced folder of presets, scripts and comps, and a plan for loading it on a new machine.
