Old Final Cut Studio and Color hand-offs failed on things like speed ramps, which the two programs treat differently [33:54-34:26]. In Resolve the shots and their metadata are the same across edit, colour and audio, so a creative can hand the project and media and the colorist relinks, with no conform [34:26-35:26]. Because the editor then has his project file, his contract says anyone who changes it must add an additional-colour credit [35:58-36:30].

**Use it:** Hand off the project, not an export, wherever the tools allow, and log who changed what.
