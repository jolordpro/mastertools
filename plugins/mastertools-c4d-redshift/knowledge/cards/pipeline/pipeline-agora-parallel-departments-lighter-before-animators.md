Sebastian (cinematic director at Agora Studio; surname garbled in captions) said handing off like a relay race did not fit real time; department work runs in parallel [15:33-17:36]. A lighter joined before the animators to pre-light the environments [17:06-17:36]. Lighting and post-process were pushed to 70 to 80 percent of the look for the whole sequence before tuning shot by shot [19:39]. With no compositing step at the end (goal: final pixel in engine), the compositing work was absorbed into lighting and post-process layers [16:36]. It is likened to a set where everyone adjusts between takes; it needs a clear vision or it is chaos [17:36-18:37].

**Use it:** Pre-light the sequence before the shot-level look-dev; in C4D, lock a base light rig first. Needs a clear owner for the vision.
