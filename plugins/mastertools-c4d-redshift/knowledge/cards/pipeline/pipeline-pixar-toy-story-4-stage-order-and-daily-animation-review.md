Order [00:35-12:25]: storyboards cut into a story reel with scratch sound and temporary dialogue; concept and character design with research trips; colour scripts of single frames to set lighting scheme and mood; modelling with real-world photos of toys beside objects for scale; rigging; shading; voice recording (filmed to guide facial animation); layout (staging and camera, sets not yet fully dressed); animation; simulation; lighting with hundreds of virtual lights; render at 24 frames per second; effects artists add dust and atmosphere last [11:24-12:25].
Numbers: up to 1000 avars per character [07:47]; 8 weeks or more per shot; each day the department reviews the one or two seconds just produced [08:18]; about five years total [12:25].
