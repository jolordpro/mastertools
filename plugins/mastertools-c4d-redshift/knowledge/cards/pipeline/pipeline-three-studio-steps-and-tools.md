Asset steps are modeling, shading (surfacing), rigging (setup) at all three, plus an "animation bank" at Cube [20:45] [21:18]. The animation bank is facial expressions and cycles authored on the character and reused in shots [20:45].
Shot steps are layout, animation, FX, lighting or render, compositing [21:49]. Difference: Cube builds FX as reusable assets, the other two make them in the shot [22:20].
Tools: Blender for 3D in all three, Kitsu to track progress in all three, standalone Python tools, Nuke in post for two, OpenPipe at one, After Effects and Premiere at one, Storyboard Pro for pre-production [22:53] [23:24] [23:57].
The scope studied was production only, not pre-production or post [20:45].

**Use it:** Use as a baseline list of departments and handoffs when writing pipeline checks for a small studio. Compare against own project step list.
