Turned-over shots are blue [29:52]; a vendor's "version 0" (frames and format check, no effects) is swapped in and made orange; a delivered effects shot gets another colour such as lilac [49:08, 49:40].
Uncoloured shots still need to go to the vendor, visible without opening the spreadsheet [29:52].
Vendors with sync locks on see where their handles end because the plate sub clip ends there [30:23].
Cut the markers onto an empty layer, not over other shots, and do it last, after the turnover is actually sent, because the colour is the record that it was done [31:24, 32:25].
Version 0 exists to test that frames, format and handles are right before real work [49:08].

**Use it:** Mirror shot status as colours in review timelines, and require a version-zero conform check from each vendor or render stage before real work starts.
