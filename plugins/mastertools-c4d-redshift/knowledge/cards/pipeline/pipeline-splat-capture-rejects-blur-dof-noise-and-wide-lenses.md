Not tolerated [02:46]: motion blur, depth of field, noise (low light), and wide lenses because of radial distortion; use 35 mm or longer. Aim for a small aperture, high shutter speed, low ISO, which needs a lot of consistent light [03:27]. The presenter (an Octane/Houdini VFX artist) found high-speed-sync strobes bounced off white walls (three flashes, flattest light) work better than video lights, and he shoots RAW stills like a fast video [03:58-04:30]. Outdoors pick overcast, skip ND filters [04:35-05:02]. A tiny action-cam is not sharp enough for macro detail [05:32].
Move the body, not just the camera angle: parallax, not look-around; keep moving, since stops confuse training [05:32-06:03]. Nothing may change in the scene (people, TV, lights) and the subject must be completely still [09:40-10:42].

**Use it:** the capture checklist for scans; transfer to our work is limited to scan-based set pieces.
