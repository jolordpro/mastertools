Short Avid clip with three editors (speakers assigned from the chapter list; captions do not name them): Lee Smith screens his film in a theatre once a week to remember its scale, pace and rhythm; he also watches out of the edit room so he stops reaching for the keyboard to trim [00:05-01:08]. Jon Gregory says the film should be seen on a screen after each cut [01:08-01:37]. Paul Machliss says cloud dailies let everyone watch alone anywhere, which loses the shared sense that a take was good or needs a redo, and calls it depersonalised [01:41].
Also: when someone else joins the cut, you start seeing the film through them [00:06-00:34].
Transfers: broadcast and motion reviews gain from a shared screen at final size; a laptop review hides pace and scale problems.
