Audrey Havey [01:58-04:00]. Producer or project manager: budget, client and artist expectations, schedule, present in client meetings on revisions and concepts. Creative director: writes the script with the client, sets narrative and tone, checks vision fits budget and timeline, walks the client through concepts. Art director: look and feel, keeps direction inside budget and scope, quality and consistency, walks through art-related revisions. Technical director (not always present): for complex 3D or VFX, avoids broken pipelines and crashes. Designers: style frames, characters, assets. Animators: follow the script, VO and animatic. Sound designer: part of VO and mixes to the end. Client feedback has deadlines too, to protect the schedule.

**Use it:** Roles list for scoping a job and for the coach to explain who to ask for what.
