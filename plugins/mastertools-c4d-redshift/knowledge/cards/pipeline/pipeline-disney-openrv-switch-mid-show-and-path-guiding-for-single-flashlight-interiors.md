They switched to OpenRV for artist reviews during Zootopia 2; when review problems arose they could dig into the source and their own customisations, and pushed fixes back [11:20-11:55].
Path guiding (OpenPGL) was used in the hardest lighting and volumetrics, such as interiors lit entirely by a single flashlight; what the rendering team expected to be extraordinarily difficult was described as relatively easy; OpenPGL joined the Academy Software Foundation [11:55-12:26].
