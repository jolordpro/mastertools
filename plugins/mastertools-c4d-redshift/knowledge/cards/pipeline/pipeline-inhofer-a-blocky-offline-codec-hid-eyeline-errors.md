On early Avid (AVR3 codec, 1992) the compression was so blocky that editors could not see the pupils, so eyelines that were wrong only showed at the online finish from tape, and alternate takes had to be chosen [16:50-17:21].
Lesson: judge things that depend on fine detail (eyelines, edge quality, fine texture) at finishing quality, not in a proxy.
Transfer: draft renders at 640 can hide edge, noise and fine-detail problems; do a final-size check on detail-critical frames before sign-off.

**Use it:** The checks require a full-size look at eyes, edges and text before a frame is approved from a draft.
