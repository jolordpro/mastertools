Besides the VR tools, Legato says the other advance was the people: because the same group had done The Jungle Book, the animators were much better at mimicking animal behaviour and finesse, and the rendering and grooming skills had matured too [01:35-02:06].
He says it would not have advanced as much if a different group had made the next film [02:06].

**Use it:** Keep a proven crew together across consecutive jobs; the learning stays in people, not in files.
