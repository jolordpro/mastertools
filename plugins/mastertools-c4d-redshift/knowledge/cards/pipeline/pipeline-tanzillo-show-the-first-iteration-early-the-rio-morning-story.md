Mike Tanzillo 55eY1-LlY3k [31:42-32:44]: he is the opposite of people who hold work until ready. On Rio he nailed a plane-and-birds shot to the colour key as sunset or evening, then the art director asked if it had been changed to morning. He was crushed and decided never again to wait that long.
His lesson: it does not matter how well something is nailed if it is wrong. Showing early means nobody is married to it.

**Use it:** Show a rough lit frame against the colour key before polishing, especially time of day.
