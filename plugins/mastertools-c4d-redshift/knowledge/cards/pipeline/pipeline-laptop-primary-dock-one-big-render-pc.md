Chad (guest) moved to a laptop as his main machine because he wanted to act on an idea anywhere. Speed is comparable to his old 4090 PC, and he says it is not much of a sacrifice [04:48, 07:56].
Two cables run to a Thunderbolt dock with wired Ethernet and two monitors; a matching dock, keyboard and mouse sit at home, so the setup is the same in both places [10:36-11:39]. A small adhesive pocket on the lid holds a pocket SSD for extra 4 TB [14:15].

He also says Mac screen-capture tools make content faster to produce [08:28].
