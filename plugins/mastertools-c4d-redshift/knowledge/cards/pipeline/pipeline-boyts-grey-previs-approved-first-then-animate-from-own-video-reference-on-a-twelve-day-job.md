Job one: a trailer for a small game studio with about 12 days [09:34-10:06]. Order: ask for their storyboards and logo-first idea, get a rough character design from them and elevate it (first version rejected as not organic enough), then a grey previs with simple shapes and camera cuts, done in about a day and approved after a couple of revisions [08:32-10:37]. He explains grey means previs, and after approval the client said move forward [10:06-10:37]. Character animation is then keyed from video he films of himself, overlaid on the final to check eye darts and timing [10:37-11:08].
Job two: a one-minute ad in 3 weeks with his wife splitting design and music from build, previs timed to the music first, then environments (landscape object, tileable grass, a recycled metal-flake water material with Fresnel for edge colour), then a second animatic [23:16-30:13]. The entry won the contest prize.

**Use it:** Quote this order when budgeting a tight job: previs approval is the gate that stops rework.
