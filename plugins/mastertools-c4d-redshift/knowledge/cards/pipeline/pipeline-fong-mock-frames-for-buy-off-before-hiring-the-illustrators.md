Order she describes: (1) concepting, with the team convincing itself first; (2) mock a couple of frames from collaged references, working out palette and ideas as you go, even if the art never reaches the final [58:15-58:46]; (3) take showrunner input on which scenes and characters matter [59:18]; (4) rough sequence with the editor, crude frames set to music, to test pace [1:00:21]; (5) once a few good frames get a yes, hire illustrators and keep the work "wet clay" through adjustments [1:00:53]. Illustrators come first because animation cannot start before the drawings exist [57:14-57:44]. Decisions to settle on the drawings: do the gods look like the actors or an idea of them, and how literal [1:01:23].

**Use it:** A cheap two-to-three-frame pitch gate before booking expensive artists. In scheduling, put design/illustration ahead of animation in the dependency order.
