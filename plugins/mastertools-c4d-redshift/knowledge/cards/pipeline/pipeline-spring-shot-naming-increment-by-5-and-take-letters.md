Scenes got individual names prefixed with a number (eleven scenes including credits) [21:11] [21:44]. Shot numbers climbed by 5 so a shot can be inserted later; on a bigger show he would step by 10 or 100 [21:44] [22:15]. A trailing letter marks alternate takes [22:15]. Blend files get a dot suffix naming the task, for example shot name plus anim [22:15] [23:18]. Actions are prefixed with shot and character [27:01].
Spring had 102 shots [11:45].

**Use it:** A check can verify shot-number gaps of 5 and the task suffix. Recommend a step of 10 when the count of later additions is unknown.
