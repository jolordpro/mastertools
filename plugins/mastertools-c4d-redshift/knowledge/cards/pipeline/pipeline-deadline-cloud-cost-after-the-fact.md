The budget manager and usage explorer report spend; they leave out idle time, storage and customer-owned licences. The Wait & Save option (Jan 2026 session) lowers cost if the user can wait, which is the same trade as a quality-versus-cost dial.

**Use it:** show a price before submit and offer a slower, cheaper lane; time is the currency.
Transcript support: speaker also notes the budget dashboard shows estimates and elapsed time; fine-grained reporting is in AWS Cost Explorer or CloudWatch (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PjafAJ0-6SA [18:49])
