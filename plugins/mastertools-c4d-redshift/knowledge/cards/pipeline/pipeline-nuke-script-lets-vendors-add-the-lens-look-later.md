Chris Maclean [07:55-08:57]: the team agreed a visual language early (some scenes shot with special lenses). When a director forgot to use those lenses, or the effect needed to be stronger, they built a Nuke script they could hand to vendors to put on top of those shots. The language is mapped across seasons, not just one, so setups pay off later [09:27].

**Use it:** A reusable idea: when a look must be consistent, package it as one shareable preset or comp template that anyone on the job can apply.
