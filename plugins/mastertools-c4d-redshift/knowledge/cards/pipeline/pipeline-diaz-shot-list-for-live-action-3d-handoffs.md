On the Microsoft film there were many hand-offs between live action and 3D, so she storyboarded and wrote a list of shots needed, for example a mouse click and typing on a keyboard [04:54-05:26].
On the day she ticked them off. It saves a reshoot when a 3D beat needs a specific live moment.

**Use it:** for hybrid jobs, derive the shoot checklist from the board, listing every beat 3D depends on.
