On Batman Begins the animatics of the fight inside the monorail chase were cut under one second per shot because they were clear and cartoon-like; Franklin warned lit versions with steam jets and lights would confuse, and it happened: the rendered shots then needed 14 frames more at head and 14 at tail, which hit the effects simulation pipeline because frame counts were baked in [40:11-41:45]. Since then he gives early animatics with generous handles, 50 or 100 frames if affordable, and notes that final rendering cannot afford unused frames [41:45-42:48]. Real-time engines like Unreal now let previs show how complex an image will be early [42:48-43:20].

**Use it:** Cut animatics with longer handles and add shading, light and effects clutter early enough to test readability at real cut length.
