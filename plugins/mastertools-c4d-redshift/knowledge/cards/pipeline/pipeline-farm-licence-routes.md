Route 1: metered licences via Deadline Cloud or Conductor, no Maxon talks, margin sits under the platform. Route 2: own nodes and a Maxon farm deal (what Drop & Render, SRF and RebusFarm claim). Route 3: customer licences on rented GPUs; Maxon's per-node rule means the customer needs enough node seats. This is our reading; no Maxon term sheet was found.

**Use it:** first action is an email to Maxon partner sales asking about terms, sublicensing, per-node billing and BYOL.
