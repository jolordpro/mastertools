He stumbled into stacking timelines on top of timelines so each works as a real-time bin: scrubbing a timeline switches the screen to it. It makes takes easier to find, lets him try edits without hopping between projects, and he can send shots to After Effects to blend two takes or improve the timing of a performance. He also uses Asana for goal scheduling because time management has long been a struggle. [§3]

**Use it:** Keep alternative takes on stacked timelines and comp the best parts together.
