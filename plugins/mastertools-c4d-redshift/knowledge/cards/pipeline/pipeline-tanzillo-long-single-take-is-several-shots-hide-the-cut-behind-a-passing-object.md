Tanzillo, speculating from a film clip [00:37-05:45]: a "long shot" is usually two or three shots joined at an opportunity: a phone or person right in front of lens, a column, another character passing, or a big camera move that blurs everything. A photograph seen through a camera in shot can be a third render comped in.
Why split: smaller chunks for several lighters and compositors to work at once; a single huge shot feels heavy in a pipeline built around A then B then C; each shot can be optimised (remove crowd characters so files stay small). He mentions a game studio animator given a 10,000-frame shot, "six minutes of footage", and says he cannot picture how that works [05:15-05:45].

**Use it:** when planning a long continuous move in C4D, plan the split points and the blocker for each before building.
