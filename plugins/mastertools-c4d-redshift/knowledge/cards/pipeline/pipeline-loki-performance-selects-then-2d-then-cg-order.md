Overall VFX supervisor Chris Townsend (Loki season 2) on 50-60 one-off time-slip shots [17:48].
Order: actor gives 10-15 passes per setup; editorial cuts several performances together for timing; directors approve; vendor (Framestore) gets all takes plus the editor's picks, makes a simple 2D cut and returns it for editorial sign-off [18:19, 18:50].
Only then do tracking, digital double and simulation (taffy-like strands) begin; an interim 3D assembly is shown as FYI; lighting, render and comp follow [19:20, 19:52].
Directors review a clean comp; kickbacks sent shots back to step one but were uncommon [20:22].
Last, hand-paint individual frames for a drooping jaw or popping eyeball [20:53].
Reason: expensive CG waits until timing and performance are signed off.

**Use it:** Order any expensive-shot pipeline as: performance/timing pick, cheap 2D version approved, then CG build, then frame-level polish.
