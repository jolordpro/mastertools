She describes the meetings as almost like school: you put up your work and the group says "this is great, but did we want gradients that strongly?" or raises a client note, for example a concern about how Medusa looked and whether her eye could change [1:03:58-1:04:29]. The point is to keep everyone talking and the look on track. Working with a team also yields surprises beyond the plan.

**Use it:** Review ritual to copy: short daily group look-check with each artist's frame on screen, questions about look decisions, client notes read out and assigned.
Transcript support: editors say solo, cloud dailies viewing loses the group reaction; weekly theatre screenings keep scale and pace (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cVcdkjgysng [01:41])
