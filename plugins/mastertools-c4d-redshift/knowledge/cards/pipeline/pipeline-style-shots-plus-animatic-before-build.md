Bas Helmig and Bart Brinkman (Italian Giants, Dutch 3D studio; Maxon 3D and Motion Design Show): after a storyboard and feasibility talk, they build style shots and an animatic, because clients often cannot read an animation brief [36:25-36:55].
It makes later feedback easier and nobody does double work; it also times music to the shots and lets the team see motion in real time when the viewport is slow, e.g. 15 fps [36:55-37:28].
It exposes how little screen time a shot gets: a favourite shot may be on for two seconds, so a day on it is waste [37:28-38:03].

**Use it:** Planning check: assign effort per shot from on-screen duration seen in the animatic.
Transcript support: Hamrick times stills to scratch VO and music in Premiere before animating and swaps in animated shots as they land, to show the client progress (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bnFRXVbi0ZA [06:23])
Transcript support: Boyts gets a grey previs approved first, in about a day with a couple of revisions, before detail (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=27b5cFk-Pjk [10:06])
