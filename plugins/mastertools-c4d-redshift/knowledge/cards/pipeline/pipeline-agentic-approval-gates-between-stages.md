Cyrus Rahgoshay (Autodesk, ex-Ubisoft; paper at DigiPro 2026) described: brief to asset casting, concept to asset discovery, asset list to scene layout, with approval between every stage [2:05:12]. Scope was fixed up front: rapid prototyping and pre-production, placeholders replaced later, not ship content [2:05:44].
Stage 1: meeting transcript becomes a structured brief (environment, period, mood, prop density), a ComfyUI Flux storyboard and a mood board; the VFX supervisor edits or swaps any step [2:06:15-2:08:18]. Results he states: first casting draft from 8-10 days to under 30 minutes; asset search 2-3 days to minutes; rough scene population 4 hours to seconds [2:16:43-2:17:14]. Gain was "iteration bandwidth": one concept a week became several directions a day [2:08:51].
Adoption advice: start from a real production problem, serve each role differently (supervisor autonomy, artist discovery by creative intent, manager decisions) [2:19:50-2:25:32]; automate your worst 30 minutes of clicking first, one small MCP at a time [2:33:21-2:35:26].

**Use it:** Frame any platform automation as removing mechanical setup while keeping an artist review gate.
