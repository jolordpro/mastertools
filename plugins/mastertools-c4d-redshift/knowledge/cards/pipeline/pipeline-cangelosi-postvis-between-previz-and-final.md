Cangelosi's Rome company was approached through a contact from Centropolis; it had no pipeline for this, so he and a friend built the workflow and pipeline from zero and started with previz [17:40, 18:12, 18:43]. The studio then moved to post-vis for the final alien-ship attack and later did some final shots [18:43, 19:14]. He says the director wanted post-vis with high rendering quality, between previz and final, which made scenes heavy [33:16]. They also produced the studio logo for the anniversary [19:46].

**Use it:** Gives the stage order previz, post-vis, final, and a tell for budgeting: post-vis at final-adjacent quality can cost nearly as much as a shot, so quote it as its own stage.
