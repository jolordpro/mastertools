On a spec commercial, the team found the exact piece of reference they had searched for over weeks when the shot was about 80% done [56:08].
It differed enough that they had to re-render, re-matte-paint and relight with new shaders, which put them back near 25% [56:41].
He phoned the director on sending the new image, said the work would not finish on the original date, that quality would improve visibly within about a week and need a couple more weeks after that [57:45].
Because the spot was a studio's own spec job, the failure was theirs to own, so they took the harder fix [57:13].
Transfers: lock reference before look build; a late better reference is a re-plan conversation, not a quiet slip.

**Use it:** Add a reference-locked gate before look-dev starts, and when a late change forces a rebuild, state the new date and the visible-progress milestone at once.
