Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour): the cinematographer's mockups of camera positions and lenses went into an Unreal environment built from a matchimated Hamilton lap at Silverstone, with other cars added [07:17-08:19].
They checked how each lens frames the straight and a battle with another car, since wide lenses distort edges [08:19-08:50].
Then positions went to Mercedes AMG to engineer safe mounts, batteries and RF gear for cars at 150-180 mph [08:50-09:21].

**Use it:** Pre-production habit: validate camera choices in a cheap real-time proxy before expensive hardware or shoots.
