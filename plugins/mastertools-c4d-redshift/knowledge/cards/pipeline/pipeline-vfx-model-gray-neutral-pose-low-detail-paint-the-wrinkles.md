Modelers build gray models with as much detail as needed and no more; wrinkles and scales are painted later by the asset team, because modeled-in detail makes the model too heavy [29:04, 29:37]. The creature is posed with tail straight, wings flat and legs spread so parts sit on straight lines, which makes adding bones for rigging easier [31:10]. A generic six-foot man is placed beside every creature as a scale check [28:33]. Animators work on a light proxy with gaps and holes, and render swaps in the finished model and textures [40:03]. Wrong tests from home: too many polygons and bad UVs make a model unusable at a studio [1:18:00].

**Use it:** For logo and trophy builds, keep a clean neutral pose and a scale reference, push surface detail into maps, and animate on a proxy.
