With 30 to 40 setups in a scene and a dialogue show told in cuts, he diagrammed camera positions himself [51:57-54:04]. If a 40 mm over-the-shoulder is shot on one side, the matching 40 may not be shot for two days, so he tracked lens and position [53:01-53:33]. Diagrams were drawn during blocking rehearsal, or built up gradually with directors who worked differently [53:33].

**Use it:** Keep a per-sequence camera log (lens, position, height) so reverses and later shots in a sequence match.
