Booth (Super Motion Collective, ex-MPC virtual production) [06:26-10:04]: real-time shortens the wait between decisions but he argues the gain drops off in groups, and long gaps between client views create anxiety. His team pushed everything into Unreal, then struggled to get assets back to other DCCs; clients wanted realistic Houdini water and a Blender look for part of a film, so work split across two engines.
That job made him decide the next project would use USD.

**Use it:** when a client prefers a look from another app, plan a neutral exchange layer first.
