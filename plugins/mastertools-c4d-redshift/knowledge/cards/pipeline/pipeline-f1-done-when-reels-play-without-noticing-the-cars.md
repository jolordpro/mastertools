Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour) says the goal was that viewers just believe the races are happening, not that effects stand out [47:42-48:13].
He knew they were there when he watched reels and stopped thinking about the cars in the footage, forgetting what was done [48:13].
They accepted tiny misses a super fan might catch because they knew when they were letting something go [40:28].

**Use it:** Final-pass test: watch the cut for story, and if you still notice the CG, keep working.
