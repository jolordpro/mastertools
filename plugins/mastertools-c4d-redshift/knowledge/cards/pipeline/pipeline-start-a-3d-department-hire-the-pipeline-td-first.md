Ross Denner [34:45-37:51]: the first version was one artist, Maya and V-Ray, with referenced files of referenced files, and he asked himself how to render 20 shots alone. The owner invested and listened, a good TD joined, and the TD built a pipeline spanning Nuke, Houdini and Maya in about six months (the speaker corrects himself from six weeks) [35:47].
His rule on joining any studio: make friends with the TDs and engineers first (he jokes about donuts), since they get a million renders out for you [36:19].
Department size: three or four, aiming for five or six, with freelancers filling seats; the balance is turnaround, money and not killing everybody [37:19-37:51].

**Use it:** Cheap reminder when sizing a pipeline: a TD hired early saves more than an extra artist.
