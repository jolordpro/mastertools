Storyboards were post-it notes on the wall because storyboard tools were still new and got in the way; the team scanned them in batches, cropped them and dropped them into the sequencer to make the first animatic [12:19] [12:47] [13:18]. That edit file lasted the whole production, changing as shots changed [13:18] [13:51].
His reason: even in the simplest form, a version of the whole film at all times helps everyone get their mind around a complicated thing [13:51]. He adds that "pipeline" suggests a linear flow but work overlaps and the pipeline is there for communication [02:49].

**Use it:** Teaching and review rule: keep a living cut or animatic from day one and hang all later steps (previs, renders) on it.
