Alexander Williams (animation veteran, says he worked on Lion King and Roger Rabbit; now teaches) lists the opening of a dailies slot: name yourself, say whether it is a first pass, work in progress or asking final approval, and say what is missing (e.g. only one of two characters is done) before the director spots it [01:48-02:20]. Acknowledge each note aloud rather than sitting silent; if you disagree say what was agreed before [02:20-02:52]. Speak up even when remote with camera and mic off being passive is the trap [01:16].
Use screen left and screen right, shot numbers or shot-type names, never "this shot over there" [02:52-03:23]. A frame counter burned into the playblast lets everyone say "frame 49" [07:29-08:00].

**Use it:** A dailies template for the platform's review step: status line, what is missing, frame counter on.
