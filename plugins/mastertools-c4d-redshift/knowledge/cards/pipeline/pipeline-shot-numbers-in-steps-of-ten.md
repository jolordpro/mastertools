Speaker is the TD Academy host (surname garbled in captions). If shots run 1, 2, 3 and an extra shot is added you end up with 2a or 95. Steps of ten leave room: a new shot between 10 and 20 is 15, or 5 before 10 [10:28-11:32]. Inside each shot, department folders (animation, FX, lighting, compositing) each with work and publish.

**Use it:** Apply to board numbers and shot IDs in sports packages and open sequences.
