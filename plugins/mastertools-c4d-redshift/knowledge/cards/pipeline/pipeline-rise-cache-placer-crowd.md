Rise filled the harbor with agents pre-cached once, about 1000 frames including Vellum cloth, then scattered in Solaris [30:37-31:09]. It is more art directable and avoids a cloth sim per shot and clip landing [31:09].
About six agents with two variants each, randomised costumes and animation, gave a varied crowd [31:40]. One base crowd was pushed to all shots through a principal shot, with shot switches to add or remove agents; brightness per agent set via colour-variation [32:12].
They found this used far more often than a full crowd sim, except for large battles [32:42].

**Use it:** Estimator: prefer pre-cached looped crowds for background fans; reserve simulation for hero crowd.
