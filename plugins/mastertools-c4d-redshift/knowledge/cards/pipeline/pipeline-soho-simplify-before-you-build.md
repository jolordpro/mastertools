Razon's goal is the least resource for high-quality work, and getting the shot done on time [10:10].
Overthinking leads to a complicated setup that is hard to adjust, and may not be what the client wants; stop and ask whether to keep going down that road [10:10].
Assess the problem with the team and pick the most efficient solution [10:41]. Some work has no shortcut: water still needs a long simulation [11:14].
He says 70-80% of Soho's work is invisible effects [12:17].
Pattern: a Winterfell set built only partway, extended with matte painting plus procedural geometry rather than unique models [15:25].

**Use it:** In planning, ask for the simplest build that meets the approved ask before adding tools or setup layers; flag shots whose setup is hard to re-time.
