Cole [27:36-28:07]: Avatar's team designed all the films early and works on 4 and 5 by revisiting earlier work, not starting from scratch. He finds it frustrating when "that is an old concept" is treated as a flaw; if it was working and has not been used, make it work now.
Fire and Ash features paintings (wind traders, ash people) made about 10-11 years earlier [28:38].
Designing just to design is pointless; the touchstone is always the script and the story, and how best to serve it [37:27].
Cole also notes the early cadence: a lot of art up front, then late work is marketing art and last-minute vfx, where new design is "a problem" [29:10].
