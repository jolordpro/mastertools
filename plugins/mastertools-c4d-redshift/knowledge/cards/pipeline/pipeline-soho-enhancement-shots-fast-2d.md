Game of Thrones enhancements: added blood to swords, smoke to battles, filled a sparse set tree; mostly 2D with few CG elements and shorter turnaround [22:41, 25:47].
Cloning leaves fill looking like clones past a point, so Soho mixed in painted leaves [26:18].
Blood amount had to stay under a ratings limit; early versions were too creative (hose-like) and were dialled down [23:43].
A rubber sword is awkward: track, remove and recreate it in 2D or 3D [23:12]. Ember effects and wire removal often done fully in Nuke with a 3D track [1:17:49].
Practical elements in the plate make matching easier, but you must work with the chosen take [27:49].

**Use it:** For add-on effects, budget by stage: track, paint/clone, then match to the plate; pre-agree a ceiling on intensity (blood, smoke) before the first version.
