Goralczyk says developers were focused on Blender 2.8, so the film side was a side job and the pipeline ended up "manual, grungy, down to earth" [05:27] [05:57]. Everyone covered many tasks; he directed and also did lighting, environment modeling, simulation, hair, shot assembly (which ate most of his time) and rig fixes [00:41] [01:13].
The studio moved the film to 2.8 half way and had to port its workflow while the software was in development [03:53].

**Use it:** Failure-story reference: expect a manual pipeline when nobody owns tooling. Budget for one TD or accept slower hand-offs.
