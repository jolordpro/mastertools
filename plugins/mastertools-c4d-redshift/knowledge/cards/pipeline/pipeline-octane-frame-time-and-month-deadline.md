Bas Helmig and Bart Brinkman (Italian Giants, Dutch 3D studio; Maxon 3D and Motion Design Show) produce long product animations of three to four minutes that must be done in a month, sometimes less [28:40-29:04].
Frame time on one system was around a minute and on another about half a minute, so 3,000-4,000 frames were manageable; render time is kept low deliberately [29:04-29:37].
They also printed a coral still at 14,000 by 10,000 pixels (about 140 megapixels) within VRAM limits [44:36-45:10, 50:24-50:55].

**Use it:** Estimator sanity case: studio-scale GPU job with minute-per-frame and four-figure frame counts.
