To get a more immersive realism, as if a real cinema camera filmed the characters, the team re-studied rendering with the new Hyperion path-based system. From that decision they had 14 months. Jacomini says the city was re-imagined with huge data complexity, comparable to Frozen and Rapunzel together, and the new tech simplified rendering, giving artists more time. The directors asked for a tangible world that looks as if it exists. [§2]

**Use it:** Use as a benchmark: a renderer switch at feature scale is a roughly one-year decision.
