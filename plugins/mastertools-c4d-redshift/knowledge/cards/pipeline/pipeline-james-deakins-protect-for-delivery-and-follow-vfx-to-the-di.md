James Deakins describes her role [01:41-03:13]. In prep she takes technical meetings, especially with vfx, who want blue screen when Roger does not, and offers alternatives. On set she watches the monitor as another set of eyes and thinks about the next scene. After shooting she handles the lab and daily workflow, and makes sure they know what they are protecting for: shooting IMAX means protecting a wider frame.
She follows the work through to the DI, tracking which vfx are done or still coming back so nobody worries about them.
IMAX release meant a new transfer and DCP [1:32:16].
Transfer: confirm the safe/protected area for each deliverable before lighting and framing, and track open comps against the final grade.

**Use it:** Add a 'protect for' line (aspect, safe area, delivery) to the pre-render checklist.
