Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour): an F1 car does not look the same race to race: signage, liveries and helmets change [21:45-22:46].
If one thing was under-planned it was the number of asset variants; each CG driver had to wear the right helmet and car the right livery for that race, which became a whole tracking process [22:16-22:46].
Their own car had CAD data from being built for real, plus scans [22:46].
Other teams' cars could not be scanned, so the team shot photos from the pit lane and grid like press [21:14-21:45].

**Use it:** Asset plan: list variant dimensions (sponsor, season, colour) up front and budget for each.
