Brady says AMGI sets up an internal competition: one team makes the version as if rendered for a high-end feature, sometimes two hours per frame, and the Unreal team tries to match it [34:55]. He was fooled a couple of times into thinking an Unreal frame was a V-Ray render; an artist told him the whole scene, about a minute of animation, took six seconds to render [35:25]. Playback is now faster than the time to write frames to a folder [35:56]. Company promotion in a panel setting, not a test, and the quality gap on hair was real early on [34:24].
