Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour): final count 2,500 shots [10:54-11:26]; 4K final delivery from 6K or 8K capture for wiggle room [41:16-41:31].
A VFX editor kept track of continuity such as tyre colours; they let a few small things go knowing which ones, noting they were for the super fans [39:57-40:58].
ILM owned the Daytona sequence and flashbacks, giving a clean break and different vibe; for Daytona they put the lead's face in the reflection of a rear-view mirror [42:03-42:33, 44:35-45:39].

**Use it:** Scale reference for estimates: 2,500 shots with two large vendors; plan a continuity tracker.
