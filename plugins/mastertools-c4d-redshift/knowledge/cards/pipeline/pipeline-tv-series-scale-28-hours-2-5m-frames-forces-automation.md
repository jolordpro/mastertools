Cube Creative (Tony Weyland, CG supervisor) [09:43-11:22]. Three series of 52 x 11 min each total just over 28 hours of footage and roughly 2.5 million frames to render. Budgets and schedules are tight, so render times must stay very low and everything possible is automated so artists focus on creative work, not technical details. They locked to Blender 2.7 for the whole production because a mid-production version switch was unaffordable, despite wanting 2.8 features.

**Use it:** Reference scale for episodic cost thinking: multiply per-frame render time by the real frame count before committing to a look. Lock the tool version for the length of a production.
