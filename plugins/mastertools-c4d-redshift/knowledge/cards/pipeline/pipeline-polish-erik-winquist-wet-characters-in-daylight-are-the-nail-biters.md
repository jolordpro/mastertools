Erik Winquist (VFX supervisor, Kingdom of the Planet of the Apes) names the riskiest work: a river sequence and a flood where complex water simulation meets wet apes, all in daylight, plus a human hunt in tall grass with splashing in a river, apes riding CG zebras, and live stunt photography to integrate [03:53-04:55]. Performance capture ran on location in Australia rather than on a stage [01:48-02:19]. The director pitched sequences animatedly and the director and DP had a shorthand, giving cohesive blocking where information is revealed just in time [02:51-03:53].
**Use it:** When bidding, flag shots where fluid, hair/fur and hero characters combine in plain light as high-risk and give them extra iterations.
