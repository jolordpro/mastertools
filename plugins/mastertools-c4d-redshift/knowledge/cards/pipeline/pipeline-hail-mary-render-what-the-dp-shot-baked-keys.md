For exterior space shots Michael sent the VFX vendor hard-baked keys for camera, lens and lighting, with the stated aim to render exactly what Greig Fraser had shot [51:21].
Adjustments came only after viewing in the edit, and were minimal [51:51].
Before this, shots were lensed by the DP himself in a virtual camera on joysticks, with the sun on per-axis dials and real distances, so the real set was a realistic base to art direct from [28:03].
Transfers: a locked layout camera and light rig from approval should reach final render as baked data, not recreated.

**Use it:** At lock, export camera, lens and light values to the render stage as data and treat later changes as a labelled revision.
