The studio first put out many interpretations (the studio was excited about the show) and the showrunners chose the fungus-that-looks-like-things idea [11:30-12:02]. Roughly four or five months went into back-and-forth on approach, camera and pace before execution [12:02-13:03]. The second guest says the concept locked quickly; then months were spent mapping out the moments to include before production [12:32-13:03]. They never saw episodes, only publicity stills of the fungal makeup on actors, used for colour palette [13:34]. An earlier idea of Americana moments and a journey was more singular and then became one evolving growth [13:34-14:37].

**Use it:** Estimator note: budget the concept/iteration phase separately; it can be longer than production. Pairs with the "concepting buy-off" cards.
