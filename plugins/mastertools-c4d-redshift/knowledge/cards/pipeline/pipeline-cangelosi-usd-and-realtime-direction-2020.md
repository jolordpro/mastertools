In 2004 his team pitched a modular 3D package with a GPU renderer and a standard scene-description format to Mental Images; it never got funded, but he says that format idea is what USD became [37:28, 38:28]. He notes Houdini (Solaris), Clarisse, Maya and Max adding USD [38:59]. Real-time used to be previz-only, but he expects it for backgrounds and environments, with offline and real-time combined [39:29]. The host (a Chaos staff member) adds that people still swap OBJ and FBX and that Alembic was not enough [40:30, 41:02]. Opinions, not tests.
