Big VFX jobs could not go remote before 2020 because of security. By the pandemic, large companies moved the data onto their own secure workstations so artists can sit anywhere with a connection and the skills [54:30, 55:32]. Host Chaos staff add that remote desktop also frees artists from buying hardware and software [56:34]. Cangelosi, as a freelancer, usually works on three workstations at once: one simulating, one rendering and one for building the scene, which is a big cost [56:34]. He had worked remotely from Rome for about 13 years and says time zones with clients in the US and Singapore are the hard part [21:50, 43:36].

**Use it:** Frames the value of cloud or farm render: a studio of one needs to offload sim and render to stay productive. Quote when explaining why render machines are part of cost.
