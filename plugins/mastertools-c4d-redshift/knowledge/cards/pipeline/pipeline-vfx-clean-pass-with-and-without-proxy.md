Fallout S2 VFX supervisor Jay Worth: for the power armor vs Deathclaw fights the crew shot a stunt puppet and a bodysuit stand-in, choreographed all the collisions with them, then repeated the action as a clean pass [06:15-07:49]. The rule: add a creature or add the armor, never both. Editors pick per take whether the practical clang or the clean air swing sells the beat. The wider ethos is never a full CG build; always keep something real in frame, then ask what must be added [23:27]. Practical wire-fired armor and mortars were kept as real [08:21].

**Use it:** when comping CG into footage or video, plan for one hero CG element per shot and keep a clean plate version. Transfers to composited sports graphics over live footage.
