On Independence Day: Resurgence his team ran 3ds Max, which he calls hard to push because parts of its code are 15-20 years old; other vendors on the film used Maya [30:42, 31:12, 31:43]. The alien queen ship brought huge textures and displacement, and one post-vis scene flew to a dead planet with a mega-city bigger than the queen's ship [32:14, 33:16, 33:47]. Max made external references and large scenes hard, so he moved effects to Houdini and look-dev to Clarisse, which now holds most of his projects [34:19, 34:51]. He still loves Max and moved for speed, since he cannot work 15-20 hour days [34:51].

**Use it:** When a scene gets heavy, shift the heavy parts to instancing or referenced proxies and keep the DCC for what it does best; same principle for C4D scenes with many instances.
