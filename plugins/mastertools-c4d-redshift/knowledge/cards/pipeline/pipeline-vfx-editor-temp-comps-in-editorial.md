Richard Sanchez: postviz is the editor's quick temp comp done after the shoot; previz is crude 3D made before it to plan shots [12:40-14:22]. Temps rarely reach final in film (needs EXR plus mattes, colour range), but can in TV on tight deadlines [02:17-02:51]. He prefers building temps in Avid because revising in After Effects is slow under deadline, yet says not to be locked to one tool, and accepts paid plug-ins when they get the job done [19:57-21:37].

**Use it:** for fast client previews, keep rough versions in the quickest tool and flag them temp.
