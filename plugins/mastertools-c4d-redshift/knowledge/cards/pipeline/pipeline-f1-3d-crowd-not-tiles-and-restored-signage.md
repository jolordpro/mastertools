Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour): race weekends are only three days but filming at a track lasted weeks, with empty or removed grandstands, so a huge share of work restored crowds, signage and atmosphere [31:07-32:09].
They used a full 3D crowd system instead of plates or tiles except for lock-offs, because light changes and the camera moves in compound ways [32:09-32:40].
The array's capture documented sponsors so signage could be put back faithfully [31:07].

**Use it:** Venue-dressing rule: crowds move with light and perspective, so choose 3D unless locked off.
