Booth [10:35-15:51]: creative work goes back and forth; USD lets one person go heads-down on an asset and finish it while others use the previous state, a jazz-quartet model rather than all editing at once. USD is just files and folders, no big server. They use Anchorpoint for version control, which he says is far cheaper than Perforce and easy to reach. Nvidia Omniverse live collaboration is not what they want most of the time [15:18]; live multi-user Unreal sessions or VR are used for specific problem-solving.

**Use it:** for small C4D teams, the aim is parallel ownership of files, not live co-editing.
