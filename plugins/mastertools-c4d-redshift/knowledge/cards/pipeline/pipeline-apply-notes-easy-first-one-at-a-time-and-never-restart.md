Williams' order at the desk [08:00-09:02]: number the notes, do the easy ones first (the hard one often becomes easier or disappears), change one thing, playblast, keep it only if better, then move to the next; changing everything at once often forces a restart. Never throw the shot away and start from blank [09:02].
Be a problem solver: bring a fix with the problem; his example is Roger Rabbit's animator putting the rabbit on tiptoe to fix an eyeline [09:34-10:38].
Help rules: Sony's 15-minute rule, solve it alone for 15 minutes then ask [13:46]; for a rejection loop get a trusted fresh pair of eyes [11:41]. Be positive; morale drives output [10:38-11:09]. Show early and with confidence; the director's trust makes approval likelier [13:15].

**Use it:** Revision tickets in the platform can follow this order, with a 15-minute stuck timer prompt.
