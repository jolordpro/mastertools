Epic Games panel with fxguide (Mike Seymour), Sam Nicholson (Stargate Studios), Felix Jorge (Happy Mushroom), Matt Madden (Epic). Nicholson [06:10-07:44]: what you see is what you get on set. If the director knows what they want, there was time to prepare materials, and tests prove it, it is fantastic (their Akira test). If the studio is not behind it, or the director may change things, green screen keeps decisions open. Money: a green rag is cheap to put up and the cost goes to post; a 100-foot LED wall plus its previs tools costs a lot in pre-production, and producers balk at spending big on set and again in post. His advice: use the wall for previs, pre-comps or finished material on set, but do not throw away your post budget. Madden adds that some shows use close-but-not-final assets because believable light on the actors is worth it [05:39].

**Use it:** For virtual-production quotes, split the budget as prep plus on-set plus a retained post line. Panel is vendor-sponsored (Epic), so read it as a pro's view with a vested audience.
