His view: stay in close contact with the DIT and dailies person, review dailies each morning and give comments, not just receive the project at the end [45:05-45:50]. He says a colorist (Peter Doyle, Warner Brothers) worked this way and could grade a movie in three days when everything and the VFX had been managed from the start; no panic about what the look is [45:50-46:52].
He admits it is not always possible [46:52].

**Use it:** Ask for the grade pipeline from prep on big jobs: one owner for look, dailies, and VFX colour.
