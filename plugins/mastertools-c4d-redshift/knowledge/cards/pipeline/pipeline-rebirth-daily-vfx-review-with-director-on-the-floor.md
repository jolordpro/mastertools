Edwards says nine months of post in Soho London had editorial on one floor and VFX above it. He would cut with the editor, go up to review shots with Vickery's department, and return to the edit, for roughly six hours a day with VFX [04:13, 04:45]. When he could not crack a visual problem, he let Vickery try it in the review, which cost him no strike of his own [22:17, 22:47]. Vickery says there was no shouting or finger pointing when shots failed, and ILM artists came with 'try anything' attitudes [22:47, 23:18]. Edwards said working this closely was unusual and they felt like they were making a film with friends [05:16].

**Use it:** Review ritual to copy: short loops between edit and VFX, the director in the room, and a second pair of eyes allowed to try the fix. Fits the review step of the pipeline.
