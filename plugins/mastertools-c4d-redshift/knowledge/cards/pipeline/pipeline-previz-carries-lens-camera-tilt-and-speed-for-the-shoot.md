Thomas shows previz of a dragon fly-by with the lens in the corner (75 mm), subject speed, camera tilt (about 2 to 3 degrees down) and height along the bottom [12:33, 13:03]. If the client likes the shot, the physical shoot is planned from that lens, and the real actor does not need to move at dragon speed, only match relative speed to the dragon's back. The later Maya camera for the match-move uses the same 75 mm lens [18:14]. Lens is key data for the solve.

**Use it:** Burn lens, focal length, camera height and tilt into every previz or animatic render so the final C4D camera and any plate shoot start from the same numbers.
