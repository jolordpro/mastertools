The unnamed guest says that after the film is thought done, the colour timers (who grade the whole film so sequences flow) sometimes ask for a matte of one element across many shots, sent out to the grading facility; lighters never learn what they do with it [45:15-45:46]. Shot-to-shot balancing is the lighter's job; global balance is the colourist's.
Transfers: keep AOV/cryptomatte or object-ID passes renderable after delivery, so a late matte request is a re-render of one pass.

**Use it:** Plan the render budget with an allowance for late single-element matte re-renders.
