After the 10-minute rig, he duplicates the camera for each new frame, hides the old camera (which also hides its camera-parented backdrop), frames a new angle, takes a snapshot, and repeats [11:14-14:24]. For close-ups he turns on depth of field and sets a high aperture value (he used 55 because the scene is small) [12:20], rotates or moves the backdrop so frames do not look identical [12:51], and lowers the backlight if a close-up blows out [13:54].

**Use it:** suits a style-frame stage of the pitch process: lock the rig, then vary cameras and backdrop, one snapshot per frame.
