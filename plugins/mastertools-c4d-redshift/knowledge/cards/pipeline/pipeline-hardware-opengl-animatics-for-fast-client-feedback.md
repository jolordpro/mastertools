Bas Helmig and Bart Brinkman (Italian Giants, Dutch 3D studio; Maxon 3D and Motion Design Show) use the hardware renderer with Enhanced OpenGL, turning on shadows and depth of field and filtering out lines, to get quick previews of camera motion and speed [39:17-41:54].
Their example ran 240 frames in about 20 seconds; a five-minute animation could be done in a few minutes, versus slow Octane, Redshift or Standard/Physical renders [42:27-43:32].
Antialiasing raises render time a lot [41:54].

**Use it:** Feedback pass: run a hardware animatic before any final render; keep final-quality GI out of review rounds.
