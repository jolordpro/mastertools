Bas Helmig and Bart Brinkman (Italian Giants, Dutch 3D studio; Maxon 3D and Motion Design Show): they design the kitchen around the main take and only the region used, since no one sees the rest [13:52-14:56].
They model from primitives, and buy intricate non-hero items like appliances or a mixer, but model the tap that is the star piece themselves [14:25-15:27, 21:42-22:15].

**Use it:** Scope rule: build to camera coverage; set a buy-versus-build threshold by hero status.
