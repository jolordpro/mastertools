Richard Sanchez [14:22-15:59]: if two shots come from one long take, the vendor works one shot and the editor cuts it back into two pieces. Pro: the vendor lines the shot up only once, which saves cost. Con: the editor now tracks two separate elements. He calls it common but controversial.

**Use it:** the estimator can price a repeated-setup pair of shots as one render plus a split, but flag the tracking cost.
