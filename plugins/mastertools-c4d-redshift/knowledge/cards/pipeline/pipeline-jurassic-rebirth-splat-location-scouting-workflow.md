Paul Franklin (creative director, Proof Inc.) used splats because the schedule had no time to previs everything [01:42]. Capture was iPhone photos or video, later 360 cameras for better results [04:50]. Order: review footage, cut unusable parts, train in the splat tool and watch it build, kill it early if it will not work, then bring into Unreal [04:50][05:22]. Scale: include a static object of known length (about a metre), place a matching object in Unreal, scale and orient [05:22][05:52]. Then V-cam. Director Gareth Edwards held the cam low on his shoulder, so the rig was adapted and buttons added [06:54][07:24]. Even rough splats worked: the director moved a production meeting into the splat to walk departments through [03:15][03:46].

**Use it:** an inexpensive route to previs a real location: capture, scale by known object, walk it with a virtual camera.
