In test screenings with friends and crews, a few viewers could not get into the film because they were apes, but most settled in after about the first three to five minutes, and it became a regular movie rather than a VFX movie [15:00-15:32].
Ball also notes the film spends patient time with the characters before the action, which asks some patience of the audience [15:00].

**Use it:** Start a film or reveal on calm, well-lit character time before the spectacle so viewers can accept the synthetic subject.
