Red built a self-contained body with wireless video, timecode and focus enclosed, so assistants did not keep fiddling with arms, monitors and cables [22:24-24:29]. Capture technology was identical to a normal Red; the change was logistics [23:57]. The stated priority is to maximise the time the director has with the actor, because the time between takes is what eats the day [25:34-26:04].

**Use it:** Process principle: find the non-creative waiting time (setup, re-linking, re-exporting) and remove it with presets or templates, so iteration time goes to the image.
