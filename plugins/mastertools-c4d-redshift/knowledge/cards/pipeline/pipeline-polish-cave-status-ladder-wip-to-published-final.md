CAVE groups statuses into prep (bidding, omit), work in progress (waiting to start, ready to start, on hold, in progress), version (pending review, review, declined, approved), tech check (pending, declined, approved), client review (pending, review, declined, approved) and publish [§4-§5]. A declined version or element goes back to WIP [§4-§5]. Done means published final: internally approved, tech checked and client approved, then locked [§6]. A hold must have its reason found out [§5].

**Use it:** Use these as a checklist for 'is it done': internal approval, tech check, then client approval before locking.
