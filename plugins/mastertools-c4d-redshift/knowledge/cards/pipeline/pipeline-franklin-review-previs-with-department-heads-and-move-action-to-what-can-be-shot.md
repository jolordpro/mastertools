Paul Franklin says the opening aerial heist was fully previsualised and storyboarded, but then analysed with the stunt coordinator, aerial crew and camera department to shift the balance toward reality [21:26-21:57]. When the stunt coordinator Tom Struthers said a man on the wing would die in the prop wash, Franklin moved him a little back and it told the same story and could be done for real [21:57-22:28]. Previs is not a bible, he says; visual effects are saved for what cannot be done otherwise (the plane hauled on its tail, wings snapping) [22:28-23:30].
Host: Jamie Benning, Filmumentaries Podcast.

**Use it:** Before building a shot, ask the person who would shoot or simulate it what is unsafe or impossible, and move the action instead of fighting it.
