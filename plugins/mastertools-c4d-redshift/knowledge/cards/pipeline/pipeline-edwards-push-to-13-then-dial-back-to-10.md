Vickery recalls Edwards' brief style: do not do a little bit and then spend six months adding a little more. Go all the way to 13, because it is easier to dial back to a perfect 10 than to creep up [31:40, 32:10]. The team broke finished, good shots just to check nothing slightly better existed, and Edwards asks everyone, not only VFX, to try to be slightly better [32:10, 32:41]. In contrast, he walked the line on homage by nudging left and right [31:09].

**Use it:** For revisions: ask for an overshoot version first, then pull back. A coach can offer this as a way to find the limit of a look quickly.
