Van Helsdingen moved his workstation to an office and logged in remotely from a weak laptop; he tried many remote tools and settled on two [03:06]. AnyDesk is his work pick: snappy enough for editing, multi-monitor by opening a second window per monitor, more options [04:07-05:40]. Parsec is aimed at game streaming, has even lower latency (he played a hard-timing game over it) but compresses more and cannot switch monitors [04:38-05:10]. Demo: Fusion, Premiere, and Houdini with rigid-body sim and Redshift IPR all stayed smooth [08:16-11:24]. His one warning: do not finalise composites or grade over a remote link because streaming always adds compression [06:43]. The argument: you need a strong remote machine, not a strong laptop [06:11].
