Ross Denner says the usage flipped from about 70% Maya and 30% Houdini to the reverse within a couple of years [39:53]. Reasons: Houdini needed less TD support [38:52], it can handle effects, groom, destruction, volumes and liquid in one package [39:23], and a tweak at one point propagates through the shot, which Maya's history does not [20:16]. A shared hamster-fur job at MPC took about twice as long in the Maya feature setup as in Houdini [19:44].
Younger staff became strong in under a year [40:23]. The hiring trouble [42:28-43:30]: fewer artists, and the talented ones are at extreme prices; so he wanted to train from inside. Switching time he was told: a year to 18 months for a Maya artist.
