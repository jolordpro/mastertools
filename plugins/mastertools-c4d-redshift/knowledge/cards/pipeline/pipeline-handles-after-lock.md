Handle length is set in the contract and can be negotiated per shot. Our illustration: a 120-frame shot with 12-frame handles renders 144 frames, handles are 17%; at 4 frames, 128 frames, saving 11%. Packages timed to a live rundown save less than pieces cut into an edit.

**Use it:** ask how much of the work goes through an edit before building this.
