Edwards chased the early-90s anamorphic film look; he and DP John Mathieson argued the production into shooting only on film [11:28, 12:30]. Vickery says film is a canvas to work into, with aberrations around highlights, distortion and warping, in contrast to a clinical digital image [13:01, 13:33]. Every shot was scanned in 4K, dailies flew from Bangkok to London overnight, and the team saw results up to 48 hours later; the camera had digital taps [33:44]. Edwards says almost nobody commented on the image, which removed second-guessing, and he suspects older films were helped by that [34:47, 35:20].

**Use it:** Supports adding lens character (distortion, edge aberration, grain) in comp, and limiting who can send picture notes late in a job.
