Order he describes: look tests [24:08], then a set of looks specific to scenes when shooting began [24:40], then sitting in rooms together once cuts reached the DI [25:12]. Trailers helped, since a 3.5-minute compilation shows chunks of the film against each other, and that experiment led to new looks [25:12-25:43]. After the first trailer and a couple of teasers they followed that guide into the film grade, and it fell into place [25:43].

**Use it:** Use a short cut-down or promo as the first look-consistency test: grade it as a set, then use it as the reference for the full piece.
