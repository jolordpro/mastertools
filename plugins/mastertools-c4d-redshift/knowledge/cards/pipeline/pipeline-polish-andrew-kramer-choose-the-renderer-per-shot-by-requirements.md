Andrew Kramer (Video Copilot) says each project starts with: what are the requirements, what is the art, how much time. Then split: some shots in Cinema 4D, close-ups with depth of field in C4D, others in Element 3D inside After Effects because they render faster [05:34-06:36]. Element works at "compositing speed", so look decisions (light, colour) are made with glows and text present in the comp [06:36]. Example: a car for an energy-blast shot was knocked up in about an hour; fine as background asset extension, not for a hero close shot [07:06-07:37].
**Use it:** In planning, tag each shot hero (full renderer) or support (fast comp-level 3D), and give support shots a time cap.
