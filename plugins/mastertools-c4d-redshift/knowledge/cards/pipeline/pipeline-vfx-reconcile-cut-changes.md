Richard Sanchez: the heart of the VFX editor job is tracking cut change, "entropy is the name of the game" [04:02]. A reconcile (also called differential or cut count check) walks every VFX shot and records extended, trimmed, omitted and added shots [15:59]. Doing it for a full film can take a day or two. It also keeps vendors current on notes and new assets.

**Use it:** after each client revision to a sports package, diff shot lengths and list changes per shot before re-rendering, so nothing renders at an old length.
