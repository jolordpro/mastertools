VR review sessions used a Meta Quest 3 (a 2 at first). To avoid headsets for the whole room, a cinematic camera with a chosen film back and lens was attached to the headset and its location and rotation stabilised, so the TV showed a steady view of what the user saw [16:37, 17:40].
Execs saw the same context and could change a design live; sessions were driven by an operator plus an artist [18:12, 18:44].
Horizon or gravity changes were a triggered, announced action with a seat in the room because horizon motion makes people fall over [19:15].
Team on site through art department was four or five people; about 15 to 20 in the capture volume [07:14].

**Use it:** When presenting a 3D space to a room, show it through a locked, stabilised review camera instead of a shaky live view.
