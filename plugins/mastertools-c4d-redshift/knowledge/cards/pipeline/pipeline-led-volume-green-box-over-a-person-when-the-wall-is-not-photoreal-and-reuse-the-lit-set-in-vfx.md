Felix Jorge [05:07-05:39]. When someone objects that a person standing in front of the wall will fail if the wall is not photoreal, put a green box around that person; the human is still lit by real lighting. With proper preparation the same set is used by VFX, and because Unreal now offers photoreal lighting, the lighting is accurate in a way that V-Ray or RenderMan can replicate. Vendor-friendly panel (Epic), so the accuracy claim is his, not a test.

**Use it:** Hybrid plan: green patch where the wall is weak, full volume where it holds. Cross-check any renderer-match claim by a real test frame.
