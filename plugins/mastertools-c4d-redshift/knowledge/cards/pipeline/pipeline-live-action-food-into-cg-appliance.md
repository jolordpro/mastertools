Bas Helmig and Bart Brinkman (Italian Giants, Dutch 3D studio; Maxon 3D and Motion Design Show): for a kitchen-appliance client they filmed real food in the oven, then merged the two worlds, keeping the technical product part in 3D because it is easier to maintain [10:37-11:41].
For a lasagna they cut out the oven window and composited the filmed bake with steam, giving pre, during and post-bake in a time-lapse [11:41-12:11].
The collective's film, photography and illustration skills made this crossover possible in a small team [05:55-06:27].

**Use it:** Hybrid planning: decide per element whether it is 3D or shot, based on appetite appeal and control.
