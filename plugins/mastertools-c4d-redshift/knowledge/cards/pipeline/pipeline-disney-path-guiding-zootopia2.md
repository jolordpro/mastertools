Snow was built from real ice crystals and rendered by brute force; scales switched from texture to real geometry. A UsdSkel crowd instancer cut time and memory. The film is 97 minutes in 41 sequences and 144,355 frames (search summary). No per-frame time or memory figures for recent films are public.

**Use it:** studios spend special techniques only where a shot is hard; flag hard shots early.
