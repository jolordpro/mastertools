Director, VFX supervisor and key creatives watch the latest versions on a large screen. The supervisor leads, and a VFX production coordinator or assistant transcribes the notes in real time; they are then cleaned, organised and distributed to studios and artists as the basis of the next day's work [§2]. Notes are tracked and archived in production management software [§1]. Low grade reference-wiki source.

**Use it:** Keep one note-taker per review who writes in the shot-and-version format and sends a clean list the same day.
