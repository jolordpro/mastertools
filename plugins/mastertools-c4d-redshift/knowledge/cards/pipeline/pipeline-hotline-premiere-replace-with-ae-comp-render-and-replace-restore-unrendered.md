[22:57-26:04] Trim the clip first, right-click it, Replace with After Effects Composition: it builds a comp of the same specs and the change is live in Premiere. Fine for light work, heavy for much. Undo in Premiere cancels the link; copy-paste of clips between apps keeps their place on the timeline. When a linked clip gets slow: Render and Replace; Restore Unrendered brings the editable one back. Habit: duplicate the animatic track, replace, and if heavy duplicate again and render on a top track, giving levels of the process. Also keep one Premiere and one After Effects project open to avoid duplicates [21:56-22:27]. He also drags comps straight into Premiere as live linked titles and lower thirds [44:30-46:12], and imports a Premiere timeline into After Effects either as a live linked single clip or as an editable layer comp [41:50-43:35].

**Use it:** note for versioning rules; keep one open project per app and name rendered stand-ins.
