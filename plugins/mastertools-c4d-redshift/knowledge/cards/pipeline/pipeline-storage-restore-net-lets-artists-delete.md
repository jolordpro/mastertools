A restore safety net for a few months lets artists remove files freely [08:21-08:51]. Buying flash early was called wise [09:22]. A global namespace allows multi-site work, with vendor lock-in as a trade-off [07:49].

**Use it:** a render-clean step may delete intermediates if it has a recoverable copy.
Transcript support: Snapshots a couple of times a day with self-service restore; tape restore can take days (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lCr3vIKUpwo [34:46])
