Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour): an early reskin test proved long-lens broadcast shots with zooms and pans were very hard to track, so he planned for thousands of shots [17:40-18:43].
Clear Angle Studios lidar-scanned key tracks to millimetre accuracy at night, a drone circled for textures, and a camera array on a vehicle shot 24 pictures per second with a skinny shutter along the race line [18:43-19:43].
The array sat at about 50 cm (driver eye height): three cameras front, four back, one up for reflections [20:14-20:44]. It captured signage and crowd as they were on race weekends [19:13-19:43].
Result: cameras could be matchmoved, cars matchimated and a 3D environment recreated to light reflective cars [19:43-20:14].

**Use it:** Environment capture checklist for any recurring venue: scan, texture, plates at eye height, reflection plate.
