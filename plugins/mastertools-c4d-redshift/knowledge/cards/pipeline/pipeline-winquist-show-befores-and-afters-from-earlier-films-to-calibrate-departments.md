For the fourth Apes film he pulled clips from the three earlier films showing the process, what was shot and why, and made a presentation for all heads of department and the cast during prep [10:59-11:33].
The aim: everyone understands why things are done this way and what the benefits are, and where they wished they had done it differently, for example judging that some shots do not need a clean plate [11:03-11:33].
Result: departments calibrated early on how a performance-capture movie differs from a normal film.

**Use it:** Open a hard-to-explain pipeline with a short before-and-after reel from a previous job so other departments see the why.
