He sees inconsistent dailies from facilities new to a streamer, for example one day to another [38:33-39:04]. His fix: throw reference shots at the model, preview it fast, and fix only what is off [39:04-39:35].
About two hours of material a day needs lots of precision but no genius, so let the computer do it and check that it followed instructions [39:35-40:06].
He says two big companies were integrating it into their dailies pipelines first, as low-hanging fruit [37:02, 40:06].

**Use it:** In dailies or review prep, automate the matching and keep human time for approving the reference.
