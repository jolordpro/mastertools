Alex and Thomas give ranges [1:14:51-1:17:27]: a project can be a couple of weeks or a couple of months; a shot with one extra department (a smoke puff) is finishable in about two weeks; two dragons over a snow environment around five months; the crashing dead dragon in a fort about a year, started in pre-production then redone for post. If time is short, add artists, and bid shots correctly. Most artists feel a shot is never finished, so the client's schedule (the poster announced air date) is the real finish line. These are their own estimates, said off the cuff.

**Use it:** In the cost estimator, show depth-of-departments as the main driver of time, and treat the delivery date as the finish line.
