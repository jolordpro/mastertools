Ball says the hard part of directing is keeping in mind that the actor will become an ape: in the edit you see people in suits, with cameras covering half their faces [07:50-08:21].
He told himself not to forget how impressive it will be to watch an ape just sit and dream, and sometimes accounted for that in the cut, accepting a beat that feels too long with a silly-looking actor because it will work on the finished ape [08:21-08:51].

**Use it:** When cutting from grey or proxy renders, judge beat length for the finished frame and keep a note on which holds are waiting for final quality.
