Because capture is fast, the team is not precious about a performance: use it to get to the final image, or throw it away and do it better [36:27]. Brady wants art, technology, character design and story to feed each other, with more tries clearing mistakes sooner [36:27]. The test of the speed is flexing on the spot when a director such as Teddy Newton suggests a gag: move that wall, scale this up a hundred times, make it a gag now [36:59]. Rich Moore adds that the spontaneity of improv finds funnier moments than a fully plotted script [17:44, 18:14].

**Use it:** The coach can encourage cheap throwaway drafts before committing a long render, in the spirit of the 640 px drafts.
