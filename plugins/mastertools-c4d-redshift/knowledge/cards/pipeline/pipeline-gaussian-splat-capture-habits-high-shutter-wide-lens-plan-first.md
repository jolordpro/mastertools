Panel speakers (Joao of ARRI CSS Studios, virtual production supervisor Katherine Brillhart, host Michael Rubloff): splats are not magic, you must plan and there is a learning curve; capturing for a shoot tomorrow "can go really bad" [05:45-06:15]. Joao's capture advice: very high shutter speed, wide angle, reduce blur in every frame [23:30]. Scanning apps show zebra-stripe coverage so you scan until stripes are gone; a 360 camera on a head rig lets you crop the lower third instead of painting yourself out [22:28-23:30]. Expect good results after one to two weeks of tries [22:28].
Old photogrammetry or NeRF data can be re-run as splats with better quality; the capture is similar, the processing differs, and the AI step fills holes photogrammetry would leave [19:20-20:54]. Good lens, low distortion and parallax still matter [19:20].

**Use it:** Before any scan job: shutter, lens, coverage check and a spare day.
