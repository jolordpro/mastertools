Feinberg (2024) [07:43-09:15]: Pixar uses no generative AI on its films yet, but has used a denoiser since Toy Story 4: the machines stop calculating earlier than finished and the image is run through a denoiser trained by machine learning to give the final frame.
On generative image tools she finds them inspirational but cannot get the exacting image in her head from a prompt; Pixar likes control over everything [09:15].
Her view: AI can help with things artists care less about, but the human experience of making art to express who we are stays [09:46].
Date-sensitive: policy is as of June 2024.

**Use it:** Estimator and coach: early-stop plus a denoiser is a studio-standard way to save render time; keep control-critical shots on conventional sampling.
