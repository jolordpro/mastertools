Nick (host): about 15 years ago his mirrored multi-drive Drobo set-up (two drives could fail and data would survive, he says) failed with no recovery and he lost two years of work [17:22-17:55].
Later his Puget PC's SSD died. Support had "never seen this", and because he tried to troubleshoot for a while first the machine was out for a few months [25:13-27:17].
His takeaway is to keep things the same and not change a working set-up, but the craft lesson is that redundancy is not a backup.

**Use it:** Preflight and delivery: a project is not safe until a copy exists on a different device; ask where the second copy is, not whether the array is mirrored.
