Feinberg [00:07-01:11]: lighting is the last creative step, so when a film slips, the lighting schedule shrinks and extra hands are pulled in (how she moved from rendering into lighting on A Bug's Life). She says there is never a sense of "good enough" at Pixar; if something is a story point, the studio figures out the technology rather than debating whether it can afford to.

**Use it:** the framing for the platform's budget conversations: tie a technical request to the story point it serves.
