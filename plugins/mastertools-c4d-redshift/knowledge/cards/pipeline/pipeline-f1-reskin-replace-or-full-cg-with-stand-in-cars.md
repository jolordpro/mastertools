Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour): about six picture cars on an F2 chassis were modified, plus F3 cars as stunt stand-ins [24:20-25:23]. Minor fixes were wider tyres and removing rigs and antennas [25:23-26:24].
Reskinning swaps the digital car into the real one's place and matches its wheel, suspension and driver performance; they paint out anything that sticks out beyond the new car [29:01-29:32].
When a rival car was needed they filmed another car as lighting and performance reference and replaced it; fully animated cars were the last resort [27:28-27:58].
A digital F1-length version of their car was used when parked with real F1 cars [30:04-30:35].

**Use it:** Rule: a physical stand-in on set gives free light and motion reference; keep full CG for what cannot be filmed.
