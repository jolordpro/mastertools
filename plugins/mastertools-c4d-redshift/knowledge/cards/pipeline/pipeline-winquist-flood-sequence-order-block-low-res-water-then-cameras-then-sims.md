The final flooded-silo sequence has no real water. It was built from production art and the car park and stage locations, extending their visual language and the stabilised handheld camera language already established [16:02-18:38].
Order: animators block gags and the chase with back-and-forth with Ball (what if he tackles him here and goes over the edge) [19:09]; the effects team gives animation a rough low-res water surface so characters bob and fight currents [20:09]; cameras are then set so they sit at the turbulent surface and not under it [20:09]; sims take multiple days, so a choice is seen a couple of days later; there is constant back and forth between effects, animation and reviews [20:41].
Ball adds that cameras had a tethered 'cameraman' feel, as if the water pushed against the operator [17:05].

**Use it:** Settle blocking with a proxy fluid surface before committing to the expensive sim; plan reviews around sim turnaround so each pass answers a specific choice.
