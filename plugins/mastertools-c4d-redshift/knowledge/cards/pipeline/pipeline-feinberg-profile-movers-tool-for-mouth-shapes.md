Wanted mouth shapes (cat, bean, tiny, huge) were hard for the usual tools. A long-time articulation artist proposed profile movers: a line the computer solves to hit a shape instead of a person moving points. She judged it the better, more artistic way from her small rigging experience, knew it was unproven and that the decision carried extra pressure, and it worked, leaving animators not struggling [§2-§3].

**Use it:** Weigh a new tool by what it does to the artists' daily effort, and say up front that it is unproven.
