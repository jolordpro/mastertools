Fraser [01:42-03:46] says a studio film gives a huge machine for action sequences, but dialogue on a dune needs four people, not 400; the skill is to switch between the big machine and a small independent-style setup so characters still connect. He adds that Villeneuve is stubborn about vision but adapts to conditions (weather), and that he himself came from tiny Australian films and prefers small-world work.

**Use it:** apply the same split in production planning: big crew and tooling only where the shot demands it.
