The plate car carried 12 gyro-stabilised cameras (8K Helium, sometimes Monstro, with Canon lenses); plate focus was set to match the taking-lens focus lined up on stage, such as a 40 mm driver POV at about four feet, so the optics look consistent in the composite [38:08-39:42].
Plate shooting was often done right after first-unit work at the same location and was fast [38:08].
The 8K sensor was oversampled for a 4K finish, which Messerschmidt compares to oversampling audio [35:25].

**Use it:** Match depth of field and focus distance between background plates and the foreground render so the join does not announce itself.
