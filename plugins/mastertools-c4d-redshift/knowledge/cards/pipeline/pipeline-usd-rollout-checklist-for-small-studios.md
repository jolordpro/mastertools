Checklist from Scheller: developers learn the terms; kinds and schemas are only JSON; fix data IO first by building one export workflow per data type (instances, skeletons, deforming meshes) so artists do not decide [29:25-29:55]. Make production tracking handle layers and dependencies [30:27]. Do not tell artists everything will be ten times faster; conversion cost exists [32:31]. Communicate early because layering means seeing other departments' work and adds overhead [33:03]. For variants on complex assets, define naming conventions and nesting rules before building [57:46-58:17]. Game engines convert to their own format, so USD organizes assets and an optimization step follows [1:00:00].

**Use it:** Use as a pre-flight list when proposing USD or Solaris handoffs.
