The remedy given: point out what went wrong by evidence, and the blame disappears; the artist then understands [01:33].
Artists arrive stressed because failing or starved jobs threaten their deadline [02:04].

Transfers to a render platform: give users a clear log-based explanation of why a job failed or waited.
