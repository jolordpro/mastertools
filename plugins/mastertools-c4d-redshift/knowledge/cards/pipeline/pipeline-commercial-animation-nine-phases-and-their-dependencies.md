Audrey Havey [04:00-10:06]. Scope: ads of 15 seconds to 2 minutes, not films. (1) Pre-production: meet the client, get budget, timeline, brand guidelines; sign contracts before work; the key output is the creative brief, approved by the client. (2) Script and scratch voiceover (creative director writes with the client; scratch VO once the script is nearly locked, to give timing). (3) Initial art direction in parallel: mood boards, sketches, style frames, a few key moments shown to the client. Without a locked script and look, designers and animators cannot start. (4) Storyboards: simple sketches that also hint at complexity and budget. (5) Animatic when boards are about 75% done: boards over the scratch VO to judge length, drag or rush; boards can be swapped later. (6) Full design only after the look is approved, with files prepped for the animator. (7) Animation with the animatic as timing reference and VO approved, technical director on call. (8) Sound design on the final cut, since timing changes break a mix. (9) Delivery: QC, export in the right sizes and specs, archive files, high-quality masters, send.

**Use it:** Check order: nothing downstream should start before its input is locked, and the sound mix waits for picture lock. Supports the twelve-step process card.
