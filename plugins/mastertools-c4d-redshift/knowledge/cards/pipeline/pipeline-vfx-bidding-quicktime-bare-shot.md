VFX editor Richard Sanchez lays out bid clips with just the shot, unlike reference clips, which get one shot of context either side [06:12]. Adjacent shots run back to back; single shots are separated by a second of black. He leaves sound off for security. Burn in the work order and shot number, and watermark with vendor and studio ownership [06:43-07:47].

**Use it:** the estimator's "send for quote" export (to a freelancer or a farm) should crop to the bare shot, strip audio, and burn in shot ID plus a watermark.
