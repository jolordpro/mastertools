Bas Helmig and Bart Brinkman (Italian Giants, Dutch 3D studio; Maxon 3D and Motion Design Show): Cinema 4D is the host software; ZBrush is for characters and very dense realistic meshes, and Houdini for intricate simulations where particle limits would block them [06:27-07:30].
Rendering is mostly Octane inside Cinema; textures come from Substance, Quixel and RizomUV; renders go straight to After Effects for raw composition and post, with Premiere for edit and music, Illustrator for technical textures such as tyre wire patterns, Photoshop for stills [07:30-08:31].
They became a multidisciplinary collective (illustration, 3D, 2D animation, film, photography) about two and a half years earlier so crossovers need no outside hire [04:20-06:27].
For particle work they prefer X-Particles inside Cinema over a second program because the whole scene tweaks and times in one place [38:45-39:17].

**Use it:** Reference stack for a small commercial studio when sizing a farm: Octane/GPU render, AE comp, stills pipeline.
