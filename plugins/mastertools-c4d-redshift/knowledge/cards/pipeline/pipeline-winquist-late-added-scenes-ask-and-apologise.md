Whole scenes were invented from plates shot for other scenes and performance takes moved between shots. A threshold-crossing moment that fell away in editing was reconceived and put back, and a one-minute dinner scene was added three weeks before delivery. Ball says he apologised in advance and showed what he wanted, and was usually told it was better. He says requests were worth it only if they helped the story. [§3]

**Use it:** Bring a late change with a story reason and a plain apology; test it with the person who must animate it.
