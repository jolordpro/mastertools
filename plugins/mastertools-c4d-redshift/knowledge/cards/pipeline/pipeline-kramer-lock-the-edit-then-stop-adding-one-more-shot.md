His short film Intercept slipped over a year after Star Trek work took his time, and each re-look invited more additions [1:28:18-1:29:20, 1:37:38-1:39:00]. His fix: decide how it will get done, lock the edit, start the visual effects for what exists and do not create new shots [1:29:20-1:29:51]. Client work is easier because someone else's schedule sets the end; on his own work he is precious [1:36:06-1:36:38].
The host adds his own finding from digital painting: the last 40% of the time was tweaking small details that changed little and taught least [1:30:21-1:31:24, host, low confidence].

**Use it:** Lock cut and camera before any polish pass, and time-box finishing; judge each extra tweak by whether the result changes.
