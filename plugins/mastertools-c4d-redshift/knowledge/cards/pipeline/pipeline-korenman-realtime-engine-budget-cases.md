Feature film: an Oscar-nominated animated feature made in Blender Eevee (title not in the captions) cost about $3.8M, used fewer than 50 people (he heard about 40), and the director rendered it on a home PC with no farm [12:22].
Live: Moment Factory built a fully real-time scene for a jam band (captioned Fish, so likely Phish) at the Sphere so lighting, camera and effects could follow a band that never plays the same set twice [14:24].
Virtual production: a small team shot about eight locations in a day for a few thousand dollars; a normal crew gets two or three locations a day, so the same shoot-day budget buys more setups, and the DP can ask to move a tree [15:56-16:58].
Broadcast examples he names: a Super Bowl package with a flyable Las Vegas built in Unreal and a Rocket League live-event package; the usual engine in this space is Vizrt [19:00-20:36].

**Use it:** context only. Real-time raises what a small team can promise on budget and schedule, which sets the bar clients may quote back.
