In the first week of production, if the set is roughly known, AMGI mocks up layout blocking, often just cubes and spheres, takes it to the stage and plays with the camera, which doubles as location scouting [08:54]. An establishing shot needs no thumbnail because its rough 3D move can be cut straight into the animatic [08:54]. Brady noticed polished layout shots often felt cold, so they use the stage to make moves more organic and handheld [05:14]. They still keep storyboard artists for heartfelt, emotional moments, and a novice reads a real-time cut more clearly than drawings [20:20, 20:51, 21:22].

**Use it:** Same stage order as the C4D previz for a promo: cubes, camera, animatic, then detail. Reserve boards for beats that need emotional timing.
