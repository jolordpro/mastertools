Old model: editors ran client sessions with the client on the couch. 3D could not, because watching render buckets while the client waits kills the session, and even GPU renderers only show an approximation [16:58].
With a real-time engine the client sees essentially the final image, so a change like a faster camera is one click and a re-watch, and approval can happen in the room or on a Zoom call [17:30].
He pitches this as a differentiator for an outgoing 3D artist, because few offer it yet [17:30-18:00].
