Speaker: Jakob, CG generalist at bEpic (Berlin, mid-sized, VFX-heavy commercials). Reasons to move to Solaris: the same data flows across departments so scenes are not rebuilt between animation and lighting, good lighting tools, and procedural scene editability enabling a larger multi-shot pipeline later [05:15-06:16]. They picked a project with a humane timeframe and few shots so few artists were needed, leaving freedom to experiment, which short commercials rarely allow [06:16-06:48]. The Mars-canyon spot ran in Redshift inside Solaris and they reused volumes (a storm) across shots [14:05-14:41]. He says they are not fully in Solaris yet; a working pipeline is in a transition phase [20:00].

**Use it:** Trial a new pipeline on the smallest low-risk job and write down why before starting.
