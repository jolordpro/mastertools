After the capture-based cut was locked, voices were recorded together and cut like a radio play with three witness cameras for facial reference [07:21]. In principal photography they measured how far the boy moved, camera and lights, and built just that much set, with blue screen [07:51]. Each shot was cut into the movie as shot and shown to the performer, so by the end every shot already had the boy in it [07:51-08:23].

**Use it:** Mock up the whole sequence first, then shoot each live element to the measured blocking and drop it into the cut immediately for review.
