Each note is tied to a coded shot and version (example format SEQ_010_SHOT_0040_v012). It splits technical feedback (lighting does not match the plate key, more spec on the hull, smoke sim too slow for the wind) from creative direction (more fear than surprise, a chemical green not fiery orange), and visual annotations, draw-overs, drawn on the frame with a tablet, are attached [§1]. The cycle repeats until the director finals the shot [§1]. Low grade reference-wiki source.

**Use it:** Write polish notes in this shape: which shot and version, what is technically off, what the feel should be, and a marked-up frame.
