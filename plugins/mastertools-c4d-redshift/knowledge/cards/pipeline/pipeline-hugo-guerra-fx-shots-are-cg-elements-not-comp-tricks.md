Asked why Nuke has no 'bullet from a gun' tutorials, Hugo explains that in a film pipeline the gun and bullet are modelled and rendered in 3D, a scar is tracked and applied as a matte painting or projection, and one shot involves modelling, shading, rendering, Houdini and Nuke, so no single comp app holds the whole effect [53:58, 54:29, 54:59]. After Effects tutorials show all-in-one effects because it is a broader prosumer tool and rarely used in advanced VFX [55:30]. Summary of the view: a shot is a group effort.

**Use it:** Supports building effects as separate 3D elements with passes and finishing in comp, which is how the C4D plus Redshift pipeline already works.
