Ferrari had no second unit; Mann directed everything, with a few inserts [34:19-34:50]. Messerschmidt says he heard 50 days, the host says 58, so the count is unclear.
His view: second unit is usually decided by a line producer for schedule, and it turns the film into procedural storytelling with actor reactions cut to separate action footage, often visibly different in light [35:53-36:56].
He adds that the frame is the director's tool, and a director who wants to operate should [37:27-37:58].

**Use it:** When action and hero shots come from different teams, lock a shared lighting and colour reference so they match.
