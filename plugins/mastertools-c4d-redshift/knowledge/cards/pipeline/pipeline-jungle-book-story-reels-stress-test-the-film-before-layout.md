Favreau says animated films have a better batting average because story is stress-tested, sometimes through eight reel passes [05:15]. He hired a head of story and had rough sketches of the whole film while giving notes [05:46]. Once the story was approved he moved to layout, which here was a motion-capture volume with the DP, as in an animated film's layout stage [06:16].

**Use it:** Pipeline order for long-form work: story reel, approve, then layout/previs, then final; keep reels for review.
