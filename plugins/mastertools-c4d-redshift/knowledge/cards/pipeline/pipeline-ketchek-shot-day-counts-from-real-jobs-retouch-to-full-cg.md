Michal Ketchek (VFX supervisor/producer, Magic Lab, Czech studio) in a CEE Animation webinar; auto-captions, so some figures may be misheard. Figures are what he states for his shots and are rough, with captions unclear in places. Static wall-switch removal: about a third of a day in comp, minimal in a grade but more through a full pipeline with approvals [1:02:11]. A dark-jacket figure behind black hair: about 1 day matte painting plus 5 days comp, because tiny wires had to be rebuilt [1:03:18]. Static set extension on a blue screen: 2.5 days matte painting plus 2 days comp, which he says would really be 4.5 [1:04:22]. Complex DMP with ground, CG objects and actors on blue screen: 27 days [1:04:55]. Projected matte painting: asset about 15 days (heard as 'base'), 3 days of comp, tracking and layout, 7 days for a shot like that [1:05:26]. Full CG plane shot: half a day layout, 4 days animation, then FX, lighting, DMP and comp [1:07:30]. Studio of 100 artists: about 2 tracking, 62 CG, 6 DMP, 30 comp [13:39].
