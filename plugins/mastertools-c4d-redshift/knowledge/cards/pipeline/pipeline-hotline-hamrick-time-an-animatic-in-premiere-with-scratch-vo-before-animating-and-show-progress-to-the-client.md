Kyle Hamrick (guest on Evan Abrams' Adobe Live show, 2023) [06:23-16:12]: design the storyboards, let the client see them, then time the stills to a scratch voice-over and a music track in Premiere (hard cuts mostly); it exposes gaps and overcrowded beats before animating. The same timeline then takes animated shots as they are exported, so the client sees real pieces and the rest stay stills. Colour-coded tracks let him orchestrate several artists. He offers the animatic over a few music tracks. He uses Frame.io comments to flag "rough animated, not ready". Reason: clients cannot visualise from sketches and a linear start-to-finish pass leaves them seeing garbage until the end; incremental proof builds confidence [15:10-16:12].
Evan's note: every problem starts with a missing codified process [02:46].

**Use it:** the platform's review stage can mirror this: a living timeline where stills are replaced by renders.
