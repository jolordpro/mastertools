Departments in the stated order [01:00-14:16]: story (thousands of drawings pitched to director and producers), editorial (cuts a story reel with temp voices and music; present from the start and throughout) [02:34-03:36], visual development (look approved by the art director or production designer, then the director) [04:08], modelling, rigging, surfacing (flat grey to materials from a library with layered paint, dirt, rust, scratches), rough layout (storyboards recreated with a virtual camera; the gateway from pre-production to production) [07:22], final layout (anim prep swaps proxy characters for high-res and sets a start pose; after animation, final set dressing and a stereo pass) [07:52], character animation (a character has about 3,000 controls, dragons 5,000-6,000; reference shot in an acting room) [08:34], crowds, character effects (cloth, hair, fur, squish on contact), FX, matte painting (uses the art department's colour keys as touchstone) [12:06-12:37], lighting then composite with the matte painting, image finaling (cleans popping, buzzing and smudges) [13:46], music and mix. He notes that real shots loop and skip stops [01:00].

**Use it:** A short checklist of department handoffs for a broadcast package; image finaling is the equivalent of a last artefact-cleanup pass before delivery.
