Splats were made for the bar, three beaches (boat crash placed in different spots) , the swamp (walkway and T-Rex positions decided) and the helicopter in the canopy [03:46][04:18][04:50]. The director did not rely on heavily previsualised dinosaurs; the team ran many more post-vis experiments and used Unreal looks learned from the splat work [08:27][08:58]. Rigs were shared with ILM so animation tests moved both ways, giving continuity [08:58][09:29]. For the Quetzal sequence, the redesigned background used photogrammetry of the real falls, three versions around the nest; a drone shooter was briefed from those views, and post-vis then used his real footage in each direction [10:00][10:34][11:05].

**Use it:** shows how to hand a plate shooter exact angles from a quick CG layout before shooting.
