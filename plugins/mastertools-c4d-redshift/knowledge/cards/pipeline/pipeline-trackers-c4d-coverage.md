Frame.io V4 (Adobe, 2024) is the review layer tied to Premiere and After Effects. Whether the ftrack C4D integration is current in 2026 is unknown. No evidence was found that small motion studios run full tracker pipelines; folders, naming rules and Frame.io are more likely (our guess).

**Use it:** build a Kitsu bridge first, other trackers later; do not assume C4D is a first-class citizen anywhere.
