Williams: do not trust memory or a production assistant's later summary; write numbered notes in a pocket notebook, then clarify them at your desk [03:43-04:24]. Next daily, open with the last notes ("A, B, C fixed; blink not yet"), which reminds the director and shows you listened [04:24-05:26].
Ask for clarification if a note is unclear or cannot be done as asked, but not twice on the same point [05:26-05:57]. He learned the "thick hide" lesson on The Lion King when he got frustrated by many notes on a hard shot and says he would handle it differently [05:57-06:58].
Two later points: with notes from non-animators, ask a teammate to translate the problem [11:09]; with several directors or supervisors giving conflicting notes, hold the original idea and be confident [12:12-13:15].

**Use it:** The notes tracker should store the numbered list and show last round's items at the top of the next review.
