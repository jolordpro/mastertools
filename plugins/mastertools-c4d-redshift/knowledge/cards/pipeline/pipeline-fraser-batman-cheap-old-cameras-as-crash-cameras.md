For the car chase a second-unit DP did the testing: cameras mounted on bikes and cars and crash tests. They bought older Alexas to use as crash cameras so cameras could go closer to the destruction, knowing a loss would be a write-off. Smaller lenses rehoused by Iron Glass were nimble enough to mount [§4]. It took a lot of R&D and takes to get the angles [§4].

**Use it:** Buy or rent a disposable body for any shot where the camera may be destroyed.
