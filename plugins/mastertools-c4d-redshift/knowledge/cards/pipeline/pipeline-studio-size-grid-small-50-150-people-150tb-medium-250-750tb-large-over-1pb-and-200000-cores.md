The host (Joe, A Cup Of Joe) uses an overly general grid in every episode [17:38-18:41].
Small: about 50-150 people, up to roughly 150 TB. Medium: 250-750 TB, two to four projects live and two finishing together. Large: five-plus studios, over a petabyte, tiered storage, about 200,000 cores.
He stresses studios grow through these stages rather than jump between them.
His tip for small shops: start with flash because it hides many tuning problems when there is no storage team [20:20-20:48].

**Use it:** keep it as a size sanity check for cost talks; a single-artist workflow sits far below the small tier.
