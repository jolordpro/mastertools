The DP joins much later than the designer, so she asks early about aspect ratio, expected lenses and whether the look is wide or tight [38:25]. Romantic comedies lean to long lenses, 50 mm and up, while horror and drama often use anamorphic wide lenses [38:56]. She also needs to know what lights go on set, since LED strips can be built into sets and production design now often supplies practicals [38:56-39:58]. Lighting of illustrations matters too [37:53].

**Use it:** Handoff checklist from design to camera/lighting: ratio, lens range, light types. Ask before building the set.
