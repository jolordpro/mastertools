Every one of the dozen hero apes was a speaking role, and Winquist says there is more speech than in the whole previous trilogy combined [06:17].
Higher-fidelity capture data plus deep learning in the facial solve gave the facial animation team a consistent baseline solve to build on, so they did not have to work through the nuts and bolts of complex deformation [06:48]. That let them take on a lot more work with the same size crew as the earlier films [07:19].
The location capture was also lighter: less infrastructure and fewer trucks, because the sites were hard to reach [04:45-05:46].

**Use it:** Look for a consistent automated first pass, then spend artist time on the notes that sell the performance.
