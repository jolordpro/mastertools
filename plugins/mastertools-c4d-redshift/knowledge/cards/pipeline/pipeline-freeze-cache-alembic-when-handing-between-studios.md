Asked about push then pack as in VFX, the researchers said Cube Creative does something similar when interacting with other studios: animation is cached to Alembic (ABC) and sent to the next step, sometimes arriving from another company [46:54] [47:26].
Autour de Minuit uses pull at every step on purpose, because the pipeline is modular: it can start on any step, such as only modeling or only animation, take data from another studio and hand data on [47:58].

**Use it:** Handoff rule: when an asset leaves the building, send a frozen cache plus a version number, not live links.
