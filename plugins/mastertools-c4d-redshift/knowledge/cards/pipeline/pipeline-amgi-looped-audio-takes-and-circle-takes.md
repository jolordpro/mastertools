AMGI's studio founder Colin Brady says performance works best when body and face are captured at the same time while the pre-recorded dialogue loops, like recording a voice. Three, four or five takes, usually right by the third [06:46]. Each character is a layer: record a second character against playback of the first, then props such as a flying airplane [07:17]. Takes are circled live, a technician assembles the selects in one scene, the team reviews it live, and once the performance is right they move on and add a camera pass only if needed [07:17, 07:50]. Panel includes directors Rich Moore, Jill Culton, Teddy Newton.

**Use it:** Review-by-take pattern for a motion-capture or live-performance reference: loop, 3-5 takes, circle, assemble selects, review live, move on.
