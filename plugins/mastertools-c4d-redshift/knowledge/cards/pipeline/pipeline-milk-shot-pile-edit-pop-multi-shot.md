Pile queries ShotGrid and lists publishes with a cap on versions, skipping declined ones; edit lets supervisors (not artists) enable or disable prims and choose latest approved only or latest regardless; pop reads the cast, applies those edits and stacks the layers [17:03-20:40]. A shot switch makes one branch per shot, so sequence lighting sits above and each shot gets branch-specific changes; the active branch goes green to red [20:40-21:43]. A live render shows cast assets with look dev; sending to the farm renders every shot, and proxy folders are made from the master shot for each client shot [21:43-22:46].

**Use it:** Make approval filters a supervisor control, not an artist toggle.
