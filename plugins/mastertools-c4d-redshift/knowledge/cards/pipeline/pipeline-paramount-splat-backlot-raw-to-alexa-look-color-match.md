Joao (ARRI CSS Studios) on a CBS VFX job: sets vanish after principal photography, and re-shoots, marketing interviews or ICVFX need them; hand-modelling hundreds is impossible [10:54-11:56]. The team built a wheeled totem with five Nikon cameras (one would work, five go faster) that scans sets between takes [11:56]. RAW stills were converted to the look of the production camera (Alexa, Red Raptor) before splat training; processing in Volinga Suite kept HDR and ACES colour consistent, and a side-by-side matched an Alexa capture [12:28-12:58]. Same assets served an actor interview on an LED wall within hours [13:30].
Fire Country: drone-captured silo splat, about 2 hours of capture, relit and sky swapped in Volinga, running on the LED wall in 1-2 weeks, which the show could not do by modelling [14:32-16:39]. Trend noted: one splat asset reused in previs, ICVFX and post (Nuke now has native splat support) [09:52].

**Use it:** Reference for colour-managed capture: convert to the delivery camera look first, keep ACES/HDR through the splat tool.
