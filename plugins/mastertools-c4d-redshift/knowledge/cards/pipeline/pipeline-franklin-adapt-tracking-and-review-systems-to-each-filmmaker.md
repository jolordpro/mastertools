At Double Negative (about 900 people at the time, per Franklin), Inception had 250 artists working for about six months [02:12]. He says almost as much effort goes into the management systems and software that track shots, organise reviews, submit work to the client and load work onto the system [02:12-02:44].
Every show is different and every filmmaker wants to work in a different way, so the systems are constantly modified to fit the filmmaker [02:44].
Numbers differ from the other Inception card (about 230 people, 13 to 14 months); his figure here is the artist headcount on the show and the delivery crunch, and the captions are poor.

**Use it:** Treat the review and tracking setup as a per-client configuration (how they want to see work, how often, in what format), not a fixed studio template.
