On Mission Impossible: Rogue Nation, he covered for the main supervisor for about eight weeks. In the opera sequence 40 to 50 extras were moved around and shot in blocks all night to be pieced into a full house [1:11:57-1:12:57]. For stunt doubles in helmets he noted how each shot was filmed so heads could be replaced [1:13:29-1:13:59]. When a driving scene would need re-shooting on green screen, he photographed the whole location and checked dailies for clean-plate coverage [1:14:00-1:15:00].
You need to be there to understand the movie, he says [1:16:02].

**Use it:** On any live shoot, capture the reference, clean plates and notes post will need before the set is struck.
