For the walk on the gangway, the actor wore a full suit, pulled back by a bungee with wind machines, shot at about 40 fps for a slightly slower feel [31:49, 32:21].
Episode one was mostly practical with a CG visor, tube and environments; patches were tracked on for disintegration [32:51].
By episode four the CG suit model was so good, and there was more destruction, that shots became full digital driven by the matched live performance [33:22, 34:25].
Townsend says he would use the same shoot-the-actor technique again because it gives a believable physical performance [34:25].
Final sequence: the actor mimed strain, tests used ropes and pool noodles for physicality [37:03].

**Use it:** Capture a real performance as reference even for full-CG shots; decide per shot whether the destruction justifies replacing the photographic element.
