Guests Connor Ling and Michael Zaman (Framestore pre-production services) on the virtual-camera work for DP Greig Fraser.
All blocking and lensing was open gate, with thin white frame lines for each target deliverable ratio laid over the plate, so he could see where the subject sat in each [48:45].
Michael recalls only three or four re-racks or scale changes across all the postviz shots he did (interiors); exterior had a bit more play [50:48].
The point made: the sweet spot differs between theatrical, IMAX and home screens, so composition is a staging problem in its own right [50:18].
Transfers directly to broadcast: build in the widest working frame and overlay 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 and safe-area guides during camera and layout.

**Use it:** Give the layout stage a multi-ratio frame-line overlay and review each shot against all deliverable ratios before locking the camera.
