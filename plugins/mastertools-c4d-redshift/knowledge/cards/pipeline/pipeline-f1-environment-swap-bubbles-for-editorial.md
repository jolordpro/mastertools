Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour): for Monza they filmed at Silverstone, then swapped in a digital Monza from the lidar and array data [33:43-34:14].
Fans know every corner, so signs alone would not sell it. They gave the editor bubbles: the foreground performance dropped quickly over an array backdrop as a simple A over B, not perspective-correct [37:50-38:55].
Only after editorial approval did a big team rebuild it as a 3D environment with parallax, trees and blur, and they kept working until nothing stuck out against the real footage [38:55-39:57].

**Use it:** Gate: cheap editorial approval of swap shots before expensive environment builds.
