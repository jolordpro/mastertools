The step order (boards, layout, modelling, look-dev, set dressing, animation, FX, lighting, rendering, comp, colour, editorial) is common industry knowledge; the check lists are our adaptation, not a studio publication. Comp readiness: passes sum to beauty, depth edges handled, Cryptomatte present, vectors present if blur is post.

**Use it:** one reviewer per department with its own scope; never comment outside it.
