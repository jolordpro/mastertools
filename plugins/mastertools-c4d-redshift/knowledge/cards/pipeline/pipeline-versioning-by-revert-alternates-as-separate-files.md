Alberelli [41:36-42:39]: every save is a checkout point and they revert if someone goes too far. Normally there are no alternate versions; if one must live beside the original, make a separate file. Kitsu keeps all comments and playblasts over time, and the team reviews them to choose the version.
They check out the whole project, not parts, at their scale. A noise-sampling question got only 'push samples as far as we can' [47:46].

**Use it:** keep alternates as new files and keep the review history, not the folder names, as the record of what was chosen.
