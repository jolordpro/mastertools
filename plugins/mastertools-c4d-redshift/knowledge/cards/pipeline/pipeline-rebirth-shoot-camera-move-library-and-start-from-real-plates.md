Edwards asked second unit to shoot many moving shots from idea A to B on every axis, not knowing the palette; in the edit he picked those where move and light both worked [14:34, 15:05]. One shot played a drone reveal backwards, flopped, and added a cave, so the team starts from little real 'Lego bricks' and breaks them apart instead of building a cave and coastline in 3D [15:36, 16:06]. Vickery quotes the saying that a great plate can make a great VFX shot, but a bad plate cannot [25:26]. When Edwards framed handheld shots of a creature that was not there, he marked where it would be with his hand over the monitor so the operator could compose [20:12, 20:46]. Little puppeteer or virtual stand-in was used on this film [17:38].

**Use it:** For broadcast 3D, shoot or render a library of camera moves and light variants and choose in the edit; start from the best real or rendered element and rework it.
