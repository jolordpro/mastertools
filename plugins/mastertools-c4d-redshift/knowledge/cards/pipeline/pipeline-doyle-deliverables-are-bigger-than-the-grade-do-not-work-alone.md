Doyle's advice to a junior colourist [1:06:22-1:12:46]: you are handling large budgets and creating the final product, so take delivery specs seriously and make sure the master passes QC. If that is not your area, get someone in charge of it; do not be a lone wolf.
Things break: a graphics card, a scan, an EDL turned over with a thousand vfx shots and a change of heart; will you redo it by hand [1:08:28]?
In a typical DI or post budget the grade is one component and the deliverables are a larger financial one [1:11:44]. A small boutique that takes a monster series without a facility partner "will kill you" [1:12:16]. Plan who does the conform and deliverables before taking the job.
Cullen Kelly agrees: the last impression is the lasting one [1:12:46].

**Use it:** The pre-render checklist includes a named owner for conform, QC and delivery specs before work starts.
