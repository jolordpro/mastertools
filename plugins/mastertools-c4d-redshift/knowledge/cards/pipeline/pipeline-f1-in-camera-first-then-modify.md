Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour) believes visual effects should rely on a variety of techniques that the edit cuts between so the audience stays on its toes [04:43-05:14].
Real operators tracking real action give a foundation; VFX then adds or removes things from that footage [05:14-05:45].

**Use it:** Planning principle: shoot or capture a real base wherever possible and put CG on top.
