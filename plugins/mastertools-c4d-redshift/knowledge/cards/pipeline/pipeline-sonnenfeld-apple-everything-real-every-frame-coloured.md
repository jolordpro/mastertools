Sonnenfeld says Jobs did not want visual effects trickery: phone screens were real, not comped in as a competitor did, and every frame was graded perfectly [26:29-27:34]. Product reveals ran under secrecy ("double black") and the team did photoreal CG, such as the watch before it existed, along with keynotes and making-of films [28:35-29:36].

**Use it:** For product or hero-object work, ask what must be shown real. A cheated screen or reflection is the thing a perfectionist client spots first.
