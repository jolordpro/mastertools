Brady says for episodic and streaming work captured shots can be nearly final, but he is a perfectionist about eye direction and eye animation: capture is decent, yet rock-steady focus or darting around a room needs cleanup [11:33, 12:03]. He puts animators back in control of the camera to push silhouette and poses, so overall: about 60-70% from capture and about 30% cleanup by his team [12:33]. Where it does not fit, such as a squirrel chasing an acorn across the sky, animators go ahead by hand, though he sometimes puppeteers just the timing for first blocking [13:04, 13:35]. At the end of the panel he repeats about a 60% head start on lip-sync and eye darts from face capture [42:10].

**Use it:** Hand-off rule of thumb for any automated first pass: plan for roughly a third of hands-on finishing, and name which parts (eyes, silhouette) always need a human.
