DP Erik Messerschmidt says the show gave directors twelve fixed camera angles inside the car, with tilt, pan and lens height previsualised [19:01-19:32]. A floor grid registered both car and camera in the same space, and plates were shot for those exact angles, so post only dragged them in with no skewing or de-warping [19:32-20:34].
He presents the limit as creative: visual style built on restraint, with a limited target to work within [19:01].

Transfer: a fixed shot list with locked lens, height and angle makes plate, render and comp line up first time.

**Use it:** Lock a small camera set per setup before building backplates or CG; match every plate to a named angle instead of one wide plate to be re-fitted.
