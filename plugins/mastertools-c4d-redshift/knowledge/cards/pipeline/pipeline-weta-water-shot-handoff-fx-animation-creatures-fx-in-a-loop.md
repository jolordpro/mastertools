Order he describes: (1) effects makes a low-res river simulation around the bridge; (2) animation uses it to place the character in the water; (3) the creatures team runs the body and hair simulation with hair referencing the water flow; (4) effects takes the final high-res hair back and simulates the water draining from the saturated fur each time he lifts an arm [07:50-08:51]. He says it took a lot of coordination and communication to keep efficient [08:20].

**Use it:** When one shot depends on two sims, schedule an explicit handoff loop and set the order, resolution at each step and who owns the returns.
