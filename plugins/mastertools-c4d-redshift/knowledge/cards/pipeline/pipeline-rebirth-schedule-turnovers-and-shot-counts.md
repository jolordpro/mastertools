Vickery gives 1,500 shots total, the most of any Jurassic film (the last had 1,250), with ILM doing nearly 1,200 (caption garbled, unsure) across London, San Francisco, Vancouver and Mumbai with north of 600 artists [40:18]. About 100 more came from a second vendor, plus an in-house team (Midas VFX) and Territory and Blind for graphics [40:32]. The film began mid-March 2024 and delivered at the start of May 2025: roughly 11 weeks of prep, 13 of shoot and 30 of post [41:34, 42:06]. During the shoot they had to turn over about 150 VFX shots, which Edwards found painful because it ate the planning time for the next day [41:34].
