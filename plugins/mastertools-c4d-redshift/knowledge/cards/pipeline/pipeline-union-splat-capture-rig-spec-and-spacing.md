ClearAngle's volumetric capture rig is a half dome with 40 machine-learning cameras, genlocked, with built-in LED lighting that can be dialled flat and neutral [09:23]. Capture volume is about 3 m a side for pin-sharp focus [10:27]. To use people as separate individuals later, capture at most two or three with space between them, because occlusion hurts [10:27]. Groups that push, hug and dance can be captured together for believable interaction but cannot be separated afterwards [10:58]. Takes run 60, 90 or 120 seconds, limited only by storage [24:20]. A cyclist on static rollers was moved through the CG scene by estimating the speed he would have travelled and applying it to the splat [13:31].

**Use it:** use as the capture checklist when planning any splat or volumetric crowd shoot: spacing, take length, lighting.
