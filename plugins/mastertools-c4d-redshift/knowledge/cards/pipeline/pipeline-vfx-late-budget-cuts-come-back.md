Jay Worth says the roach finale was reduced to save money, then found not to work, so very late the vendor was asked for 20 to 50% more roaches in half the sequence [10:25-11:27]. The vendor delivered. His working habit is to plan as much as possible at the start, then expect the sequence to shift once the story needs it [10:25].

**Use it:** estimator should carry a contingency line on the hero sequence rather than trimming it to hit a number.
