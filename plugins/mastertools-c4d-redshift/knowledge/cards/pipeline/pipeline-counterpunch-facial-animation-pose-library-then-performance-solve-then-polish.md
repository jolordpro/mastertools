Josh Hartman (head of animation) says once the rig arrives, animators make character-specific pose libraries (sometimes from scratch, sometimes from reference to nail likeness) that can be pushed or pulled toward realistic or stylised [00:02-00:32].
Those libraries drive the process: facial performance capture software produces an initial solve, which gets to the polish phase faster than hand-keying; polish is where most animator time goes, checking each control on a hybrid rig and adding realism by hand [00:32-01:34].
A good pose library means the solve is closer to final; skilled animators take it to the client's direction and reference [01:04-01:34].

**Use it:** Face pipeline order: rig, pose library, solve, polish; budget the polish pass.
