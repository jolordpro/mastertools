One shot's route as told by each department [00:02-26:47]: story artist boards from script pages and pitches them in a story room (the fishbowl) doing the voices [02:07]; art director's colour script syncs everyone's imagination, built from reference trips and sent to the director who reviews it, with many "final finals" [03:44-05:20]; set dressing builds ground only as far as needed plus cheap set extensions and sky paintings, keeping 100-shot sequences consistent, and judges how much detail blur will hide [06:24-07:56]; layout pitches alternate camera setups [09:34]; animation, then character sim, then FX, then lighting, then Lightspeed optimisation [11:13-25:44]. Everyone's updates are checked in for the nightly render that starts around 19:00 and the team looks at it together next morning to see what looks great or what broke [17:52].

**Use it:** A model for a sports package: nightly renders reviewed together each morning, each department checks in before the cut-off. Dressing is cut to what blur and camera will show.
