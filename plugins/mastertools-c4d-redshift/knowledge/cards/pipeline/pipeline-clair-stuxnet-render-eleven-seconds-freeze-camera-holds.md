For the Stuxnet piece he had about three weeks for roughly four minutes of animation and no time to render a minute of 3D, so he rendered about 11 seconds and let the camera freeze at different points so shots could hold on a frame [20:00-20:33].
He also screen-captured the wireframe view rather than working out how to render a wireframe pass [20:33-21:04].
He says he made the piece largely by himself in about three weeks [28:31].
Transfers: design holds and freezes into the edit so a short render covers long screen time, and grab viewport captures for technical-looking screens.

**Use it:** When a render budget is tight, plan holds and freezes at storyboard stage so a short render covers more screen time; capture wireframe or viewport views instead of rendering them.
