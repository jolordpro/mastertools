The old route of roto artist, then paint, then compositor is gone because companies outsource roto and paint [50:46]. Juniors must come in as compositors who already do roto well (not everything is outsourced), paint, and CG compositing, and know the software in and out [51:17, 51:49]. A compositor does not need Python; he has worked about 20 years without it and asks the TD department when needed [49:14, 49:45]. A lighting TD role does need solid maths and usually a related degree [48:44]. Interview from 2019.

**Use it:** Tells the coach what a junior can be expected to cover when sizing roles for a job; a generalist covers comp, roto and paint.
