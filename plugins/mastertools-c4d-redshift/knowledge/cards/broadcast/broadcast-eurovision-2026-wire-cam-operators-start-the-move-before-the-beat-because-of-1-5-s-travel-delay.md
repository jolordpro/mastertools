Kai and team fly a 2D wire cam on the stage centre axis: top speed about 7 m per second, rail about 56 m, drop down to 30 cm from a performer's face to an extra wide 50 m back [13:11-13:43]. System delay: roughly 1.5 seconds to move 10 m down above the floor, so the pilot anticipates the musical cue and starts a little before the beat [14:14]. Three people make one shot (pilots plus a remote head operator doing pan, tilt, roll, zoom and manual focus) [14:45-15:47]. Cine lens would need a separate focus puller; the broadcast lens allows self focus.

**Use it:** when designing graphics around live camera moves, expect the move to begin before the musical cue; build tolerance.
