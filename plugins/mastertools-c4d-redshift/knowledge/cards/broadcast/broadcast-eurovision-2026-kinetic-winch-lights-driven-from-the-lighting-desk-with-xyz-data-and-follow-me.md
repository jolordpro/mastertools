Lighting designer [19:59-22:36]: the winches could have been run by the kinetic company, but they chose the lighting desk because it takes XYZ data. Almost nothing is programmed with pan and tilt apart from key lights. Position feedback (PSN data) lets a coordinate be tracked in space while a winch moves. The key-light operator can take one winch light at any time as backlight or key and follow a performer ("follow me"). Lights are used twice as much as people think, e.g. simple backlights around steadicams. A semi-final 2 performance was a ballet of winch lights.

**Use it:** the idea of position-driven rather than angle-driven lights echoes camera-tracked virtual sets.
