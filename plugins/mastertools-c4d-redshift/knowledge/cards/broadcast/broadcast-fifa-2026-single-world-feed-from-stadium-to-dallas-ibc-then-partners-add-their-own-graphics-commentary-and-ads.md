A beginner channel (Manuel Gray, July 2026), so low confidence and no sourcing.
- Chain: field cameras and mics, stadium compound (routing, power, comms, backup), fibre network to IBC Dallas from 16 venues, then media partners [05:09-06:44]. He says 45 cameras per match and "7 terabits per second" on the fibre; satellite is only the backup [06:44-08:47]. Treat numbers as unverified.
- IBC teams: replay, graphics (line-ups, score, stats), audio, shading (matching cameras in open stadiums where sun and shade differ), VAR support, QC, distribution [09:18-11:51].
- Partners get the same pictures: one broadcast error showed on every network. They add commentary in many languages, studio shows, analysis, their own ads and graphics; half-time and hydration-break material is theirs [11:55-14:57]. Versions for TV, phone, vertical social [14:57-15:28].

**Use it:** broadcast graphics packages split into the world feed layer (rights holder) and the local layer (network): know which one you deliver.
