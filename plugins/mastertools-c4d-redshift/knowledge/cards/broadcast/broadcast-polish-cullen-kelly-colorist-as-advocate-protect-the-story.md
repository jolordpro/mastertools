He calls himself a creative advocate for the image's author, not its creator. The grade should protect and lift the story and draw the viewer in without distracting. He translates a director's or DP's intent into visuals with colour theory, close attention to detail, intuition and empathy.

**Use it:** A finishing note is judged by whether it serves the shot's intent; a flashy grade that pulls attention fails the test.
