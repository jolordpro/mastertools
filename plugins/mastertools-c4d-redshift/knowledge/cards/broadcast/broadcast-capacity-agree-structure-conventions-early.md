Melissa (Capacity art director) said the tricky part was being overwhelmed by choices: many modifiers, many ways to lay out the outliner, many ways to mask [1:49:18]. The questions were who builds it which way and what the team's best practice is [1:49:50]. They found a happy medium late and admitted it was not necessarily best. The second hard stretch was late bug-fixing of things built wrong [1:48:47]. Renders of 3D scenes that took ten seconds made fast iteration possible [1:54:33].

**Use it:** For any multi-artist toolkit, write the layer, naming and mask conventions on day one, before the second artist starts.
