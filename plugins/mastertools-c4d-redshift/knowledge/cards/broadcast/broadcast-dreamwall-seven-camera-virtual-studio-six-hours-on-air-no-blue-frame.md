Baras says their 400 square metre virtual studio runs seven cameras on seven Reality engines, and a separate 40 square metre weather set with two cameras produces over 25 shows a day [03:49-04:24]. For Europa League nights they are on air from about 6 pm to midnight or 1 am; he states no blue or frozen frame in five or six years [22:56-24:02]. A client asked for a backup solution during Euro 2020 and he could not provide one [24:02].
This is the vendor-side partner's own claim, so treat it as low confidence. Useful as a reliability benchmark for how long a live real-time build is trusted to run.

**Use it:** Use as a reference point when someone asks how long a real-time engine setup should run unattended, and flag the lack of a failover in any bid.
