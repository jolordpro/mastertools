Spike Shakuni of ESPN on winning the 2017 Sports Emmy for post-produced graphics, the third year running [00:02-01:05]: the team receives the story and content, then asks what the story is and what the graphic should look like; they ideate for a couple of days, and over the weekend people put in long hours so the pieces are ready for Monday; graphics must be delivered by 3 pm Monday because the production truck needs them by then. He credits the whole team.

**Use it:** Real turnaround rhythm for a weekly show: ideation midweek, build over the weekend, hard delivery before the truck loads in.
