Speaker (Popup Media 360) calls this a key factor in making AR/XR look real [10:04-11:35]: virtual lights must affect the real set, and real lights must affect the virtual one. They rebuilt the venue lighting layout inside Unreal, controlled it through Zero Density, and exchanged DMX with the lighting console so both sides stayed in step the whole show.
He also names the next tools: a new EasyTrack box with a Swan tracker and a lens calibration and profiling tool, because lens calibration is key to virtual production [11:35-12:06].

**Use it:** If a Redshift/C4D look plate will be composited into real LED sets, plan the light match both directions early, not only virtual onto real.
