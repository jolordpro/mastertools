Baras on projects that add AR to a real match [47:37-1:11:05]. A second row of LED boarding can be virtual while the first row is real. The same pitch banners can be swapped by region or sponsor, and virtual screens can show replays or giant player images. During empty-stadium Covid matches a virtual stand and virtual fans filled the gaps. A virtual carpet, a mascot animal and score or lineup graphics on the pitch were all used.
A Charleroi job overlaid animated ads on fixed panels, and he admits the client piled on too much [57:19]. Tennis in Belgium got a full virtual stand, virtual banners, a virtual ad on the net and scoring effects [1:03:58-1:06:10]. A Belgian national team training ground became a virtual stadium as a demo (no real players allowed to be shown) [1:07:21]. His pitch: small leagues and minor sports cannot get a full stadium, so adding stands and partner space raises visibility and credibility [1:09:30].
Virtual ad revenue was a side effect: virtual advertising was not the first aim of the Charleroi system, but partners asked for more space [51:19].

**Use it:** Pitch stadium AR to smaller clubs and leagues as a sponsor-space tool first, and keep clutter limited: one virtual row, not every surface.
