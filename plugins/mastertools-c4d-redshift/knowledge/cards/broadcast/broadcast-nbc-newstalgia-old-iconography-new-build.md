NBC Sports art director Chad Hudson and designer Alex Suarez, with Tendril Studio and loyalkaspar, described bringing the NBA back after two decades by leaning into the iconography of the league's 1990s NBC era while making it current. The goal was a package that lands with fans who remember the original and those who only heard about it [§2].

**Use it:** When reviving a look, keep the recognisable symbols and rebuild everything else; test on someone who never saw the original.
