3Play takes 8 channels in and 2 out; one output can feed the in-venue screens and the other the stream [21:37]. It records everything live, so alternate angles of a play are available [21:37]. Highlight clips are cut into chunks for the break roll-out: four or five slow-motion clips before commercial are all replays held in the system [22:07] [22:37].
Plays are labelled with player and team, letting you pull lists later, such as every 10-yard catch or a player's runs over 20 yards, for recap shows [22:37] [23:08]. Coaches use it to review multiple angles [23:38] [25:12].
