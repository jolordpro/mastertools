Bodega's creative director started brainstorming a few weeks after finishing the Super Bowl XLIX open, challenging the team to think differently [§1]. The concept was an inside look at the set with a superstar and NFL players, peeling back the curtain [§1]. Dixon says the open sets the stage for in-game intensity in a fun, unexpected way [§1]. Zoic combined the kinetic stage graphics with live-action footage of the performance, led by an executive creative director and a VFX supervisor [§1]. The open debuted in a game watched by nearly 27 million viewers [§1].

**Use it:** For a recurring show open, start the next year's idea immediately after the last and ask what behind-the-scenes angle the audience has not seen.
