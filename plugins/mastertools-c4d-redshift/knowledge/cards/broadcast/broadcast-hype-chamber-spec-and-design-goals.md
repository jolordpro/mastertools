Goals: artist-friendly methods, full suite of primetime assets, content that is easy to update for an open tournament, and a reconfigurable stage [03:22-04:25].
Specs: HD for Twitch at 59.94, real-time ray-traced reflections, show targeted a 3090-class PC; sample also runs on weaker machines. Team stayed on 4.27 even after UE5 early access to avoid disturbing the schedule [02:18-04:55].
