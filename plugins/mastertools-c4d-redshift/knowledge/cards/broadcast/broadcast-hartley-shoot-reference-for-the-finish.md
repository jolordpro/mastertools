Hartley's plan was a photographer in locker rooms getting every player in fun poses with dramatic lighting, as reference for illustrators. The image each artist works from informs the direction of the whole look [§2]. COVID protocol blocked individual shoots, so all networks shared one league shoot, some teams (Philadelphia) shot only new players, and graphics producer Danielle Stocki pulled photos from the NFL shoot and Getty, often joining a head from one photo to a body from another [§2]. Hartley says the next step is photos Fox takes specifically for the purpose, which would let them push a heroic, stylised look further [§3].

**Use it:** Reference decides the ceiling of the finish. For a hero still, light and pose the reference for the final look instead of patching mismatched sources.
