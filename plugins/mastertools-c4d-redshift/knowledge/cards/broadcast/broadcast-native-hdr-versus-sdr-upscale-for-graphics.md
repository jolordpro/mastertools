Girraphic [14:04-15:42]. Heard second-hand, not data: Amazon's testing found HD in HDR changes the perceived quality more than 4K in SDR, so Thursday Night Football is all HDR. Typical graphics today render SDR and upconvert to HDR via a LUT or hardware converter. Unreal can render native HDR, giving a chance at true-HDR graphics, but artist workflow and monitoring must be rethought.

**Use it:** Flag in a delivery spec whether graphics are native HDR or SDR-upconverted; do not treat the claim about HDR over 4K as established.
