Gil Haslam (Troika Design Group) at the SVG forum, Feb 2016 [00:02-02:05]: the work covered brand strategy through every component, including the logo mark, for the first time top to bottom; the team had to think about each property and how the package would live. Launch point: Super Bowl 50 in Santa Clara. NFL games shared the black-and-gold look first; the following week it went to college basketball, golf at Pebble Beach and others, with more to come.
Reception reported by him: leadership proud, the in-house designers felt ownership, fans said it felt future-facing [02:05-03:06]. He is a vendor describing his own work.
