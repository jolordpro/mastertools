The Verge, Fox NFL [03:44-05:48, 12:08-12:39]. The director decides and talks continuously: 'ready one, take one, ready two, take two'. The call tells the camera operator to settle (no swish pan as they go to air) and the TD to be ready to cut at a precise moment, so cuts can be very exact. Director and TD speak in shorthand after years together. 'You get one shot' with no edit, which is why everyone aims for a clean product: mistakes air to millions, though viewers often do not notice them. The producer is sold replays and angles by short, intent voice calls from the replay and camera operators; trust comes from track record.

**Use it:** For on-air graphics, expect the same protocol: ready cue, take, and no retakes, so graphics need to be stable on first use.
