He says the pipe did not grow going from HD to 4K, so 4K to 8K means compressing four times as much for no visible gain, and at home you cannot tell an HD pixel from a 4K one [03:09-05:44].
Even in a theatre, 20/20 vision struggles to see a 4K projector pixel at a 50 to 60 degree view angle [04:42].
Camera lenses barely resolve 6K, and he asks what the point is if compression is visible before the pixel is [05:44-06:46].
Streaming services cut rates for everyone during the 2020 lockdown because capacity could not serve the demand (caption says 2019) [07:48-08:21]; he expects more content, not better content, for the same pipe.
Transfers: fine hairlines, grain and tiny type are first to die in a broadcast encode.

**Use it:** Design for how the picture survives delivery encoding: test fine detail through the actual delivery encode, not the master.
