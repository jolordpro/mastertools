NFL RFID (2014): two tags per player, linemen carry a third, 24 ultra-wideband antennas, more than 300 tracked devices, positions updated 10 times per second [07:19-07:49]. Sky cam uses real-time 3D tracking to keep the virtual field aligned [08:20]. Brooklyn Nets: 110 Canon cameras around the arena produced volumetric players in seconds, where earlier golf captures took days [08:51-09:23]. Disney's Toy Story game: tracked data plus a motion-trained animation model piped into Unity with a 20-second delay from the live broadcast [09:53-10:54].
