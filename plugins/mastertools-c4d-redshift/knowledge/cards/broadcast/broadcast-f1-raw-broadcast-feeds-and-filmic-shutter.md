Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour): the director chose to lean on broadcast cameras (about 30 around any track) because no film crew could place that many [03:38-04:13].
Apple had a team at each race capturing the cameras' raw data streams before they were downgraded for broadcast, giving higher quality as a start [04:13-04:43].
Broadcast shutters are very skinny so ads stay readable; after tests they negotiated a more filmic shutter for certain races, and the 50p material needed conversion to 24 with extra motion blur [05:14-06:16].
Transfers: for sports packages that mix live feed and graphics, check shutter and frame rate before matching blur.

**Use it:** Estimator and comp note: broadcast sources arrive with short shutter; plan for added motion blur when converting to 24p.
