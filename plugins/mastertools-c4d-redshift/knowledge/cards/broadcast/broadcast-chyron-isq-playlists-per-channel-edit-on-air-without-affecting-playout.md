Gonzalez demo [09:30-13:40]:
- ISQ imports the newsroom rundown and keeps each channel's graphics as its own playlist; installs run up to eight channels, from two hardware shuttle boxes or from an automation system [09:30-10:01].
- Selecting a story pre-stages it in preview and shows what is next; channel 1 and channel 2 play independently [10:01-10:33].
- Prime's editor stays open, and changes there do not touch what is on air [10:33-11:04]. Transitions between graphics are built into the graphics; macros and GPIs (tally workflow) also fire [11:04-11:36].
- A crawl runs on another layer fed by a separate crawl product, while the main graphics keep cycling [11:36-12:37].
- Right-click a graphic to edit it in its scene, even out of order, or move it to another channel live [13:08-13:40].

**Use it:** Graphics that need late changes should be editable in place and layered (crawl separate), so one fix does not require a new rundown.
