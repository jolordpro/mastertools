Each of three virtual cameras gets up to eight camera paths, defined by an A point and a B point; speed is seconds per path; bounce and loop buttons control repeats; three smoothing modes are linear, ease at both ends, and sudden start with soft stop [27:01-33:55]. Use the tool's own undo icons, not ctrl-Z, while editing a path [33:55]. Hold ctrl or shift while moving the camera for fast or slow motion [34:25]. Switching camera or path restarts it from its start; it does not carry over position [29:14].
Preview output can show the program, or a matrix of all three cameras; inactive previews are drawn at low resolution to save CPU. That lets the operator change a path on a camera not on air before cutting to it [27:41-28:43]. Billboards default to the same-numbered camera, but a visibility box lets one camera show two billboards, for example two guests from different locations placed in one set [35:27-37:32].
Live use: map camera buttons to keyboard keys, GPIO, MIDI or DMX with right-click [39:08-40:29].

**Use it:** Borrow the pattern: pre-set several camera moves, preview the next off air, and map the cut buttons to hardware.
