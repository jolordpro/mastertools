Inhofer [19:27-21:03] names two shifts: colour management has fully matured from camera to finishing (so affordable systems produce amazing images), and HDR changes the canvas, "watercolor to oils".
Dolby Vision's point is to deliver one full dynamic range master, which is then optimised for each viewing device and so gets as close as possible to what the filmmaker saw [21:03].
He also notes that a laptop can now do the fundamentals of work once needing a million-dollar room, apart from 8K or stereo real-time [17:51-18:54].

**Use it:** The delivery notes say to grade and archive the widest master and treat device versions as derived trims.
