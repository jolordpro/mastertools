Unreal's default outliner sorts alphabetically and nests badly, a problem when broadcast scenes reach about 1,000 objects [24:12-24:43]. Fixes shown: filters (text, lights, cameras), colour layers that parent-swap children, middle-click solo, quick icons for modifiers, materials and animation, and animating on nulls grouped with Ctrl-G [24:43-26:19].
A batch renamer (prefix, suffix, search and replace) exists because sports scenes have many 'player 1, 2, 3' items [26:19-26:50]. Copying settings by dragging from one item to another was 'coming next' [25:46].

**Use it:** Name sports template objects consistently (home/away, player slots) and group under nulls so a data feed or batch tool can find them.
