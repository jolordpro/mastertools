Winters says the best way to stay on schedule is to build a padded one, with more truck rehearsal days than you may need. Do the big milestones first since they inform all other design choices, and hold regular set meetings with execs and freelancers for a feedback loop [§2].

**Use it:** Book extra rehearsal days up front; lock the biggest design decisions before the small ones.
