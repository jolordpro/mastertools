At the 2026 SVG GFX Forum, ESPN's Brian Girardin and Lisa DiNello, with Two Fresh Creative and Compadre, described a college-football package built for more than 130 teams and more than 100 games each weekend, with a projected lifespan of five seasons. The identity is rooted in the pageantry and energy of the sport (helmets, gloves, crowd electricity) and scalability stayed at the centre of every decision [§1].
