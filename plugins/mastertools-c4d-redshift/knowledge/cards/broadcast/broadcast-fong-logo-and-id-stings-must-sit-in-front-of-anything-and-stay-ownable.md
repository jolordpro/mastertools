Fong says the logo brief is the ultimate case of making something ownable in the shortest time; durations are typically 2, 3, 5 or 12 seconds depending on use [1:00:21-1:00:51].
The sting has to translate to a theatrical lead-in, a trailer blip or opening an app and give a burst of beauty fast [1:00:51-1:01:22].
She judges a project successful if it could not run for another brand or story, which she also applies to titles [1:01:22-1:01:53].
Unlike a title, a logo must feel right in front of anything from a serious film to a loony animation, so it needs a breath of neutrality [1:02:24].
She says most of the studio's work is not title sequences [59:18].

**Use it:** For a channel or show ID, test the same sting against three very different programmes, then ask what in it is unmistakably this brand.
