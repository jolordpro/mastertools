The Verge, Fox NFL director [04:47-05:48, 07:50-08:23]. Live cameras are pushed to get all the subtleties of both sides: quarterback reaction, receiver isolation, coach, sideline. Some of the best shots are a locked camera on a player on the bench who has just cost his team a game. He asks his crew not to sell reactions where a player is acting for the camera; he wants wink, smile, helmet tap, the defensive back with his head down. The goal stated for the whole crew: tell the story and give producers the tools to do it.

**Use it:** Informs shot-selection logic for highlight packages and graphics tie-ins: favour unposed emotional beats.
