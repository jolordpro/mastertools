Capacity defined team colour chips that are the only source of colour for any element [20:15]. The material designer's edge-colour layer references a chip by one of nine preset edge positions, or custom coordinates for more [37:54-38:24]. Because slot 'left edge' must suit every team, the chips were tuned so any two teams still look right side by side, which was a puzzle [38:55]. The real-world luxury here was being allowed to change team colours to make matchups work [20:47].

**Use it:** For a team-package toolkit, assign roles (slot names) to colours rather than hard-coding team values, and test every pairing in a versus screen before locking a palette.
