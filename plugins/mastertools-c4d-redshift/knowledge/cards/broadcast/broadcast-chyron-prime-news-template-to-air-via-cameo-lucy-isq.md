Juan Gonzalez (Chyron), NAB 2022 vendor demo, so a sales view. Four hand-offs, in order:
- Designer builds a scene in the Prime editor from layers (text, clip, 3D, video DVE); every object is keyframeable and the effects column holds crawls, blur, auto-spacing, masks [00:37-01:41]. Prime 4.5 added more tools [01:41].
- Designer right-clicks the template and uploads it to a named look in Cameo (asset server); a sync service called Hub Drive copies folders to every device, so no one carries files by hand [02:43-03:46].
- Producer opens the template inside the newsroom system (ENPS shown) through the Lucy plug-in, edits it, and sees an auto preview of look and animation [03:46-05:20].
- Rundown goes to the director's ISQ software, which splits graphics into playlists per channel [09:30].
Stylesheets let a designer reskin a template without rebuilding it [02:12]. Chyron also offers free training and a licence through its academy [02:12].

**Use it:** Model a template pipeline as design, publish, fill, play, with one named hand-off between each, and note that the template author chooses what the producer can touch.
