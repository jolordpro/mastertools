Camera operators sit up top so they see the whole field; for the ESPN game one camera sits at each 30-yard line to cover half the field each, and to work with the telestration software so the line of scrimmage and first-down lines are not too distorted [06:39-07:09].
FingerWorks: a computer draws the lines; once you see the camera (usually the main one) you pick the tool and draw; if a line isn't visible you select a mask and redo it [05:22-06:01].
The 'Madden cam' operator uses a Ronin 4D gimbal on the sideline [08:13]; pylon cams (Marshall cube cams with SDI and power) give red-zone beauty and goal-line replay angles [17:10-17:41].

**Use it:** When designing sports graphics that sit in the field of play, plan camera position first; lines stretch with angle.
