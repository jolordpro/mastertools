DuppyAnimations, uploaded 2024-09-03; a re-creation of an ident for a news channel with eight segments (main news, local, international, health, business, sports, entertainment) sharing one layout.
- Parts: world map traced in Photoshop (magic wand, work path from selection), exported to Illustrator, extruded; a Wrap deformer to spherical (width 400, height 300 and radius 200) bends it onto a sphere; atom-array shell, Helix swept with a rectangle, tubes and discs for rings [01:08-09:09, 18:52-21:29].
- Materials are legacy: luminance, reflection with roughness off, glass at refraction 1.24 and a thicker one at 13 [13:25-17:50]; compositing tag with reflection off for the atom array; ambient occlusion in effects [16:39-17:50].
- Animate the sphere rotation, then set F-curve tangents to straight lines so the globe turns at constant speed instead of easing in and out [24:06-25:15]. Cameras are keyed separately [22:29-23:36].
- Rendered as a sequence, then arranged in After Effects: same dotted world map background, only the colour changes per programme [25:48-26:51].

**Use it:** Shows the "one build, recoloured per show" ident system; the technical steps are basic.
