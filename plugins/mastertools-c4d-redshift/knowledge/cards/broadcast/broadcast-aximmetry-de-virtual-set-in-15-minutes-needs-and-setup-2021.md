Jamie Pence (Videobred), an Aximmetry customer, in an Oct 2021 demo. Gear listed [00:38-02:13]: a camera with SDI or HDMI out, an edit PC with a good CPU and NVIDIA GPU plus a Blackmagic or AJA card, a green screen, and the free DE trial. Shoot was 1080 into a DeckLink Duo 2.
Order [02:13-06:31]: download a pre-built Unreal set (he quotes roughly 56-59 to 200 dollars for paid ones, plus a free large screen studio), open its Unreal project with the Aximmetry-DE Unreal version, accept the prompt to update the virtual camera, run File > Cook Content for Windows, close Unreal, open the Aximmetry project, choose the output (DeckLink output 4, 1080 29.97 progressive).
He suggests shooting talent in portrait to gain keyed resolution but shot landscape [10:19].
