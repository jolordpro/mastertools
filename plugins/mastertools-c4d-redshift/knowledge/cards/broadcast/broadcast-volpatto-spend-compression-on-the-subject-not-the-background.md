Volpatto argues that most shots have one point of attention, yet the same compression is applied across the frame, spending bits on a glass behind him instead of his face [09:24-10:26].
His proposal is a smarter encoder that compresses the subject less on purpose; the host notes newer compression specs aim at similar AI-driven prioritisation [10:26-10:57].
He gives the eye as only a two to three degree window in focus, even on a 100 inch TV [10:57-11:28].
Idea only; not a standard he is certain exists.

**Use it:** Put the sharpest, cleanest detail on the subject and let background detail go; it is what the eye and any smart encoder will keep.
