New MNF logo built in four versions: an executive version, a black-on-black "phantom", a white-on-white "storm", and alternates that sit on the "battle mesh" fabric texture [05:54][06:27]. The Countdown show got its own logo in the same family, and the other studio shows got type treatments of the logo [06:27]. For team logos: one main treatment plus five more, six per team (the Rams example), so artists could pick across a spread when building finals [06:27][06:58]. Black-on-black and white-on-white were the two key looks; white-on-white also suited brighter shows [03:12]. Numbers are as heard in a whisper transcript.

**Use it:** a deliverable checklist for sports branding: dark, light and textured logo variants, and a fixed count of team treatments.
