From the same NAB 2022 demo [04:17-09:00]:
- Template fields can be free text or a designer-built pull-down (teams shown); changing one choice can change other templates too [04:48].
- Saved Items stores graphics a producer fills in before knowing where they will air (city names, politicians, locators) and can be searched by the typed text, so base templates are not rebuilt [06:23-07:24].
- A template can expose an image slot; the designer decides which lines and images are editable. A Compose Image tool gives a producer up to three layers (upload, move, scale) for things like a player comparison, and the labelled result becomes shared for the whole newsroom [07:24-09:00].
- Render Media outputs a graphic as a still or movie for social or an editor, with render flavours per category [05:52].

**Use it:** When designing a template, decide per field: free text, pull-down, or image slot, and give the producer a way to reuse filled graphics.
