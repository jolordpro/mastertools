Epic says esports company Blast made the walk-ups for the last Rocket League championships in Unreal 5.4: six teams, each with its own shapes, text, 3D graphics and material-designer motion graphics, previsualised in engine and then played out live [04:08]. The content was synchronised with DMX lighting and mapped to LED ribbon strips [04:38]. Epic presents it as design, iteration and live output in one tool [05:10].
This is Epic's account, not Blast's, and no timings or team sizes were given.
