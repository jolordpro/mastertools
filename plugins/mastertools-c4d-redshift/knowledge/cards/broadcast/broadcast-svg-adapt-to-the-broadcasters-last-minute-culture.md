Rapetskaya says her shop avoids stress by setting realistic deadlines, and accounts for clients whose culture is last-minute decisions. It is on the vendor to understand how the broadcaster works and wants things delivered, as the vendor is an extension of their team [§2].

**Use it:** Ask how a broadcaster actually decides and delivers before setting the schedule.
