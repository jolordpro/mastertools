A billboard casts a virtual shadow, but that alone does not root the feet [22:57-23:28]. The show AO option adds a small dark spot from the person's shape. Adjust: front offset (depth position), front scale (elongation), width, fatten (radius, keep modest), blur (raise it, or the outline shows) and opacity (keep subtle) [23:28-25:03].
Moving the billboard moves the shadow with it, and some crops make the person look raised. The lift parameter moves the person vertically without moving the shadow [20:54-21:55]. Other switches: look at camera (on by default, switch off for special camera paths), flip, cast shadow, receive shadows so a virtual graphic can shadow the host [21:55-22:57].
Transfers: the contact shadow under a keyed person is the first thing to check in any cheap virtual set.

**Use it:** In a virtual-set QC, test feet contact: AO spot soft and faint, lift raised until shadow and shoe meet.
