The invented league needed its own marks and players. Teams had varied silhouettes so they did not read as a row of shields [11:55], each with a brush script and pattern [12:27]. Players were illustrated to avoid a photo shoot, with three pillars: graphic geometric shading, rich friendly colour, and strong hairstyles, plus dot-texture and brush details [14:31-15:35]. A versus screen borrowed fighting-game references so star matchups feel personal [16:37-17:40]. Soccer was chosen over American football for fewer fixed notions and more room for player personas [1:44:05].

**Use it:** A hook for pitching a sports package: pick one cultural reference for the matchup screen and one detail layer (texture, brush) that sets the look apart.
