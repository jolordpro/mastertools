Pence's order for placing a live talent in a set [08:43-13:14]:
- Billboard source: switch off the test clip, set the input device and frame rate to match the capture (DeckLink, 1080 29.97) [09:13].
- Monitor on raw input, then Crop left, right and bottom to leave the talent over green [09:46-10:19].
- Key: pick the background colour, view the matte, clean noise with low cut and high cut [10:52]. He rates the keyer very highly, comparable to Ultimatte (opinion).
- Placement: his feet were cut off, so move the billboard up; then raise the shadow until it vanishes into the floor plane, drop it back until feet stay, then set shadow opacity and width [11:24-12:33].
- Last: brightness and saturation in the adjuster, toggle for before and after [12:33-13:03].

**Use it:** Same checklist applies to any live-keyed comp: crop first, key, matte clean, then ground the subject with a shadow.
