The concept ran about three months in development, then the network gave less than three months to build it. Graphics needed many programmed locks to move from one to another, using Vizrt's Client Slide Library. Content was built in Trio and played out through a custom front-end, so associate producers could still build content on laptops before arriving at the event. A custom app controlled both the clock and score and the information graphics. [§1]

**Use it:** When introducing a new graphic system, keep the existing authoring path and put the new rules in the playout layer.
