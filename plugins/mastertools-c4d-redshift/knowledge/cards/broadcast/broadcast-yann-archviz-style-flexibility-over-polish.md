Yann Dupont on arch viz [1:41:00-1:42:34]: grime at the base of buildings was rejected, as the client wanted clean "plasticky" CG, because the building should stand out from the picture. The route to success is flexibility: the client says the tiles should be pink or the wallpaper is wood, and you switch live.

**Use it:** build graphics with switchable materials/colour variants so client changes are minutes, not rerenders.
