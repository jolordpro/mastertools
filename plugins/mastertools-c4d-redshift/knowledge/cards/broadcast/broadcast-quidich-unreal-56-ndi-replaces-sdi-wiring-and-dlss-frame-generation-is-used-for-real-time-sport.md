Short talk by Shawn, lead technical artist at Quidich Innovation Labs (channel name; captions render it as Courage) [00:00-03:30]. For about two years his team struggled to use Unreal for sports broadcast [01:34]. For sport they use DLSS for optimisation and performance, and frame generation because everything is real time [02:06-02:36]. His line: film is content-driven storytelling, sports broadcast is data-driven storytelling, a good place for people with motion graphics and VFX skills [03:08].
