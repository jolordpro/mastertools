A basic cloner copies a shape in one line, a grid or a radial array, with spacing and a transform offset that accumulates per instance, live in the 3D view [19:21-20:25]. Effectors, which would push the clones procedurally as a volume passes through, were still in development [20:25-20:58].
BK's line: 3D motion graphics is largely designing these patterns and then diving in with a camera and depth of field [20:25].

**Use it:** When a client wants a pattern-driven look, note that the engine route had cloner but no effector yet in 2022; check current Motion Design mode before promising it.
