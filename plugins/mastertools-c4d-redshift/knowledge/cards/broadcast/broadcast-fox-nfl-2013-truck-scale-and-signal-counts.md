The Verge, inside the Fox NFL control room (2013) [01:06-03:12, 06:49-08:55]. Eight fibers link two trucks and move over 3,500 signals; about 150 to 200 people are credentialed for a weekend (stage managers, statisticians, runners, camera, TD, audio, utilities). Director: 15 cameras on a given Sunday, about 13 tape machines; three 53 ft trucks hold the show and a fourth 53 ft truck is only cable cam. The crew had done all 32 stadiums in 11 years. Replays: 11 EVS machines, most with four inputs and two outputs; operators know which machine to go to before the player is back in the huddle. Audio console: 96 dual-layer faders, 192 inputs.

**Use it:** Scale reference for what graphics must fit into: a live show with many inputs and no retakes. Numbers are one network in one season; treat as order of magnitude.
