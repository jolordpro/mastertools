Epic's David Grownick told the Capacity team early on not to worry about what Unreal could do, and to get the design right first in whatever software they liked [19:12]. They drew grayscale wireframes, then quickly prototyped the snappy motion, keyframes, easing and curves in After Effects as the baseline [18:11], then recreated a couple of hero graphics in the engine. Capacity said they ran into few real limits. Late in the job they skipped the AE step once the engine felt comfortable, since designing twice was double work [1:09:13].

**Use it:** Do the look and motion language in the comfortable tool first; port to the real-time or C4D build only after the design holds. Drop the prototype step once the build tool is as fast as the sketch tool.
