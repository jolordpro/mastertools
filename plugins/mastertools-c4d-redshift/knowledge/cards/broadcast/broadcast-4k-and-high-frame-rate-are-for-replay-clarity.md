The Verge, Fox NFL [05:48-06:49]. The head of remote-operations engineering says clarity of replay is the recurring request. 4K has about 10 times the pixels of 720p (the speaker says 10; the exact ratio is nearer 9), so replay can zoom into an area and keep detail. High frame rate cameras give more images per second so a replay zoomed on a foot shows the exact frame instead of a blur across frames. 2013 claims: the numbers are from a trade interview and may not match today's standards.

**Use it:** Context for why a graphics package may be asked to work over zoomed, slow-motion replay and over 4K frames.
