Fox hired production company Engine Room, which finds illustrators around the world. It took roughly a month to illustrate all players, with 20 to 50 artists at a time. The team prioritised players in games Fox was broadcasting, beginning with skill players and what Hartley calls defensive studs, then the rest as the graphics appeared in studio shows and games [§2-3]. The first rollout was the February Super Bowl, and the full-league plan was for the season [§2].

**Use it:** Stage a large asset set by airtime: finish the faces that will be seen first, then fill in the rest.
