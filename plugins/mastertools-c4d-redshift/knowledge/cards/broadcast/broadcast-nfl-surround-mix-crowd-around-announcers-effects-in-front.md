The Verge, Fox NFL senior mixer [08:23-10:34]. Aim: the viewer feels part of the crowd. In 5.1, crowd goes all around the surrounds and around the announcers in the centre, with the front left and right used for the effects mix, because the field of play never changes. He listens to five to eight things at once (director, producer, associate director) and mixes largely by feel, reacting to camera cuts: opening mics that suit the picture. He balances being close to sidelines against coaches who study broadcast tape for signals. Philosophy: let people do their jobs, 'let's not overdo it' in sound, tight pictures and graphics alike.

**Use it:** The mix side of a package: when graphics hit, sound design should respect a mix that already puts effects in the front.
