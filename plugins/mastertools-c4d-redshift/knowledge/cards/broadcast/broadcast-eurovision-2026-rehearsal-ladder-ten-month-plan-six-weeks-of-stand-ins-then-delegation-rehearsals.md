A member of the director's team [43:33-45:08]: the director's role grew from one person doing everything (2015) to four. Six weeks before arena they rehearse with stand-ins; then each delegation rehearses in turn and every moment is checked; planned for over ten months and still more could be done "if we had talked about this earlier". The culture is to find solutions rather than say "doesn't work". Team: 45 audio engineers [37:12]. Rotations: max six people on stage at once, devices pooled in rotations of six [31:30-37:42].
