Fox Sports runs its graphics under a single corporate vision, a gritty action-packed attitude, so NFL, MLB and NASCAR share one look.
Concepts go through extended rounds of review and revision overseen by Gary Hartley before an artist rolls the approved look across every element of a show, typeface included. (Older trade piece; titles and process may have moved on.) [§1-§2]

**Use it:** The coach can tell a Fox-style brief that the look is fixed first and then propagated; push for one decision on type and attitude before building elements.
