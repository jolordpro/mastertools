Design began as green screen (known from the Charlotte NASCAR set); after seeing early LED/XR demos they switched, fearing green would be obsolete on install [01:04][01:38]. Live constraint: only about two or three minutes during a tape roll to reset, so changing the whole environment in seconds matters [02:10]. Seven tracked cameras with Stype Spider; Stype's Unreal plug-in maps environments across three walls plus floor without seams and with matching colours [04:13][04:46]. A launcher (name garbled as "Erizos" in captions) opens the same Unreal project on 16 machines together [04:13]. Talent benefit: real lighting on them and interaction with AR [05:17].

**Use it:** shows constraints for live-sports virtual sets: switching speed, multi-machine launch sync, seam and colour matching.
