Speaker is David Grownick, Epic senior product specialist and ex Fox Sports real-time graphics, so vendor view. Operators save text to pages in a rundown and take the next; only the changed region animates (a top line update leaves the rest alone) [16:03-16:33]. Before, three separate levels (pop-up, talent area, bottom) were needed; now one level with tagged layers, with in/out animations per region plus a global out [16:33-17:34]. Improvements planned in later versions [17:34].

**Use it:** Design every sports graphic as independently updating regions with their own in, change and out.
