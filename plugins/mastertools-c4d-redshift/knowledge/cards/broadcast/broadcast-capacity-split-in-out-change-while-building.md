Each graphic needs separate in, out, loop and change animations so they can play one after another [1:38:25]. Capacity first designed and animated everything, then understood the choreography and split animations out, and in hindsight said those phases could have run together [1:39:26-1:41:29]. Seeing it all play back to back in the rundown was when it clicked [1:38:55].

**Use it:** Write the in/out/change split into the build plan for any broadcast graphic; do not animate one long clip and slice it afterwards.
Transcript support: Player-stats level has four sequences: in, change-out, change-in and out; Preview Next plays out, then in with the new data. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ty6etYkvaC8 [10:32-12:05])
