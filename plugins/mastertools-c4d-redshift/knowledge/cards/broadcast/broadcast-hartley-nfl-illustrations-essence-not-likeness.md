Fox's NFL player illustrations came from CEO Eric Shanks's idea that players are talked about like superheroes; the vibe migrated to a graphic novel look rather than a cartoon [§2]. Gary Hartley says the mission is to exaggerate features and make players bigger than life (no way Drew Brees is that buffed) [§3]. Players reacted well, Hopkins first thought his was a photo [§1], and Aaron Donald said they look like the guys [§3]. For next round Hartley wants Fox-controlled photos made for this, to push a more heroic, expressive graphic-novel look and capture the essence of who they are rather than a photographic representation [§3].

**Use it:** When a frame pushes stylisation, exaggerate the defining feature and check viewers still recognise the person.
