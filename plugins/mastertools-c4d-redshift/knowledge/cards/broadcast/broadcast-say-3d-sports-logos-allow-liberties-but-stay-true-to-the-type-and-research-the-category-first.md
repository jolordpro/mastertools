Say starts a logo with research: see the category's status quo, know trends so you can follow or subvert them, and understand what a client means [02:07-02:38]. He walked through Monday Night Football logos from 1973 to 2019, finding the trend of ever more extrude layers, then a pulled-back 2019 version that favours design plus production quality [02:38-04:42]. Brand guidelines forbid changing type or colour in flat use, but in 3D 'all bets are off': designers are trusted and clients mostly complain only if it looks bad [06:18-06:50]; still be true to the logo's intent and typography [06:50]. He expects future guidelines to include extrude rules [07:20]. Visible scratches, as in the NFL Sunday Night Countdown package, now signal a lived-in physical object because render expectations rose [05:47-06:18]. Reference packages he cites: FIFA 2018 (patterns and marble), NBA Finals (six extrude layers, several textures, gold trim) [05:13].
A Hyundai Sunday Night Kickoff car intro, same logo principles, pushed the company to Redshift for high-end car renders [29:13-29:44].

**Use it:** Use as a briefing checklist for broadcast package logos.
