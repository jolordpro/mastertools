The tape breakdown is the pre-rendered openings, closings, interstitials, bumpers, bugs and stock graphics, made in Los Angeles in After Effects, Illustrator, Photoshop, 3ds Max and Cinema 4D, then dubbed to D5 tape and distributed. The Duet breakdown is the live graphics generated on site on Chyron Duet character generators over those server elements [§1]. About 50 elements sit on server per program so the operator can add scores fast while the look, down to the typeface, holds [§2]. Piece is from 2009-era and may be out of date.

**Use it:** Design the stock kit as the identity, and leave live data slots flexible but locked to its type and look.
