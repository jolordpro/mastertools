SVG Sports Graphics Forum, ESPN designer [02:14-05:10]. One month of early development with a couple of concept designers before choosing an agency. ESPN works in 2 to 3 second animations, Elastic in a minute thirty, so Elastic delivered one 1:30 title sequence ('college football the movie') and ESPN set parameters (team IDs, match-ups, typography) so it could be segmented to feed all deliverables. Early look work: a team logo system in a machine-forged aged-metal look mixing transparency next to metal, rendered in Octane as 4K stills so they can punch in (a human hair detail was added to one logo for the first time); team-coloured fabric wrap accents with frayed edges beside polished metal for the inserts; hero mark frames in cold, heat and earth.

**Use it:** Pattern for package work: commission one long master piece with deliverable parameters, and plan the cut-down into short elements up front.
