Music mixer [41:26-43:33]: live sound only remotely resembles the studio record, so "make it sound like the record" is taken with a grain of salt, but the original is on a switch for an instant A/B with careful level matching. The team tries to stay within 1 dB of LU (his words, taken to mean 1 LU) so songs have equal chance; soft songs and metal tracks make that hard; dynamics of about 5 dB are normal in pop in the final mix. Mix runs on a console as a matrix with processing in Pro Tools automated to time code [39:20-40:22]. FOH engineer had 12 reverb engines for per-act settings [35:38].

**Use it:** compare to the EBU R128 card; the A/B habit transfers to comparing a graphic to its reference.
