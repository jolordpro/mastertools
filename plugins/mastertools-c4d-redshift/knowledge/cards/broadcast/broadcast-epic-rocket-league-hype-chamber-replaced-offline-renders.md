Epic worked with Rocket League and Capacity Studios on a hype-chamber environment treated like a major league sport: teams and clips swapped dynamically via Blueprints, aiming for ray tracing at 60 fps and live control through the remote API [11:53-12:57].
The target was to end the 'workflow nightmare' of offline rendering every match-up and clip. One lesson: they could not do 2D graphics effectively, which drove the Avalanche 2D work [12:25-12:57].
Packaged as a downloadable sample.

**Use it:** A data-swappable sports opener is the real-time alternative to rendering each fixture; check what must stay offline for quality.
