[10:36-12:11, 18:55-19:59] Lighting department grades the shots live: every country has a LUT (cold, warm, grungy, dirty). The lighting programmer fires LUT, exposure and shutter triggers in the show controller, which triggers a preset in the truck, which sets the cameras. Stage-ready/activation mode shows a LUT that makes beams look good, while the next performance LUT is applied in the background and swapped in at the start. ARRI built a version of its master setup unit that the show controller can drive; the senior vision engineer made custom setups: e.g. for Sweden, two changes: one at start and one inside the performance for laser lights (a specific shutter setting). Settings and look changes combine on the fly.

**Use it:** the same idea for broadcast graphics QC: per-segment look presets are applied by trigger, not by hand.
