A MOGRT seller explains [00:40-07:10] (name not given; shop is ballerfx.com in the captions, unverified):
- MOGRT format launched April 2017; it lets a motion designer hand editors a file they can edit without motion skills [00:08-00:40].
- Third-party effects are not embedded, so the editor needs the same plugins or it breaks [03:20-03:51].
- Creative Cloud Libraries accept files up to 1 GB, but even that loads slowly; under about 300 (units garbled, probably MB) is quick [04:35]. His largest, a basketball stats graphic, was 133 (MB), a text-only one is tiny, an image-containing starting pitcher about 4 MB, a title card 34 MB from its rigging [05:07].
- A property must be added to AE's Essential Graphics panel or the editor never sees it [05:37].
- Adobe Rush only runs MOGRTs built in Premiere [06:07].

**Use it:** Template checklist: no unlicensed or third-party-only effects, expose every control in Essential Graphics, and keep files small for library use.
