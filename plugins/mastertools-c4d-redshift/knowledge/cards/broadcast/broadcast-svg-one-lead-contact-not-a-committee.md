DiNapoli (drive studio) says to work to a consensus and one lead who speaks for the group and keeps it in the loop, because a committee gets out of hand with different opinions. With proper briefing, that person can give good feedback on what the team is seeing [§1]. Szykowny (ESPN) adds that a mutual-respect relationship, not a boss-and-vendor one, jells best [§1].

**Use it:** Ask the client for one named feedback owner before the first review.
