A Chyron product manager shows the feature [00:19-06:35]:
- A workflow action targets items (a media button, or several at once) with a type: add to program, or remove, tested by right-click then Test [01:53-03:28].
- Another type, Prime scene action play, calls a keyframe action by the exact name given in Prime Designer (zoom in, zoom out in the example) [03:28-04:29].
- Four actions then sit behind remotes in the ClickEffects network, assigned with a Workflow button, so one remote button can add a scene, zoom it, and remove it [04:59-05:33].
- Actions can also drive channels, or boards on a DVI system, and clips or audio [06:04].
The name must match, since the action is called by name.

**Use it:** Name animation states in the design tool with operator-friendly, unique names, because playout buttons call them by name.
