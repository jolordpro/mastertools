EventElevator behind-the-scenes, part 2, uploaded July 2026 (interviews with ORF, ARRI and crew).
- Decision: a more cinematic look; single large sensor gives shallower depth of field and a different colour processing; Alexa 35 Live first time on Eurovision [00:42, 06:26].
- Camera count: 28 (8 long lens on pedestals or tripods, 4 steadicam, 4 handheld, 3 track, 3 2D wire systems, 1 gimbal, 2 cranes, 3 PTZ) [09:04]. ARRI's hub watched 24 active cameras [18:28].
- Lenses: classic broadcast zooms for flexibility plus PL lenses on handhelds and green room [10:06].
- Approach: "each performance as a little music video"; look book from the lead colorist; an opera-like, theatrical rather than concert feel [07:30, 17:20].
- Result: 80 to 90 percent there; complex colour space; for music 100 percent successful, a LUT swap between takes can transform a performance [12:11-12:41].
- Over 50 LUTs predefined [02:47].

**Use it:** supports a look-per-segment approach for broadcast: a lookbook, then one LUT per piece.
