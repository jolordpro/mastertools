Layer Lemonade's list [03:10-05:48], from a Portuguese-language whisper transcript so some terms are inferred: bumper is a transition into or out of a break, about 5 s, reminding you what you watch; play off appears before ads and play on after; bug is the corner logo, static or animated; snipe is the lower box announcing what's next; swipe/transition between segments (e.g. a replay); cluster buster is a bundle of transitions and boxes inside a program; mortise is a small picture-in-picture composition; lower third carries a name or place; promo shows the next program; ident ends on the channel logo; packshot is end information with date and time; billboard is the sponsor card (this programme is brought to you by); classification shows the age rating. Channels hire one studio to keep a package consistent [01:33-02:38].
