Vendor explainer [0:01-2:39]: Showmaker is a browser-based rundown tool for what you can plan (pre-match analysis, opening graphics, half-time summaries, sponsor items), usable without a newsroom system. Mosart automates camera cuts, playout of clips and graphics; its new scoreboard panel lets operators edit names, scores, red cards and timers in an HTML control panel built by the user and updates show on air instantly [1:04-1:34]. Flowics (cloud graphics) takes live data over WebSocket or REST, or local scoreboard feeds via OCR tools or ScoreBridge, with integrations such as Stats Perform and Sportscast [1:34-2:06]. Works alongside Viz Pilot Edge. Marketing video; no latency or cost numbers given.

**Use it:** Reference architecture for sports graphics hand-off: plan, automate, then data-drive; pre-build graphics so a live scoreboard only needs data changes.
