Epic surveyed what broadcasters use real-time for; AR and virtual studios were a small part, bigger with studios added, while overlay graphics, data visualisation, animation and 3D packages took the larger share of time and money [09:12-09:42].
At a broadcaster summit about a year before (2021) with 30 of the largest global broadcasters, 65% were interested in 3D motion graphics with Unreal and 20% had already started [10:13-10:45]. For 2D motion graphics 81% said yes and 5% were trying without much success [12:57-13:30].
Context: BK (ex-Fox Sports design director, now Epic industry manager) and Andy Blondin (Epic product management) were presenting early Project Avalanche tools [00:36-01:39].

**Use it:** For sports packages, expect clients to ask for live-engine delivery of the same package; a 2D bar graphic is wanted in real time even more than 3D.
