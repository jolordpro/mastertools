Guest: Stefan Sonnenfeld. He starts in HDR when a show will not see theatre, asks how the look should read in all formats, and works in the widest gamut and highest resolution, using 16-bit EXR files [25:47-26:48].
The SDR version is derived from the HDR master with the better colour-management down-conversion boxes now in use; reviewers tell him every format looks "the same" in intent [24:14-25:47].
On Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 3 nine rooms ran at once, with all formats going at the same time [35:38-36:41].

**Use it:** Build the master in the widest space first and derive the smaller ones; check each derived format against the master's intent, not against a separate grade.
