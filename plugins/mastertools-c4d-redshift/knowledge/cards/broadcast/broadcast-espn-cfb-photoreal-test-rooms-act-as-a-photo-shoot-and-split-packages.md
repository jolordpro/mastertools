SVG Sports Graphics Forum, ESPN [04:18-07:38]. Concept: game DNA (short abstract transition moments such as a heart-beating football or a glove being knit), a football factory test facility (paint room, heat, cold, pad, sound and wind rooms), and the field above it. Because each room was modelled in photoreal detail in Cinema and Octane, the camera can move and give multiple angles, like a photo shoot, so shots come quickly. Footage combined into end stamps and multi-angle screens. All became After Effects toolkits that keep Octane quality while teams swap easily. Because ESPN has so many games on air, they split it into packages (wind, heat, cold, DNA, pad, sound) and gave different games different ones, so a fan sees the whole test facility only by watching several games.
