Guest: Danielle Feinberg (visual effects supervisor, Turning Red; former lighting DP). When the film moved from theatres to Disney+, she says Pixar puts a huge effort into every release type. At the end of the show she sits in colour timing and does six to eight different colour passes over the entire movie, so each version looks as close as possible to what the team wants [00:36-01:41].
She expects the film to "sing" on the small screen as well, because of the time put in [01:41-02:12].

**Use it:** Plan separate grade/trim passes per delivery (TV, social, venue screen) and budget time for each rather than one grade converted by LUT.
