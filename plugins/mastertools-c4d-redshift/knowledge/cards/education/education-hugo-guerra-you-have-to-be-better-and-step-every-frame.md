At the Mill, Hugo says, the bar was a client expecting a perfect job on every project, and in dailies he would joke 'you have to be better' to each artist; it became the name of his tutorial series [12:37, 13:08, 13:40]. His rule for students: whatever the job or client, try your best on it [14:11]. Host Matt (a teacher, low confidence) adds his classroom rule: a black or unchecked render handed in scores zero, and a comp is finished by stepping every frame with the arrow keys until it is perfect [14:42].

**Use it:** A coach 'done' line: before sending, step the shot frame by frame with arrow keys; nothing is shipped unchecked. Fits a final-pass checklist.
