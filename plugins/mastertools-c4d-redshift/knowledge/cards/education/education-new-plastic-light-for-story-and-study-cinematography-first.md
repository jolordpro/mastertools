Teaching pattern in his 2022 series: start from "what mood do I want" and give every light a motivation, not just to show the subject [01:39][02:10]. He tells students to learn lighting from photography and cinematography channels (StudioBinder, In Depth Cinema, Cinematography and others) and from the work of Roger Deakins, Robert Yeoman and Mihai Malaimare, and from paintings [01:07][01:39]. He paraphrases Deakins: do not be enslaved to technicalities, feel what is right for the scene, with the technicals as tools [02:42]. Light elements he lists: number of sources, direction, size, colour [02:10].

**Use it:** a coach prompt for any lighting question: ask what the light is for, then pick the settings.
