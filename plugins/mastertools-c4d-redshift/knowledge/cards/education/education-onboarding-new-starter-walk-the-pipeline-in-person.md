Maria Walla [07:49-10:21]: for each new artist she holds a one-to-one session of two and a half hours or more: how to open files, where the project, comps and renders live, how to save. A colleague asked why she bothers; her answer is that they learn faster, start earlier and are confident, and person-to-person is better than videos and docs. She explains that she lacked this in earlier jobs. She also tells juniors that no question is shameful and she asks her own leads and strong comp artists questions [12:25].
