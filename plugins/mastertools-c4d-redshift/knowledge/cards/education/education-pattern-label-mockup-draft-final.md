Photoshop's Contextual Task Bar has a model picker; Firefly Image 5 (preview) gives one variation in the Properties panel where older versions gave three. The labelling rule is the researcher's proposal.

**Use it:** every preview carries its label, and a mockup must say it is an AI guess.
