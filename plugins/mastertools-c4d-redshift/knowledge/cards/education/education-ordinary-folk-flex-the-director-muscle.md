Greg Stewart: surround yourself with people who push you (he keeps a group chat of friends in production for feedback); post work even when the community looks intimidating; and flex the director muscle by offering "I think this might be a better way" ideas, which the studio loves from freelancers [§38-39].
Victor Silva: learn to take feedback. Put yourself into the work so it is good, but not so much that "change this" lands as "you hate me" [§39]. Victor also notes you are hired to realise the director's vision, not your own [§28].

**Use it:** When coaching note-taking, separate the work from the person and invite one improvement idea per review.
