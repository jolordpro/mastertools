Rioforce (whisper transcript): in conversation, characters keep the same facing each time they appear [03:48]. Many directors send a character screen-right to advance the story and left to retreat; avoid flipping direction between opposite shots [03:48]. Do not cross the 180-degree line unless the audience sees the swap, with his own error shots (a king filmed from the wrong shoulder) as the example; the fix was moving to the other shoulder [04:23]. Eye level immerses; high looks powerless, low looks powerful, but an establishing shot may leave eye level [05:27]. A Dutch tilt works best after a level shot, since overuse kills it [09:39]. Split frames into foreground, middle and background planes, and a little depth-of-field blur goes a long way [07:43].

**Use it:** supports the existing 180-rule card; the direction-for-progress rule is a quick check on graphics wipes and player run-ins.
