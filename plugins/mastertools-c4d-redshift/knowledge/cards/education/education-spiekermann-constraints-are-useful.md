Setting and printing type by hand makes the limits plain, and he finds that helpful. He calls the volume of choice on screen (16.7 million colours, a few hundred thousand fonts) frightening. Fewer choices and slowing down feel refreshing, like cooking instead of eating from a box. [§2]

**Use it:** In a style exercise, cap the palette and fonts to a short list on purpose.
