From his 'Ten Commandments': pick the simpler, faster tool that is inherently better; also look for 'cost free' tools, since some tools do the job but demand compensation elsewhere and trap you in a compensation loop [9cee63c0 §22]. Applying these two rules cuts about 12 candidate tools to one or two, then he picks one and stays consistent [9cee63c0 §22-23]. In part two he applies the same test to plugins: if it gives greater gains for less effort it counts in its favour [ccd7f3b8 §23-24].

**Use it:** When a user lists several ways to do a step, rank them by effort for gain and side effects to repair, then pick one.
