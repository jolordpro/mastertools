Chad Ashley's first MCP day [44:49-45:51]: he did not ask for a crazy setup. He asked it to name everything sensibly and nest items in nulls his way, then went a bit further each time, until he was building a real scene for a social frame.
Nick Campbell said the same worry applied to him: he was slow to bring AI into the C4D workflow, and the Rubik's Cube result was the moment it clicked [57:42].

**Use it:** the coach can offer a first-run ladder for agent tools: rename and organise, then small edits, then build, then style, checking the result at each rung.
