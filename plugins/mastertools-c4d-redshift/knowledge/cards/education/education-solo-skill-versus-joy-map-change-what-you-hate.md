Prime directive of his personal project: if it is not fun, change it [07:09-07:39]. He maps tasks on skill versus joy; focus where both are high, improve where joy is high but skill low, avoid or change the rest [15:55-16:58].
He noticed he disliked animation, so after three years he rebuilt his characters in a few months: controls cut from 50-55 to about 45, and squash-and-stretch added, which also sped up work [14:22-19:04]. Rigify, for scale, has about 255 controllers [18:32].
Warning sign: if you keep stopping halfway through a project, you may have hit the task you do not enjoy [17:29].
