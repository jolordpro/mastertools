Inhofer [31:54-35:00]: Mixing Light lessons ("insights") are mostly 6-7 to under 20 minutes, each exploring a single thought, not the whole app. He mixes 101 to PhD levels so beginners hear experts and experts are reminded of basics [27:16-28:18].
Contributors are asked what problem they solved this week, because the excitement shows and a small share of members will be going through the same issue [34:28].
Old lessons stay useful (about 80 percent still relevant after eight years) and are refreshed by moving comments into forums [32:56].
He also warns that standalone courses must be re-made constantly, so a membership works only with many contributors [26:45-27:16].

**Use it:** The coach uses a one-thought-per-lesson shape, and starts from the learner's latest real problem.
