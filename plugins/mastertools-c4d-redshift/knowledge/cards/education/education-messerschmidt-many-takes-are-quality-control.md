He says the take count is overstated, but if the operator misses the stand-up on four of six takes only two are usable, and background extras, lapels and props must also work, so 10-12 takes start to seem reasonable [26:36-27:37]. Some directors were happy with five or six [38:59]. Actors get a chance to explore, and each pass often improves the operating and performance [31:15-31:45]. He also says editing choices should come from content, not from covering mistakes [33:16].

**Use it:** Review ritual lesson: budget several passes so every element can be right in one pass, rather than patching in post. Cuts should be creative, not rescues.
