He says the technical jargon (PQ curve and so on) was a rabbit hole; focusing on what makes colour pop or a shadow dark without crushing was a better route to the technicalities [05:24-06:29]. Once the relationship of highlights to shadows is mastered, adding colour becomes an easy ride [46:54-47:27]. The thesis was best when he stopped collecting information and tested a hypothesis, 'trust the process' [09:36-10:08, 54:18-54:50].

**Use it:** A coach could ask the learner to describe the effect they want before naming the tool.
