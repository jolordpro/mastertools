The team's saying was fix it in prep [55:06]. Producers backed rehearsals, where the beats, power dynamics and off-camera looks show themselves, which he cannot judge from the page [55:36-56:09]. They watched rehearsal from each actor's shoulder and from a step back, then built the coverage plan [56:40]. He used previs and storyboards for car work, big logistics, stunts and VFX, not for long dialogue scenes where actors should find their choreography first [57:10-58:44].

**Use it:** Order of prep: rehearsal or animatic first, then coverage list; use boards and previs only where logistics or VFX demand them.
