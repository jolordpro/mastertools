He says only intention matters: how close is the result to the vision, and is there consistency across the body of work, since without it the result may just be random [41:39-43:09]. Effort does not make it better; if it is bad it is bad [42:39]. He works about 18 hours a day, not less because of AI [42:39].

**Use it:** In review ask 'what was the intent, and does this match the rest of the set?' before looking at how it was made.
