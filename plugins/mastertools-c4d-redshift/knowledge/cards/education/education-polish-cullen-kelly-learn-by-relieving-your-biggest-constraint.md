Asked how deep a colorist must go into colour science, Kelly says no lane (gear, image science, grading, LUTs, client work, selling yourself) is wasted, so the useful question is what is constraining you now [9cee63c0 §10]. He applies the theory of constraints: find the weakest point, clear that bottleneck hard until it stops being one, then move to the next [9cee63c0 §10]. Examples he gives: not grasping development, or not having looked at enough well-reproduced images [9cee63c0 §10]. He adds that some highly successful colorists know little science, so that is not a bar to copy [9cee63c0 §13].

**Use it:** When a learner asks what to study, ask what is holding their work back today and teach only that, instead of handing over a full curriculum.
