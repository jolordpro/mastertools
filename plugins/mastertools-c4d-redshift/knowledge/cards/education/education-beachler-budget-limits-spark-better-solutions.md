She names meetings and budgets as the worst part [41:04]. Ideas often must be cut or re-solved with what you have, and the constraint can send you somewhere unexpected [41:35]. What she values is nailing a design that serves actors, director, DP and story, and making bold choices and sticking to them [39:58-40:29].

**Use it:** When a scope cut lands, ask the brief's core job first and rebuild the cheaper way; don't just shrink the original.
