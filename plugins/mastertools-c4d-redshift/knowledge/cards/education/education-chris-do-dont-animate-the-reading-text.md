He asked a studio to write its intro statement out in full, linear and structured as about four sentences [13:32]. Animated type makes someone who lands mid-animation lose the thread, and readers may read faster or slower than the animation [14:03]. Being fancy with animation and web programming doesn't help here. He also said the process section was thin and should be expanded with a card per step, like a brand audit with examples [14:34].

**Use it:** Motion check for web/broadcast text: any information the viewer must read should be held long enough or be static; reveals are for decoration.
