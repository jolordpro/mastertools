In 2017 GSG had a free intro series of over 15 hours and roughly 400 to 500 hours of free content [52:28-53:30].
Beginners get shiny results; those entering the business get podcast episodes on first jobs and negotiation [53:30]; working pros are too busy for hour-long tutorials and want 5 to 10 minute workflow tips, such as ways to speed rendering or set up a fast render so a client can approve [54:01-55:35].
His line: the further in a career, the less shiny you want it, and the more you want to know how the engine works [55:35].

**Use it:** keep coach tips short and tied to a deadline task (render a quick approval cut) for working users.
