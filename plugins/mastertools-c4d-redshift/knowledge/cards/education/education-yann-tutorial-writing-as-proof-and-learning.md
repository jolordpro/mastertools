Yann Dupont: you learn most when you write a tutorial, because you must be sure it works. To learn Combustion he decided to teach it [24:03-25:07]. Turning a feature on at home is not enough to propose it at work; doing a full shot or tutorial lets you say "I evaluated it and it worked" [26:08-26:38]. People who share knowledge with the team are the ones noticed and promoted [34:59]. Arvid says he does tutorials to stay in shape and hit professional accuracy [25:38].

**Use it:** coach habit: write the one-page "how I did it" for each new technique tried on a job.
