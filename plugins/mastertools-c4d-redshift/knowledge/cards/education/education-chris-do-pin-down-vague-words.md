Clients use words like epic, clean and modern, or organic, and designers think they know what they mean, but he calls it dangerous to walk away and assume. He says to stay in the discomfort of asking until the problem and the outcome are clear. [§1]

**Use it:** Turn a vague note into one measurable change before acting on it.
