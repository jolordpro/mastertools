She advises students to test each discipline (does the process and the hours it takes suit you?) and not let doors close early [1:06:04-1:07:08]. Generalist skill matters because so much of the job is getting the idea out so others see its potential: a sketch or little animatic [1:07:38-1:08:10]. Once you know what you are good at, go deep in one to three crafts. She gave up animating early, but stayed at it long enough to give notes "dangerous" enough [1:08:40]. Directors on her team (Henry Chang, Alan Williams) dip into other roles like editing.

**Use it:** Coach tooltip for a growing artist: learn enough of the neighbouring craft to prototype your idea, then deepen the strength.
