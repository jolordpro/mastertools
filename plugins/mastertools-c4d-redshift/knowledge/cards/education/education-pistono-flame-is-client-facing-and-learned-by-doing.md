Flame is more accessible than five years earlier, but the job needs hands-on experience and the ability to interact with clients, since the artist finishes the client's content. A common route is a foot in the door at a post studio and a lot of perseverance. The one thing every Flame artist needs is a good attitude and just the right amount of confidence. [§2-§3]

**Use it:** For finishing roles, practise live sessions with a client, not only the software.
