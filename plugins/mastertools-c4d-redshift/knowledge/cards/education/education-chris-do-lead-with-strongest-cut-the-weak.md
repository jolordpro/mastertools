Chris Do reviews live: the top-left piece gets seen first, so it must be the best work. Thumbnails should show the logo applied, not the bare mark, and lettering the reviewer likes less should be edited out. A yellow mark on white reads as low contrast on a T-shirt mock-up, and a poster with type jammed into corners needs negative space across the whole page. Keep sketches only if they are beautiful. [§2]

**Use it:** When picking frames for review, drop the weakest and show the best in context first.
