On his first feature grade (about three weeks) he regraded scenes two or three times, showed peers, and was ready to hire a colorist if they disliked the result. He says repetition matters more than which method you choose, and at roughly 1,600 shots, moves must be near reflex.

**Use it:** Plan a second look pass; expect the first match to be rough.
