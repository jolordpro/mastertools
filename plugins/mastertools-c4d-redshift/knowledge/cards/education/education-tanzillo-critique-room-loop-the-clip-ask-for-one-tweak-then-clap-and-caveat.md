How Tanzillo runs the room (350th daily critique, Sep 2021 [00:06 T2]):
- Loop a near-final animation three times and ask the room if anything should be tweaked [22:04 T1]; "clap first, then caveat" when a piece is done, then post it to the hall of fame with the artist's OK [44:43-45:49 T1].
- Open a question to the group, then step in and decide: "I like that it exists, it is too strong, pull it back" [25:29 T1].
- He walks the artist through a technique on their own file, and says "a million ways to do anything" [18:58 T2].
- Challenge judging: a very good image was out because the brief subject (the library) was secondary [02:12 T2]; a late entry was ineligible, "a good lesson of deadlines" [03:13 T2]. Winner chosen for pushing stylisation [05:49 T2].
- Admits when he is speculating ("the conspiracy theory world of lighting") [12:00 T1].

**Use it:** a review ritual for the coach: show, ask for one change, decide, then end with the one thing to keep.
