Francesco Giroldini (lead lighting/comp artist at DreamWorks, ex-Pixar and Blue Sky; Academy of Animated Art interview): a lead must handle complex shots, support struggling and talented artists, and write documentation when there is no time to talk to each of three or four people [18:18-19:24].
He sets up rigs, gives an overview and answers questions [19:24-19:56]; he thinks the unnoticed part is keeping tools and pipeline improving since leads know it best [19:56-20:26].
Scripting: little at DreamWorks (sine and noise); if you can state what you want, someone better at scripting can help [15:32-16:43].
On a new tool, a lead says so and learns from the team [25:28-26:23].

**Use it:** Lead checklist: document the setup, route pipeline requests, and admit tool gaps.
