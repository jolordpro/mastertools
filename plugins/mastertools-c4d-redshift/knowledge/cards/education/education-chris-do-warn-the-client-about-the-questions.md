His late business coach's advice: say at the start that it will be grueling, that you will ask lots of questions and they may be annoyed, and promise that you will not start without a clear understanding of the problem and what they want [§1]. Do not walk away from a client afraid to ask what you need to do the job [§1].

**Use it:** Open a review or brief by saying the clarifying questions are coming and why.
