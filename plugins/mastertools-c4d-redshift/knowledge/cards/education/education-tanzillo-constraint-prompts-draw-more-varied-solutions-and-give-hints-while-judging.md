Students say the underwater axolotl challenge worked because it forced a scenario nobody would pick alone, and everyone solved it differently [20:11-20:42]. Tanzillo agrees that more limits draw more creativity [20:42-21:14]. He says judging can feel like a baking show where the criteria are discovered late, so he feeds hints as the month goes: for the bat character, getting the specular right, separating the nose from the face and getting the leathery wings [21:14-22:17].
The same AMA notes a render-time limit on student challenges: a laptop animation can take over a month, so the academy was testing a render farm for students [19:07-19:37].

**Use it:** Coach prompts can set a constraint (place, material, time of day) and name the one or two things the judge will check.
