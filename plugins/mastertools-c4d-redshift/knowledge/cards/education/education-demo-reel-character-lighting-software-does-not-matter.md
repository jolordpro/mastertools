Francesco Giroldini (lead lighting/comp artist at DreamWorks, ex-Pixar and Blue Sky; Academy of Animated Art interview): most reels show interiors and architecture, but 99% of the shots are character shots, so he looks for characters: beyond Lambert, hair, refraction, reflection stacks, subsurface and the eyes [22:34-24:16].
Software choice does not matter; every studio uses different tools and what counts is eye and getting shots done [24:46-25:20].
TV versus film differs mainly in time per shot and thus quality; characters are how you get hired [27:26-27:58].

**Use it:** Reel advice for the coach: lead with character or product hero shots; name the look, not the app.
Transcript support: Tanzillo independently says studios do not care which software; proprietary workflows are taught on the job (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDcjIk7L-0Y [31:12])
