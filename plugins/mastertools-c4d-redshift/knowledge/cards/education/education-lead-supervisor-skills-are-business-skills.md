Courtney Price [1:16:04-1:18:07]: mid-weight is about technical artistry, senior adds the soft skills and management, and lead or supervisor adds budgeting, scheduling, hiring, on-set troubleshooting and liaising with production. She mentions team makeup: for example one senior, three mids and a couple of juniors. Way in: tell your company your aim, or take small commercials and lead them yourself.
She also says [1:09:20] that a Flame artist is valued less for software and more for handling a room of agency clients, relaying ideas and mentoring.

**Use it:** Context for what a producer expects from a senior hand: client-room skills count as much as the software.
