She learned by being on sets before running one and by doing tabletop and run-and-gun shoots herself [50:40]. Clarity in your own mind lets you tell when you have caught it and when it is time to move on [51:41]. She still comes to set with more planned storyboards and angles than free-form directors, and surprises such as wind on a sundress shoot or a left-handed hockey player taught her to leave room [1:06:46-1:08:52].
