Marco says render time is not only an end-of-project problem: artists subconsciously keep subdivision low, skip 6K textures and avoid pushing frame rate because they expect the wait [13:10-14:12].
His claim: if rendering is solved early, the creative process starts without that brake, and rendering locally will look as odd as keeping all files on one computer [13:42-14:12].
He also shows a 250-frame, 2000 px, 500-sample test finishing in 3 minutes on the farm [11:36-12:39].
Vendor pitch, but the observation is a useful teaching point.

**Use it:** coach prompt: ask whether a scene was simplified for render-time reasons and test whether a farm or draft setting would allow the original idea.
