Zak builds ACES intuition with a Lightroom example: a sun-lit room shot RAW with four exposures [06:17-06:49].
He picks the one exposure that just starts clipping shadows, shows pulling highlights and lifting shadows from one RAW (noise appears, limited latitude), then merges the four to HDR and tone-maps it to the sRGB display [07:20-09:54].
His point: the data exists, the display cannot show it, and tone mapping chooses how to squeeze it; ACES does that in a principled way [10:29-11:01].

**Use it:** Coach: use a real photo the learner can see before showing the colour-space diagram.
