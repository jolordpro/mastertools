From two Offset Podcast transcripts: favour ideas over node recipes because recipes go stale. Check whether a tip works across cameras, shots and genres. Know why a step works; host Robbie Carman (not Kelly) cites LUT-chasers who do not know the input space. Judge a tool by gain per effort. Diagnose before prescribing, like a doctor, since a client's request may not match what the work needs. Kelly skips footage in YouTube videos where effort will not visibly change the result, since a video has to show a good outcome to do its job; deeper teaching sits in paid courses.
