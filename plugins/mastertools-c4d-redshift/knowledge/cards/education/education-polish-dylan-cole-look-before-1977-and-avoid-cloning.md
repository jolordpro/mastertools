Cole, introduced to the Hudson River School by Syd Dutton, says they would have made great matte painters [§4]. He sees some young artists who look no earlier than the 1977 release of Star Wars, and many trying to be one popular concept artist [§4]. He wanted to be Ralph McQuarrie as a child but widened his view [§4]. He adds it is not fair to generalise about all young artists [§4].

**Use it:** When a user copies a current artist, point to older painters who solved the same problem (light, focal point) and ask what to borrow.
