He says tutorials help you see what is possible, but the point is to find the balance between that and your own skill level so limits do not get in the way of what you want to create. The only way to find the balance is to go and make things. [§4]

**Use it:** Choose projects slightly beyond current skill and ship them; use tutorials to learn what is possible.
