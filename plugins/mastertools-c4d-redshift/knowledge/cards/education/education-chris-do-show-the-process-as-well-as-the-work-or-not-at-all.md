He tells designers to spend as much time presenting process as the work, otherwise don't show it [11:59]. Sketches that are not beautiful should be cut, as with a student's sketches below finished posters [24:14]. Order the case so the flashiest image comes first and backstory later [22:41-23:12]. A mood board or outline version can show thinking [07:48-08:18].

**Use it:** Process slides in a deck/case study: only include if photographed, lit and laid out as well as the final; lead with the final.
