Kelly borrows a framework from Neil deGrasse Tyson's class: the earth is round, then it is slightly flattened at the poles, each level offered with 'would you like to know more' [9cee63c0 §11]. Kelly's version: give a handhold, then the next when the learner is ready, and check whether it suffices for the client work at hand before climbing further [9cee63c0 §11-12]. Host Joey D'Anna adds that the basic science may be a small share of success but it is the first part you need, like knowing the earth is round before navigating [9cee63c0 §12].

**Use it:** Answer in layers: give the usable level first, name the next level, and let the user decide whether they need it.
