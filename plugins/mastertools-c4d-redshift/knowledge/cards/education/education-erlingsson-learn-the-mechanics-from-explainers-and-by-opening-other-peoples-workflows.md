He names Banodoco (a board and Discord), the early ComfyUI tutorials by Matteo (Latent Vision, creator of IP-Adapter), and downloading every workflow to open and figure out [21:14-23:18]. He values tutorials that explain the reason for each node: for example, a negative image is not a Photoshop invert but a degraded version showing the model what not to do [22:15-22:47].
His point: if you break models down at node level you must understand what the text encoder, latent, shift and CFG do; changing values without that is guesswork [23:49-24:21]. Knowledge of older models (pyramid flow, SVD) is now useless [24:52].

**Use it:** Build a workflow by dissecting a working one node by node and writing what each does.
