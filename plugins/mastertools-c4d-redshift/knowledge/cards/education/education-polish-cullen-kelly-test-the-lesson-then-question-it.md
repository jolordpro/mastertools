Kelly's last 'commandment' is to question assumptions: he tells students something, and they must test it and come back asking what they are missing if it fails, or decide that teaching is not for them [ccd7f3b8 §5-6]. He teaches some things that are the opposite of what he was taught and still disagrees with mentors on points [ccd7f3b8 §6]. This is his guard against students becoming copies of him [ccd7f3b8 §5].

**Use it:** Hand the user a way to check the advice on their own footage rather than asking for trust.
