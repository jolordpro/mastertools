Louis Moss: most people reference by attraction (see it, like it, add it), which is collecting. Real referencing asks why: the colour combination, page composition, asset quality (textures, fabrics), cultural reference, lighting, mood [10:19-10:56].
Once the specific quality is known, combine specifics from several sources into something ownable; he compares it to art school, breaking down painters' styles [10:56-11:26]. He now categorises each saved reference by its reason, and says doing that consistently shows patterns in his own taste [11:26-11:57].
