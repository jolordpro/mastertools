Deakins [10:28]: he would watch movies and try to work out why he responded to something, and replay them with the sound off to understand editing and what propelled the story without dialogue.
James adds a habit of looking at ordinary things, such as trees across the street, and asking why a look pleases her, so you build a library of images [37:40]. After a bad day on set, Deakins goes out and takes one photograph and feels okay again [40:19].
He says he has used documentaries to shape a minimalist lighting style, sometimes many lights for one simple effect [15:50].
