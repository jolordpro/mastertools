He learned type drawing from a teacher whose smallest measurement was 'a mouse hair' (about 2-point tracing on a 16-point drawing, per the captions). If you tried to cheat and did not take the mouse hair off, he noticed the next time round. That, he says, was good training for drawing type [09:59-10:30]. (The teacher's name is garbled in the captions ('Lana/Langer'); probably Berthold's Gerhard Lange, unconfirmed.)

**Use it:** Train the eye with a task where tiny corrections visibly matter, and check them against a trusted reviewer.
