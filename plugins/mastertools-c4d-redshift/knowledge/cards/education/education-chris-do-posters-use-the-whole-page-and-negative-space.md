Reviewing ping pong, mini golf and netball posters, he liked the tone-on-tone treatment (blue on blue, green on green, pink on pink) and the imagery, but said type shoved in the upper left and copy in the bottom right does nothing [24:14-25:16]. He asked for the whole page to be thought of as one composition with negative space [25:16].

**Use it:** Poster/graphics QC: find the type block's relationship to the whole frame; flag corner-hugging text.
