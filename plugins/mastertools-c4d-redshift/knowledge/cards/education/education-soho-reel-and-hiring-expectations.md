Razon interviewed heavily in autumn 2020 and says he skims most reels and watched fewer than 10 from start to finish; 40-60 seconds is fine for a student [58:26].
He asks comp candidates how they QC their own work; some cannot answer, which worries him [1:02:06].
Reels should include normal work (paint, wire removal) not only hero CG, and FX should be shown integrated on a live plate, with a breakdown that proves it is not a tutorial or shelf button [1:03:09, 1:06:47, 1:07:19].
Show procedural Houdini workflow [1:07:51]. Get the reel judged by professionals and ask several people which shots to cut [59:29, 1:01:35].
Juniors: first assigned things they should be able to do, paired with a senior [1:18:51]; a typecast artist gets out by showing speed on the task they are given [1:25:39].
Career advice is context for hiring; details like salary and visas skipped.

**Use it:** The coach can tell a learner to add a self-QC checklist and a breakdown to their work, and cut weak shots by polling several reviewers.
Transcript support: Tanzillo watches reels to the end, wants 2 minutes or less (about 1:30), 3 strong pieces; the Soho supervisor says 40-60 s for students and skims. The lengths differ (not a contradiction: different roles and studios) (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDcjIk7L-0Y [28:35])
