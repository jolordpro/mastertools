Courtney Price [46:28-50:00]: the title is arbitrary (a senior at one studio may differ from another), but seniors usually show wide experience, need little supervision and mentor others [17:21-19:25].
Soft-skill habits she names: ask questions and get regular feedback because version one is rarely good enough; get the brief before day one; know when to lead, follow or mentor; bring two or three options to the decision maker, with your opinion, since managers care about options, not technicalities; admit your own mistakes and fix them; be upfront when a deadline will be missed.
Her reasoning: non-technical people want the options [49:06].

**Use it:** A coach pattern for client and producer messages: when something is wrong, present options and a recommendation.
