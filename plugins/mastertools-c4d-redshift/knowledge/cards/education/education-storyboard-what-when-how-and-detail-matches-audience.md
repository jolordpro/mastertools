Jordan (Motion Array, beginner class) says a motion board is not 24 frames per second but the base beats, drawn as boxes with arrows and written notes (including voiceover that cannot be drawn) [00:34-01:05, 04:11]. Cost argument: a bad idea costs a 10-minute sketch instead of a week of animation [01:05]. Prompts per frame: what shapes are visible, when does it progress, how does it transition [02:07]. Keep it reorderable with sticky notes or cut-out frames, or phone photos organised on a computer [05:46-06:50]. Write enough that someone else, or you in two months, can read it [05:15].
Where it ends: big commercial jobs need detailed boards so each department knows its needs and the client cannot say it differs from their picture; personal work can stay as scribbles [07:51-08:21]. Styling frames in the animation app before animating is his own refinement step [07:20].

**Use it:** teaching pattern for the coach: for pitch boards ask 'what, when, how' per frame and match detail to the people who must sign off.
