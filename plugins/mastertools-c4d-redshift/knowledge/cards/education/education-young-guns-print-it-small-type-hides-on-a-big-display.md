One designer says text read as legible only because he worked on a 5k display with vectors; printed it is too small (another noted tiny effects text and rules on the edge) [§1]. Another learned net weight must sit on the pack front after seeing it missing [§1]. Same session: first ideas are usually not the best, so make more versions [§1].

**Use it:** Check small type printed or at real viewing size, not zoomed on a big monitor.
