Myth 1 destructive: true only for LUTs with narrow bounds [10:33-12:08]. Myth 2 cheating: using a LUT is no more cheating than camera processing or colour management; it is borrowing a transformation you could not build yourself [12:38-14:41]. Myth 3 untrustworthy: LUTs misbehave when users ignore input/output or source badly; he compares judging LUTs as bad to judging a screenplay without reading it [16:15-20:21]. Teaching pattern: he asks the audience for their 'dumbest' LUT question because someone else has it too [15:12].

**Use it:** A coach tooltip on LUTs can repeat the three reasons to check range, input/output and source instead of banning LUTs.
