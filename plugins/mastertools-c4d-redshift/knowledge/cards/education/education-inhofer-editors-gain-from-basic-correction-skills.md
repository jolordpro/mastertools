He treated colour correction early on as an adjunct to editing [44:28-45:00]. An editor who can make intentional quick adjustments can tell a director if a shot is usable without calling a colorist [45:00-45:30]. He points to network news: about 45 seconds on a two-minute spot setting black level, white level and colour temperature would measurably improve it [46:01-46:35]. He also notes producers overestimate colorist cost for a 25-minute show, but some jobs just lack time [45:30-46:01].

**Use it:** A minimum viable correction (black, white, temperature) is the baseline before any look work in any pipeline. Consistent with the brief's QC gates.
