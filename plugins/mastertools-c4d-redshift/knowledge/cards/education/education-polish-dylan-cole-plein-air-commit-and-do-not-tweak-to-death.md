Cole [40:35-41:41]: painting outdoors in oil forces you to be decisive; you cannot fiddle endlessly. A friend's line he likes: "I may not be right, but I am certain" [41:09], so put even a wrong stroke down boldly.
Thousands of digital paintings are in his head, which informs the oil work, and the oil work feeds back into digital: simpler, bigger shapes, good decisions up front, a solid foundation, and no reliance on digital tools to tweak endlessly [41:41].
Everyone has tweaked a painting to death, and sometimes it is better to start over [41:41].
Transfer: lock the big light and colour shapes early in a render, then stop adjusting small things.

**Use it:** The coach can ask, when a render has been tweaked many times, whether the big light and colour shapes are right or a restart is faster.
