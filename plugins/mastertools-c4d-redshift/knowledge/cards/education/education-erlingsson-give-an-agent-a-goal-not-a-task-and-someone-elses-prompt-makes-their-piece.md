Advice given (he heard it from ChatGPT): give an agent a goal, not a task [1:29:07-1:30:08]. People who chase the longest video or one model's trick have no end point [1:30:08]. Hunting prompts is looking for someone else's goal; their prompt will make their thing [1:31:09-1:31:41].
Workflows are the best way to learn: find one, pick it apart, make your own; prompts matter less now [1:32:13]. Be unattached to tools since everything will change [1:30:38].

**Use it:** When studying a reference, extract the intent and structure, then rebuild it for your own goal.
