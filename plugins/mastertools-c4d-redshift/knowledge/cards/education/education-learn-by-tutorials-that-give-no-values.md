Austin Bauwens went through every tab and effect, putting each on a solid to see what it did [09:14]. He avoids prescriptive tutorials with numbers to copy, because they teach one sequence only [09:46]. A free Tip Tut video stated up front that it would give no values and just work in the panels, which forced him to experiment [10:16].
He had already thought about easing and acceleration from hand-drawn frame-by-frame animation before he used graphs [09:14].
He also says he started with static branding, so composition came as instinct [08:43].

**Use it:** A teaching pattern for the coach: explain what a control does and let the learner find the value on their own scene.
