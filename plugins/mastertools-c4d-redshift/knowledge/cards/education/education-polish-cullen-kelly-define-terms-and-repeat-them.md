Kelly says he does not water down terminology for beginners; he defines each term and repeats the same terms week after week, so a newcomer picks them up after two or three live sessions [9cee63c0 §16-17]. He needs context (a problem that makes him read a given chapter) and repetition, 8 to 10 times in his own case, and teaches the way he learns [9cee63c0 §16-17]. D'Anna adds that material only sticks once applied to a real project, which sends learners back to the reference [9cee63c0 §17].

**Use it:** Coach with a consistent term list, define each on first use, and tie it to the user's current shot.
