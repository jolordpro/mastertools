He describes his process as finding little things along the way without knowing they will become necessary later [21:31]. On his most challenging project he found himself using older tutorial techniques: a shock wave made from fractal noise wrapped into a circle, a particle storm past a reflective ball, a camera-projection city from a 'submerged' tutorial, a glass look [25:57-27:17].
He says experiments make you a better, more well-rounded artist [25:57].

**Use it:** Keep a library of small, named experiments and revisit it at the start of every hard job.
