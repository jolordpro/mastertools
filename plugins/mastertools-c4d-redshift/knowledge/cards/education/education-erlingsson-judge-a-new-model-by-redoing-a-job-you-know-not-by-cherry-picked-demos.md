He does not trust the best-of clips in model channels (cherry picked) [27:01]. He re-creates something he got in a model he understands, and judges how close the new one gets to what he wanted [27:31].
The danger he names: models are tuned to give a great result whatever you ask, so a good result is easy but one that is exactly what you wanted is not [28:03]. His example was a 360 orbit around a person, once very hard and expensive; a new model did it first time as a perfect loop [28:03-28:35].

**Use it:** Keep a small personal test set (one orbit, one hand shot, one text-in-frame) and run every new model against it.
