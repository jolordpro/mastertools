She learned Photoshop at work from manuals taken home at night, back when it had no layers and every element was a separate saved file with its own alpha matte [12:38-13:41]. Her advice: set yourself a deadline and force the tool to teach you by finishing something in that time [14:42]. Host Chris Do adds that old tools had one undo level and outline-only Illustrator preview, that constraints push creativity, and that chasing the latest hardware is a never-ending chase while fundamentals compound [15:13-16:16]. She also learned to step away from a stuck problem rather than push through [1:14:32].

**Use it:** Coach tip for learners: pick a small finished piece with a date, not a tool tour. Host's opinion on gear is low confidence.
