He opens advice with 'may I have permission to give design advice' [09:52]. Then he picks specifics he likes (good stroke endings, nice application, tone-on-tone posters) before the hard note [10:23; 21:08; 24:46]. He admits he'll be brutally honest about weak sketches [24:14] and keeps each note to a concrete change: edit out weaker pieces, refine specific letters, fix low-contrast yellow on white [10:54; 21:40]. Obvious limit: he often can't talk with the person.

**Use it:** Template for review notes in the coach: permission, one praise tied to a detail, one problem with a concrete fix.
