Nick Campbell asked Chad Ashley which AI he used for minimalist Braun-inspired glass shapes. It was the same tool Nick used [29:08].
The gap was method: when a result missed, Chad reworded it, tried a different angle, and named an artist from decades earlier as a reference [29:38]. Three things did the work: art history, taste, patience [30:11].
Chad's view: the floor for bad work has risen, but good work still comes from artists with taste and vision [31:46]. Directing an image model felt natural to him because a creative director spends the day writing notes and marking up playblasts [9:00].
Counterpoint from Chad on the emotional side: image generation felt like a slot machine, around 1,000 images and an empty feeling at the end [5:43-6:45].

**Use it:** the coach can teach prompt revision the way a CD teaches note-giving: say what is wrong, name a reference, try another angle, repeat.
