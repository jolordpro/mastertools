He tries not to chase what will go viral; the weirdest random posts often do best, while a piece he laboured over may get little [19:08-19:40]. He tends to dislike work a week after finishing, reinvents at least one part of the next, and makes one or two full pieces a day with thousands of generations a week [19:40-20:43]. He knows a style is exhausted when he is bored of it [20:43].

**Use it:** Keep a short list of what is the same as the last piece and change one of them each time.
