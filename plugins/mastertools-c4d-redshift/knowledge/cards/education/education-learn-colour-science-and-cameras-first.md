Asked what to learn outside the daily job [56:56-58:27], she names: a 3D package (Houdini, Blender), Python basics, colour science and cameras and lenses (use a camera at home and see what vintage lenses do). For colour she points to Daniele Siragusano's Filmlight talks on colour management (two parts, about an hour each) [1:06:12].
On maths [1:12:24]: basic multiply and add, not sine and tangent expressions; understanding of colour spaces and why a uniform working space exists is more important, but not at scientist depth.

**Use it:** Colour-space understanding is the lesson to prioritise in the coach for new users.
