Greg Stewart (freelance motion designer, School of Motion alumnus; interviewer Ryan Plummer) chose challenging shots from a shared list on an eight-minute explainer so he could lean on people who knew more [45:03-45:34].
The best idea should win even when it is not his; he felt shame asking but they were kind [55:59-56:29].
A lead spent three or four days making a bubble effect in After Effects, threw it out, and rebuilt it in Cinema 4D, showing the right move is not being precious [56:29-57:00].

**Use it:** Coaching: show juniors that discarding days of work for the right tool is normal.
