Franklin started as a computer animator at a British games company, then TV, then film from the mid-1990s. He says the games lesson that still holds is that artists and programmers must work closely: strong ties between creative and technical staff produce the tools that make extraordinary imagery possible [01:09-02:12].

**Use it:** Keep a technical artist in the room from the first look-dev review so tools get built to the look, not after it.
