Maxon's Demystifying Post Production weeks 1 and 2 (Feb 2024) cover a minimal AOV package, Raw and Filter and ACES. CompositingMentor's LightGroup / AOV Paradox (27 May 2025): subtraction rebuilds misbehave, division is more stable, mixing light groups with material AOVs clashes. Compositing Academy (6 Apr 2026): relights look plastic when the normal pass has no surface detail.

**Use it:** check that light groups add back to the beauty and normals carry bump detail.
