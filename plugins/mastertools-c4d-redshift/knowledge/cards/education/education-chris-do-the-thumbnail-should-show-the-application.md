Buyers browse fast with short attention, so the thumbnail is cover art [05:12-05:43]. He picked the application shot over the logo mark as the thumbnail because it is more enticing [06:45-07:16], and advised posting where buyers of design look, such as Behance and Dribbble [05:12]. Treat the top of the project like the area above the fold: if it fails to grab, they won't scroll [07:48].

**Use it:** Reel/portfolio check: first frame or thumbnail shows the work in context at its best.
