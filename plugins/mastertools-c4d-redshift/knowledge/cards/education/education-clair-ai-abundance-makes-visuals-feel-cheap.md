Clair says the flood of AI images makes everything visual feel cheap and has dulled his own enthusiasm; he had once considered a PhD on AI and creativity, then lost interest [1:28:25-1:30:30].
When he makes something now he faces a choice: AI gives something that looks great but not quite the right version; doing it by hand costs far more time, money and effort [1:29:28].
He still uses chat models for script feedback and finds Claude the better writer of the models he tried [1:27:20].
Opinion, not a method; confidence low.

**Use it:** Use as a coach talking point: speed is not the test, whether the image is the right version of the idea is.
