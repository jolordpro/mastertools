He says experience comes from gaining experience. Students should be making four to six short films a year. If a YouTube piece looks good but poorly graded, contact the maker for source footage. At the bottom rung the job is to handle images from other people constantly. [§9]

**Use it:** For practice sets, request raw material from real projects rather than regrading stock demo files.
