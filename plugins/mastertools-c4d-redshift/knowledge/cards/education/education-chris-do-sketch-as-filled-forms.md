For logos and symbols he wants thumbnails filled in (fast broad pencil fill, or paint bucket) so the form reads, not just the line [§2]. He says to spend as much time presenting process as the final, or leave it out, and to put the wow image first and sketches lower [§2].

**Use it:** When showing process frames in a review, flood the shape or value first so the form reads.
