In his Tao of Color courses he filmed himself grading a short film shot by shot, and the learner graded the same footage. At agreed points the learner sent their project file and received a recorded critique with what to look at, made revisions, and moved on; a final critique followed the finished grade [12:33-13:36].

**Use it:** A coach could use checkpoint critiques on the learner's own file, not just a model answer.
