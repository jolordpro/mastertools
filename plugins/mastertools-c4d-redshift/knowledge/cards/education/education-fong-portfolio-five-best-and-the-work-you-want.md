Her advice: make what you love, not what you think others want to see [1:18:10]. The early work you show sets the tone for the work you will get, so a funny character reel is needed to be hired for character work [1:20:46]. A hiring story: designers with finance books but no wit were hard to place in humour [1:21:16]. Show ten, preferably five, because weak pieces beside strong ones make the viewer doubt your judgement [1:21:48-1:22:18].

**Use it:** Reel review check: does each piece prove something the owner wants to be hired for? Cut anything that makes the reader question taste.
