Steps [26:30-27:34, 31:08]: page through a font site by basic category; screenshot ones you react to; label each by gut feeling (calm, exciting, feminine, aggressive) without overthinking; then group them and look for shared traits (lighter weight, generous spacing, round bowls). Then walk each feeling back to its source (wedding invitations, a band flyer) to see which styles an industry has "taken over" [31:39-32:42]. She also says to check what competitors look like and decide to lean in or react against it.
**Use it:** A reusable taste drill for the coach: feeling first, traits second, source of the feeling third.
