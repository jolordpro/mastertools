He argues art directors exist because choosing and directing matter more than who draws; people are hired for ideas and layout thinking, not best draw-quality [1:22:17-1:23:51]. His advice: if the craft itself is your joy, do it for that; do not expect to be paid for it, and use AI to amplify what you bring [1:21:44-1:24:52]. In hiring for AI work, competence with tools is not enough; people with taste, style and storytelling are rare [1:48:05-1:48:36].

**Use it:** For the coach: grade work on intent and taste first, tool fluency second.
