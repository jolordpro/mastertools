He says the biggest learning at his studio job came from sitting among creatives who would critique his work daily [35:50-36:21].
A senior designer told him his work was ugly and asked to help; he asked that person to explain why every day, and the blunt answer taught him [36:21-36:52].

**Use it:** coach prompt: ask a reviewer why, not only whether, and invite the blunt version.
