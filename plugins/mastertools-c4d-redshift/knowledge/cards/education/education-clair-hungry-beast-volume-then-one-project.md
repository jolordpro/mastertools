On Hungry Beast (ABC Australia, 2009-2011) Clair and two other animators produced six to nine minutes of original motion design every week, usually in three or four days; the writer calls this his ten thousand hours and where he found his voice [§2]. After about eight weeks away through illness, with four weeks of the series left, he put the remaining time into a single project he could be proud of: Stuxnet: Anatomy of a Computer Virus, which got over half a million views in days and a Vimeo Staff Pick [§3].

**Use it:** Pair high-volume practice with an occasional single piece given full time; treat the second as the portfolio step.
