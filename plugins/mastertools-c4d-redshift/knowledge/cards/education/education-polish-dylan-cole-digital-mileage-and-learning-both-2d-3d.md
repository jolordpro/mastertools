Cole says analogue skills underpin drawing, composition and painting but digital teaches faster because you can do many more paintings, so it is a mileage thing [§4]. He recommends learning both 2D and 3D; 3D is necessary, even in art departments, but it should not be a crutch for artists who cannot draw or have not mastered perspective [§2]. He began using Cinema 4D around 2006 because it has strong camera-projection tools and then started modelling the geometry to project on [§1].

**Use it:** Recommend high-volume digital studies while keeping basic drawing and perspective practice.
