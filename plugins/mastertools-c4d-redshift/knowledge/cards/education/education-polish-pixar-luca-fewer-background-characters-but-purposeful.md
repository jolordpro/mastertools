Background characters were designed as one community, each with a prop showing vocation; groups stay in set spots, as they saw on location [33:20-34:24]. Clothes came from hundreds of period photos; shared items are fine since people used the same tailor [34:55]. Pixar's usual crowd approach reuses a population and creates texture that is more like noise; here they wanted less but purposeful, used to accent place and time [36:27-36:58]. Advice: stay curious, practise noticing, have a point of view [37:28].
**Use it:** For crowd or background fill in a graphic package, use fewer elements, each with a clear role.
