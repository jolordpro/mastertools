Sarofsky [41:29-42:31]: with a fire hose of new tools, a goal gives direction; she tests what gets her to it, and the three-year feed of her personal project shows the pipeline changing.
Summers [43:03-45:37]: older motion designers built identity around a toolset, which no longer works with 300 tools; he moves from how to what to why as fast as he can. 'Finished is better than perfect' and released beats sitting on a drive.

**Use it:** for the coach: when a learner asks 'how do I learn X', ask what they want to make first.
