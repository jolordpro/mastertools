Maria Walla's view of ranks [10:52-14:27]: juniors (under about a year in) get small, detailed, non-technical tasks and mentoring on colour and composition; mids mix technical and artistic; seniors take master shots from the lead and set them up for the team, either focused on their own work or also teaching.
In a reel she looks for lit environments and characters, the light angle chosen (long shadows versus hard noon shadows) and use of cinematography references [29:01-31:17]. If two candidates are close, she picks by personality: someone she can ask for help and who is not egotistical [44:56-45:26]. A host adds that optimisation shows well for game roles [31:49].
She suggests one very high quality piece over many [18:34], and a cover letter styled like the CV [25:48].

**Use it:** Useful for the coach's tone on review: what a lead looks at in a junior's lighting first.
