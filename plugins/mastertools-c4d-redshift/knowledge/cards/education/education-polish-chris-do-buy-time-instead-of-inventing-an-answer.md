Two moves for hard questions in interviews or client calls [36:08-36:38]: (1) "Can you phrase that another way" gives thinking time; (2) if you truly lack the answer, say it deserves more thought and offer to follow up, or name an expert on your team who can answer. He says this gains respect and often the follow-up is never needed. Mindset reframe: the interview tests fit, you give your best information, and the outcome is outside your control [37:10].
**Use it:** Coach pattern for reviews and client calls: a stalled answer should become a promised follow-up with a date, not a guess.
