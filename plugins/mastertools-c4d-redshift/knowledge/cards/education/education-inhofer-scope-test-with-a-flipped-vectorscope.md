His boss, hiring for a duplication job at a Manhattan post house, had him set black level, brightness, hue and saturation so bars matched on the scopes; she had rotated the vectorscope by a random amount to see if he understood what he was looking at. He spotted it and corrected it, and was hired on the spot [35:12-36:13].

**Use it:** A good practical test checks reading the instrument, not clicking the controls. Idea for a coach quiz: show a scope with a known error and ask what is wrong.
