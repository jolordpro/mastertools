Debrief yourself at project end: a skill like denoising renders on one show fades unless noted [19:29-20:00].
Open scripts of people in the role you want, 15 minutes a day, write down attributes that differ from yours, drop what does not make sense and revisit it later [20:00-21:02]. Ask them to explain why, not only to show [21:02-21:33].
At project end ask the lead who the best comper was and study that person, since leads rarely give blunt feedback [42:40-43:11]. Asking questions is a strength [14:19-15:52].

**Use it:** coach habit: log one thing learned per job and compare a shot to a better one.
