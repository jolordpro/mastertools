Brady noticed on the first Toy Story that the best animation came from animators who got out of their chairs and acted shots with their bodies or hands [01:37]. He wondered why the physical acting could not simply be captured for first blocking, though at the time people called mocap cheating [02:07]. At AMGI they start from the hardest problem, expressive faces with eyes always focused, then work backwards to fast animatics [03:09]. Humans judge human motion instantly, so even a two-year-old notices when it is off [04:43].

**Use it:** A teaching pattern for the coach: have the user act out or time the motion themselves before keyframing.
