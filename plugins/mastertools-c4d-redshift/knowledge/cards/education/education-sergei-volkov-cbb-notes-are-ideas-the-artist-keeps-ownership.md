Sergei Volkov [02:25-03:25, 17:20-18:22]: after praising the shape and depth, he lists 'CBB' items (could be better, a German industry shorthand): already good, push if there is time and bandwidth. He frames them as ideas to explore, not notes; if one helps, great; it is the artist's creation. Also he squints at an image, as painters step back, to see the big picture [25:21].

**Use it:** A pattern for coach feedback: praise, then labelled optional improvements.
