Cangelosi tells students the software matters less than understanding how the thing works, because a supervisor will ask for something the button cannot do [36:26, 48:48, 49:19]. He teaches older, manual ways for the same reason. For Houdini he starts from how an artist thinks and brings in code later, because Houdini's reputation as a black box scares people [59:09, 59:40]. Pattern he saw: students from a code-first course came back to repeat it, since they could script a procedural model but could not make an explosion [1:00:42, 1:01:13]. Directors with little pipeline knowledge ask for an ocean storm by tomorrow and assume there is a button [50:22]. Host compares it to knowing how to change a car's oil.

**Use it:** Coach pattern: explain what a setting does physically before giving a preset, then say what to do when the preset is not enough.
