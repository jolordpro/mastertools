Nora Shramek prefers technical people for lighting roles; being a good artist is expected [07:44-08:14]. In a new project or changing hardware, lighting is the department asked why something looks wrong and how to make it run, and lighters see the whole product, so they must talk to both art and engineering [10:17-11:51]. She became an unofficial leader because nobody else owned the problem, learning from experts in the specific field [12:53-13:56]. Whether to study or self-teach depends on learning style [05:41-07:44].

**Use it:** coach path for a motion designer moving into lighting: show technical debugging evidence, not only pretty frames.
