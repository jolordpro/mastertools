From a film-school writing teacher: to write well, rewrite, rewrite, and watch movies like a writer. Kelly transposes this to colour: grade, grade more, watch movies like a colorist [ccd7f3b8 §14]. He says these three work even with mediocre sources, and two bonuses are to prefer concepts over techniques and to judge who teaches things that hold for many projects over years [ccd7f3b8 §14].

**Use it:** Recommend volume of graded work plus analytic film watching before more courses.
