[01:57:51] Add a Liquid FX emitter to a liquid group; it creates three particle groups. Bubbles form inside liquid, foam where little liquid surrounds a particle, spray where almost none [02:00:59].
[02:04:06] They pass through the liquid, are influenced by it, and do not influence it. Default resolution makes 500% extra particles per liquid particle [02:04:36].
Scene scale matters: liquids look wrong small, so scale the tank up [01:57:20]. Suited to soda fizz, rivers and shorelines.

**Use it:** warn that FX particles need the liquid's motion and scene scale; the estimator should treat them as extra particle load.
