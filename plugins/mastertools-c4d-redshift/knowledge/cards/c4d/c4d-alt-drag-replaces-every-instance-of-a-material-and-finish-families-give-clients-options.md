Greyscalegorilla promo for the Plus library (400 new materials) [0:34-2:08]: drop a material onto the object, then hold Alt or Option while dropping to replace all instances of the existing material at once [1:06]. Pattern families (perforated, embossed) come in metal, black metal and plastic variants, so switching only the plain gold and embossed gold materials turned a gold logo to chrome in a few clicks, for showing the client or rendering [1:37]. Library claimed compatible with Redshift, Octane and others; extras are badges, patterns, podiums, stadium lighting models. Sales video: treat feature claims as unverified.

**Use it:** Keep a logo scene with one material slot per finish role so the client's "try silver" request is a single swap.
