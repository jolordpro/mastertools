Arrangement sets how clones nest: interlock for spheres, stacked for cylinders such as cans, with sizing from radius or bounding box [09:47-10:50]. Shape options are triangle pyramid, quad pyramid and the two grid variants [08:11-08:41].
Scaling the source sphere makes the pile grow and shrink, because the distribution is scale-aware [10:18].
Adds the arrangement choice to the existing cannonball card, which lists only the shapes and colour modes.
