Create the camera after choosing Redshift as renderer so it is an RS camera; choose a real lens (100 mm for a tabletop product) and work at real scale [11:05-12:54].
Studio-lit tabletop: around f/5.6; shallower depth at f/4 or f/2.8 [23:55-24:59]. Show the focus plane in the display tab to see what is sharp.
Focus on a small coloured null stuck to the surface so focus tracks or can rack; turn the display off afterwards. Save the finished camera as a preset [14:59-17:04].

**Use it:** the coach can set photographer-style defaults and teach the focus-null approach for pre-2027 versions.
