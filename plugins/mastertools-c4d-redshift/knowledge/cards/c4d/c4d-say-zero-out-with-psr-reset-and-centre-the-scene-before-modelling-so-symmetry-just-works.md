Say's first step is to zero everything out and centre the scene; he calls PSR Reset (position, scale, rotation) in R21 the best feature for that [08:22]. Because the objects sit at zero, grouping the wing pieces and dropping them in a Symmetry object mirrors them correctly [26:22-26:39]. He also lists a Scroll-to-First-Active command in the Object Manager to jump to the selected object in a scene with thousands of elements [34:31-35:02].
Transfers: scene check could flag objects with a non-zero origin before symmetry or cloner work.
