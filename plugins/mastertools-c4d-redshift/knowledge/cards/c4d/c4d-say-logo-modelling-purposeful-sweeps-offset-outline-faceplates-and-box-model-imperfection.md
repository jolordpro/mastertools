Spline-based modelling plus Sweep is fast to iterate and lets you dial rounding [10:59-11:30]. Every sweep should be purposeful; layering many gets messy fast, and thick edges betray the typography [12:04-13:06]. Create Outline offsets a spline in or out, used for a gold trim or an inset faceplate with a tiny bevel, which Say copies from the NBA Finals look [16:13-17:17]. R21's rounding feature is preferred to a four-step bevel for a smooth flight-school look [17:17]. For the wings he box models: a cube rotated 45 degrees, made editable, Ctrl-drag faces along the shape in an ortho view, a Hypernurbs, then loop cuts with the knife; the loose alignment adds human imperfection and interesting reflections, versus a procedural sweep that came out flat on top [18:18-21:57, 25:37]. A Spline Smooth tool makes a clean spline in one click [24:02-24:33]. For lofts keep the same number of points in the same order on each spline, or the surface twists; Set First Point repairs order [44:52-45:55].
Transfers: know what each approach yields and pick one per job.
