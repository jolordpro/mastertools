[31:33] Drop another object or a whole scatter into the Proximity list and clones near it vanish. Mode is Mask (erase) or Scale (shrink), never both. Distance cannot go negative; bias sets the transition; invert, inside and outside flip it [32:03 to 33:36].
[33:36] Working from large to small: boulders erase trees, trees erase bushes. Objects inside a null often do not register because the bounding box is lost [42:54]; use single objects.

**Use it:** give the coach this order of work for layered scatters, and the null warning when a proximity seems ignored.
