Mirror text on Y, then Remove Outside chops at the plane and Weld joins the halves into one spline that can feed a Sweep [30:20-30:51]. Hold the 7 key to move the mirror parent without its children. Radial mode plus mirror gives kaleidoscope shapes [31:23].

**Use it:** Useful for logo or type construction; one continuous spline sweeps cleanly.
