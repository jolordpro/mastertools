Shape one channel (for example X: fast in, long ease out), select its first and last key, Copy Ease, then select the other curves and Paste Ease [38:35-39:06]. The curves adapt to their own scale, so the camera keeps the subject framed while timing changes.

**Use it:** Reshape time, not path: a curve metric can compare normalised curves across channels of one move.
