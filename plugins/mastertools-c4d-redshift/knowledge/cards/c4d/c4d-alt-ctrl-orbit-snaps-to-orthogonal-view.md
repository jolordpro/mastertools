Jonas (Maxon trainer) showed that Alt-orbit with Ctrl added snaps the view to the nearest orthogonal view, and the view stays there until Alt+Ctrl is used again to leave it [27:52-28:23].
Saves switching to the four-view layout just to check an alignment.

**Use it:** coach: tip for aligning elements in a layout without leaving the perspective viewport.
