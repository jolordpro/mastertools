Greyscalegorilla, 2023 [20:17-26:32]. Credits Chad Ashley for the trick.
- Key light from an HDRI dome; a second dome acts as fill/background and adds reflection and diffuse from its background colour; those contributions can be dialled in Details [20:17-21:19].
- Background: eyedrop a main colour from the scene (orange here), then open the picker and nudge a bit brighter or darker and less saturated. He checks both; here brighter tan beat the grey [25:00-26:02].
- Add a rim area light inside a null and rotate the null to aim it, which keeps placement easy; dim it, because the first value was far too bright [21:51-22:22].
- Output 1280x1280 at 30 fps; he tests animation at low or medium quality for timing, then raises it to remove grain [24:30].

**Use it:** A fast way to keep a flat studio background in the same family as the subject.
