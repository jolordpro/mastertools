Imsety (Set Angle) needed a face extruding out of an iPad ZModeler demo. He built it as two objects, a cube animated from the world axis, rather than keyframing the actual model's vertices [13:36-14:36].
Reason given: faking it kept the animation flexible and easy to change. He recreated ZBrush elements in the background inside C4D too [13:36].

**Use it:** Coach: a faked move on separate objects is easier to retime than point-level animation; choose it when the client may change the beat.
