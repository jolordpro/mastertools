[18:36] Altitude min/max crops clones by height; 'breakup' turns the hard edge into a sprinkle [19:06]. Slope runs 0 (flat) to 90 degrees (wall): raising the min removes flats, inverting keeps only flats [19:38].
Boundary keeps clones near or (inverted) away from open edges so rocks do not hang off the map [20:40]. Curvature [21:42]: convex means open crests, concave means crevices (cave = dark), values are clamped; 'both' is confusing.
Example rules: boulders not in water, trees on convex ground, bushes at the shoreline [29:28 to 36:11].

**Use it:** teach scatter looks as rules (where it may be, where it may not) and list the four filters when the coach explains how to avoid objects in the water or on steep ground.
