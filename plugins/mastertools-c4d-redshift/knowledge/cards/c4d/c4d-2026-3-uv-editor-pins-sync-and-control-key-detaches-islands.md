Noseman, 2026.3 [08:02-11:10]. Sync Selection between UV and 3D (a button at the bottom): he keeps it on nearly always; off gives independent selections. Pins: set pins on a polygon and it will not move; you can now deactivate pins temporarily, move other things, then reactivate, rather than clearing them as before [08:32-09:04]. Keep UV Connectivity (found by Shift-C, hidden on purpose because it is on by default) means moved UV polygons stay attached to neighbours; hold Control to break one off, reusing the key that makes copies in 3D, since a UV copy does not exist as a concept [09:35-11:10].

**Use it:** Pin a good island, tweak the neighbours, un-pin, without losing the pin set.
