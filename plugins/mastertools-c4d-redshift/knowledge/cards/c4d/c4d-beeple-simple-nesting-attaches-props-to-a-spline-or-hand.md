To make a goat follow a character spline he just nests the object under it ("simple hierarchy, not big magic") so it moves with the spline [24:48-25:19]. To put an item in a hand, nest it under the hand [25:19]. For thrown toilet paper he does "a nice little sweep" with the roll to cheat the action instead of simulating, then adds a streak by spline wrap [36:19-37:23].

**Use it:** In quick turnarounds, parent first and cheat motion with a sweep before reaching for a sim.
