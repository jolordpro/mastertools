Say recommends Substance Source materials as prepackaged bump, height, normals, colour and reflectivity channels that work in Redshift and standard, native in C4D [32:10-32:52]. The Substance engine by default tops out at 2K; switching the engine in preferences from the default to a Direct option (caption garbled, probably a graphics-API engine; unverified) unlocks up to 8K, with image-based and procedural substances behaving differently [32:52-33:50]. After tiling, check bevel and pattern scale (eight repeats each way in his demo) [34:00-35:35]. Substance layers give uneven metalness response under area lights [36:07].
Verify before use: version-specific and possibly outdated.
