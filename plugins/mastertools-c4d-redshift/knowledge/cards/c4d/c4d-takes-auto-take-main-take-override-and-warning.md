Bas Helmig and Bart Brinkman (Italian Giants, Dutch 3D studio; Maxon 3D and Motion Design Show): a take lets different values (a camera animation, a door open or closed) sit on the same object, avoiding extra cameras on one timeline or extra files [22:15-23:48].
Before takes, you had to continue frame numbers past 150 so file names never overwrote; now a render token puts the take name in the saved file [23:48-24:19].
Auto Take records changed values into the active take; a button jumps to the Main take so a global change affects all [26:28-27:30]. A take can also carry render settings such as a resolution [27:30-28:01].
Their warning: practise on a small scene first, because takes can become chaotic [28:01-28:33].

**Use it:** Scene check: confirm the output path has the take token and that Auto Take is not accidentally recording unrelated changes.
