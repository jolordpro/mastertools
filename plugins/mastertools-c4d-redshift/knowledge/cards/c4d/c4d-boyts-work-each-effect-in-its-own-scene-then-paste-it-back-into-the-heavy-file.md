Boyts (freelance motion/character artist, Los Angeles; NAB 2014) says once the scene gets heavy he stops working in it: to key a light's timing he copies the light into its own scene with three planes, settles timing, then pastes it back; timing and object are identical [11:40-12:41]. The energy shield is a sphere with two noises animated separately, used in both alpha and reflection channels, built alone, then attached to the character and scaled up to activate [12:41-13:12]. For the glass cut he again removed everything except the tube, the slice and the break [14:18]. His line: work on one thing at a time and focus [12:10].
Transfers: for heavy C4D + Redshift scenes, lookdev and timing in a stripped scene saves IPR time.

**Use it:** Scene check can suggest isolating a problem object in a new document when load or IPR is slow.
