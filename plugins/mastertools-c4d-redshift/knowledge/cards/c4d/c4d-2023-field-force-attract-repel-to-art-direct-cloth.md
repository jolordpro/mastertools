EJ [19:18-24:00]. A Field Force shows vector lines; a spherical field with positive strength pulls cloth into its centre, negative repels. Moving the field drags the cloth around, a pulsing wave if it goes up and down [19:19-20:53]. Strength 250 in the demo [21:24].
At that version Turbulence forces did not work together with a field force (he hoped for a fix); workaround: add a Random field in the Field Force's list, set it to Multiply with lower opacity, animate it, or Overlay and Max to push undulation across the whole cloth [21:24-22:58].
Record the field's motion by hand: Cappuccino (Animate menu, real-time start) records mouse movement into keyframes [22:58-23:29].

**Use it:** Art-direct a cloth hit or reveal by animating one field, with no keyframing on the cloth itself.
