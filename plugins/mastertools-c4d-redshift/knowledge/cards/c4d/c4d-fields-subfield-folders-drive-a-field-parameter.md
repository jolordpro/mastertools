Maxon trainers (Ask the Trainer: Fields, Apr 2022, Cinema 4D 2022 era). Unfold the plus on a field in the field list and a folder appears for a parameter, for example Offset on a radial field. Any field placed inside that folder drives that parameter, so a spherical field inside Offset spirals the radial field [04:18-05:50].
A Decay modifier layer on a moving spherical field leaves a trail; a Random field dropped into the decay's Effect Strength subfield folder breaks the trail up [06:23-07:58]. Opacity on the sub field sets how strongly it steers the parent.

**Use it:** Reach for a subfield before stacking more effectors when one field parameter needs its own variation: offset spirals, ragged trails, uneven growth.
