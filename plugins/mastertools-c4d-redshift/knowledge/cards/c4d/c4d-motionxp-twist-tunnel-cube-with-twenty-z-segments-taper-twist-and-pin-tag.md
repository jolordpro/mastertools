Cinema 4D Lite comes with After Effects. The tunnel render (H.264, 15 Mb/s) is loaded as a luminance material with colour and reflectance off, so colours stay flat; projection set to flat, tile off, with scale 960 by 540 [05:17-08:25]. The cube needs 20 Z segments or the twist fails; a taper at 100 percent strength with curvature 0 makes the tunnel, then current state to object, points optimised to one tip and the front polygon deleted [05:49-07:22]. The twist deformer only affects the texture once a pin material tag is added; angle keyed from about 50 degrees from frame 30 to near the end [08:25-09:58]. Match FPS to 24 and 1920 by 1080; washed colours fixed with +10 saturation [09:58-10:29]. Ordinary Folk's own process page revealed the C4D method [04:46].

**Use it:** For a twisting tunnel use a segmented cube with a taper, a twist and a pin tag, and a luminance texture.
