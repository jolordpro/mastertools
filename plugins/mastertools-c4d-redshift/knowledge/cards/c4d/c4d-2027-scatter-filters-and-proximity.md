Scatter generator in MoGraph: children are the items, a surface object is set separately. Area-weighted is the most natural distribution; relax avoids intersections [02:37-04:44].
Filters: altitude (no boulders in the riverbed), slope (none on steep faces), boundary (grass along walls, invertible), curvature [05:47-08:20]. Clustering groups species naturally; a Scatter tag overrides per item including probability [10:27-12:00].
Proximity: restrict or scale items near another scatter, for small rocks around boulders or moss under a tree; spline projection puts mushrooms in a circle [08:20-13:33].

**Use it:** scatter-based set dressing for sports or product scenes; the coach can name these filters.
