The default attractor force grows near the centre, so particles whip past and fly off [59:48]. A newer falloff type list includes Constant, which pulls at constant speed [1:00:19]; far particles can be flung to the opposite side until the pull wins.

**Use it:** Fix for particles that overshoot a target; was previously done with a field falloff.
