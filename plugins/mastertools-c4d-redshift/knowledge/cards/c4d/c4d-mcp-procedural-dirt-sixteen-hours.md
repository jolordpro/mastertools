[02:53:33 to 03:00:45] First answer was a single noise through a ramp. After two days of examples (combine several noises, mask layers, recipes for pebbles, clumps and cracks) results improved.
Python cannot place nodes, so they arrive stacked on each other; he arranged them by hand [02:56:08]. One useful idea from the assistant: extract slope from a bump map to find pebble walls, though a bug stopped it working [02:58:12 to 02:59:14].
He estimates it would have been faster alone.
