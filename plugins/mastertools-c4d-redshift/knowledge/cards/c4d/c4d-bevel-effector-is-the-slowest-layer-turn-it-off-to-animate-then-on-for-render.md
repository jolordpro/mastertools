Greyscalegorilla, 2023.
- The Bevel effector keeps the object non-editable and works on swapped objects. Use proportional rather than fixed distance, and raise subdivision to round more [04:11-05:14].
- It is the heaviest part of the stack; when playback is slow, disable it and animate, then re-enable for the final render [05:14, 17:12].
- His look rule: if the result looks too sharp or too 3D, add a bevel, because small bevels catch light and improve the look [05:14].
- Heavy geometry plus bevel slows the machine; a much denser replacement object will not hold up [23:26].

**Use it:** A rule for the coach: bevel late, and a light-catching edge is the first fix for "too CG".
