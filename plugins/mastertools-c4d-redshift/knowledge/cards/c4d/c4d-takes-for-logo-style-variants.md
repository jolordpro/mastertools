For a 2020 VPN piece Imsety built a logo that passes through different styles, inspired by a similar moment of Sakane Solomon, and used the Take system to hold the style variants [30:32-31:04].
One look was built without modelling: cloned cylinders with a Bend deformer, with a glass look [31:04-31:36].

**Use it:** Coach: one scene plus Takes beats duplicate project files when a logo needs several looks.
