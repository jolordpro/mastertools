Jay Worth's litmus test: he can still see and feel the entire performance [28:37-29:39]. Shots can look polished and still fail because, laid next to the original, something in the eyes or rhythm is missing, so the check is a side-by-side with the actor's take, not a vacuum look [29:39-30:11]. His view: an old actor playing young fails on body and movement, not only the face. AI tools in the pipeline are still artist led, with creative notes steering the result [27:04-28:37]. Subtle stabilisation matters too: on a practical head prop, even faint breathing motion broke the illusion, so it was stabilised [24:29-25:01].

**Use it:** review rule for any retouch, tracking or cleanup: compare against the unprocessed source for the feel, and hunt motion that should not be there.
