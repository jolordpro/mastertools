In a short first-person piece, Beeple says colour grading is perfect for emphasizing a graphic rather than overwhelming it, and Red Giant's Magic Bullet Looks gives footage the final touch; for Flying Lotus's "Kill Your Co-Workers" colour sharpened the visuals [§1]. For fly-throughs he draws basic geometry in Cinema 4D then perfects particles with Trapcode Particular in After Effects, because particles can be tweaked and re-rendered fast without hours of rendering [§1]. The text reads as tool promotion.

**Use it:** Keep the grade as a late, light pass; if it fights the graphic, back it off.
