Exposure multiplies the whole range, like sliding the gradient left or right; gamma changes the curve so tones move by different amounts [1:23:08-1:24:42].
The trainer says gamma is not really contrast but is easier to think of as contrast. His own description of what stays fixed is muddled on the captions, so treat the detail as unsure.

**Use it:** tooltip wording for a grade step: exposure for overall light, gamma for mid-tone feel.
