On the Rampage creatures, a gorilla head proxy and grey and chrome balls were filmed under the production lights. Digital proxies were lit with image-based lighting until they matched the plates; only then did lighters add beauty lighting to strengthen shots. Winquist: "Once we had a match, we knew that the foundation was solid." The albino George choice was partly a lighting decision, since a black gorilla would be lost in dust. [§4-§5]

**Use it:** Lock the neutral match against reference first; polish only starts after the match.
