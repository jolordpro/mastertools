USC ETC Pathways [22:40-28:16]: a Jetset camera plate, a Blender-built background, retracked camera, a quick slap comp, then relit by SwitchX. The result looked soft on a theatrical screen.
A frequency-separation pass of the source plate was multiplied back onto the SwitchX output for detail and cleaner edges [25:13-27:54]. SwitchLight passes (normals, plus a better masking tool) let the cinematographer shape bounce on faces [26:16].
The first shot with a stable workflow arrived about a year after the project began, and every shot tried a different model [27:54].

**Use it:** after any AI relight or upres, rebuild fine detail from the source plate before judging sharpness on a big screen.
