He says the skin node exists because especially in TV people ask for small refinements to an actress's skin: less wrinkles, pimples and similar details. He wants a node where he can select the skin and do what is needed, and he has used the Resolve beauty plug-in extensively because it is fast and gives a good result. The core balance still comes first, with no secondary on skin. [§1]

**Use it:** Treat skin cleanup as a separate, late, optional step after the primary balance is right.
