Kelly [1:15:28-1:18:33] contrasts two kinds of job. On a small indie with limited lights, night exteriors need nursing: make them legible, clean and not noisy. On Early Risers (Alexa, healthy negative, shadow detail intact because ISO was not cranked) he asked what could bring out texture and variation in the shadows even more. A better starting image made matching easier and left time for creative suggestions.

**Use it:** triage a frame as "rescue" or "push" before touching it; push frames earn texture and contrast work, rescue frames earn only legibility.
