Kelly [11:44-15:25]: the eye has far more rods (contrast) than cones (colour), so the first question in a match is not which wheel to turn but whether exposure and contrast ratio sit in the same place. Get the shadows, mids and highlights of two shots roughly overlapping and he says you are well over halfway done; skip it and no amount of lift/gamma/gain will fix a contrast mismatch.
Then balance by broad, soft moves (his rule "macro beats micro") and watch the two signal masses overlap in the vectorscope. A colorist sees more than a viewer will, so an overlap is a reasonable exit, and further nuance is a bonus of budget and time, not a failure if skipped.

**Use it:** order for comp and look-matching of renders: match luminance and contrast first (with a waveform or false colour), colour second. Supports Inhofer's order-of-work cards from a second pro.
