He did one of the first fully digital films, Collateral, whose night LA needed sensitive cameras to render pockets of lamp and building light with fog [02:43-03:13]. He notes film characteristics are easy to forget until you grade Star Wars or Wonder Woman shot on film [03:13-03:45]. Digital cameras from Red or Arri are all good now; the choice is what suits the project [03:45-04:17].

**Use it:** Pick the render or capture path by the look and cost needed, not habit.
