He says scopes measure code values but the eye does not; two images can look matched with very different RGB values [17:51-19:23]. After more than a year he restarted the tool on a perceptual basis, after help from a neuroscientist [18:22-19:23].
Several single-task networks pick frames, identify content (shirt, face, close-up) and lighting, and assign a different matching approach for close-up to wide versus wide to wide [19:55-20:57].
The only other firm he names doing this is Colorfront [18:22]. Claims are about his own product, so low confidence.

**Use it:** When judging a match, compare by eye and by skin or neutral reference, not scope values alone.
