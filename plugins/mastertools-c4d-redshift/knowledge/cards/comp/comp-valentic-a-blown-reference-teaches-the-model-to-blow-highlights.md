He calls the model a copy machine for your looks [34:58-35:29]. When the reference had blown highlights, it tried to recreate blown highlights in every shot; the team had to add case-specific logic to protect highlights, one failure at a time [27:41-28:44].
The model stays fixed after release; later versions could be selected per type of show, as a documentary and an eerie drama need different models [34:00-36:31].

**Use it:** Check that a reference frame has headroom before matching other shots to it.
