Inhofer and host Vincent [38:21-43:18]: awards depend on parameters (time, direction, raw footage) so they often reflect trends more than skill or obstacles overcome [39:41]. To judge fairly you would want the raw footage viewed through a normalised LUT to see what had to be overcome, but DPs seldom allow raw state to be shown [40:12].
A Beijing contest he judged: 8-12 colourists graded the same unseen footage, one hour, hands off at the end; year 1 a promo with 7-8 shots, year 2 a mini documentary with more shots, so time management mattered [41:14-42:47]. He found the process each colourist used as interesting as the score, and the criteria vague [41:44].
