For Halt and Catch Fire the palette was an iridescent red that stopped short of hot magenta, graded so the images stayed readable.
Grain, texture and undulating light flares kept the red from going flat. Clair's working line, as reported: "If it doesn't make your eyes bleed, then it needs more saturation."

Page spot: §3-§4.

**Use it:** If a saturated palette feels flat, add grain and moving light instead of lowering the colour. Keep a readability check on the grade.
