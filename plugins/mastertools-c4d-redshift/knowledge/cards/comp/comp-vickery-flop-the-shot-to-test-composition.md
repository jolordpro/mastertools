Vickery says that midway through work, after seeing a shot a thousand times, he flops it to see if it still works. If it fails mirrored, the composition is inherently broken. Edwards' shots survived a flop of the whole film, which Vickery credits to his cinematographer's eye and the time spent on composition in post [18:40, 19:11]. Edwards adds that unmotivated or oddly choreographed camera moves destroy the feel of realism, so the camera should look motivated by what is in the frame [17:08, 18:08].

**Use it:** Add a quick flop test to the final-pass for frames and cuts: mirror the frame and see whether balance, lead lines and weight still hold. Avoid on anything with text or logos.
