The opening ten minutes show the client the colorist has an approach, even if they scrap it. Preparing earlier (drive in the night before, conform checked) keeps technical problems out of the creative time. If the client is not ready, tell them in advance what they are walking into. [§5-§6]

**Use it:** Open a review with the plan and a checked file, not a fix-it session.
