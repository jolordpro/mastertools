He says physical explosions always give an unexpected result because everything happens as one connected system, which you cannot hand place [37:04-38:37]. The Inception mountain-face blow-up was built as a roughly 45 ft miniature in a Santa Monica parking lot and blown up with pyrotechnics, then extended digitally (extra bits, painted landscape and background) [37:35-38:37]. He separates special effects (on-set, physical, production phase) from visual effects (post phase, separate budget) [34:59-36:33]. Existing card on practical-first work covers the same stance.
**Use it:** When a CG sim or burst looks too placed, shoot or find a real reference of the full system and use it to judge the randomness and spread.
