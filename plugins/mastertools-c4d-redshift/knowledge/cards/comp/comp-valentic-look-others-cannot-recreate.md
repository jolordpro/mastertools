He says a colorist's value is a creative decision that people with the same software cannot reproduce, and gives a story where agency staff copied his first-pass grade and then could not continue it without his tool [§19-§21]. Own every tool and try them all, since each does a thing well, but one-trick tools stamp their own signature on every image [§22-§23]. Source is a tool maker, so read it as a sales-adjacent view.

**Use it:** Judge a look by the idea behind it, not by which plugin made it; note when a look reads as a tool's default.
