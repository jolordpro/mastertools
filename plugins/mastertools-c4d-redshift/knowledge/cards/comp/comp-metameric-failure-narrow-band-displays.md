Walter Volpatto (feature colorist, Picture Shop; captions spell him "Valpado") compared a digital projector to a film print, measured the same white point with a spectrometer, yet he saw the projector as greener and his producer saw it as more magenta [36:56-38:29].
Matching it by eye in the grading system made it worse for the other person, proving it was a metameric mismatch, not a measurement fault [38:29-39:32].
Cause: a narrow spectral band hits each person's cone response at a slightly different point, most visibly in green. Projector makers widen the band, for example by using several diode frequencies [39:32-41:37].
Takeaway: humans judge only by comparison [37:27]. Transfers: in a client room or LED wall, use a reference side by side before arguing about hue.

**Use it:** Review note: when two reviewers see different casts on a wide-gamut or laser display, compare both to a measured reference before changing the grade.
