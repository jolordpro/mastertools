For tense moments he asks whether the dread visible on the actor's face reads on the ape. If it does not, the team asks why and keeps working until the emotional read exactly mirrors the performance. With 12 speaking characters, the facial data came from two head-mounted cameras, giving depth for lip and jaw detail, and this translation was the biggest focus of the film. [§2]

His summary is that it is a translation [09:53]. The question the animator asks is what emotion reads on the actor's face and what the ape face must do to give the same read [09:53-10:25]. Casting looked for actors who play truthfully as humans, not ape impressions; they spent about eight weeks in 'ape school' in prep, so the movement was already internal when cameras rolled [10:25-10:56].
