Inhofer accepts instinctive client notes if the picture still looks good on his reference display, and objects when a note, formed on the client's own screen, would clearly make it worse. For remote clients he asks whether they have an iPad 3, which he expects to be more accurate than most devices (2016 advice). He grades to a common standard so programs sit together, rather than adapting to a monitor that is off spec.

Page spot: §6-§7.

**Use it:** Always state the viewing device for a note. Check phone and tablet as well as the reference monitor.
