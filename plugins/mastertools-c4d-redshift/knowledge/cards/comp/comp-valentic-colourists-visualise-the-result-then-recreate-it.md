While he graded in a brain scanner, scientists saw activity tied to imagination just before he moved controls, which he reads as picturing the look and then building it [21:27-22:59]. He also reports steady alpha-wave activity, like meditation, when work flows [23:30-24:02].
He argues tools should be learned so well that the hands run without looking at the interface, since looking at menus pulls the brain back to logic [24:32-25:04]. One person's report, so low confidence.

**Use it:** Keep controls on a surface or shortcuts so judging looks is not interrupted by menu hunting.
