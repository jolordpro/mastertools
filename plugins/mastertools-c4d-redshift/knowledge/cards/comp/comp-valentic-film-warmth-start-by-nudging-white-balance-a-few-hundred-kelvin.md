He says to move the temperature dial for film emulation by about 500 Kelvin or 200 Kelvin, because film work used D60-type light rather than D65 and the small shift reads as more natural [21:58-22:30]. Captions are garbled around the numbers and the D60 claim; treat the exact figures as unconfirmed.

**Use it:** If a film-like grade looks cold, test a small warm shift before changing colour balance.
