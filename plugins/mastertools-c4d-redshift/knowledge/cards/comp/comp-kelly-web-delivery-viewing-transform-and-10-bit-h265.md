Kelly [8:35-11:11] admits he has little control over compression, and does two things: judge through a web viewing transform so tonality on typical devices matches the reference monitor, and encode H.265 with the Main 10 profile (10-bit instead of 8-bit), which keeps the bottom end from clumping and falling apart. He says Resolve 18 made Main 10 the default, so he had seen shadow breakup before that. Beyond this he calls himself naive about encoder settings.

**Use it:** a delivery check for dark scenes: 10-bit encode and a look at the shadows in the encoded file, not the master.
