CGStirk [09:35-11:54]. Matching the clipped hot core of a reference light looks like poor colour work, so match the glow instead. Order: Curves to crush self-illumination, Glow under it (raise radius; threshold is the brightness at which glow starts, lower = easier glow), then Hue/Saturation under that. Through fog and distance, lights look less saturated: take Saturation down about 30 to 40, rotate hue 5 to 10 degrees toward the reference, and expect far lights to drift bluer. Finish with a small Curves to pop it.

**Use it:** Reference for emissive or light-up logo comp: build the glow, then desaturate and shift for distance. Do not match clipped plate highlights.
