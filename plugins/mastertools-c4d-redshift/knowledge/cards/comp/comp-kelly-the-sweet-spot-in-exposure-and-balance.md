Kelly [1:11:50-1:14:26] says that with a good pipeline, a look and client trust, two simple global moves (where exposure sits, and how much red, green and blue) each have a spot where the picture is clearly right and an inch either way is not. Colours then separate: warm feels warm, cool feels cool, and the whole thing looks natural but artful. He treats finding it shot after shot as the anchor, and only then asks how far to take nuance.
It is a shared reaction in the room, not a private taste.

**Use it:** give the coach a two-step "done" test: scrub exposure and balance across a small range and look for the single point where the frame snaps right before touching secondary tweaks.
