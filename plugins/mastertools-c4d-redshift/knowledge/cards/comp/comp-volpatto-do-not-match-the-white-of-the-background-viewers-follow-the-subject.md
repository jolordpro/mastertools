He makes the subject of the scene right in exposure and balance, not the background; a warm or sad day or a heavy green look has no neutral white in it [39:35-40:06]. In Dunkirk the audience watches planes and bullets, not the clouds, which were never consistent [23:56-24:26, 40:06]. He then matches adjacent shots with minimal moves [40:38-41:08].

**Use it:** Judge white balance on the subject and skin, and allow the world to drift where nobody looks.
