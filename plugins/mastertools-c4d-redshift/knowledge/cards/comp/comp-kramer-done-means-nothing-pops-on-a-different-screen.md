Kramer [15:31-17:07]: a shot is never fully done, but you stop when you feel good about the overall design and then spend time making sure nothing stands out.
Compositing is layers melted into the shot: it needs the blur and the grain so it feels part of it and the viewer's mind does not say something looks strange [16:04].
His check: view the shot on a phone, a TV or another screen and look at first glance for what pops out or draws the eye [16:35]. After staring at it on your desk it is burned into your mind, so change the context. Also lean back and squint; squinting (blurring everything) makes the odd bit obvious [17:07].

**Use it:** Add to the final-pass checklist: review on a phone-size view and with a squint or blur for pops before sign-off.
