Matching only needs shot-versus-shot judgement, easy even when tired, but setting a scene's colour is absolute judgement [37:00-37:30]. He takes a walk about hourly, but daylight then blinds his eyes for a while; switching between a colourful and a colourless project can need 20 minutes to adjust [36:27-37:00, 35:57-36:27]. HDR makes the adjustment harder because of its greater presence [36:27].

**Use it:** Schedule look decisions before matching passes, and re-check a frame after a long break.
