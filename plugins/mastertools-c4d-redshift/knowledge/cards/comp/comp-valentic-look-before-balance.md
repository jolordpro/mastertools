His order: design the look pre-shoot, shoot tests and finalise from them, apply it in camera and check, cut and review with it on, start the grade with the looks already on the timeline. He rejects balance-everything-then-add-look for HDR.
Claimed gains: executives see the intended result in dailies, VFX fits better, and no time is spent reinventing the look.

**Use it:** For render batches, fix the look on the first draft and apply it from the first review frame, not at the end.
