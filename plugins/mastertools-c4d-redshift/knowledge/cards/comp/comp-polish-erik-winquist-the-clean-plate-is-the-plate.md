Plates are shot without actors so apes drop straight in with no paint-out. Handheld gave a fluid feel but made clean plates harder; motion control keeps plates aligned but slows the shoot. Winquist judged the handheld result worth the cost. His guiding rule: "The clean plate is the plate." Priority: carry the actor's performance faithfully into the ape.
