Remote grading in 2020 meant starting in 709 because HDR could not be done at home [13:28-14:00]. He built a colour-science model to go from 709 to HDR and 709 to theatrical, and looked at both at the end of each day to see if the scenes still worked [14:32-15:33]. Near the end a calibrated room streamed 4K for a full HDR trim and a projection trim [15:33]. Before starting he watched the director's cut start to end and read the source comic for original intent [12:57-13:59].

**Use it:** Grade in a base space and test the HDR and SDR conversions daily, rather than at the end.
