In a May 2015 interview he says his job is bringing soul back to digital imagery, which is crisp, detailed and clean but often leaves viewers without emotional connection. His answer is to render and display digital images in a more analog, film-like way, comparing it to the richer sound people seek from vinyl, tapes and old amplifiers. [§1]

**Use it:** Texture and warmth are part of finish. If a frame feels sterile, check micro-texture and tonal warmth before adding sharpness.
