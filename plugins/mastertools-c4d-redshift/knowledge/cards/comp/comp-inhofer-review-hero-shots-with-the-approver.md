Inhofer pulls a small representative set to work on with the person who can say no, because the client who walks in is often not the one who can force a redo. The set is the best and worst looking shots plus important ones, about 15 or 20 for a long-form project, used to learn quickly how the client sees. The approver may only be available for an hour or two. Prep helps: he gets the drive the night before to check the conform, and tells a client whose edit is still moving to expect waiting while it conforms.

**Use it:** Gate reviews on a short hero set with the approver, not the whole batch.
