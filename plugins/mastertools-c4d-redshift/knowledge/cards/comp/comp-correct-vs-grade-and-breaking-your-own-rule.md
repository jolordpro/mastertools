Walter Volpatto (feature colorist, Picture Shop; captions spell him "Valpado") divides it: correction fixes mismatches so a scene feels like one place and time; grading is the visual palette, better the more it supports the story [47:51-48:52].
Story case: a flashback language was very light, very warm, glowy; but a funeral memory in that language gave the wrong message, so the team broke its own rule and backed off the warmth [46:16-47:51].
Overdone colour becomes a thing in itself and may stop helping the story [48:52].

**Use it:** Package check: when a template look meets an off-tone moment (injury, loss), allow a deliberate exception and log it.
