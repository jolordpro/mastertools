Mouse grading is one thing at a time with the tip of an index finger, slow and tedious when you want variations of a theme [51:50-52:22]. With two hands on the panel he can work contrast with one hand and colour balance with the other and run through 9, 10 or 15 variations in 45 seconds to a minute [52:22-52:53]. He stresses it is not that he is a better colorist but that his setup lets him explore more options and settle quickly. Moving four or five controls against each other reaches looks you would not find one control at a time [53:26]. Entry advice: the small Ripple panel (price said as "350", currency not stated; speaker unclear) is a low-cost start [51:20].

**Use it:** The principle transfers to 3D: set up lookdev so several parameters can be varied at once with a quick compare (live IPR plus presets), not one slider at a time.
