Kelly [1:00:46-1:04:22]: the line between enhancing and changing photography is felt, not ruled. He states his role early ("I want to take what you did and build it up") and repeats it when needed. If a client keeps pushing a direction he is not suited to, he may tell them mid-job that he is not the right person, take responsibility, and bill the time so far, rather than blame them. He says admitting a direction is outside his comfort often opens a useful talk about what is misaligned. He warns not to bluff this.
Separately [6:06-8:02]: in a new medium (animation, HDR, a first commercial) don't talk down your own thumbprint because someone says "you can't do that in animation".

**Use it:** a scoping line for pitches: state what you are best at and what you are not, early.
