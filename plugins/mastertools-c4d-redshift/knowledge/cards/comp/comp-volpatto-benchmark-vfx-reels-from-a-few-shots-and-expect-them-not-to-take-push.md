For the last reel of Rampage, about 450 shots, he began with only three close-ups of hands; the temp effects sent were unusable [26:37-27:08]. He eyeballs a benchmark from the few shots available, then readjusts as more come in; he says visual effects usually do not withstand a lot of push and pull [27:08-27:39].

**Use it:** Ask for finished anchor frames early; accept that composited elements limit how far the grade can go.
