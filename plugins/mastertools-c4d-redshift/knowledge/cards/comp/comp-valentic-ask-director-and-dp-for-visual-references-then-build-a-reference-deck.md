He asks the director and cinematographer to show inspiration, whether poetry, a photography book, an artist or a painting, because a picture speaks more than a verbal description [03:19-03:54].
Workflow: build a deck of stills, import them into the grading tool, pick a still as the hero reference, match the shot to it with an auto-match, and play with it, cycling through candidates to find which one "speaks" most [10:02-12:41]. Judge on how skin tone feels first [12:41-13:14].
About four reference stills are attached to the project for later client documents [33:57-34:28].
He rejected references that were too desaturated or low-contrast for a happy romance, i.e. choose references to the story's mood [07:13, 04:26].

**Use it:** Brief template: ask for 3-5 visual references per job and log which one is the hero.
