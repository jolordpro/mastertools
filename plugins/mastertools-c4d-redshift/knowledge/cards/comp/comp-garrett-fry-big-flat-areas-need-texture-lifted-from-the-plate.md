Garrett Fry [09:35-10:05, 13:47-24:40]: avoid solid-colour areas; add small kicks of light, corrugation and little dark lines. Painted lines on the ground vary in thickness, fade and crack: cut one from the high-res plate and squeeze it into the distance. The shadow shapes were nice but lacked ground tooth under them, so a soft split appears; stretch plate ground into the right perspective. A contact shadow that was too thick can go. Interesting tidbits sell it.

**Use it:** Texture-check every large flat area, borrowing from the plate.
