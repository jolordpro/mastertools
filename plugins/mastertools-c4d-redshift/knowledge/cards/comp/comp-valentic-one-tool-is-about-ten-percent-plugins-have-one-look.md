Guest: Dado Valentic (colorist and colour scientist; he sells Look Designer and ColourLab AI, so treat his claims as a vendor's). He says people ask "which tool gives this look?" and he puts the right tool at roughly 10% of the path [51:24-52:25].
His reason: some tools are one-trick ponies. A film-emulation box or a bullet-style look pack will always look like that tool no matter what you do, and he says "everything looks like" the latter. He keeps every tool anyway, as a mechanic keeps every key, and judges the creative decision as what gets valued [51:24-52:57].
He adds that agencies re-doing a grade with another colourist's own settings cannot reproduce a look made with his subtractive colour model; there is no curve that does the same [47:10-47:42].

**Use it:** Reviewing a look: if it is clearly one preset's signature, the note is about the decision (what story it serves), not about swapping presets.
