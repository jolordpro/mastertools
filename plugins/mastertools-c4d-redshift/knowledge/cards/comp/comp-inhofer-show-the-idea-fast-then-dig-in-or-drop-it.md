Discussing a mock competition whose last round changes direction mid-session [43:18-44:49], Inhofer says the real skill beyond shot matching, balancing and reading scopes is client management: the discussion, sometimes talking them out of an idea, or showing it as quickly as you can so the idea can be dropped.
His rough rate: about two times out of ten the idea surprises you and works, and then you dig deeper into it [44:19-44:49]. Captions blur who says the "genius" reaction; this advice is attributed to Inhofer with moderate care.
The mock contest: round 1 match shots, round 2 a given mood, final 12 minutes with directions that change live [43:18-44:19].

**Use it:** In a client session, the coach says to show a quick rough of the odd idea, then decide, rather than debating it.
