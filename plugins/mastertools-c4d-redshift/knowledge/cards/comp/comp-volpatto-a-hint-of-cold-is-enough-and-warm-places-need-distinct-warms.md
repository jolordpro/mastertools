A happy scene graded all blue conflicts with its emotion, like dramatic music on a plain scene, unless deliberate [19:44-20:47]. On The Old Guard, three desert locations were all warm but with three different tones, so the audience knows where they are [20:47-21:20]. In a restored Antarctica documentary, an old transfer was so cyan the emperor penguins' neck colours were lost; making the snow white restored colour and it still felt cold [21:20-21:51]. This supports comp-correct-vs-grade-and-breaking-your-own-rule (the warm flashback that had to go cold) [44:15-44:45].

**Use it:** State the emotion first, then pick the colour; keep temperature to a hint if colour must be preserved.
