Kramer (Video Copilot) calls overdone light wrap his big pet peeve [46:58]. Light does not simply glow round a person; background bleed over a subject only happens where it is bright enough to bloom the lens, and matching ambient colour is a different job from wrapping the edges [47:30]. He notes a heavy wrap once helped hide a CG look but is now the typical sign of a green-screen composite.
Transfers: any CG or keyed element over a plate, including C4D/Redshift renders comped over footage in AE.

**Use it:** In a comp check, look for a visible halo at the subject edge; keep wrap only where a bright source sits behind the subject, and keep it small.
Transcript support: Tanzillo also says light wrap must not be obvious; edge softening 1 to 2 px and the wrap graded down on hair (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cP3AELeZf6o [16:38])
