The slower AGFA profile gave a limited colour container for skin even across dark and pale tones, so faces read as one unified family with no patchiness [1:00:42-1:01:47]. He says sometimes saturation is really desaturation: what you take out enhances something else, and since the character is the important thing, not scenery, limit everything else [1:01:47-1:02:52]. The reds look stronger partly because greens and blues are constrained, a perceptual effect [1:04:28-1:04:58].

**Use it:** In a hero frame, pull back competing hues before pushing the skin or brand colour.
