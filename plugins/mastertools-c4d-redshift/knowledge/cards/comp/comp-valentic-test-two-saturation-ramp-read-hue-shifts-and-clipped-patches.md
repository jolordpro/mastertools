The ramp shows every RGB value's saturation change. In his example green drops and moves toward yellow, strong blues become teal, and some blues and greens vanish from the scopes, which he says is normal for subtractive looks and fine, because intensity can be dialled back [43:10-46:46]. Danger sign: patches that look flat, meaning clipping and lost detail, so open the shoulder and find which part of the look causes it [47:48-48:19]. Keep the look on a node, not the timeline, so you can blend its overall strength [46:46-47:48].

**Use it:** Run a saturation sweep through a look and reject any that flatten colours into clipped patches.
