The DVResolve page introducing Cullen Kelly's series says an overall look is difficult because it needs to work for your whole timeline. Part of the trick is to use broad tools and constantly check how the look works on many different shots [§1]. On the 'Building Looks' page the series starts with the contrast curve in part 1, applied to the whole timeline, not to single shots, then tweaked across multiple shots, and it covers setting black and white points [§1]. Part 2 adds colour separation with custom curves, part 3 harmonises colours with hue-vs curves, part 4 is a bold colour wash that feels organic, not like a filter; the page calls the result your own custom film print stock [§1]. Correction (checked 2026-10-30): the earlier 'contrast comes third' order and the 'difficult to work for the whole timeline' phrasing are not on this page; the first two sentences above rest on the other source and are unchecked here.

**Use it:** A polish pass builds the look with broad tools and judges it on a contact sheet of varied shots, not on the hero frame alone.
