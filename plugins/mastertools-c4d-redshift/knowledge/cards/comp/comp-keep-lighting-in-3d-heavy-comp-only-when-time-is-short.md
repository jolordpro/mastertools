Maria Walla [55:17-56:50]: on a sequence of about 20 shots split between artists, each comp must be checked one by one and continuity suffers when many fixes live in comp. She prefers fixing in the 3D renders; when time is short, work shifts to comp. It depends on the company and deadline.

**Use it:** Ties to a decision rule: fix in the render when it affects many shots, in comp when time is short.
