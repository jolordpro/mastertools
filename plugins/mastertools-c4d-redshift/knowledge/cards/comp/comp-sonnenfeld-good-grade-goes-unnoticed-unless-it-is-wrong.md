He calls colour a latent, hidden tool that, done right, evokes emotions and storytelling without being noticed [18:44-19:15, 20:16-20:46]. People may come to recognise a colorist's style, liking or disliking what a film's colour brings out in them. He adds that nearly everything on any screen needs manipulation [20:46-21:16].

**Use it:** Done-test for grading: no viewer comment on the colour, only on the mood; if colour is the first thing mentioned, check for error. (Matches the cinematography card from Young.)
