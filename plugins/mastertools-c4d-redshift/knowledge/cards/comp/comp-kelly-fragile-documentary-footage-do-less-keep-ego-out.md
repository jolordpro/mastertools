Kelly [1:18:33-1:22:12] graded a documentary shot with limited cameras in uncontrolled daylight. He framed it as "dusting off bones": do as little as possible to bring out the image's essence, and resist the urge to give it his thumbprint or a Hollywood-blockbuster treatment, which would have served the subject badly. His test is to ask what small handful of moves lift each image without changing its nature.

**Use it:** a restraint check in finish reviews: if a treatment would look the same on any subject, it is signature, not service.
