The Paris mirror reflections were built so subtle that viewers notice little digital work until late in the sequence. After removing the rig and crew, his team added secondary reflections and the surrounding environment, and added debris moving through dense clouds to make odd physics feel consistent. Slow motion was shot at the highest acceptable frame rate then retimed in comp with speed ramps.

**Use it:** A secondary element that obeys the same physics is what sells an impossible primary one.
