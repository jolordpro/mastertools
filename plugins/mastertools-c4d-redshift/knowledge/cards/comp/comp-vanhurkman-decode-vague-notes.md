Clients struggle to describe colour, so the colorist asks where and what. For terms like cross processed, find out whether they mean a vintage film look or a modern phone-filter look.

**Use it:** When a note says too warm or too shiny, ask: where in the frame, highlights or midtones, whole image or one object?
