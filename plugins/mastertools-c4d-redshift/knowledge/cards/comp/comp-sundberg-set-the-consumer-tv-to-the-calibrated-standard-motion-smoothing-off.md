Sundberg of Company 3 helped Panasonic tune an OLED TV so home viewing matches the grade; he says colourists have urged people to calibrate sets to the industry standard for years [00:37-01:41]. He compares a $40,000 Sony monitor in his room with this consumer set and says most visitors cannot tell the difference [01:41-02:11]. Smooth motion is a big no-no in his world, and the maker does not enable it by default [05:21-05:52]. Interviewer: AVForums, CES 2019; claims are promotional by nature.

**Use it:** Judge reviews on a calibrated standard mode; tell clients to turn off smoothing and vivid modes before judging.
