Walter Volpatto (feature colorist, Picture Shop; captions spell him "Valpado") describes a scale: documentaries stay true (a tiger is graded as a tiger, popped a little against a quieter background) at one end; underwater in an insanely blue world with red swimsuits at the other [49:23-50:56].
In the early years of the digital intermediate, people pushed teal and orange to the point of orange skin against teal everything else [51:27].
If colour is bolder than the photography was shot for, it stops being the cinematographer's picture [48:52-49:23]. A viewer can feel punched-up colour versus colour that was designed in from capture [49:23].

**Use it:** Check: write down where on the scale a package sits and whether the content (live data, athletes) supports it.
