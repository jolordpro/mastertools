Garrett Fry [02:37-07:26, 24:40-28:13]: mask out the plate in Photoshop, detach the mask, and move the little window around the paint. The plate had little atmospheric perspective and the paint was too hazy and blue; pull the black point, pull blue and red from the top end, and a touch of green. Do the same for the sky: it was too green, too bright and too blue; correct until the swatch edge disappears.

**Use it:** Use a plate window to compare blacks and sky before adding atmosphere.
