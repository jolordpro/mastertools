Film colour is less bright and shows its richness in dark tones, so the quickest way to emulate it is to reduce the brightness of colour, not its saturation [48:19-49:52]. On the ramp, check for clipping or bending [49:52-50:23]. Same test is available in the plugin version as tone map, saturation ramp and lightness ramp [50:53-52:33].

**Use it:** When a render looks too digital, try darkening the colours before adding or removing saturation.
