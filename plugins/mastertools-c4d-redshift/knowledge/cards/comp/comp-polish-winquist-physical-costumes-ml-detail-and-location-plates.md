Weta used dual-camera facial rigs for higher-fidelity performance capture than earlier Apes films, with evolving software for muscle simulation, facial animation and grooming [§1]. The Deep Shapes machine-learning tool added subtle wrinkling and tissue detail such as eye bags as an extra layer on the artists' work [§1]. Costume pieces were built by Weta Workshop and then matched digitally by Weta FX artists [§1]. Shooting primarily on location in Australia helped actors feel in a real space, with environments often augmented or fully digital in final shots [§1].

**Use it:** For CG characters in plates, prefer a physical reference build to match digitally, and shoot the performers in the real environment where possible.
