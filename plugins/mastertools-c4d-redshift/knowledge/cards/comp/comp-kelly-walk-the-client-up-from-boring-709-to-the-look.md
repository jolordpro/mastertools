Kelly [39:25-45:10]: clients live with the edit's rendering for months and call it "my image", so a bolder grade can feel like a shock (he compares it to temp-music love). His approach: arrive with many ideas, keep quiet, and let them lead. Replay the build in miniature: a plain Rec 709 transform first (close to what they have seen), then add contrast, then a global cool, auditioning each piece, so they take the same trip he did. If the client is attached to raw log or a manufacturer LUT, the same applies: meet them where they are.
When a request seems wrong he says "let's do exactly that", looks at it together, then asks what they want next, and resists the urge to say "but you don't like it". [44:08]

**Use it:** reviews of a stylised pass: show the plain baseline, then the stacked steps, rather than only the final.
