He groups the shots of a scene as a block and applies a correction to the group; most shots then fall into place, and only the odd ones need work [53:05-53:36]. Related: the look is built in layers, big look for the film, look for the scene, shot matching, then detail [33:20-35:27].

**Use it:** Apply scene-level corrections once, at the group level, then match outliers.
