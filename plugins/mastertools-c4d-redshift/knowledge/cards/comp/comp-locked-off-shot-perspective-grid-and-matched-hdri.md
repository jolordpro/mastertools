A lockdown shot has no parallax to track, so he mapped the perspective in a vanishing-point grid (like Photoshop's tool), then placed 3D objects that follow it in C4D [21:33-22:04].
A second plane took about two days and was mostly a copy [22:04-22:34]. Lighting used probes to rebuild an HDR that matched shadows and highlights [22:34-23:05]; for a sundown shot a new HDR and a recreated lens flare, masked behind the plane's tail [24:39-25:42].

**Use it:** no camera data? use a perspective grid and capture a probe HDR on set.
