He likes that choosing a negative and its strength changes only colour, leaving contrast alone; he hated LUTs and film emulations that change the whole look and exposure so you only wanted some tonality [26:40-28:14]. He recalls a lab owner's remark that chemicals used to be mixed by hand and now a dial does it [26:40-27:44].

**Use it:** Keep colour and tone decisions in separate stages so a note on one does not undo the other.
