The director and DP wanted a movie that reminded them of 1980s films, and gave Volpatto many references, even partial scenes [14:36-15:09].
Because the Alexa 35 looks nothing like the 1980s, they shot a real film camera test and graded it to taste; that became the North Star: if they could shoot film it would look like this [15:09-15:40].
The recipe must be simple, since they cannot tweak every scene or shot, especially during the dailies process [15:40-16:12].
In the final grade they pushed contrast and colour a tiny bit more for lushness after seeing the material for so long [16:12-16:43].
He wants it to feel like film without claiming to replace it [16:43].

**Use it:** Anchor a digital look on a real reference test, keep the daily-use recipe to a few steps, and save extra push for the final grade.
