Done remotely so the client can review. Park on the hero frame first, then export a PDF with your logo, project name, page note, the reference stills used (so the client sees what you built from) and a scene overview with all looks applied [32:38-37:56].
The palette strip is computed from five exposure zones, after Ansel Adams: a middle (skin) zone plus two above and two below, using stops of exposure, so you also see how colours and contrast bounce off each other; absolute black and white are never shown [36:14-37:20].
He says the more interactive and involved the client, the better the result [37:56-38:28].

**Use it:** Look-approval pack for clients: references, hero frame, palette by zone, scene grid; keep one version per PDF.
