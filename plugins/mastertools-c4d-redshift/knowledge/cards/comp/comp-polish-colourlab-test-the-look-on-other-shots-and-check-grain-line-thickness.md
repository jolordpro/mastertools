After building on one shot he tests on two more from different cameras: lifting offset to fix exposure, desaturating only the teals so skin keeps intensity, and adding a window for a cooler background [32:02-38:44]. He accepts that a flat, overlit, very sharp shot needs stronger texture and does not rescue everything [38:44-39:45]. Rule: the show LUT/look should do most of the work; per-shot trims are exposure, background, small tweaks [39:45-40:17]. Show-LUT rule: set the tone map so the LUT does not move exposure, only contrast and tonality [22:02-22:32]. Grain check: compare thickness of fine line edges against the reference [29:18]; a notch of film gamut at 0.2 trims skin saturation edge [23:03].
**Use it:** Before delivering a look, run it on at least three differing shots and check what the LUT changes (exposure should not move).
