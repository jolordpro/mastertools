He says the mark of an amateur is treating the first pass as final. When a client asks for a change, what you have is not working, even if the client's particular fix may or may not be right; people without experience confuse the two [§3]. The diplomatic move is steering toward the change of "least harm". If he dislikes the suggestion he should have a useful alternative immediately, and "that's where your creativity comes in" [§3-4].

**Use it:** Read each note as a symptom first (what is not landing?), then choose the smallest fix, and have your own option ready.
