He balances realism against stylisation and emphasises the right moments, because a literal crash can look dull. Use live footage when it is cheaper and faster; save full CG for what cannot be filmed. [§1]

**Use it:** Add a grain or dust layer to a too-clean render and judge by eye on the final frame.
