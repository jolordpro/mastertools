Kelly [55:37-1:00:14]: asked about a client who wants a specific film-stock LUT on weak footage, he picks the most difficult shot and tries it first. He frames it as making the look work uniformly (nobody wants seven good shots and three bad ones), gives it his best, then stays silent and lets the client look at the ugly result. If they hate it, he agrees and pivots, and the abandonment is their idea; if they love it, he accepts and leaves it off his reel. He calls himself a member of the cabinet, not a dictator.

**Use it:** stress-test a proposed look on the hardest frame in the set first, and show the client that frame rather than explaining the risk.
