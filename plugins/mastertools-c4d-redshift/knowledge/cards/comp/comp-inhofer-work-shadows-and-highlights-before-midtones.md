Midtones look like the place to start because they affect both ends, but he says start with highlights and shadows, get them looking good, and then look at midtone controls [54:00-54:29]. A grading panel (even a small one) helps by letting you push highlights against shadows together [53:57].

**Use it:** Tonal order for a look pass: black and white points first, midtones last. Pair with the "match then look last" card.
