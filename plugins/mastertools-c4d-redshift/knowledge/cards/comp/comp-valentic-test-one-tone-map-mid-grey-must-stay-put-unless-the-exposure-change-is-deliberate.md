The test image is a grey ramp with 12 stops of Log C. He reads red and blue moving off neutral grey as warmth in the look, while green still sits on 400; a small blue push brings neutral grey back to 400 [38:32-40:04]. Rule: a show LUT should not change exposure, because the cinematographer meters light and the monitor must show it accurately [40:04-41:05]. The measure is what goes into and out of the look, not the camera's own mid-grey, so the 400 in his design space stays the reference for any camera [56:14-56:45]. Switch output to Log C to read the curve linearly [42:07-42:39].

**Use it:** Run a neutral grey ramp through any look before approval and confirm that mid grey is unchanged.
