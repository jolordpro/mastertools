Find out what the client wants and what they like and dislike. By the second or third hour your sensibility should be adjusted to their look and grades should land quickly. Structure each grade around the project (a 30-second spot differs from a documentary).

**Use it:** Spend the first review on discovering taste with 3-4 test frames, not on polishing.
