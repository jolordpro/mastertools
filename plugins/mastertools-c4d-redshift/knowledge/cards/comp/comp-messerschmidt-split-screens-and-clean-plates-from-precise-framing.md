Split screens fixed things like a candlestick moved between takes, and also sped up the pace of a two-shot where a cut was not possible [39:33-40:37]. Most were decided in post by the editor, though some were known in advance [40:37]. Exact, repeatable frames register well for the compositor [41:07]. For exteriors they put a B camera into the A-camera frame to match changing sun, then erased it or pushed in for a clean plate [41:38-42:09].
