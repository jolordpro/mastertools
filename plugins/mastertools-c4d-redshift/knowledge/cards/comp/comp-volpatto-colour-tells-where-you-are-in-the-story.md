He compares grading to music: audiences feel the score rather than hear it [§2]. A scene can feel flat because drab colour does not support the intended emotion, and people may leave feeling deceived without knowing why [§5]. He praises The Matrix for defining inside and outside worlds by colour, so the image says where you are in the story more subtly than a line of dialogue [§5]. Even in a controlled room, tiny grade shifts followed the changing relationships (Kylo, Snoke and Hux) [§3].

**Use it:** Name the one story fact each colour world communicates and check each cut against it.
