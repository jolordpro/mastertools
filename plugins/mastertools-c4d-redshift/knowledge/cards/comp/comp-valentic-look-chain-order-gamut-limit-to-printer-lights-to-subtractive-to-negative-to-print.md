Input is colour managed (40+ cameras) and output can go to any space, so the look is always built in an ideal space [06:17-06:49]. Stage order: gamut limit (tames bright saturated LED sources) [07:21]; lift/gamma/gain plus printer lights that work in stops [07:53]; conversion to subtractive colour, modelled on three emulsion layers where more density darkens colour while saturation behaves film-style, not simple CMY sliders [08:24-08:56]; a density control [09:29]; a negative control (palette character, from cinematographers choosing negatives) [09:59]; a print control, the most visible change, with stock and lab variants [10:30-11:00]; a contrast curve and crunch; post (ENR bleach-bypass or FPE film print emulation) [13:09-13:40]; and a master control for overall look intensity [14:11]. He sells this tool, so treat claims as his method, not a standard.

**Use it:** Use the order as a template for a house grade: exposure, colour, character, contrast, post, then one intensity control.
