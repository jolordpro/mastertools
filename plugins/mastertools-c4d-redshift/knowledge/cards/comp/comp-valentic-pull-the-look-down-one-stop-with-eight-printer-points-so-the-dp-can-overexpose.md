Sometimes a show LUT is deliberately darkened so the DP can overexpose a little in camera and get less noise, which he does regularly with RED cameras. In printer lights, bringing the value down 8 points equals one full stop, so the look shows a stop less on a monitor [41:05-42:07]. Otherwise he keeps the look's exposure neutral [40:04-41:05].

**Use it:** State any intentional exposure offset in a look's notes, with its stop value, so nobody reads it as an error.
