Hugo Guerra's team reviews: daily call, every artist gets room to share and brainstorm [24:50-25:22]. Keep a water-cooler chat before the review [25:22].
Advice: do not spend the first shot giving far too much feedback; avoid 'pixel fudging' (debating what nobody will see); shots do not need to be perfect at 800 percent zoom [25:54-26:57]. Do not loop shots too much, since no one watches a film on loop [26:57].
Draw on the screen and bring reference; recreate the walk-to-the-desk chat on calls [27:27-27:59].

**Use it:** Coach: before sending notes, check the note matters at normal viewing size and speed.
