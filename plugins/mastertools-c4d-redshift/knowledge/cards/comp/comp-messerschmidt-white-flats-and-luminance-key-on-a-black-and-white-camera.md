For the monkey cage in the San Simeon sequence they built theatrical flats shaped like the cage, painted them white and lit them, and post pulled a luminance key to separate them [37:54-38:24]. Elephants and giraffes were rotoscoped into the background [38:24]. His summary: sometimes old-fashioned means such as white theatre flats, sometimes state-of-the-art VFX [38:55].

**Use it:** Plan matte method to match the capture: a luma-keyable background (white or black) when colour keys are impossible.
