Kramer loves unexpected, 'stupid' solutions such as turning a camera upside down or building a water tank [01:09]. The Episode I waterfalls used filmed salt pouring against blue or black instead of a water sim, extracted and composited [01:41].
From the behind-the-scenes clip he shows (artist Dean Yorker): the salt elements are combined on a grid to give the waterfall texture and depth, a displacement map gives edge and shape, several elements are combined into the overall shape, and the grids with texture are bent around to make the water; the looking-down shot reuses the same principle [02:14-03:15].

**Use it:** Before simulating, test a filmed or stock element mapped onto simple displaced geometry; for stylised broadcast water, smoke or flow it often reads better.
