The DVResolve page lists the order: part 1 contrast curve for the whole timeline with black and white points, part 2 colour separation (colour contrast, not luminance contrast) via custom curves, part 3 harmonising colours with hue-vs custom curves, part 4 a bold colour wash that feels organic to the shot rather than a filter [§1]. The aim is a look that works as your own custom film print stock for the project [§1]. The look is applied to the timeline and checked by moving between multiple shots [§1].

**Use it:** Teach looks in this order: luminance contrast first, then colour contrast, then harmonising, and only then any wash.
