For distant memories he started from the period LUT and added colour on top, then in Resolve added subtle effects to reduce the sharpness of high-res Venice 2 footage and used OpenFX to introduce lens flares and chromatic aberration that old optics might have produced [§1].

**Use it:** For a faded or archival look, soften first, then add small optical artefacts rather than a heavy filter; start from the LUT for the period.
