Look at other departments; knowing what Houdini can give opened a new approach when a particle sim arrived as an odd data pass and was fast enough, after he just talked to the artists [§3]. Even a shot called final may come back [§3].

**Use it:** After a lesson or review, redo the technique yourself that day.
