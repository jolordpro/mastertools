Yedlin wanted the feel of The Empire Strikes Back. He worked with the colour team before production to devise and calibrate the pipeline: start from a photochemical look, then apply colour science to all digital sources so they read as film, with his mathematical models built into the pipeline. By the end, most of the intended look was already there. Volpatto also reviewed the original trilogy and The Force Awakens to gauge audience expectation [§3].

**Use it:** Lock the look pipeline (reference, LUT or model) before shots arrive; polish then becomes small corrections.
