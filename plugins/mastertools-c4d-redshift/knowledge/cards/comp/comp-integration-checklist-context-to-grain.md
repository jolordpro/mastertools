Courtney Price (freelance Nuke artist, ex-Foundry) learned a ten-point integration list in 2012 on an fxphd Nuke course and keeps it as a reminder: context, perspective, tracking, colour, sharpness, grain [23:01-23:33].
Context first: choose an element whose light and scale already fit the plate (a sunlit wooden bench on a beach, not a harshly lit desk), or you fight the image [24:05-24:35].
Perspective: get it right and you are about 80% of the way; corner pin, grid warp or spline warp to fit it [25:06].
Transfers to broadcast: the same order works for a CG logo or lower-third over live footage.

**Use it:** Use it as the fixed order for a 'does it sit in the plate' review of any element comped over footage.
Transcript support: Fry matches blacks, hue and value by cutting a window in the DMP over the plate, then repeats for each material and the sky (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u7p4RnOuaNQ [03:10])
