Her own check before the supervisor's more thorough one [36:32-38:35]:
- Look at R, G, B channels individually.
- Gamma up and down: every added element should respond like the rest of the image.
- Add double saturation at the bottom of the tree: everything should shift together, so harsh grades in finishing do not split the elements.
- Reformat and crop last; deal with bounding boxes and make sure the format is the delivery format.
- Difference key of before and after, boosted and eroded, key-mixed over the plate, so only changed pixels come from the comp.
- When addressing notes, difference the old version against the new to confirm only the requested change happened.

**Use it:** Candidate lines for a pre-delivery comp check: gamma and saturation stress tests plus a version-to-version difference.
