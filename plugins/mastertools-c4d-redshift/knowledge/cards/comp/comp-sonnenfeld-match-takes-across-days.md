Shots for one scene were taken days or weeks apart in changing weather. He evened them out using split-screen comparisons [§1] (the stills detail is not on this page) so the scene reads as one moment.

Page spot [panorama §1]: much of the work is smoothing subtle differences between takes shot days or weeks apart in changing weather, compared in split screen.

**Use it:** Check consistency by putting frames next to each other, not by judging each alone. Applies to a set of shots in a broadcast package.
