He is amazed when he gets no notes [30:26-30:58]. A client who says "make it prettier" gives a note you must unpack, and he says nobody is trained to talk in hue and saturation values [30:58-31:30]. He and his team discuss how to talk with clients about colour on their podcast mailbags. A job may be five days with the client absent for the first three and a half, then arriving Thursday to say they hate a choice: you handle it in the room, and a recovery can make you the hero by the end [29:55-30:26].

**Use it:** Ask for specific words, a reference frame or a before/after toggle, never more than one change at a time. Add to coach tips: no notes is a warning sign, not praise.
