For Rey's lightsaber practice on Ahch-To, shot over a day and a half with little control of light, they first chose hero shots with cinematographer Steve Yedlin and asked what the sequence is about (hopeful, training, a bright and beautiful scene). That set the world to hold, then he changed elements shot to shot to hide the swing from sun to rain so it read as one continuous moment. He calls it the film's hardest scene [§2]. For the controlled-light throne room he did little beyond fine-tuning to blend real shots with VFX [§2].

**Use it:** Open a polish pass by agreeing on two or three hero frames and one line about what the sequence is for; match everything to them.
