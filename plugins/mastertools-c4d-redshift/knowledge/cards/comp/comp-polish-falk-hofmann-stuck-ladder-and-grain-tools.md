The ladder: if he cannot find the fault in 15-20 minutes he searches the internet, then walks away from the desk, then draws a diagram of the flow on paper. After another 20 minutes he finds someone and explains the problem aloud; in about 80% of cases the answer arrives while talking. If still stuck he restarts, splitting the task into small parts [§4-§5]. Tools he names: a shortcut that cycles a node's channel (alpha, rgb, rgba) used hundreds of times a day, ITransform, and DasGrain by Fabian Holtz, which he calls a game changer for re-graining and is building a grain tool on [§2]. A teacher made students write their own despill rather than use new tools [§1]. [§2,§4-§5]

**Use it:** Re-grain after processing so matched grain unifies CG and plate.
