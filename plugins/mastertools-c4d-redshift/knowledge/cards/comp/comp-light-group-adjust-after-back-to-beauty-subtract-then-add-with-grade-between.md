He calls it a myth that light groups and back-to-beauty are mutually exclusive [09:17]. The sun group is already inside the rebuild, so first subtract it, then add it back; with nothing between them the result is unchanged (bypass test) [09:50]. Put a colour corrector between the subtract and add to warm the sun and the change is live [10:23]. His reason: the render took two days, so he wants control in comp [10:57]. He admits he forgot a sun group on the interior and could not match it later [24:57].

**Use it:** a plan note: decide which lights the client may want to change and give them groups before the long render.
