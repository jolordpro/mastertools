With little money, build a lookup table with the colorist for set, and do the lighting right; if the light is right, colour is a breeze, and almost nothing is needed [31:47-32:49]. The worse the photography, the more of the colorist's time just makes it work [32:19-32:49]. Production design, costume and wigs set the look early; changing it later in post is very hard [11:25]. On a big budget, the order is just don't mess it up; indie and small projects are more challenging [09:18].

**Use it:** Spend polish effort on lighting and set colour before the grade or comp stage.
