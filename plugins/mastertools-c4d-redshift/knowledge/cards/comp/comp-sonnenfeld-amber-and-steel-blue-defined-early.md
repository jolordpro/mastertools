Warm tones: a golden amber for candle and torch scenes, backed by real firelight and tungsten practicals, with a few amber looks defined, which helped keep consistency across the film [§2].
Cool tones: a controlled steel blue in daytime interiors and open shade, enhanced slightly and made consistent with the rest as each case required [§2]. Targeted moves keep warm/cool balance from looking artificial.

Page spots [panorama §2]; correction made above: the page does not say the amber was defined early, only that some amber aesthetics were defined, which kept consistency.

**Use it:** Lock one named warm and one named cool treatment per project and reuse them; tune cool lightly.
