KITPLUS (short explainer) [01:06-02:38]. An ACES project in Resolve: on the colour management page choose ACES, tell it what the footage was shot on (his example Sony S-Log3 from a drop-down) and choose the output: Rec.709 for SDR or one of the HDR options. Switching between SDR 709 and HDR is a menu change [01:36]. The pitch is that under the hood ACES converts footage into an internal format that makes grading controls and exposure changes more intuitive [02:38]. Resolve has a free version. The video gives no numbers or versions.

**Use it:** Platform note: a delivery with both SDR and HDR versions is where a colour-managed route saves the most work. Speaker is selling the idea; see the Kelly card for the opposing caution.
