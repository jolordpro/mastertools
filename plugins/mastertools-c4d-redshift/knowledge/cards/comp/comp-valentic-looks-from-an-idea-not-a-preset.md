Distinctive results come from thinking differently, not from one tool or preset. He sees colour as a communication tool that guides the emotional response, with the colorist as facilitator for the people who own the work.
Quote: "The tool that you used is not really important in it." (Valentic, Color & Coffee) [§22]

**Use it:** If a note says make it look like X, ask what the idea behind X is before copying its settings.
