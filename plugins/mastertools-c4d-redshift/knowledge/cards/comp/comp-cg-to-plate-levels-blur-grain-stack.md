For a predator drone he found reference images were clean, so he cleaned his dirty textures [08:58-09:30].
The CG was too sharp and contrasty. He added Levels, a chroma blur (captions also name an unclear 'firewalk' effect) and Match Grain to bring it to the plate's values before the final grade [11:05-11:35].
For the CG plane: Levels to match the black level of the washed-out plate (4K, downscaled to 2K), curves for highlights, a matte tool to clean the layer's shadow edges (captions garbled), then grain, heavy blur, and another grain layer on top, which looks bad close but works at normal size [23:36-24:39]. Principle: no camera is perfect, so mess up the image [24:39].

**Use it:** a match recipe for CG over plate: black level, blur, grain, then grade; compare at normal size, not zoomed in.
