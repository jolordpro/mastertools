Part 1 of Cullen Kelly's tutorial, in the page's order: what is a look; anchoring middle grey with an exposure chart and a qualifier; applying contrast with Custom Curves; creating colour contrast with split toning in Custom Curves; reducing the intensity of the look with Key Output Gain [§1]. Part 2 covers hue with Hue vs Hue, colour density with Hue vs Lum ("why it's not ideal") and with a Layer Mixer and RGB Mixer [§1]. No numbers are given on the page.

**Use it:** Give the coach a fixed order: grey anchor, tone, colour, then back it off. Reducing at the end is the built-in restraint step.
