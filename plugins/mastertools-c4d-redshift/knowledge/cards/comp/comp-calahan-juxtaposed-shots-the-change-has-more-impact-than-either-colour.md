She pays attention to how colours sit on screen at once and over time, for example two shots cut together; going from bright to dark or green to red, the change matters more than each colour alone [17:13-17:43]. She often notices colour pairs in the real world, like turquoise beside orange [17:13].

**Use it:** Review the cut order and the frames each side of a cut before judging a frame alone.
