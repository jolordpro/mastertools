He describes the job as Photoshop for video, but where a Photoshop artist spends half a day to a day on one image, he works with as few as 30 shots or up to 500 in a day [01:54-02:28]. His recent travel show had 400 shots, done in about nine hours (roughly 80 seconds a shot on average) [02:28-02:59]. Main focus: match shots so the story stays fluid, across wide, close-up and reverse angles, often multi-camera with found light [02:59-03:29]. Method: adjust white balance and exposure and set black points and highlights so viewers read the scene as shot at once in one place and only notice the story [03:29-04:00].

**Use it:** Cost and schedule: a grading pass on a show is a throughput job (about a minute or so a shot). In QC, judge the cut for cross-shot consistency before any single-frame polish.
