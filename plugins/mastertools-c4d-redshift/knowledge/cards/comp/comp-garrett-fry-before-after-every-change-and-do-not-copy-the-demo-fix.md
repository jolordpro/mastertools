Garrett Fry [29:15-32:23]: he saved his demo for reference but told the artist to apply the notes herself first, then drop his image into her file and see how far off she is; hers may be better since he worked quickly. Always flip before/after: sometimes work makes it worse or only different. He shows how nitpicky notes get to reach photoreal.

**Use it:** Review rule: compare each revision with the last before accepting it.
