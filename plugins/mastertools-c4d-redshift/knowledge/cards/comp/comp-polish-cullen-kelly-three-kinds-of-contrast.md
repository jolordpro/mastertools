His contrast series splits the idea in three. Tonal contrast is more than lifting highlights and dropping shadows. Colour contrast goes beyond teal and orange and shifts with colour temperature and curves. Spatial contrast uses windows and secondaries so the subject stands apart from the background, and he weighs when a tracked window is worth it. (Summary of the series; no numbers.)

**Use it:** When a frame feels flat, the coach asks which of the three contrasts is missing before suggesting a blanket contrast boost.
