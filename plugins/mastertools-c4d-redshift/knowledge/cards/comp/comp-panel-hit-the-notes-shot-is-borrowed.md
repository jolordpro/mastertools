A common development mistake: an artist gets feedback, disagrees and keeps making the shot look the way they want [23:40-24:10].
Panel view: care about it but hit the notes, because you only borrow the shot and give it back [24:10]. Dailies is for asking opinion, not arguing [24:10-24:42]. Enjoying the process of comping makes changes ego-free [24:42].

**Use it:** coach wording for revisions: implement the note first, then add the artist's counter-option as a variant.
Transcript support: a senior tells a junior who hit the note to go home, since the art director can flip direction tomorrow (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yDcjIk7L-0Y [51:02])
