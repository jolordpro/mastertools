Winchester [31:25-32:59]: setup and import time had made After Effects optional on tight jobs. His script imports every shot of a sequence, now by look ID, in order, so similar grading and effects are done once across shots. That cuts 200 or more comps to about 10.

**Use it:** organise comp work by look group, not by shot, when many shots share the grade.
