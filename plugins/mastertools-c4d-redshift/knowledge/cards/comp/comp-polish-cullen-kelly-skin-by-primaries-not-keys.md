Skin is what viewers are most sensitive to, so he optimises it in the primaries and expects it to be right before secondaries begin. If skin is off after secondaries, he returns to the primaries and does not patch it with a key. He says he has pulled a skin key perhaps once in five years. (Blog summary of an interview.)
