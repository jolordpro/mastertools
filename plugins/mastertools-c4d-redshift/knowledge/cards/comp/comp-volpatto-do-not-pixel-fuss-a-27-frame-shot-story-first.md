At speed viewers look at the subject and the story, while a colourist freezing a frame refines far more than anyone will see [13:35-14:06].
His advice to newer colourists is to stop polishing details nobody sees: ask whether the whole story and scene work [54:14-54:47].
Experience comes from grading many shots: offer to grade student films for peanuts, and expect the first projects to look poor in retrospect [54:47-55:49].
Each time you move a control, ask whether it brings the client's intended vision to the final viewer; the vision comes from art direction, cinematography, lighting, costume and makeup [1:00:29-1:01:00].

**Use it:** Review a shot at playing speed and ask what the audience sees in a second; reserve frozen-frame fixes for what reads in motion.
