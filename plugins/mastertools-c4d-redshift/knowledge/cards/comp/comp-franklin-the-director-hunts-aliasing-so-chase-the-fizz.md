Franklin says Chris Nolan was knowledgeable enough to know aliasing is one of the hardest things to get right and costs compute, and so had his team chasing small fizzes at edges; those appear in many films if you know where to watch [24:33-25:35].

**Use it:** In a polish pass, check straight edges and thin lines at full size, not just the overall frame.
