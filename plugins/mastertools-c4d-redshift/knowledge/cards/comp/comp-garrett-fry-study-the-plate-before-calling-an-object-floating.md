Garrett Fry [01:04-02:37]: an element looked as if it floated on the right, but the ground in the plate slopes (the left point is about a foot higher), so the artist was correcting for it. Dropping the horizon helped. Also [08:30-09:03]: if the camera is correct a layer should drop on and match; mismatch means the camera is wrong.

**Use it:** Verify against the plate's geometry before a 'floating' note.
