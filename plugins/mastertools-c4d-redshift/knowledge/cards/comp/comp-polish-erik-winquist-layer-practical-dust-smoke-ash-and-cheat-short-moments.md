Compositors layered rendered and practical dust, smoke, fire, falling ash and paper into the Federal Plaza scenes. A stunt shot was stitched from two takes with rigging removed and re-speeding, with added sparks and debris to read as one take. For brief flights they used a winged character version instead of simulating flapping skin. Scaled-up animal motion lacked energy and needed adjusting. [§3-§6]

**Use it:** Atmosphere and debris layers do much of the finish; cheat what is on screen too briefly to be noticed.
