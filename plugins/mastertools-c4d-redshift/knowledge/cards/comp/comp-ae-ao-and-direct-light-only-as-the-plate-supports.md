CGStirk [07:12-09:35, 11:54-12:58]. Overcast, foggy reference has no direct light source, so the direct lighting pass is dropped. AO catches fine shadow that an overcast reference does not show, so use 5 to 10% opacity (10% gives slight engine definition) or none. A weak reflection pass can be revealed by soloing and crushing whites in Curves; how much to add is a choice, since the CG material may differ from the plane. Specular: crush it to keep variation without a front light look, and add blue to remove a warm glint. After the grade, compare the first beauty against the reference and add back direct light only if it still reads as lit.

**Use it:** The coach can say: use passes to match the reference, not all passes at full strength.
