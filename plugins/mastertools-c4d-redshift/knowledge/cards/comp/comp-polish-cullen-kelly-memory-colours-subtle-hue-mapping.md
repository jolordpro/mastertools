In a Mixing Light look-development lesson he treats skin, sky and foliage as memory colours that viewers store as an ideal that differs from reality. He compresses and saturates skin hues, rotates yellow-greens toward green, and lowers luma on blues and reds, using a 16-vector warper. The text calls the method subtle. The full lesson is paywalled, so limits are unknown.

**Use it:** A palette-sweetening step is a small hue push toward the remembered colour, not a global saturation change.
