Zak: ACES 2065 holds all colours the eye can see and many stops of range (he says about 30), so if a new display arrives you swap the output display transform and keep the render [04:11-05:46].
He also says everyone can use ACES for the built-in tone mapping, not only people comping CG into live action [05:16].

**Use it:** Coach: store masters in a scene-linear wide space and keep the display transform as a separate, swappable step.
