Guest: Stefan Sonnenfeld. In commercials he had the lab make three or four prints of a 30-second spot (about 40 shots), low and high contrast and so on, to get suggestions from the print process. He then either mixed prints or stayed on the negative, which had more range, and emulated the print looks [30:27-31:58].
Experimentation is part of successful projects; he sometimes pushes a grade too far just to see what it sparks, and the colorist must come up with options and show them [30:27-30:57, 31:28-32:31].
Today's trend is film emulation giving a print profile (low or high contrast, reversal) and old lenses with digital cameras [29:56-30:27].

**Use it:** Offer the client two or three visibly different treatments of the same frames, not one, and keep one a deliberate overshoot to calibrate taste.
