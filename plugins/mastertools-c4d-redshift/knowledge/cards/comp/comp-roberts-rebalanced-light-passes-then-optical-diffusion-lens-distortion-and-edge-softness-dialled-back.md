Roberts shows an unfinished Redshift render of Insection with no colour correction, balanced with exposure and lights; in After Effects he adds the passes in an Add stack and pushes contrast and bias toward chosen lights [37:23-41:49]. A Redshift environment gives mist, and he renders the light groups with their volumetric contribution as extra passes [38:28-39:35]. Final lens effects from Red Giant Looks: optical diffusion, lens distortion and edge softness, with presets coming in too hot so he dials them back [43:01-44:02]. Glow, bloom amount and lens effects are what bring realism to CG [43:01].
Whole point: a render that looks fine in the box becomes more cinematic when the lights are balanced independently.
