He adds a grade adjustment layer just above AO, track-matted with the chair matte (Luma), and uses Curves: read the darkest values of the plate (about 0.04 and 0.01 at different points in his case) and drag the black point just enough to crunch the CG blacks toward them; brightest plate values are far below 0.9 when no direct sun hits the subject, so pull the white point down only slightly, less than the black change, then adjust mids [06:49-08:59]. He chose to leave the CG chair slightly desaturated as an older chair, and notes grain should be added at this point if the plate has noise (this plate was clean) [06:49, 08:59].

**Use it:** a match checklist: blacks, whites, mids, saturation, then grain, in that order, using measured values.
