Del Toro and cinematographer Dan Laustsen wanted a classic film made with modern tools, using sets, practical effects, real locations and miniatures to make the world feel tangible [§1]. Laustsen designed the production so dailies and final colour were closely aligned, with costume specificity and window light warmth chosen on set rather than a new look in post [§1]. Sonnenfeld says Del Toro had a defined art direction and costume design from the start, and Laustsen knows how to use lighting and framing to realise it [§1]. The tools used were DaVinci Resolve Studio [§1].

**Use it:** When a package has strong art direction, ask what is already decided in camera before planning heavy grade moves.
