In-camera air-mortar blasts were shot at up to 1000 frames a second so debris looks suspended. Compositors then made speed ramps so each explosion begins in real time and ramps down, extending the sense of abnormal physics. Slow motion used the Photosonics 4ER on 35mm film and the Phantom digital camera, always at the highest rate that still kept quality, with more slowdown added in post. Falling rain that could not be shot slow was made in CG. [§2-§3]

**Use it:** Start an effect at normal speed so the viewer feels the break from reality.
