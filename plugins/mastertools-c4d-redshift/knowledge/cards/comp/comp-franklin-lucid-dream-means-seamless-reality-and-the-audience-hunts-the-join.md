Franklin says Nolan's film dealt with lucid dreaming, where the dreamer thinks it is reality, so the team never aimed at surreal Dali-style images. The target was a seamless illusion of reality at all times, with photoreal technique used to show surreal events such as a city folding in half or the collapsing shores of limbo [00:37].
His reason it raised the bar: once something strange happens (a street folds up), everyone knows something is going on and looks for the joins between real and digital [01:09].
