Walter Volpatto (feature colorist, Picture Shop; captions spell him "Valpado") repeats a mentor line: a film can be made in ten brightness and warmth variants and all work, but a mismatch between shots reads wrong [45:44-46:16].
He assigned a class a short, then saw students break rules before they could match two shots [1:10:40-1:11:10]. Host Cullen Kelly agrees matching is hard to bluff, unlike taste [1:11:10-1:12:13].
Consistently dark can work; inconsistently dark does not [1:12:13].

**Use it:** Check priority: shot-to-shot continuity (exposure, white point, contrast) before taste adjustments.
