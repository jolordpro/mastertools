He says a modest drama with few visual effects usually has about two weeks; he spends the first half on a complete unrefined pass in which the film works from beginning to end, then refines [25:33-26:37]. Unlimited time makes everything perfect, as on The Revenant [26:06]. For TV he has about 16 hours for an episode and perhaps 900 cuts [34:23]. Numbers as stated, 2020.

**Use it:** Plan a polish budget as a whole-package pass first, then refinement with what time remains.
