Doyle [39:01-41:08]: ACES has calmed things down and given a base; scene-referred and display-referred are now clear ideas. The frontier is colour appearance models, grading in the space we actually perceive.
The issue: working in a huge space (ACES, Arri wide gamut) and pushing it through a LUT or LMT means the control called red may be seen as burgundy [40:04-40:37]. Some commercial colourists "go commando" and grade in display space, which can work but be problematic elsewhere.
His advice: choose what to learn; understand what a colour space and tone/gamut mapping are so you can say "that is because of that" [43:13-44:15], and make developers your friends. The look differs between Arri, Baselight and Resolve transforms, which Kelly raised [42:09].

**Use it:** The coach explains that identical-looking grades can differ per display transform, and records which transform a look was signed off in.
