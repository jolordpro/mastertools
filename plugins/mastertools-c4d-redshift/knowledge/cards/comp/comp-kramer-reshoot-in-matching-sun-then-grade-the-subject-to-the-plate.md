A rooftop scene needed an extra line after the edit, so the team shot a blue-screen take outdoors at the time of day when the sun hit the actor from the same side, with a reflector standing in for sky fill [48:00-49:03]. The goal was not to composite into the background but to make the shot indistinguishable from the original, judging how much wrap and ambient really fell on the foreground and colour-correcting the actor to the background from the plate [49:03-49:34]. Matching something that already exists beat creating it from scratch.
He adds that matte-painted skies in TV VFX often sit too dim; real sun would be much hotter [49:34-50:04].

**Use it:** Match a CG element to a photographed reference taken in the same light, and check that bright sources and skies are as hot as the real ones.
