Speaker's claim, Cullen Kelly (colourist), intro to an ACES series [00:02-03:37]. Definition: ACES is a colour management framework that standardises turning what the camera saw into what a display can show, with an intermediate stage for colour and VFX work [00:32]. His three reasons not to use it. 1: you do not understand colour spaces and colour management; learn that first, like learning to drive before buying a Tesla [01:02-02:04]. 2: you expect better-looking images; ACES is about accuracy and efficiency, not aesthetics, and only may help you make better grades through a systematic approach [02:04-02:57]. 3: you are satisfied with your current workflow; without a reason to grow you will fall back under pressure [02:57-03:37]. He uses ACES himself.

**Use it:** Use as a calibration note: pitch ACES in the platform as consistency, not as a look. Contrast with the KITPLUS card, which stresses ease of grading.
