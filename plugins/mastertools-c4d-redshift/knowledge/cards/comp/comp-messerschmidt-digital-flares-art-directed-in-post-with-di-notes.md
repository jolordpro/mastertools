Fincher liked the quality of digital flares and the ability to art direct them in post; season two pushed them further than season one [07:22]. In the bridge scene they spent time in the DI perfecting the flare, sending notes back and forth, such as a little longer here and more saturated there; the VFX team tracked headlights well [29:43]. One exterior flare also drove the choice of time of day for a morning shoot [19:14].

**Use it:** Treat a flare as a designed, trackable element with saturation and length notes, not a one-click lens effect.
