Kramer [06:15-07:18]: don't stop at "done". You might spend 10 minutes making particles in Particular, but if you spend a couple of hours filming real dust particles and combine them, you have an element that no one else in the world has.
When a hiring VFX producer sees that, they see someone who got the details, not the bare minimum [06:47].
He mentions his studio-days stock library of fire and smoke footage, which inspired his action essentials packs [02:37-03:39], and that his tutorials said "get the gist, then make it good" [07:18].
Transfer: shoot or build a one-off texture or element (dust, grain, lens dirt) for hero frames rather than rely on packs everyone uses.

**Use it:** The look-dev checklist suggests one custom element (shot or simulated) per hero frame.
