Before exporting a LUT he runs all the ramps. With an old-school classic stock the saturation ramp looked fine, but the lightness ramp showed bending, meaning the stock cannot reproduce those colours well [52:33-53:37]. He reduces the look's intensity a little until it goes; a clean blue sky may otherwise show banding even if it is invisible on a test shot [53:37-54:41]. Test early and often with the look toggled on and off.

**Use it:** Run look tests on pure ramps and a clean sky gradient, not just on beauty shots.
