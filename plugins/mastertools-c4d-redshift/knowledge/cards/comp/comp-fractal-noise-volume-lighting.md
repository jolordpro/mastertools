Take the volume lighting pass alone, add Fractal Noise on Overlay, animate it with the swirl/twist options [1:20:05-1:21:08]; Exposure and Gamma deepen the light, per-channel tint adds colour [1:21:08-1:21:40]. The noise can spill into the scene, so check it.

**Use it:** Cheap way to animate haze without re-rendering; mark as comp-only.
