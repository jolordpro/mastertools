Change map = rebuild A / beauty (always beauty as denominator), then rebuild B x map [32:08-35:21]. 1 means no change, 0.25 quarter, 0 black, no negatives [36:22-38:25]. Order of A and B does not matter (8/4 x 6 = 12; 6/4 x 8 = 12) [38:56-39:28]. Darkening both setups holds up, killing a pass twice just multiplies zero twice [41:33-43:37]. Nuke Merge divide is A/B only; B/A needs an expression [43:50].

**Use it:** comp planner: default to the division method when combining light groups with material AOVs.
