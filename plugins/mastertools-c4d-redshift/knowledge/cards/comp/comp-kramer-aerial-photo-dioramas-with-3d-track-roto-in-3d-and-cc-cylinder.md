The idea: take real aerial footage, stabilise or 3D track it, then roto only what to keep. Because the track exists, the roto can sit in 3D space and sticks to the layers [15:13-15:45]. CC Cylinder wraps a texture into a 3D cylinder sized to the object; add a fake shadow [15:45-16:16]. Trees placed over the scene hide mistakes (a joke: cover up all your mistakes) [16:16-16:48].

**Use it:** A track makes roto cheaper because the roto layer can ride in 3D with the plate.
