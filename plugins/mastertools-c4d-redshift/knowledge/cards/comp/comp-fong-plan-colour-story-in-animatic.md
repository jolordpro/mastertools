On Boardwalk Empire the team planned the colour and light of each beat in the animatics, so backlight or a pink sky glow could be matched with the DP's footage.
Art director Lauren Hartstone helped build a master colour palette so the blue sky and weather progression stayed consistent across composited shots. The final plate was shot in golden light. [§6]

**Use it:** Before comping several sources, write the palette and light direction per beat and check each plate against it.
