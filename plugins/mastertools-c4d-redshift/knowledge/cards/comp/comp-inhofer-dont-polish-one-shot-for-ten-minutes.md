Beginners get lost on a single shot for five or ten minutes. Too many layers make it hard to match other shots later, and if the client then dislikes the direction the work is back at square one. His cure is a global view first: quick basics on every shot, then matching, then the look. [§3]

**Use it:** Time-box the first pass per frame and stop before the stack gets deep.
