The brief he and DP Todd Banhazl (caption: 'Ben Hazlehurst') settled on: pretend the footage was shot in 1980 on 16 mm, forgotten, and found now, so they restricted themselves to what that workflow could produce [10:59-11:29].
They shot modern 16 mm tests and iterated on how faded and grainy to go and how soft the lenses would be, then asked how to fold NTSC-style broadcast cameras into a modern workflow [11:29-12:32].
Out came a recipe: shoot this way, apply this, and you land in 1980. The DP then shoots normally with a light meter, and Volpatto starts from the recipe [12:32-13:02].
They allowed a good degree of wrong so the show never looked ultra polished [13:02-13:33].

**Use it:** Settle a look as a named recipe (capture settings plus a fixed transform) from test shoots before production, and leave deliberate imperfection in it.
