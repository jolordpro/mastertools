DPs from music videos and commercials were used to manipulating images extensively but could not in features until Company 3 developed the process, then made prints from it and later digital projection [22:20-22:50]. He says 300 would not be 300 without that look [22:50-23:22]. He adds that a music video grade took three or four days in the early tools and now takes about four or five hours on non-linear tools with many more options [18:12-18:44]. Numbers are his recollection, not measured.

**Use it:** A distinct, repeatable look (grade plus grain) can be a core selling point; define it as part of the pitch rather than a late pass.
