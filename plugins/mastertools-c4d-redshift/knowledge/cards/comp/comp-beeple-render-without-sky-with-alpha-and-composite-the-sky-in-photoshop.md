He says he exports without the sky and with an alpha channel, then drops the sky in Photoshop because it gives more control and lets him dial it in with curves [39:16-39:48].
In the demo the room picked a pink sky; blues and pinks go together, and it tied the picture together [43:09-43:41].
A mist layer needs lower opacity and a different blend mode to read as mist, not a cloud [49:17-49:52].
Co-hosts also noted fog can come from a depth pass in post rather than a render fog [23:07-23:40, 40:22].

**Use it:** Render the sky separately (alpha plus depth) so it can be graded or swapped after the beauty is done.
