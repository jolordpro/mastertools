He thinks of the colorist's pipeline as a laboratory: first the negative (the stock's colour response), then development, then print; that structure came from the Look Designer plug-in, which models the whole pipeline from negative to development [50:38-52:43]. Because he understands the pipeline he can rebuild it from his notes [51:11-51:41].

**Use it:** Structure a look as capture response, then development, then print/display, so each can be swapped alone.
