Kelly [45:28-49:20]: any remote review is a reference display plus an unknown client screen, so the goal is a matching reproduction on both. His recipe: send one feed to the reference monitor and a second from the video interface (SDI/HDMI) and loop it back into the computer through a capture device, so no OS-level colour management touches it; read that as a camera source in Zoom; apply a viewing transform for the receiving device. For colour-critical work he ships calibrated iPads to clients, with a LUT profiled for each iPad applied in line.
He also coaches clients: dim to moderate room, no light on the screen, brightness not above roughly 60-70%. Without that, an accurate feed does not help.

**Use it:** the remote-review checklist: clean unmanaged signal, a viewing transform for the device, and client-side room and brightness limits.
