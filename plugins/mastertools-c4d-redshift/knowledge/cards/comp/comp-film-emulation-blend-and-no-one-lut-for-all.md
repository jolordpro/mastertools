Walter Volpatto (feature colorist, Picture Shop; captions spell him "Valpado") warns against sliding the same LUT onto any project; adapt the look to the job [27:59-28:30].
He describes a blend of a full print emulation with a neutral camera transform, sometimes 50/50, to keep a gentler grey scale and some filmic tone without all of it [28:30-29:34].
A telecine of a film negative or interpositive drops the print-stage look, leaving only some negative character and a video curve; the print is where most film look lives [29:34-30:39].

**Use it:** Look setup: if a LUT is dialled to taste, offer it as a blend slider against neutral instead of full strength.
