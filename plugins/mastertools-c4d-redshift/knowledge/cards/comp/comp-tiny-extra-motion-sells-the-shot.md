A shot of men checking drone wings was too static on a big screen, so he filmed a long rotor cover blown by a friend in front of green screen on an iPhone in portrait mode, keyed and distributed it [12:05-12:36].
In night-vision UI shots he added a barely moving CG drone, and background activity like another drone getting ready, because slight motion sells the shot [14:39].
On the plane he added a four-frame dust speck crossing in front of the lens, so the plane reads as beyond it [25:42-26:14].

**Use it:** ask for one small secondary motion when a CG element sits in a still frame.
