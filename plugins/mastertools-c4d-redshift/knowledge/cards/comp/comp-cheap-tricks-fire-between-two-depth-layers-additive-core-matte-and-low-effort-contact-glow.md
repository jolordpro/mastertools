Drop stock gas fire behind the foreground layer, move the anchor to the fire origin and scale from the bottom, parent to the 2D track null, then tune the foreground's depth slider until the fire sits between the two subjects [38:36-39:38]. Additive alone brightens but loses the stock colour; the Supercomp Explosion Core preset keeps the body colour with a core matte, adds light wrap on the foreground and heat blur on whatever is behind [39:38-40:41]. Contact glow: duplicate the fire layer, squash it and add Fast Box Blur, put it under the fire in additive mode with a low opacity for a hint of ground light [40:41-41:17]. Heat Wave on an adjustment layer with its mask limits where the shimmer lands; Magic Bullet Looks finishes [41:17-41:52].
Transfers to CG compositing: the contact-glow cheat is a quick way to add bounce light without a re-render.

**Use it:** Use to sell a stock FX element on a plate or a CG shot.
