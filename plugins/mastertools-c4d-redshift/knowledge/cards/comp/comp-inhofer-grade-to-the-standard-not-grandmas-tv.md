His 'grandma's blue TV' image: do not correct for one odd old set, since the next viewer may have a red one. If every program is graded to the same standard, shows run together consistently on any given screen. He also says cinemas often run lamps at half power to double bulb life, which muddies the picture, so a standard cannot be assumed there. [§6-§7]

**Use it:** Judge on a calibrated display and treat consumer-screen complaints as a viewing-device question first.
