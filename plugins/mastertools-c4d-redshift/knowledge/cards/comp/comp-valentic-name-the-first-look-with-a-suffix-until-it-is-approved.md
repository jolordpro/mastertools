He never names a show look as final at the start. He calls it a candidate and adds a code word at the end (Hebrew 'omer', roughly first look, so the whisper spelling may be off) meaning it is the look we think we want but not yet the one in use [25:04-25:36].
After a week or two of shooting and everyone being happy, he removes the suffix and it becomes the show look, because he wants room to tweak and let it grow before it is locked [25:36-26:12].
Exports from the tool also name themselves with the camera and output so a late-night job does not produce typos [41:54].

**Use it:** Use a version suffix on any look or style frame until approval is written down; strip it only on sign-off.
