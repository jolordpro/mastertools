After the merge he used a Grade to put a subtle common tint on both layers so they blend, then a Grain at the very end [39:49-41:24]. Grain is needed only because the plate had noise; if the plate is clean, he says it may not be needed [40:54]. Check before/after on the noise level.

**Use it:** Use as part of a final-pass checklist: tint to unify, grain last, match the plate.
