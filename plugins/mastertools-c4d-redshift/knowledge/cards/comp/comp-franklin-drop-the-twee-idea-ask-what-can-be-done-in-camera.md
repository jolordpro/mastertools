Concept animations of the Paris bridge assembling itself in a blur always looked slightly twee and too magical. Nolan wanted something elegant and simple in concept that viewers could not easily analyse. The turn came when Franklin noted the arches looked like infinite reflections, and Nolan asked what could be done on set with a really big mirror. Effects built an 8 by 16 foot hinged mirror, held to about 800 pounds, and Double Negative animated what it could get in camera before the shooting plan was set. [§2]

**Use it:** When a CG-first idea looks cute, ask what a physical object could do instead, then use it as the base plate.
