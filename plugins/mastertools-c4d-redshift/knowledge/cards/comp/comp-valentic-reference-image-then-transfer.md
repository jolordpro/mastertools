He uses a structured prompt template (style, lighting scenario, colour palette) in Midjourney to make a photoreal reference still, transfers its look to his own footage with ColourLab AI, pushes that one shot to the timeline and matches the rest of the sequence by hand, as he always did. He says primary correction stays the foundation even inside his Look Designer tool [§25-§26]. His claim is that grading from an idea, not from what was shot, gives looks he would not have reached by pushing the raw file. [§23-§25]

**Use it:** Fine for exploring directions; the final pass stays manual. Check client AI policy before using any client frame.
