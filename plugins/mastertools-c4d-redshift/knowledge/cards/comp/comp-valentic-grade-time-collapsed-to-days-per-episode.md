Valentic: a 90-minute film once got about four weeks of grading, then two weeks, then a week; streaming now gives about four days for one hour, in HDR and SDR, and it must look better than cinema because the look decides whether people watch [10:09-11:13].
Result: corners are cut, such as not tracking every window, and colourists finish wishing for another day [11:13-11:43].

**Use it:** When estimating grade time, include HDR plus SDR, and budget for tracking and windows explicitly.
