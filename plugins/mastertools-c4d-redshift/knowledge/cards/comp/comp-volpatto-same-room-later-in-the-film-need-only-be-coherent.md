Create a world and a rule and stick to it (his example is the Matrix green-yellow inside, silvery blue outside) [22:21-22:53]. For a house revisited at the start, middle and end of a film, he matches time of day and psychology, not the old frame; if a viewer after 90 minutes feels the scene does not match the third scene, the story is already lost [22:53-23:56]. A rule is only for three acts to hold together, not for every shot to be identical [24:26].

**Use it:** Check returning looks against story time and mood, not pixel match.
