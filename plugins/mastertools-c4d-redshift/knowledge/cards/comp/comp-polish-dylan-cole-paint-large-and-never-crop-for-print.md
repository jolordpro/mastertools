For his Disney art book Cole insisted images not be cropped or zoomed; the aspect ratio is preserved so the whole image shows, and he was "crazy about color correction" to make print look as good as possible [53:10-53:41]. Images are large and given room to breathe.
He paints at very high resolution out of habit from art departments making giant prints: the work stays on a wall a long time and he does not want to be embarrassed by it [57:20]. Prints at his gallery event reach about 3.5 x 7 ft [58:22]; the collector's edition is about 15 x 18 in with 250 made [56:47].
He says seeing paintings in print or on big screens is totally different from Instagram at 1080 [57:50].

**Use it:** The coach reminds to render stills at final size or larger when they may be printed or shown big, and not to crop a hero frame in a layout.
