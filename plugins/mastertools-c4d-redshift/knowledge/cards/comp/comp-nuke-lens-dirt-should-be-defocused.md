Take a free lens-dirt or surface-imperfection image, run ColorCorrect + blur to colour it, multiply by the highlight source and plus it over the image [44:47-45:52].
Then defocus the dirt layer: dirt sits on the lens, and a sharp dirt layer looks pasted on [45:20-45:52].
He also crops early in the tree to keep render times down [46:23].

**Use it:** check item for any dust/dirt overlay: is it softened and driven by bright lights, not a flat sharp texture?
