CGStirk [17:12-20:14]. Make a null called Defocus Control above the CG, add Camera Lens Blur, rename the effect Master, and pick-whip each pass's blur radius to it; changing the master from 2 to 1.5 matched the plate. Do not paste it on the ship colour-correct layer. Match Grain goes at the very bottom of the stack: set the viewing mode to Final Output, choose the plate as the layer to match, then raise Intensity (about 2 here) and check shadows. Plate compression distortion cannot be matched easily.

**Use it:** For many passes, use one master control for blur and grain checks.
