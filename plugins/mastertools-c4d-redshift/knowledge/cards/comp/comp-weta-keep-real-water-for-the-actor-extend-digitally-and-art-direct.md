For the river scene, the special-effects department built a partial bridge set and a real tank with a weir wall and pumps rushing water past the cast [06:17-06:48]. The key work was deciding for close-ups how much real water to keep and where the digital extension starts; Weta simulated about half a mile of river rapids, set up so features could be art-directed [06:48-07:19]. The art-direction mattered because an earlier shot had to establish a dangerous stretch of water that could plausibly kill a character [07:19].

**Use it:** Plan real/CG splits shot by shot, and build the CG so it can be art-directed to support the story setup, not only match the plate.
