Feeding random frames from many movies to an AI model showed palettes that keep appearing, a recognised aesthetic behind 'filmic'; the cine styles encode that, not a film stock [25:06-26:09]. A set of street-photography profiles is based on Fuji camera recipes [24:04-24:35]. He compares palettes to music: a major-key palette suggests uplift, a minor one something going wrong, with no single right answer [23:32-24:04].

**Use it:** Describe a palette by the mood it carries and the films it echoes, then test one major and one minor option.
