Valentic sees consistency work (balancing and matching many shots, e.g. a reality-show episode of about 2000 shots from 10 cameras in one day) as the part AI will do better than a human, with no artistic expression in it. His host adds that consistency is about 80% of the problem and he would rather spend that time finessing [§17-§18]. He predicts high-end work gets more AI-assisted and more expensive, and the middle of the market loses its final grade for shows shot with care.

**Use it:** When a task is balancing or matching, expect to automate it and keep human time for the look.
