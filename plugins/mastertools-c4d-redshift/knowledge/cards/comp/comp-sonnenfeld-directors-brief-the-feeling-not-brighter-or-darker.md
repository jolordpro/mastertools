Mann describes where the character is emotionally and what he has just been through, and says how he wants the audience to connect [19:15-19:45]. The colorist then interprets that into colour. Sonnenfeld says this is why long partnerships last: it is rare to find someone you connect with creatively on a steady basis who can interpret that [19:45-20:16]. Other directors ask for something unique nobody has done, and he sits with DP, director and producers to build it [20:16].

**Use it:** Coach tip for notes: write feedback as the feeling wanted ("tense, cold, dry") and let the artist choose the colour moves; push back if a note is only "warmer" with no intent.
