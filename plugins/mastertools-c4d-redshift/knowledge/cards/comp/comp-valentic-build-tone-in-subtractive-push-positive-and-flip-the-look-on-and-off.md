After contrast he works on a black-and-white image, then sets tonality in subtractive controls, always adding positive values; moving cyan also changes magenta and yellow because light passes through all three layers [19:57-20:58]. He notes subtractive gives a pleasing colour balance easily and builds the look this way, giving an overall tint [20:58-21:28]. He keeps flicking the look off and comparing with the plain Rec.709 version to see what changed [21:28-21:58, 54:41-55:11].

**Use it:** A/B every look decision against the untouched transform, and add colour in one direction instead of fighting channels.
