A person in the room who is not grading soon asks for one more thing here and there, and you lose track [28:09-28:40]. A quick pass puts the whole scene in the ballpark, then you view it as a scene, not at the little thing in the corner; staying long on a shot reveals little things that bother and breaks consistency [28:40-29:14]. Starting at shot one and perfecting each uses all the time on the first reels [29:14-29:45]. He adds: viewers at home do not stop and look at the frame, they follow the story [14:32].

**Use it:** Run consistency across the whole package before refining; keep the reviewer on playback, not still frames.
