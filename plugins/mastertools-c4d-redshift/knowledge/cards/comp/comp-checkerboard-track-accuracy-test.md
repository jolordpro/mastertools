Place planes for walls, floor and ceiling and apply a 50 cm checker; scrub and look for sliding or floating [03:44-04:48].
Count rows to verify scale: 8 rows of 50 cm is 4 m, matching the real height [04:48].

**Use it:** turn the check into a step in shot QC for tracked work.
