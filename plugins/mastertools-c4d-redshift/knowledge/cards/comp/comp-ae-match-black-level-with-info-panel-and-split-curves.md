CGStirk [12:58-17:06]. Use the Info panel on a reference: the plate's darkest area read about 0.005 to 0.03 (the speaker reads 0.2 to 0.3 and 0.005 aloud; the exact scale is unclear in captions) while the CG went to about 0.01. In fog all blacks must lift to the level of plate objects at the same distance. Magnify the Curves for fine moves, since lifting is sensitive; compare numbers again (0.05 vs 0.06) and pull mids down if the bottom is too bright. Use one Curves for contrast and another for colour (pull in a little blue and red/green to remove a yellow-green cast). Where leftover colour sits in the underside, find which pass carries it (here only GI), darken it and drop Saturation about 40 to 50 on that pass.

**Use it:** For a plate match, measure both sides with numbers, not by eye, and find which pass holds a stray cast before grading the whole layer.
