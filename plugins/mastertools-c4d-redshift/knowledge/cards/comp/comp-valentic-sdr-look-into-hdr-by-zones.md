Judging HDR on set is unreliable. He translates the approved SDR look zone by zone, then tidies with windows, keeping parallel timelines. He argues HDR is a new medium, not a translated SDR grade. (2018 episode; tools have changed.)

**Use it:** If a deliverable has HDR and SDR, grade one hero and derive the other, and compare them in parallel.
