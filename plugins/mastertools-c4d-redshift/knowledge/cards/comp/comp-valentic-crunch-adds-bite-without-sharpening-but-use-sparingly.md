Printing film leaves a small air gap, so light disperses and the result has a bit stronger shadow and gloomier highlight, and it does not behave linearly [12:38-13:09]. Turned up a lot the image seems sharper though nothing is sharpened; he says be careful not to overdo it and calls it a useful trick that curves cannot reproduce [18:54-19:25]. Use it to control a strong highlight after adding warmth [21:28-21:58]. Captions are partly garbled here.
