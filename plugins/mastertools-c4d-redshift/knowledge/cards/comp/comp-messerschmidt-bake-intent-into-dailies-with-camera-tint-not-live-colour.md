There was no live LUT on the monitors. Instead the camera colour was nudged so the dailies reflected what the team wanted to achieve: about 4000 to 4200 K on camera for a typical day interior, shifted with tint to influence the grade [1:09:01-1:09:31]. Reference points given for the lights were 5600 to 6000 K for day and about 3000 K tungsten for night [1:08:29-1:09:31].

**Use it:** Make the first review frames already carry the look direction, so the client does not judge a neutral frame that will change.
