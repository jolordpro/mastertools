The colorist role has grown to cover pre-production and integration through shooting and post [01:41]. He asks what the production is shooting on, then builds look-up tables that give a characteristic look for the show, not necessarily the final one, and that gets baked into what editors and studios see [01:41-02:12].

**Use it:** Create a show look early and apply it to review images so everyone sees the intended direction; leave final decisions for the grade.
