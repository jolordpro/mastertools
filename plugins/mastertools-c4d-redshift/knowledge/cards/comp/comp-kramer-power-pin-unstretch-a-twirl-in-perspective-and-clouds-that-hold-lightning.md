For a storm vortex over a NASA Earth image, he needed a twirl on a perspective surface. Use CC Power Pin like a normal corner pin to 'unstretch' the vortex, apply the twirl (any 2D deformation) on top, then duplicate the Power Pin after the deformation and click unstretch to return the image to its original perspective; some perspective settings fine-tune it [13:40-14:42].
Other parts of the storm: a cloud element made a negative cutout so lightning sits inside the clouds, and a particle system (using Orb for 3D spheres) threw small cloud flashes over the hurricane texture [12:03-13:40]. His joke: do not use your whole lens-flare budget at once [14:42].

**Use it:** To deform something that sits in perspective, flatten it, deform, and re-project with the inverse pin.
