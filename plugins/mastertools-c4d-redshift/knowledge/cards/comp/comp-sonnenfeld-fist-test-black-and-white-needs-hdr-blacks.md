Guest: Stefan Sonnenfeld. He says black on a xenon projector in P3 is really grey. In his room he holds his fist up into the light: the fist looks black against the screen's black, but only because it casts a shadow on a lit screen. In Dolby HDR the screen black matches the shadow [27:50-28:52].
He sees black and white as a format like a film stock chosen for storytelling, and worked on Maestro and a black-and-white version of Nightmare Alley; his favourite stock was a reversal black-and-white one with "inky" blacks and sharp clarity [27:19-29:23].

**Use it:** Judge a low-key or mono frame on a true-black display; a frame whose blacks look rich on a glowing monitor may look washed on a theatre screen.
