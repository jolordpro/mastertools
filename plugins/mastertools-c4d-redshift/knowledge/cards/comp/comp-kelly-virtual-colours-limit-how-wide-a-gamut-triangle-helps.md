Kelly [54:06-58:35] explains that three primaries form a triangle, and one big enough to hold the full horseshoe also holds large regions outside it: "virtual" colours that are only data and have to be handled later. Trial and error showed that a perfect enclosure is not enough. He says the new Alexa 35 scaled its gamut back from ARRI Wide Gamut 3 for that reason (as stated by him, not checked). A six-primary display project he consults on (6P Color) adds degrees of freedom so more of the real colour range is covered without going outside it, by working in chromaticity coordinates at reproduction. [59:49-1:04:01]

**Use it:** reason for choosing a working space: wider is not automatically better; out-of-locus values can become the clean-up problem in comp.
