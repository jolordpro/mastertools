One thing artists wish they knew earlier is photography: each lens's attributes, and how a camera works, as it is the basis for plate matching [29:26-29:57].
On Ready Player One they could invent any camera, so they had to decide what to expect from one [29:57-30:28]. Vintage anamorphic cameras are booked every day because modern digital images are too accurate; lens artifacts are part of film's visual language [30:28-31:30]. Advice: watch films, shoot photos, make short films [32:35-33:40].

**Use it:** when a CG shot feels fake, check lens traits (flare, aberration, depth of field, focal length) before changing the model.
