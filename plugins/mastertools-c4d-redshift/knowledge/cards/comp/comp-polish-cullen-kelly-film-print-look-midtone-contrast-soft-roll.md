Public summary of a members-only tutorial: a print-style look pairs firm midtone contrast with soft rolls into highlights and shadows. Shadow smearing eases the fall into black by pulling the darkest values closer, using a soft luminance qualifier with lift and gamma. He works in a colour-managed DaVinci Wide Gamut timeline and tests the node on more shots before keeping it. He credits the technique to Company 3 colorist Tom Poole. No values are published.

**Use it:** Over-glossy CG often has hard clipped ends; a soft roll at both ends is the print-look fix to propose before touching midtones.
