Cinema and TV behave differently. His team uses an approach developed in 2002 called Scene Based Image Manipulation, a perceptual model that lets one grade be converted automatically for more than one display. He says they keep improving it and it is one of the industry's biggest challenges [§2]. He names two drivers of today's look: HDR TVs with deep shadow detail and rising sensitivity in digital cinema cameras [§1]. Text is German; read in translation.

**Use it:** Grade the look once in a display-independent way, then check each target display.
