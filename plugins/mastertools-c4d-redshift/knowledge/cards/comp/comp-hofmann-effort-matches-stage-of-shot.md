As a supervisor he expects layers-in work to be fast with no attention to black levels, and expects a compositor heading to final to spend about two minutes on black levels, defocus and track, plus checking every note [§5-§6]. He also gives trusted artists a morning briefing and an evening version without midday check-ins, since rushing from dailies to dailies leaves no creative time [§5].

**Use it:** Match review depth to the stage: rough for early layers, a basics sweep before final.
