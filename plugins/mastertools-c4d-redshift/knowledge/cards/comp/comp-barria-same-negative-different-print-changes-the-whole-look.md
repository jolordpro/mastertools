A lot of what makes something look like film is the negative-plus-positive combination [1:05:01-1:05:31]. With the same negative and no other changes, pairing with different print emulations (Kodak, Fuji, and AGFA-lineage positives, names unclear in the captions) gave very different images [1:05:31-1:07:07]. He used the combination as a base grade, and got four starting points from balancing only [1:07:41-1:08:44]. Print names in the captions are garbled.

**Use it:** Offer a client two or three print-stage options over the same base, not different grades.
