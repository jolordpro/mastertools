Client says too dark: raise exposure on the GI pass, which is bounce light, to bring back wall detail and lower contrast; nothing burns because the comp is ACEScg [35:00] [1:19:58].
Same idea per pass: exposure on SSS to push a candle, gamma to calm specular [36:08].
Optical glow on the specular pass only gives subtle bloom; exposure up on the emission pass first, then glow, then a crude mask for one area [1:20:30-1:21:01].

**Use it:** the coach can suggest per-pass grading before offering a re-render for a lighting note.
