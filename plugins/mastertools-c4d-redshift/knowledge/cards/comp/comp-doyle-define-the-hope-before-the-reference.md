Doyle [03:43-14:19]: first define what is expected of you in each film's "little kingdom". Capturing on any medium is already interpretive, so the question is whether technology dictates the image or you take control of it [04:48-06:20]; he thinks you should take control: a quote he took from Alexander Kluge is that we use the oppressor's tools to take back our dreams [11:12].
Establish what the hope is, then interpret it, rather than copying references [12:15]. He cites the new field of colour linguistics: the words we use change how we perceive colour, and Munsell harmony rules ignore culture [13:18].
Film era: colourists knew how each stock interpreted tone and skin, so lighting included filters and print stock [08:31-09:03].

**Use it:** The coach opens a look discussion with the intended feeling and who it is for, then picks references to support it.
