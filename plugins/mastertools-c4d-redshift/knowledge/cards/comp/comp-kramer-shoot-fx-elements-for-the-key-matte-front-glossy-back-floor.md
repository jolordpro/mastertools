Old explosion elements were cut off at the ground. For the new set the dirt had to land, bounce and interact with the floor and still key cleanly [38:09-38:39]. The floor was sprayed matte at the front so the explosion did not reflect in it, and left glossy behind the blast so it reflected the blue wall and blended with no seam or horizon line [39:42-40:43]. The floor could be mopped and the charge reset.
His rule: do not make a generic element, make the element a specific composite problem needs, such as fire that rolls up a surface [38:39-39:10].
Transfers: planning a catcher or backdrop reflectivity in a CG-on-plate shot.

**Use it:** When a render must be keyed or comped, decide the reflectivity of the ground and backdrop first so the edge is clean, and add ground contact (dust, debris) to hero elements.
