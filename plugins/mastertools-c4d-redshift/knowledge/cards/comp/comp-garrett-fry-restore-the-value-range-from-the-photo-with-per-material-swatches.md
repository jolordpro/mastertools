Garrett Fry [10:36-13:47]: shipping containers in the plate go from the brightest ambient to the darkest dark with a range between; in the paint some areas went mushy. Restore the range that was in the photo. Copy a patch of a container as a swatch and move it over the paint to check, then do the same for red and blue, checking hue and value consistency across the shot.

**Use it:** Swatch-test each material in a composite.
