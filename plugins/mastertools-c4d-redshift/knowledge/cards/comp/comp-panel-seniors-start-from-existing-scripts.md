Josh Parks sees juniors and mids start the shot by hammering nodes, while seniors first look for shots that already hit the brief and grab grade nodes or green-screen setups [12:44-13:48].
A 2D supervisor adds that seniors spend a lot of time looking at the elements and the shot: where the light comes from and what colour the shadows are [13:48-14:19].
Tip: collage the script from existing work, then work into it, instead of making everything from scratch [13:17-13:48].

**Use it:** ask what existing look or setup already fits before building a new one; and read light direction and shadow colour first.
