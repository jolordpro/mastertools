Walter Volpatto (feature colorist, Picture Shop; captions spell him "Valpado") says the colorist can make photography infinitely worse but not infinitely better, and credit goes to the photographer, so the role is to help what was shot [08:48-10:22].
His mentor taught him to find the original photography and the director of photography's intent before modifying or breaking any rule [58:17].
He says a finished shot should make the audience believe it was one scene, one moment, with continuity; where lights sat on set does not matter to viewers [25:25-27:29].
A DP and director know every light they placed, so they see it even off-frame; a viewer does not [25:25-26:28]. Transfers: in sports packages, treat the brand and broadcaster look as the DP's intent.

**Use it:** Review habit: ask what the original lighting intended before adding contrast or colour on top.
