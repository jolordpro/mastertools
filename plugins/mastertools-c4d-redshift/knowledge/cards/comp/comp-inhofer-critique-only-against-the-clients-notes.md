Inhofer [36:02-38:07]: when he ran critique training he handed out projects with an interview of the actual director, so everyone worked from the same notes and his feedback was based on the director's or producer's wishes, not on his own taste. He says he has no way to say a grade is good or bad "in relation to what" without that context; it is unfair to the client to critique with no direction.
His structured workflow teaching ("colorist flight school" idea) makes people avoid attacking the look before they finish shot matching and basic balancing, so they do not create more work later [37:35-38:07].
Why: clients all differ, so critique is about their preference, not about a trained eye.
Transfer: when a render is reviewed, record the brief it was judged against.

**Use it:** The coach asks what the brief or note was before giving a verdict on a look, and does matching and balancing before look.
