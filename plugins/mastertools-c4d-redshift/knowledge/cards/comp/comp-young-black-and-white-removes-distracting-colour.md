He cites Haile Gerima, who said he shot a film in black and white because he did not want the green of the grass and blue of the sky to become the conversation [1:02:01-1:02:32]. Young uses black and white for the same reason, plus black objects, to focus on texture and materiality [1:02:32-1:03:02]. He adds it is also the purity of light and shadow, and even in colour you must know where your mid-tones, shadows and highlights sit for the story [1:03:02-1:03:33].

**Use it:** Review trick: view the frame desaturated to check value structure and focus; if the story reads, then add colour back.
