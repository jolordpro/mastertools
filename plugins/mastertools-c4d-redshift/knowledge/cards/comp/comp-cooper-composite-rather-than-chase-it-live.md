Cooper says he is happy to take a live-action plant shoot and build the sky digitally, and calls this another arrow in the quiver [04:55].
He admits wasting money trying to do something in a live-action setup when a composite would have done; shoot the person against green, shoot the plate separately, and do not chase the real thing [05:25-05:56].
Reason: it is much more economical, you can add or remove a building, and the assets are more malleable [05:56].
Transfers: sports packages built from plates plus CG have the same trade-off.

**Use it:** Before a costly practical shoot, ask whether a plate plus composite gets the same frame cheaper.
