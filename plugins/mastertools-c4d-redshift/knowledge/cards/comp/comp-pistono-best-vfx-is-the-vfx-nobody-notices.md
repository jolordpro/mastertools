She says the public thinks VFX is all spectacle, but much of the job is making sure the work goes unnoticed. Her example is Nike's You Can't Stop Us, where a transition between two pieces of footage across one axis is handled so well that the hours of work vanish and only the story remains. [§1-§2]

**Use it:** Judge a seam or transition by whether the viewer ever thinks about it.
