He splits the colourist's job: look creation is instinctive and human; matching shots shot on different days and cameras to that look often takes longer than creating the look [09:37-10:09].
On Netflix shows he graded only key shots in each scene and an assistant matched the rest to the reference. That is how colourists learned: a senior says redo, you redo, you improve [16:20-16:50]. Working at home he lacks an assistant, so the tool became that assistant [16:50-17:21].

**Use it:** In grade handoffs, mark the reference frame per scene and give the matching pass a clear target.
