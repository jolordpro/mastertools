On Frankenstein the cinematographer set palette and contrast mostly in camera, so Sonnenfeld described his approach as intentionally simple and natural, with targeted adjustments rather than heavy stylised treatments. Those he saves for projects that call for them.

Page spots (Spanish text translated): cinematographer Dan Laustsen designed the shoot so dailies and final colour aligned, with palette and contrast mostly set in camera, from costume to window warmth [panorama §1]; Sonnenfeld calls his work refinement, not reinvention [§1]; he calls his approach intentionally simple and natural, with elaborate techniques kept for films that need them [§2].
