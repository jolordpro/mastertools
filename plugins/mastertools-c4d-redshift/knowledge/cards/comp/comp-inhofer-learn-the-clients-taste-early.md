Inhofer calls this the toughest, most important part of an experienced colorist's job: seeing through the client's eyes. Settle early the right amount of skin tone, the colour of blue sky and green lawn, brighter or darker overall, more or fewer pools of light, and whether vignetting is liked. Finding out late means redoing work.

**Use it:** Open a project with a taste check on those six traits, using sample frames.
