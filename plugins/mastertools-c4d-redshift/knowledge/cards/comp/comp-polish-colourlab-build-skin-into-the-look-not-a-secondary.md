He tried several print stocks until skin sat right, dialling one stock back, because print emulation reshapes skin saturation and cools shadows (2393 mentioned) [16:19-20:00]. Reason [17:19-19:28]: a show full of close-ups cannot rely on a clean skin qualifier every shot (chin edge, hands drift to a different colour), so the face should be right from the primary and the background, which is easier to change, gets the secondary. Warning: film emulation changes saturation strongly with exposure, so match skin exposure before matching tone [15:47, 33:57].
**Use it:** In a look build, pick the stock/curve against the face first; use qualifiers only for the background.
