He begins with a contrast curve because it has the biggest effect on the image, comparing it to a composer choosing tempo first; he cycles through curves (a dreamier highlight versus a Kodak-style curve, for example) until one feels right, then sets its strength [17:53-18:54]. Real film transfer curves behave in ways hand-drawn curves rarely match, which is why a curve library helps [11:32-12:38].

**Use it:** In a look pass, fix the contrast character on a black-and-white image before adding any colour.
