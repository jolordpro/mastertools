In SDR, picture modes alter the image drastically; HDR (Dolby Vision) gives a more precise way to represent intent, with less room for error [05:52-06:22]. SDR-to-HDR conversion in TVs is a marketing feature; he advises getting the content in its purest HDR form [06:22-07:23]. HDR not done well is not pleasing, the process cannot be done quickly, and a wide dynamic range gets you into trouble [08:25-08:57]. The cinema and home grades must both be careful [08:25].

**Use it:** Plan more time for an HDR pass and review it on a proper HDR display.
