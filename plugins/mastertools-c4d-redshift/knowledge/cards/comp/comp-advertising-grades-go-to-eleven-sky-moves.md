Walter Volpatto (feature colorist, Picture Shop; captions spell him "Valpado") says ads sell an emotion in 5 to 20 seconds, so colour goes to 11: blue sky with puffy clouds, green grass, even when the photography makes no sense [18:21-19:43].
On a coast-to-coast spot the agency wanted the same sky blue across morning to sunset; he cringed as a colorist but accepted the product was the point [19:43-20:46].
His method: avoid sky replacement because it looks fake, grab the sky loosely with a mix of keys and soft grades, then swing it with curves, a web or trackballs [20:46-21:16].
He has to remind himself it need not look real in this setting [21:16].

**Use it:** Coach: match the grade ambition to the format; a 10-second broadcast bumper can go hotter than a drama, but grab and soften, do not replace.
