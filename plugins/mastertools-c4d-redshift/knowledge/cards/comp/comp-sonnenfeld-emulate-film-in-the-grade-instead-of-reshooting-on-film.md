On Michael the cinematographer (named 'Deion' in the captions, probably Dion Beebe) first considered going back to film for the look since it was shot digital [23:05]. Sonnenfeld said he could match it exactly and with more control and variability than shooting film [23:36].
He tested, without shooting, the film process: contrast, colour, grain, film curves, the basic mapping of the colour profile of different film stocks [24:08]. They picked looks that suited the period, then made scene-specific looks once shooting began [24:40].
Company 3 uses proprietary LUTs and colour science plus engineers and artists, and he says it can emulate many stocks, even a TV show shot on a particular film stock, so it is a matter of imagination [12:02, 14:07-14:38]. He still loves shooting film [23:36].

**Use it:** To offer a film look, build it from its parts (curve, colour mapping, grain) and show it on a test frame before anyone commits to a film-shoot route.
