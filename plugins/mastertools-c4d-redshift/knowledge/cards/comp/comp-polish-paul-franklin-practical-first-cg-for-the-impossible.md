On Inception his team used a full-size train, a rotating corridor and triggered avalanches, then CG for things like rain too hard to shoot in slow motion. The only digital doubles in the zero-g sequences were two replaced heads. Rigs and wires were removed digitally. Concept animation was made before the shoot to set the camera angles the mirror rig needed.

Page spot: §2-§4.

**Use it:** Use real photographed elements as the base and CG as the extension; pre-viz the camera before building.
