Her sequence: make a lead drawing or style frame, pick the one that is nearest finished (here a minotaur frame), and colour it first. After a couple of hero frames "things fall into place," because later choices can be checked against the palette already set [1:01:23-1:01:55]. Every small choice becomes a rule: how much shadow, whether texture tracks, how graphic to go [1:01:55-1:02:26]. The art director and lead animator raises look-affecting questions people forget, such as whether to use motion blur at all, which can change the aesthetic hugely [1:02:26]. On this job a gold gilding effect did a lot to sell the mural look, so he ran R&D on it [1:02:57].
