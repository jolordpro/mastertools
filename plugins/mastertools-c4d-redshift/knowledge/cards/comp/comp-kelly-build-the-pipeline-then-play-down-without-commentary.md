Kelly [1:07:11-1:11:19] had about four hours to grade a short film. He spent the prep on a custom pipeline (a technical part plus a creative part), then asked the director and cinematographer to watch a straight playdown with no pausing or comment, each noting what felt good or bad. The first reaction was that the unfinished image already looked good. The grade was then mostly global, photometric moves (open a thin exposure, knock down an unimportant area), driven by the cinematographer's notes.
His takeaway: a strong foundation lets him grade less and let the client drive after watching.

**Use it:** the first review of a new look should be a continuous play with notes kept silent until the end, after the base pipeline is set; it keeps early notes about the whole, not the pixels.
