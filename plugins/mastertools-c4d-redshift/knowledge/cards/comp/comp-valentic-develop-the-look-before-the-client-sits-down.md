Valentic says to firmly establish how things will look before the client rolls in or full grading starts, because an amazing look at the press of a button is hard to find once the clock and suite charges are running [01:39-02:43].
He starts with whatever footage exists, even test footage or camera tests shot before the real shoot, and talks about the look well in advance, so grading time is spent adapting and fitting it across the sequence [01:09-02:43].
His reference tool for starting points is a still-frame library (ShotDeck): filter by genre, colour, time period, interior/exterior, save a deck, export full-size stills [02:43-09:40].
Whisper transcript (accent): names of films and tool buttons may be off. Transfers to broadcast grade/finish: lock the look with the director before the render queue starts.

**Use it:** Add a look-lock step before final renders: references agreed, one hero frame graded, then all shots adapt.
