R20's Cloner instance mode: set Multi-Instance rather than plain Instance, so a scene of 50 to 100 million polygons stays movable; viewport shown as points, switch to object mode to place precisely though slower [02:39-03:40]. The final image took 11:30; without the water (reflections) 5:40; early default-setting steps took about 40 s, enough to judge reflections [00:34-01:36].
He says it ran on one PC with one 1080, no farm [05:45]. Compare with `redshift-instance-mode-per-clone-colour` for the per-clone data trade.

**Use it:** forest or crowd shots: draft at default samples, final only after layout; use multi-instance and viewport points.
