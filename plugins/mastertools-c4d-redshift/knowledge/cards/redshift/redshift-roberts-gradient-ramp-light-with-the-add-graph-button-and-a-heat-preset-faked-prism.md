Make a very slim area light at the front with the spread turned down, press Add Graph (a shader graph for the light), open it, add a Ramp node and connect it to the light's general colour [26:08-28:19]. Cinema 4D's ramp presets load directly; a rainbow preset makes a rainbow light; he used the Heat 2 preset and changed the green to bright white so the light looks like it splits through a prism [28:19-28:49]. He calls it a trick, a fake, with strong visual impact [28:49-29:21]. A backdrop gives a bounce light in the scene [26:41].

**Use it:** Use a ramp-coloured area light for stylised rim or edge colour on logos.
