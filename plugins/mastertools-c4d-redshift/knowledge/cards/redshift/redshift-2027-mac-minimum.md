The 2027.0 coverage gives the Mac floor as an Apple M-series chip with 8 GB. It says nothing about a higher bar for Live.
C4D 2027 itself needs macOS 15 (see the C4D 2027.0 price and OS card).

**Use it:** do not assume an 8 GB Mac is a good draft machine; the floor is a minimum, not a recommendation.
