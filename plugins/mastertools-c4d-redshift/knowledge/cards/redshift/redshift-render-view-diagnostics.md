Also in the view: Show Output Before Denoising, Render Info tokens and snapshot compare with render time. Clay mode lets a light setup be judged before material noise gets in the way (Maxon Pro Tips talk).

**Use it:** teach 'snapshot, change one setting, compare' in the coach and use clay for the first light check.
Transcript support: render modes regular, clay (judge key to fill and colour temperature) and samples; fixed scaling plus render region up to 250 percent to inspect a highlight; LUT slot in render view (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LcxjkHFS-2k [17:06-21:47])
