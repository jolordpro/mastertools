Derek Kirk [07:49-08:50]: with depth of field on, set shutter to 180 degrees (his note: the most filmic) and load an RGB bokeh image (a red-green-blue split shape found by searching images) into the camera bokeh image slot. Out-of-focus areas then split into coloured fringes, an Into-the-Spider-Verse feel. He pairs it with toon shading. The shutter and bokeh numbers are speaker taste; check against the ledger before using for sports motion work.

**Use it:** offer as a stylised DOF option; test cost at 640 first because bokeh images cost samples.
