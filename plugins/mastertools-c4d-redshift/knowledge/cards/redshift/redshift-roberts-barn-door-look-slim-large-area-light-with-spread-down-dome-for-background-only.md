Roberts recreates the Bond-style strip of light across a face [07:22-11:38]. Camera at 300mm. Dome light as backdrop only: open it, go to the Project tab and drag the hero model into its exclude folder, so the dome lights the background but not the object. Add an area light, make it large but very slim, move it back, and bring the Spread all the way down; drop exposure (he uses minus 3, adjusted per target) so only the logo and switch he wants are lit [09:35-11:06]. He asked at Q&A why he changes exposure not intensity: habit from real cameras and levelling lights with the camera, 'you don't have dimmers' [47:19-48:20]. He says he had been learning Redshift for only a month, coming from Octane, so treat the settings as one user's workflow, not tested values.

**Use it:** Use for tight beam on logo faces and sports hero shots; keep a single area light with low spread.
