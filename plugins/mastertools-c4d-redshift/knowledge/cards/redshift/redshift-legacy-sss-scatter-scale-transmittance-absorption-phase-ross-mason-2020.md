Ledger-check: this is his claim for the old SSS model, before random walk; do not mix its names with the new standard material. Soap bar 6 x 3 cm [02:03]. Disable diffuse and reflection, enable the scatter scale and push it up; he used about 0.08, noting it works together with the other sliders [04:37-05:38].
Transmittance colour is the colour absorbed; absorption scale (about 0.6) sets how much of it shows [05:38-06:41]. The scatter colour is the glow that bounces back out (a lime green, about 70) [07:12-07:42]. Phase about 0.6 gave a backlit look with glow at the edges and a thicker middle [08:44-09:46].
A black and white speck texture plugged into the scatter amount makes dark patches and bright flecks, which sells thickness [10:10-11:18]. Reflection comes back last, tinted like the object [11:48-12:20].

**Use it:** use only when the project still runs the old SSS; for current work use the random-walk cards.
