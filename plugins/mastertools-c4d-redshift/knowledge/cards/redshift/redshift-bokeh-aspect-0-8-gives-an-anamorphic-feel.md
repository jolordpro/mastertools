Working in a small IPR region, he changed the bokeh aspect to 0.8 for an anamorphic lens look and stepped through maps with heavy chromatic aberration, which gave a camcorder feel [08:19-08:50]. All maps work with the HDRI link tag so options can be A/B tested quickly [08:50].

**Use it:** Offer anamorphic aspect as a look preset toggle, with moderate chromatic aberration only.
