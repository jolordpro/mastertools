ISO, shutter speed and f-stop only change exposure, and he finds their numbers arbitrary [47:30-48:02]. White point is a white balance: set it warm and the picture turns cool, and the reverse [48:02-48:35]. Vignette: about 10 looked fine, 100 far too heavy [48:35-49:05].
Tone map: the over-exposure threshold limits how far highlights may blow before they taper (he settled near 0.2), the black threshold crushes shadows (left alone), saturation about 0.8; 0 gives black and white and 2 looks ugly [49:05-50:06].

**Use it:** coach: use for explaining the camera tag; verify against the ledger because later versions moved this to photographic exposure and post effects.
