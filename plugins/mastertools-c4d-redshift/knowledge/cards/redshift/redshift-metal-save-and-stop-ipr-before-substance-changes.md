He turns IPR off and saves before touching Substance resolution or loading a new file, since substance can crash and lose the project [27:09, 30:52, 31:54].
Resolution rule: 1024 is enough for a wide shot; go to 2048 only if the camera is close and scratches look soft, then restart IPR [26:38-27:42].

**Use it:** a pre-step in any texture-resolution change; the coach can say save first.
