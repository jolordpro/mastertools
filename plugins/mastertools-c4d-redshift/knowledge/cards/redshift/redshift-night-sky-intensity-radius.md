[17:22] Night Sky lives in the Sun and Sky rig and uses non-physical intensities for moon, stars and Milky Way, so you do not push camera exposure.
[19:56] A bigger moon radius needs more intensity to show; smaller radius needs less (radius slider tops at 50).
[24:38] Trick: moon intensity high enough to look right (about 40 in the demo), then duplicate the sky as a second sun-and-sky for moonlight, raised until the landscape is lit; the visible disc and the lighting are tuned apart.
Position modes: time and location, altitude and azimuth, directional vector. Sun gizmo rotation still moves the moon.

**Use it:** use as a recipe for night shots instead of raising camera exposure.
