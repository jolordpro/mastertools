Before opening Advanced settings, render a frame at Low and Medium: Low shows how fast it can go, Medium shows acceptable quality [43:45-44:16]. Only then tune toward Low's time with Medium's look.
Definition of a good time depends on still versus animation: 5 minutes is fine for a still, ruinous across 300 frames [29:54].

**Use it:** the estimator's first two drafts are Low and Medium; the coach reports both times and the quality gap before suggesting changes.
