The Render View has a Clay toggle for judging light without textures, and it makes displacement visible [24:42-25:37]. For Picture Viewer renders, right-click in Render Settings > Material Override, choose clay or a custom material (not an area-light material) [25:49-26:08]. Drag materials into the exclusion list to keep glass [26:39].

**Use it:** The clay pass can be a real render pass, not only a preview; exclude glass.
Transcript support: Material Override with a toon material gives a one-click toon blockout (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZCojy8uF3FY [08:50-10:25])
