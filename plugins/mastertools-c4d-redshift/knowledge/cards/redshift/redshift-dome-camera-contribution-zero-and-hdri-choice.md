Dome light Details tab, Contributions: camera down to zero gives a plain background while the HDRI still lights [41:57]. Use the camera background override for a colour [1:12:56].
For glass and refraction avoid dark studio-type HDRIs: they reflect and refract dark areas; a real-life interior reads better [40:53]. Rotating the HDRI changes light direction fast; left-to-right feels natural to Western readers [41:26].

**Use it:** scene check: dome with the HDRI visible to the camera on a product render; the coach suggests HDRI changes for glass look.
Transcript support: Second dome used as fill/background with its own diffuse and reflection contribution from the background colour (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7-q1JKG8638 [20:48])
