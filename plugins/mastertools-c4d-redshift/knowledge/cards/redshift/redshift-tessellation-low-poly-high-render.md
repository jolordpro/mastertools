Override geometry in the tag and enable tessellation (subdivision) so the viewport stays low and the Render View shows the smooth mesh [1:03:23-1:03:55]. Scene dependent.

**Use it:** Suggest it when a scene is heavy only because of subdivided meshes.
Transcript support: Adds numbers: uniform subdivision 1-2 enough for low-poly toy models. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MuVzbVhMUvI [29:17-31:20])
