Chad renders a frame with the render-view button instead of the Picture Viewer so everything stays in one place [09:20-09:50].
A copy-to-clipboard button puts the image on the frame buffer so it can be pasted straight into Slack or Teams to show where the work is [09:50-10:21].
Use original size for the render and Fit for the IPR window; Fill expands the view and shows areas beyond the camera frustum, which will not be in the final [10:21].

**Use it:** Review habit: share an in-progress frame in one step and make sure the person sees the real frame, not a Fill-mode crop.
