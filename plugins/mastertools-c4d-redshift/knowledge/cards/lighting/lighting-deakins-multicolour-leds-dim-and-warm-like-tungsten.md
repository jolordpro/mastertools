Asked how dimmed LEDs avoid the cold shift tungsten does not have, he answers that the LEDs he used on Empire were multicolour, so he could dim them and warm them up to get the same effect as a tungsten source. He also says a clear bulb gives a cleaner shadow, and in a practical either clear or frosted may look right. [§3]

**Use it:** For CG lights that emulate a dimmer, tie colour temperature to intensity so dimmer means warmer.
