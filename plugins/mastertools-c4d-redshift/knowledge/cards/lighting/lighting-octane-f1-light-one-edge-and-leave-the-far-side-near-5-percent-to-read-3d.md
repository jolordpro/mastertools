Kapustin [15:39-17:45]. Rear wing: add a cooler small light, find the angle where the paint shows dark places, a nice falloff and a few bright places. Compare on and off: without the light the edge vanishes into black, with it the geometry reads [16:09-16:40]. His rule: make one side light and give the other maybe 5 percent, then it reads 3D; brightening everything makes it flat [17:12]. Same check on the rear wing pair, where without the two rear lights the form is lost on a dark background [19:50-20:21]. Bottom line: when everything is dark you do not need an HDRI, place each light where it is wanted [23:31].

**Use it:** A fast on-off A/B per light is the check; if a light only brightens and adds no edge, delete it.
