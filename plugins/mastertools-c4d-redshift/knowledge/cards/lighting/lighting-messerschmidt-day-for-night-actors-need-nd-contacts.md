Guest: Erik Messerschmidt. The Hearst Castle walk-and-talk was shot day-for-night because two garden locations could not be lit at that scale [53:45-55:48].
Plan: sun stays back or side light about 90% of the time, so each shot gets a time slot; the whole day was blocked on the first day. He lost sleep over cloud risk [55:48-56:48].
Underexposing daylight in the grade makes faces go dark, so heavy extra light went on the actors. In the test the actor said he could not perform because he was squinting. They made ND-tinted contact lenses (about ND9, as he recalls), ordered in mid-September for a December shoot [56:48-58:52].
The lenses were arranged in prep after a camera test, not on the shoot day [58:52-59:53].

**Use it:** Any shortcut that fixes the picture can hurt the performance; test it with the talent in prep.
