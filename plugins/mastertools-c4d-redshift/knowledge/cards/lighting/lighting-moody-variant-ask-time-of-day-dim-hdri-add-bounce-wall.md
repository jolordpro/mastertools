Chad Ashley's starting questions for a room scene: what time of day is it, are the blinds open or closed, what vibe? [39:31]. His first pass was bright, modern and studio-lit from the side [39:31-40:02].
For a moodier variant he changed the angle (still a longer lens, a slightly looking-down POV), put the lamp in a corner, and dimmed the HDRI that supplied most of the light way down [40:02]. The cyclorama wall was floating far back, so he added a cheap nearer wall to give the lamp something to bounce from [41:03].
He kept adjusting the framing and the falloff on the wall until the light felt motivated [46:17]; a straight horizontal edge of falloff read as fake, so he moved the spread.

**Use it:** an advisor can ask the same three questions before suggesting light values, and check that a practical lamp has surfaces near enough to bounce from.
