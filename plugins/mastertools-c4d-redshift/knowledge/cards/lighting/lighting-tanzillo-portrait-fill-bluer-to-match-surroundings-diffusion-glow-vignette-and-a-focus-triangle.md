Tanzillo to a student's portrait [38:02-44:13]:
- Losing one eye: add fill; fill looked greenish for the space, so make it bluer to match surroundings.
- Lift mid values; overall a little brighter.
- Add a diffusion glow over the skin detail and a vignette because edges get bright.
- Hair: thick chunks go dark and heavy; soften or paint down sharp dark edges (fine in a one-off, not for a character used in hundreds of shots).
- More spec on lips.
- To darken the chest and keep light in the face-neck triangle, in order of preference: narrow the key if it is a spot; use a gobo or light blocker; worst case render the key separately and roto it out in Nuke. Do as much in the render as possible, then mix in Nuke [43:11-44:13].

**Use it:** same fixes work in C4D: fill colour from the set, glow and vignette in post, spot cone or light linking before comp tricks.
