With one light group per role, each contribution at the subject is its own image, so the ratio is measured, not guessed from pixels.
Report as a ratio and in stops (for example 5.2:1, 2.4 stops), with the dial that moves it, and show it on a false-colour view.
This is a proposal from the research, not a sourced practice.

**Use it:** the coach states the ratio from passes and names the light to change; needs light groups on the draft.
