The team asked how the world would be lit before touching a render. With the whole world on the inside of a sphere, the light source had to sit at the middle: the sun, or whatever lights this world. Second-source note (The Ringer, not on this page): in the season 8 rebuild the torch-lit Winterfell crypt took the most revisions until it matched a real space, and opening panels let in outside light.

Page spot: §3.

**Use it:** Every light needs a source you could point to. Revise the hardest low-key interior most, and test it against a real space.
