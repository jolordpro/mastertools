Her jellyfish example from Finding Nemo: the scene struggled while reviews talked in technical numbers. She rebuilt it with balanced blues and pinks, caustics on the bells and undulating fog beams, and the director's reaction showed the art had returned. She warns that over-reliance on technical data can drain life from a scene, describing the scientific tether as strangling the scene. [§7-§8]

**Use it:** Reviews of a polish pass should be in look-and-feel terms first, with numbers as checks after.
