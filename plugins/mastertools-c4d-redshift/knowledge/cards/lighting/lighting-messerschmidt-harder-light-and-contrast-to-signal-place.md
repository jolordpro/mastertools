He used more hard light through windows and was more aggressive with contrast in the Atlanta-set material so the audience felt the sunny heat of the city [49:24-50:26]. He looks at the script first: what does the scene need, and is the place establishing itself without an establishing shot? [50:57] His rule is to find the bare minimum needed to tell the story, which is where the look comes from [51:27].

**Use it:** Differentiate locations with a deliberate change in light quality and contrast, not with colour grading alone.
