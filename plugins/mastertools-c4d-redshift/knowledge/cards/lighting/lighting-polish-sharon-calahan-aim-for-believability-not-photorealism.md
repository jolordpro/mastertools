CG sits at a distance from the audience, so authenticity, place and believability create connection, not photorealism [07:00-07:30]. How real depends on the director: Toy Story 4 aimed nearer photoreal with real lens and filter behaviours, Onward took a lens package and exaggerated parts of it to stylise [12:09-12:39]. Tools stay physically grounded with hooks added for any look; method is to envision the result, then work backwards to the tools [10:35-11:08]. Core skill that lasts is judging an image and knowing what makes it stronger and more emotional [08:31-09:03].
**Use it:** Pick a realism target per job and, for stylised looks, start from a real lens package and exaggerate specific traits.
