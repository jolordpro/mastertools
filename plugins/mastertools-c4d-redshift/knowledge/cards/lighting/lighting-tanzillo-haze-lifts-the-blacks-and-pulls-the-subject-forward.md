Mike Tanzillo 55eY1-LlY3k [24:26-26:01]: adding a little haze and lifting the darks behind a motorcycle separated it; push further, make background lights interact so they glow within it, and add depth of field when the camera is close.
sCDCs5At8uY [17:02] on a small scene, try about three to five pixels blur at the back of the table, more outside.
Quan Tran ETgt9YXSaeg [09:29-10:33] likewise: more depth haze behind a night walker, but volumetrics around him matter less once he is near camera.

**Use it:** Treat haze as a depth tool: lift blacks in the distance and let lights bloom in it.
