Francesco Giroldini (lead lighting/comp artist at DreamWorks, ex-Pixar and Blue Sky; Academy of Animated Art interview): ideally one sequence at a time, but he is setting up one while finishing another and supporting lighters on a third [20:26-21:37].
One day: reviewing nearly done shots and giving notes with paint-overs, building the look of a new sequence with look-dev material artists, and sending very low-quality frames just to see the look [21:37-22:10].
He pushes struggling shots up so the next sequence starts in a good place [22:10-22:41].

**Use it:** Review flow: low-quality look frames early, paint-overs for notes, spare effort for the weakest shots.
