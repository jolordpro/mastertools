Alessandro Jacomini (Disney Animation, director of cinematography for lighting on Encanto), Italian interview, whisper text so approximate. For very complex scenes (he mentions a Frozen scene) the team begins as early as possible, even if camera choreography and the environment are not final, using approximations and moving rough to fine; otherwise hard problems land when time is gone [04:24-05:44]. Genesis of such a scene ran to months in double figures, and enabling technology R&D can start two years before the film ends [05:01-05:44]. Departments overlap more over time rather than waiting for each other, and the lighting team grows sharply in the final three or four months [03:15-04:24]. He expects lighting to move earlier toward animation using real-time previs tools, harder for stylised looks [13:01-14:45].

**Use it:** Light the bad version of the hardest shot first, then upgrade. Book the heavy render and finishing push for the end and size the farm for it.
