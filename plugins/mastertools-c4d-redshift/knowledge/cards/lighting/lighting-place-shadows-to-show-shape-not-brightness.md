Two mindsets compared on the same figure: "too dark, add a light" versus "needs shadows to emphasise the shape" [03:11]. With the second, he moved a point light until shadow sat on the chest, biceps and stomach, from just above the character, and the form read instantly [04:13][04:44]. Swap to a big area light for soft shadows; small light gives hard shadows [05:14]. Set world strength to 0 to raise contrast and judge only the key [06:17].

**Use it:** coach prompt: ask which shadows the light is placing, not whether the frame is bright enough.
Transcript support: shadow perspective points back to the light, which helps match a still (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dT4Vl3PGe08 [05:58])
