Maria Walla [42:52-43:54]: she goes back to the same place two or three times at different hours with a camera, then edits the photos to see where a rim light or shadow would be better. In museums she asks why she likes a painting and analyses the light [33:55-34:26]. She argues that the industry's rush leaves no time for the artistic part and that eye and sensibility matter more than technology, which anyone can learn [34:26-35:29]. A host aside [36:00] says lighting studies by portrait painters apply to characters.
