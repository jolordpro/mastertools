Lionel lit a metal part with three lights because it is simple and what real pack shots usually use [41:16]. Key is always the small light (he swapped the textures by mistake and corrected it) [40:13]. The fill is larger and softer; the rim is a duplicate of the fill moved behind to counter the shadow side [40:45-42:29].
He switches each light on and off to see what it contributes and balances from there [42:29]. Area lights use Add Target so they always face the part [28:10]. Studio backdrop from the C4D content, reflections off, brighter background [18:39-22:27].
At the end he was unhappy with the lighting (shadow too thick) but said it is the most important part.

**Use it:** coach: for a metal product, start with this three-light order and solo test it.
