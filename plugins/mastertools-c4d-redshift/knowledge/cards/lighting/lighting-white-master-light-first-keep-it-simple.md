A sequence like the dinner table locks master lighting in anywhere from a week to four weeks; shot lighters then take those lights [§1]. A shot of Mom might take three days to craft and be reused across many shots at that angle; a wide with every character and the whole set can take a couple of weeks [§1-2]. White works from broad to fine and keeps lighting as simple as possible so it renders faster and someone else can take over a shot; when it gets too complicated you can see it in the image because things aren't adding up [§3].

**Use it:** Build the hero lighting once, simple and handoff-friendly, then derive shots from it.
