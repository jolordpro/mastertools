Mike Tanzillo [19:13-20:48]: the master lighting shot sets the rig. Begin the other shot with the same rig and judge whether it feels like the same light. The student had added a third face light with a gobo and planned to switch lights on and off across a cut.
His fix [24:28-25:30]: use the window light in the position it has on the first character plus the desk lamp, with no character-specific lights and no light linking, then see where it lands. Animated lights read as lights being animated.
A lamp near the table also lets the characters step in and out of brightness, which adds dynamics for free.

**Use it:** When a sequence's second angle fights its own look, rebuild it from the first shot's rig with only the motivated lights and add back one at a time.
