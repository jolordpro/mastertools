Deakins says a director has never asked him to make a film look like a specific other film, and he would be upset if one did [20:38]. One director wanted a film to look like Shawshank Redemption; he declined because he wants each film to look like itself [21:09]. On Blade Runner 2049 he and the director agreed the sequel had to stand alone and he could not shoot like the original [21:41].
Allowed: a reference for feel, such as the small-town California sense of a Hitchcock film, not how it was lit [20:38, 22:12].
He argues old-film looks came from the technology and limits of their time [22:42]. Period work should not default to sepia, since eyes in 1500 saw as we do; a stylised grade needs a deeper reason than "it is old" [23:14].
Transfer: references on a look-dev board should name the feeling and constraint, not ask for a copy of a frame.

**Use it:** When a brief says 'make it look like X', the coach asks what about X (feel, era, limit) and builds that, not a lookalike grade.
