Gaffer Danny Gonzalez says the gels sold as sodium vapour looked too rich, so the standard became a straw-hat green, dropped a quarter, over incandescent; on LED panels they dialled a matching colour [49:09-50:42]. The cobra-head street lights were real sodium, and for a night driving road the team had a lamp post added for continuity where only trees stood [48:06-49:09]; on another street they had 70 W street-light bulbs swapped for 400 W, and residents left them in [43:51-44:56].
The lesson: test the gels, choose the one that keeps skin settled, then fix it as a standard.

**Use it:** Define a named colour preset for each recurring practical type (sodium street light, fluorescent) and check it on skin before reusing it.
