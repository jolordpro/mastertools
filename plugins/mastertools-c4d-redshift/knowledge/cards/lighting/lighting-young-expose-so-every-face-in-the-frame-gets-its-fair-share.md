He says to be very conscious of how you photograph all characters in one space; do not meet a technical goal for one at the cost of another. The approach should be "more egalitarian" [44:19-44:52]. Use skin tone and a realistic test (see the lookbook test card) before the shoot. No numeric exposure values given.

**Use it:** In a multi-athlete or group shot, check each face or key surface on the waveform or in a probe, not only the hero.
