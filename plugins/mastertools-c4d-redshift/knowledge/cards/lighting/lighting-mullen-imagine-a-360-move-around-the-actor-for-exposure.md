For a sequence, he suggests imagining a circular camera move around the actor in sunlight: first with one f-stop for the whole move, then opening up for the shadow side and closing down again in frontal sun. His general rule: the shadow side of a face should feel like the shadow side, so he would not expose a backlit face at full exposure; decide how many stops under key look natural [§1-2].

**Use it:** When setting exposure or light levels for a 3D orbit, check the darkest and brightest angles against the 'it should feel like shadow' rule.
