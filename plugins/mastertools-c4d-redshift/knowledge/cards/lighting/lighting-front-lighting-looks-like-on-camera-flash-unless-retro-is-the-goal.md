School of Motion [05:28-06:16]. Avoid front lighting that creates an on-board-flash look: it flattens detail and reads as amateur, especially when the light sits at nearly the camera angle. Front lights from above or slightly to the side are better, and a front light as fill can be nice, but as a key it usually does not work. Exception: renders that deliberately recreate 80s flash photography look convincingly retro and gain photorealism that way. Also mentioned: stylised night-city HDRIs (mostly dark with neon) give interesting reflections on cars and wet pavement, so HDRI-only lighting can work when the HDRI is the style.

**Use it:** Add to the key-light advisor: flag a key within a small angle of the camera axis unless a flash look is requested.
