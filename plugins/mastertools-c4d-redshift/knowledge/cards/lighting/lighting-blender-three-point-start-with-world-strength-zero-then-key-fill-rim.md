Steps: build a curved studio backdrop (extruded plane with a bevelled edge); split the view into camera and working views; set the world strength to zero to control the lights fully [00:33][01:03]. Key: strongest, an area light at about 45 degrees from the camera (not in front or behind) to shape the subject; use a rectangle and lower the spread for sharper shadows [01:03][01:34][02:04]. Fill: opposite the key, bigger area and weak, so shadows soften without extra highlights and the key's contrast stays [02:04][02:37]. Rim: rectangle, strong, lowered spread, makes a bright outline to separate the subject from the background; the narration says "behind the camera", probably a mistake for behind the subject [03:08][03:38]. A beginner channel, so low confidence.

**Use it:** beginner baseline; compare lighting-key-angle-flat-45-behind and lighting-wallace-hair-light-metered-about-half-a-stop-over-the-key.
