Young says the foreground decides how the visual effects look, because effects are added later; they shot the human foreground and let VFX enhance it [05:48-06:19].
Rule: rarely or never throw focus to an effect or CG element, even added helicopters or the alien ship in the field; the film is about the people in front of the camera [06:19-07:21].
A shot about a human should be about the human, and trusting the audience means not reminding them to look at the ship [07:21-07:51].
Be 'not precious' about the effects element [06:49-07:21].

**Use it:** In a comp or CG shot, keep focus and exposure priority on the live subject and treat the CG element as a supporting layer.
