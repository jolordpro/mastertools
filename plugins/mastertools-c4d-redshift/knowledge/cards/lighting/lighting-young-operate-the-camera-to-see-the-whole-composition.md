Because he stays close and intimate with subjects, Young often sticks behind the camera as both DP and operator, which is rare. The profile ties this to his wish to see and capture compositions as holistically as possible [§2].

**Use it:** Judge framing and light together in the same pass instead of splitting the decisions.
