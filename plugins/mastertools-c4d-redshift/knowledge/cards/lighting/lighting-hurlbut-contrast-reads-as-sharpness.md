He says lenses under about $3,500 get their sharpness from heightened contrast; stills glass also has contrast built in. A Cooke resolves more than a Zeiss, but the Zeiss looks sharper because of extra contrast and a cooler tone, and cooler looks more clinical [19:29-20:00].
That clinical look is what reads 'videoesque' on the Sony G series and the Arri-type lenses he tested [18:57].

**Use it:** If a render looks 'digital', lower contrast and warm it before adding blur; sharpness may be a contrast and colour issue.
