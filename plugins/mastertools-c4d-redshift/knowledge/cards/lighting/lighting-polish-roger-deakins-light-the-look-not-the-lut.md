Per the article's account of an ARRI interview (second-hand, no numbers), Deakins has used the same LUT for most of his recent digitally shot films, with slight contrast and saturation adjustments to suit each film's look, and he crafts looks on set through lighting. The warning that crews lean on the LUT, and that strengthening the lighting may be the better fix when the image disappoints, is the article writer's view, not a Deakins quote.
