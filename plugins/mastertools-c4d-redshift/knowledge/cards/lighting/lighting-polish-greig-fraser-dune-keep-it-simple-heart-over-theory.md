His approach was to keep it simple: not over-lighting, over-complicating or over-theorising, using what he had learned in twenty-plus years, while giving the right amount of soul and heart. The look had to be interesting enough but simple enough for a large audience, unlike earlier arty versions of the story [§1]. He credits alignment among four to seven key creatives for the cohesive result [§3]. [§1,§3]
