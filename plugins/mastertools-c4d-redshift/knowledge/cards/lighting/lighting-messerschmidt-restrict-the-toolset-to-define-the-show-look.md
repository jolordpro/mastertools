He and Fincher wanted a respectful, non-exploitive show, so they limited tools: prime lenses with three or four focal lengths for the whole show, the camera rarely moving, and a restrained colour palette developed over two seasons [01:17-02:50]. Result: a specific flavour that is easier to hold because you do not wander. The show is told in cuts and static frames; movement is for pace or to punctuate pivotal moments, and the directors agreed to let performance play [13:40-14:43].

**Use it:** Pick a kit for a shot or package (few focal lengths, few move types, a set palette) and write it down before building, so exceptions are visible.
