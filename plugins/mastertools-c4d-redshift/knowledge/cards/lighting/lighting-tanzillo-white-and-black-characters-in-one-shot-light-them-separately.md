Tanzillo says colleagues pointed him to Bolt as the nightmare case: a white dog and a black cat in one scene [26:28-27:00]. No single light setup keeps the white fur from blowing out or going grey while the black fur still looks good, so the two are lit separately and combined. Student Eric Dorman, in the same session, singles out a Zootopia shot where one character stays in shadow and a warm light divides the frame to carry feeling [24:54-25:57].
Transfers to Redshift/C4D: render the bright and dark subject on separate light groups or object-masked passes and balance them in comp.

**Use it:** Suggest light groups or object masks when subjects sit at opposite ends of the exposure range.
