Sam Nicholson [00:58-03:02]. The big gain: the DP sees the composite, so light and exposure are right on set; LED screens are bright and do a nice ambient fill, but they do not give hard light or hard shadows, so Stargate used projectors for hard-cut sunlight through the windows [00:58-01:30]. The same two-step as a green screen comp: perfect the screen exposure and tracking, then adjust the light on the actor separately, adding a hard backlight or window light [02:00]. A misconception: nobody needs an electrical department. Some volumes are built without truss so nothing can hang from the rafters [02:30]. Dramatic shows need a different setup per scene; a game show or news show is the same each week, so a fixed rig suits them [03:02]. Actors get eyelines and a real environment; he cites 12 weeks inside a train set [04:36].
