Empty house with no practicals: light entirely through windows, with 18K units and large diffusion frames outside to copy bright northern daylight; HDR shows the benefit because the window holds detail while the room still falls off [20:18-20:50]. Airplane interiors got a cloud-flicker effect from pumping nitrogen past the windows with fans, so shadows move rather than dimming a light [21:21-21:53]. In a window-lit basement, smoke lifted the shadows just enough [22:26]. Scenic floors and carpet were built by the production designer [19:48].

**Use it:** In CG: window light with a large area emitter, volume or fog to lift deep shadows, and animated cloud-shadow gobos for flicker instead of animating intensity.
