Deakins says he learned by doing documentaries, making his own gear, including solar panels for batteries and lights from household bulbs [00:43-01:14].
He still does this: crews have found a ring of 40 W bulbs overhead as the only light on a set [01:14-01:47].
He does not claim to light like the old greats; he lights the way he likes [00:13-00:43].

**Use it:** In CG, remember the source can be small, cheap and ordinary: model the real bulb and its falloff rather than a studio kit.
