In bright sun the eye reads detail in both highlights and shadows and sees colour more vividly in under- and over-exposed areas, while a camera has a fixed range that exposes well. On The Good Dinosaur she aimed for how a scene feels to look at, not for a film stock or sensor. She calls the result painterly rather than photoreal: detail and a natural palette give believability, and the enhanced reality does not come from the 'photo' part. [§1]

**Use it:** Grade or expose CG to the eye's wider range instead of clipping to a camera curve.
