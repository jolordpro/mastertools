Windowless concrete sets got patterns of moving light that read as sunlight, and water-reflected caustics on ceilings. A silhouette for Officer K came from architecture as much as lamps. Colour effects were made while shooting, not in post. Where VFX was needed he lit both actors on his set instead of using a blue screen so the composite matched his light. Sequences used bold palettes while framing stayed restrained.
