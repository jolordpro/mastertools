When asked why spend so long if no one will notice, White says audiences may not be able to say why a film like The Godfather is beautiful, they just feel it, and without lighting care a film loses extra complexity of emotion. Pixar wants to make A movies, not B movies [§4]. She also notes lighters get faster across a production as they learn the characters and visual goals, and one master lighter owning a whole sequence (like Abstract Thought) works faster [§2].

**Use it:** Justify polish by the felt effect it creates, not by whether viewers can name it.
