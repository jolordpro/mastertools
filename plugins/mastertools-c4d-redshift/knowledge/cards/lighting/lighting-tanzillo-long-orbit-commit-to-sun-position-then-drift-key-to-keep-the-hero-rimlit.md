Tanzillo [05:45-11:58], reading a 360-degree move from an animated feature clip:
- First step: decide where the sun is and keep to it whatever the camera does.
- Place the sun so the background falls into shadow and the foreground subject stands out.
- Keep the hero rim-lit by subtly animating the key around him; audiences accept it because the camera move hides the drift. Balance believability against the hero look, which are always at odds.
- Eye highlights ("eye dings") stay at about the same position, favouring the key side.
- Crowd characters stay in one shared light rig (they are almost set); hero characters get their own rigs, with occasional light linking for a hero extra.
- Paired characters keep the same angle but are made brighter to stand out.

**Use it:** for a C4D hero orbit, key the light rotation with the camera and check three frames (start, middle, end) for rim and face read.
