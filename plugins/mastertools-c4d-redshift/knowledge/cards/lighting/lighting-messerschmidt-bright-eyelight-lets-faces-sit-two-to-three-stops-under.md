References were 1970s thrillers (Three Days of the Condor, Klute and Mississippi Burning, caption spelling uncertain) that used hard frontal eyelights; the team built a small soft unit they called the puck, the size of a hockey puck [1:15:45-1:17:51]. A bright highlight in the eyes lets the audience forgive an actress's face being two or three stops under, which gave them room to go darker than their comfort level [1:17:17-1:17:51].

**Use it:** In dark portrait or athlete frames, protect a small catchlight in the eyes before lifting the whole face.
