Tanzillo on a challenge entry [04:14-05:49]: check visible noise (around a candle and at the sides) and turn up render settings on the very last render; add AO so the backs of bookcases get darker for depth; amp up the fall-off of light as it crosses a room; reduce saturation of strong green leaves. A related lesson: a late submission was ineligible however good [03:13].

**Use it:** a pre-final checklist for stills: noise at the brightest small source, depth cues, falloff, foliage saturation.
