On Mindhunter the screens were the only lighting for car scenes. Their white point is 6000 K; to make white read as white, the camera is set to 6000 K, and any other white balance shifts every reflection at once so the team chases its tail [21:06-22:10].
London Fog diffusion sat about four inches in front of the panels to blur individual pixels in reflections [22:10]. The panels were about 5 mm pitch, driven through a media server and a lighting board [22:41-23:46].
White or black layers over a plate could raise or flag the light, and there was usually more stop than needed so they could drop intensity [22:41-23:13].
Because the walls are RGB only, the team restricted the palette away from magenta and red tones, and relied on the sensor handling the spectrum [43:21-44:23].

**Use it:** Treat the display white point as the camera white point for any screen-lit or screen-referenced shot, and avoid saturated reds and magentas when the light source is RGB.
