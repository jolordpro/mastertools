The Nemo team recreated real underwater footage in the computer, then broke it back down into its parts: red lost first with depth, then green, leaving blue at depth; surge and swell, an invisible current that moves particulate; caustics, ribbons of light like those on a pool bottom, made when sun bends through surface ripples; and fog beams that give colour depth cues and show which way is up. The particulate was lit only by caustics so it appears and disappears in them. They decided which elements had to be accurate and which to push; colour had leeway (a sad Sydney Harbor scene pushed green) and water visibility was pushed far past real life so the turtle scene read.
