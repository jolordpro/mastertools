McNally [00:08-01:42]: with two lights ratios become part of your language; equal power is 1:1, a main twice the fill is 2:1. His urging: do not get hung up on numbers; the goal is an emotionally beautiful response, not a numeric one.
Workflow [01:12-03:00]: background lights fixed on manual groups; foreground lights ratioed against each other in TTL groups, so the ratio stays fixed while camera flash exposure moves the whole scene. Discover the exposure in TTL, then flip the remote to manual (flash lock) when the subject must stay the same for 100-200 frames, since TTL can vary.

**Use it:** same habit in CG: find the look by eye, then freeze light values and change only global exposure, so repeated frames match.
