On season two the team kept most interiors at T2 to T2.5, an aesthetic choice to keep depth of field consistent across the show [27:58-28:29]. Setting the stop and leaving it gave the DP and gaffer a handle on how much light every set needed; he estimates about 12 to 15 foot-candles on the floor, with the gaffer measuring it [28:29-29:00].
This supports the existing T2.5 card.

**Use it:** Fix one working exposure and depth-of-field target for a look, then size lights and render exposure to it instead of re-deciding each shot.
