Legato's rule: in a shot of the boy under a tree, expose for the subject (he says a stop under) and let the background sit two or three stops over, because audiences have seen that look in real films. He also says lighting need not match shot to shot; pick the light that makes each scene work best. Before the shoot, MPC pre-lit key angles with the DP using domes and soft boxes as sky, bounce cards and sun, settling time of day, angles and ratios.

Page spot: §4-§6.

**Use it:** Let backgrounds roll hot when the subject reads; don't flatten to match.
