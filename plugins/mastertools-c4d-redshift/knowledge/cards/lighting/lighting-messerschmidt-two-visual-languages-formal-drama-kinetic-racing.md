The film is about opposing forces (racing against finance, family against passion), so the visuals split to match [21:51-22:21]. Drama scenes are classically composed with little camera movement; racing is kinetic and visceral [22:21-22:53].
They skipped classic racing coverage meant to explain who is ahead; scripted plot beats such as an overtake or forcing a car off the road are camera directed, while the rest is about energy and emotion [22:53, 25:30-26:32].
Methods: handheld in the passenger seat at 150 mph with a driver who is a race driver, a biscuit rig (drivable platform with a precision driver) for less experienced actors, and a Sony Venice 2 with a detachable lens block on a cable so the lens can sit in the actor's space [23:25-24:58].

**Use it:** For a sports open, pick two camera languages (controlled hero, kinetic action) and keep each pure instead of blending them.
