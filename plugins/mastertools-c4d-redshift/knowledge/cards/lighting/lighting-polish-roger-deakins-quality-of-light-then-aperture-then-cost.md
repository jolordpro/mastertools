He gives the order of priorities as quality of light, aperture and cost. In Unbroken's restaurant a large bounce on stands was more efficient than a grid of small lamps. Clear bulbs give cleaner shadows, while frosted bulbs may suit practicals. He does very little digital intermediate work, does not use a histogram, and says a full answer to most forum questions would fill a book.
