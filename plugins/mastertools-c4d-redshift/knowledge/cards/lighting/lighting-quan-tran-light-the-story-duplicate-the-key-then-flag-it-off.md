Quan Tran [12:41-16:26] on a girl smiling outwardly but sad inside: cheat a helper area light or duplicate the key and swing it for a Rembrandt look while she smiles; when she turns, flag it off so her face goes darker, with the flag placed so it affects her only when turned. He is explicit that it may or may not work.

**Use it:** Use animated flags or a duplicate key for a character beat, not a whole-shot compromise.
