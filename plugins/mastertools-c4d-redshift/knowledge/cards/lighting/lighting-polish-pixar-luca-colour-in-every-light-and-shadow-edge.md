Lighting lead remarks [29:14-31:15]: colour pushed into both lights and shadows ("no white lights", "no grays"), colour put at the edges of shadows, and a texture they called fringe broken into some shadows to feel handmade, but not everywhere because the underlying texture already carries detail. They keep areas of calm (sky nearly empty, clouds detailed only at edges) so the picture reads as style, neither too simple nor too real. The first land shot was pushed brighter and then settled so it reads as "a different place" after the muted underwater [29:14]. Source of ideas: coloured bounce between buildings seen on location in Cinque Terre [30:45]. Underwater was restrained, top-down, soft, low contrast; warm colour only above water [06:50-07:53].
**Use it:** For stylised lighting, set where colour goes (shadow edges), where detail goes, and where it rests; check frame for a flat area of calm.
