The Asset Browser has an HDR folder (some are Maxon One only). A dragged HDR creates the dome and texture; the next drag finds the existing dome and swaps its HDR [53:07].

**Use it:** Use for fast HDRI auditions; one swap per candidate keeps comparisons clean.
Transcript support: drag an HDR from the Asset Browser into the Object Manager to make the environment object (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HlQhMbjrM6M [03:23-03:56])
