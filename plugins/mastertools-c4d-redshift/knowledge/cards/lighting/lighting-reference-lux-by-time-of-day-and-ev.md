From a lighting cheat sheet he shows (not his own): noon sun about 100,000 lux, paired with EV 14 on the camera [10:55-12:29]; the true unscattered sun is about 130,000 lux (answer to a question) [31:05].
Overcast: 5,000 to 20,000 lux; he used 10,000 and EV 7 to 10 for a low-sun interior look [20:25-25:00]. Dim interiors are lit with local lights, not by raising the sun [13:34].
Once the sun and exposure are set, he adjusts only the directional light and exposure, not every light, to move between looks (outside to a dark den) [27:19-27:51].

**Use it:** starter numbers for believable exterior-to-interior light ratios; source sheet not named in the captions.
