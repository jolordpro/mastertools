Mike Tanzillo [01:44-03:14] on a ruby that sits still for much of a shot: put a plain square plane behind the camera, start it screen right and rotate it slowly so the reflection walks across the facets. Matte white keeps the reflection true; colour can be tried later. Render it as its own pass so the amount can be dialled.
He says use a plane with a material, not lights, because a card picks up more. The read is a dancing shimmer rather than visible movement.
Transfers: any held reflective hero (trophy, logo, glass) in a broadcast open or sting.

**Use it:** Suggest a slow-moving off-camera card reflection, on its own pass, for a reflective hero that would otherwise sit dead on a held frame.
