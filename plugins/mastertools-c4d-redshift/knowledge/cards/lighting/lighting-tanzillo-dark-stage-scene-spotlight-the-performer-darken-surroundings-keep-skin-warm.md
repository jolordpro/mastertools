Tanzillo on a church-stage performance [25:38-27:46]: push the idea of a spotlighted centre and let surroundings go darker, since the set is simple geometry with little detail (so darkness plus some depth of field helps). Skin in dark scenes tends to go cool purple, so keep warmth and speculars while holding dark values. Watch dark shoes. Keep a nice balanced, symmetric image with matching sources either side.

**Use it:** a quick lighting direction for dark broadcast looks: spot the subject, darken the set, check skin does not go purple.
