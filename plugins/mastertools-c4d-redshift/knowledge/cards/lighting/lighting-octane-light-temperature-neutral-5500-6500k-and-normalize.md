Per the video: temperature follows the kelvin chart; neutral bright white sits at 5500 to 6500 K [06:18]. With Normalize on, brightness stays about equal across temperatures; off, warmer light gets darker and you must add power manually, which he calls more physically accurate because warm light feels dimmer than blue [10:25]. He leaves Normalize on [10:25]. Use light colour rather than a texture by ticking Use Light Color [12:32].

**Use it:** for lighting presets: record the K value, and know whether the renderer normalizes brightness when you change it.
