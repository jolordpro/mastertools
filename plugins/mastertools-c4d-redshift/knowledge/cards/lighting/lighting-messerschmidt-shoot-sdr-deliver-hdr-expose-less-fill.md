He says shooting for SDR and finishing in HDR is like shooting film but judging it on a video tap, seeing a narrow band of what the camera holds [45:38-46:08]. HDR monitoring changed his habit: he uses a lot less fill and protects highlights less, and plans to monitor in HDR even for theatrical SDR work [46:08-46:39]. Story use: hot windows or explosions can hit the audience [45:06].

**Use it:** Judge highlights on an HDR-capable display where possible, and treat SDR as a trim of the HDR master.
