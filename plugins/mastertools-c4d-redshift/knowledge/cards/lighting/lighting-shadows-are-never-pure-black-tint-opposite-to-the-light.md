Leo in Color [05:34-06:43]. A key observation from the film breakdown is that shadows in a stylised film are still not pure black; real shadows carry colour from surrounding light. The author sets warm lights with deep orange or red shadow colour, and cool lights with green, teal or dark blue, comparing intensity, colour temperature, shadow density and shadow colour in one panel. Also: keep the rig and adjust a few intensities and angles for new props or a character, so the next piece starts about 90% done and the look stays consistent.

**Use it:** A check can flag large areas of exact black in the shadows, and the coach can suggest a shadow tint opposite the light's temperature.
