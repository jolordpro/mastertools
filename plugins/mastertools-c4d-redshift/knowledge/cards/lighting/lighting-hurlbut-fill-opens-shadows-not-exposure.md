He treats ambient and fill as nearly the same thing, but fill is placed to lift the shadows and soften contrast; it is not an exposure balance [28:30-31:39]. He puts it close to the camera, over the lens, and likes a 180-degree arrangement: back light or three-quarter back, then side, wrapping round to front fill [31:08-32:11].
Horror uses less fill; a romantic comedy uses more [32:11]. He says he lit his night class with a full ratio [32:11-32:42].

**Use it:** Use fill strength as a mood dial and quote it as key-to-fill ratio in the lighting note (see lighting-ratio-stops).
