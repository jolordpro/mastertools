The show's slightly green-yellow tone is deliberate: tungsten emitters tended to shift slightly magenta, so the green emitter was added at about 3 to 5 percent, and diffusion warmed things a little further [1:04:17-1:04:48]. An eighth or a quarter green went onto nearly every LED light; the fluorescent tubes were embraced for their natural green, and some units got about three-quarter green (the caption is unclear which) [1:04:48-1:05:50].
The light engine's daylight (blue) emitter also let them take the colour to 10,000 K for cool scenes [1:05:20-1:05:50]. An anecdote: even with no budget, there was always money for green gel [1:05:50-1:06:22].

**Use it:** Pick one tint direction for the whole look and apply it as a small constant bias on every light; remove magenta cast deliberately if the palette is green-warm.
