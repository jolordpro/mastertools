Daily life scenes had to look mundane, grey and underlit; as the lead nears the ship, lighting and frames open up a little so the audience gets relief [02:43-03:49]. His method: bury the rest of the film in the toe so that when she stands in front of the screen the exposure feels super bright relative to everything before [03:49-04:19]. The aim was that it blinded us as it blinded her.

**Use it:** Plan a piece's brightness arc: hold the base low and reserve the brightest, cleanest frame for the payoff so it does not need extra intensity.
