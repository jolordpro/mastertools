Manuel's IBL recipe for a live-action short with CG ship: capture a 360 on set with a Canon 5D, 15 mm fisheye and a tripod panorama head set to fixed angles; per tile shoot 5 to 7 exposures, turn, repeat until the whole sphere is covered [21:46-22:48].
Merge each tile to 32-bit HDR (tone-mapped only for the slide), stitch in PTGui, colour correct and clean-paint, then use directly for lighting [22:16-23:19].
Transfers: same capture logic for any plate shot that needs a CG object lit to match.

**Use it:** Plate-lighting checklist: capture HDRI on the day, same time and place, bracketed, stitched and cleaned before lookdev.
