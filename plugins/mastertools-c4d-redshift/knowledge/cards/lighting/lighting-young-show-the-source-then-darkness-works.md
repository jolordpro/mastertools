Once the audience knows where light comes from, darkness is accepted. In Arrival a white screen was the practical: frontal shots got soft light, reverses went nearly silhouetted. He works with set decorators to place practicals.

**Use it:** Put a visible lamp, screen or window in the frame before leaning on low-key light.
