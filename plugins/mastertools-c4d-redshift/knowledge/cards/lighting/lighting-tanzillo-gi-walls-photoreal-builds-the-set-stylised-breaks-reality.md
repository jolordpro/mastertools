Mike Tanzillo SORLKsHaHSM [20:58-25:39]: for photoreal, build the real space, HDRI and lights; closed pink walls will flood a room pink, as in reality. Stylised work that needs to break reality (warmer characters) should leave the wall out. The skill is knowing what the renderer is doing: a student's front faces were bright because a wall behind the characters bounced; paint it black or remove it. GI is secondary bounce; no silver bullet. Light can also bounce off skin.

**Use it:** Before adding fills, check which walls bounce into the subject.
