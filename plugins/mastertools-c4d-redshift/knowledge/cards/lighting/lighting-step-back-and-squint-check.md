[43:41] A working art director steps back and squints between tweaks: it resets the eye and blurs detail so only value shapes and silhouettes remain; is this an interesting blob of pixels regardless of subject?
A coach equivalent is a blurred or downsized thumbnail beside the frame.

**Use it:** the platform's draft review can show a blurred thumbnail with the contrast read.
Transcript support: Tanzillo has artists look at their Discord post at thumbnail size and step back or zoom out, because zoomed in they lose the whole composition; Volkov (RCCaQmwxNB4 [25:21]) squints like painters stepping back (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TdTxV_ACVhU [16:35])
