With no set and no water sim, early Nemo shots were fish swimming in empty air, so she broke down what makes water look like water by snorkelling and watching pool-bottom caustics [10:24-10:59].
She loved the chromatic aberration in the caustics and exaggerated it for style, aiming for believability and appeal rather than realism; same with light refracting through fog [10:59-11:34].

**Use it:** Reference real phenomena first, then push the signature element (colour fringing, caustic edges) to taste.
