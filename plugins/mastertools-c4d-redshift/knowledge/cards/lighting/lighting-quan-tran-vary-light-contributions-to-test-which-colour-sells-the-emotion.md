Quan Tran [27:11-30:19]: on a purple-and-green character lit evil, he asked for an experiment: choose two emotions, keep all lights, and change their intensities (and positions) so purple dominates and green less. Green is a classic monster or villain light and warm orange is a hero one; the exercise shows how much each colour's share changes the read.

**Use it:** A coaching exercise for colour-mood choices with a fixed rig.
