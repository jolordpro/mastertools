Vistek, light meter demo [01:17-06:29]. Light values double per stop, so the ratio is 2 to the power of the stop difference: 1 stop 2:1, 2 stops 4:1, 3 stops 8:1, 4 stops 16:1, 5 stops 32:1; a gaffer asked for 8:1 fill knows it is three stops under the key. Meter the key side with the dome pointing at the key and not shielded, so fill wrap is included in the key reading; meter the fill side with the dome retracted or cupped to keep the key off. Demo at 24 fps, ISO 800, 180 degree shutter: key f/11, 1:1 reads the same on both sides, then fill f/8 (2:1), f/5.6 (4:1), f/4 (8:1), f/2.8 (16:1, needed negative fill because bounce kept the light from dropping), f/2 (32:1). The presenter insists on this standard form so a team means the same thing; he rarely works beyond 32:1 on a face.

**Use it:** Gives the ratio check an exact reading method (key side includes fill, fill side shielded) and the stop table; agrees with the existing stop card.
