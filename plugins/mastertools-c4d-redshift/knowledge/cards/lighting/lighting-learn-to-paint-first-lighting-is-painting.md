Francesco Giroldini (lead lighting/comp artist at DreamWorks, ex-Pixar and Blue Sky; Academy of Animated Art interview) says painting is the base skill: if you can take a shot into Photoshop and make it look as wanted, the rest is achieving it in software [10:59-11:32].
The most common difficulty he sees in others is not knowing what it should look like [11:32-12:03].
He splits it: lighting is like painting and you can cheat the light, while compositing is like photography with limited data to push and pull before it breaks [12:34-13:05].

**Use it:** Coaching line: define the target image (reference or paint-over) before touching light setups.
