Feinberg [03:36-04:06]: as lighting director she sat in the theatres checking that colour on the monitor and screen was correct and "dialled in exactly". Lighting is the last creative step, so she is the eyes making sure the image is what the film wants.
She notes that on Coco the immersive sound (Atmos) arriving in theatres freed the sound designers to think about creative nuance instead of delivery mechanics; she was in the room asked whether the picture looked exactly right.
Her path: lighting then modelling, becoming lighting director on Wall-E, Brave and Coco and VFX supervisor on Turning Red; she returned to hands-on lighting for about 4 months on Inside Out 2 [01:33-02:04].
Transfer: sign off colour in the room and on the screen it will play on.

**Use it:** The final-pass checklist includes one review of colour and light on the target display, by the person who owns the last look.
