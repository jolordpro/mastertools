David Mullen recalls Douglas Slocombe overexposing the Raiders desert scenes to keep the feeling of heat, which also opened shadow detail, while Robert Richardson shot Four Feathers sand dunes with exposure set to silhouette the actors, helped by large negative-fill frames. His point: it all depends on the look you want [§2].

**Use it:** State the feeling first (heat, menace) and choose the exposure to serve it.
