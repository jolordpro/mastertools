Messerschmidt rated the camera lower than is typical in digital work: 640 ASA, with most of the show at a 2.5 stop, which he says gave a little shape and bite when lighting. The camera colour balance was set under 4,000 Kelvin for incandescent scenes. About 98 percent of the show used Leica Summilux lenses, mainly the 29mm and 40mm, chosen to keep one visual language. The kit mixed HMIs, incandescents and L-7 and L-5 Sky Panels, with cool-white fluorescents and sodium vapor for night exteriors. (Tools snapshot of a 2017 interview.)
