Light came in through windows and blinds so the source reads as real. In the ranch house, small tubes against a wall bounced off the ceiling gave subtle ambient glow, and haze gave a dusty feel. Modern scenes were softer, top-lit; flashbacks used harder, lower, more contrasty light. [§1-§2]

**Use it:** Pick one big window-like key direction, add a faint bounce for ambient, and use haze or atmosphere to give light a body.
