Guest: Erik Messerschmidt. For the election-night party the room moved through lots of coverage and the schedule gave no chance to relight each shot, so he and designer Don Burt built the 1934 sign and the results board, and the sign did about 90% of the work. A couple of Source Fours added shafts on the stage, a follow spot picked out the mayor, and the rest was practicals [49:07-52:43].
Table lamps had dimmable LEDs so they could be cued, and tables off camera could be turned off to keep the foreground dark. The gaffer brought four boxes of bulbs to test; they picked a pricey hand-blown period bulb [49:37-51:41].
Depth came from moving tables so each face was lit, plus a few second-floor lights for backlight where needed [52:13-53:14].
Transfers: in CG, place bright emissive set pieces in the shot that justify and carry the lighting before adding extra lights.

**Use it:** Plan a hero emissive element per scene so most shots light themselves; add helper lights only to fix faces.
