Common reads: 2:1 soft and flat, 4:1 natural dimension, 8:1 dramatic.
3:1 is exactly 1.58 stops; sources round it.
Writing differs: one source calls it (key + fill) : fill and credits the ASC; the ASC document was not found, so that credit is unverified.
In practice DPs often set ratios by eye, with false colour or a waveform as a check.
Merged from `lighting-jared-films-count-stops-for-ratio-and-window-rule`: Jared (working gaffer, medium). Formula key plus fill over fill, but in practice count stops [02:42-03:13]. Uses: communicating in numbers, recreating a look on a later scene, working out gear. Rule: with a window or other visible source in frame, keep the face's key two to three stops below the window to feel believable [03:13-03:43]. Demo [04:06-10:37]: 128:1 is seven stops (key f11 and fill at 7 on the meter); 8:1 three stops (key about f11, fill f4 range); each ratio step is a stop of fill added, wrapped from the key side so it reads as one source; fill source ends near above camera. High ratios need a hard source at about 80 degrees so only one side of the face is lit; at a 45-degree wrap the achievable ratio range narrows, and 1:1 is only possible with frontal light. Fill added also raises the key side a little, so recheck the meter after every change. Tools: light meter gives numbers instantly, false colour is harder to read for ratios [10:06]; separate key and fill sources let him move 8:1 to 4:1 fast [10:37].

**Use it:** print the ratio definition next to every number (key:fill or key+fill:fill) and quote it in stops.
Transcript support: Judge ratios between lights, not absolute values; exposure rescales. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kN00Z7UsW5U [10:26])
Transcript support: Tran gives fill notes in stops: three-quarters to one stop down for fill, half to a quarter stop for a night background (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ETgt9YXSaeg [11:39])
Transcript support: metered example: background one stop over key is 2:1; 1:1 less dramatic; 5:1 severe key to fill versus about 3:1 pleasing (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7UG1aBlH1lo [03:08])
Transcript support: McNally: 1:1 equal power, 2:1 main twice the fill; ratios are language, aim for emotion (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yALctlSEakE [00:40])
