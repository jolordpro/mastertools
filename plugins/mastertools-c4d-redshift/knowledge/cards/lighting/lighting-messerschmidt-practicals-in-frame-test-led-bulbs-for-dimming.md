For a bar set, the team lit almost entirely with practicals: bulbs, neon and signs in frame. They tested LED bulbs, were surprised by the colour and dimming of one 25 W model (the speaker thinks Westinghouse, 2018), and had the art department buy hundreds [52:20-53:52, 57:59-59:03]. A tin ceiling was pressed plastic for budget, so a hot lamp could not go near it [53:52].
They also worked with the set dresser on height: the dresser was always next to the monitor and moved practicals by inches [51:14-51:46, 55:26-56:27].

**Use it:** Put practical sources in frame as the key light where possible and ask what the dimmer range is before relying on them.
