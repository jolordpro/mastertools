Chad: pick a gobo that matches what is visible, such as a window, not tree dapple with no tree anywhere near. Without a motivated source the light feels off and viewers do not know where it comes from [09:22-10:23].
For an outdoor look he blows the highlights slightly through a very bright light and camera exposure or f-stop, with the new ACES workflow in Redshift [10:57-11:28]. Tint and gamma via a colour-correct node are possible in the graph method [10:23].

**Use it:** Add the visible source (window, branch) to the frame or camera, or change the gobo.
