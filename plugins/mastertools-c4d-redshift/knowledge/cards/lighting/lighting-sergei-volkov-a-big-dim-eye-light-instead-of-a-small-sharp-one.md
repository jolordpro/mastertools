Sergei Volkov [03:56-08:52]: audiences connect through the eyes, and the dings were missing or small and sharp. A small light catches in some frames and not others, a chase. Use a bigger light (even larger than the board in the room, rectangular) with lower intensity; it will almost always show in the eye as it moves, and fits a dim room with large reflective surfaces. He suggests a separate eye-spec light and asks whether the student adds dings in 2D [07:15]; a Blue Sky reference at [08:52] is garbled in the captions.

**Use it:** Move to a large, dim eye light when eye movement makes a small ding flicker.
