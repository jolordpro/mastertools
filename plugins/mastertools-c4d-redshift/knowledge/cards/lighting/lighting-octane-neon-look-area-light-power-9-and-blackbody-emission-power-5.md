Grace Casas (beginner channel). Two area lights aimed at the centre of interest, one pink and one blue, because a warm and cool pair is the staple of the aesthetic and vaporwave look [00:35-01:35]. Her go-to area light power is 9, with colour from an RGB spectrum texture in the light's texture and distribution slots, saturated a little [01:35-02:07]. For big scenes, make the light bigger so it reaches more areas [02:07-02:37]. Neon is luminous geometry: a diffuse material with blackbody emission, surface brightness power about 5 (the default was far too bright), colour again from an RGB spectrum, dragged onto the neon geometry [02:37-04:18]. Raw render saved as TIFF [05:52]. These numbers belong to Octane's scale and her scene, not to Redshift.

**Use it:** Starting values for an Octane neon scene. For Redshift, rebuild the idea (small coloured lights plus emissive geometry) and tune by eye.
