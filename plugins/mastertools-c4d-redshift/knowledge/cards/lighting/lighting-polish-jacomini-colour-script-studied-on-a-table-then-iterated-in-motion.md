For choreographed scenes with many transitions, he names "We Don't Talk About Bruno" in Encanto as the example, he says studies and a colour script help most [10:14-10:46]. The script is a storyboard with colour, worked out on a table with cards and pens by production designers, the DP, the lighting supervisor and others, not one person [11:16-11:48]. Danger of enriching: you lose the story and the emotion, so it is additions and subtractions [11:48-12:27]. It is iterated, because ideas that work on paper must be adjusted once they move in animation [12:27].

**Use it:** Do the colour/lighting plan per beat before building; then re-check in motion at speed and expect to change it.
