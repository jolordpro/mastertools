Larger source puts out more light; farther away it wraps the form and softens shadow edges [49:46-50:18]. Smaller, closer light = high contrast, vivid hard shadows. Trainer says it "depends" and points to photography courses as a reference [50:49-51:20].

**Use it:** the coach uses this when a shadow is too hard or too soft: change size and distance together, then re-tune intensity.
