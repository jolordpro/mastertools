Fraser [04:16-05:50] says Villeneuve wanted the light to feel natural, and that this is partly why he was hired. Fraser is "precious" about anything that feels lit or manufactured and gets anxious walking onto a stage where film lights are obvious. On Dune they used real sun for the most part and many light shapers to create shadow; the exception was an atomic explosion where powerful lamps (he says "vortexes", as captioned) were the only source that could beat the sun.

**Use it:** for the lighting check in a CG scene, ask what the light source in the world is and whether the shaping (flags, shadow shapes) is something that source could really do.
