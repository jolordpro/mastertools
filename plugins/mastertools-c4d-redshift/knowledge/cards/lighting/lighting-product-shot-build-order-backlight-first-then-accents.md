The creator (a motion designer answering Instagram questions) builds in this order [01:38-06:23]: backlight (rect light 25 by 25 scaled and stretched, strength 10) giving contrast and a silhouetty read; front light at half the backlight strength with spread 0.5 as a small sliver on the toe; heel light from behind and beside at strength 2 to create micro shadows that show surface texture; bottom light at strength 1 to stop the underside fading into the background; finally lift the front light for a top highlight. Reason: a dramatic base makes it easier to light up details one at a time, and the aim is texture visible in the style frame sent to the client [02:43, 04:50].
The numbers are scene-scale dependent (he calls the scene small), so use ratios: front about 0.5, heel about 0.2, bottom about 0.1 of backlight.

**Use it:** the advisor can offer this accent-by-accent order as a default product look; the ratios (not the numbers) are the takeaway. Redshift setting values to be checked against the ledger.
