Trainer demo [46:07-46:38]: key straight on = flat image; 45 degrees plus a dome adds depth; behind the subject = dramatic. Build key, fill, rim from duplicates, switching lights off one at a time to see what each does [47:10-47:41].
Fewer, more thoughtful lights are easier to control than many [48:11]. Key and dome should cast shadows in the same direction [55:39].

**Use it:** the lighting checks flag key light within a few degrees of the camera axis; the coach uses the three angles as vocabulary.
