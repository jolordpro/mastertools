Tanzillo on a winning challenge entry [08:59-10:33]: each coloured source looked good alone, but three saturated ones (fire warmth, blue window, bluish-green glow on the mantle) together made everything saturated and hid the focus. If the fireplace is the focus, tone the window and fire a little. If the mood is ambience, consider turning off the glowing element. One saturated colour looks great; three compete. He also says he is not sure what story the lighting serves [10:02].

**Use it:** flag any frame with more than one strongly saturated light; ask which one is the story and lower the rest.
