Fraser says shooting a sunset in the Volume can run 12 hours [§1]. Deakins notes the advantage: he shot a twilight scene in No Country For Old Men over 7 to 8 days, which a Volume could do in one [§1]. Fraser adds that when the script calls for last light, location coverage has to avoid the hillside to make the schedule, but in the Volume every angle is perfect; he calls it another tool in the toolbox, better than bluescreen [§2].

**Use it:** Use the 12-hour sunset versus a week of dusk windows as the schedule argument for a screen-lit stage.
