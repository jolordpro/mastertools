She says expensive shots include crowds of characters, hair, and water or any surface where light penetrates and bounces many times (the Good Dinosaur river), since iterations take longer and use more people time [11:34-12:40].
They call these money shots because they are technically, creatively or computationally difficult up front; it is a conversation with the director about what matters most and where to spend [12:40-13:14].

**Use it:** Estimator input: flag hair, volumes, refraction, many-bounce surfaces per shot, and show the director the cost choices.
