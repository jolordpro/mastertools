A 'bubble' is one lamp of a Maxi Brute or Dino (24-light and 36-light Dinos, 9 and 12-light Maxis) [07:23-07:54]. Each 1K par comes very narrow, narrow, medium or wide at roughly 10, 20, 60 and 90 degrees (his round figures) [07:54-08:25].
He asks for 'turn off six bubbles' to dim in steps; six bubbles is one bank, a breaker flick [08:25-09:29]. On a Maxi the banks can be fanned out; he starts straight and pans a bank to catch a tree and backlight it [09:29-10:00].

**Use it:** When writing a lighting brief for CG, give beam spread in degrees and the number of lamps (or area-light size), the same way a gaffer would.
