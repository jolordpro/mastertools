A flag beside the key (a sider) limits spill on the background [25:03]. The farther the flag is from the light, the cleaner the cut, since fewer oblique rays reach the shaded area [25:03-25:34]. With diffusion, place the flag between the diffusion frame and the subject, not between the lamp and the diffusion; the cut is cleaner and the softness and level are less affected [25:34].

**Use it:** in CG, mimic this with light linking or a cutter/gobo object placed away from the area light, keeping its edge soft by distance.
