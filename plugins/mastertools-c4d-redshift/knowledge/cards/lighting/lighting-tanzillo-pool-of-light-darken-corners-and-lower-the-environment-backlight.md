Mike Tanzillo sCDCs5At8uY [10:36-13:26]: the character was lit well by a frontal light, but a second backlight added too much to the environment. Reference shows a pool of light with dark corners. Darken the corners and take the backlight down on the environment, not on him; the contrast of the shot rises. Darkening raises saturation, so pull it back a little [12:46]. Depth of field behind a small character works.
The first step in his own crit is making sure the skin and material read right [09:34].

**Use it:** Darken the room not the hero: environment light off first.
