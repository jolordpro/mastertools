A button on the light adds a Target tag and a null; move the null and the light follows [22:20-23:20]. Dropping an object in the tag aims at its axis centre [23:56].

**Use it:** Give as the standard way to aim lights so moving them does not change aim.
Transcript support: Maxon trainer targets each area light at a null, moves the null to aim (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RpiCCLHjlWQ [39:17-40:19])
