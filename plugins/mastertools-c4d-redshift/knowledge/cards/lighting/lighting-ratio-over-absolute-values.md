Effectatron: solo each light, note what it does, then judge ratios between lights; absolute numbers (5 versus 100) are rescaled by camera exposure [D 10:26-10:57]. Brightness depends on distance, size, intensity, spread and any texture on the light, plus the material [21:21].
Test setup: a 5-intensity back light and 25-intensity key at similar distances; the point was the ratio. Play safe by going a bit darker than needed because more light is added later [25:29].

**Use it:** supports the existing ratio-in-stops cards; recommend saving ratios not intensity values when comparing setups.
