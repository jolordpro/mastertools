Roger Deakins at the SMU masterclass [53:26]: for the Las Vegas casino in Blade Runner 2049 the stage space and money did not exist, so he proposed no walls, blacking the whole stage and putting atmosphere (haze) in, then lighting it. It became the cheapest set in the film.
On Sicario [53:57] the van interior was a plywood box on a turntable with a light swung through the window to sell movement; he says a close study would show it is crude, but nobody looks because the scene grips them.
Limits become ideas: James Deakins adds that you can leave the unaffordable part of the set off screen and make the audience guess [52:56].
Transfer: in C4D + Redshift, a dark void with volume haze and one moving light sells a space for far less set-building than modelling the room.

**Use it:** The coach can suggest haze plus a moving key before extra environment modelling when a scene is short of time or assets.
