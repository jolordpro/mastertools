The colour journey is where he first sees a film; he dislikes gratuitous vivid colour [00:36-01:06]. Plan: Caladan and Giedi Prime muted grays; Arrakis interiors sandstone and darker; exteriors blown-out white skies and very desaturated, not vivid yellow, sands [00:36-01:06]. Part two sits mostly on Arrakis, so to avoid one palette throughout the film the southern sequence goes to black and darker sand [01:06-01:37]. He says the director commits to what is shot and does not ask for more colour later [01:37-02:07]. Sponsor video for a light maker.

**Use it:** Give each location in a long piece its own muted palette and avoid reusing one for the whole piece.
