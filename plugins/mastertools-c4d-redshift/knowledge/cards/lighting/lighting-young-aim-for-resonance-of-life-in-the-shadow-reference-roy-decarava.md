Young (DP of Arrival) names Roy DeCarava as his first point of departure, tree of life, and says every film he tries to achieve that resonance of life and energy in the shadow, like DeCarava's Coltrane images [13:09-14:51]. He links DeCarava, Harris Savides and Gordon Willis: arrive at a moment in human life, photograph it with little interruption from the artist [14:51-15:59].
He also names painter Chris Ofili: perspective is part of the work, you must be at the right angle to see the detail in the painting [15:59-16:36].
Atmosphere is his goal: film cannot be touched, so the image must create vibe, feel like a remembered smell or a room's heat [10:03-12:36].
