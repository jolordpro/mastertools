The poster (not Deakins) reports that close bounces of muslin or sheets felt unnatural and made talent squint, that big reflector frames (4x4 m and 2x2 m with 2x1 polys) wrapped side light decently, and that black cloth under subjects limits unwanted under-chin light [§1]. Another forum reply suggests big sources far away, with fill kept to what feels natural, and warns that nearby white walls or distant glass reflections can look unnatural even when real [§2].

**Use it:** Treat as community suggestions to test, not Deakins's rules; compare against his stated point that blocking and time of day come first.
