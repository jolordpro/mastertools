Once nets and lights are set perfectly, you never moved the lift because it never returned to the same spot. Now most control is from the ground [11:03-11:35].
He ran four 2400-class units with 45 and 60 degree reflectors on a dimmer board and swept colour temperature from about 2800 to 6500 K, with a green shift if wanted, plus levels, all remotely [11:35-12:07]. Wind means bringing a person down and losing time [12:07].
(The caption says '2400bs'; unit type not certain.)

**Use it:** The same idea applies to a CG light rig: expose temperature and intensity per light in one place and save states, rather than hand-moving lights per shot.
