Basic concept: three sides [01:26-01:56]. Start with highlights: area light behind the product with moderate intensity [02:28]. Sharp light on the side opposite where the product faces: duplicate the back light, set the 3D cursor to the product centre so it rotates around it, lower intensity and spread to sharpen [02:58-03:29]. Third light is a duplicate rotated 180 degrees with higher spread [03:29]. Area lights give harsh reflections on glossy surfaces, so he uses a reflection image texture [03:59-04:30]. For glass use emission planes with a gradient (ramp to spline, bright side to the back) and hide them from camera ray visibility [05:04-06:09]. Light linking keeps the lights from wrecking the ground reflections [06:41]. Spread width controls hard versus soft shadows.

**Use it:** A teaching pattern for the coach: three roles (rim, hard shadow, soft fill) before fine tuning. Related: lookdev-glass-gradient-emission-backdrop-renders-faster.
