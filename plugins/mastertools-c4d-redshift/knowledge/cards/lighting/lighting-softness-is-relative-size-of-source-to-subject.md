A big source far away is hard (the sun); a small source on a tiny subject can look soft [10:26-10:58]. Diffusion softens only because it makes the source bigger as seen from the subject [11:33]. Hard light has little or no penumbra; soft light has a big penumbra with little umbra [12:09-12:44].
To soften: diffuse, move the source closer, and fill the diffusion frame evenly. To harden: move it away or spot the unit [12:44-13:47]. When extending a visible source (window, lamp) with added light, soft is safer than hard [13:47].

**Use it:** in C4D/Redshift raise the area-light size or move it closer for softer shadows; the advisor should explain shadow softness through relative size.
