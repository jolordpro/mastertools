Blender Guru (2019) [01:13-07:19]. Rim light carves objects from each other and the background (two overlapping animals in a render read as two only because of the back light). Overuse looks staged: a film scene with every character perfectly outlined reads as carefully crafted, while a comparison shot with her hair vanishing into the background feels more natural. A single bedside-lamp scene can let the head disappear if that suits the story. Separation needs value contrast, not only a rim: a car shot used the rear window behind the face. Decide per shot what the story needs.

**Use it:** Use as a note when a render has rim light on every element: ask whether each separation is motivated, and whether value or camera placement could do it.
