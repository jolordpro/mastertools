Setup one mixed a tungsten-colour foreground light with daylight lights, and the camera tint was shifted green to match the green of the next setup [04:08-04:39]. Setup two: double green party gel on a daylight unit with barn doors closed down so it hits only the seamless behind the singer; the gel was light, so the lamp was kept low or the green would look "almost neon" [04:39-05:39].
Key kept to the side and warm, the talent angled to it while looking at camera, fill from a white bounce also supplying an eye catch light; orange and green is less common than orange and blue, so it reads as more unique [05:39-06:41].
Low camera on 50 mm anamorphic compressed the background so stacked chairs looked like more [01:03].
