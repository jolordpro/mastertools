Gaffer Danny Gonzalez describes a curved dome built from a Maya design: three rows of LED ribbon at top and bottom, under diffusion, shooting toward the centre, tested first with a quick photo of one panel [12:44-14:21]. Green was first choice, but so many plates had green foliage that they added a blue set of ribbon and shot almost everything on blue [14:21-14:52].
The wall was usually dimmed to about 30 percent and never needed more [14:52-15:53]. Falloff radius and distance were previsualised to minimise blue spill on the car, and the cast was surprised at the first result [16:24-16:55].
He warns the setup suited Fincher's locked shot design and may not suit others [17:27]. A full video wall needs content ready and synced, where green stays more forgiving in post [26:56-27:28].

**Use it:** Pick the key colour from the plate content, not habit, and plan spill radius and distance before building or rendering a key backdrop.
