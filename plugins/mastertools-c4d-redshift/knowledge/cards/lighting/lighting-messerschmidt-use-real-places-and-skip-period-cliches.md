Mann insisted on Italy; the home, the barber shop and the family mausoleum were real, which helped the performances and the tone [19:19-20:49].
He did not want a formal costume drama look. Real places, real cars and costume do most of the period work, and the obsession with 'period enough' (wet cobbles, top hats, smoke, formal dolly) is what makes films look fake [30:11-30:43].
The period matters as context, apart from historical accuracy; the focus is on what the audience should feel [31:14-31:44].

**Use it:** For historical or retro looks, find the real texture and let it carry the era, not a stack of effects.
