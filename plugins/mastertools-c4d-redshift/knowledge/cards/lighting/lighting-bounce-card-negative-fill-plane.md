Place a plane out of frame with a white diffuse material to add bounce, or black to absorb light [19:45-21:16]. Material colour sets the strength [20:45]. He says there is no extra light to balance, no stray reflection, and no real performance cost; it can also tint a scene. Learned from Dustin Valkema [18:42].

**Use it:** Suggest as a cheap way to lift or deepen a side before adding lights; measure render time rather than trusting the claim.
