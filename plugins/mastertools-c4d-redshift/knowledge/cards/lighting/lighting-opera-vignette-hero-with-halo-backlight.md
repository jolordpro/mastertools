Thorup's art-history lead-in [02:44]: stage designers focus the eye by darkening the surround (a vignette), lighting the hero in the middle, adding a front fill so action is readable, and a large back light that makes a halo through the figure so she reads as a goddess or angel [03:18] [03:49].
His point is that even lighting would break this scene because the hero would no longer be the centre.
Rembrandt is cited for a 45 degree key that leaves a triangle on the shadow side [01:41] [02:14].

**Use it:** Use as a teaching pattern for focus: darken the surround before brightening the hero. Cite it when a broadcast hero frame has no clear single focal pool of light.
