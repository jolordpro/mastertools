Guest: Roger Deakins. Order of thinking: the scene in the script, then the space with the director and production designer, then blocking, then where windows and sources are, and whether a location offers a usable trick. All must fit the mood and be worked in unison [00:04-01:06].
In a motel-room scene the character is lit by a lamp that falls off a table; the team worked out how it would fall and what hard pattern it would throw on the person on the bed. The bathroom beyond, with a body, was bright cold fluorescent, built into the set [01:06-02:08].

**Use it:** Decide the light sources, their motion and their hardness while blocking, so lighting is part of the set, not added afterwards.
