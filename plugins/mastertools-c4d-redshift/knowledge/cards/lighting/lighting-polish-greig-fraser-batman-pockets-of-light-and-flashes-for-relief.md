Each dark scene had a brighter pocket (a diner is bright, but not garishly). In the club, set up effectively as a real one with an events company and the gaffer, the lights flash as Batman comes down the stairs and the blinders flash behind him as he lands the first hit, so the place lights up and eyes are not tired by constant dark. Timing and flash work in the Falcone scene was helped by an economical shot structure. [§2-§3]

**Use it:** On a night-time board, plan one bright accent and its timing, not an even dim wash.
