He likes a scene's look to be justified by a window or practical shown somewhere in the scene, and light that feels real even when stylised, as with the soft orange overhead sky in Blade Runner [02:58-03:29].
Complaints: a blue backlight with no source, fill that creates a second shadow (a punched-in light plus sun), and the old blue arc exteriors. Not that they are wrong, but unjustified [04:12-05:12].
His goal: cinematography should dissolve into the film and never stand out [05:12].

**Use it:** In a CG light check, ask for each light 'where is it coming from' and remove double shadows.
