Guest: Roger Deakins. The only fixtures in the building were ugly mercury ones, so he had the set department make about 50 large half-circle conical lights where he could fit his own bulbs; he put 2K blondes inside each. The wide shots showed the whole space and a crowd, and he wanted a graphic pattern, so he needed that count of lights [03:10-04:02].

**Use it:** When a wide needs a visible light pattern, make the number of sources part of the design instead of hiding them.
