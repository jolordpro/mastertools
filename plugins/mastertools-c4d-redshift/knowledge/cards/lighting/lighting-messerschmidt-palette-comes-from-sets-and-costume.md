Messerschmidt says the palette is the director's and that the production designer and costume designer carried much of it. His own contributions were deliberate source choices: cool-white fluorescents, sodium vapor for night exteriors, incandescent at camera balance under 4,000K. He calls himself the last link before colour correction. [§5]

**Use it:** When a palette is wrong, check surfaces and wardrobe before regrading.
