Reeves does not shoot coverage for its own sake; he knows the close-up and the gun-firing shot he is there for. Fewer cuts mean the crew can work hard to get the timing, the flash and the sync of that one shot right [§3].

**Use it:** Plan the single hero frame of a shot and spend lighting time on it; skip coverage nobody will use.
