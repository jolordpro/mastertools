White begins by asking how lighting can support the story: what to say about characters, environment and themes, and how the whole film should look [viewconference §1]. She also watches for what will be hard for lighting, such as a complicated character design, a sequence, or new technology, so those things are developed and explored before production [viewconference §1]. On Cars 3 the risks were reflective characters, a quick schedule and new technology that changed the approach from earlier Cars films [viewconference §2].

**Use it:** At project start, ask for a short list of the three hardest looks and test them first.
