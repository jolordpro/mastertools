[02:32]: plus/minus buttons step exposure a quarter stop; Shift makes it a half stop; right-click resets to 0. Temperature presets (halogen, candle, blue sky) switch every selected light at once. Spread and cone have the same nudges.
[01:31] Solo can combine several lights; LT (look through) aims a light then returns. [03:17] Lights can be grouped by layer such as exterior and interior.

**Use it:** use quarter-stop and half-stop as the step sizes in the platform's light-match controls.
