Spread at minimum gave harsh shadows and lost the rim [D 23:56]; raising it back, intensity went to about 1 to keep the same look [24:28]. Personal rule: key lights with lower spread (sharper), fill lights higher (softer) [29:11].
Spread at 1 diffuses away any gobo texture [27:04].

**Use it:** the coach gives this as a starting habit, not a rule; use before changing both size and intensity.
