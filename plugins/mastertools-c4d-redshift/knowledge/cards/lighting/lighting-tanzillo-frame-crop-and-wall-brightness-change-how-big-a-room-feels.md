Tanzillo [55:08-58:17]: the wallpaper in the background was too white; make it darker. Wallpaper that gets smaller with distance can make the room look longer; consider a wall in frame. If the story is a small, cosy bedroom, frame size is a way of compacting the space. A square crop of the shot also tightened it.

**Use it:** when a room feels too big for the story, change frame size and the brightness of far surfaces before remodelling.
