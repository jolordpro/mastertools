At a location, he and Fincher first stand and look toward the background, then place actors in front of it [57:29]. The lighting method: see what the practicals give, turn off everything behind the camera, and add the bare minimum [57:29-57:59]. All light is judged from a motivated standpoint [57:29].

**Use it:** Start a CG relight from the practicals and the scene's own sources, then add one light at a time only when the frame needs it.
