Richard Sanchez [07:47-10:33]: the chrome ball shows where lights sit so a 3D artist can rebuild the lighting; the gray ball shows shadow falloff against light position. Strictly, shoot both whenever the camera moves or the light setup changes, but crews often reuse balls from another setup, which he calls imperfect. Lens grids go over early, one per lens and per serial number (three 50mm lenses = three grids), because no two pieces of glass match. One show needed 80 to 90 of them.

**Use it:** when matching CG into a plate, ask for the chrome/gray ball for that setup and the lens grid; transfers to compositing C4D over shot footage.
