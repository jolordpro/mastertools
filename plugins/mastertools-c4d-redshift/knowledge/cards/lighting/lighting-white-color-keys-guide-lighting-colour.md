White directs the master lighter from the colour keys the production designer and she made and showed the director, then makes small shifts warmer, cooler or in colour, using the keys as the guide [§2]. From A Bug's Life she remembers saturated sunrise lighting on the grasshoppers cut against grey, fogged Ant Island shots, making the island feel even greyer and more anxious; her lesson was that it isn't about the individual shot but how shots cut together [§4].

**Use it:** Review colour in cut order; a saturated shot makes its neighbour look dull.
