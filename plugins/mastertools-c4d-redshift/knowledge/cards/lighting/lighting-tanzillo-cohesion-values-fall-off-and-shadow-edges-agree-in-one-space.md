Mike Tanzillo 55eY1-LlY3k [52:31-55:04] praising a reference and noting what to strive for: look down a mushroom stem, brighter and more saturated at the top and falling off below, with soft shadows and back plate blending seamlessly. In the student image the values at the top and bottom were similar, some shadows were darker than those near the foreground, and a hard shadow edge on the face sat beside soft ones.
The check is world cohesion, not just colour and value.

**Use it:** Check falloff direction, shadow depth and edge softness across the whole frame.
