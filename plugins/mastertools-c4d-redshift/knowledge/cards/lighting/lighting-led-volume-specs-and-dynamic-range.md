Figures as stated by the host (2025): 86 panels, 192x192 px each, 31 m x 6.5 m, about 30 megapixels, 206 m2; a cabinet 50 cm square is 256x256 px, 1200 nits peak, 8.3 kg, four cabinets per square metre [10:31-14:51]. Power 25 kW black, 80 kW full, about 50 kW average [11:07].
Panels are calibrated once a year; native white point 6000 K; changing it lowers primary purity [28:33-29:04]. Dynamic range is limited at the dark end, giving about 13 stops, similar to a photo wall or painting [29:04-29:34].
At glancing viewing angles colours wash out or tint and seams show [12:42-13:13].

**Use it:** Budget exposure for LED backdrops in sports studio sets; do not push past about 13 stops.
