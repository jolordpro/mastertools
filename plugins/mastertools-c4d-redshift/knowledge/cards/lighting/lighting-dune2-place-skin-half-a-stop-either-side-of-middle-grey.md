In an EL Zone analysis (system by cinematographer Ed Lachman, per the video) of a Dune Part Two frame, the key side of the face is never above half a stop under middle grey and the edge light is half a stop over, the brightest element [03:05-04:41]. The reviewer's guide: darker skin sits half to one stop under, lighter skin half to one stop over [05:11].
His rule: do not aim at exact 18 percent grey; use middle grey as the base for where other exposures land, because a low-light shot exposed to exact middle grey would be flat [03:38-04:09]. Each camera also places middle grey at its own level, so know your sensor [05:11-05:43].
The edge light is also cooler than the warm face light, so it pops [04:41].
Outside analyst, not Fraser; numbers are his readings, low confidence.

**Use it:** The false-colour check treats skin bands as a range (plus or minus one stop) rather than hitting a single grey value.
