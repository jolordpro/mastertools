CineFade syncs the lens iris with a motorised polarizer, so a shot can start with the whole room sharp and end with only one person sharp while exposure stays level, a subtle effect [§3]. For the Welles hospital scene he set the lens on a swing/tilt system and shifted the focal plane so only the eye was sharp as the actor leaned in [§3].

**Use it:** To guide the eye in black and white, change what is sharp mid-shot instead of cutting.
