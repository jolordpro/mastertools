He tells people to go to a hardware store, look at the available bulbs, and set up a practical lab: take the shade off, use a bare bulb, try different diffusion and materials in front, and move it around [06:39-07:10]. Look at the quality of light on a face or an object, bounce it, black one side of the bulb and bounce off materials [07:10-07:42]. The only way to learn lighting is experiencing light and life [07:42].

**Use it:** Run the same experiment in Redshift: one small light, then vary size, distance, diffusion and bounce on one object.
