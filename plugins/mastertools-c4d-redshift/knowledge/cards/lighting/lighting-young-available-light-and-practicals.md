The CBC writer describes his visual signature as frequent use of available light, relying on practicals. Photographers he cites as inspiration include Roy DeCarava and Gordon Parks. This is the writer's characterisation, not a Young quote, and it fits the underexposed, richly toned skin the writer saw. [§1]

**Use it:** Anchor night scenes on a visible lamp or window and let the rest fall off.
