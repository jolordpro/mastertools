With a Pro-Mist, black Pro-Mist, Black Satin, Black Magic or Pearlescent, a bulb in frame gets a halo and the viewer notices the diffusion [20:00-21:03]. He saves them for flashbacks, dream sequences and period vibe, and has used Hollywood Black Magic and Glimmer Glass the same way [21:03-21:36].
For a normal scene he uses digital diffusion, which acts like a window around a clipping highlight in the grade: it defocuses the highlight so the clip does not look electronic [23:46-24:17].

**Use it:** Treat bloom or halation in post as a story decision: add visible glow for memory or dream; keep a restrained highlight roll-off for real-time scenes.
