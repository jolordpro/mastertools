Quan Tran [33:06-37:51] on a ball character studying a film frame: the purple key, pink rim and blue hat rim were right but the dark core on the face was off. Swing the rim further round to widen it, shift the dark core closer to the centre so the purple key contrasts with it more, add spec and subsurface because the film shows roughness-map variation on the face (nose shinier than under the eyes). Praise first for the dark bits.

**Use it:** When matching a still, shift the core shadow and rim width before changing colours.
