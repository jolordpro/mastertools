A short student talk. He describes lighting as the basis of how CG images look: it must show the hour of day, interior or exterior, artificial or natural, but the most important thing is using light to emphasise the emotional side of the scene, as a film score sits behind and underlines the storytelling [04:07][04:43]. Background: Milan-trained computer scientist, PhD, moved to Los Angeles in 1994, worked at Rhythm and Hues, then Disney from 1998; lighting supervisor on Chicken Little, Bolt, Tangled, Frozen and Big Hero 6 [00:36][01:44][02:15].
