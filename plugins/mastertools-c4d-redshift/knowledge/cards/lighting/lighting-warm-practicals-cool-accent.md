[22:26] The demo tried 3500 K as too warm, then a second value the captions garble (heard as 48 Kelvin, unclear); the hosts say indoor bulbs should be 4000 K or lower and joke about 2700. The 2700-3500 range here is an approximation.
[46:17] Make the light feel motivated: lower the spread so the wall falloff is not a straight line.
[47:20] Add a very small, saturated, cool area light up on the wall at low intensity, as if from a sign or window, to offset the warm lamp. Changing the wall colour later made the accent clash, so retune it.

**Use it:** use as a colour-contrast check: one warm key, one weak cool accent.
