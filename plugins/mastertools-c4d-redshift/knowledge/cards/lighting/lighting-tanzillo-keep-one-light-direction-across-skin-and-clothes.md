Mike Tanzillo sjiuEeAp-JE [12:31-14:03]: the face and arm were side-lit but the shirt looked frontal with dark edges, because separate shirt lights from another angle were linked to it. He suggests moving the shirt light round to the same side so the chest gets the same shaping.
Second case 55eY1-LlY3k [29:38-30:09]: when an arm goes almost white and the shirt barely gets light, the balance is off. Materials differ so they need not match, but one near white and the other near black is a red flag.

**Use it:** Use as a squint test: compare skin and cloth exposure and light side before polishing either.
