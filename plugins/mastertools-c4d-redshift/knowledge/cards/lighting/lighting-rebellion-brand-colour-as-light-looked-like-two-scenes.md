Client direction was thin, so each villain's lair leaned on branding colours. Two of the three worked at once because the brand colour and set colours matched, so the first version was already close [09:58].
Emma's lair had warm and dark red set colours with a blue brand colour: a dim set lit by blue monitor light looked like two separate scenarios. They pushed the lighting to a cooler neutral and used the brand colour in the cutout graphic [10:28].
The game's in-engine references were bright and ambient because the roofs are removed for gameplay, so they were poor mood references [09:27].

**Use it:** when a brand colour fights the environment, move it to graphics or accents rather than forcing it into the main light.
