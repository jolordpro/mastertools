Quoting an ASC interview, the page says Young prefers finding ways to bounce light or use more subtle strokes of light. Once bounced light reaches the meter it is technically underexposed, but really the light is decreased so the richness of the skin tone increases [§1]. Soft light and underexposed skin tones recur from Pariah to Arrival [§1]. (A report that he tests how far under he can go comes from another source, not this page.)

Page spot: §1 (CBC).

**Use it:** For dark skin and dark materials, lower the light level before adding contrast; test the floor.
