Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour): the track could not be wetted down (a water truck is dry by the time it comes round), so spray and rooster tails were added digitally to dry footage [34:14-35:16].
A driver's note said the water was too much for cars on slicks; the team had to re-sim and redial everything to look real, since you cannot just fade it down in comp [35:16-36:17].
They let half the track be wetter than the other for the accident, to justify variation [36:17-36:48].

**Use it:** Review rule: expect domain-expert notes on physical plausibility; give sims a controllable amount parameter.
