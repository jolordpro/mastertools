Guest: Roger Deakins on 1917. The first bunker is lit by period oil lamps the prop department found in France. With nowhere to put another light, he put two bulbs in each lamp in line with the camera angle: the one facing the lens always dimmed down, the one facing away brighter [02:08-02:38].
He hopes it just looks like a lantern on a table, but it took research on how the bulbs fit in and rehearsal with a dimmer operator to sync it [02:38-03:10].

**Use it:** In CG, a lantern can carry a hidden second light that sits behind the visible glow, so face light never blows out the flame.
