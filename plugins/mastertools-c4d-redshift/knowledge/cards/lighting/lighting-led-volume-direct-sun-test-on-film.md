Jay Worth: the team pushed for direct sunlight on the volume for the airship set. They used 360 degree plates, placed the sun, opened panels and blasted a 10K through to get the real light without contaminating the background, working out the amount with the volume DP [18:46-19:50]. Result needed only panel cleanup, seam repair and a colour lift. Because they shoot on film, testing means wedging maybe 20 bracketed versions on colour intensity, science, brightness and spill, then judging in dailies [20:52-21:55]. He credits four LED projects of practice. They only use the volume when it gives final pixels. The tundra night set let fire as the main light, with the grade pushing blacks down so darkness is felt more than seen [04:12-04:43].

**Use it:** lighting rule of one dominant source with a controlled fill; brackets before committing. Mostly background for CG lighting in sports sets.
