Ryan Tudhope (production VFX supervisor on F1 The Movie, fxguide podcast; host Mike Seymour) says the cinematographer works with the natural light, including artificial lighting at night races, and avoids forcing it [14:02-15:07].
He says every artificial thing added (costume, lights, effects) accumulates, and not forcing the lighting helps a VFX supervisor [14:35-15:36].
Direction of shoot and time of day were chosen to maximise light [15:05].
Transfers: venue-lit sports looks are a grounding reference for matching CG.

**Use it:** Lighting principle: match the venue's existing light first, add fill only to serve the shot.
