He plans for the worst and for the one thing that is predictable, the sun's path [34:51-35:22]. Complex exteriors were shot across multiple days so all coverage matched the same sun position, with some blocking cheats [35:22]. He did not want to cheat light direction, following Gordon Willis in lighting once and moving the camera, so a scene may be backlit then front-lit [35:53-36:23]. They scouted for sun and backgrounds, and worked with the AD to move on when the sun left the position needed for remaining coverage [36:23-36:54].

**Use it:** In multi-shot CG sequences fix one light direction per scene and reuse the light rig, rather than relighting each shot to flatter it.
