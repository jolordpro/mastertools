Vistek [06:07-08:55]. With a 32:1 key to fill, the hair light was set about one stop under the key (f/8 against f/11), enough to accent without standing out; dark hair absorbs light, so blonde or sheen-covered heads need it tempered. Background: about a stop under key. Measure it by looking at the reflective brightness through the camera eyepiece or a false colour view, not with the incident meter; at f/4 it was three stops under, then brought to f/8. If the background equals the key it sits too near the skin tone and she melds into it. Spot-meter beside the camera. Result: 32:1 key to fill, 2:1 background to key, 2:1 hair to key. Stating ratios lets the team ask for a 2:1 backlight or a 16:1 background.

**Use it:** Starting values for a CG portrait or product-with-face set: hair and background one stop under key, fill several stops under; check the background by reflected brightness.
