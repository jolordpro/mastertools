The base package was a few 1200D, 600X and Nova lights plus MCs, expected to cover roughly 90% of the shoot, with the rule to carry as little as possible and expand as needed; most prep time went on scouting [04:08-05:12]. Colour-changing lights and cameras to 12,800 ISO solved what they put at 95-98% of problems, and they had to own their choices once made [03:38-04:08]. The method let the director operate and keep rolling; MCs were quick fixes for a splash of light [02:37-03:38]. Sponsor video by the light maker; treat vendor praise accordingly.

**Use it:** Plan a small default light rig per shot type and add lights only when a shot proves it needs them.
