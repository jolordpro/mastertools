Lights: area light with area shadow, inverse-square physical falloff, show in reflection; duplicated for front, top, back and sides; the top and back lights are smaller squares [01:45-04:47]. Left light intensity 120 with a blue (hue 200, sat 50, value 100); right light red [04:47-05:51].
Render: ambient occlusion on, maximum quality 128 samples, filter, 1080 output [07:22-07:52].
