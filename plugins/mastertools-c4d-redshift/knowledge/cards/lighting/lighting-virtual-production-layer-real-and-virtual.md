Nicholas's advice [43:21-46:24]: prefer a volume to green because talent and crew have something to react to, eyelines are right, and semi-transparency and wind-blown hair are easy. Layer shots with set, props, special effects such as fire, heat waves and smoke to fuse virtual and real [44:22]. In a spaceship airlock sequence the wall was only a distant backdrop while set and light did the work [44:52].
Choose a facility whose owner-operators know the system; empty-stage rentals are a risk [45:23].
Lessons from the 2-day Mars short: the sun and lens flares were added in post since a wall cannot show a sun [38:34]. Visor reflections were built by shooting a backdrop of backlit diffusion with the camera covered in black muslin [40:06].

**Use it:** Broadcast sets: add practical lights and physical elements to sell a virtual backdrop.
