With the HDRI as the only light he swapped environments while the room voted better or worse; a co-host joked each change cost another ten minutes of render [30:03-30:35]. He settled on option 29 unless something better appeared, and scaled the HDRI down [31:10-31:52, 32:55].
After many tries he said the composition was bad, so no light would rescue it; there is a ceiling to what lighting can do for weak composition [31:52-32:24].
Reflections and floating objects (the crabs) were then fixed in comp [32:24-32:55].
Low confidence: a loose live session.

**Use it:** If three different HDRI looks all feel wrong, stop swapping light and check composition and scale first.
