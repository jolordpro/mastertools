Fraser [05:50-07:23]: a long desert scene could not be lit at night (spaces are too big) and could not be shot in morning or evening light (it runs too long for the short window), so the script moved to an eclipse. He first asked the lab (PhotoKem) to film-out backwards, as if shooting through the back of a negative, for a dense red eerie look; that was not technically possible then, so they used an infrared filter that cut blues and greens. The same production used a digital capture plus film-out process.

**Use it:** supports the Giedi Prime infrared card; use a constraint on time-of-day as a reason to design a new light state rather than faking a magic hour.
