An eight-page interrogation outside a house, shot over several days in Pennsylvania summer, swung between rain, cloud, humidity and lightning every five minutes. Trees and low power lines ruled out big grip, so he used condors. At a dump the dolly could not enter, so two Chapman hydrascopes covered the scene and the show's rigid proscenium method held, with no switch to handheld. [§6-§7]

**Use it:** If the setup cannot hold the visual rules, change the tool, not the rules.
