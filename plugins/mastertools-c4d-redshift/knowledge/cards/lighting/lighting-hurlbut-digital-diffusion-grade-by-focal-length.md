Hurlbut's own scale for a digital diffusion filter [21:36-23:46]: 105 mm -> 2; 150 mm -> 1; 35 mm -> 4; 21 or 18 mm -> 5; 14 or 12 mm -> 6; 10 or 8 mm -> 7. Wider lens, heavier grade, because a wide lens looks through more of the glass; a tight lens uses a small area of it [22:39].
Adjust for the lens: sharper still-photo glass such as Sony G series goes one grade heavier; Leica, Caldwell Chameleon or Arri Signature primes go one lighter [23:12].
Effect: skin softens without showing pores, highlights bloom gently without a halo [23:46].

**Use it:** Match a 'soften skin' pass in post to the virtual lens: wider cameras need a stronger, tighter-radius softening than long lenses; keep the default light.
