Notes that recur across both sessions (counted by reading, not by his claim):
- **Spec or warmth on wet or oily parts:** tongue [31:16 T1], lips [42:10 T1], eyes and mouth interior [13:43 T2], books and metal hooks [08:00 T2].
- **Defocus extreme foreground objects** that are crisp and pull the eye [25:59 T1]; depth of field and motion blur too strong on a close hero [22:33 T2].
- **Pull back saturation** on leaves [05:17 T2], window light and fire [08:59 T2], cabinet handles that read strong gold [58:17 T1].
- **Push dark values into corners** to hold the eye in a band of the frame [06:25 T2, 25:38 T2].
- **Make the hero a touch brighter** with more key, then tone the set down [46:51 T1].
- **More shaping** where a form reads flat: ear, bunny body [47:22 T1].
- **Add depth with extra practicals further back:** a few glowing sources floating near the hero read odd; scatter more of them deeper in the set, out of focus [48:24 T1].
- **Fix unexplained edges:** a rim, line or shadow with no cause [48:55 T1, 59:50 T1].
- **Sharp edge of a dark shape looks chunky** (hair): soften it [40:06 T1].

**Use it:** use this list as the first-pass sweep before the full order of looks. Each item is cheap to test on a C4D still.
