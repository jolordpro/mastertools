Lighting actors inside dark suits is a classic problem; reality and film reality diverge, and at some point a face needs illumination that does not detract from the suit [07:46-08:17]. The lights were on little dimmers: those closer to camera dimmer, those farther brighter, giving contrast and shape on the face; the practicals blow out anyway so the brightness difference is not visible in them [08:17-08:48].

**Use it:** Fake a face light with small sources whose intensity falls with camera distance, and check that the practical itself still reads.
