Nora Shramek (lead lighting artist, games; host is Amit of Lighting Bot) says the two most important areas are real-world lighting and photography, and PBR [28:01-30:35]. Many lighters cannot say how lumen, lux and candela per square metre differ, though they are related by maths [28:32]. Know the PBR set: diffuse vs specular reflectance, subsurface scattering, what roughness simulates (she points to the Substance PBR guides, updated yearly [30:05]). A game that runs day to night may span roughly EV 5 to 16, so teams compress the exposure range with a curve system [29:03]. Goal: fiddle less, so a result takes a day or two rather than a week or two [30:35]. Host agrees few know it deeply [31:06].

**Use it:** coach lighting tips in units and causes (exposure, light units, roughness), not just slider moves.
