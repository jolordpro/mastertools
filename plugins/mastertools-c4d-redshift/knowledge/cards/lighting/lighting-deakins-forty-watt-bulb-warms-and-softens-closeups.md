Guest: Roger Deakins. Table practicals with light diffusion, set in the middle of a table, lit both characters well. For close-ups he wanted warmer and softer, so he put a plain 40-watt bulb on the table and dipped it way down. The actress was surprised at so little light, but he felt she looked fabulous [04:02-04:41].

**Use it:** For skin close-ups, a small low warm source near the table can beat a bigger one; try dimming a practical rather than adding a light.
