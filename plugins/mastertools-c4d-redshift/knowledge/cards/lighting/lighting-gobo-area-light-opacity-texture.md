Set Spread to 0 or very low or the pattern washes out [12:26]. Drop the grey texture into the Object tab opacity texture slot; black blocks, white passes, grey is partial [12:58-13:59]. A PNG sequence plays as animation in Simple, Loop or Ping-Pong mode [14:31]. Opacity reduces the effect; the texture can carry colour and the light can be rotated.

**Use it:** Use for window or foliage light patterns instead of geometry blockers.
Transcript support: Gobo textures on key/fill lights soften reflections on metal; a gradient works as well. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TonSU8Ljm5c [35:45-38:04])
