One location scene was expected to shoot on a sunny day and a cloudy day [48:54]. They tinted the windows and pushed light through them because editorial continuity mattered more than natural variation [48:54-49:24]. Where windows should blow out, as in a school, they used natural light in the available time window [49:24].

**Use it:** Lock exposure and window brightness per scene in CG too, so cuts between shots do not show changing daylight.
