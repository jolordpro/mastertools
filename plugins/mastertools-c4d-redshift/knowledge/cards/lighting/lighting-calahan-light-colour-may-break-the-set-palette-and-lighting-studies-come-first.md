Surface colours follow the plan; by the time lights are chosen it goes beyond that, with the rule set aside for what is right for the moment, a subjective call [14:10-14:40]. Light a dark, disturbing moment bright and colourful and the reaction differs from the expected cold grey: breaking expectation makes a stronger reaction [14:40-15:40]. Sometimes you must try things; the team does lighting studies as paintings ahead of the show [15:40].

**Use it:** Make a few quick colour studies of the key moment before lighting, and include one against expectation.
