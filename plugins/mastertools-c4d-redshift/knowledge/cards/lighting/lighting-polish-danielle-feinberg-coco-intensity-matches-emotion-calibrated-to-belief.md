Feinberg says it is easy to pull the audience out if you go too far and that visual overload is a balancing act; she laughs that the team is not sure they know when it happens, but you want to up the ante where needed to feel it emotionally [§2]. The article's writer contrasts a quiet song scene lit by hazy sunlight with the climax's vistas and crowds [§3]. Code from The Good Dinosaur's fireflies treats many lights as one, reused for the City of the Dead's street lamps, probably a million, which could not be rendered as separate lights [§3]. [§2-§3]

**Use it:** Scale light complexity to the emotional beat of the shot and watch for the point where it distracts.
