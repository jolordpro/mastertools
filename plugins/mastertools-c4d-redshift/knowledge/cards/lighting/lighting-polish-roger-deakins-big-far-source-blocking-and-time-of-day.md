In forum replies in March 2024 Roger Deakins says that apart from fall-off, a big source far away is similar to a small source close in, and that only one of the poster's reference frames had a bounce source wrapping light on the face, which he would not name [§2]. He calls blocking and time of day key: "Why is one character backlit and the other in full sun? That is a conscious choice" [§2]. For a river scene they shot in the morning so Josh would be backlit and fit the sequence timeline; position and composition made it three-quarter backlight, though he would have preferred the light on the river as a direct backlight [§3]. No ratios or distances were given.

**Use it:** Before adding lights to a CG scene, check whether camera angle, sun direction and subject placement already give the intended light.
