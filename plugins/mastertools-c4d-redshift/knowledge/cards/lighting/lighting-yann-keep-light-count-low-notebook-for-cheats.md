Yann Dupont on lighting outdoor shots for the film Gravity: one sun, and the Earth as a bounce card with global illumination doing the rest [48:57-49:28]. He has seen 30 lights used on one character, which breaks when anyone moves one [49:28-49:59]. One shot was 35,000 frames (about 12 minutes), with sunsets passing four times while the character drifts; he was allowed to cheat the sun angle so she always looked good, but animating the sun invisibly was a "nightmare", with shadows and rim light having to agree [49:59-51:32]. He kept a notebook just for that shot to record why each cheat existed, after forgetting once [51:32-52:02].

**Use it:** shot notes in the scene: record the reason for any light that breaks physics, so a later revision does not undo it.
