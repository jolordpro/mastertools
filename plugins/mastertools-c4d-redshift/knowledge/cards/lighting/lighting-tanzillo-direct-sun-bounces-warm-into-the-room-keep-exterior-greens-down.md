Mike Tanzillo sCDCs5At8uY [14:58-18:34]: sun hitting hardwood makes the room glow warm because it bounces a lot. Add warm bounce, brighten and warm the wall around the window then fade to cool, lift the dark value a bit, and tone down the saturation of greens outside, which look punchy. On a tabletop scale use focus: foreground sharp, three to five pixels blur to the back of the table, stronger blur outside. Search 'hygge' for reference.

**Use it:** Use as a cosy-interior checklist: warm bounce, window greens, and a stepped depth of field.
