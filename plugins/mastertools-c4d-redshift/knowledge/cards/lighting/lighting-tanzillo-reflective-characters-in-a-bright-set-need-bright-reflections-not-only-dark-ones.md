Tanzillo on a robot fight [22:33-24:36]: the set is mostly light, but two reflective characters pick up dark corners and pipes, so they look darker than the space. Add reflections, especially on the back when one lands, while keeping some variation. The ratio of dark to light in the reflective bits leaned too far to dark. For a make-or-break pose (the hero gathering strength), he wanted more under-light and a toned-down top light for shape [24:05]. Motion blur on a foreground character and depth of field at the start also felt too strong [22:33].

**Use it:** when a reflective hero reads dark, look at what its environment shows; add cards or bounce that reflect light, not only more key.
