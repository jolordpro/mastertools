Quan Tran [18:01-22:23]: on a toad on a mushroom the eye went to bright flowers, so add a gobo to make dappled light and stop them reading hot. In the push-in, the opening foreground read grey and flat while the end frame was good: go darker in the foreground and ground, matching the corner values of the end frame, and optionally add a gobo light so it is not completely dark.

**Use it:** Eye-trace test: find the brightest non-subject spot and gobo or dim it.
