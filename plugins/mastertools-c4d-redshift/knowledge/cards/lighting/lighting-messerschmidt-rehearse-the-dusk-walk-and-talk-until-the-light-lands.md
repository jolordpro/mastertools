A 3-4 minute scripted dusk scene: the director rehearsed with actors on a stage mock-up of the street to pace the beats, then on location on a Saturday, then placed extras, cars and dogs [08:56-10:30]. The grips laid 350-400 feet of dolly track the day before; shooting began after lunch and rehearsal ran to 5-6 pm; the keeper was around take seven or eight at magic hour [07:54-08:26; 10:30]. The hillside meant track could not be level, so the camera ran on a Ronin gimbal, operated from wheels off the dolly [10:30-11:34].

**Use it:** For timed light (golden hour, dusk look in CG or live), rehearse movement and camera before the window and keep the window for takes.
