Sareesh Sudhakaran's definition: meter the lit side (f/5.6) and the shadow side (f/2.8) to get two stops of contrast; film crews speak in stops, not photographers' 2:1 style ratios [00:00-01:08]. Same exposure both sides is flat; half a stop is possible; 4+ stops for moody, dark scenes (Gordon Willis often used large ratios); average about two stops [01:08-01:41]. A second ratio, key to background, is commonly one stop with the background darker, a technique from early cinema [01:41]. Fill side usually matches the background since a very dark shadow side in a bright room looks unnatural [04:18-04:54].
