Villeneuve (spoken as "Denny") is a big storyboarder: boards in prep and every day with artists; on set they honoured, used or ignored them, but the practice shows the possible visual landscape, which was new and useful to Young [01:06-01:36].
They wanted an overcast, muted, mundane, darker film, and Quebec in that season supplied it; they left the schedule open so they could choose on the day which places to shoot [01:36-02:09]. All the cloud cover was real.
Natural-light work has a small window of good light; they raced to finish scenes at day's end [04:50]. Handheld for the flash-forwards, composed frames for the rest, he likes both equally [02:09-03:48].
Transfers: weather is a lighting decision; plan flexibility around it.

**Use it:** Plan CG lighting boards as a possibility map and expect to deviate; build schedule slack around the light you cannot control.
