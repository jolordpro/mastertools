Quan Tran praised the key-to-fill ratio on the character [07:18]. [11:39-12:41] too much fill in a scene (IBL or area lights) competes with the character: take it down about three-quarters of a stop to a stop. [10:33] for a night shot, lower the background by half a stop, even a quarter: the student said he did not want it pitch black in the rocks. A rim that is slightly overexposed was liked.

**Use it:** Give fill trims in fractions of a stop, not vague 'darker'.
