Synthesis across two of Mike Tanzillo's daily critiques (Academy of Animated Art, 2021). He never states a checklist, so this order is read from what he says first and what he saves for last, which makes it partly inference.
1. **Focus and value:** where the eye lands and whether darks and brights steer it there. He keeps asking for darker corners, a dimmer surround, a brighter hero, a "focus triangle" [42:41 T1; 06:25, 25:38 T2].
2. **Shaping:** does each form show light-to-dark falloff, including on curved parts like an ear, and does light fall off across a room [47:22 T1; 04:14 T2].
3. **Surface cues:** speculars on wet or oily parts (tongue, lips, eyes, books, metal), reflections on shiny characters [31:16 T1; 08:00, 22:33 T2].
4. **Colour:** does one saturated source lead, or do three fight; does fill colour match the surroundings [38:32 T1; 08:59 T2].
5. **Black points and levels:** are the darks lifted into a hard wall [58:48 T1].
6. **Technical flaws last:** geometry poking through, noise, odd rims, crunchy corners, blown teeth [59:50, 48:55 T1; 05:17, 19:28 T2].
He also tests an EXR by sliding exposure up and down to see whether any area is dead black [18:58 T2].

**Use it:** order a critique pass the same way (focus, shape, surface, colour, levels, flaws) so big problems are named before small ones. Transfers directly to C4D + Redshift stills: the same order works on a styleframe.
