Fraser says he was never more stressed than on The Mandalorian, because any problem with the Volume shuts the shoot down, whereas on location you can recover the day [§1]. Early on none of the sets existed, so there were no location scouts and blocking was like a stage set with no locations [§2]. He still sees no reason a serious drama could not shoot in a Volume, and expects a mix of a few outdoor locations and Volume work within 10 years [§2].

**Use it:** When planning a virtual-set shoot, ask what the fallback is if the wall or content fails, and build in earlier set design to block with.
