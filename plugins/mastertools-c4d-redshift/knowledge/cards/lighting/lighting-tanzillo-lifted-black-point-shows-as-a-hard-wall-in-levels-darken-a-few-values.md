Tanzillo on a bedroom render [58:17-59:20]: in the levels there is a "hard wall" of nothing at the dark end; everything is lifted a handful of pixels, so darken the darks a little. Also play with brighter mid-tones, reduce saturation on the strong gold handles. On an earlier piece "black points match a lot better" was a pass condition [23:39]. Line on the dresser looked too sharp and was then softened [59:20].

**Use it:** add a levels check to review: look for a gap below the darkest values, and decide whether it is intended.
