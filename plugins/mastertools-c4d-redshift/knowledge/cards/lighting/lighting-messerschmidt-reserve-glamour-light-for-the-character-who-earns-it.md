Low-key modeled light is historically tied to glamour (cinema of the 40s, Rembrandt, Caravaggio), which he did not want for the people in Mindhunter's interviews; fluorescent top light and removed front light described space and character instead [59:47-1:00:49]. It was still done with nuance, not by switching on every overhead [1:01:20]. On Mank he lit Marion Davies with glamour light on purpose, tongue-in-cheek, because the scene orbits her [Mank 40:58-41:29].

**Use it:** Choose per character whether the light flatters or describes; lighting the whole cast the same way is a default, not a decision.
