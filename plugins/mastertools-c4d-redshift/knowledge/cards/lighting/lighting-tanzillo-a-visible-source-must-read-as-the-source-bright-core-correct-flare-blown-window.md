- Fireflies that are meant to light the bunny: keep each core as bright as the others, and add small lights on the character so they seem to cause the light [46:20 T1]. Floating orbs at uneven heights read odd; more of them deep in the background (out of focus) add depth [48:24 T1].
- Window in an interior exposed for the room: the sky outside should be near white, brighter than a mid sky, because the sun is strong enough to light the room [04:14 T2].
- Flame flare: a star-like flare off a candle looked like a car-hood highlight; make it a soft glow and place it on the flame, not below it [10:33 T2].
- Dark strip in the sky that is really a tree line reads as black: lift it to match the sky [50:28 T1].

**Use it:** a motivated-light check in review: for every visible source, ask whether it is the brightest, shaped and placed the way a real source would be.
