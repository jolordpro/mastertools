The director's brief was fireworks that are the opposite of fireworks, a technical head-scratcher [§1]. Fraser ran video through the lights, using footage of arc welders, inverted fireworks and jellyfish, an idea from production designer Patrice Vermette; the jellyfish are not visible on screen but shaped the fireworks [§2]. The result is inky black bursts over the planet behind two actors in a dark hallway [§1].

**Use it:** For an impossible-sounding brief, think of existing footage you can play through practical lights and test it.
