Guest: Erik Messerschmidt. Colour choices on set mattered only as brightness values. He sat with designer Don Burt over tiles and paint, and costume designer Trish Summerville sent fabric swatches. Some crowd extras wear ridiculous colours because those render certain greys [1:00:26-1:02:30].
Testing went in steps: wardrobe and makeup tests; stills on a phone desaturated to judge; favourites in front of the real camera; then test grades on the values. They did not wing it [1:02:30-1:03:31].

**Use it:** Before locking a monochrome or heavily limited-palette design, check each material's value in the final chain, not by eye in colour.
