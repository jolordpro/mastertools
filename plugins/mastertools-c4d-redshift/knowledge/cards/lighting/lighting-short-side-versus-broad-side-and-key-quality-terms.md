StudioBinder [05:57-08:36]. High key = low contrast ratio, background and subject evenly lit, bright happy scenes; low key = high contrast ratio, darker dramatic scenes; chiaroscuro is the extreme version seen in film noir. Hard light is directional with stark shadows (intense scenes); soft light is diffused (happy or romantic); golden hour just before sunset and after sunrise gives the best natural soft light. Placement: lighting the near side (broadside or 'dumb side') gives a more approachable subject; lighting the far side (short side, 'smart side') gives a more dramatic look.
