Maxon's Redshift pro tips session shows a clay step for judging light first; the Render View also has a Clay toggle.
Evidence is the video description and help page, not the spoken content.

**Use it:** start a look-dev sheet with a clay pass, then add materials.
Transcript support: Clay mode removes the effect of light or dark materials on bounce while lighting; D (kN00Z7UsW5U [06:16]) says light before materials. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t9hy0YCq0I8 [56:10])
