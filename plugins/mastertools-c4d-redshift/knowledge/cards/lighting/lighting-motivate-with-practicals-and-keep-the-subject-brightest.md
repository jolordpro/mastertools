Josh Toonen [11:17-14:20]. Practicals (candles, lamps, torches in frame) give a reason for off-camera lights, so a harsh point or a soft rectangle placed next to the actress is believable even if it is not physically right for a torch. Test a scene by turning the extra light off: what is left is the light the set itself provides, and interiors are built up one light at a time from there. Rule of thumb: make the character the brightest object so the eye knows where to look; a bright light in the background unbalances and loses the motivation, even if real life would brighten the walls. Know what reality would do, then choose.
