Maria Walla [37:02-41:46]: the Rembrandt look puts a small triangle of light on the cheek on the shadow side, adding volume to the face; she uses it on her game-cinematic shots and points to the eye. She admires a cinematographer who uses backlight, volumetrics and silhouettes and who separates a bright face from a dark background, or a dark face from a bright background, so the character does not sit flat. She says the technique is old and the cinematographer added colour to it. Host Lighting Bot's own advice: take photos with a phone without filters to learn this [42:17].
Transfers to C4D and Redshift: a rim-light and value-swap check for hero objects or faces.
