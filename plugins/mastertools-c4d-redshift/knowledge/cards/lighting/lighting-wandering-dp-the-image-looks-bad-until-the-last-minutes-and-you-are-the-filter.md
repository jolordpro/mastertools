Patrick says the job is to constrain variables so everything funnels into one imagined image [07:17]. Many people (makeup, script supervisor, first AD at monitors) will comment; most do not share the lighter's taste, so thank them and keep the plan; ideas can come from anyone but you filter yes/no [07:17][08:19]. Nothing balances until everyone is in place; the image looks bad until about two minutes before shooting, like a cake eaten five minutes early, so do not panic [08:50][09:22]. Opinion, flippant tone; a working DP's view, medium to low confidence.

**Use it:** useful coach line for panic mid-build: judge the shot when all layers are in, not halfway.
