He worried about going too far and not far enough, and says they only did things when it seemed right for the movie. Shafts of hard light through blinds left the main characters in deep shadow in the more sinister offices, but he worked to avoid drawing attention to it [§1]. Modern methods were accepted; the question was how far to go, and he thinks they hit the balance [§4]. [§1,§4] [Part I §2]
