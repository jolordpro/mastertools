Prerequisites from Blender Studio: a first blocking pass, every asset in frame shaded at least basically, and a colour script or lighting guide.
Order: key shots first; close-ups and inserts follow from the wide.

**Use it:** preflight asks whether the shot has what lighting needs before look-dev advice starts, and suggests which shot to light first.
Transcript support: Effectatron order: camera, lighting, materials, re-tune lighting. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kN00Z7UsW5U [06:16])
