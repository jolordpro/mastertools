Without a light from behind the object the side fades into the background [D 13:30-14:02]. A rim only forms where the surface curves; a hard 90-degree edge gives shadow, not a rim [14:35-15:08]. This is the geometry reason for the existing bevel card.

**Use it:** elevate: if the subject merges with the background, check for a back or rim light and for a bevel on the edges that face it.
