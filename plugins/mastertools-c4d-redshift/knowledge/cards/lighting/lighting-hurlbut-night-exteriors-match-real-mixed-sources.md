Shane Hurlbut ASC answers a member question he says he got 12 times [01:06-02:10]. Real night streets mix street lamps, porch tungsten, cool-white fluorescent shops and sodium vapour (about 2900 K). Setting the camera high (4800 K, 5600 K lights) turns cool-white fluorescents yellow-green, tungsten saturated orange and sodium a bad brown [02:10-04:15].
5600 / 4800 only works on a stage where every light is controlled, even the practicals [04:15-04:46]. Outside you cannot swap neighbours' bulbs; his night reality sits near 3200 K [05:17].
His advice: do not experiment on night exteriors, test elsewhere [05:47].
Transfers: a CG night scene should pick a white balance that keeps the practical colours believable, then colour the moon light against it.

**Use it:** When building a night look, set the base colour balance first, then test how lamp, window and signage colours land before tuning the moon key.
