She likes low-light and single-source scenes. In Onward the brothers are pulled over and the scene is lit completely by the police car; the sky is black and nothing beyond the characters is seen [14:57-15:29].
Animation tends to celebrate every detail with enough light to see it; removing light outside the cop zone kept the scene intimate and tense [15:29-16:01].
