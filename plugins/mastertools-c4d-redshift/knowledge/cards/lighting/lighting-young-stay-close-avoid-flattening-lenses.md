The PremiumBeat profile contrasts DPs who use long lenses to flatten the world for an objective view with Young, whose style is increasingly defined by intimacy: he stays close to characters. Citing a CookeOpticsTV interview, it says he believes closeness gives a more meaningful portrayal of emotions and story, and that his shots move from line-by-line coverage of conversations toward close-ups of faces and reactions (Selma, A Most Violent Year) [§2].

**Use it:** Move in for a hero shot to keep intimacy; test a shorter lens rather than a long, flattening one (our inference).
