A student's setup described to Quan Tran [04:10-06:45]: three lights at the doorway (ground spray, doorway, rim), character with his own key, a background key facing a different direction (shadows would not match), an environment map and general background fill. Warm bounce from the cave floor at the start swaps to a cool moonlight bounce once he passes out; a kicker and a small eye light stop it going black. About 12 lights, most on the character because he moves. Quan liked the warm-to-cool transition.

**Use it:** A model for tracking a character through changing light: bounce colour follows the ground.
