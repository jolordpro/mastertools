Her tip: if lighting something, think how you would do it in real life and place lights that way [14:10-14:40].
The host adds: for realistic moves ask where the cameraman, crane and track are; an ultra-wide lens flying around looks fake [14:40]. Knowing real cameras and lights makes 3D look premium [13:38-14:40].

**Use it:** check a camera move against what real gear could do (path, speed, lens) before blaming lighting.
