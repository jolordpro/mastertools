Technically shooting in sand and wind is similar from film to film, but aesthetically it was night and day, because you come from a different point in the story and end up in a different place. He says the desert is a character, and that saying 'it is the desert, so the same' is like saying an actor played one character [§2].

**Use it:** Write one line on what the place means to this story before copying a look from another film in the same setting.
