Valentina V (four-minute film school, Aputure) set ISO and shutter on a light meter and took incident readings of each light [03:08-03:38]. Background light on the gold chairs was one stop above the key on her face, a 2:1 ratio. She chose it because the background light was on a faulty bulb setting that flickered on and off, and both states had to look dramatic without the key being overpowered [03:38-04:08]. A 1:1 setup looked even but less dramatic [04:08].
Second setup: the face needed severe ratio from key to fill, 5:1 where "most pleasing" ratios are about 3:1 [06:10-06:41]. Lights were on one Bluetooth network controlled from an iPad, with false color checked on the monitor [02:36-03:08].
Transfers: compare as stop differences between lit and shadow sides, then put that number on the CG lights.
