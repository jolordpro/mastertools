School of Motion [03:41-04:17]. In a very large environment, a single source would have to be enormous to light everything, so real big spaces show many little pools of light (a film marketplace set used tens of thousands of lights). In his concert-visual example, an HDRI alone or a couple of huge area lights looked flat, while a bunch of smaller lights looked far more convincing.

**Use it:** Scale check: if the environment is meant to be huge, ask whether the light count and size read as large.
