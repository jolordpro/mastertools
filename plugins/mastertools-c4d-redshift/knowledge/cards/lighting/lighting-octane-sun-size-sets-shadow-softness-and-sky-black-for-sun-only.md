Rotation sets the sun height and the colour: low is dark sky and a red sun; higher is a brighter sky and whiter sun; past 180 degrees the sun is gone [18:14][18:45]. Turbidity makes the sky cloudier, so the light is more diffuse and shadows less strong [18:45]. Power drives sun and sky together; sun intensity affects only the sun [19:16]. Sun size does not change its look in reflections but a bigger sun means softer shadows [19:16]. He sets the sky black to have only the sun as light, and for night makes the sun light blue and the sky dark blue, then darkens more in post [19:47][20:18]. Moving the sun object does nothing, only rotation counts, as it is an infinite light [19:47].
