In the Westworld titles the host bodies are built from layered anatomy (skeleton, organ, muscle, skin). Light does the storytelling: skin stays whole where the light falls, the grotesque layers sit in shadow.
He studied lighting and symmetry in Chris Cunningham's Bjork video All Is Full of Love, and the team gathered over 10,000 reference images before modelling. [§4-§5]

**Use it:** Decide what the light is allowed to reveal. Put the part you want hidden in shadow instead of dimming the whole frame.
