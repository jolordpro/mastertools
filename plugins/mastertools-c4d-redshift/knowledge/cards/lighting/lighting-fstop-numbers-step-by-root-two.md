F number is focal length divided by aperture diameter. A 50 mm lens at f/2 gives a 25 mm hole, at f/8 gives 6.25 mm [19:43-21:14].
Doubling light means doubling area, and area scales with radius squared, so the radius grows by square root of two (about 1.414) per stop [21:44-23:48]. The scale reads 1, 1.4, 2, 2.8, 4 and every second step doubles [23:48].
Shutter speed and ISO simply halve or double per stop. ISO is a gain applied before digitising, so it adds noise [15:37-17:40]. Raising one side of the triangle one stop means lowering another by one stop [12:34].

**Use it:** Redshift physical camera or DoF coaching: convert f-stop talk into stops of light with 2 x log2(ratio), so one f-stop step equals one stop of exposure.
