The ladder at Pixar on Inside Out: production designer Ralph Eggleston and White view the sequence unlit and choose the key shots; they make colour keys and show the director; White directs the master lighter from the keys; a meeting with the director, master lighter and production designer asks if all feel good before shot lighting begins [§2]. Shots are distributed in groups that make sense (all of Mom to one person); shot lighters check in daily or every couple of days, and White finals shots herself [§2-3]. Each week she shows the director the finaled shots, 10 to 100 depending on the stage, so he gives few notes; she watches in continuity, and finished sequences go on the big screen with leads from every department to check flow and continuity [§3].

**Use it:** Mirror it: approve the look on keys, then shots, then view the finished set large.
