The DP wanted the virtual set to work like a physical set: the team talked in lumens, ISO, f-stops and apertures, and modelled how light behaves at space distances, including the sun's distance and position per axis [12:29, 13:00, 28:03].
For hard sci-fi the plan was to push ISO very high for the right look [13:00].
Guest Zaman: digital can do anything, but that does not mean it should [12:29].
Transfers: speak photographic units with a camera-minded director so notes translate straight to settings.

**Use it:** Expose lighting and camera in photographic units and real scale when working with camera-trained reviewers.
