On Need for Speed he had a bucket truck take out the street lamps on one side for five city blocks. Because he shot from that side, only the lamps behind and beside the cars remained, giving immediate contrast and edge light [05:47-06:52].
He says the same logic is how the two hosts are lit: camera on one side, all light from the opposite side [06:19].
He notes this level of control is only possible on a big-budget picture or on stage [05:47].

**Use it:** In CG night scenes, turn off lights that sit on the camera side of the subject and keep rim and edge sources for the contrast.
