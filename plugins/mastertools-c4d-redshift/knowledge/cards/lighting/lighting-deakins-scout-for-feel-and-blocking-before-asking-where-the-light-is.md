Deakins: on a scout, asking first where the light comes from is "death" [01:55-02:27]. Ask instead if it suits the scene's feel and picture the actors and blocking; the practical can be solved later.
Going in with a fixed idea of how it should look makes the work paint-by-numbers and less interesting [02:27]. He often designs practical sources with the production designer [01:16].

**Use it:** Pick the room and action first, then place light sources to suit it.
