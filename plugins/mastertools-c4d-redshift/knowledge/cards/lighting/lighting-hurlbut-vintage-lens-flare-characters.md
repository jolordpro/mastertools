Kowa lenses from the 1960s have lower contrast and flare with a golden centre inside a whitish-yellow halo, his 'sunny side up egg', and he calls them among the most beautiful flaring lenses [25:21-26:24]. Cooke lenses show a rainbow-coloured halation from the side; Panavision lenses flare from the side and from above [26:24-26:55]. Canon K35s are close to Kowa but with a less striking flare [26:55].
Old stills lenses are rehoused with cine gears and the same front diameter so a matte box fits [25:21]. Test lenses for the project [27:26].

**Use it:** A flare or glare pass in post should choose one lens character and keep it consistent across a package, not mix them.
