Blender Guru (2019) [10:30-15:08]. Fill: a second, larger lamp on the other side, low, to bring a little information into shadow (ear, hair back, chin) so it does not fade to total black; larger = more fill and wrap, smaller = punchier. Rim: duplicate the key and place behind the subject to recover hair and ear edge; how hot is up to taste (a stylised casino look has blown-out rims). White in front and white behind merges into a stripy pattern; giving the rim a warm colour makes the source clear, implies an environment (fireplace, street lamp) and an optional bluer kick on the other side gives a complementary look. Think of what each lamp is for before adding it, not the name.

**Use it:** The advisor can ask 'what is this light for' when a rig has lights without a stated job.
