Order the presenter follows: lock the composition first (a long lens, he used about 150 and prefers longer; lighting moves if the frame moves) [01:06]. Light in a grey or clay override so texture does not mislead [03:16]. Then key from above for an uplifting direction, small and a little behind for drama and falloff [03:16]; cooler colour temperature; then rim lights from below and the side to carve edges [09:22]; a separate logo light so the product reads [13:15]; then a dome fill, then a reflection-only light [16:02]. Textures decide how everything reads: shiny, flat, and a white-to-black range were his hardest cases [02:12]. Light the same rig once, then reuse it across other camera angles by moving the camera and nudging lights [25:41].
