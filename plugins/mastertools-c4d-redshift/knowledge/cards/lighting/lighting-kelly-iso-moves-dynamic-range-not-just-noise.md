Kelly [47:03-51:54] says ISO is a distribution of range around the middle exposure, not just a noise setting. Cranking to 1600 or 3200 for a night exterior with a small lighting package leaves less range below middle exposure, in exactly the area the shot needs. Lowering ISO starves the highlights. At neutral, half the range sits above and half below, so ISO can be chosen on purpose to hold shadow nuance.
He recommends engaging post early and shooting a test, so the director sees the difference rather than taking his word. [52:36-53:36] Compromises not tested come out too late.

**Use it:** the physical-camera and exposure notes in the platform: the same logic applies to choosing where middle exposure sits in a render and how much shadow range survives the display transform.
