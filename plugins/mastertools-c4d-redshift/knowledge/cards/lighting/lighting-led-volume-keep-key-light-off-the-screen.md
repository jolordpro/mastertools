Host-DP Nicholas (Media Division) at 10 Dots, Bulgaria. Screens alone are often too dim and RGB-only, so skin and cloth can pick up magenta, and fixing it loops back on the wall [24:53-25:25]. Add key and ambient from LED panels, COBs, HMIs or tungsten; sky panels and RGBW Mimics (400 W, 120x60 px video) can follow colour picked from the screen [25:25-26:27].
Direct light on the panels lifts the black level and shows a floating flare or shadow [26:58-28:02]. Flag lights, aim strong lights out of the volume, rig sun lights on the rail. A single parallel source (COB with Fresnel) works for sun or moon [26:27].
Rule: judge what the camera sees, not what the eye sees [28:33].

**Use it:** Same flare/black-level logic applies to compositing CG onto LED-wall plates for sports broadcast.
Transcript support: Sam Nicholson: LED gives soft ambient light but not hard light, so real lights and projectors still do the hard work. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0o4i6eBHUY [00:58-03:02])
