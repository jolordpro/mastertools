StudioBinder, The Shot List Ep 12 [02:49-05:57]. Light intensity falls with 1 over distance squared: doubling the distance leaves 25% (a 75% reduction). Daylight is about 5600 K; tungsten about 3200 K, which varies by source; mixing them adds orange and blue. Lumens measure source output; lux or foot-candles measure light arriving at the subject (incident); reflected light is what reaches the camera. Each f-stop doubles or halves the light; T-stops measure what actually reaches the sensor. Contrast ratio compares light and shadow between two areas, such as the sides of a face or subject versus background.

**Use it:** Basic constants for the lighting advisor and for explaining why moving a light a little changes a lot.
