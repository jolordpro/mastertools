Sergei Volkov [33:21-36:34]: a branch lit by the key made viewers look at it before the house and expect a bird; keep it in shadow, darker and lower contrast like a vignette, or feature it with a story. A bright spot on the side of the frame runs out of frame and steals attention: it is the location, not the intensity. Put it in front of the house or on the steps and frame it with mid-tones.

**Use it:** Edge hot-spot check: move bright areas inward or darken them.
