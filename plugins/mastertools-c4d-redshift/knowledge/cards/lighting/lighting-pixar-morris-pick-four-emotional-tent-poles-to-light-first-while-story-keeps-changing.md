Layout artists get a section of the film (one to five minutes at most) and propose staging and blocking to the director, working on top of other departments that are still changing [04:16][04:47]. His advice for lighting under a moving story: choose about four emotional tent poles likely to stay, light those as the guide, then fill the gaps as the story comes in [04:47]. Pixar adds imperfection to its cinematography, because it comes from a "perfect world", and animators may send back shots asking for a tighter frame for a performance idea [05:22]. Camera work from layout reaches the animators, who use it directly [04:16].

**Use it:** a shortcut for sports packages with moving content: lock the look on the emotional peaks (the hero shots) and build the rest around them.
