Light sources are motivated. He avoids adding light just for contrast, avoids relighting between setups (a backlit actor can be followed by a front-lit reverse), and will go dark or flat if the staging asks. Lighting should stay unobtrusive and support the story.

Page spot: §4-§5.

**Use it:** Ask of every light in a frame: what is its source? Remove highlights and kickers with no motivation.
