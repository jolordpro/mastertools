Front (on axis) flattens the face and lights the background [01:59-02:44]. Butterfly: high and in front, nose shadow butterfly-shaped, easiest to keep off the background [03:34]. Rembrandt: about 45 degrees off axis and 45 degrees down, triangle of light on the shadow cheek [04:33]. Side: about 90 degrees from the eyeline, practically about 80 so the cheek does not shadow the eye [05:22]. Kicker/edge: 15-45 degrees behind the subject, wraps the cheek; backlight sits directly behind, no wrap [05:57-06:44]. Lights are usually high, pointing down.
Nose-shadow test [07:44-08:19]: no shadow = front; butterfly shadow = butterfly; triangle of light on the far cheek = Rembrandt; face split in half = side.

**Use it:** the check or coach can label a render's key direction and name the nearest setup; set 45/45 as the default hero key and 15-45 behind for a rim.
