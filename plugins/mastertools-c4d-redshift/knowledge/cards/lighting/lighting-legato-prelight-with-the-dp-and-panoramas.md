MPC sent a lighting lead to LA to sit with DP Bill Pope. Each scene came out of previs, then key angles were pre-lit in RenderMan 19 with domes and soft boxes standing in for sky, reflection cards and sun. Time of day, angles and ratios were fixed precisely. Two or three spherical renders per set went to an iPad panorama app, so the crew could see where dapple, rock walls and open sky would fall while lighting on stage. [§6]

**Use it:** Fix light direction and ratios in a pre-light on the hero angles and hand the crew a reference they can look in any direction.
