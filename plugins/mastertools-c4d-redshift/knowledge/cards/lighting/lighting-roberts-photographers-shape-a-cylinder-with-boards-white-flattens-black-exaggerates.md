Phil Roberts (London generalist, Raid Zero; ex photo retoucher) shows three Bose speaker photos [05:48-06:50]: key from the side with falloff makes it feel round; a white reflector on the shadow side flattens the cylinder; a black board on that side exaggerates the curvature. Perfume photographers light the body, then move lights for a lid highlight in a separate shot and combine in Photoshop, which he equates with AOV light groups [05:18]. His retouching background taught coherent lighting direction, scale, depth of field and colour across 20 or so elements [04:12].
Transfers: in Redshift use black cards or negative fill (exclude from dome or use dark geometry) to deepen shape, white geometry for bounce.

**Use it:** Coach tip: ask whether fill flattens the form; try black fill to exaggerate shape.
