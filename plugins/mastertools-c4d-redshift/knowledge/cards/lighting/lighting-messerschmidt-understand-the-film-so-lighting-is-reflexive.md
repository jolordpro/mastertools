Messerschmidt says cinematographers get more credit for the look than is accurate, and production and costume designers deserve more [18:16-18:46].
He wants to talk about the film's pace, structure and story as well as the look, so decisions made on the spur of the moment become reflexive and he does not over-intellectualise a light [18:46-19:16].
On Ferrari, painting was a main reference: look at a couple of dozen paintings, pull out what they share, and turn that into cinematography terms such as soft top light [19:16].
He also compares his two big directors: Fincher is methodical, precise; Mann is spontaneous and visceral but detail oriented, and uses the camera more gesturally [13:36-14:37].

**Use it:** For a look-dev brief, gather 20+ references and write the shared traits as lighting terms before opening the scene.
