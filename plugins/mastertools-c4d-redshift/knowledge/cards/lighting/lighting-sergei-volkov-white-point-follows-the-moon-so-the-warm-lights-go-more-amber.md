Sergei Volkov [25:21-30:34]: a night shot with blue outside and warm inside improved with more separation. An auto white balance follows whatever fills the image, here the outdoors, so as the white point shifts to the moon, tungsten gets more saturated. So push the practical towards amber, nearly candlelight, as far as it still holds. Keep some blue; it need not become fully white. A less saturated moon needs a more orange lamp to compensate.

**Use it:** Pair this with the colour-temperature card when judging night windows.
