The evening dinner scene is saturated, lit by a strong light from above with no shade. White avoided showing the bulb because it was distracting, and kept the light from being harsh or an "interrogation". The top light lets the kitchen and entry stay dark, so appealing foreground characters sit against darker backgrounds, which gives visual complexity [§1].

**Use it:** Use a single dominant top source and let secondary rooms fall darker behind it; hide the source if it pulls the eye.
