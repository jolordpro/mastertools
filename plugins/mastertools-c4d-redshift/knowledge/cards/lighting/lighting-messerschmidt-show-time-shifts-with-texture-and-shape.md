Parts of the film are lit soft with slightly modern technique, while flashbacks get more texture and shape [18:21-18:51]. He admits it is subtle. The reference spectrum is wide: glamour of the early 30s through deep noir of the mid-40s, which look dramatically different [19:22].

**Use it:** Differentiate story time zones through a notch of texture, shadow depth and softness, not a big colour change.
