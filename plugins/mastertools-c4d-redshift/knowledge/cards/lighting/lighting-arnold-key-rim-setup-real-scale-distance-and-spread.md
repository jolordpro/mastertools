Lights should be near real-world scale; too-small sources give tiny painted reflections [03:08]. Do not park lights close to the subject: close lights burn out and a moving subject easily leaves the pool, while distance gives natural falloff [03:08]. An area light's default range is 180 degrees; reduce the spread so it beams like a softbox with flaps, 0.5 being half of the full opening [04:13][04:44]. Kelvin: higher reads cooler, lower warmer; colour can replace Kelvin when a tint such as teal is needed [05:18][07:58]. Place the key by matching reference: slight downward angle and from the correct side [04:13]. Smaller source gives harsher shadows, bigger gives softer [09:00].

**Use it:** diagnostics for the coach: sources tiny, too close, or spread wide when the user wants a directional look.
