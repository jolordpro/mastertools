Jim Costa: measure light on the key side, the fill side and the background, then compare [03:31]. A Jude Law close-up read about 8:1 in light, which he states as 3 stops (his 'three times' wording is wrong; 8:1 is 3 stops) [03:38]. Background is usually one or two stops under key in drama, a broad generalisation; the example read key on the left and 1.5 stops under on the right [04:27]. Fill may be a bounce, ambient, a window or negative fill; ratio is about light levels, not light count or hardness [02:19].
