Mike Tanzillo [31:10-33:38], comparing a render to its reference side by side: the reference has lower contrast, so lift the back of the room by raising the secondary bounce, not by adding fill lights. Plug the light leak at the top corner of the window pane.
The reference's lower window half was frosted glass. Making the outside planes frosted turns the exterior into colour and haze and also diffuses the sun hitting the rug. Finally warm the centre and cool the surround for a value shift.

**Use it:** For interior comps with a reference, raise bounce and soften windows before touching key lights.
