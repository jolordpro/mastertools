Product reads badly when the logo is lost, so he duplicated the key as a logo light, tightened its spread and set its project tab to Include on just the side panel [13:48][15:25]. For the reflection light he did the opposite: Exclude the main case and side panel so only the knobs get highlights, not "hit over the head" [20:40]. Result: a short stack of key, logo, two rims, fill and reflection lights [23:59].

**Use it:** when one surface blows out, isolate it with include or exclude lists instead of lowering the whole light.
Transcript support: dome light excluded from the hero object so it lights only the background (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oANXbk9OUbI [09:35])
