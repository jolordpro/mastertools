The last time Deakins put a filter on the camera was in 1986; he shot the whole film with a light diffusion and still regrets it [1:16:21, 1:16:53]. Since then he used polarizing screens only, and diffusion only for a couple of shots (a gauzy plane shot on Sid and Nancy).
With digital cameras he and James say they shoot it the way they want it to end up, not planning to add things later, as film once forced you to [41:56].
Why the Alexa: the colour space [41:25]. He picks the sharpest, cleanest, lightest lenses [40:53].
Transfer: bake the intended contrast and light in the render; add glow and bloom in comp as a reversible layer, not baked in.

**Use it:** The coach reminds that diffusion looks are comp layers kept separate, not burned into the beauty pass.
