Kim White says Riley's shots were harder than Mom or Dad because she looks down; the light went dark and made it look like she had a beard, so they cheated the lights to make her appealing and well shaped, which takes more time [§2]. The first pass is not necessarily about reality; some lights do not do what they normally would, because the aim is the colour key's goals [§3]. The cheating also serves feeling: Riley turns obnoxious, so she must stay cute and vulnerable so the audience doesn't think "good riddance"; Bonnie in Toy Story 3 got the same care [§4].

**Use it:** If realistic light makes the hero look worse, the realistic light loses.
