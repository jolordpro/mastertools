Tony Lines names the usual groupings [04:44-09:25]. Key is the main source. Fill lifts shadow, for example sky dome or bounce. Rim catches edges to separate foreground and background. Practicals are lamps and screens inside the scene. Interactive lights change over time (police strobes, fire, lightning).
Magic or FX emission needs its own group, since the compositor must time it to exact moments [09:00-09:25]. His Blender demo grouped key, environment and rim, so the scene builds up one light at a time [02:36-03:06].
Transfers: for broadcast glow, edge-light and animated light passes, give each its own group.

**Use it:** Coach: when a scene has animated or branded emission, suggest a dedicated light group for it so timing and colour change in comp.
