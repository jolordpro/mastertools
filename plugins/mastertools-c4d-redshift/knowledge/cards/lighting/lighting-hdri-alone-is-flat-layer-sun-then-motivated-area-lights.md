School of Motion, David Ariev [00:45-02:39]. HDRIs are baked and infinitely distant: you can only rotate them, and you cannot light one object or move a light closer. Barn example: HDRI alone is flat; add a sun for direct light and strong shadows; add an area light as shadow fill; add a second area light for a strong side highlight. With a colour temperature close to the sun, the area lights read as motivated and look like an extension of the sun. For exteriors, a daylight rig (optionally mixed with an HDRI via the Mix Sky button for sky and reflection detail). Interior tunnel order [02:39]: star-map HDRI, practical neon lights, overhead area lights above spots invisible to camera, side area lights, optional sun. Cyberpunk order: HDRI, neon signs, coloured sun with shafts, area lights between buildings, lights on metal awnings, lights to boost background volume, shop interiors, warm balcony accents kept dim so the eye does not drift to the foreground, final warm, cold and pink wall highlights.

**Use it:** A useful build order for the lighting advisor: environment, practicals, sun/key, fill and accent lights, with colour temperatures related to the main source.
