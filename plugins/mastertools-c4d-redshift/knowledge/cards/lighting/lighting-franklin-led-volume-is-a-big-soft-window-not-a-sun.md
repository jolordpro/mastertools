Franklin says a giant TV screen cannot make hard shadows; it works like a big soft window [59:27-59:58]. Skin and shiny costumes pick up useful reflections, which green screen never gives [59:58-1:00:29]. But hard light must come from real lamps, and that light reflects back into the screen and flattens the contrast, so the screen must be further and larger, and costs more [1:00:29-1:01:00].
A volume change takes up to an hour; magic hour can be shot all day from one setting [1:01:00-1:01:31]. This supports the existing LED volume cards.

**Use it:** When planning a screen-lit set, budget for the distance from screen to action and for real lights beyond the screen's reach.
Transcript support: Nicholson says the same: no hard light or hard shadows from LED screens; projectors used for sunlight. (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F0o4i6eBHUY [01:30])
