Toonen worked 8 years in Hollywood VFX and on the virtual-production set of Avatar: The Last Airbender, where he needed 20-30 lighting setups a shoot day [01:03-02:04]. His four questions: direction, size, colour, intensity.
Direction: trace the shadows back through the tops of the heads; the perspective of the shadows points at the light, even off screen [05:42-07:17]. Size: the sun is tiny and gives crisp shadows; under cloud the sky is a big softbox. To match an overcast reference, desaturate the sky and widen the sun's source angle; his example used 100, and he described source angle as diffuse and source soft angle as reflection [07:19-08:48]. Colour and intensity: match hue and saturation, and avoid a strongly saturated directional light because it tints every material [08:50-09:21].
Method: one environment, five or ten looks matched to film stills teaches faster than ten new environments [00:32-01:03]. He joked every daylight case is sunny or overcast [09:21].
