Mike Tanzillo [26:32-27:33]: getting warm lit snow against cool shadow without anything drifting grey is really hard, and the student had done it. His two notes: with heavy foreground blur already there, put about five pixels of blur on the far mountain, and add a little diffusion because moist air makes snow bloom and blow out in spots.
His review habit: praise what holds, then give only the one or two changes.

**Use it:** Use the five-pixel far blur and light diffusion as a quick depth and air note on cold exteriors.
