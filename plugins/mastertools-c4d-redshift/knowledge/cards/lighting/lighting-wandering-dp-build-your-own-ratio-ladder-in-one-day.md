Patrick (The Wandering DP) refuses to give his own ratios; the lesson is to learn yours [01:33]. He learned them by pumping footage to false colour and spotting repeated patterns across Deakins-style references [01:33]. One-day exercise: rental house, camera, key, fill, backlight and a controllable ambient; ladder each from -3 to +3 stops (seven levels) [02:37][03:39]. Example: key reading T2.8 with shadow side T1.4 is two stops [03:08]. Do not shoot every combination: drop impossible ones, such as fill above key [04:10]. Review in Resolve and keep up to about five looks (moody, high key, etc.) [04:40]. Use: say "plus one on the key" and the crew can act when it reads plus three [05:11][05:43]. Like a recipe, the numbers repeat across locations [06:14]. For a one-person crew only key-to-background matters much [04:40].

**Use it:** adapt for CG: render a stop ladder of key/fill/rim/ambient intensities once and store the looks the coach can name.
