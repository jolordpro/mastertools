Names were cut out and taped in a long scroll, lit from behind so the light bled through, then embellished with found objects and shot on video.
Fincher had the scroll run downward, an inversion that echoes the film's psychology. [§2]

**Use it:** A glow that comes from light passing through a real edge looks truer than a glow added in post. Model the translucency.
