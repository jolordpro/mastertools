He says audiences perceive a zoom differently from a dolly: a dolly is a planned move needing a crew, with a formality that can be effective [27:35-28:07].
Mann often operates and holds the zoom controller himself, picking moments to squeeze in on an actor, a different emotional response from a rehearsed push [28:07-28:39].

**Use it:** In camera notes name whether the move is planned (dolly) or reactive (zoom) before choosing.
