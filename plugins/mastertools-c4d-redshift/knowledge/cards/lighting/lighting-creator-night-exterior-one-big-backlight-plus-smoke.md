Original intent was true moonlight, but thunderstorms made scheduling impossible, so they looked at stills from Alien and set up one big light, backlit, with a lot of smoke in the background [05:12-06:14]. It broke the film's naturalistic rule, which was a conscious trade. The 1200s lit huge fields that would normally need big lights on cherry pickers [06:14-06:45].

**Use it:** For a night exterior, one large backlight through haze defines the space with little else needed.
