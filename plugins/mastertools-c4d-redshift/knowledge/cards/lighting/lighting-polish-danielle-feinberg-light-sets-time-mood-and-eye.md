From her 2015 TED talk: lighting is the step that turns models into story. It tells the audience when it is, how to feel, and where to look, and it separates appealing characters from busy backgrounds. Ground fantasy in real-world logic, decide which rules to bend, and aim for a believable world, not a scientifically correct one. [§3]

**Use it:** Every light on a frame should answer one of: time, mood, or where to look.
