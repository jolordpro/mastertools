He quotes James Wong Howe, who lit with about 100 lights, then 10 or 11, and wanted to get down to one, searching for simplicity [04:48-05:05]. On a boardroom night scene his designed rig was not working and he kept messing with it; he ripped it down, put up three China balls and was ready after lunch [05:05-06:09]. Better to admit the first decision was wrong than to struggle to make it work [06:09].

**Use it:** If a lighting setup needs more than a couple of tweaks, delete lights and rebuild from one.
