A season-one shot that ran handheld and frantic at the end of an episode got attention because the show had never done it [22:56-23:27]. Season two repeats the idea as the characters join an active manhunt: handheld once the investigation is live, and a static dolly pull-back that breaks into handheld at the stop [23:27-24:00; 30:14-30:48]. In one episode the camera also goes much lower and wider than normal [21:53]. The audience's trust in the rule is what gives the exception its weight.

**Use it:** Define the rule first, then spend an exception once, on the beat that matters.
