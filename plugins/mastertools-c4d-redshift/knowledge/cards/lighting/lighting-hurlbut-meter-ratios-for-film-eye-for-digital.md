Film shooters had only a black-and-white video tap, so a light meter and key-to-fill-to-back ratios were how they knew the levels [32:42-33:14]. Hurlbut now lights by eye, which works on digital because the monitor shows the result if the LUT was designed by a colourist and has no odd swings [33:14-33:44].
On film nothing can be seen until it is processed, so ratios and spot meters matter [33:44].

**Use it:** With a trusted, calibrated viewer, judge by eye; add numbers (ratios, histograms) when the viewer can't be trusted or the result can't be previewed.
