Roberts adds a little blue to the dome because most real things pick up a slight blue cast from the sky, and shadows in sunlight take blue from GI [16:02-16:33]. He wants white products to gain colour contrast, since lights in real life are rarely pure white [14:21-14:54]. Key: small area light over the object kept a bit hard so direction reads, exposure 1, warm using colour and temperature; rim: small area light behind with a Target tag on the ear cups, cool blue, to pick up the edge of the round shape; third a low bounce light, warm like the key with a touch of cool to separate from the background [17:04-21:48]. He thinks in a cube: a highlight, a midtone and a shadow on every form [18:07]. He counts four lights including the dome even though it is called three-point [21:48].
Backdrop trick: add a backdrop object so the gobo light bounces off it as fill via GI, like a board [22:52-23:56].

**Use it:** Use as a starting rig for a product or hero logo; tint dome slightly blue.
