Messerschmidt says he and the gaffer often wanted to put a light on the floor for more shape, but with long dialogue scenes and lots of coverage that would break continuity: the audience sees past rewritten lighting [1:07:26-1:08:29]. Large soft overheads (the show used Mirage-engine boxes of 16 to 18 units at 400 W each) gave a base that also matched their references [1:02:09-1:03:46, 1:07:26].
Colour temperature of the overheads stayed consistent across them: about 5600 to 6000 K for day, around 3000 K incandescent for night [1:08:29-1:09:01].

**Use it:** For multi-shot packages, set one large soft source as the scene base and add shape only where it does not change direction between cuts.
