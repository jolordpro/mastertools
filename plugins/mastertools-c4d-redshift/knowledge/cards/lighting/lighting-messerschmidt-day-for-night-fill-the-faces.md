Day-for-night was chosen so the whole environment appears lit, which fixed lights could not do. Sunlight is contrasty and underexposing it puts faces easily five stops under, so a lot of fill was added to keep faces felt. Tests showed the actors squinting under the fill, so sunglass-tint contact lenses were made for them. [§4]

**Use it:** When darkening a scene, check faces separately and give them their own fill.
