She says cold-grey for sad works but often a result that does not fit expectation causes the strongest reaction; a dark disturbing scene lit brightly and colourfully reads very differently from the expected way [14:42-15:45]. Because that is subjective, they run lighting studies as paintings ahead of time to explore options before settling [15:13-15:45]. Colour impact also comes from contrast between neighbouring shots over time (bright to dark, green to red); the change matters more than any single colour [17:18-17:49].
**Use it:** Before committing a look, make quick paint-over studies of at least one conventional and one against-expectation option; judge by the cut, not the single frame.
