Early digital (Sony F900 era, first Arri and Red cameras) with about 10 to 12 stops could not bloom highlights organically [34:44-35:17]. He gives film about 13.5 stops and a new camera, captioned 'Barano' (probably Sony Burano), 15 to 16 stops, which gives the bloom and non-electronic highlights [35:17-35:49].
He adds that film still allows in-camera changes (shutter, colour temperature, frame rate) that digital locks at record [36:19].

**Use it:** Highlight roll-off in a render or grade should keep a soft shoulder rather than clip hard; treat 'clippy video' as a missing-range problem.
