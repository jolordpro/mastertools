In Encanto the image is rich, but built "with criteria": environments are made so they do not turn into objects of interest, and artists play tone and detail to reduce or emphasise parts in execution as well as in design [08:32-09:43]. His order of priority for the frame: first clear communication, the viewer looking where story and performance need, then context (time of day, period), then emotion, such as intimacy or distance, and the character is not always the centre [06:24-08:32]. The light should not read as a theatre light that becomes obvious and distracts, and that sensibility takes years to refine [09:43-10:14].

**Use it:** Review each frame in this order: is it clear where to look, does it say when and where, does it feel right. Cut or quiet any detail that pulls the eye off the subject.
