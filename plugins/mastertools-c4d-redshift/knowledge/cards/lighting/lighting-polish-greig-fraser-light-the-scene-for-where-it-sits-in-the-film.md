Greig Fraser's answer to how to light a scene [00:01-00:32]: understand why it is in the film, where the story has come from and goes to, and what it means to that part. His Dune Part Two example: the ending on the sand dune had to echo the earlier moments of innocence and water between the two leads, and the look in her eye at the end was unlike anything seen before. The first conversation was about getting there timing-wise [00:32].
**Use it:** In lighting or look briefs for a piece in a series, write one line on what earlier beat this one echoes and what it changes.
