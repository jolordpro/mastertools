Sergei Volkov [10:58-17:20]: one lamp had a volumetric cone and the other none, so they read as different worlds; give both some. The lamp that is the source blended into the wall like a painting continuation, so the viewer must search; change its material, brighten it, or rim it. A small red cigarette tip is a contrasty secondary point: let it pulse up and down by animating its matte gain, brightest when he pops in.

**Use it:** Check at a glance where the light comes from; if you have to search, separate the object.
