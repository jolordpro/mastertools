Anyone who has lit knows it is hard to make a sharp shaft that does not throw multiple shadows people can pass through and still feels like one source; high-output LED units let them do what he thought impossible [02:38-03:09]. In the desert it is easy to keep the frame as drawn lines, dunes and a small person, and the simpler the frame the better for a story about the psyche [03:09-03:33].

**Use it:** For a god-ray look, check shadows from every object: one source should produce one shadow.
