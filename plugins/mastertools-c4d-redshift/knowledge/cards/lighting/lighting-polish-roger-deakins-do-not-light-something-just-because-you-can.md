A CineD article on the launch of the Team Deakins podcast (May 2020) pulls the quote "Don't light something just 'cos you can light it" and pairs it with the same rule for camera moves: do not move the camera just because you can [cined §1]. The article is about restraint and a peaceful, low-tech-talk podcast covering beginnings, collaboration, practical lighting, composition and lens choice [cined §1]. The No Film School page does not contain the quote. The earlier claim that he distrusts flat light and over-reliance on post fixes is not on either saved page (checked 2026-10-30), so it is dropped.

**Use it:** The coach cuts lights that do not add shape; a fill that flattens a face is first to go.
