She calls free world-building awful as well as awesome because you can do anything, so where do you start and what are the rules? The team kept basing the Land of the Dead on things seen in Mexico so it still feels Mexican yet fantastical. Research trips let real light and colour shape her approach, and she picked pieces she felt and saw to bring back to the film [§2].

**Use it:** When any look is possible, pick one real place as the rule source and note what you will bend.
