StudioBinder [08:52-12:57]. Soften: softbox cloth, diffusion paper, natural diffusers (cloud, fog, smoke), or bounce off reflectors; a bead board scatters differently from foam core, which bounces evenly. Cut or shape: flags (opaque boards) block spill, barn doors on the light itself, egg crates stop spill, cookies (patterned boards) make dappled light. Reduce: neutral density gel on a light or a window. Colour: gels used with colour temperature. Lamps: tungsten (warm, powerful), HMI (daylight, stronger), fluorescent (soft, cool), Kino Flo tube banks, LED (control of colour and strength). Tools to measure: light meter (incident, plus spot), waveform, histogram, false colour.

**Use it:** Maps to CG: a flag is a blocker object or light linking, a cookie is a gobo texture on a light, barn doors are spot or area-light shaping.
