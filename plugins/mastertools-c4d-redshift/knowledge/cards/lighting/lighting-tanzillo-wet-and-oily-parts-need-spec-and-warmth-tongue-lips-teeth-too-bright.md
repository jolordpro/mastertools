- Tongue: spec reads dehydrated; he says this is a note he gives often. Add a little warmth and specularity so the mouth is "the worst place" with sharp teeth and wet tongue [31:16].
- Lips: more spec than the skin gets [42:10].
- Teeth flashing bright on a turn: he likes that it exists (danger) but it is too strong and has no light direction behind it; pull it down as the character wraps around [24:21-25:29].
- Scales: a lizard whose scale is too small and too consistent; vary scale by body region (bigger at shoulder), keep the belly smoother and the jaw smoother (armour on top) [27:06-30:13].

**Use it:** wetness checklist for creature or character work: eyes, tongue, lips, nostrils.
Transcript support: U1 staging card; here teeth get SSS only at the thin edges and a green cast from blue white lights is pulled (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TdTxV_ACVhU [00:37])
