The key is thinking in range and knowing, without hours with a meter, that one part of the room will fall to black or hold little detail and a hotspot will not go nuclear [03:31-04:03]. He used to obsess; he stopped being a slave to the meter, takes one reading of a scene and then judges without it [04:03-04:48].

**Use it:** Take one exposure reference per setup and judge the rest by eye, as with a scene's average.
