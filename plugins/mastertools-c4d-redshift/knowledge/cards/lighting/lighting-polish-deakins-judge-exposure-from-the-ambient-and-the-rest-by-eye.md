Asked how to expose a low-key shot with a couple of practicals and daylight, Deakins says he judges exposure from the ambient light, setting his stop where he wants the daylight to fall, and judges practical level and other detail by eye [§3]. He adds that there are many ways to judge exposure, his is one, and a spot meter can give an exact reading if used correctly [§3]. To a first-time shooter he advises jumping right in at full speed [§3].

**Use it:** Offer ambient-first metering as one option and tell users to check the rest by eye, not as a rule.
