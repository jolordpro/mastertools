Beginner overview of eight VFX steps by a director-cinematographer (Brad Hamilton), low confidence, order as stated: tracking, modeling, rigging and animation, texturing and shading, simulation and particles set up and baked before lighting, lighting and rendering, compositing [01:13-05:20]. Two lists are reusable. Lighting a CG element into a plate must match four properties of the light: quality, quantity, colour and direction [04:18-04:48]. Studios export many passes so comp has control [04:48]. Comp combines plate, stock, matte paintings, sims and passes, and must match overall white levels, colour values, edge detail and lens imperfections [05:20]. Sims are baked before the lighting step [03:47]. Little else is new; the closing section is encouragement.

**Use it:** The four-property list works as a quick review prompt when an element sits wrong; for sports graphics replace "plate" with "stadium/studio footage".
