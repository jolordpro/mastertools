Natural light could not dig through the reflections of two masks, so the gaffer and costume designer fitted remotely controlled bi-colour LEDs inside the helmet with a strip of Depron around them so the dots could be photographed but were softened [06:24-07:27]. He could dim them and switch between daylight and tungsten. He says roughly 95 percent of the film was lit with LEDs, with a few bounced HMIs and tungsten for time-bending moments; the tent and ship were LED-driven [07:27-08:30].

**Use it:** For CG faces behind visors, add a controllable local fill inside the visor and keep it dimmable and colour-switchable per shot.
