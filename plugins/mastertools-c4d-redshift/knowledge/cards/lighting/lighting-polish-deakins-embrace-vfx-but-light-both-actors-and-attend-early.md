Though not a fan of VFX, Deakins used it for the Jarhead oil fires and in Blade Runner 2049, and says to be open to technology that realises the idea [§2]. For the Hologram Sync scene the VFX team wanted one woman on blue screen, but he insisted on shooting both women with his lighting so it looked as he wanted [§2]. He joins location scouting, set design and key style talks so the location allows planned camera moves and lighting [§2]. For 1917 he chose a camera rig light enough to run for hours over rough terrain, since no camera is perfect and each has strengths [§2].
