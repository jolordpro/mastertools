Ambient is the light of what you see through the windows: blue sky, green trees, grey-brown bark, grey pavement, sometimes a white building [29:00-30:05]. For years he built bounce boards checkered with blue, grey, brown and white squares spread evenly, and lit into them, so the ambient had the right mix of colour [30:05-31:08].
He says that bounce changed the way he lights [30:36]. (He lists green trees in the scene, then gives the squares as blue, brown, grey, white.)

**Use it:** For a dome or HDRI, sample the actual surroundings for colour and mix ground, sky and wall tints rather than one grey-blue fill.
