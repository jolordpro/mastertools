Setup: ambient env fill toned down by about a quarter to half a stop for the dark scene, so there are no pure blacks [11:40]. A hard top-back highlight (forehead and helmet) from a small light (exposure around 15 to 20 in his Arnold units), and a plane light blocker (with density) scaled and moved to feather the light off the shoulder [13:48][14:49]. The face beam is a small, horizontal area light with narrow spread and high exposure (he tried 18), which gives super harsh shadows; enlarge a little and shrink spread again to soften [15:20][17:02]. Rounded edges soften the beam; a warm yellow beam against a strong blue rim and white sun gives separation [17:32]. Presenter says these setups make portfolio renders stand out for studios [09:31].

**Use it:** recipe for a dramatic spot look; the exposure numbers are Arnold-specific and not directly portable to Redshift.
