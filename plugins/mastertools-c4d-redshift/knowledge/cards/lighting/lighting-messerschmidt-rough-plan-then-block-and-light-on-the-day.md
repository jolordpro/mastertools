Highly structured scenes were discussed at length beforehand; others, like the living-room argument, were worked out by Mann and the actors on the day. Messerschmidt watched the rehearsal, agreed coverage, then lit relatively fast [32:15-33:16].
Reason: it is disrespectful to tell actors where to stand from an office plan, and dramatic scenes should not feel rigid [32:45-33:47].
Race scenes involved safety and location logistics (roads closed, viewing areas, truck parking), worked out with the AD, producers and locations [33:47].

**Use it:** Plan the logistic and look in advance; leave blocking-dependent decisions until the action is known.
