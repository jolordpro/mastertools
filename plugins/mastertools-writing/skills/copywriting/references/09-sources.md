# Sources and reading list

- **Agency craft:** Luke Sullivan, *Hey Whipple, Squeeze This* (1st 1998; 3rd 2008; 6th 2022, Wiley); Dave Trott, *Predatory Thinking*, *Creative Mischief*, *One Plus One Equals Three*; Rory Sutherland, *Alchemy*; Bob Levenson, *Bill Bernbach's Book*; Paul Feldwick, *The Anatomy of Humbug*.
- **Seth Godin:** *Permission Marketing*; *Purple Cow*; *All Marketers Are Liars / Tell Stories*; *Tribes*; *This Is Marketing*; seths.blog.
- **Brand science:** Byron Sharp, *How Brands Grow*; Romaniuk & Sharp, *How Brands Grow Part 2*; Jenni Romaniuk, *Building Distinctive Brand Assets*; Mark Ritson's Marketing Week columns.
- **Effectiveness:** Binet & Field, *The Long and the Short of It* (IPA 2013), *Media in Focus*; Peter Field, *The Crisis in Creative Effectiveness* (IPA 2019); Orlando Wood, *Lemon* (2019), *Look Out* (2021); Kantar on humour.
- **Direct response:** Ogilvy, *Confessions of an Advertising Man*, *Ogilvy on Advertising*; Schwartz, *Breakthrough Advertising*; Hopkins, *Scientific Advertising*; Caples, *Tested Advertising Methods*; Sugarman, *The Adweek Copywriting Handbook*; Halbert, *The Boron Letters*; Collier, *The Robert Collier Letter Book*.
- **Conversion and voice:** April Dunford, *Obviously Awesome*, *Sales Pitch*; Ann Handley, *Everybody Writes*; Mailchimp Content Style Guide; Monzo tone of voice (monzo.com/tone-of-voice); GOV.UK writing guidance; NN/g tone-of-voice articles; Copyhackers; Marketing Examples; VeryGoodCopy; Unbounce Conversion Benchmark Report 2024.
- **AI writing:** Doshi & Hauser, *Science Advances* 2024; Moon, Green & Kushlev 2025; Wikipedia "Signs of AI writing".
- **Exemplary work:** D&AD Annual, The One Show, Cannes Lions, Communication Arts Advertising Annual; *Art & Copy* (2009 documentary).
- **Reference library (read directly, October 2026):** Sullivan, *Hey Whipple, Squeeze This*, 3rd ed. (Wiley, 2008); Ben Burns, Copy-Arsenal, Style Guide Kit guidebook and Pebble style-guide template, and *Paid in Full* with its email templates (The Futur); Column Five toolkits (brand strategy, voice questionnaire, competitive analysis, personas, B2B and content strategy, brand identity, long-term strategy); Onboardly/Column Five *Ultimate Guide to Content Distribution*; The Futur creative brief questionnaire, first-meeting worksheet, pitch evaluation, personal branding and visual vocabulary worksheets; SCADpro customer-discovery questions; brand guides from Hyundai, the LA Chargers and Hamilton Family Brewery; Blind and BLVR capabilities decks; Found's *Intelligent Beauty* book.
