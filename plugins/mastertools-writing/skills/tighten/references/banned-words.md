# Banned words

The kill-list. Add your own entries. Scrub every one of these on the Ship pass. This file is read by
`scripts/tighten.py` (one entry per line, `#` lines ignored). Single words match on
the token; multi-word or hyphenated entries match on substring.

The point is not the specific words. It is the tell. Every entry here is a word
that signals LinkedIn-brain or ChatGPT-default. When one shows up, the sentence
was reaching for the sound of importance instead of saying the thing. Replace it
with the plain verb or the real noun underneath.
