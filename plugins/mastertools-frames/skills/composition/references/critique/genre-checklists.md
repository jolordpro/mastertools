# Checklists by job

## Quick answer
- Pick the checklist for the job, run it after the rubric. Each item links to the facet that explains it.

## Portrait (photo or frame)
- Eyes sharp and lit; catchlight present (`light`, `texture-detail`).
- Look room if the face turns (`spatial-composition`).
- Background darker or calmer than the face (`value-contrast`).
- No crop at neck, wrists, knees unless intended (`framing-cropping`).
- Lens flattering for distance (`photo-craft`).

## Landscape
- One dominant element (mountain, tree, shoreline) (`hierarchy-focal-point`).
- Foreground anchor for depth (`depth-space`).
- Horizon level and placed by intent (`line-direction`, `framing-cropping`).
- Aerial perspective in background (`depth-space`).
- Light angle reveals texture (`light`).

## Sports action still
- Peak moment, face visible, object of play in frame (`sports-action`).
- Moving into the frame (`spatial-composition`).
- Background quiet (`clutter-peripheral`).

## Poster or key art
- One idea readable at thumbnail size (`clutter-peripheral`).
- Title placement in planned negative space (`negative-space`, `typography`).
- Strong silhouette or shape (`shape`).
- Hierarchy: image or title first, then credits (`hierarchy-focal-point`).
- Works in all required ratios (`reframing`).

## Social thumbnail or feed image
- Gist in under a second at phone size (`clutter-peripheral`).
- Big face or big word; high value contrast (`value-contrast`).
- Few elements; avoid fine text (`typography`).
- Safe from platform UI overlays (`reframing`).

## Lower third (broadcast)
- Inside graphics safe 90% (`broadcast-standards`).
- Not covering mouth or key action.
- Name > title in hierarchy; contrast ratio passes (`accessibility-legibility`).
- Hold long enough to read twice (`broadcast-standards`).
- Motion settles before reading (`motion-principles`).

## Full-screen stat graphic
- One hero number or comparison (`hierarchy-focal-point`, `scale`).
- Grid inside safe area (`grids-layout`).
- Background motion slow and low contrast (`motion-principles`).
- Team colors separated by value, not only hue (`color`, `accessibility-legibility`).

## Score bug and persistent graphics
- Fixed position, minimal motion (`broadcast-standards`).
- No overlap with play-critical areas.
- Legible over bright and dark video.

## Interview framing (film or broadcast)
- MCU or MS with look room toward interviewer (`shot-grammar`).
- Eyes near upper third, comfortable headroom.
- Background separated by light or depth of field (`depth-space`, `light`).
- Space reserved for lower third.

## Title card or open
- First and last frames strong as stills (`motion-principles`).
- Intensity curve matches music (`visual-structure`).
- Reveal order matches reading order.

## Logo or mark
- Reads at the smallest size it will run (score bug, social avatar, favicon); judge it there, not on the artboard (`scale`, `accessibility-legibility`).
- Works in one colour and reversed; legibility never depends on a hue change or a gradient (`value-contrast`, `color`).
- Recognisable as a filled silhouette (`shape`).
- Could it be simpler? Remove one part; if it still reads, keep it out (`fluency-complexity`).
- Memorable after one or two views; fits the brand and audience; no category clichés.
- Clear space and minimum size defined; over video it sits on a quiet zone or a plate (`negative-space`, `broadcast-standards`).

## Web or app screen
- Clear primary action; hierarchy by size and contrast (`hierarchy-focal-point`).
- Grid and spacing consistency (`grids-layout`).
- Contrast passes WCAG (`accessibility-legibility`).
