# Master photographers

## Quick answer
- Cartier-Bresson: geometry plus timing. Adams: planned tonal structure. Fan Ho: light shapes and shadow geometry. Leiter: layered color and obstruction. Salgado: monumental black and white with deep value. Leifer: sports geometry from bold vantage points.
- Common thread: they pre-visualized structure, then waited or worked for the moment.

## Case studies
### Henri Cartier-Bresson (1908 to 2004)
- "Decisive moment": capturing the instant when content and form peak together (*Images à la sauvette*, 1952). `DOC` concept; wording `[Unverified]`
- Trained in painting; known for strong geometric organization. Golden-section overlays on his work are often `RETRO`.
- Lesson: find the stage (lines, light, background), then wait for the actor.

### Ansel Adams (1902 to 1984)
- Zone System with Fred Archer: visualize final print tones before exposure. `DOC` `[Unverified details]`
- Grand landscapes with deep tonal range, foreground anchors, dramatic skies.
- Lesson: value planning is composition.

### Fan Ho (1931 to 2016)
- Hong Kong street scenes built from shafts of light, smoke, shadows and small figures. `[Practice] [Unverified]`
- Lesson: light shapes as the main compositional elements; figures as accents.

### Saul Leiter (1923 to 2013)
- Color photographs through rain, glass and reflections; large dark or blurred areas framing a small focal patch. `[Practice] [Unverified]`
- Lesson: obstruction and layering create depth and intimacy; small color accents carry focus.

### Sebastião Salgado (1944 to 2025)
- Monumental black and white documentary work; deep blacks, epic scale, dramatic light. `[Practice] [Unverified]`
- Lesson: tonal drama and scale to give subjects dignity.

### Neil Leifer (sports)
- Overhead and ringside frames of Ali vs Liston (1965) as models of sports composition: graphic geometry, dominance, clean backgrounds. `[Unverified details]`
- Lesson: unusual vantage points simplify cluttered sports scenes.

## Patterns `[Infer]`
- Pre-visualization (stage first, actor second).
- Value structure carries black-and-white masters; small accents carry color masters.
- Simplification by vantage point and timing, not only by lens.
