# Typography as composition

## Quick answer
- Type is shape, value and hierarchy before it is words. Squint at a layout: type blocks should form clear masses. `[Practice]`
- Build hierarchy with 2 or 3 levels using size, weight and space, not five fonts. `[Practice]`
- Text over images needs a quiet zone or a plate; texture behind small text kills legibility. `[Practice; clutter evidence E-strong]`

## Hierarchy tools `[Practice]`
- Size (strongest), weight, color and value, case, position, space around.
- Modular type scale: sizes stepped by a fixed ratio (e.g. 1.25, 1.333, 1.5). The golden ratio is one option, not a requirement.
- Limit to one or two families.

## Three setting rules `[Practice]`
- **Flush left, ragged right; no first-line indents.** Separate paragraphs with space. Centre only short display lines (a title, a one-line full-screen).
- **Skip a weight.** Pair Regular with Bold, not Regular with Medium: neighbouring weights read as a mistake, not a choice.
- **Double or halve.** When two levels must read apart at a glance (hero number vs its label), step the size ×2 or ×0.5 rather than one notch of the modular scale.

## Reading comfort `[Practice] [Unverified exact numbers]`
- Line length about 45 to 75 characters for body text (Bringhurst).
- Leading about 1.2 to 1.5 × type size for body; tighter for display.
- Tracking: tighter for large display, looser for small caps and small sizes.

## Type and image `[Practice]`
- Place text in the image's negative space, aligned with image structure.
- Ensure luminance contrast (see `accessibility-legibility`).
- Use scrims, gradients or plates over busy footage.
- Do not let text sit on the subject's face or the focal point.

## Scanning patterns `[E-mixed] [Unverified]`
- F-pattern and Z-pattern come from eye tracking of text-heavy web pages. Useful for web and documents; do not generalize to images or posters.

## By medium
- Broadcast: heavy, open sans-serifs at large sizes; short lines; strong plates. `[Practice]`
- Posters: display type as the main image is valid; then type is the focal point.
- Motion: kinetic type follows motion principles; hold words long enough to read.
- Social vertical: large type, few words, inside the safe zone for platform UI.

## How to measure
- OCR boxes → size ranks vs intended reading order.
- Characters per line; line spacing ratio.
- Text vs background contrast ratio; background clutter under text boxes.

## Failure modes
- Too many sizes and weights; flat or chaotic hierarchy.
- Near-miss contrast: neighbouring weights or sizes a notch apart, so two levels look like one level set wrong.
- Long lines of small text on screen.
- Text over busy footage without a plate.
- Widows, orphans and awkward rags in display type.

## Critique questions
- What is read first, second, third?
- Do the type blocks form clean shapes in the layout?
- Is every line readable at the delivery size?
