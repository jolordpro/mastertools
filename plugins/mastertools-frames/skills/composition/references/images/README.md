# Canon images

## Quick answer
- 22 public-domain or CC0 images, approved on 2026-10-23 and downloaded the same day.
- Use them to show what a procedure or push move looks like. Open the file with Read to view it.
- The modern canon exemplars (BF, BG, SP, PS) are under copyright and are not here. Describe those by ID and link.

## Files


### Dow plates

- `dow-12-opposition.jpg` — Dow plate 12, Opposition. Serves: Dow principles (Procedure 7) · balance. Shows two lines meeting at a right angle as the simplest strong structure. A clean reference for "what is the frame built on". License: Public domain (published 1914; Project Gutenberg, US). Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45410
- `dow-16-subordination.jpg` — Dow plate 16, Subordination by size. Serves: Procedure 7 · PM-02 Strip to one hero. One ruling element, the rest tied to it by size. The textbook picture of hierarchy. License: Public domain (published 1914; Project Gutenberg, US). Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45410
- `dow-34-main-lines.jpg` — Dow plate 34, Landscape reduced to its main lines. Serves: Procedure 5 Payne armature · Procedure 11 crop and mass. A scene cut down to its few big lines. Exactly the step the skill asks for before naming a structure. License: Public domain (published 1914; Project Gutenberg, US). Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45410
- `dow-37-three-proportions.jpg` — Dow plate 37, A landscape in three proportions. Serves: Procedure 4 step 6 · PM-61 Rebuild vertically. One scene composed in three frame shapes. The 1914 version of a 16:9 to 9:16 rebuild. License: Public domain (published 1914; Project Gutenberg, US). Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45410
- `dow-39-masters-notan.jpg` — Dow plate 39, Dark and light harmonies from the masters. Serves: Procedure 7 step 7 · PM-10 Two or three masses. Master works blotted down to their dark masses. Shows what a notan pass should produce. License: Public domain (published 1914; Project Gutenberg, US). Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45410
- `dow-47b-corot-daubigny-hokusai.jpg` — Massing in two values, from Corot, Daubigny and Hokusai. Serves: Procedure 7 step 7 · Procedure 9 value grouping. The three masters the canon names, already reduced to two values by Dow himself. Saves three separate painting downloads. License: Public domain (published 1914; Project Gutenberg, US). Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45410
- `dow-notan3-hokusai-three-values.jpg` — Landscape compositions by Hokusai, three values. Serves: Procedure 7 step 4 · Procedure 4 value plan. The three-value step: black, white, one gray. A reference for the k=3 notan number. License: Public domain (published 1914; Project Gutenberg, US). Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45410
- `dow-58-value-steps.jpg` — Dow plate 58, Three, four, five values. Serves: Procedure 7 steps 8 to 9. Same subject at three, four and five values. Shows when more values add and when they turn to mush. License: Public domain (published 1914; Project Gutenberg, US). Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/45410

### Painters

- `caravaggio-calling-of-saint-matthew.jpg` — Caravaggio, The Calling of Saint Matthew (1599-1600). Serves: Painters · Caravaggio · PM-01 One light event · PM-13. One beam and a pointing hand lead to the subject. The canon calls this "one light, one focus". License: Public domain (painting 1600; faithful photo, Yorck Project). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Michelangelo_Caravaggio_040.jpg
- `rembrandt-night-watch-1920.jpg` — Rembrandt, The Night Watch (1642). Serves: Painters · Rembrandt · PM-13 Lightest against darkest at the focus. Light pools pick two figures out of a crowd. Good model for a busy frame with one clear read. License: Public domain (Rijksmuseum image). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Nightwatch_by_Rembrandt_-_Rijksmuseum.jpg
- `vermeer-milkmaid-1920.jpg` — Vermeer, The Milkmaid (about 1660). Serves: Painters · Vermeer · Procedure 9. One soft window light, calm verticals and horizontals. The quiet counterpart to Caravaggio. License: Public domain (Google Art Project scan). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Johannes_Vermeer_-_Het_melkmeisje_-_Google_Art_Project.jpg
- `hokusai-great-wave-met.jpg` — Hokusai, The Great Wave off Kanagawa (about 1830). Serves: Painters · Hokusai · Procedure 5 · PM-34 Radial break pattern. A huge near shape frames a tiny far one. Scale, diagonals and curves in one print. License: CC0 (Metropolitan Museum Open Access). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:冨嶽三十六景_神奈川沖浪裏-Under_the_Wave_off_Kanagawa_(Kanagawa_oki_nami_ura),_also_known_as_The_Great_Wave,_from_the_series_Thirty-six_Views_of_Mount_Fuji_(Fugaku_sanjūrokkei)_MET_DP141063.jpg
- `degas-place-de-la-concorde.jpg` — Degas, Place de la Concorde (1875). Serves: Painters · Degas · PM-30 Lead room · rule-breaking. Figures walk out of frame and get cut by the edge, on purpose. The best picture of an edge crop that works. License: Public domain (painting 1875). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Edgar_Degas_Place_de_la_Concorde.jpg
- `monet-impression-sunrise.jpg` — Monet, Impression, Sunrise (1872). Serves: Painters · Monet · colour facet. The sun has the same lightness as the sky, so it glows. Teaches colour contrast without value contrast. License: Public domain (painting 1872). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Claude_Monet,_Impression,_soleil_levant.jpg
- `whistler-nocturne-old-battersea-bridge.jpg` — Whistler, Nocturne: Blue and Gold, Old Battersea Bridge (about 1872-75). Serves: Dow cites Whistler · Procedure 7 caution. A deliberately narrow, dark value range. Shows the skill why low key is a style, not a failed notan. License: Public domain (Whistler died 1903). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:James_McNeill_Whistler_-_Nocturne_en_bleu_et_or.jpg
- `hiroshige-sudden-shower-met.jpg` — Hiroshige, Sudden Shower over Shin-Ohashi Bridge (1857). Serves: Dow cites Hiroshige · Procedure 8 · rule-breaking. Parallel rain lines that Bacher says to avoid, used on purpose. A case for a rule broken well. License: CC0 (Metropolitan Museum Open Access). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ōhashi_Atake_no_yūdachi-名所江戸百景_大はしあたけの夕立-Sudden_Shower_over_Shin-Ōhashi_Bridge_and_Atake_(Ōhashi_Atake_no_yūdachi),_from_the_series_One_Hundred_Famous_Views_of_Edo_(Meisho_Edo_hyakkei)_MET_DP123602.jpg
- `sesshu-haboku-1920.jpg` — Sesshu, Splashed Ink Landscape (1495). Serves: Dow cites Sesshu · Procedure 7 · negative space. A few ink masses and a lot of empty paper. The extreme case of two values and open space. License: Public domain (Tokyo National Museum, 1495). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sesshu_-_Haboku-Sansui.jpg

### Gurney value grouping

- `pyle-the-mermaid.jpg` — Howard Pyle, The Mermaid (1910). Serves: Procedure 9 Gurney value grouping. Gurney's example: many tones fuse into a few large shapes even with figures overlapping. License: Public domain (Pyle died 1911). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Mermaid_(1910)_by_Howard_Pyle.jpg
- `sargent-padre-sebastiano-met.jpg` — Sargent, Padre Sebastiano (about 1904). Serves: Procedure 9 Gurney value grouping. Gurney's example: hat links to coat, papers link to sheets, one oval of light on the face. License: CC0 (Metropolitan Museum Open Access). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Padre_Sebastiano_MET_DT2990.jpg
- `forbes-the-seine-boat.jpg` — Stanhope Forbes, The Seine Boat (1904). Serves: Procedure 9 Gurney value grouping. Gurney's example: dark clothes and net kept flat so they frame the light shapes. License: Public domain (Forbes died 1947; painting 1904). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Stanhope_Forbes_The_Seine_Boat_1904.jpg

### Photographs

- `adams-tetons-snake-river-nara.jpg` — Ansel Adams, The Tetons and the Snake River (1942). Serves: Photographers · Adams · PM-10 · Procedure 5 S-curve. Value planned before exposure, plus a river S-curve. The one Adams photo that is public domain. License: Public domain (US government work, National Archives). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:%22The_Tetons_-_Snake_River,%22_Grand_Teton_National_Park,_Wyoming.,_1933_-_1942_-_NARA_-_519904.jpg
- `muybridge-horse-in-motion-1920.jpg` — Muybridge, The Horse in Motion (1878). Serves: PM-03 Pick the beat · Procedure 6 step 6 outtake compare. A burst of frames on one sheet. The skill can practise picking the strongest beat from a sequence. License: Public domain (1878; Library of Congress). Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:The_Horse_in_Motion_high_res.jpg
