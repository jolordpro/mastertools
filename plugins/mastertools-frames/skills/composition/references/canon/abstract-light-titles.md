# Canon: abstract light and line title frames

## Quick answer
- The light is the subject. There is no mark to resolve into until the final card, so the frame lives on its value plan.
- The pattern in the user's references: **a black field (60-82% of pixels under L* 8), one light event (1-11% clearly bright), and a calm
  area for type (17-25% of the frame as the largest empty rectangle)**. Measured with `scripts/value_plan.py`.
- Counterexamples that still work do it with a full light field and one dark accent (AL-05, AL-06): pick one plan, don't mix.

## What separates exceptional from solid
1. **One light event in a dark field** (AL-01 to AL-04). Solid work lights every strand equally and reads as mid-key mush.
2. **Colour shifts along the length** for depth (hot core, body, darker tails). One colour end to end reads flat.
3. **A planned title zone**: the event sits off-centre and leaves a calm third (AL-01, AL-03).
4. **Shape is smooth**: round arcs, no creases or kinks; a 3/4 view keeps arches round (straight down a curve flattens it).
